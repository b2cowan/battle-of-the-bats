The plan-ending words, from the owner’s chair

Written 2026-11-17. One purpose: read what a club owner, a coach and the operator read when a plan is cancelled, moved down, kept, past due or ended, in the windows, the emails and the help. Nothing here is a price, a tax or a gate.

What changed

  • Cancel and move down end on the renewal date. Nothing is taken away early, no more plan payments are taken, and Keep [Plan] undoes it.
  • One set of words. “Payment method”, “Reactivate”, and a past-due plan is told its end may come sooner, on the day the payment provider stops trying.
  • A plan change waits behind a waiting cancel or move: “Keep [Plan] first, then change it.”
  • The help says the same in the club, Coaches Portal and operator help articles, and the operator’s email log now names two emails “Plan ended” and “Premium ended”.

It passes when

  • A–C The paying club’s Cancel window, waiting state, refused change and Keep all use the new words.
  • D–E The trial and past-due windows never promise the date or “your card”.
  • F Moving to the free plan waits for the renewal date and refuses a change behind it.
  • G–H The Coaches Portal and the operator read the same story.
  • I–K The emails, the help articles and the rail say what the windows say.
