# Facilitated Payments (Scoping) — PM Brief

**What it does:** Lets families and teams pay their organizer *through* FieldLogicHQ — online checkout at tournament registration, payment status appearing automatically on the organizer's registration dashboard — with the platform earning a small fee on each transaction, the model every dominant Canadian competitor uses. This brief covers the **scoping effort**; the build plan comes out the other end for an H1 2027 launch.

**Where it stands (updated 2026-07-28):** The owner-commissioned investigation has answered most of the scoping questions ahead of the Q4 2026 schedule. We know the market rate band (about 3–3.5% + $1–2 per transaction, normally paid by the family), the recommended money-flow design (payments go straight to the organizer's own payment account — we never hold the money — which is both the lower-liability and the lighter-regulation shape), the cheaper Canadian bank-debit rail that makes the economics work, and the build size (usable first version in ~3–4.5 weeks; complete feature in ~6–10 weeks). Organizers who prefer e-transfer/cash keep the manual tracking they have today — online collection is an addition, not a replacement.

**Why it matters:** A flat subscription alone leaves the biggest source of value — money flowing through the platform — uncaptured, and is a hard sell against free competitors. Payments revenue scales with club size and funds the free floors.

**Who benefits:** Organizers (no more chasing e-transfers; registration dashboard fills itself in), families (pay online at signup), and the business (usage-based revenue).
