Club events, then camps: a club puts its banquet, champs day and camps on its calendar and site, and families sign up without an account

Drawn on Riverdale Minor Ball as of Monday, April 12, 2027: a house league of 14 divisions, six rep teams, a Lions Park permit, and its first summer camp. Stage 5 is the base (an event, where it shows, a free sign-up). Stage 7 is the layer (sessions, spots, a price, a waitlist, a roster and the books). They are drawn on one page because a camp is an event. Built on Stage 4’s form, the house-league sign-up path and its “Your sign-up” page, the club schedule’s permit slots, the club calendar, the club site and Stage 6’s pattern for a club-owned thing with a price. Card payment is drawn as it will be once online payments are switched on, and as it must be until then. Click any number, tag or red flag to read what it refers to. The last tab holds your asks; the first answer is my recommendation and is already selected.

A champs day gathers that day’s house-league games under one event; it doesn’t invent playoffs

House league has no playoff, round or final in the code. Playoffs exist only for tournaments . So a Champs day names a house-league season. Every game that season on the event’s day at its venue shows under it, scheduled as today by the league admin. Nothing about a game changes, and its scores come from where they already do. A sponsor can show on the event, because “a club event” joins sponsorship’s places with this stage (ordered 2026-11-20).

What this leaves out, on purpose

  • No brackets, no “final” flag on a game. Making house-league playoffs is a project of its own (ask 5, option B), sized separately and kept in the ideas backlog if not chosen.
  • No team schedule is touched. A game is shown under the event; it is not moved or renamed (D3).

What holding time does, and doesn’t do

  • Only open time is taken, from either side: the event’s window ticks open slots; the schedule’s “Give it to” offers events.
  • A team’s slot is never taken by an event. The club frees it on the schedule first, as today, and the team is told the way it is today (D3).
  • The camp’s calendar entries come from its sessions, not from the slots, so a camp held at a school gym with no permit still shows.
  • Removing a session hands its slots back as Open, with “handed back by Summer skills camp”, the shipped wording.

  • A child’s answers open from the child, never in a list or export, as Stage 4 ruled for health.
  • Withdrawals arrive as requests, as in house league. A refund is the club’s call, and for a taxable camp it issues a credit note, as Stage 6 does (/accounting).

They show only for this session, from the day before it starts to the day after it ends. Each opening is recorded, and if the record can’t be saved, the answer doesn’t open. No money, no email, no address, no other sessions, no download or print. An instructor must be 18 or older, as every account holder is.
