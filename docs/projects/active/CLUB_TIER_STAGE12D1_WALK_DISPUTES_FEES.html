Disputes and Stripe fees

Written 2026-10-27; steps C7–C8, E8–E9 and part F added the same day for the owner’s rulings on Part 4’s asks (Part 5b). One purpose: a card dispute is seen and answered by the one person who can, and every Stripe fee a club’s payments cause reaches that club’s bill, a club with no subscription included.

What this walk is for

Until now a card dispute was dropped without a word. You check that it shows on the payment, quietly for the desk and in amber for the owner who set up payouts; that only the owner gets Answer in Stripe; that Copy the facts holds what we know; that no refund is offered while it is open; and what a win and a loss do (parts A to D).

Then the money it costs: a card issued outside the club’s country, a dispute fee Stripe charged FieldLogicHQ, and the club’s own monthly invoice, which UAT Rep Club gets because it has no subscription for the fees to ride on, just like a complimentary club (part E).

Last, a family’s dispute is lost: “Email families who owe” leaves the family out, and one Email the pay link asks before it sends (part F; a team’s is C7–C8).

It passes when

  • A disputed payment stays paid and in the book while it is open. The owner sees amber and Answer in Stripe; everyone else sees it quietly, with the owner’s name as the one who answers.
  • No refund is offered on a payment under dispute; a bank that is only asking blocks nothing.
  • Won: plain paid again. Lost: the payment is taken back beside its receipt (nothing deleted), and the child or team owes again.
  • “Fraud” never appears. Nothing is sent to the family or team.
  • Every fee on the club’s list names a receipt and a day, never a child; the amounts come from Stripe or the published rate and are never typed.
  • The club’s own invoice arrives, is paid on Stripe’s page, and reads paid.
  • After a lost dispute, the bulk send leaves the family or team out; one Email the pay link asks first and sends when told to.
  • An answering fee Stripe gives back goes against the invoice that charged it, and never waits for a later fee.

Not in this walk

  • Paid twice: walk §395.
  • A bank debit that comes back, and its fee on the club’s invoice: it can’t be produced on localhost (as in 12c). It goes in the Amplify end-to-end pass. Since the owner’s ruling 3 it is billed at Stripe’s own figure, and not at all when Stripe charges nothing.
  • A cancelled club still seeing its Stripe fees list (ruling 2): cancelling UAT Rep Club would close every other walk on it. It goes in the end-to-end pass.
  • A fee taken off a subscription that was cancelled or discounted before its invoice (ruling 6): UAT Rep Club has no subscription. The end-to-end pass.
  • The email three days before an answer is due, and the unpaid invoice’s reminder: both are the daily job’s (running on dev since 2026-10-27), and both are emails, so they go in the Amplify end-to-end pass.
  • Emails, beyond the held boxes. Localhost cannot send.
  • The Payouts tab, leaving, and the payments terms: those are 12d-2.

Dev server
Running at http://localhost:3000. PAY_LINK_SECRET is set in .env.local.
Stripe windows
Three. Window 1 (payments, now with every dispute event):
C:\tools\stripe\stripe.exe listen --events-from '@accounts' --events "checkout.session.completed,checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded,checkout.session.async_payment_failed,checkout.session.expired,charge.dispute.created,charge.dispute.updated,charge.dispute.closed,charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn,charge.dispute.funds_reinstated,refund.created,refund.updated,refund.failed" --forward-to localhost:3000/api/payments/connect-webhook
Window 2 (the club’s account):
C:\tools\stripe\stripe.exe listen --events-from '@self' --events "v2.core.account[requirements].updated,v2.core.account[configuration.merchant].capability_status_updated,v2.core.account.closed" --forward-to localhost:3000/api/payments/connect-account-events
Window 3 (FieldLogicHQ’s own billing, for the invoice in part E):
C:\tools\stripe\stripe.exe listen --forward-to localhost:3000/api/billing/webhook
STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET in .env.local must be the value stripe listen --print-secret prints; restart the dev server if you change it.

Answering in Stripe
Answer in Stripe opens the club’s Stripe dashboard. In the dispute’s form, put winning_evidence or losing_evidence in the text box and submit; Stripe’s test mode then decides it within a minute. If that dashboard offers no form, run the helper with the record’s Stripe reference (it starts du_):
node --env-file=.env.local .probe/s12d1-p5-answer.cjs du_XXXX win
(or lose).
A team’s pay link
Find the team’s id with node scripts/db-query.mjs --dev -q "select id from teams where name='TEAM NAME'", then:
node -e "const c=require('crypto'),fs=require('fs');const s=fs.readFileSync('.env.local','utf8').split(/\r?\n/).find(l=>l.startsWith('PAY_LINK_SECRET=')).slice(16).trim();const id=process.argv[1];console.log('http://localhost:3000/uat-rep-club/uat-rep-club-invitational-2026/pay/'+Buffer.from(id.replace(/-/g,''),'hex').toString('base64url')+'.'+c.createHmac('sha256',s).update('team-pay:'+id).digest().subarray(0,16).toString('base64url'))" TEAM-ID
The monthly invoice
The club’s own invoice is sent on the 1st for the fees before it. To see one today, part E runs that monthly step as if it were next month:
node --env-file=.env.local --import ./tests/ts-resolver.mjs .probe/s12d1-p5-monthly.mjs
It uses the test key only, and bills every club with fees waiting on dev, in Stripe’s test mode.
