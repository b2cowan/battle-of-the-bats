# PM Brief — The Tournament plan's missing settings

**Plan:** [TOURNAMENT_PLAN_MISSING_SETTINGS_PLAN.md](TOURNAMENT_PLAN_MISSING_SETTINGS_PLAN.md) · status on the board.

**What it does:** gives the owner of a Tournament or Tournament Plus organization the two settings they have no page for
today: changing the organization's **name**, and **asking us to delete the organization**.

**Why it matters:** a typo in the organization's name at sign-up stays on every tournament page until the owner emails us
and we fix it in our own admin. Someone who wants to leave has to find an email address in the privacy policy. Every other
plan can do both from its own settings. (The web address can already be changed on the Tournament plan; it is not part of
this.)

**Who benefits:** the owner of a Tournament or Tournament Plus organization. Who else sees the name (read-only, or not at
all) is a question for the drawing. Clubs, leagues and Premium coaches are unchanged by this item; the Premium coach's
version is the sister project, retiring the old tournament-only menu.

**Expected impact:** the owner fixes their own name and sends their own deletion request, without contacting support. Both
end up looking the same as the Premium coach's settings, because the two are drawn in one session.

**Priority:** Medium-low. Nobody is stranded: the privacy policy's "ask us to close your account" works by email today,
and we can rename an organization from our admin. But it is a real dead end, and it is cheap, because what sits behind
both settings already exists and only the doors are missing.

**Order:** the shared drawing (with the Premium coach's settings), then `/legal` on the deletion request's wording, then the
build, then one walk.

**Success criteria:** a Tournament-plan owner and a Tournament Plus owner can each rename the organization and send a
deletion request from their own settings; the wording has passed `/legal`; the Premium coach's settings and these match in
shape; a club's Settings page is unchanged.

**Added 2026-11-25: "Who can enter" on a tournament.** A club hosting a Saturday jamboree for three neighbouring teams
needs a tournament that isn't advertised. Today a tournament is public or a draft. A new setting gives three answers:
**Anyone**, **Not listed** (off the club site, the directory and search; anyone with the link can open it) and **By
invitation** (one link for each team the host adds). It changes no plan: who may register themselves is still a Tournament
Plus feature, and on the free plan the host still adds every team, so there the choice only decides whether the event is
listed. It came from Board Ready's team sign-ups (ruled 2026-11-25). It is drawn in its own session, then ruled and built,
and the project closes after it. Success: a host runs an invitation-only jamboree that no stranger finds, and every
invited team gets in through its own link.
