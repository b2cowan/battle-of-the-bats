The families’ game emails, and the history that keeps them

Written 2026-10-28 for Stage 9d-2b, Part 4. One purpose; sign it off on its own.

What this walk is for

When a league admin publishes a division’s schedule, or sends the changes waiting on it, each family gets ONE email about its own children’s teams, and the season’s Email families history lists what was sent and who it missed. This walk reads, on dev, the words those emails will say (dev has no email key, so nothing is mailed) and the history that follows each press.

It passes when

  • Publish schedule’s “See the email” shows the real email for the household with the most children: one email, one part per child, only that child’s team’s games, and a named back to the question.
  • Changes to send’s “See their email” shows a moved game as Now / Was, Was naming only what changed, and a household with two children on two changed teams gets ONE email with both.
  • The season’s Email families history gets one Automatic row per press (Schedule published, Schedule changes), each counting its families.
  • A send that stopped partway reads Stopped, offers “Send to the 2 it missed”, and finishes from there; the division’s line says the same and links to it.
  • An urgent cancel can be sent on its own while another change keeps waiting.

Not in this walk

  • Real mail arriving. Dev has no email key: every household is recorded as skipped, so “reached 0” is right on dev. The first production send and the coaches’ copy are read there.
  • The coaches’ copy of a change and the coach’s own games page (Part 5). Walk U11’s teams have no coaches on dev.
  • The public schedule (walk §412), and editing a published game’s window (Part 3’s walk).
  • The help articles: they are rewritten once for all of 9d-2b in Part 7.
