What this walk is for

Drawn on the 9b drawings, tab "9b-1 Sign-up"; words in HOUSE_LEAGUE_STAGE9B1_COPY.md. Your four rulings from the build session apply: a child's price is recorded when they sign up; a division's pencil opens its whole record and saves as you go; a child waiting for review holds a spot; the date picker went first.

It passes when a season can be set up and opened only once its checklist is done, its dates open and close the form (and the page says whether the date or you closed it), a family sees each child's outcome first and gets one email and one reference, nobody can take the same spot twice or jump the waitlist, and the club gets one notice per sign-up.

Not in this walk: the Registrations screen (rebuilt in 9b-2; its old buttons still work), correcting a price, withdrawals and refunds, the Owed / Still owing figures, teams on the status page, online payment, help articles (/docs runs with this build: check them last if they are in).
