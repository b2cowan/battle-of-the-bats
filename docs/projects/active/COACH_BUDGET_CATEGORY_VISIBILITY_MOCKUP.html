Coach Portal · Budget Plan Design decision sheet 9 September 2026

The Category Goes Missing

A coach files budget money under a category. The product then throws that category away — twice, on two different screens, for two different reasons.

Decided — 9 September 2026

Forgetting list: Option A. Plan: Option 2 — the category is the shelf on both sides, on every surface (the owner found the Statement and By activity already do this; the plan, the grid, the exports and the Months view converge on them). Duplicate words: two rows are fine when a coach tracks two things; the standard vocabulary gets its own review. Plan: COACH_BUDGET_CATEGORY_IS_THE_SHELF_PLAN.md. Round 1 below is kept as drawn.

01 — The finding

One root cause, and it explains both of your questions

Every budget word a coach picks lives in a category, and the picker is organised entirely around that: TOURNAMENTS, FUNDRAISING, SPONSORSHIP. It is the only vocabulary they are shown at the moment of choosing.

Then the category disappears. In the What am I forgetting? list it survives only as a desktop tooltip — invisible on a phone — so fifty-seven words arrive as one undifferentiated wall. And on the money-in side of the plan it disappears completely: the row carries the item name alone, and the heading above it is not a category at all.

That is why "where is that tournament item I just put in the budget?" is the right question to be asking. It is the same missing thing, one screen later.

Before we go further — the third one is already your ruling

Your fundraising ruling of 8 September settled it: "Tournaments reports as other income, grouped as its own category inside other income, beside Other Income." The first half shipped. The second half — the grouping — appears once in the plan, inside the ruling, and never in a build step. It was recorded and never scheduled.

So this is not a decision to reopen. It is a decision to finish. The only open part is how far to finish it, which is §05.

Worth knowing — it is not only Tournaments

Every money-in category that is not literally Fundraising or Sponsorship lands in Other income, including any category a coach invents. Tournaments is simply the only standard one, which is what makes it look like a one-off. The moment a coach makes a "Bingo" category and puts money-in words in it, their bingo money reports as other income too.

06 — Recommendation

What I would do, and in what order

  • Option B for the forgetting list. It shows the seven words a coach most often forgets, in full, with their category — and indexes the fifty they already know about. Option A is the fallback if the asymmetry bothers you once you see it on your phone.
  • Option 2 for the plan, on the argument that one rule beats two: a coach files by category on both sides, so both sides should report by category. Option 1 is the lower-risk way to keep your ruling's promise if you would rather not move the grouping rule across four surfaces at once.
  • The duplicate vocabulary is its own decision, and it needs you. Merging two shelves that mean the same thing moves any budget line already filed under the loser, so it is a data change with a blast radius, not a spelling fix.

None of the three is blocked by the others; they can ship in any order.

What I need from you before anything is built

Question 1 — the forgetting list

A or B?

Open the phone specimen in §04 on your actual handset before answering. The whole case for B is that the seven money-in words are visible without a tap, and that only reads true at real size.

Question 2 — the plan

Finish the ruling as written, or go the whole way?

Option 1 is your 8 September ruling built exactly as recorded. Option 2 is the simpler end state and the bigger change. I lean to 2, and I would understand 1.

Question 3 — the standard vocabulary

What happens to the two Insurances?

Three ways out, in rising order of effort. Leave them — grouping by category at least makes them legible. Stop offering the older duplicates in the forgetting list while both categories stay — cheap, and no existing line moves. Merge the categories properly — the honest fix, and the one that moves data.

I would take the middle option now and hold the merge until you want to touch the standard vocabulary for other reasons.

Not in scope, deliberately

Colour coding by direction. Ruled out on 2 September — colour is reserved for cash, because cash is the one figure with a stable good/bad reading. Green on Grant and red on Umpire Fees would congratulate a coach for planning income and scold them for planning a cost, when both are just the plan being filled in.
