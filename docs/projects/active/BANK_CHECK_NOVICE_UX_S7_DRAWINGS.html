8 · Asks

Your three open questions from A18 come first, then six things the strip itself raised. Each has a recommendation. Then what the build will need, so you can rule knowing it.

1 · Does a second treasurer or coach see someone else’s saved work? (tab 6)

A club treasurer and an assistant treasurer, or a head coach and a team manager, can both write checks on the same account.

  • A · Mine only. Each person’s work is their own. The second person sees a normal card and one quiet line, “Avery Chen has a check of Oct 31, 2026 saved for later (Nov 20).” Either can save the check. The second to save is told “Oct 31, 2026 is checked already. Saving again replaces that check.” That sentence already ships.
  • B · The account’s one open check. Anyone who opens the account carries on the same work, with “Started by Avery Chen” on it. Two people can overwrite each other’s marks with no warning unless we build a lock or a who’s-editing rule.

Recommend A, with the quiet line. The saved check is already the shared record, and the replace warning already handles two people checking the same date. B makes one person’s unfinished work something another person can wreck. It needs a lock to be safe, and the working-draft ruling shut it out for that reason. The quiet line gives the coordination B was after, without the overwrite.

2 · How long does saved work last? (tab 7)

  • 14 days idle, as the browser does today.
  • 30 days from the last change.
  • Until the check is saved or the work is discarded.

Recommend 30 days from the last change. A statement comes monthly, so a treasurer who saves for later can come back with the next one in hand. 14 days was chosen for a browser on a shared computer, a risk the server removes. “Until discarded” leaves clutter nobody owns.

What the person sees: the notice when they press Save for later names the end date. In the last 7 days the line under the heading names it again in amber. No email is sent. Saved work also goes when the person loses write access to that account, when the account is deleted, and, for a coach, when the season rolls over or closes (it is live-season work).

3 · Both portals? (every tab)

Yes. The same card, the same strip and the same two buttons, in each portal’s own words (books and entries for the club; the Ledger and recording for the coach). Who sees Save for later is whoever can save a check today. At a club that is everyone who can open Accounting, since no read-only seat exists. On a team it is anyone with money write access; a view-only coach still sees no card.

  • Coach only: cash not deposited yet is fixed with the statement. It shows in the strip when typed, and Change edits it. The “Holding cash you haven’t deposited?” link moves into the fields before Start and into Change.
  • Club only: with two or more accounts, the Account pill says which account holds your saved work (tab 4). A coach has one account, the team’s, so there is nothing to choose.

4 · Kept as you go, or only when Save for later is pressed?

The ruling names a Save for later button. The question is what happens to someone who leaves without pressing it, for example to fix a line in the Ledger.

Recommend: from the moment Start is pressed, every change is kept as you go, as the browser does today. Save for later confirms it, names the end date and restarts the 30 days. Kept only on a press, a volunteer who opens the Ledger to fix a line loses their marks, which is worse than today. The resting sentence says it honestly: “Kept as you go, on any device you sign in on, until you save this check.”

5 · What Change does to marks (tab 3)

Recommend: a new balance keeps every mark (the marks are about lines, not the balance). A new date is a new statement, so the strip says before you confirm: “A new date clears the 1 line you marked. Changing only the balance keeps it.” Keep Oct 31 backs out. The same applies when “Check this date again” is pressed on a saved check while other work is saved: one question first, never a silent swap.

6 · A balance that matches first time costs one more press

Today a treasurer whose balance matches can type it and press Save once. With the strip, it is Start, then Save this check.

Recommend accept the extra press. The alternative is to show the band before Start and offer Save there too, which means two filled buttons in one card and a card that changes shape as you type. One press buys a card that always reads the same way: the statement, then the work, then the save.

7 · How the card tells the person the books moved (tab 5)

Recommend as drawn. A mark whose line was deleted is taken off, with the count, in the amber box that already ships. An edited line keeps its mark at its new amount, without a note. A day the account no longer takes (the start deleted, the season rolled over) shows the day’s shipped refusal. The box can’t name the line, because the work keeps which lines were marked, never their amounts or names. Keeping names would mean storing pieces of the books a second time, against your “keys, never figures” rule.

8 · Where saved work is found again (tab 4)

Recommend: on the card itself, where it was left, opening straight into the work. At a club with several accounts, the Account pill marks the account that holds it. The Overview’s bank line stays one line, as you ruled (S2), with no in-progress state of its own. That would be a second place telling the same story, and it would crowd the one-line rule.

9 · The words a volunteer reads once (tabs 2 and 4)

  • Notice on Save for later: “Saved for later. Pick it up here, on any device you sign in on, until Dec 22, 2026.”
  • Resting sentence (replaces “Kept in this browser only, until you save this check or sign out.”): “Kept as you go, on any device you sign in on, until you save this check.”
  • Strip: “Checking Oct 31, 2026 · Change”; the Change step’s “Use these” and “Keep Oct 31”. Your ruling put the date and the balance in the strip. The drawing shows only the date there, because the balance is the band’s first figure directly under it. Printed twice, 12px apart, it adds a row on a phone and says nothing new. Change still edits both. If you want the balance in the strip anyway, say so.
  • Start over moves from the restore line to the card’s foot, so it is always there once work has started.

Recommend these as the drawing’s words, settled with /marketing in the build.

One more call, from the design review: you ruled no confirmation on Start over when the work lived in one browser. Now it discards work kept on every device, perhaps an hour of marks. Recommend a one-line confirmation inside the card, “Start over? The 1 line you marked and the balance go.” with Start over / Keep working, and only when lines are marked. A card with nothing marked starts over at once, as today.
