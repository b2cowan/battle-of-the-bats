Where the "way up" arrow sits

Every drill-in screen in the coach portal carries a small arrow back to its parent. This sheet lays out how it's built today, the inconsistency that build causes across screens, and two ways to close it — all drawn at the real pixel sizes the coach sees.

Scope12 screens, 1 shared header Rev.2026‑09‑15 StatusFor review
Correcting the last note I described the back link as sitting on its own row above the title — it doesn't. An owner ruling on 2026‑08‑26 already moved it onto the title's own line, in the leading corner, specifically because a separate row "pushed the page down and read as a stray blue link." Figure 1 is what actually ships today; Figure 3 is the retired row, kept here only so it isn't re-proposed by accident.

iWhy the label survives on desktop but not on phone

House rule 3 says words become symbols on a phone, for every header control. The arrow already carries the destination in its accessible name either way — a screen reader still says "Back to Practice plans."

iWhy grey, not link-blue

The ruling that put this arrow here is explicit: on its own row, blue said "this is a link." Inside a row that also carries the record's name, blue would be the loudest thing in the row. The ink is the same secondary grey the header uses for quiet chrome.

!This is the whole complaint

The row itself is fine — one line, nothing wraps. What reads as misaligned is that a coach clicking from Practice Plans into a practice sees the page's identity (icon + name) jump sideways, and it jumps back the moment they leave. Roster, Schedule and every hub landing page never move; only drill-ins do, and by a different amount depending on the label's length.

+What it fixes

GAINEvery standard header's icon & title starts at the identical x-position, with a back link or without. It's also just the phone treatment (icon-only, aria-label carries the name) applied at every width, so it's one idiom, not two.

−What it costs

LOSSThe section icon — the clipboard that says "you're in Practice Plans" at a glance — disappears on every drill-in. A coach reads "back" instead of "practice plans," and the destination word is gone on wide screens too, not just on phone.

+What it fixes

GAINIdentical alignment everywhere, and nothing visible is lost — the destination word stays on wide screens exactly as it reads today. A label longer than the gutter truncates with an ellipsis rather than pushing the tile.

−What it costs

COSTRoughly 150px of permanently reserved, usually-empty space on the ~20 pages that never carry a back link — Overview, Roster, Schedule and the rest. It reads as a quiet left margin rather than a gap, but it is real space nothing else can use.

RECOMMEND

Option B

It's the only one of the two that costs nothing a coach would notice losing — the destination word stays exactly where it reads today, and the fix is purely a spacing change to one shared component. Option A is the leaner build and worth it only if there's appetite to push the portal's "words become symbols" idiom onto desktop as well, which is a bigger call than this one screen.

Either one is a single change to CoachPageHeader — it repaints all twelve back-linked screens at once, not a per-page fix. Per this repo's own process, a change touching every drill-in header gets its own mockup sign-off before build; this sheet is that sign-off, not the build itself.
