Who owns the look

Whether a club's tournaments wear the club's look or their own, and what the club's Public site editor and a tournament's Branding page offer under each case. Asks 9–13 are ruled; this page draws them, with your three 11-02 rulings (the banner goes, Tournament Plus falls back to plain, share images move into this stage). Everything here sits behind the preview switch until Stage 8. Specimens are drawn at true size: open this page on a phone for the phone frames.

The club's rule for its tournaments

One setting on the club's Public site editor, for League Plus, Club and Club · Association. It is applied when pages are shown and never writes a tournament's saved look, so switching back returns every tournament to exactly what it had (Ask 9). The owner and admins who can edit the club's Look set it (Ask 13). That is the same group as the Look row, which is the main reason to put it there.

Asking before "Always"

Choosing Always the club's look asks first only when at least one tournament has its own colours today; otherwise it saves at once, like the default. The question names what will change. "Has its own look" means its own colours; a tournament's own logo does not count, because it keeps it (Ask 10). The window follows the shipped question shape: a title, one sentence, the answers; the reassurance sits in the foot on the left; "Not now" has the focus; Escape or a tap outside leaves the choice where it was (the coach-names precedent).
