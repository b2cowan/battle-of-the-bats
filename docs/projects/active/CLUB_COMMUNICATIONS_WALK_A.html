Written 2026-11-17, after Session 1 (the engine, committed 95cac82d6 and 817c95523) and Session 2 (the place, committed 684ee0b38). One purpose: the place and who sees what. The coach and board windows are Session 3’s and Walk B’s.

What this walk is for

Families › Emails has become Communications, in the Club group after Families. You find it as the owner and email every family. Dana Kowalski, an admin who does not hold the Families book, does the same without ever landing on a Families screen. Riley Okonkwo, the registrar, sees the club-wide email with its message and counts and no family names, and the coaches’ email with only her house-league coaches named. Avery Chen, the treasurer, has no place yet. Then you turn Jules Moreau’s Communications switch off and every group-email button leaves his screens.

It passes when

  • 1–2 The owner finds Communications between Families and Public site, reads the list, and emails every family from it.
  • 3 An admin without the Families book emails families from Communications, never sees a one-family message or a Families list, and the old Emails address lands on Communications.
  • 4 The registrar sees the club-wide family email with no family names, the coaches’ email naming only house-league coaches, and not the rep-team email.
  • 5 The treasurer has no Communications row; its address says it isn’t theirs.
  • 6 With the switch off, the league admin has no place and no Email families button anywhere; on again, it all comes back.
  • 7–8 Families has two tabs; on a phone Communications is a More-sheet row and the bottom bar is unchanged.

Known before the walk (not defects of this walk)

  • The “?” on Communications opens the old emailing article, which still describes Families › Emails. The Close session rewrites it.
  • The treasurer’s “not yours” sentence reads “…and the people on the club’s board open it”, though a treasurer is on the board. Session 3 gives the treasurer the place (Email the board), so the sentence goes away for them.
  • No “Send again to these people” on the coaches’ email record: it opens the coach window, which is Session 3’s.
  • Nothing leaves the machine. Every family, guardian and coach address in this copy ends in .local, which the product never sends to. The sends are real; the mail service is never asked.
