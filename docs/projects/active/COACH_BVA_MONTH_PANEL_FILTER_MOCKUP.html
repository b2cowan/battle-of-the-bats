Money by Month · category panel

One rule for every figure you tap

Today, tapping any month's figure — on a category row or a single item — opens the identical whole-season list, no matter which month you actually tapped. The revision below narrows a month tap to that month's own items, and moves "show me everything" to the one place it belongs: the Total column, which currently does nothing when tapped at all.

The one rule
Category figure — the sum of its items tap a month → only that month's items Item figure — the sum of its budget lines
A category is an aggregate of items same rule, one level down ↓ An item is an aggregate of budget lines

Any month — only what's actually scheduled that month, whichever level you're looking at. Total — everything, unfiltered, for the season.

Stays exactly the same

  • The figure at the top of the panel — still that column's total, category or item.
  • Each item's amount — still its whole-season total, never divided by month.
  • The "Currently <date>" line under each item.
  • The edit link into an item's payment dates, for coaches who can write.
  • Read-only assistants see the same narrower list; nothing about access changes.

What changes

  • A month figure now lists only what's actually scheduled that month — at the category level (which items) and, the same way, at the item level (which of its own budget lines).
  • The Total column becomes tappable and takes over "show me everything" — today's whole-season list doesn't go away, it just moves to the one figure built to mean the whole season.
  • An item split across several months still appears under each of those months, same as today.

One rule, applied consistently instead of two behaviours pretending to be one — a category figure and an item figure are both just aggregates, so they open the same way. Nothing about how budget items are stored needs to move. If this reads right, it's a small build.
