Four push-backs, before the drawings

1 · "The Families window" is the wrong noun. What the ruling wants is one engine behind one window.

  • What a season already has. The season's own Email window, opened from Registrations, already offers everything Q12b listed: the whole season, a division, a team, a status, the families you ticked, one family . "Message registrants" is already gone. So the audiences are not the missing part.
  • What it lacks is the engine. It sends one email per child, one at a time, with no limit and no lock , and reports a send nobody got in green . The Families engine already sends one email per family, in batches, with a double-press guard, a daily limit and an honest "Reached 8 of 10".
  • Three honest options:
    • A · The Families window learns the season audiences and opens from the season. The window's "Who" list grows season rows beside "Every family" and "Some rep teams". Cost: a convenor sees club-wide choices they can't use, unless the window hides them per door, which is option C by another name.
    • B · The season keeps its own window and sends through the Families engine. Cheapest to build. Cost: two windows that look different for one club. A registrar who is also granted Families meets both, with two "Who" designs, two foot sentences and two result lines. The ruling says one composer, and this keeps two.
    • C · One window, two doors (recommended). The Families window's face (statement, Who, subject, message, See the email, Last sent, the foot sentence) is the only composer. The door it opens from decides its "Who" list: a season's door lists only that season's audiences; the Families door keeps its own list. Cost: the window takes its audience list from the door, one change, and the season's old window and page are deleted rather than kept beside it.
  • What each costs the people who use it. A registrar keeps every door they use today (row ⋯, the bulk bar, Tools) and gets the better engine. A convenor keeps Email families on the rail. 9d-2's schedule notices don't use the window at all: they are automatic, so they call the same engine directly, like Publish teams does.

Ask 1. Drawn as C on "The window" tab.

2 · A season's door must not need Families. Families is a grant, even for an admin.

  • What the code says. No role gets Families by default, the club admin included (owner decision, 2026-08-17, pinned by a test) . Today a season is emailed by the owner, admin, league admin and registrar, on every season status.
  • So "the composer is the Families window" must mean the window, not the permission. Recommended: the season's door is gated by the season's email gate (the runners plus the registrar, finished seasons included, per 9a), and shows only that season's families. No Families book, no other season, no "Every family".
  • An admin with no Families grant keeps exactly today's season email (every door, every status) on the better engine. They never see Families on the rail, as today.
  • A registrar who was granted Families sees both doors, each scoped as it is: from the season, that season; from Families, the club. Same window, same words.
  • The Families window's season choice reaches only open and playing seasons . The season door must reach every status: Registration closed (between sign-up and the first game, when convenors email most), Completed and Archived (9a). Draft Test 2027, in Registration closed with 101 families, is unreachable from Families today.

Ask 2. The full table is on the "Statuses, who, families" tab.

3 · One send, two places to read it. The season's history is a view of the club's record, not a second log.

  • The ruling: the season keeps its own sent history, because the registrar has no Families access. Ruled, kept.
  • The risk: two logs that disagree. Recommended: a season send is one record, the same kind Families › Emails already keeps. The season's Email families page lists that season's records, read through the season's gate. Families › Emails lists every record, the season's included, for the people with Families (ask 7).
  • The old history doesn't fit the record . It never stored which division or team, mixed three reasons into "skipped", counted children and kept no words. Converting it would invent fields. Recommended: show the old rows read-only under "Earlier sends", saying what they are, and convert nothing (ask 3). The dev fixture has no old rows; whether production has any is checked at build.
  • Automatic notices: recommended that a notice that went to many families at once (Publish teams today, schedule notices in 9d-2) shows as a row tagged Automatic, so a convenor can see why families heard something and why the 3 didn't move. One-family status emails (approved, waitlisted) stay off it (ask 4).

4 · Two allowances a convenor will confuse. Show only the one that applies, where it applies.

  • The ruling: each season has its own 3 typed sends a day; club-wide sends keep the club's 3; automatic notices never count. Today the count is club-wide and ignores the season, and the double-press guard is club-wide too .
  • Recommended reading. The season's door shows the season's allowance and never mentions the club's. The Families door shows the club's, unless "A house league season" is picked, when it shows that season's (ask 6: the allowance follows the audience, not the door). A season send never touches the club's 3. One quiet line in the window's foot says what is left (ask 8); the refusal names the time the next can go.
  • A convenor running two live seasons has 3 for each. The page title already names the season, so "2 of 3 left today" never needs to say which.
  • Does a one-family email count? Recommended no. The owner already ruled it for the club on 2026-10-22: a message to one household never counts toward the 3, and has its own limit of 3 a day to the same family. The season should match, so the same press means the same thing from either door (ask 5).
