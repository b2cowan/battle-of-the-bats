Close a place

When a park or a diamond is closed, the club says so once. Its site carries a strip at the top of every club page, the house league games there are postponed and their families emailed, the rep game there is marked for its coach, and Reopen takes the strip down. This walk closes Lions Park on Kestrel Bay, follows it to families and the coach, then reopens it.

Four walks, 34 steps, in order. Written Nov 26, 2026. Everything runs on Kestrel Bay on this computer. The emails go only to made-up addresses that can never receive mail.

Before you start

Make the day first, between 6:00 a.m. and 5:30 p.m. Ask me, or run this yourself. It also clears any earlier closure, so it is how you start the walk again:

node --env-file=.env.local scripts/seed-on-sale-pictures-extras.mjs --closure-walk

  • It adds Lions Park (Diamond 1 and Diamond 2) to the club’s Venue library, and puts these there later today: two house league games on its diamonds (U11 Lynx v Foxes on Diamond 1, U15 Tides v Ravens on Diamond 2), a third (U11 Mariners v Ospreys) whose place was typed by hand as “Lions Park — Diamond 2”, the U13 Ospreys v Marlow Heights rep game on Diamond 1, and one Fall Classic tournament game.
  • The games start about four to five and a half hours after the day is made. Walk within about three hours, before the first one starts.
