What Is This Against?

The budget is written line by line. Spending is only recorded by category — so the two can never quite meet. One question, asked at the moment money is recorded, closes the gap and fixes a number that is wrong today.

1

The budget says category and item. Spending says category.

"In the create expense/payable, it looks like we can only select a category but not an item — don't we need the item to align with the budget?"

Correct — and the gap is one level below the form

Building a budget line asks for a category and an item: Facilities / Dome rental. Recording the money that pays for it asks only for a category — as free text, chosen from the whole organisation's list rather than from the team's own plan. The report downstream then matches spending to budget by category name.

Category totals come out right. Everything finer than a category is either blank — or, in one place, wrong.

2

Expand a budget line and you see the whole category's money

Confirmed defect — every line in a category is shown the same spending

Facilities holds two lines. A single $340 dome invoice is paid in November. The line rows themselves are honest — they show a dash, because no per-line actual exists. But open a line's months and the actuals appear, in green, with a favourable variance.

Both lines show the same $340. Field rental has had no spending at all.

① The same $340 dome payment, reported a second time against a field-rental month six months later. With three lines in a category it is reported three times.

Why it happens, and why it's fixed here

A line's monthly actuals are computed from the category's spending and then drawn under each line in turn. There is no per-line figure to use, because no expense has ever recorded which line it was against.

It only shows up when a category holds more than one line — which is what a real budget looks like. Same root cause as the question in §1, so it is fixed in the same work rather than patched over.

3

One field, on both forms: what is this against?

The team's own budget lines — not the organisation's item list

Pointing at the line beats pointing at the item. Two lines can legitimately share a category and an item — "Tournament entry", twice, for two different tournaments — and the pair can't tell them apart. The line can. It is also what the coach means: this is against that row of my budget.

5

The cases that decide whether this is safe

CaseAnswer
Records made before this ships Left exactly as they are and still matched by category name. Nothing is guessed retroactively — inferring which line a past expense belonged to would be confident and wrong, which is worse than a dash.
A season of mixed records Linked and unlinked rows both roll up into the same category totals, so no figure ever disagrees with itself during the changeover. That is what makes shipping without rewriting history safe.
A budget line is deleted The spending survives untouched and falls back to its category. Deleting a plan row must never delete a record of money.
No budget plan at all The field is the category picker it is today. Nothing gets worse for a team that hasn't planned.
The spreadsheet importer Unchanged in v1 — its template has a category column, so imported rows land at category level exactly as now. A "budget line" column is a clean follow-up, not a reason to hold this.

Two questions for sign-off

1. Link to the budget line, as drawn — rather than to a category + item pair, which can't separate two lines that share one?

2. The coach demo (Riverdale Ridge) has both a budget plan and seeded spending. Should its Budget vs. Actual show real per-line actuals once this exists? It is the screen a prospect is most likely to open, and it will keep showing dashes unless someone decides otherwise.
