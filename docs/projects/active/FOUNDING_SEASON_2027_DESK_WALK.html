Before you start — three commands

The desk reads real data, so the dev database needs the four account states worth looking at. The summer ask only renders inside the card window, which is env-overridable precisely so it can be walked nine months early. And the dev Stripe sandbox has no configured prices at all, so the billing flows run through the mock. 

node scripts/seed-founding-season-fixture.mjs

Three things this walk deliberately does not prove, and why.

1. Card-on-file recorded at the webhook. It needs a real Stripe event, and dev's sandbox has no configured prices — every checkout falls to the mock, which never calls Stripe and so never fires a webhook. The desk's card column is proven against a seeded card instead; the webhook arms themselves are covered by code review. The first real proof is a card saved on production.

2. The October 2027 charge. Nothing here can advance a year. The no-early-charge design is proven by eleven unit tests that fail if any path could bill sooner.

3. The "no card yet" email audience. It was not built — see Part I.

4. Withdrawing a choice. Cancelling a 2028 choice happens in Stripe's own portal, which dev cannot reach. The review found that this used to suspend the whole account; the fix is covered by tests and by F6 below, which checks the state it leaves behind.

A

The desk exists, and it holds both kinds of account

Sign in as the platform admin. The nav's Billing & Product group has a new Founding Season entry.

A1

The nav entry is where a billing question would look for it

Open /platform-admin. Find Founding Season in the sidebar.

It sits in Billing & Product, above Approval Queue — not in Growth. Expect: clicking it opens the desk.

It is read on the day money is decided, not on the day someone is sold to.

A2

Five accounts, both kinds, one list

Read the table.

Expect exactly these five rows, sorted by name: Dev Standalone Team (Coaches Portal), Dev Test Org, Dev Tournament+ Org, Test Minor Softball, UAT Plus Org (all Organization). One list — not a section per kind.

A comped Coaches Portal is already an organization record underneath, which is why one query answers for both.

A3

Each row answers who, what and how to reach them

Read Dev Standalone Team's row across.

Expect: kind Coaches Portal, plan Premium Coaches Portal, free through Sep 30, 2027, card —, choice —, a "Used it for" reading in players (not tournaments), and coach@dev.local as the billing contact.

A coach's usage is a roster; an organization's is events. Same column, different question.

A4

The account name is the link — the row is not

Click the account name on any row. Then click a blank part of another row.

Expect: the name opens that org's platform-admin page on its billing tab. Clicking elsewhere on a row does nothing.

B

The four figures, and the one amber chip

B1

The figures agree with the rows below them

Read the four tiles, then count the rows.

Expect: 5 founding accounts (4 orgs · 1 portal), 4 with no card, 5 with no 2028 choice, and "Free season ends September 30, 2027" with a days-left figure beside it.

B2

Amber means somebody still has to do something

Look at which tiles are amber.

Expect: "No card yet" and "No 2028 choice yet" are amber; "Founding accounts" is lime; "Free season ends" is plain. Nothing else on the page is coloured.

B3

The Legacy chip — a row our own backfill never reached

Find Dev Tournament+ Org's "Free through" cell. Hover the amber chip.

Expect: it reads Dec 31, 2026 with an amber Legacy chip, and the tooltip says the backfill has not reached this row and the account is still recognised as founding. Every other row reads Sep 30, 2027 with no chip.

Migration 279 moved every free-season record to the new date, but it is data-only — no automated check can prove it ran. This desk is the only place a missed row would ever be visible.

B4

A card reads as a card, not as a tick

Read Dev Tournament+ Org's "Card on file" cell.

Expect: Visa ••4242 in lime with Jun 3, 2027 beneath it. Every other row shows a quiet em dash.

Support needs to tell one card from another on the phone; "yes" cannot do that.

B5

"Owner last seen" is named for the fact we actually hold

Read the column heading and its values.

Expect: the heading says Owner last seen — not "last activity". Values read as a relative age ("3d ago") when recent, an absolute date when older, and Never for an owner who has never signed in.

The product keeps no activity metric. Calling it one would be a claim we cannot support.

C

The two questions the October turn-off runs on

C1

"No card yet" gives the chase list

Click No card yet.

Expect: the button fills solid, the count line reads 4 of 5 accounts, and Dev Tournament+ Org disappears — it is the only one with a card. A Clear filters link appears.

C2

The footnote says what the filtered list is for

With the filter on, read the line under the table.

Expect: it says these are the same accounts the "no card yet" reminder would reach, minus anyone opted out.

C3

Kind and search narrow it further

Clear filters. Set the kind dropdown to Coaches Portals. Then clear, and type uat into the search box.

Expect: Coaches Portals leaves Dev Standalone Team alone; the search leaves UAT Plus Org alone. Search matches the name, the slug and the billing contact.

C4

The export is what you were looking at

Turn on No card yet, then Export → Excel. Open the file.

Expect: 4 rows, not 5 — the export honours the filter. Columns match the screen in the same order, plus the slug, and "Free through" carries (legacy) where the chip appears.

C5

The desk cannot act, on purpose

Look for any control that changes an account: a plan dropdown, a cancel button, a "send reminder" action.

Expect: there are none. The only controls are search, two filters, a kind dropdown, Export, and the account links.

Converting and cancelling already live in Bulk Operations and on each org's page. A read-only desk cannot convert the wrong account by accident.

D

The phone shape — hand-measured, because no gate measures it

No platform-admin screen is in the automated layout sweep, so this part is the only thing standing between the desk and a phone defect. Use the browser's device toolbar.

D1

At 390px the list becomes cards

Set the viewport to 390 × 844.

Expect: the column heading row is gone; each account is a bordered card with its name as the title and every other value labelled beside it. The page does not scroll sideways.

D2

At 361px nothing is clipped

Set the viewport to 361px wide — the narrowest band the product supports.

Expect: still no sideways page scroll; a long billing address wraps inside its card rather than pushing it wide; the filter controls stack to full width.

D3

At 768px the columns come back and scroll inside their own frame

Set the viewport to 768px. Swipe the table sideways.

Expect: the heading row is back and the table scrolls inside its bordered frame — the page itself still does not move sideways. Every filter control and every account link is comfortably tappable.

D4

The two known shared-chrome misses

At 390px, look at the Export control and the sidebar's Sign out.

Expect them to be slightly short of the touch floor — they are shared console furniture used on ~34 screens, not this desk's own. Flag only if either is hard to hit, not merely small.

Fixing them changes every admin export surface at once and does not belong in a billing change. Recorded rather than smuggled in.

E

An organization's summer ask

Sign out. Sign in as uat-owner@uat-test-org.local and open /uat-plus-org/admin/org/billing.

E1

The banner names the date, not the ask

Read the lime Founding Season banner.

Expect: "Your Founding Season ends September 30, 2027." and copy that says to choose a plan for your 2028 season below, that nothing is charged before October 1, 2027, and that you can change your mind until then.

E2

Annual leads; monthly is one tap away

Read the "Choose your 2028 plan" card.

Expect: two options as real radio buttons — Tournament Plus · annual $390/year selected by default with "Save $78 — 2 months free", and Tournament Plus · monthly $39/month. Selecting monthly moves the highlight and changes the button's label.

A seasonal buyer charged monthly from October has nothing to use until spring.

E3

The promise is printed beside the button

Read the line to the left of the choose button.

Expect: "First charge October 1, 2027 · nothing before then".

That date is not marketing copy — it is the single value the checkout is built on, printed where a customer can hold us to it.

E4

The card-save door survives as the quiet second option

Read the footnote under the chooser.

Expect: "Prefer to decide later?" with Save a card without choosing as an underlined link — not a button competing with the choice.

E5

What happens if they do nothing is on the page, not only in an email

Read the "If you choose nothing" card below.

Expect: the organization moves to the free Tournament plan on October 1, 2027, and its tournaments and records stay.

E6

The page never asks for the same thing twice

Scroll to the "Billing" block further down.

Expect: it states the card position ("No payment method on file yet. Nothing is charged before October 1, 2027.") with an Add payment method button — it does not repeat the plan ask the chooser above already makes.

F

Making the choice, and reading it back

Billing is mocked, so choosing writes the choice directly instead of opening Stripe. That is the only path dev has.

F1

Choosing confirms the promise, not just the plan

Leave annual selected and click Choose Tournament Plus · annual.

Expect: a success dialog titled "Your next season is set", saying nothing is charged before October 1, 2027, that the Founding Season stays free through September 30, 2027, and that the choice can be changed until then.

F2

The confirmed state names the amount, the card and the date

Close the dialog and read the green card that replaced the chooser.

Expect: "Your 2028 season is set", "Tournament Plus, billed annually from October 1, 2027", and a sentence saying $390 will be charged on October 1, 2027 — "your first charge, and nothing before it". The chooser and the "If you choose nothing" card are both gone.

F3

⚠ The plan has NOT changed

Look at the plan card at the top of the page.

Expect: it still reads Tournament Plus · free through September 30, 2027 · no credit card. Choosing did not start anything, did not change the plan, and did not end the free season.

Your ruling: the plan moves at the first charge, not when the choice is made — so the free season is worth the same to every account.

F4

The desk reads the choice back

Sign back in as the platform admin and reopen the desk.

Expect: UAT Plus Org's "2028 choice" now reads Tournament Plus with annual · today's date beneath it, "No 2028 choice yet" has dropped to 4, and the No 2028 choice filter no longer returns that row.

F6

Choosing again replaces the choice — it does not add a second one

On the confirmed card, use Change plan or card (or re-run the choice from the desk fixture) and pick the other cycle.

Expect: the confirmed card now shows the new cycle and price, and there is still exactly one choice on the desk row — not two.

The review found that a second choice used to create a second live subscription with the same first-charge date, and both would have charged on October 1. The old one is now cancelled before the new one is made.

F7

The success message never appears beside the chooser

Immediately after choosing, look at the page behind the dialog. Then reload it once.

Expect: no "Choose your 2028 plan" card is visible at the same time as the success message. Flag it if you can see both — that is an invitation to choose twice.

F5

An account with nothing to renew is not asked to choose

On the desk, note Test Minor Softball is on the free Tournament plan.

Expect: it appears in the list as a founding account, but it is a free plan — there is nothing for it to renew, and the billing page offers it no chooser. Flag if the desk implies otherwise.

G

A coach's summer ask — the correction

This is the part that changed most. Sign in as coach@dev.local and open /dev-standalone-team/admin/org/billing (from the portal: sidebar → Admin → Billing).

What this looked like before. A comped Coaches Portal saw no Founding Season message at all — the whole block was suppressed because its wording only ever spoke about Tournament Plus. What it did see was a generic "Billing tools" card with a button called Payment method & invoices — which quietly opened a card-save session, in January, with nothing on the page explaining why anyone would use it.

G1

The page now speaks for the coach's product

Read the banner.

Expect: the same Founding Season banner an organization gets, with the same dates. Nothing on this page says "Tournament Plus".

G2

The coach's chooser is their own product at their own prices

Read the "Choose your 2028 plan" card.

Expect: Premium Coaches Portal · annual $290/year (with "Save $58 — 2 months free") and · monthly $29/month. Same shape as the organization's, different product and price.

G3

The unexplained year-round card door is gone

Look for a card called Billing tools with a "Payment method & invoices" button.

Expect: it is not there. The only card ask is the Founding Season "Billing" block, gated to the summer window, saying what it is for.

G4

"If you choose nothing" promises only what the product does today

Read the "If you choose nothing" card.

Expect: the Premium portal ends on September 30, 2027, the team's Basic tournament records stay available, and the season's work is kept. It must not promise a read-only season.

Your D1 ruling: the read-only window is built in the spring with Phase 3. Until it exists this copy may not describe it.

G5

⚠ "Cancel Premium access" is also gone — deliberately

Look for a cancellation card.

Expect: it is not shown during the free season. This now matches how a comped organization has always behaved, and it agrees with the offer's own words: there is nothing to cancel.

Called out because it is a real behaviour change riding along with the fix. Say so if you disagree with it.

G6

A coach can choose, and the desk sees it

Choose Premium Coaches Portal · monthly. Then sign back in as the platform admin and reopen the desk.

Expect: the coach's confirmed card says $29 will be charged on October 1, 2027, and Dev Standalone Team's desk row reads Premium Coaches Portal · monthly.

Nobody is asked for anything yet

Open the billing page as the founding org owner.

Expect: the banner says the plan is free through September 30, 2027 and that a plan for 2028 is chosen in September 2027. No chooser, no card button, no "if you choose nothing" card. The Billing block says no action is needed.

H2

The desk says the ask has not opened

Open the desk as the platform admin.

Expect: a note at the top saying the summer ask has not opened yet and that the two columns fill in over the summer. The list, the figures and the export all still work.

I

What is not built, and what to do about it

I1

The "no card yet" email audience was stopped, not forced

Open /platform-admin/email and look for a card-reminder campaign.

Expect: there is none. The Phase 1 chat moved the email-audience registry into a file this phase is forbidden to touch and has uncommitted edits in both email screens, so wiring the audience meant editing their work mid-flight.

The operator can still get the exact list — that is the desk's "No card yet" filter and its export, the same rule. What is missing is only the batch-email wiring, and nothing was going to send this autumn.
