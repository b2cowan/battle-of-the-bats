Owner QA walk · Fee invoice alerts · Part 4, the overdue banner

Once an invoice is overdue, every club admin page opens with one red line and a Pay button, and it ends when the invoice is paid

Written 2026-11-05 for Part 4. Dev only. Small database changes make the invoice overdue, paid and the club cancelled; every one is put back by a step that follows it, and Part F reads everything back.

What this walk is for

Parts 2 and 3 told the owner they owe FieldLogicHQ: a count on the Plan & billing door and one line at the top of that page. An owner who never opens that page still never saw an overdue invoice. Part 4 adds the banner: once an invoice is overdue, every page of the club’s admin opens with one line with a red edge on its left, the words and a Pay invoice button. It has no close button, it scrolls away with the page, and it goes when the invoice is paid. On Plan & billing the banner stands in for the red line, so you never see two. A cancelled club, which can open only Plan & billing, gets the same red line at the top of that page.

It passes when

  • With the invoice overdue, the owner sees the red banner at the top of the content area (beside the left rail, above the page title bar) on Plan & billing, the hub and a third page, with the invoice’s number, amount and due day, and a lime Pay invoice button that opens Stripe in a new tab.
  • On Plan & billing there is one red line (the banner), not a banner and a second line under it.
  • The banner has no close button and scrolls away with the page. On a phone it is one row with a button at least 44 px tall.
  • An invoice that is open but not yet due shows no banner anywhere (only the amber line on Plan & billing from Part 3).
  • Paid in another tab: the banner clears when you come back to the tab, with no reload.
  • The club’s admin and treasurer never see the banner.
  • A cancelled club’s owner sees the same red line at the top of the one page they can open, and no banner above it.

Not in this walk

  • Two or more overdue invoices at once (“2 invoices for Stripe fees are overdue. The earliest was due …” with a See invoices button). The dev club has one invoice and a second cannot be made by hand without inventing fees. The words, the button and the data are pinned by tests.
  • The banner, the red failed-payment line and an amber line all at once. That needs a second open invoice. With one overdue invoice and a failed plan payment you would see two red items (the banner and the failed-payment line) and no amber one.
  • A real payment. Paying is simulated in the database. Opening Stripe’s invoice page is allowed; do not pay it. Help articles are Part 5.
