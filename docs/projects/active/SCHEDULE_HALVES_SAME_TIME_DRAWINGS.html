A field in two sides, and teams at the same time

Round 1 drawings for review. Nothing is built. Each screen is drawn at its true width from the styles that ship today. The example is the winter dome from Stage 1: one permit for Fields 1–6, Sundays 9:00 a.m.–1:30 p.m., Jan 3 – Mar 7, 2027 (10 Sundays, three 90-minute starts), $18,000.00, so a whole field is $80.00 an hour and a 90-minute slot on it $120.00. Fields 5 and 6 are at "Half cost", as in the demo. The permit, the dome and the teams are made up for the drawing. The words "side", "side A" and "side B" are working words for the marketing pass. These are desk screens, so no phone is drawn.

A field can be split into two sides, A and B. You say so once, in the venue list. After that, a schedule's row can use the whole field, both sides (one team on each), or one side while the other side goes elsewhere. Everything outside the club's schedules still sees one Field 1: permits, house league, a coach's own event, tryout days and tournaments.

Why the same-time rule is drawn with this. The rule looks for one start where every team in the group is free, with enough free places in one building. A field in two sides offers two places at that start. So the rule has to know about sides before it is built, and one question follows: may a group land on the two sides of one field? That is Ask 7, because ruling R3 was made before sides existed.

What stays as it is

  • Permits. A permit holds whole fields. Its time, price rows, "Half cost" and Held hours do not change.
  • Every other picker. House league, a coach's own event, tryout days and tournaments still offer Field 1 as one facility (Ask 5).
  • The venue table. The Venue library page still lists "Field 1 · Field 2 · …".
  • A side is never shared, as a "Half cost" field is never shared today.

What a missed reader does

With the recommended storage (Ask 1), anything in the product not yet taught about sides reads a practice on side A as a practice on the whole of Field 1. It may call a clash that isn't one. It never misses one. The other storage turns each side into its own facility, and anything not taught then double-books in silence.

How the ticks read

  • Tick Field 1 (whole): the whole field, one team at a time (as today).
  • Tick side A and side B: Field 1 in two sides, a team on each.
  • Tick side A only: one side here; side B is left for something else.
