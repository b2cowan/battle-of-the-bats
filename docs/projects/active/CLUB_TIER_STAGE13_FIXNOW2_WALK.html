The club’s promise, the way back, setup step 4 and the two programs

Written 2026-11-07, after the second fix-now build (built, simplified and reviewed). One purpose: four things the external review found are now true on screen. No migration, no new screen.

What this walk is for

(1) A rep coach is told the truth about what the club sees of their money: each family’s dues, never their other spending or the rest of their books. (2) A treasurer, registrar or club admin who also coaches can get back to the club’s admin from the Coaches Portal, and lands on their own page, not the owner’s. (3) Setup step 4 (“Put your club online”) ticks only when both the tagline and the contact email are saved, and says which is missing. (4) Someone who coaches a house league team and a rep team is told, once and quietly, that the rep team is in the Coaches Portal.

It passes when

  • 1 The coach’s Money › Club screen ends with the new sentence naming Families and “other spending”, and the club’s rep team page has a third money row, “What each family owes and has paid (on Families)”.
  • 2 A treasurer who coaches finds Admin at the foot of the Coaches Portal sidebar and, on a phone, under More › You, and it opens Accounting. The registrar lands on House league. The club admin lands on the Overview.
  • 3 A member whose only club role is coach sees no Admin door anywhere.
  • 4 If the owner has switched a person’s own Accounting access off, their Admin door opens the Overview, not a “not yours” screen.
  • 5 Setup step 4 stays unticked with only a tagline and says “Still needed: a contact email”; it ticks, with “Your tagline and contact email are saved”, once both are saved; “Setup: n of 5” follows.
  • 6 A house league coach who also has a rep team sees “Your rep team is in the Coaches Portal. Open the Coaches Portal” on their team page and the link works; a house league coach with no rep team sees nothing.

Not in this walk

  • A league admin who also coaches. The fixture has no league admin who coaches; the registrar takes the same House league landing (same rule), and a unit test pins the league admin. Not walked in a browser.
  • The owner’s own door. The owner (Morgan Ellis) does not coach in this fixture; the club admin takes the same Overview landing.
  • A cancelled club’s rep line. A club whose billing is suspended shows no line; there is no safe way to cancel the copy, so it is covered by a unit test only.
  • The Families page itself. Unchanged: the walk does not re-test it.
