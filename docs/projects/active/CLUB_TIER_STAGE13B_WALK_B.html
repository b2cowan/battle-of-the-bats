The arrival: invite each role, read the email, land on the role’s home

Written 2026-11-07, after Session B was built, simplified, reviewed (high), word-checked and legal-checked. One purpose: a person the club invites is never given a role by accident, is told in the email what the role opens and what FieldLogicHQ is, and lands where their work starts, by either door. Help then opens on their own first steps. This walk sends real emails, to plus-addresses of the company inbox.

What this walk is for

(1) No default role: the invite’s Role field starts empty, Send stays off until a role is chosen, and the role’s sentence shows once one is. The setup checklist’s first step opens Members with the invite already open. (2) The emails: the new-account invitation and the “You’ve been added” email both say what the role opens and one line on what FieldLogicHQ is, with the role’s screen name. (3) Both arrival paths end on the role’s home: accepting an invitation, and the “added” email’s Sign in button. (4) Help by role: “New to FieldLogicHQ?” opens on the reader’s own first steps.

It passes when

  • 1 The Role field starts on “Choose a role”, Send invite is off until a role is picked, and each role shows its own one-line sentence; the Staff kit’s door still opens on Volunteer.
  • 2 On a club with no board yet, setup step 1 opens Members with the invite open and no role chosen; once the step is done, its button just opens Members.
  • 3–4 Each of the six invitations arrives naming the club, Morgan Ellis, the role by its screen name, what the role opens and what FieldLogicHQ is; the volunteer’s carries its job instead of a second sentence.
  • 5 Accepting lands each person on their role’s home: admin and staff on the Overview, the treasurer in Accounting, the league admin and the registrar in House league, the volunteer on Check-in.
  • 6 Adding someone who already has an account sends “You’ve been added” (with “You’ll …”), and its Sign in button lands a treasurer in Accounting.
  • 7 A treasurer and a registrar each see only their own first steps in Help, open, ending with “What your role can open”; the owner sees theirs plus the others folded.

Not in this walk

  • A coach re-opening an old invitation (now lands in the Coaches Portal). The copy has no such invitation; a unit test pins it.
  • The resend builds from the same email as the first invitation; a unit test pins that it carries the same sentence.
  • The invite on a phone. The dialog is unchanged in shape; only its starting value changed.
  • Session A’s homes (the club band, “Still to do”, the league mornings) were walked in §485.
