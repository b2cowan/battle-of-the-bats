The Money tab says which sales need a look at their tax, and staff can mark one as looked at

Written 2026-11-07 for the Stage 3 closeout. Walk B of three: run it after Walk A, on Walk A’s Tax Record Team.

What this walk is for

Platform admin reads each sale’s tax from our own record, and checks it against what was kept with the sale. A sale whose answer is missing or contradicts the address is listed under Needs a look. Today counts those sales, the club’s Money tab carries a dot before you open it, and staff with refund rights can mark a sale as looked at (with a why) or put it back. A mark never changes the record or the tax return. Support can read the list but cannot mark.

It passes when

  • The Walk A refund with no credit note is flagged, and the flag shows before you open the tab (Today’s row, the Clubs filter, the dot, the strip).
  • A sale Stripe taxed for a province other than the one kept with the sale is flagged with both rates named.
  • Mark as looked at needs a why, takes the sale off the list and off Today’s count, and Put it back returns it.
  • Support sees the same list but not the mark door.

Not in this walk

  • “Show older sales”, a year with no sales, an older page that half fails to read. The dev database has no sales older than twelve months (the tax records begin 2026-05-01). The unit tests cover the paging, the gap year and the half-failed read.
  • The eighth check (Stripe zero-rated a sale to an address in Canada, ruled 2026-11-07). Stripe test mode will not zero-rate a Canadian line on request. Unit tests cover it.
  • A refund covered by a smaller credit note (check 7 tightened 2026-11-07): Walk A’s sale is that case. Its $11.30 note covers the $11.30 refund, so only the $5.00 is listed.
  • A customer shared by two portals. Ruled 2026-11-07: recorded, not built. Its sales are on no club’s list; Stage 4 lists them on the return.
