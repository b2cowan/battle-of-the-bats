Six rulings this round needs

1. The Teams health row becomes a header button that opens a window.Recommended: yes. It is the move you made for Schedule health on 10-19, and a page-level button says "every division" better than a row under the 15U picker does.
2. The button sits first in the header, before Export.Recommended: yes. That is where Schedule's sits, so the two screens teach one habit.
3. The dashboard gets one health row at the top of the Registration card.Recommended: yes, weeks-before board only. It opens the same window. It is a row, not a sixth card, because the card already lists the follow-ups. The score and the empty-spots gap are what the dashboard does not show today.
4. The page stops naming the problems in words.Recommended: accept. Today's row says "4 missing an email · 1 division short of teams". The button shows a score and a count of 3, as Schedule's does. The words move into the window, and the dashboard's follow-up rows still name them.
5. The At a glance card is removed. Registration open or closed becomes a chip beside the picker.Recommended: yes. The chip says the state and the spots and opens a small menu with the one-sentence consequence and the Close or Reopen button. This row holds the only switch for a division's registration, so the switch has to move, not disappear.
6. Payments stays, as one slim line above the team list.Recommended: yes. Health counts the teams that owe. This line has the dollars, deposits and due dates for the division. It opens to the same detail it has today and appears only when fees are tracked. The alternative is the one-line total with no detail.
