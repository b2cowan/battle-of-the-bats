House league’s time: the piece’s level, the schedule page, and the league admin in the club’s schedule

Drawn for the Part 3 build. Parts 1 and 2 built the club giving house league time. Part 3 is where a league admin divides it. The list (k2) was drawn last round; this round draws what its rows open into, what changes on the house-league Schedule page to reach it, the empty states, and the league admin’s own view of the club’s schedule (built second). Words marked are not settled; words without the tag are settled. Click any tag to read what it means.

Whole desk screens are scaled to fit; use the button for true size (1440 wide). Windows and phones are always true size.

What you are asked to rule on

Why these were not in the earlier drawings

  • The last round (k2) drew the list: sections per stretch, a row per division, and Give it to on a Not given yet row. It gave the whole row. It did not draw what the › on a division’s row opens, so giving or taking part of a row had no screen.
  • The house-league Schedule page was drawn only as it would look once everything was built (9d-2). The one step that makes the list reachable, the time line and the Tools menu on today’s page, had no before-and-after, and Export’s place was never settled.
  • k4’s empty states were drawn for Generate games. House league’s time has its own, and they differ for a league admin and an owner.
  • The league admin’s view of the club’s schedule was ruled (a league admin divides house league’s time, never gives or takes it) but only the rep slot read (h3) was drawn.

1The piece’s window saves with one Save

Recommended: ticks stage; one Save at the foot applies them all at once. The foot says “Nothing is saved yet.” until a change, then “3 changes”. Alternative: every tick saves on the spot.

Why: one request, one practices question, and no half-state if something fails. Giving time and taking it back in the same visit stays one decision.

2One editor, the piece’s window, plus “Choose parts…”

Recommended: the piece’s window is the only place parts are ticked. It opens as a level inside House league’s time, with a named Back. “Give it to” on a Not given yet row still gives the whole row in one pick, and gains a last item, “Choose parts…”, that opens the piece with nothing new ticked. Alternative (Option B): Give it to first opens a parts checklist.

Why: Option B still needs the piece’s window to shrink, move or give back, so it means two checklists to build and learn.

3Group ticks, per weekday and per stretch

Recommended: within a stretch, parts are grouped by weekday. A weekday line and the stretch heading each carry a tick that ticks or unticks everything under them, with a “some ticked” state in between.

Why: one stretch here holds sixteen parts; U12 gets Tuesdays and Thursdays in two ticks, not eight.

4A mixed part reads “8 of 12 dates”

Recommended: when the club extends a schedule, the new dates arrive as Not given yet, so a part can be partly one division’s. It shows a half tick and “8 of 12 dates”. Ticking takes the remaining Not given yet dates; unticking gives back this division’s.

5A part another division holds: no box, a link to its piece

Recommended: the right column names the holder as a link (“U12 practice time ›”) that opens that piece; Back returns. To take the part, untick it there first. Alternative: a tick on a held part moves it with a question.

Why: one gesture never changes two divisions at once.

6Giving back time with practices on it asks first, in red

Recommended: when Save would give back time that has practices still to come, a question names the teams and the button says what it does (“Save and remove 6 practices”). Past practices stay. Telling coaches and families comes with Part 5; this window never promises a notice. When no practices are on the time, Save just saves.

7Export inside Tools — already ruled (9d-2), and the toolbar waits for 9d-2a

Ruled, not asked: the 9d-2 drawings put Export (with Show and Generate games) inside Tools. Part 3 adds Tools holding “House league’s time”, a separator, then an “Export” heading over Excel, CSV and Calendar file (one level, no flyout). Owner, 2026-10-27: Part 3 adds only Tools and the time line; the views (List · Week · Month), the Show filter and the phone symbols come with 9d-2a’s toolbar rebuild, so nothing is built twice.

8The time line shows only when there is club time or the viewer builds Schedules

Recommended: it shows when the club has house-league time in a final schedule, or when the viewer is an owner or admin who builds Schedules (so a division with none yet has a way to start). Otherwise it is absent, so a club that hand-books never gets a permanent nag.

9Hours only, until a length exists

Recommended: “6 hours a week”. A count of games needs a game length (Generate games) and a count of practices needs a practice length (Practice time, Part 4). The count joins the row when its length exists.

10The empty states

Recommended: a league admin whose club gave nothing reads a note and Close, with no link. An owner or admin reads the same sentence plus a hint and a lime “Give house league time”. When everything is divided there is no empty state: every row shows, and only an owner or admin gets a quiet “Give house league more time” at the end.

11The league admin in the club’s schedule (built second)

Recommended: a page that reads the club’s schedule week and opens only from House league’s time. Rep team names never reach a league admin (“A rep team”). A house-league slot gets a one-date “Use” list that saves on the spot; rep and open slots read with no controls. No bill, asks, replies or send history; no nav rail entry.

A piece’s level: tick the parts a division has, then Save

New. A division’s row (›) in House league’s time opens a level inside the same window, not a second window, with a named Back. A part is the same weekday, start, end and facility across the weeks. Example: U12 games in Draft Test 2027. Every other part of the club’s time is shown too, so you see what U12 has, what is free, and who holds the rest.

The league admin in the club’s schedule

Built second, after the piece’s level. A league admin may open the club’s schedule to see where house league’s time sits and to change one date of a house-league slot. They cannot touch a rep team’s slot or open time. The page is reached from House league’s time, not from the nav rail. Rep team names never reach a league admin who cannot open Rep teams: the server strips the name, so the grid and the windows say “A rep team”.
