The tiles won’t add up, and that’s expected

Someone will subtract Spent from Collected and expect Cash on hand. It won’t match, because the report counts spending when it happens and cash counts it when money leaves the account.

Collected$8,979.09 less Spent− $5,993.00 plus the balance the season started with+ $500.00 What the subtraction suggests$3,486.09 Cash on hand$3,687.69

On the test team the gap is $201.60. The report already names three reasons a gap opens up:

A family paid a vendor directly. That counts as spent ($660.00 on this team), but the money never went through the team’s account.

Money came back, such as a refund. Or money went back to families. Each moves the account without being spending.

My recommendation is no note on the band. Its one note line is for warnings, and the Statement’s cash note (“In cash, this season spent …”) already walks the difference line by line.

Four calls for you

Each has my recommendation. Reply in the chat by number.

1

What is the first tile called?

Options: Collected, Revenue, Earned.

Collected. It is a verb like Spent, so the two read as a pair. “Earned” reads oddly for player dues. “Revenue” would also work, since it is the report’s own section name.

2

Does the export summary follow the screen?

The PDF and Excel summary currently print a Headroom row and a Spent off-plan row.

Yes. The export prints the same four figures with the same names, so a printed copy matches what the coach saw.

3

Should the Money Overview lose Headroom too?

The Overview also headlines Headroom, beside its spending bar, and has the same October problem.

Not in this change. The Overview card is a different design with a bar and its own links. Keep this change to one tab and look at the Overview separately.

4

What colour is Cash on hand?

The Overview prints this figure in green when it is positive. On this band, colour is kept for verdicts, and a balance is a total.

Plain ink, red below zero. The cost is that one figure has two colours on two screens. If that bothers you, the alternative is to drop the green on the Overview.
