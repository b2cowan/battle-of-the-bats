4

D3 — no "Add a goal →" shortcut on a ghost section RULED, AGREED

Nothing on this screen is stored — not the ticks, not the typed next step. A door that sends the coach away to write a goal silently discards whatever they had already chosen and typed, and they come back to a screen reset to its defaults. The one existing exit ("Save this next step as a review →") earns its place because it carries the words with it; a "go and add a goal" link carries nothing.

The empty-record case (iii) is the exception: there is nothing to lose there, and that door already exists.

6What /review changed after the build

Two defects, both in the new code, both fixed 2026-09-22. Recorded here because each is a trap the next nudge like this will walk into.

5

The invitation was gated on the wrong permission FIXED

Every ghost line ended "— add one and it appears here", which reads as an instruction. But reading a shelf and filling it are different grants: reading goals needs Internal notes alone, while adding a goal or an observation also needs the Development grant, and recording a result needs it too. An assistant with notes but no Development grant was being told to add a goal the server would refuse — the exact failure the ghost’s own permission gate was written to prevent, one level down.

The rule that came out of it: a "nothing here yet" line that NAMES AN ACTION is gated on the write grant, never on the one that let the coach see the shelf. Without it the line states the fact and stops.

6

Naming the help answer renamed the ? button FIXED

Landing the drawer on the handout’s own answer needs only the sub-topic id. Passing a label alongside it also renamed the masthead control to "Help: The development handout" — breaking this one page out of the portal’s "Help: <section>" convention, and making the rendered layout gate report the button’s long-accepted 34px geometry as two brand-new findings, because baseline entries are keyed by accessible name. The label is gone; the drawer still opens exactly where D4 asked.

7

⚠ The rendered layout gate is green over an empty fixture

The sweep’s fixture player is seeded with goals, observations and results — so none of the ghost branches ever render in it, and "✓ No new layout findings" proves nothing about this change. The case to open in a browser is the one this project started from: a player with test results and nothing else.

Not in scope

  • The paper never changes. No ghost sections, no "this could say more" on the printed side — the preview shows what prints, and that stays true.
  • Nothing becomes stored. The line is computed from the record each time the screen opens, like every other choice here.
  • No new "what's missing" logic. The screen already knows which sections it is hiding and why; the line reads the same answer.

PM brief — what else goes on a handout

Draft, 2026-09-22 · Coaches portal → a player → Skills & Goals → Preview development handout

The problem, in one sentence

The handout chooser only shows the sections a player already has something in — so a coach whose players have test results and nothing else believes a handout is a table of test results, and never learns that a goal or an observation would print on the same page.

What the coach sees differently

  • A section of the handout that this player has nothing in keeps its heading instead of disappearing. Under it sits one quiet line: "No goal this season — add one and it appears here."
  • So the chooser always shows the handout’s real shape — what we’re working on, a recent observation, selected test results — in the order they print, whether or not this player has reached them yet.
  • A section that is full looks exactly as it does today. A coach whose record is complete sees no change at all.
  • The ? at the top of the page now opens the handout’s own help answer — the five parts of the paper and what each one prints — instead of the general development guide.
  • Nothing else moves: no new buttons, no links out, and the printed preview beside the chooser is untouched.

Why it matters

The handout is the one thing in the development section that a family actually holds. A coach who prints a page of sprint times has a weaker conversation than one who prints the goal, the observation and the next step alongside them — and until now the product gave them no reason to think the second page existed. This is a discovery fix with a direct pull-through into the features underneath it: goals and observations are the parts of Skills & Goals that go unused.

Access differences

A coach without Internal notes cannot print goals or observations at all. For them those two headings are not drawn — the product never advertises something it will refuse to print. The boundary line at the foot of the chooser ("Not on the paper: …") is unchanged for everyone.

Tradeoffs taken

  • No shortcut to go and add a goal. The screen stores nothing, so any link out of it throws away the coach’s ticks and their typed next step. Showing the heading is safe; sending them away is not.
  • The long explanation stays in help, reached from the ? only. The screen says what is missing; help says what a handout holds. One description of the paper to maintain, not two that drift apart.
  • The printed preview is untouched. It shows what prints and nothing else.

Success criteria

  • A coach with results-only players can say, without opening help, that a handout also carries goals and observations — because the headings are on the screen.
  • No coach is shown a section they are not permitted to print.
  • The chooser is no taller for a coach whose record is complete, and a player with nothing at all still gets the empty state rather than a column of empty headings.

How to test it

  1. Open a player with test results and no goals → What we’re working on and A recent observation are drawn as headings with a quiet line under each.
  2. Add a goal to that player, reopen the handout → that heading fills with the goal, ticked, and only the observation heading is still a ghost.
  3. Open a player with everything on the record → no ghosts anywhere; the column is unchanged.
  4. Sign in as an assistant without Internal notes → results only, with no goal or observation heading drawn.
  5. Open a player with nothing on the record → the existing empty state, unchanged.
  6. Open the ? at the top of the page → it lands on The development handout, not the top of the development guide.
