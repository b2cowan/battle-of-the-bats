The owner is told they owe FieldLogicHQ an invoice: an amber count, red when it is overdue, gone once it is paid, and seen by the owner alone

Written 2026-11-05 for Part 2 (34227e6e4, and the More-pill ruling e1ba62c90). Dev only. Four small database changes make the invoice overdue, paid and late; each is put back by the step that follows it.

What this walk is for

Until now nothing outside Plan & billing told a club owner that FieldLogicHQ is waiting on an invoice. This walk checks the signal itself: a small count on the Plan & billing door in the club admin (and on More and the Plan & billing row inside the More sheet, on a phone). You make the dev club's one fee invoice overdue, then paid, then fail its plan payment, and watch the count change colour, number and disappear, on a desk and on a phone. Then you check the people who must not see it.

It passes when

  • With the invoice open and not yet due, the Plan & billing door wears an amber 1 on every admin page, on the desk rail (full and collapsed) and on the phone (More tab and sheet row).
  • Once the invoice is overdue the same count is red, in the same places.
  • Once it is paid the count is gone.
  • A failed plan payment alone is an amber 1; with an unpaid invoice as well it reads 2.
  • The club's admin and treasurer see no Plan & billing door and no count; a tournament-only owner with nothing owed sees no count and nothing broken.

Not in this walk

  • The words a screen reader speaks ("1 invoice to pay", "1 invoice overdue", "Plan payment didn’t go through"). The tests pin them; a screen reader is not part of this walk.
  • More with unread messages as well. The ruling (More’s pill shows only the invoice count while one is owed) is pinned by a test; the dev club has no unread messages to show it with.
  • A tournament-only owner who owes an invoice. No such invoice exists on dev; a probe confirmed their brief now loads and reports zero owed.
  • A cancelled club (Part 4), the line at the top of Plan & billing and the fix-your-card button (Part 3), the overdue banner (Part 4), help articles (Part 5). None of those exist yet, so their absence is correct.
  • A real Stripe payment. Paying here is simulated in the database. The real payment arriving from Stripe is not part of this part.
