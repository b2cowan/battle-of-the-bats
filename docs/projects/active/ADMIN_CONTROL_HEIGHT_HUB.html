Product-manager brief

One control height

What changes

On a computer, every button and every toolbar field in the club and tournament admin becomes 34 px tall, the height of a coaches portal button, instead of 38 px. Toolbars get a little more compact, and the club Ledger's Tools button stops sitting short between Export and Add entry. If you say yes to question 2, the portal's lime buttons also rise about 2 px so they match the white buttons beside them, and the whole product shares one button height.

Nothing moves on a phone, nothing is added or removed, and no workflow changes.

Why it matters

The 1 October decision ("go ahead with A") set out to give the admin the portal's button. It copied the portal's padding, type and corners, and set the height to 38 on the grounds that the portal's buttons are about 38. They are 33.4 (white) and 31.4 (lime). So the admin was matched to a number the portal never used, and the first portal component borrowed into an admin toolbar showed the gap.

Customer impact

Everyone who uses the admin on a computer: club owners, treasurers, registrars and tournament directors, on every screen. The change is visual only. Doing nothing leaves the admin and the portal 4 px apart for good, and every portal piece borrowed into the admin (the Ledger's Tools today, more as the club tier grows) needs its own fix.

Priority

Small and self-contained. It belongs with the one-standard formatting work you asked for on 28 September, and it is cheapest before more portal pieces are borrowed into the admin.

Success criteria

  • A re-measure of the 33 club and tournament screens from 1 October finds every button and toolbar field at 34 px.
  • The club Ledger's Export, Tools and Add entry are one height.
  • Phone sizes are identical before and after.

Findings

F01 — The 38 rests on a misreading of the portal

The 1 October record gives the reason for 38 as the portal's buttons being about 38, the same as the admin's field height. Measured, the portal's buttons are 33.4 and 31.4. The admin's 38 came from its own field height, not from the portal.

F02 — The club Ledger's Tools is the visible symptom

Tools on the club Ledger is the portal's menu button, borrowed on 2 October for the "rare tools behind Tools" ruling. It sizes itself the portal's way (33.4), so it sits short between two 38 px admin buttons. Under this proposal it fits without any fix of its own. (The tournament Teams screen also borrows it, but that screen already forces it to 38.)

F03 — The portal has its own 2 px gap

The portal's lime button has no border and its white button does, so in one toolbar the lime is 31.4 and the white is 33.4. Giving portal buttons a 34 px floor removes it and touches every portal button by 3 px at most.

F04 — About twenty tournament controls are fixed at 38 by hand

Teams, Results, Communication, Settings, Data tools, Archives and Branding set 38 directly instead of following the shared admin height. Without converting them, those screens stay at 38 while the rest moves. The build converts them so the height lives in one place again.

F05 — The club's dropdowns are 40

Recorded on 1 October as left alone: the club screens' dropdowns (Rep Teams "Show teams in") are 40. Under one control height they come to 34 too. The old Budget year picker (32) is left to the Stage 3b redraw that replaces it.

Scope

  • The admin's one control height goes from 38 to 34 on computer widths, so every button and toolbar field in the club, tournament and volunteer admin follows.
  • The fixed-38 controls (F04) and the club's 40 px dropdowns (F05) are moved onto the shared height.
  • If question 2 is yes, the portal's buttons get a 34 px floor.
  • The build check that pins the admin height at 38 is updated to 34, and the 1 October button measurement is re-run as the proof.
Out of scope. Phones: every tap size stays exactly as it is, so the change applies on computer widths only. Filter pills (30) and status chips (32) keep their heights unless question 3 says otherwise. Row actions inside tables follow the shared height already and move with it.

Approach, and the two it beat

  • Chosen: the admin comes down to the portal. The portal is the benchmark you set, so the benchmark stays put.
  • Lift only Tools to 38. This is the quickest fix and leaves the admin consistent, but the admin and portal stay 4 px apart and each borrowed portal piece needs its own patch.
  • Lift the portal to 38. This gives one height too, but it moves roughly 230 buttons on the screens that took the most work to get right.

Rollout

Styling only: no data, no migration, nothing for the demos to re-seed. In-app help is unaffected.

Build record (3 October 2026, on dev)

  • Measured on every screen, before and after. Every button and field on 175 coach and admin screens at computer width, and a third of them at phone (390) and tablet (768) width. At computer width, only the intended moves: admin 38 → 34, the portal’s 31.4 and 33.4 → 34, the Rep Teams dropdown 40 → 34. At phone and tablet width, nothing moved.
  • Three more fixed-38 controls found by the measurement and moved onto the shared height: the shared row button (Check-in’s Check in and Undo, Results’ Finalize), Check-in’s dropdown and search, and Check-in’s bucket bar. The bucket bar would otherwise have sat 4 px above the dropdown beside it.
  • Left as they are, on purpose: a registration health issue (a list row, not a control); the Results lens pills (chips); phone-only rules; the setup and onboarding form grids (form fields); icon tiles; the old house league screens; the platform console.
  • The portal’s 34 is a floor at no weight, so any button that sets its own size (a 44 px touch row, the lineup builder’s 40 px Tools) keeps it, and the Ledger’s small in-row Record stays small.
  • Checks: the repo’s standard checks pass (5,558 tests); the layout sweep on 15 changed screens at computer width found nothing from this change (one unrelated finding, below).
  • Found, not fixed: on the coach Schedule in Dark, the Game day link beside a game is dark text on a dark ground (1.06:1). It shows only near a game, so the sweep met it today; this change touched no colours.
