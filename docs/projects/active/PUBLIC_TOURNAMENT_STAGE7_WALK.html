A coach opens a pay link and sees one job: paying the entry.

What this is. With the new look (“preview”) on, the team pay page becomes a focused page. It has a slim band with the event’s name, then the pay block exactly as it is today, one way to ask a question, and a short footer. None of the event’s tabs, rail, games line, dock, prompts, badge or banner appear on it. With the preview off, the page stays exactly as it is today. This is one walk with real links, including a real test-card payment.

Nothing is pre-ticked or pre-approved. The build’s automated checks passed (measured at 360, 390 and 1280 on every state), but they are not your verdict.

Known and accepted, so they are not fails

  • No contact line on the Invitational’s pages. That organizer hides its contact on purpose, because the fixture’s address is your own inbox. The line is absent when the organizer hides the contact (ruling A1). “Stripe Walk Pay Direct” has a made-up public address (payments@stripe-walk.example) so you can see the line there.
  • The pay block itself is unchanged: its words, amounts, choices and buttons. The receipt email box starts empty for these teams, and “The $200.00 balance is paid later.” is because the fixture has no due date. Neither is a Stage 7 change.
  • Offline is not walkable on this server. The installed app’s offline worker only runs in a production build; the dev server switches it off. That a pay page is never stored for offline is proven by an automated check that runs the real worker. It is walked on a production build when the preview is switched on for customers (Stage 8).
  • The small “Skip to content” link appears only when you press Tab, on every page of the site. It is not new.
  • Tapping an email address may open your mail app or may do nothing, depending on the device. The Copy button is the reliable way.
