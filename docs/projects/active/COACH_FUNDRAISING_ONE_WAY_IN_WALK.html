The one open question

Ruling
Should a whole-team entry be allowed on a drive that has a family share set at all? The Chocolate sale pays 15% to whoever raised the money, and you have just logged an entry on it that pays nobody. I built it allowed, because refusing it would mean a team running one drive with both a bake table and per-player selling has nowhere to put the table's takings — and because the board says out loud which entries carried a share (a dash) and the facts line counts team entries separately. But it is a real question: a coach could reasonably expect 15% of $450 to reach somebody.

The alternatives, if you want one: refuse a team entry on a drive with a share above 0% and say why; or ask at the moment of saving. Both cost a step the current design does not.
