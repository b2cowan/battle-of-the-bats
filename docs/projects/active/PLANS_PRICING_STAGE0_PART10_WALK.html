Staff type their password again before a change that reaches many customers

Written 2026-11-03 for commit 1e18e9e4f. Test only: nothing here reaches a real customer or a real Stripe price. About 35 minutes, most of it two waits of about 11 minutes each, because a typed password counts for 10 minutes.

What this walk is for

Two staff doors change many customers at once, or what every new customer pays: Bulk changes, and Approve & apply on a Production price. Each now asks for the password when it was last typed more than 10 minutes ago, carries on by itself once it is accepted, and emails the owner afterwards. This walk proves each door asks, that No means nothing happened, that a wrong password changes nothing, that a fresh password is not asked for twice, and that the audit log records each step.

It passes when

  • A: both screens say what will happen before anything asks, and the Production price window requires a Why.
  • B: Bulk changes asks after 10 minutes. Cancel runs nothing, a wrong password runs nothing, and the right one runs the change with no second press. A second run straight after does not ask.
  • C: Approve & apply on a Production price asks, the request window cannot be closed behind the question, and the switch back straight after does not ask.
  • D: the audit log shows each step by name, and none of the new lines reads “Not recorded”.
  • E and F (dev site only): the owner email reads as drawn, and on a phone the question sits above the keyboard.
