Owner QA walk · public face, warm · signed-out build

Signed out is warm

Checked 2026-10-23 against the dev server. One purpose: a browser with nobody signed in always shows the warm look, and a person who chose Dark has it back the moment they sign in.

What this walk is for

A shared family computer should not show the next person the last person's Dark. The device keeps the saved Dark choice, but a signed-out browser is simply not shown it, so signing back in needs no restore. This walk proves that on the consumer app, the coaches portal and the admin, on a session that ends without the button, and on the offline page.

It passes when every screen that follows the visitor's setting (sign-in, sign-up, Home, Scores, the account screens, the offline page) is warm when signed out, with no dark flash, and every sign-in returns to Dark.

Still dark when signed out, on purpose or not yet built: the marketing pages (home, pricing, the For pages) until the marketing stage; public tournament pages (the organizer's choice, dark by default); the platform-admin login; the unsubscribe confirmation; the crash screen; the tryout scorer. They never followed the visitor's setting, so this build does not change them. Do not tick a step failed because one of these is dark.

Not in this walk: the Warm / Dark controls themselves, the account's saved choice, and the marketing set's redesign (S1 onward).
