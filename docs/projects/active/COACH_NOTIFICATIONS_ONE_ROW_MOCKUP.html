Why not the arrow not recommended

  1. Every arrow in the portal goes up to a page it names: “← Templates”, “← Practice plans”, “← All opponents”, nine screens in all. Notifications has no page above it. You open it from the bell on any desktop page, or from More on a phone. An arrow here would have to be the browser’s Back wearing the same icon: same symbol, different behaviour, and no destination to name.
  2. Sometimes there’s nothing to go back to. Opened from an email, a new tab, or after a refresh, Back would leave the app or do nothing. The arrow would need a made-up fallback, and nothing on screen would say what it was.
  3. Its neighbours don’t have one. None of the eleven other pages in More (Practice plans, Lineups, Money, Insights…) shows an arrow. Adding one here raises the same question for all eleven.
  4. No room at 360px with Option A. Arrow, divider, tile, title, gear and “?” need about 311px, and the title row has 296.

D3A message that’s cut off has nowhere to be read

You asked whether tapping a notification should open the whole message, with a button on to the place that deals with it. The problem is real, and it’s worse than it looks. But it affects one kind of notification, not the tap on every row.

What the phone hides. On 09-24 we cut the week in review to two lines on a phone, on the grounds that tapping it opens Insights, “which says the rest”. That holds only for this week. Insights describes the team today, not the week a review was written about. The real text from the test team:

Sep 20: …at home · ⚠ #12 Logan Test went over the pitching cap in 3 games (14 innings pitched vs a cap of 2/game) · #5 Emerson Test has sat the bench most — 12 innings so far.
Sep 13: …at home · ⚠ #12 Logan Test went over the pitching cap in 3 games (14 innings pitched vs a cap of 2/game) · #1 Avery Test has sat the bench most — 11 innings so far.

Both are cut at ⚠ #12 Logan Tes…. The cut lands in the middle of a pitching-cap warning, and the two weeks differ only in the part you can’t see. Of the 40 notifications in that feed, the three weekly reviews are the only ones longer than 92 characters. Everything else, like “Game moved… now 3:15 p.m. at Diamond 4”, already fits on its row.
