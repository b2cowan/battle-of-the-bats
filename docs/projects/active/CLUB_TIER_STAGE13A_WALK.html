Each person sees only their doors

Written 2026-11-06, after Stage 13a Session A (committed 2cebde79c) and Session B (committed 87ddc358e). One purpose: each of the five people (owner, admin, league admin, registrar, treasurer) sees the doors they hold, finds their own work first, and meets one honest screen, never a dead end, when a page isn’t theirs.

What this walk is for

Stage 13a made every surface list the doors a person holds. A page that isn’t yours says so the same way everywhere: what it is, who opens it by name, and a button back to your own home. The Organization page shows only what you can open. The league admin and the registrar see House league first from day one. The registrations waiting for review show as a real number where the desk looks (that line could never appear before: it counted a state no registration has). The header button reads “View site”, and the live site’s way back has a word.

It passes when

  • 1 The three new uses of shipped pieces look right: the House league card for a registrar, the waiting-count pill, and the live site’s labelled way back.
  • 2–6 For each person on the club with a season: the header says “View site”, the sidebar foot has no “Public site”, the Organization page lists only their doors, a page that isn’t theirs shows the one screen naming Morgan Ellis, and the desk (owner, admin, league admin, registrar) sees 12 waiting on the Overview, the House league card, the sidebar row and the phone tab. The treasurer sees no House league at all.
  • 7 On the club with no season, the league admin and the registrar still find House league first (sidebar programs, the phone bar’s third tab, an Overview card that says who starts the season). The owner and treasurer are unchanged.

Known before the walk (not defects of this walk)

  • “View site” opens the live site in the same tab. The drawing’s caption said a new tab; the build changed the word only. That is your call (see below).
  • The phone’s More sheet program tiles still stop at “9+” (for the owner, House league is a tile there). That tile is shared with the coaches portal, which this stage does not touch. Everything else shows the real number to 99.
  • The tournaments list and a tournament’s Public site settings page keep “Public site” on the header button: tournament screens keep their words.
  • Right after you come back from the live site, the first admin page’s button can read “Public site” (it is the way back to the exact page you came from). Move to another page and it reads “View site”.
  • The sidebar’s shape (groups, chevrons, how long it is) is 13i, next. Do not judge it here.
