07

What was ruled, and what moved since

Ruled 2026-09-09 · A1

Money-in rows sort alphabetically

One rule for the whole table. In specimen 04 that puts Bottle drive above Chocolate sale — a visible change riding along with the rename, and the price of the two halves finally behaving identically.

Ruled 2026-09-09 · B2, plus one addition

A note-less sub-line takes its schedule as its name — and drops its When cell

The ruling stands as given: a merged sub-line with no note reads “Oct” rather than echoing its parent. Writing it up revealed a repetition the drawing could not show. Every line row already prints its schedule in the When column, and again under the name on a phone — so naming the row by its schedule would have printed “Oct” three times.

So the rule gains a clause: when the schedule becomes the name, the When cell on that row is empty. Redrawn in section 03. This is the same decision with its duplicate removed, not a different one.

Widened · B2 reaches spending too

The same defect is already live on the cost side

The note-less fallback is shared between both halves of the table. Your test team’s Entry Fees row prints that word three times in one column right now — drawn in section 03’s first frame. Fixing only the money-in half would leave the identical defect on the surface money-in is being aligned to.

I intend to fix both. It is a small extra edit and a visible change to spending rows you did not ask about — say so if you would rather I left the cost side alone.

Correction · my plan was wrong

No backend change, no migration, nothing rewritten

The earlier plan said the editor would have to stop sending the line’s stored word. It does not. Spending never relied on that — it names a row from the item at display time, and money-in needs the same treatment and nothing more. Re-filing a line renames its row because the row reads the word it is filed against, not because anything is re-saved. Smaller and safer than described.

The Raising for field on drives and sponsors is untouched throughout — it shipped on 2026-09-08 and is already on production.
