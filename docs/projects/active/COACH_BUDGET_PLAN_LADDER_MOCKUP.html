Budget Plan Ladder

The Budget plan's table should add up on the page. Today the four cost categories, the two money-in sections and the installments line all sit at one level, and the closing figure arrives with nothing on screen showing how it was reached. These specimens give the plan its subtotals and its groups, so a coach can check the number without a calculator.

Round 2. The word "expected" is gone from the after specimens: bands read Costs and Funding, item rows read Fundraising and Other income, and the subtotals and tiles read Planned costs and Planned funding. Specimen 01 still shows today's words.

NEW added rows or headings RESTYLED existing row, new treatment UNCHANGED exactly as it renders today Frames are the coach portal's warm skin at real pixel sizes, 1180px wide. Figures are the QA team's; line and roster counts are examples.

The plan is three numbers and one answer. Planned costs, less planned funding, is what players have to cover. Before dues are set, that is the estimated installments figure. Once dues are set, the scheduled figure takes its place and the difference is a shortfall or a buffer.

The tiles above the table already name those three numbers. The proposal is that the table's subtotals carry the same three names, so the tiles read as the headline of the table beneath them rather than as a separate summary.

09What I would decide before building

  • 02 or 04. Bands and subtotals, or subtotals alone. I recommend 02.
  • Names (settled in round 2). Group headings are bare nouns: Costs, Funding. Item rows are bare too: Fundraising, Sponsorship, Other income, the way the cost categories already are. The one qualifier sits on the number that is the plan's figure, and it is the same qualifier on both sides: Planned costs, Planned funding. The tiles above the table carry those same words. Costs less funding is the grid's existing closing label reused in the list.
  • The word "expected" leaves the plan. Everything on this screen is planned, so a second qualifier only implied a distinction the plan does nothing with. It goes from the tiles, both views, both exports, the set-dues window's arithmetic line ("less $1,950.00 planned funding"), five help articles and the demo sandbox's check. Sponsorship stays a row of its own, because Budget vs. Actual reports it apart from fundraising; it just loses the prefix.
  • Reach. The plan export prints the same rows the list does, so the subtotals and the ladder go into the export in the same change. The By period grid and its export take the two bands and two subtotals. The demo sandbox's budget narration should be re-read; it describes this screen.
  • Not touched. The tiles, the toolbar, the When column, category folding, the pencil, "What am I forgetting?", and Budget vs. Actual.
