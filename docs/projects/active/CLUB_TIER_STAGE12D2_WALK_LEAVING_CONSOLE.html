12d-2, leaving online payments and the console

Written 2026-10-27 for the 12d-2 build (ffee7864c to 2a171752a). One purpose: when a club stops taking online payments, in any of the ways it can, it is told exactly what is outstanding first and nothing about its Stripe account is touched; and the support team can read a club’s online payments, and every club’s trouble, in one place that changes nothing.

What this walk is for

One rule: no ending disconnects the club’s Stripe account. You check what each window says is outstanding: families and teams still owing, pay pages open right now, bank debits clearing, open card disputes. Disconnect (part A), cancel and the downgrade of a Tournament Plus club (part B), and a request to delete (part C) are all read and then cancelled; none of them is carried out. After a plan loses online payments the owner’s card turns into the wind-down card (part D), and an ending the owner didn’t choose is warned about in advance (part E).

Then the console (part F): a club’s Online payments tab, Health › Online payments across every club, who can open them, the operator’s own Delete, cancel and plan-change windows, and the Story lines. Emails are held (part G).

It passes when

  • The Disconnect window counts families and teams that owe, the pay pages open now (a number that rises when a family opens a page and falls when the family pays), and the open disputes with their due date. A count that can’t be read is left out and never reads as nobody.
  • Cancel, downgrade and deletion windows carry the same facts in their own section and say the Stripe account stays the club’s.
  • Nothing in this walk disconnects UAT Rep Club or deletes anything. The wind-down card appears only for a club whose plan lost online payments, and it undoes by moving the plan back.
  • The console tab is read only: no Retry on its fee panel, a child’s name only after pressing “Show the player”, and every opening is logged. Never a card or bank number.
  • Super admin, Billing and Support open Online payments (club tab, Health list, Today’s count); Product, Growth and Read only have no entry, and the page sends them away.
  • The operator’s Delete window names the money in flight and cannot be confirmed.

Not in this walk

  • The Payouts tab and the payments terms: walk §404. Steps F14 to F16 read what its part D leaves in the club’s Story.
  • Every email: the ending warning, and the warning for a failed subscription payment (part G, held). The daily job sends them and localhost cannot.
  • Actually disconnecting, cancelling, deleting or downgrading UAT Rep Club: it is the shared test club for every walk. Only part D moves its plan, and says so; if you’d rather not, mark D Held.
  • A failed subscription payment that warns the owner the day it fails: can’t be produced on localhost. The Amplify dev end-to-end pass.
  • Held for the owner, not built: the drawn line “Your How to pay text is empty · Write it now” in the Disconnect window (How to pay belongs to a season or a tournament, and the card has no such field).
