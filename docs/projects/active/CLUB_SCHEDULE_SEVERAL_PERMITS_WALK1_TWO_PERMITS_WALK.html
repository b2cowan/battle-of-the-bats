Build a schedule on two permits, from two towns

Written 2026-11-16, after Sessions A (673670185), B (da0f39b53), C (3ee39edaa) and D (e4753a86b) and the Close’s words pass (f0f36651d, b4ce14bea). One purpose: a club holding permits from two towns builds one practice schedule from both, and every screen says which permit each hour belongs to.

What this walk is for

Harbourtown holds permits from Town of Milton and Town of Halton Hills for a four-month season (Nov 30, 2026 to Mar 28, 2027): Milton evenings ($60.00 an hour, Kinsmen Park), Halton Hills evenings ($90.00, Prospect Park) and Kinsmen Thursdays (a second Milton permit for the same Thursdays). You pick permits and read what each brings, find free Tuesday and Thursday evenings across all of them, close one park on one day while another day closes both, read the permit door lines, price the same time two ways, and let a permit’s new closure send you to “Take … off”.

It passes when

  • 1 Each ticked permit brings its own card, issuer and time; unticking one takes its rows away; two permits on the same park and hours say which one keeps the evenings.
  • 2 Find the time lists every facility the club holds, says what free means, counts evenings free of held, names the days two permits both hold and who prices them, and settles your picks into one card per permit.
  • 3 A date off can name its park (“Applies to”) or close All venues, and the grid shows the difference; the permit’s own date off arrives with its time.
  • 4 The schedule names both permits at the top, each a door to that permit.
  • 5 The bill window prices the same time per permit or at one rate: the total stays, only the split moves, and the note under the table says “the permits’”.
  • 6 A permit’s new date off lands in Needs attention as “Outside the permit” and the row’s own button finishes the job.

Not in this walk

  • Adding time to a schedule already sent, taking a permit off it, and what happens to played practices and earlier payments: that is walk 2.
  • Finalizing or sending this schedule. Nothing here is emailed to anyone.
  • The “Another time” door in the finder, the Kind, Where and Town filters, and a draft’s held days (the unit tests cover them).
  • Money arithmetic to the cent. Three unit-test suites pin it; this walk checks that the screens agree with each other.
