Written 2026-11-07, after Session A was built, simplified and reviewed. What this walk is for

(1) The club band, “The club today”, sits above “This morning” for the owner and a club admin: Cash on hand, Owed to the club, This week, Needs someone. Each tile opens the lines behind it. A tile the person cannot open is simply not there. (2) “Still to do”: a club admin reads the owner’s open setup steps, can open the ones their access allows, and loses the block when setup is done. (3) The league admin’s and the registrar’s “This morning” on a live season’s page. (4) The install banner on the admin skips a browser’s first visit.

It passes when

  • 1–3 The owner’s band shows four tiles above “This morning”, both above the fold on a computer; every tile and line opens the right page; on a phone it is two by two in the same order.
  • 4 The club admin sees the same band plus a “Still to do” block of the owner’s open steps.
  • 5–6 An admin with Accounting off has no Cash or Owed tile; an admin without Families has no forms line.
  • 7 When setup is finished, “Still to do” is gone.
  • 8 A brand-new club reads in plain words, never a row of zeros.
  • 9–10 The league admin and the registrar see their own morning on the season page, on a computer and on a phone.
  • 11 The install banner does not show on a browser’s first visit to the admin and does on the next.
  • 12 The treasurer and the league roles see neither the band nor “Still to do” on the Overview.

Not in this walk

  • The band’s “didn’t load” states. A broken read cannot be made on a copy. The wording for a failed tile (“Didn’t load”) and a failed band (“The club today didn’t load.” with Try again) is pinned by unit tests.
  • An admin limited to some team groups. The copy has none. A unit test pins that such an admin is refused the setup list (it is the whole club’s, not their groups’).
  • The “Setup · n of 5” chip in the header (waits for the admin header bar drawings), “Club time” (13d) and “Find a child or a parent” (13e). None is built.
  • The invite dialog and invite emails. Session B.
