The ask

A timeline card that reads like a bracket card

On the Timeline, a 90-minute game is a block about 100 px tall, but everything it says sits in the top 35 px: the time, a tiny label in capitals, and both teams squeezed onto one line. Below that the block is empty. A finished game shows no score. It just turns paler.

After this change, the block uses its height the way the bracket card does: the time and the game's state at the top, then one team per line with its score. A short game falls back to fewer lines, because the block's height is the game's length and can't grow to fit the words.

Round 2, as built (19 October 2026). A timeline card reads the same as that game's row in the Day view: away team first, and the winning score in green for pool and playoff games alike. Round 1 drew playoff cards with the bracket's bold and check, on my mistaken claim that the Day view does the same. It doesn't. The check belongs to the Bracket view alone, and you chose the Day view's green score. The one addition is the grey italic waiting slot, which you approved separately.

What you ruled (19 October 2026)

RuledA finished game turns white instead of fading

The card keeps its colour stripe and turns white. Tinted means still to play, white means done, and the score stays at full strength.

RuledEach card reads like its row in the Day view

Away team first, with the winning score in green for pool and playoff games alike. Round 1 drew playoff cards with the bracket's bold and check, on my mistaken claim that the Day view did the same. Corrected before the build, and you chose the green score. The check stays the Bracket view's own mark.

RuledFour lengths, four layouts

Full at about 90 minutes, medium near 60, compact near 45, and one line at 30 or less. The one-line form shows the time and the teams. Its state is in the hover and the game window.

RuledA cancelled game gets a chip, not a line through it

The "Cancelled" chip, on a faded card.

OpenDoes the Day view's waiting slot follow?

The timeline shows "Quarterfinal 2 winner" in grey italic. The Day view's row still shows it in plain ink, so the same slot reads two ways across the two views.

Recommend: yes, the Day row follows. It's a small change.

Already ruledA field named like its venue shows once

Ruled 17 October ("a facility named exactly like its venue shows once") but it never reached this header. It's now fixed in the column heads, the phone's field pager and the + Field menu. A field with its own name ("Riverside Park" over "Diamond 2") keeps both lines.

What stays the same

  • A block's height is the game's length, and its top is the start time.
  • The colour stripe is the division, shifted for each pool.
  • Drag a game to move it. Click it to open the game window. On a phone, tap it to reschedule.
  • Hovering still shows the full division, pool, time and any conflict sentence.
  • Hour labels down the side. Whether they keep the typewriter face is still an open lettering question (T25), and this change doesn't decide it.
  • The phone shows one field at a time. Each card there is the same card, at the phone's width.
