Finished, honestly: a season ends one way, and every page says which

Written 2026-11-03 for Stage 9f-1 (built, reviewed, help updated). One purpose: a house-league season is either running or finished, Archive is gone, the words “Final” and “Ended” mean what they say, and the free League plan counts only live seasons.

What this walk is for

A finished season now has one name, Finished, and one rare door, Reopen. Archive is gone, and a season someone archived before reads exactly like a completed one. A season whose last day has passed but that the club has not completed reads Ended {date}; only a completed season is Final standings. The Complete and Reopen windows say what happens to player ratings, refunds and newer seasons. A coach of a finished team sees the team’s Final place. On the free League plan, last year’s finished season no longer blocks next year’s, and both refusals name the season that holds the one place.

It passes when

  • No Archive door, window or word appears anywhere a league runner looks.
  • The Complete and Reopen windows carry the new lines, with the right date (“Ended Aug 28, 2025” for last year, “Ended Oct 25” for this year) and the newer season named.
  • Families read “Ended Oct 25” for a season past its last day, and “Final standings” only after the club completes it.
  • The coach’s finished page shows “Final place: …” when families could see the table, and no line when standings were off.
  • On the free League plan, Create season and Reopen each refuse in words naming the season that holds the place, Reopen with only a Close button, and a finished season never blocks.
  • A legacy archived season reads exactly as a completed one: Finished, Reopen only, “This season has concluded.”, Final standings, the setup checklist and the birthday card.

Known before the walk

  • The dev server must be restarted first. This build adds a new shared module; it was restarted at hand-off.
  • The fixture is three throwaway clubs with their own sign-ins (below). It touches no other club, walk or sign-in, and a re-run rebuilds only them. Dev only; it refuses production.
  • Walk Fall 2025 finished “today”. The fixture completes it when it runs, so the coach’s page is open for 30 days from then. Its last day is still Aug 28, 2025.
  • No data change was needed. Dev and production had no archived house-league seasons. Walk Spring 2024 is archived by the fixture only so you can see one.
  • The free League plan is parked (off for everyone in production). The fixture switches it on for Walk 9f-1 Free League only.

Not in this walk

  • “Tied for 3rd of 6” on the coach’s line, and three or more newer seasons (“Your 3 newer seasons are not changed.”). Both are proved by the tests.
  • A request that skips the Reopen window on the free plan. The server refuses it too; proved by the tests.
  • Start next season from this one: that is Stage 9f-2.
  • The help articles. Read them if you like (Help › House league › The season’s lifecycle, Plans and limits, Closing out a season); they are not walk steps.
