Product-manager brief

Verify every sign-up

What changes for people

  • Starting a team or a league, joining the coach portal, or registering a team as a new coach: after the form, the person sees "check your email". The link signs them in and brings them back to finish: the team or league name filled in, or straight into their portal.
  • A tournament registration goes through at once. The organizer receives it whether or not the coach opens the email; the email only opens the coach's portal.
  • Invitations stay instant. Arriving from an invitation email already proves the inbox.
  • Existing accounts and sign-in are untouched. Dev stays instant, like the org sign-up there.

Why it matters

Today anyone can create a ready-to-use account for an email they don't own. A club invitation, assistant invite or tournament team claim sent to that address later attaches to their account. Once every account proves its email, a confirmed email means the inbox is theirs everywhere in the product, and no future feature can reopen the hole by forgetting a check.

Customer impact

Every new free coach and league organizer takes one extra step: open an email. Organizers' registrations are unaffected. Owner ruling 2026-06-08 already planned this as Phase 8 ("one email verification across all account creation"); this does it now.

Priority

A security fix from the second sweep (2026-10-16). It needs foreknowledge of an address and the real owner can win the account back by a password reset, so it follows the fixes that exposed data to anyone.

Success criteria

  • No account is usable until its email is proven by a link or an invitation.
  • A new coach can still go from the public form to their portal with one email click, and the organizer sees the registration before that click.
  • Sign-up completion on the free floors holds (watch the start-team and registration funnels for two weeks after release).
Full assessment

Verify every sign-up — implementation plan

Problem / context

/api/auth/coach-signup creates accounts with email_confirm: true and no link, for any unused email. Several places trust a confirmed email as proof of the inbox: invitation reconciliation at sign-in (reconcilePendingInvitesForUser), coach invitations, assistant invites, and tournament team claims. The org sign-up (/api/auth/signup) and team sign-up already verify by link in production (REQUIRE_SIGNUP_EMAIL_VERIFICATION, default on when NODE_ENV=production).

Scope

ScreenAfter the change
Start a team (StartTeamForm)Check-your-email state; link returns to /start/team?team=… signed in, name filled
Start a league (StartLeagueForm)Same, /start/league?league=…
Public registration (RegisterContent)Registration submitted first, signed out; then the account, pending; success card says check your email; link lands on the registration's portal record
Coach portal join (/coaches/join)Check-your-email state; link returns to the join route, which links the registration
Assistant invite (accept-assistant-invite)Unchanged for the person: the request carries the invite token; the server confirms at once only when the token's invited email equals the email
Out of scope

Changing sign-in, existing accounts, or the org and team sign-ups (they already verify). The two regular sign-ups' missing staff-email check (review note on the staff fix) is folded in: all three refuse a FieldLogicHQ staff email.

Approach

  • One verification rule. Move shouldRequireEmailVerification() out of the org sign-up into a shared module; coach-signup uses it. When on: generateLink({ type: 'signup', … redirectTo: /auth/callback?next=… }) and the existing signup_verification email; respond { requiresEmailVerification: true }. When off (dev): today's instant path.
  • Proof paths stay instant. A request carrying an assistant invite token is confirmed at once only if the token is live and its invited email equals the email (one read, server-side).
  • A safe return. The client sends where to come back to; the server keeps only a same-site relative path (the sign-up's existing safeNext rule) and carries the typed team or league name as a query value.
  • One shared "check your email" block for all screens, built from the org sign-up's own state, plus "Send the link again" (throttled; the org sign-up gains it too).
  • Registration order. When verification is on, the public form submits the registration signed out (the route already accepts it), then creates the pending account with next pointing at the registration's join/claim route, which links it once the coach arrives verified.
  • Interim, shipped separately (2026-10-16): coach-signup is rate-limited like the other sign-ups.

Open questions

V1 (one tap on return), V2 (the registration goes first) and V3 (resend on every sign-up, the org sign-up included) were ruled as recommended on 2026-10-16. The build’s one residual, R1 on the Decisions tab, was accepted as it stands on 2026-10-17.

As built (2026-10-16)

  • The one rule lives in lib/signup-verification.ts; the org, team and coach sign-ups, the resend and Step 1's account check all read it. All three sign-ups refuse a FieldLogicHQ staff address.
  • Coach sign-up: throttle, then the staff refusal, then — with verification on and no live assistant invitation addressed to the same email — an existing account answers “already has an account” and a new one is made by a sign-up link and waits. Otherwise it is instant, as before.
  • Send the link again sends a sign-in link, not a second sign-up link (which would overwrite the waiting account’s password); only to an account still waiting; the same answer whatever the address.
  • The screens share one block (components/auth/VerifyEmailSent), wearing each host’s look. The start forms come back with the name filled in; the join page links the registration on return; the register form sends the registration first.
  • Review fixes (/review high-risk, four lenses): the email’s click-through page followed any link it was handed — a crafted one could run script on our site (pre-existing; every sign-up now passes through it) — and now follows only our own Supabase project’s verify link; the join page no longer links when the coach’s team list failed to load, and sends one request per visit; the register form answers a waiting account with the registration and a fresh link, never “wrong password”.
  • Proof: guard tests/unit/signup-verification-guard.test.ts (23 tests; 18 of 18 deliberate breakages caught); the routes called directly with verification on (10 of 10), the invitation proof (3 of 3), dev unchanged (4 of 4); a real verify link passes the click-through check and three crafted ones are refused.
  • Walk: the QA walk tab, ledger §326 — staging only, since localhost runs with verification off and no email key.

Design review (2026-10-16)

Run on round 1 before hand-off; all seven applied in round 2.

  • D1 Start a team and Start a league were drawn in the sign-in card's style; the real screens use the start forms' own look (sentence-case heading, Title Case labels, rounded ink-on-lime button) and the titles "Create Your Team Home" and "Start Your Season".
  • D2 Register was drawn in the consumer frame; it is the event's own public page, with its step bar and the "You're In Motion" success card.
  • D3 The email was marked unchanged but drawn with words it doesn't have; it is now the real dark template, with the team named in its existing line.
  • D4 The join page keeps its real title, "Create Your Coaches Portal Account".
  • D5 "Send the link again" meets the 44px tap floor.
  • D6 "Start again" became "Use a different email", which keeps the entries.
  • D7 The resend's after-state is drawn: the button gives way to "Sent again at 2:14 p.m." until another send is allowed.

Rollout

  • No migration. Dev stays instant by default; staging and production verify.
  • No UAT spec signs up through these screens — they make their accounts directly — so none needs a change (checked in the build).
  • Demo sandboxes sign in demo accounts directly and are unaffected.
  • Help articles for starting a team, a league and registering a team change: run /docs in the build.
