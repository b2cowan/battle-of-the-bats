Design review · Coaches Portal · Money

Adjustment Band & Payback List

Two write modals a coach opens from a family's dues record, drawn at the modal's true width of 520px. The left frame of each pair is what ships today; the right is the proposal. Pins key to the notes beneath.

Round 1 · 2026-09-08 · frames use the warm portal's own colours, type sizes and spacing · open on a laptop for true size

Both defects have one cause. Two pieces of text were placed inside these modals without a size from the portal's type ladder, so each inherited the browser's default 16px. The stated kind in Add a credit and the debt titles in We paid a family back are the only two things in either form that do not sit on the ladder, which is why they read as headings beside 14px inputs. The fix is the same in both places: put the text on the ladder, and give it the room the form already has.

Proposed

Restyledkind 14px · on the ladder

Drawn with an amount typed so the saving line shows its place. The band reads the credit's own kind when an older credit is edited — Overpayment, Forgiven, Reimbursement — exactly as the Type word does today.

  • A

    The word is off the ladder. "Adjustment" never took a type size, so it inherited 16px semibold and sits taller than the inputs either side of it. It reads as a section heading, not a value.

  • B

    The kind comes after the amount it frames. Type is the last question on the form, but it is the one fact that decides what the amount means. A coach types a figure and only then learns that no money is behind it.

  • C

    The explainer lengthens one column. Five lines under Type against a one-line Notes field opposite, so the row's two halves end at different heights and the form looks unfinished.

  • D

    The stated band, in the position Record money gives it. Same olive edge, same two-line shape, no TYPE label above it because the band names itself. A coach who has recorded money already knows this band means "the question is answered".

  • E

    The teaching sentence drops to one quiet line, full width. It exists for the coach who opened the wrong door, so it points at the three other doors and nothing more. The definition of an adjustment moved up into the band's second line.

  • F

    Notes takes the whole row. With Type gone from the grid, Notes no longer has a partner; a lone half-width field beside empty space would be the same imbalance from the other side.

Alternative considered · not recommended

The band where the Type word sits today

The smallest possible change: keep the grid, swap the bare word for the band. Drawn so the two positions can be judged side by side rather than argued.

Proposed

Restyledlist 472px · titles 14px · rows keep the 44px floor

Drawn with one debt ticked so the total and the saving line have something to say. The band, the date, the method, the note and the buttons are unchanged.

  • A

    The list has half the room. It sits in one column of a two-column grid, 229px of the 472px the form has, so every title wraps and three debts run to nine lines.

  • B

    The titles are off the ladder. Same cause as the credit modal: 16px bold, inherited, taller than the 14px value in every input around it. The amounts are the same size, so the column reads loud twice.

  • C

    The date is alone in the other column with dead space under it the height of the whole list, and the How field lands beneath the list on the left instead of beside its partner.

  • D

    One row per debt, full width. Title on the left, amount flush right in tabular figures, checkbox and text on one centre line. Nothing wraps, so the row's 44px floor is the row's real height.

  • E

    Title 14px, kind and date 12px, amount 14px. The three sizes the form already uses for a value, a caption and a figure. A ticked row keeps its olive tint.

  • F

    "Paying back" becomes the list's own footer row. Today it hangs beneath the box as a separate bordered line; inside the box, on the paper ground, it reads as the sum of what is above it, the way a table's total does.

  • G

    Date paid and How share a row. They are the two short answers on this form and the grid was built for exactly this pairing. Note stays full width beneath, and the saving line keeps its place above the buttons.

For the owner

Three calls, recommendation first

D1 · Add a credit

Where the band sits

Recommended: at the top of the form, above every field, with no TYPE label.

That is where Record money places its stated band and what a coach already reads it as. The alternative, the band in the Type slot beside Notes, is drawn above; it keeps the grid but makes the band read as a second input.

D2 · Add a credit

The teaching sentence

Recommended: keep it, one quiet line under the band, full width.

It exists only for the coach who opened this door meaning another one, so it names the three other doors and stops. The definition of an adjustment moves up into the band's second line. Cutting the sentence entirely is a fair call too; the band would still say what this credit is.

D3 · We paid a family back

The "Paying back" total

Recommended: fold it into the list as its footer row.

A total under a list of amounts reads as their sum when it shares their box and their right edge. Keeping it as the separate line it is today is the smaller change and loses nothing else in this proposal.

What this does not change

Phones, and the rest of both forms

On a phone both modals already run one column, so the debt list has always had the full width there; the half-width defect is a desktop-only shape, which is the reverse of the usual miss and part of why no sweep caught it. The stated band's phone shape is the Record money band's phone shape, already in the product. Inputs, dates, the method dropdown, the footer buttons, the dismiss guard and every sentence the forms speak are unchanged.
