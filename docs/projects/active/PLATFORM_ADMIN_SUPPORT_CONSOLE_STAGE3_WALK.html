Support Console Money Walk

One question: can support find a double charge on a club’s record, and refund it, without leaving the console? Tick what holds, and write what does not. Your ticks stay in this browser; the paste-back at the foot gathers them for the chat. Everything runs on the dev server, in Stripe’s test mode: no real money moves. The guard rails (a paying club’s plan, Bulk changes, the words) are walk 2 (§385).

Before you start (about 10 minutes, once)

The Money tab reads Stripe, so the club needs a test-mode subscription with a few charges. Dev’s UAT clubs have none yet. Use UAT Test Org (the free Tournament plan; its owner is uat-owner@uat-test-org.local).

  1. As the club owner (a private window, http://localhost:3000/auth/login), open Settings › Plan & billing and choose Tournament Plus, monthly. Pay with Stripe’s test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future expiry, any CVC.
  2. In Stripe’s test dashboard (https://dashboard.stripe.com/test/customers), open that customer. Create a one-off invoice of $25.00 with the line “Extra tournament setup”, and charge it to the card on file. This is a charge that is not the subscription’s.
  3. For the double charge: on the same customer, create a second subscription on the Tournament Plus price and charge it now. The Money tab should name two live subscriptions; its charge is the one to refund. Cancel that second subscription in Stripe once section 6 is done.

If Plan & billing does not show Tournament Plus a minute after paying, the dev server is not receiving Stripe’s messages: run stripe listen --forward-to localhost:3000/api/billing/webhook and pay again (or press Read again on the Money tab, which reads Stripe directly either way).

Then sign in to the console at http://localhost:3000/platform-admin/login.
