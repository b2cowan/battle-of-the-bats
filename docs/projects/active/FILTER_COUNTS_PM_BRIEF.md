# Filter Counts — PM brief

**Ruled 2026-10-05** (all six recommendations accepted). Hub: https://claude.ai/artifact/EFWMUeiC3sLkn4CQaBqzrw

## What changes for a coach or treasurer

- In an open filter list, each choice's count sits at the end of its row in a quiet column, on a desk and on a phone
  (the way the Awards filter already shows it).
- A narrowed filter names what it is narrowed to — "Status · Actual, Scheduled" — instead of "2 selected", in the same
  words the phone's Filter sheet uses. Four or more choices still read "4 selected".
- On the club's Ledger the counts become true: tick a choice and that many rows show. "All" on Status shows every
  line, voided ones included.
- The coach's Type filter reads "Every type" at rest, like every other filter.
- The practice library's Tags filter follows the same rule.

## Why it matters

One state read two ways (desk "2 selected", phone "Actual, Scheduled"), and the club's numbers disagreed with the list
under them ("Expenses (7)" above five rows) on the screen a treasurer reconciles from.

## Customer impact

Every coach who filters a Ledger or the practice library, and every club treasurer. Small per visit; trust on the money
screens. Nothing is added or removed.

## Priority

Low effort. The club's count defect is the urgent part.

## Success criteria

- A narrowed filter can be read without opening it, at every width.
- The desk pill, the phone row and the open list never show one state in different words.
- On both Ledgers, ticking a choice shows exactly the rows its number promised.
