My recommendation

The direction is right and it is cheap where it counts: a band with the club's name in text carries the club's identity with images blocked, which is how most parents will first see it. The logo is a bonus on top. Everything below is drawn that way. Nine asks at the end; every one has a recommendation.

Where I disagree with the brief, and what the code showed

  • The sender name in the inbox matters more than the logo. A parent sees "FieldLogicHQ" in the inbox row for every club's email, and the preview line under the subject starts with the words "FieldLogicHQ" too. The band fixes the preview; only changing the sender display name fixes the row. I draw both and ask (Ask 1).
  • Wearing the club makes the reply problem worse. Only 2 of the 14 family emails set a reply address. A parent who taps Reply on an email that looks like it came from the club writes to the platform's sending address, which the deployment plan lists as a no-reply address. Putting the club's name on those emails without a reply decision is a promise the email cannot keep (Ask 4).
  • The brief is right that the club's contact lives on the public site. The organization record's contact email is always empty (the data dictionary and the TODO say so), so the only real address for "reach the club" is the public site's contact, which a club may not have filled in.
  • One premise in the brief is off. The "FieldLogicHQ does not process online payments" line is not in a family email. It is in the coach-facing tournament registration email (group D), so Stage 12's receipt does not inherit it. I flag it for /marketing and change nothing.
