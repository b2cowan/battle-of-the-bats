The coach’s side: the line, the one copy, Subscribe

Written 2026-11-01 for Stage 9d-3, Session 2 (Parts 5–7). One purpose; sign it off on its own. Walk A (the league admin telling families) is §436 and passed.

What this walk is for

A house-league coach used to see their team page and nothing about the practices’ families. Now the page says, in one quiet line under Schedule, whether the families know the practice times. The first time a team’s families are told, each coach of that team gets one short copy. And the coach has a Subscribe button on the Schedule line: their own calendar link, with a Reset. This walk reads all three, and who can and cannot reach them.

It passes when

  • The line under Schedule is true in every state: told on a date, not told yet, told different times, and “No practices yet. Nobody has handed any out.” for a team that never had one. It never says the same “No practices yet.” twice.
  • A first send by the league admin marks the team’s coaches as copied, once. A second send for the same notice does not copy them again. Another team’s coaches are not copied.
  • Subscribe sits on the Schedule heading’s line (the word at a desk, the mark alone on a phone), opens the shared window in the club’s look, gives the coach’s own link, and the link opens a calendar with the team’s practices.
  • Reset asks first, makes a new link, and the old link answers an empty calendar.
  • A coach of another team cannot reach this team’s page or link address. A removed coach’s link empties. A made-up link is “not found”.

Not in this walk

  • Real mail. Dev has no email key. The coach’s copy email cannot be read on dev (the terminal only prints a “skipping send” line per email and never names who). The proof here is the marker the fixture prints. The email’s words are pinned by a unit test and read on the first production send.
  • A finished season. It cannot be staged without ending the shared UAT season. “A finished season has no Subscribe, no schedule, no stopped line” is pinned by tests/unit/house-league-9d3-coach.test.ts (“a live season only”) and not walked here.
  • A real calendar app. Dev is localhost, so Google or Apple cannot subscribe to it. The link is opened in a browser and read as a file instead.
  • A cancelled practice marked “Cancelled ·” in the feed, a coach who is only invited (no copy, no Subscribe), and the line for a team whose every told practice was taken away. All unit-pinned.
