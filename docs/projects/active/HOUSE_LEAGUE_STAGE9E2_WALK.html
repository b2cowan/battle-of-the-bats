Rainout: call off a day’s games in one press

Written 2026-11-03 for Stage 9e-2 (built, uncommitted). One purpose: a rainout calls off a day’s games in one press, tells families or holds the changes, and can be put back while held.

What this walk is for

Before this build, a rained-out evening meant opening each game and postponing it by hand, division by division, and then sending each division’s changes. Now a Rainout button on the schedule’s header opens one window: pick the day, postpone (the default) or cancel, tick the games (each published division’s, grouped), add a line of your own for the email, and either email the families now or hold the changes in Changes to send to look first. A held rainout can be put back in one press until anything is sent. Once emails go, they can’t be taken back, and the window says so.

It passes when

  • Only people who run the season see the Rainout button, and it keeps its word on a phone.
  • The window lists exactly the games still to play on the day in published divisions, names a draft division instead of listing it, and every count, sentence and button reads the numbers the fixture printed.
  • Holding a rainout changes the games, writes the changes into Changes to send, tells nobody, and the club’s line is in the family email there, word for word.
  • Put all back returns every game at its old time, asks first, and works only while nothing has been sent.
  • Sending a division’s changes from Changes to send carries the line, and afterwards the rainout can no longer be put back.
  • Emailing now says how many families were written, offers no Undo, and “Give them new dates” opens that division’s Needs a new date.
  • A game that changed in another tab is refused, nothing is changed, and the window lists what is still left.

Known before the walk

  • Dev has no email key. A send is recorded and its families are counted (“N emails sent”), but nothing arrives in an inbox. This walk never checks an inbox: the family email’s words are read through See their email in Changes to send, on a held rainout (Part C).
  • Where the build differs from the drawing (say if any of these should change): no phone “4 of 4 ›” drill-in (the form table lists every game, with a “2 of 2” figure per division); no desk “4 more, all ticked” collapse (every game is listed); places are a white-track switch (or a dropdown with more places), never pills; “Practices” in the practices line is words, not a link (only “Schedules” links); the button counts families (“email 18 families”), as accepted.
  • The line rides the whole division’s email. If a held rainout is sent from Changes to send alongside unrelated held changes in that division, the line appears in that one email. This is the disclosed design: the email says one thing to a family.
  • Put all back does not re-check bookings. It returns games to their old times the same way Undo does; if a field was booked for something else in between, nothing stops it.
  • The practices line counts the whole season’s practices that day, not just the walk’s: U9 Rookie’s own practice on the same evening is in the “booked by hand” count when the fixture runs on a Tuesday.
  • The fixture’s day is today. Run it on the day you walk, and again at the start of D, E, F and G (each part says so). It clears what the earlier part left.

Not in this walk

  • Email delivery. No inbox is checked (no email key in dev). The sample and the counts stand in.
  • A failed send. The “one division’s email didn’t go” wording needs a failing mail transport; it is pinned by unit tests.
  • Rainout on a finished season. The button isn’t offered there; the server’s refusal is unit-pinned.
  • Coach score entry and “Final standings”. Those are 9e-3 and 9f.
