Public tournament pages · Stage 9 · the game-order rule

Out of order, on screen and at every door

A tournament game can no longer be saved to start before a game it waits for has ended: a Final before its Semifinals end, a 3rd Place game before the Semifinal whose loser it takes, any playoff game before its division’s last pool game ends. A schedule that is already out of order is shown (a red chip on both games, a health row, a Filter row), never locked. You open test games that are out of order, try to make more, and fix some.

Written Nov 19, 2026 against the dev site. Every club, team and game here is test data. 50 steps in 8 walks, plus 3 calls for you that are not counted. The steps do one thing each.

Before you start

  • The test games are built by step 1 of walk 1: node --env-file=.env.local .probe/stage9-walk.mjs --setup (also rebuilds them if you want a clean start). They sit on Sat, Dec 5, 2026, in the future, so they count as still to play (a played game never wears a chip). The script moves the event’s dates to Dec 5 to 6 and the last step of walk 8 puts them back.
  • Three test divisions: Walk U13 Boys (pool play, semifinals, a Final and 3rd Place game that are out of order), Walk U15 Tiers (Gold and Silver, each with SF1, SF2 and a Final) and Walk U11 Girls (a Final that starts exactly when its Semifinal ends).
  • Walk them in order. Walk 6 fixes the U13 order, so do walks 3 and 4 first. Walk 5 adds one game (walk 8’s last step removes it).
  • Walks 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 need a computer-width window; walk 2 a phone-width one (F12, device toolbar).

Already proved (don’t re-do these)

  • Every door refuses on the server when a stale screen or a second admin sends a bad save: the game window, Add game, the bracket editor, both generators, the rain delay and its Undo, putting a cancelled game back, the CSV import and the two default-length settings. 22 of 22 direct-request checks passed on dev, and nothing is written by a refusal.
  • The rule itself (reused codes across tiers and divisions, a game with no bracket, lower-case placeholders, equal starts, the length chain, cancelled games, pool play, before-and-after) and every sentence: unit tests pass.
  • The review found two real defects, both fixed: the event’s default-length refusal never reached its red line, and putting a cancelled game back through the scoring screen skipped the rule. The bracket editor now also judges pool play and a new game’s length the way the server does; the playoff generator works out when pool play ends from each pool game’s own length.
  • Layout: ten schedule screens, including the new Add-game and move-sheet screens, measured at 361, 768 and 1440 pixels with no new findings.
  • Not proved: I could not open these test games in a browser myself (the host account stops at the Terms screen, and I do not accept terms on your behalf), so the chips, the health row and the Filter row are seen for the first time in walk 1.

Recorded, not walked

  • Scoring a Grand Final can reinstate a cancelled second final without the rule. A direct request with an oddly written date or time (like 2026-8-9) is read as no date, so the rule skips it (the overlap check has the same gap). The build check that keeps new writers behind the rule accepts a call to the length guard as proof, and would miss a few unusual ways of writing games. A few notices and red lines go stale in rare orders (a refused drop’s error notice overwritten by a slower earlier move; the reinstate line after closing the window).
