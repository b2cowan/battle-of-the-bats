§6 Refunds

New

How a sponsor refund works, including when the card company sends it back.

• A sponsor's card payment is refunded from the deal. Your organization decides each refund of a sponsorship and makes it from the deal in FieldLogicHQ, for all of what can go back or for part of it, with a reason. We do not decide refunds or make them on your behalf. Anyone at your organization who can open Sponsorships can make one. • A refund of a sponsorship never includes the processing fee, including a processing fee that the business paid, or the GST/HST on that fee. Neither part of the processing fee comes back (§4). Whether your organization makes the business whole for a fee it paid is between your organization and the business, and is not done through the refund FieldLogicHQ makes. • What we send. When a refund reaches the card, we email the business a notice of it, with your reason, and, when the invoice carried GST/HST, a credit note for that refund, in your organization's name. Refunding everything that was paid ends the deal. • If a refund does not reach the card. If the card company sends a refund back after we have told the business about it, the refund comes out of your organization's books, the credit note we sent is marked cancelled (its number is kept and never used again), and we email the business that the refund did not reach its card and that the credit note is cancelled. Your organization decides what to do next: refund again, or send the money another way and record it. We tell your organization in its notifications. • How long it takes. A refund goes back to the card the business paid with. It can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the card company; we cannot say exactly when. • No refund while the card is disputed. We will not make a refund of a card payment while the business's card company is disputing it (§7). • A refund made in your own Stripe account is not recorded by FieldLogicHQ. We remind your organization to record it in its own books; we do not record it for you. • A payment the deal cannot take. If a card payment arrives for a deal that has been withdrawn, or that was invoiced another way in the meantime, we do not record it, and the money stays in your organization's Stripe account. Your organization refunds it in Stripe. We remind your organization that it is there.

§7 Disputes

New

A sponsor's dispute is now recorded and, if lost, taken out of the books.

• A dispute of a sponsor's card payment is answered in Stripe, and recorded in FieldLogicHQ. For a sponsorship payment, FieldLogicHQ records the dispute and tells whoever at your organization opens Sponsorships when it opens and when it is decided. Your organization answers it in Stripe, under its agreement with Stripe; we cannot answer it for you. If your organization loses it, the amount Stripe takes back comes out of your organization's books in the same way as the payment went in, the deal returns to owing, and no credit note is issued, because your organization did not refund anyone and the business still owes the invoice. If your organization wins it after that, the payment is put back. We do not decide for your organization whether to ask the business to pay again or to withdraw the deal, and we do not adjust the GST/HST on an invoice that has been issued; your organization (with its accountant) decides how to treat the tax on an amount it does not recover. Neither part of the processing fee is returned (§4); Stripe's dispute fees come out of your organization's Stripe balance (§5). A dispute that covers only part of a payment, or a payment that was never recorded here, is not booked by us: we tell your organization so that it can record it.
