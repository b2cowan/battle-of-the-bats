Families’ answers walk · Stage 4b

Parents on a rep team say In, Out or Maybe for each game and practice; the coach sees the answers beside the attendance book, can remind families who have not answered, and gets a bell for the ones that matter. Every step says what to open and do (Do) and what you should see (See). Allow about three hours. Part 0 builds the test parents; the later parts use them in order. Nothing here changes the coach’s own attendance marks unless a step tells you to press Use.
