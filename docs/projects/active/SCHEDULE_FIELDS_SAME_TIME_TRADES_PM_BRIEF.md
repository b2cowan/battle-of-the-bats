# Club schedules: part of a permit, teams at the same time, coach trades — PM brief

**Plan:** [SCHEDULE_FIELDS_SAME_TIME_TRADES_PLAN.md](SCHEDULE_FIELDS_SAME_TIME_TRADES_PLAN.md) · owner rulings 2026-11-25,
from the Milton Bats demo. Status is on the board.

## What changes

**For the person who builds the club's schedule**

- **Pick the fields.** Ticking a permit in New schedule brings in all its fields today, and they cannot be unticked. They
  become ticks, so a schedule on two of a dome's six fields is the obvious thing to do.
- **Part of a field is a real place.** In the venue list a field can be split into two sides or four quarters (owner,
  2026-11-25). One team goes on each part as its own slot, or one part goes to this schedule and the rest to something
  else. The product knows the parts are the one field, so nothing can be booked over them by accident. A part counts
  toward a team's share and costs in proportion (a side is half a practice and half the price), so two teams on one
  field bill the same whether the club calls it sharing or sides. It reaches the club's rep schedules and house league;
  coaches' own events, tryout days and tournaments keep booking whole fields.
- **Teams at the same time.** A new rule names two or more teams and the builder tries to put them at the same time in
  the same building, each on its own field. It can be entered as often as needed. Its opposite, "never at the same
  time", comes with it for a coach who runs two teams.
- **A report per rule.** When the builder could not keep a group together, it says which weeks and why (one team had a
  game, not enough free fields at that time, one team had used its share).

**For coaches**

- **Trade time with another team.** A coach sees the other teams' times on the same schedule, proposes a swap, a give or
  a take, and the other coach agrees. The practices move on both teams' calendars.
- **The bill follows the time.** A like-for-like swap changes nobody's bill. When time moves one way, the team that
  receives it is charged and the team that gave it is credited.

**For the club**

- One notice per trade instead of two requests to action. The club's total is unchanged.
- The one case that still reaches the club's money person: the giving team has already paid in full, so the club would
  owe money back.
- A switch on each schedule turns coach trades on or off.

## Why it matters

A club that shares a dome spends its coordinator's time on exactly these three things: carving up fields, keeping
families' evenings sane, and passing time between teams after the schedule is out. Today the first needs a side door,
the second is done by dragging teams by hand after every run, and the third is two hand-backs and a manual re-give for
every swap. Milton Bats asked for all three in one demo.

## Who sees what

- **Schedule builders:** the field ticks, halves, the new rule and its report.
- **Coaches:** other teams' names, times and fields on the same schedule, and nothing else about them. Anyone who can
  hand a practice back today can agree a like-for-like swap; a trade that changes what a team owes needs someone with
  that team's money access.
- **The club's money person:** the paid-in-full case only.
- **Families:** no change is ruled. Whether a trade tells families is an ask for the coach-trade drawing.

## What does not change

- A team's share stays the limit. The builder never hands a team extra time to match its partner, and it says so.
- The first bill never changes. A trade is priced the same way the club's own changes are.
- A coach cannot take an open slot nobody holds; the club still gives those out.
- A permit that is only for half a field keeps working as it does today ("Half cost").

## Priority

Medium to high within the club scheduler: three asks from a live prospect, in the area the product already leads with.
The owner sets where it sits on the board.

## Success looks like

- A schedule on two of six fields is built without the finder, and the bill covers only those field-hours.
- Two teams hold the two halves of one field in the same hour, and a whole-field booking over them is refused.
- A linked pair is together every week both can be, and every week they are not has a stated reason.
- A like-for-like swap between two coaches moves both calendars, sends the club one notice and changes no bill.
- After a give or a take, the club's next "Send the changes" bills only the club's own changes.
