A pulled date reaches everyone who must act, and the path from permit to Send is walkable

Written 2026-11-09, after Stage 13c Session A (committed 27e958f1a), Session B (committed 13e5f6085) and Session C (committed 40e86b8dd). One purpose: when two Saturdays come off a permit, the people who must act find out, each sees only the doors they can open, and the schedule built from that permit leads from the permit to the fix to the bill.

What this walk is for

The club’s December permit for Kinsmen Park lost Sat, Dec 5 and Sat, Dec 12 after a schedule had already handed that time out. Eight bookings now sit outside the permit: 6 rep practices (14U AA, 15U AA and 13U AAA, both Saturdays) and 2 house-league games on Dec 5 (Hawks vs Owls, Reds vs Blues). A third game, Reds vs Blues at 10:00 a.m., is postponed, so it holds no time and never counts. Nothing in this stage moves, blocks or emails anyone. It tells people.

It passes when

  • 1–3 The owner, at a desk, on the permit: Permits is a place with its count; the old address still lands there; the permit lists the 8 by day with a door to each fix; the form counts as a date is picked.
  • 4–5 The Overview’s “Outside the permit” card, and the Calendar’s marked days and empty weeks.
  • 6–8 The schedule’s page: the top line opens the permit, Needs attention names the pulled dates, the Bill tile opens Fair shares and the bill (Open for an admin without the club’s money), and the View menu no longer holds Fair shares.
  • 9 The league admin (Schedule health, their doors, their sentence), then 10 the registrar and the treasurer.
  • 11 The phone. 12 “Take Dec 5 and Dec 12 off” into Regenerate. 13 New schedule’s Slots column.

Known before the walk (not defects of this walk)

  • No “was $200.00” on the bill. The drawing showed the bill rising with a “was” figure. The product keeps no earlier bill to quote: a sent bill never moves when a date comes off, and a draft’s bill is worked out fresh. So neither the Bill tile nor the Needs attention row says “was”.
  • The schedule’s Fair shares read “Short” for its teams. The test schedule gives each team one Saturday hour, far below an even share, so that figure is the test data, not 13c.
  • The house-league rows say “House league · U11 Pinto” without the drawing’s “· draft” (nothing in the product marks a game a draft).
  • The registrar gets “Open 2026 Fall House League’s schedule” on the permit (the drawing showed it for the league admin only), because the registrar can open that page. The registrar gets no Schedule health lines: those are for whoever runs the season.
  • The league admin’s sentence without “in Schedules” shows when there is no house-league time to give. In this club the schedule already gave house league time, so part 9 reaches the other sentence (“no time left to give”) through U7 Tee-ball; the “hasn’t given any time yet” one is the same change.
  • Pressing Send is out of bounds. Part 12 stops at the unsent change: Send the changes emails the coaches.

Calls to make as you go

  • The View menu with three views (8). Fair shares left the menu, so a draft (and any schedule over more than one weekday) now has three views: Grid, By team, By group. The standing rule says a switch for up to three and the pill for four or more. This sent schedule has four (Overview too), so you see the pill. Keep the pill everywhere, or switch to the three-way switch where there are three? (Recommended: follow the rule.)
  • Slots that add up (13). Each team’s figure is what it could get on its own at one a day; the total is what all teams can use together, and it always matches the hint beside the setting. Here they agree (7 × 25 = 175). When teams want the same slots, the team figures can add up to more than the total. Is that acceptable?
  • “See them ›” in the schedule’s row (6) opens the house-league season’s schedule, where Schedule health lists the games. Is that the right place to land?
