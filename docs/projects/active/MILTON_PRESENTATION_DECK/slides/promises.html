What we do and do not do

Six plain statements

1 · Where

Your club’s information is stored in the United States (Ohio), with our database host, Supabase, on Amazon Web Services.

2 · Never sold

We never sell it or use it to advertise, and it is never given to an AI service.

3 · Who sees what

Your people see only what their role allows, checked on our server for every request. Two-step sign-in is available to everyone, and your club can require it for its board and coaches; FieldLogicHQ staff always use it.

4 · Encryption

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest, and player documents open only through links that expire after one hour.

5 · Backups

Our database is backed up daily, each backup kept 7 days, and we have rehearsed a restore.

6 · Staff access

Our support tools log each record our staff open and every change they make. In November we reviewed who can see what in every club; its fixes are live.

The written security brief is yours to keep

Two and a half minutes. Read them as written; each has been checked against what the product does today. Hand over the security brief here. Wording to keep to, and the answers to the hard questions: the Security tab of the prep page.
