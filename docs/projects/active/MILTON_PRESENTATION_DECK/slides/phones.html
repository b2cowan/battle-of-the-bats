FieldLogicHQ · Milton Bats

One minute, straight after the demos. Both codes open the Milton Bats demo club on our test site, the same club the demos ran on, so every name, family and score is made up. The left code opens the club’s home page: teams, tryouts and the house league sign-up they just watched on the big screen. The right code opens Battle of the Bats, a finished tournament: scores, standings, the playoff bracket and the champions. Nobody signs in. Give them a moment to look, then carry on; the pages stay open on their phones. The printed Battle of the Bats card is still the leave-behind.
