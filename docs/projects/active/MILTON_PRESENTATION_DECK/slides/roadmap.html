Ninety seconds; a skim, not a read. This is the public roadmap, shortened to fit the slide. Live means on production today; Under way means built or being built, not yet released; no mark means not started. No dates inside a quarter. Three of the Q1 items are already live, ahead of their quarter: the club’s own registration questions, schedules across several permits, and the club tools open to every club, on sale since November 23. Sponsorships sits in Q2 and its first part is already live: the club’s packages and its sponsor list. Offering a deal and the invoice are built and not yet released. Families paying online and the family app are roadmap items, not features today.
