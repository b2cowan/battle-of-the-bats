Two minutes. November 10 is the date of our last session with the club, with its head of house league; the president was not in that meeting, so say “since November 10”, not “since you asked”. Everything here has gone live in the two weeks since. Friend requests, two-step sign-in and the balance check went live in the November 15 release. The last of the access review’s fixes, group email to coaches and the board, the club’s own registration questions, our privacy policy and terms, and the public Security page went live November 22; the data terms and the list of our suppliers followed on November 23. Sponsorships: the club’s packages and its sponsor list went live November 23. Offering a deal to a business and the invoice are built and not yet released; no date. The third row will draw “what did the review find?”: “In November we reviewed, route by route, who can see and change what in every club. It found nothing that lets one club see another club’s people, and the serious and medium findings are fixed in the live product.” Yes, one serious finding was a way for a signed-in account to change another club’s rules, and it is fixed; do not say “every finding fixed”.
