FieldLogicHQ · Milton Bats

Ninety seconds. Since the board last met, the left column’s first three went live: our privacy policy and terms on November 22, the data terms for a club and the list of our suppliers on November 23, and closing a club now deletes its stored files too. The left column answers “can we get our information out?”: today the lists that offer it download as Excel, CSV or PDF, such as members, registrations and payments, and on the owner’s written request we give a club a full copy within 30 days, which is in our published data terms. A club’s owner can already send a delete request from Settings; one download of everything, and closing the account yourself, come in Q1 2027. On the right, the automatic tests include a check that each club’s data stays apart. The uptime monitor and public status page are Q1 2027, not started: if asked, say “soon” and do not give a 2026 date. If anyone asks about “a full review of who can see what, with its fixes”, answer before they push: “In November we reviewed, route by route, who can see and change what in every club. It found nothing that lets one club see another club’s people, and the serious and medium findings are fixed in the live product.” If asked “did it find anything serious?”, say yes: one serious finding was a way for a signed-in account to change another club’s rules, and it is fixed. Do not say “every finding fixed”. Our goals for the second half of 2027: every commitment on this page kept and reported on, our access review repeated every year, and a published uptime record.
