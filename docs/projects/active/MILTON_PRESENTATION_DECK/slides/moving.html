Two minutes, then the remaining time is his questions; this is the last slide. There is no set order and no deadline. A club can start with one piece and keep its other tools: house league by itself, the coaches on the Coaches Portal, the club’s books, a single tournament, the dome schedule from a permit, or the public page. Each one works alone and shares the same record as the next one the club uses, so a child registered once is the same child in the draft and on a roster. People join by emailed invitation and see only what their role allows; help guides are built in. Coaches upload rosters, schedules and budgets from spreadsheets, tournament teams and games import too, and a team already on the Coaches Portal comes over with all its history. A one-step import of a club’s older records is on the roadmap for Q1 2027. The club tools have been on sale since November 23, so any of these can start whenever the club is ready. Prices and terms are answered in the room if he asks, never on a slide. Leave the security brief with him.
