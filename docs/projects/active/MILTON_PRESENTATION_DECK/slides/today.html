Two minutes. This is the picture most clubs describe, not a claim about theirs: several subscriptions, the books in a spreadsheet, email chains to coaches and families, an older website, and nothing joining them. Don't knock any of the tools: each one works. The cost is the gaps between them: the same names typed again, information lost when a volunteer moves on, payments missed and found late. Ask which of these sounds familiar. Then: here is the same club in one place, and the demos show how each of the three is solved.
