FieldLogicHQ · Milton Bats

One minute. Replacing the club’s website is one of the reasons we are talking, so say both halves. The left column is live today: a club site in the club’s colours with a page for every rep team, tryouts, house league registration with its schedules and standings, tournament scores on a phone, sponsors and a notice. It is built from the same records the board and the coaches already keep, so nobody updates it by hand. The right column is planned work that has not started. Q2 2027 is a target: on a day the club has games, the home page leads with tonight’s games and the weekend’s results across rep, house league and tournaments, and a closed field shows on every game it affects. The second half of 2027 is a goal: club news the site posts from what the club records plus the club’s own posts, photos, a trophy case of championships, sponsors shown on game day with a report the club can bring to a renewal, and a scoreboard for a screen at the park. Photos of players will follow each family’s consent, and no player is named on a public page.
