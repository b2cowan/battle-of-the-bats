One question, not two

Adding a budget line used to ask what kind of line it is and then what it is. The two answers could disagree, the form allowed it, and the disagreement quietly produced a row the coach could never report against. There is now one question — is this money going out, or coming in — and the item you pick decides the rest. The promise underneath all of it: no figure moves anywhere.

Verdict
B2

Read the Grant row and say what it tells you

say it in your own words

Grant now sits under Sponsorship, tagged From a sponsor — and that settles a disagreement the product has had with itself since both halves shipped. The sponsorship option's own help text has always read "a business sponsor, a grant, anything given directly rather than raised by selling", while the library filed grants with the bottle drives. Before today a coach budgeting a grant had to choose which way to be wrong.

You ruled the move on 2026-09-07, and it turned out to matter more than a tidy shelf. Sponsor money is placed against the lines that claim it, at the category they agree on. With Grant on the Fundraising shelf, a team that budgeted only a grant saw every sponsor cheque report under Fundraising — and a team that budgeted a grant and a team sponsorship had claims in two categories, so the whole sponsor pool landed with no category at all. Budgeting sponsorship properly gave that coach a worse report than budgeting it carelessly. Both cases now place correctly.
Verdict
B3

The spending side has no tags at all

Every cost's actual is recorded by the coach, so a tag on any of them would say nothing — and teach you to stop reading the end of the row, which is exactly where the ownership chip's signal is.

Verdict
B4

“What am I forgetting?” opens on the word’s own side

a defect the review found

Under the plan list, What am I forgetting? offers standard words this team has not budgeted — and it legitimately includes money-IN ones, because forgetting to budget your sponsorship is a real thing to forget. Every one of those chips used to open the form on the spending side, with a money-in word already chosen underneath: the screen contradicting itself, which is the exact thing this change exists to make impossible.

It was worse before this project, which is why nobody caught it. The server then trusted whatever the form said, so tapping + Grant and saving stored the line as a cost — money coming in, filed as spending, adding its amount to what families are asked for instead of subtracting it. The server now works it out from the word whatever the form says, so what was left was a form that lied and a line landing in a section you did not choose. Both are closed.

H1

Grant moved — confirm it reads right

you ruled this; check it landed

This was an open question when the walk was written and you closed it on 2026-09-07: Grant moved to the Sponsorship shelf. It is here so you can confirm the move reads right rather than only sounding right.

⚠ One thing the ruling did not know, found by checking before moving it. Moving a shared word is a re-filing, not a rename: every record filed against it stores the category alongside the word, and the report reads those two in different orders — so moving the word alone would make one row report under two headings. One record was affected, on dev and on production: the coach demo's $250 "Association development grant" club request. Its filing moved with the word, and the demo was reseeded to prove a fresh build files it the same way rather than inventing a second "Grant" under the old shelf.
H2

your call

The tag appears in the picker, where the choice is made. It does not appear in the word list a coach manages, and it is not editable anywhere — every word a coach or club invents is "you record it", full stop. Is showing it there worth it, or is it a fact that only matters at the moment of choosing?
