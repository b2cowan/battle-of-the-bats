# Start your club in two steps — PM brief

**What it is.** The window where a Premium coach starts their club now has two steps instead of one long scroll: first the club
and its address, then a review of exactly what will be charged and what they are agreeing to.

**Why it matters.** Before, the **Start my club** button was on screen from the first moment, while the charge and the terms
sat below a scroll. A buyer could press it without ever seeing the amount. Now the amount, the renewal line and the terms are
the only thing on the screen when the button appears, on a laptop and on a phone.

**What the buyer sees**
- Step 1 of 2 · Your club: the same fields as before. Buttons: Cancel, Next. Next appears once the club has a name and a valid
  address; until then the foot says what is missing. An address we cannot sell to yet (the US, Québec) stays on this step with
  the reason in the foot.
- Step 2 of 2 · Review the charge: a short read-back of the club, billing address and web address (with Change), then the
  charge, the tax by name, Due today, the renewal line and the terms. Buttons: Back, Start my club (or Continue to checkout when
  there is free time or a trial). Monthly / Yearly sits here, over the figures it changes.
- Back keeps everything typed. Changing the province still works out the charge again.

**Who is affected.** Head coaches on the Premium Coaches Portal starting a club, from the billing page. Nobody else. Plain
plan moves between rep-team sizes are unchanged.

**Priority.** Medium: a clarity fix on the one moment a customer is asked to pay.

**Success.** A buyer cannot reach the paid button without having seen Due today and the terms; no extra support questions about
the charge; no change in how many people finish.

**How to try it.** Open Plan & billing as a Premium coach, choose Start my club. Check: empty name (no Next, the foot says why);
Next; Back and edit the province (a new charge loads); the same on a phone.
