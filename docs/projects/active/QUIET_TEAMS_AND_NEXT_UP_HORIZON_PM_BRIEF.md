# PM brief — quiet teams and far-off tournaments

**What changes for a coach.**
- The Sunday "Your week in review" only arrives when the team actually had a game or practice that week, and never says the same thing twice in a row. A team that has stopped playing is left alone.
- The Overview's "Next up: Tournament coming up" appears only when the tournament is within 30 days (or on now). Otherwise the card says "Nothing on your schedule", and a team that has gone quiet is offered the end-of-season step instead of being told a July tournament is coming up.

**Why it matters.** Notifications that repeat or describe nothing teach coaches to ignore all of them, including the ones that matter. A "Next up" for something eight months away is noise on the screen coaches open first.

**Customer impact.** Fewer, truer notifications; a calmer Overview. No new controls. Teams that are playing see no difference.

**Priority.** Small, high-trust fix; ships with the next promote.

**Success.** A team with no events in the past week gets no digest; an active team still does; two Sundays with the same findings send one. A tournament 31+ days away does not appear as "Next up".

**Role differences.** None; head and assistant coaches get the same rules (each assistant still hears only what their access allows).
