The bank check’s fee and interest doors

One purpose: when the bank is off by a small amount nothing explains, the check now offers a bank fee door (or an interest door) beside “Record the missing amount”, the long list waits behind one line, and the window opens filled with a reminder to check the banking app. You look at it in both portals on a computer and a phone, save a fee in each, see the repeat line, and answer nine calls. About 30 minutes.

Written Nov 9, 2026 against the dev site. Parts 1, 2, 3 and 6 only look. Parts 4 and 5 save, on a team and a club account made for this walk (“S3 owner walk team”, “S3 owner walk chequing”); I remove both after you report.

Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • The bank screen itself writes no line: the books were counted before and after opening and cancelling every window, and only the window’s own Save added one.
  • A window opened before the word list has loaded still gets its word when the list arrives (tested with a 9-second delay, both portals, desk and phone), and never over a word you picked meanwhile.
  • A team that cannot see the “Bank & payment fees” word (it cannot happen in the data today; tested by hiding it) gets the window with the description and no word.
  • A coach who can only read money sees no check form, so no doors at all.
  • The Bank views pass the layout check at 361, 390, 768 and 1440 px.
