7 · What every surface shows from January 1, 2027

All of the new elements key off the signup window, not the free-period end, so the day the window closes they vanish and the pages read as the list price with the free floors. Nothing new is drawn for this state because it is today's non-promo copy, already written behind the promo toggle.

Get-started chooser"Free" pills and today's bodies return; the coach door opens the Basic team again
Pricing FAQ"Can I still get the Founding Season offer?" — closed December 31, 2026; list prices; no second offer

Three choices on this sheet

  1. Offer bar: Variant A (dark, lime type — recommended) or Variant B (lime fill).
  2. Homepage panel product lines: plain text as drawn (the persona cards stay the only doors — recommended), or make each product line a link to its persona page.
  3. Pricing card amount: "$0 through September 30, 2027" (option 1 — recommended) or "Free through September 30, 2027" (option 2).

Everything else on the sheet is one proposal. Approving the sheet approves the words as drawn; the marketing pass can still polish a sentence without changing what a surface says.
