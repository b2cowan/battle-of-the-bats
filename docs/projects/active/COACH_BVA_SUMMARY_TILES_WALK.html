W1 · Read the season’s money at a glance

What this walk is for

A coach opens Budget vs. Actual and, in one row, sees what came in, what went out, what nobody planned, and what the team holds today. Each figure has to agree with the place it comes from, or the row is decoration.

It passes when

The four tiles are there, each one equals its source, and nothing else on the page changed size or moved.

Not in this walk

The notes under the Months view, the PDF and the help (all in W2). The Money Overview’s own Headroom, which stays by ruling.

  1. Open Money → Budget vs. Actual. Set View: Statement and Compare: Whole season.

    One row of four: COLLECTED · SPENT · OFF-PLAN · CASH ON HAND. No Headroom and no Season end anywhere on the page.

  2. Find the Total revenue row in the table.

    Collected’s big figure equals that row’s Actual, and its “of $X planned” equals the row’s Budgeted.

    Illustration: $8,979.09 of $15,450.00 planned.

  3. Scroll to the Total expenses row.

    Spent’s figure equals that row’s Actual, and its “of $X planned” equals the row’s Budgeted.

    Illustration: $5,993.00 of $8,500.00 planned.

  4. Off-plan is amber and says “nobody budgeted this”. Collected, Spent and Cash on hand are plain dark ink. Nothing in the row is green, and Cash on hand reads “as of today”.

  5. Open Money → Overview, then come back to Budget vs. Actual.

    The Overview’s Cash on hand card shows the same figure as the band’s Cash on hand. The Overview prints it in green and the band in plain ink, as ruled.

    Illustration: $3,687.69.

  6. Switch Compare to To date, look, then switch back to Whole season.

    The table’s plan column changes. The four tiles do not move: they always read the whole season.

  7. Narrow the browser to about 390 px wide, or open the page on your phone.

    Two tiles per row: Collected beside Spent, then Off-plan beside Cash on hand. Every caption fits on one line, and the table starts right under the band.

  8. Switch the portal to Dark, look at the band, then switch back.

    All four tiles read clearly. Off-plan is still amber.

W2 · The report explains itself without Headroom

What this walk is for

Three sentences on this report used to point at Headroom or at the Season end figure. Each one now has to point at something still on the screen, and a printed copy has to read like the screen it came from.

It passes when

Every note names Spent where it named Headroom and the numbers agree, the forward-figure sentence is gone, the PDF opens on the four tiles, and the help describes them.

Not in this walk

The band itself (W1). The Overview’s Budget card, which keeps Headroom, gets only one look, in step 6.

  1. On Budget vs. Actual, set View: Months and Showing: Cash. Look under the grid.

    A line reading “Spent counts $X — why the difference?”, where $X is the Spent tile’s figure. Open it: it walks down to “What this season spent”, the same figure again.

    Illustration: $5,993.00.

  2. Switch Showing to Difference.

    The note says “…on expenses its Total is your plan less the Spent figure above, to the cent.” The Total expenses row’s Total equals Spent’s “of” figure minus Spent’s figure, to the dollar (the grid shows whole dollars).

    Illustration: 2,507, which is $8,500.00 less $5,993.00.

  3. Switch Showing to Scheduled.

    The note “Scheduled is what’s still to come…” is there. No sentence mentions the banner’s forward figure, and nothing says what the season “ends” with.

  4. Set View: Statement, then Export → PDF, and open the file.

    Under “This season”: Team, Season, Collected, Spent, Off-plan and Cash on hand, with the same figures as the screen’s band. No Headroom row.

  5. Open Help in the sidebar and search where did headroom go.

    One result: Managing your team’s money (Premium). Open it, then its page Budget vs. Actual: the two report shapes. It lists Collected, Spent, Off-plan and Cash on hand as definitions, and says the Overview still shows Headroom.

  6. Open Money → Overview.

    The Budget card still shows headroom. That is unchanged on purpose: the Overview is a separate decision (call 3).
