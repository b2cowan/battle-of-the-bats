# PM Brief — The Payouts tab and the bank check learn sponsorship card payments

**What it does:** A sponsor's card payment shows on the club's Payouts tab as "{business} · Sponsorship · card" instead of "House league", and the bank check counts the sponsorship money (and any refund of it) as arrived or gone when the bank deposit says so.
**Why it matters:** Without it, a club that takes sponsorships by card would be told its bank is "off" by about the sponsorship amount for a month after every payment, and its Payouts tab would mislabel the money. The treasurer trusts the bank check to say "Matches".
**Who benefits:** Club treasurers and admins on the Club tier. No screen, button or setting changes.
**Expected impact:** The same two screens read correctly for sponsor money. Nothing else on them moves.
**Priority:** High: it must land before online payments are switched on for a real club.
**Success criteria:** Walking a sponsorship payment, a part refund and a full refund shows each on the right payout line, and the bank check equals the bank to the cent before and after the payout arrives.
