When does a run of changes collapse into one line?

Only when the dates really are the same change. A run collapses when the time of day, the place and (for a move) the shift are identical on every date. Anything else stays as its own line under the team. The last case is the honest worst case.

Asks

Seven calls. Each has my recommendation first.

1 · The collapse rule

Recommend: collapse only identical runs. A run becomes one line when time, place and shift match on every date. Otherwise the lines stay separate.

  • Alternative: always collapse by team and print “varies”. Shorter, but hides exactly the changes a coach most needs to read.

2 · Opening state

Recommend: every team closed. The one-line summary per team is already enough to decide.

  • Alternative: the first team open. Shows one example of what Show dates does, at the cost of 2–4 extra lines.

3 · Order and cut-off

Recommend: soonest affected date first, three teams shown. It matches the hand-back asks on the same card.

  • Alternative: most changes first.

4 · Phone button

Recommend: B, button below the body. See tab 5.

5 · Words for the kinds of change

I used gains, moves, loses and handed back (today’s lines say “No longer” for loses), and gained / moved / taken off / handed back in the one-line total. House-league divisions would say “games”, not “practices”. These are marketing-owned words, so they go to /marketing before the build.

6 · One summary in both places

Recommend: yes, the page row and the Send window show the same team summary. It also settles the place-name drift (“Prospect Park · Diamond 1” on the page, “Diamond 1” in the window) in favour of naming the park, since two parks can each have a Diamond 1.

  • Alternative: summarize only the page row and leave the window as the full record. The window is the confirm step, though, and a paragraph nobody can read confirms nothing.

7 · The email the coaches receive (not drawn)

This is a third copy of the same list. The email a team’s coaches get today is one bullet per date, each ending “Time added Nov 17”. Recommend: the same grouping, with every date written out. An email has no Show dates, and a coach may copy the dates into a calendar. The shape would be one line per kind (“Gains 17 practices: Tue & Thu, 7:00 p.m., Prospect Park, Diamond 1, from Time added Nov 17”) followed by the dates by month. Email words go to /marketing.
