Coaches Portal · the Schedule · a deep dive

The schedule, end to end, on a phone and at a desk

The Schedule is the screen a coach opens most: to see what's next, to take attendance at the fence, to open the lineup, to record the score. This walk reads it as built on 25 September 2026 at four sizes. For each station you get what's on the screen and its numbers, how it feels to use, and where I'd change its shape. Stage 1 is drawn at true size on its own tab. You read both against the product and tell me what to change or add.

How to read this

  • Nine stations, in the order a coach meets them. Each has the screen as built (captures and measurements), my read (what works, what gets in the way, what I'd change) and shape questions with Keep · Reshape · Not yet and a notes box.
  • Then Standing back (six rules that cut across the stations) and the stages. Each stage is drawn, you rule on it, then it's built and walked, one at a time.
  • Every red flag and green chip on a drawing opens its explanation when clicked, and so does every NEW / RESTYLED / UNCHANGED tag.

Where I walked

  • A read-only browser probe at 390×844 and 360×780 (phones, touch), 768×1024 (tablet) and 1440×900 (desk). It opened sheets and forms and closed them; it never saved an answer.
  • Every number comes from the browser's own geometry, not from a screenshot.
  • The phone Schedule was re-shaped in stage 2 of Coaching from a phone (C1–C4, walked 35/35 in §216). I build on it here and don't reopen it.

The list at open: a September desktop still starts in April

A coach opens the Schedule to see what's next.

Schedule · List · phone (walked §216) and desk
On the screen, as built

On a phone the list opens with today's row directly under its month, 142px down. The list itself scrolls, up into the past and down into the future. That's stage 2's C1, walked and passed.

On a desk it opens at the top of the season: APRIL 2026 · Apr 2 · @ Lakeside 2–7 LOSS and on down. On the fixture's short season today's row is the 15th, at 852px, and just clears the fold. On a real season (the demo's 12U) the next event sits at 1,916px, under 38 finished events. The tablet also opens on 2 Apr, with today at 923px.

phone: today at 142px · 6 rows on screendesk: first row Apr 2 · today at 852pxreal season: next event at 1,916px · 38 rows above768: today at 923px

How it feels to use: my read

What works

  • The phone is right and walked. The rows are the portal's one row recipe at both widths, and the month bands keep you oriented.

What gets in the way

  • The desk is the last list in the portal that opens on the past. Practice plans, Lineups and the phone all open on what's next. On a real season, a coach at a desk scrolls past two screens of results every time.

What I'd change

  • Open the desk list on today, with today's row under its month at the top and the season so far above it. Same rows, same data, the phone's rule. C1 left the desk as "re-ask after the walk", and the walk has passed.
Q 1.1 · S2

Should the desktop list open on today, the way the phone does?

This is the question C1 left open. The measurement answers it: on a real season, April costs two screens of scrolling.

Recommendation: Reshape, in stage 2.
What rides on it: nothing off the page. The deep links open a sheet, not the list, the export is still the whole season, and the demo tour's schedule stop anchors on a row, which survives.

Week and Month at a desk: Month can't say what or when

A coach checks the shape of the week or the month.

Schedule · Week · Month · 1440 (the phone was redrawn in C2)
On the screen, as built

Week draws seven columns: an empty day is a card with "—", and a day with events shows each one's time, name and a coloured rail. At 1440 that reads fine. Month draws 165px cells whose chips cut the name (UAT probe prac, Practice revie) and carry no time. The 641–768 band's Month chips (16px) are an owner call still owed from §216 C4.

How it feels to use: my read

What works

  • Week is a good desk view, one row and seven columns, and I'd leave it alone.

What gets in the way

  • Month reads as colour, not content. You can see that Monday has a practice, but not which one or when.

What I'd change

  • Put the time and the name on two lines in each chip, with "+N more" when a day overflows. Or give the desk the phone's answer: dots on the grid and the tapped day's rows beneath. I'd draw both in stage 2.
Q 2.1 · S2

Should the desk's Month say what and when, not just that something's on?

Recommendation: Reshape, in stage 2, with both forms drawn and the 641–768 band ruled at the same time.

The event sheet, by kind: the job sinks as the day goes on

A coach opens an event: before it, to see who's coming; during it, for the lineup; after it, to record what happened.

Schedule → an event · the phone orders the sheet by the clock (C3), the desk has one order for everything
On the screen, as built

The sheet has the right parts. Where attendance lands depends on the kind of event and the time of day. On a phone, before first pitch the tabs and attendance follow the place row. From first pitch the score and the awards box lead, and attendance drops. A practice puts its plan first and has no tabs at all: attendance is one inline list, while a game hides it behind Attendance · Lineup · Scouting. On the desk, every kind uses one order, with the tabs and attendance after the action row.

Kinds not on the fixture, read from the code: a tournament (the multi-day event) carries "+ Add game" and attendance. A team event has attendance only. A tryout session is projected read-only and opens Tryouts. An organizer's game (gold) shows "From the organizer" and "Only … can cancel or remove this game" where Cancel and Delete would be. All of them use the same blocks; none reads wrong on its own terms. The problem is the one shared order, not any single kind.

How it feels to use: my read

What works

  • C3 put the job first before a game, with the first player moving from 626px to 414px, and every control on the phone sheet is at least 44px. The foot row (Edit details · Cancel event · Delete) is right.

What gets in the way

  • The job sinks as the day goes on. From first pitch the score and awards push attendance to 524px, and on a scored game to 696px, with 3 players showing.
  • A practice and a game are two shapes. One is an inline list, the other three tabs. That's the difference you noticed.
  • "Attendance" appears twice: the tab, then a heading with the same word directly under it.
  • The desk still says "Enter a final score to unlock awards" on a game two days away. The phone stopped doing that in C3.

What I'd change

  • One shape for every event: the event's summary on top (what, when, where, and the kind's own block), then its jobs as rows that say where they stand, then the foot row. On a phone the sheet becomes one screen. Drawn on the 1 · The event sheet tab.
Q 3.1 · S1

Should every event's sheet be one shape: the summary on top, its jobs as rows that name their state?

Your question was "can attendance be a button that opens a drawer?". The measurement widens it: a practice and a game disagree because they're two shapes. Moving attendance on the practice alone would add a third.

Recommendation: Reshape, in stage 1 (drawn).
4

Attendance: who isn't coming is the last row

The most frequent tap on the page: who's coming, who's out, who hasn't answered.

The sheet's Attendance · the RSVP sheet · the Overview's and Insights' links in
On the screen, as built

From the list it takes three taps to a saved answer: the event, the player, the answer (the sheet opens on Attendance). The RSVP sheet is 391px, with four 52px choices and a note, and saving closes it. The Overview carries four links straight into today's attendance ("10 of 12 in", "1 late", "1 out", and the tile), and Insights → Attendance has "Take attendance" for the next event. On today's game, the one player who's Out is the twelfth row, about 1,060px down, under the score and the awards box.

When attendance and the lineup disagree, a warning sits over the tabs: "⚠ Marked in but not in the lineup: … Fix the attendance below, or edit the lineup →". That link is 97×15px, the only control under the tap floor on the phone sheet.

list → saved answer: 3 tapsOverview links in: 4 + Insights 1RSVP sheet 391px · choices 4 × 52pxLogan (Out) at ~1,060pxwarning link 97×15px

How it feels to use: my read

What works

  • The row is the tap, and the RSVP sheet is excellent: big answers, the current one ticked, Escape and Back close it. The Overview's links already land on the right game.

What gets in the way

  • The question a coach asks first, "who isn't coming?", is answered at the bottom of the list. Ten "In" rows come before the one that matters.
  • The warning's door is 15px tall, and "below" only means something while attendance sits under it.

What I'd change

  • Attendance becomes a row that names names: "10 in · 1 late · 1 out", then "Out: Logan · Late: Kai" on the line beneath. Tapping it opens a full-screen room with the whole roster on one screen, All in, Reset, the five filters and the RSVP sheet on top, unchanged.
  • The links in open the room directly, so the Overview and Insights stay at three taps.
  • The cost, stated: from the list it becomes four taps instead of three. That's worth it only because the row names who's missing and the room shows everyone at once. If you mostly mark attendance from the list at the field, you pay that tap every time.
Q 4.1 · S1

Should attendance move into its own full-screen room, behind a row that names who's out and who hasn't replied?

You asked this, and I'd answer yes, on the conditions you set: the row names people, and the room is full-screen with every control it has today.

Recommendation: Reshape, in stage 1 (drawn: E1, E2, E5).
What rides on it: the Overview's four links, Insights' "Take attendance" and the next-step card all open the room; the lineup warning's wording (E4); the layout sweep's attendance and RSVP screens get new baseline keys.
5

The doors out of an event: the desk's lineup door never turns

From the game to its lineup, the bench console or the scouting book, and back.

The sheet's Lineup and Scouting tabs · a practice's plan · Back (§219 · §222, built)
On the screen, as built

The Lineup tab is a look-only peek (the batting order with an inning flip) plus a door. On a phone the door reads "Edit ›" before first pitch and "Game day ›" after, the same clock that makes the sheet score-first. On the desk it reads "Edit in Lineups →" every time, even on a game in progress. The Scouting tab is the book's panel inside the sheet. A practice has "Open the plan →". Back from the builder or the console now returns to the game on its tab (§222, walk owed).

How it feels to use: my read

What works

  • The phone's door turns by the clock, and Back now comes home to the game.

What gets in the way

  • The desk and the phone disagree about where a started game's lineup lives.
  • Tabs inside a sheet hide two jobs behind one. You can't see the lineup's state from the Attendance tab, or attendance from the Lineup tab.

What I'd change

  • Lineup and Scouting become rows of the same shape, each saying where it stands ("Has a lineup · Ready", "2-1 vs them · 3 notes"), and the tabs go. The Lineup row turns by the clock at both widths. The look-only peek retires: since stage 3's D5, the builder's first screen is the order.
Q 5.1 · S1

On a game, should Lineup and Scouting become rows too, with the tabs gone and the lineup door turning by the clock at both widths?

Recommendation: Reshape, in stage 1 (drawn: E3). Your call on the peek. I lean towards retiring it, and it's a checkbox on the stage tab.
What rides on it: §222's tab=lineup return address now opens the sheet, with the Lineup row on screen one.
6

Adding and editing: Edit details asks, and it should keep asking

Put a game on the calendar, or change one.

Add Event (§212 · §214 · §236, built; walk owed) · Edit details on the sheet
On the screen, as built

Edit details asks: "Save changes" sits at the foot. At 390 the form is 1,286px in an 842px sheet, with 13 fields. The 24 Sep ruling says edit autosaves, create asks, but a saved change to when or where sends families a "schedule changed" notice. The event save decides that, and a series edit asks "this one or all following". An autosaving Edit details would send one notice per field: change the date, then the time, and families get two notices, the first one wrong.

Found on the way: today's game reads 3:07 p.m. – 5:07 p.m. · Arrive 5:30 p.m., an arrival after the start, and nothing flags it. A practice's fold reads "More — field, address, links, notes", but the address moved to Places. And a weekly series asks each date's opponent in a plain box, with no Scouting Book behind it.

How it feels to use: my read

What works

  • The form is flat, asks the date once, and keeps a place and an arrival lead time (the three built passes; your walk will confirm).

What gets in the way

  • An arrival after the start passes silently, because a hand-set time stays put when the start moves.
  • Two small leftovers from the built passes. The practice's label still says "address", and the series' opponent boxes skip the picker.

What I'd change

  • Keep the Save on Edit details, as the one named exception to "edit autosaves", because a save here is a message to families. Fix the three leftovers in stage 5.
Q 6.1 · S5

Should Edit details keep its Save, as the named exception to "edit autosaves"?

The ruling's own test is whether a coach can predict it. "A change families are told about asks first" is a line a coach can predict.

Recommendation: Keep.
Q 6.2 · S5

Should the form's three leftovers be fixed: the practice's label, the series' opponent boxes, and a line when the arrival falls after the start?

Recommendation: Reshape, in stage 5 (small).

The coach's calendar: an 8 p.m. game lands on the wrong day

A coach puts the season into their own phone's calendar.

Schedule · phone: the quiet "Export the season ›" row → Calendar · desk: Export → Excel · CSV · Calendar
On the screen, as built

The export is a one-time snapshot of the whole season: every kind of event, with the arrival time and uniform in each entry's description and the diamond in its location. There's no subscription. The team feed's issuer has had no caller since the family link was removed on 12 Sep, and the family feed route still exists (and gets the time right).

Two defects, verified by running the export's own logic. (1) Any event at 8 p.m. or later in summer (7 p.m. in winter) lands on the next day in the coach's calendar. The export reads the date in universal time and the clock in the phone's local time, so a Monday 10:45 p.m. practice becomes Tuesday 10:45 p.m. That's 8 of the 238 events on dev. (2) A cancelled event exports as confirmed.

How it feels to use: my read

What works

  • On a phone the one export that matters leads: the phone offers only the calendar file, and it carries arrival and uniform.

What gets in the way

  • A calendar that's wrong on evening games is worse than none, because the coach trusts it. And a snapshot goes stale the first time a game moves.

What I'd change

  • Fix the two defects now, ahead of the stages. It's a small fix: build each entry from the event's instant in the org's time zone, the way the family feed already does. Then, in stage 4, add a "Subscribe on this phone" row beside the download, carrying every kind, organizer games included, with cancelled events marked cancelled.
Q 8.1 · S4

Should the two export defects be fixed now, before any stage?

Recommendation: Reshape, now. It's a defect, not a design question, and on your say-so.
Q 8.2 · S4

Should the coach get a calendar they subscribe to, not just a download?

Recommendation: Reshape, in stage 4, drawn as a row, not a feature. It may need a new coach-scoped link token (a migration), decided when drawn.

The page itself: not a screen, a budget

What any build on this surface has to carry.

The Schedule page: 4,211 lines and 60 pieces of state, the largest screen in the portal
As built

The event sheet, the add/edit form, and the list, Week and Month all live in one file, and the sheet is drawn in three orders (desk; phone before first pitch; phone after). Every stage in this project rewrites part of it.

What I'd change

  • Split it as the first step of stage 1, before anything a coach can see changes. The sheet (and the new room) go into their own files, then the form, then the calendar views. The page keeps the data and hands it down. It's a pure move, walked as "nothing changed".
Q 9.1 · S6

Should the page be split first, as a pure move, inside stage 1?

Recommendation: Reshape, as the first commit of stage 1.

Standing back: six rules across the walk

Rule these and most of the station questions fall out.

  • R1One shape for every event. The sheet is the event's summary, and its jobs are doors. Your complaint was a practice/game difference, so the fix is one shape on both, not moving attendance on one of them.
  • R2A door names what's behind it. A bare "Attendance ›" is worse than the inline list it would replace. "Out: Logan · Late: Kai" is better than both.
  • R3Today first, at every width. The phone opens on today, and the desk is the last exception.
  • R4A record is titled by what it records. A tournament's page names the tournament.
  • R5A calendar entry is the event's own time. Every screen reads the club's time zone, and the export must too.
  • R6Edit autosaves, except where a save is a message. A change to when or where tells families, and a series edit asks which games it covers. Both need one deliberate save.
