Families pay by card

Written 2026-10-25. One purpose: a family pays house-league fees by card, and the desk sees it, emails pay links and refunds.

What this walk is for

You walk the family’s side on a phone (sign-up, the secure payment page, the receipt) and the desk’s side on a desktop (the pay link, a payment that couldn’t be recorded, refunds, a corrected price, the books). Then two quick re-checks: the tournament pay page, and League Plus.

It passes when the family never sees "Paid" before the payment is confirmed, every card payment shows the same receipt number everywhere it appears, every refund says in words where the money went, the processing fee is always shown before the family pays, and the books agree with the rows.
