Club Website Enhancements · club events in the end state

Board Ready builds what a person does with a club event (making it, signing up, paying). This project decides where events show on the club's public site. The owner split the work on 2026-11-24 and handed this project nine questions. This page draws the answers into the Stage 0 end state and asks nine things (E1–E9). Drawn on Kestrel Bay, the Stage 0 club, on a June weekday, its champs day and the day after. Every frame is true size and every frame is the end state: events do not exist on the site today, so there is no "before" capture. Click a tag to read it; purple tags jump to their ask. Nothing here is built or ruled. Reviewed by /design before you saw it. Written 2026-11-25.

What is settled and not re-asked here

  • Board Ready's one public rule decides which events are public: shown to Everyone means public, Club staff means never, and an event is gone the day after it ends. Every frame here calls that rule; none restates it.
  • Every sign-up gets a shareable link, ruled yes by the owner on 2026-11-24 in Board Ready's plan. The handover described it as not yet ruled.
  • Board Ready's sign-up form, Your sign-up, paying, the waitlist and the receipt are drawn and ruled there. Here they appear only as the door the event page's "Sign up" button opens.
  • Stage 0's rulings stand as ruled: the home's fixed order (A1), one region in two parts (A2), a tournament as one row (A3), one Close a place window (A4), and the News tab that shows only once there is something in it.

Open now means "you can sign up for this now". News means "this is coming, or this happened". An event takes the row that matches what a family can do with it today, and it moves on when that changes . A champs day's games are house league games, so on its day the club's games part carries it as one row .

Why one page holds both

  • Events and posts answer the same visitor question: what is going on at this club. One page, one tab.
  • The News tab rule widens: it shows once the club has a post or an event for Everyone still to come, and goes when it has neither. A club that only ever has events still has a way to its list.
  • On a phone the tab row is drawn scrolled, as the shipped row does: the current tab is brought into view, and Home is off to the left.
  • While a club tournament is live, the page still opens with its one door to the tournament (A7, ruled), above "Coming up".
  • No event is written up as a post by the site. If the club wants a post about its banquet, it writes one. An automatic "new event" post would put the same thing on the page twice.
  • Considered: renaming the tab "News and events". Not recommended: the label is longer on the one row that is already too wide, and Stage 0's word pass settled "News".
  • Not recommended (E2 · B): keep the News page for posts only. A family who saw an event on the home could not find it again from the tabs.

Why the window lists events but does not move them

  • The volunteer's question at 7:00 a.m. is "what is at this park today". A list without the camp gives a wrong answer.

One photo question, with one owner

Two drawings ask the same family the same thing in two different ways. This project's Stage 3 (A8, C8, A13, A14, ruled) has one built-in question, the same words for every club. The family must answer it, a No costs nothing, and it decides whether photos filed under a team may show. Board Ready's camp form (its ask 12, ruled) has an optional yes or no that can't be made required. A camp is not a team, so under this project's rule a camp "yes" unlocks nothing, and a family could answer the same question twice in two shapes .

Why this project's Stage 3 should own it

  • The question governs photos, and the photos live on this project's shelf. Its owner is the project that decides where those photos show.
  • One question in one set of words means one answer the club can read, change and record (Stage 3's answers list), whichever sign-up a family came through.
  • Board Ready's camp form keeps its fixed guardian line. Only the photo line moves.
  • The real difference is required versus optional. Both drawings already agree that neither answer is chosen for the family and that No costs nothing. Whether a family may skip the question is a /legal question, not a design one.
  • Your call, before anything else here: route this to /legal now, or when Stage 3 is drawn. Stage 3 builds before any photo shows, and Board Ready Stage 7 should not build its own question in the meantime.
  • Found while reading Board Ready's drawing: its ruling says the camp form "starts with" the guardian line and the photo permission, but the frame places the photo question among the club's own questions, ahead of the guardian line. That is for Board Ready to tidy; it does not change this ask.

  • A (recommended). The two pieces that ship early, the event page and the Open now row, are already in their final shape. Board Ready builds them to this page; this project owns the drawing.   • B · Move Stage 4's events half earlier. This builds a News page and a News part that hold only events, and changes what /news does (A7) ahead of posts. The tab would then say "News" over a page with no news.
  • C · Nothing on the site until Stage 4. This is the smallest build. But a camp's sign-up would be invisible on the club's own home while house league and tryout sign-ups sit in Open now.

Written down with no ask: rules the drawing follows

  1. The public-games rule (question 4). The champs page reads its games through the one house league rule that Stage 1 makes the single home (season not a draft, schedule published, unsent changes held back). Today that rule is repeated inline in the standings and season pages. Whichever build comes first, Stage 1 or the champs page, pulls it into one place, and the other calls it. A test stops a second copy, in the same way the rep feed's guard does. The champs page is on Stage 1's list for the 30-second refresh. It is also on Stage 6's list of score surfaces, so the motion when a final lands is applied there too.
  2. The staff calendar guard (question 9). The staff calendar now holds Club staff events as well as every program's games. No public page reads it: not the home, not the News page, not an event's page, not the champs page. Stage 1's guard test (Stage 0 foundation F2) names those four pages. Events reach the public only through Board Ready's one rule.
  3. The §3 wording (question 8). The plan line "No new no-login family doors. Public = tournaments and team public pages (BD 2026-10-29)" quotes a ruling that the 2026-10-31 decision narrowed: point 4 became "no new sent links beyond pay and unsubscribe". The line becomes: "No new sent links to families beyond Your sign-up and unsubscribe (BD 2026-10-29, narrowed 2026-10-31). Public pages are tournaments, team public pages, and what a club itself marks public: its seasons and tryouts, its news, and its events shown to Everyone, a champs day's games among them. The public team page is games only; practices are never public." An event's page is a public page, not a link sent to one family. Board Ready's Your sign-up is the pay-link pattern that 10-31 kept.
  4. Board Ready ask 19's words, placed. On the site, a family reads "Sign up" (the event page's button), "17 spots left" and "Full · waitlist open" (session rows, and Open now's second line), and "Sign up by {day}" (house league's existing pattern). "Sign-up open" and the Events tab's chips are not used, because there is no Events tab. Board Ready's camp frame drew "17 left" and "Full"; ask 19's own words win, and the difference goes back to Board Ready in one line. All of it goes through the stage's one /marketing pass.
  5. A champs day's games are never listed twice. On its day, Today leaves out every game that belongs to the champs day and shows the one champs row instead. This is the same exclusion a running tournament gets under A3.
  6. Board Ready 8A stays withdrawn. If E1–E4 are ruled as recommended, 8A's Events tab, its "Coming up" section and its "← Events" link are not built anywhere.
