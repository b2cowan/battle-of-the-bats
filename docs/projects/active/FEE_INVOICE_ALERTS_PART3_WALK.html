Owner QA walk · Fee invoice alerts · Part 3, the page line and the fix-your-card button

Plan & billing says it out loud: one line at the top when an invoice is owed, red when it is overdue, a button on the failed-payment line, and all of it clearing when you come back from paying

Written 2026-11-05 for Part 3. Dev only. Four small database changes make the invoice overdue, paid and late, and the plan payment fail; each is put back by the step that follows it.

What this walk is for

Part 2 put a count on the Plan & billing door. Part 3 makes the page itself say what the count means: a white line with an amber edge at the top (red once overdue) that names the invoice, its day and its amount and carries the Pay invoice button, and a Payment method & invoices button on the red failed-payment line. It also checks that the line, the list below it and the count on the door agree, and that they clear together after you pay in Stripe’s tab and come back.

It passes when

  • With the invoice open and not yet due, Plan & billing opens with one amber-edged line at the top, naming the invoice, its due day and its amount, with a lime Pay invoice button. The amount matches the invoice’s total in the list below.
  • Overdue turns the same line red, and the words change to “is overdue … was due”.
  • Once the invoice is paid, the line, the count on the door and the list’s open invoice clear without a manual reload when you come back to the tab.
  • The failed-payment line keeps its sentence and has a Payment method & invoices button that does what the plan card’s own button does.
  • On a phone, both buttons are full width and at least 44 px tall. A treasurer or admin cannot open the page. A portal with no fee invoices shows no line.

Not in this walk

  • Two or more invoices at once (“2 invoices for Stripe fees to pay … See invoices”). The dev club has only one invoice and a second cannot be made by hand without inventing fees. The words, the count and the button are pinned by tests.
  • The red banner on every other page (Part 4), a cancelled club (Part 4), help articles (Part 5). None exist yet, so their absence is correct.
  • A real payment. Paying is simulated in the database. Opening Stripe’s invoice page is allowed; do not pay it.
