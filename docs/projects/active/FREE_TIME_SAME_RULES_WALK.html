Free time with no end date, and the club’s ending email

Built and committed 2026-11-20 on the dev server. Two purposes: a coach can be given Premium free time with no end date and staff can still change or end it, and a club whose free time is ending emails its owner.

What this walk is for

Until today only a club could be given free time with no end date, and only a coach got reminder emails before free time ran out. Now both work the same way. You give the test coach free time with no end date, see what the coach sees, move it to a date and back, and end it. Then you give the test club a month of Club and see the owner emailed.

It passes when every step reads as written, and Sent emails shows exactly one reminder, to the club’s one owner.

Not in this walk (covered by automated tests instead): that Billing and Support staff keep their 90-day limit and never see No end date (dev has no such staff account); that a coach with no end date cannot cancel (the Cancel section is not shown to them at all, and the server refuses it too); the 3-day email, and a new end date sending both emails again; a team moved into a club ending the old portal’s free time.
