F

Two decisions that are yours

Raised by the review after the build. Neither is a defect in what was made — both are boundaries nobody was asked about.

1 · It does not reach a current full-size iPad

Measured in portrait on the real build. The iPad Mini (744px) and pre-2018 iPads (768px) get the new screen. The iPad 9th gen (810px), iPad Air / iPad 10th gen (820px) and iPad Pro 11" (834px) do not — they still show 32px cells, the tiny drag handle, and 110 controls under the standard.

The 768 ceiling came from the automated standard, and the three options you were given only ever considered moving it down. Moving it up was never on the table. So the promise — “a coach can operate the lineup builder on a tablet” — currently holds for small and old tablets only.

2 · Between ~641 and ~713px the names scroll away

In that slice the grid scrolls sideways, but the pinned batting number and player name — and the swipe hint — are still switched off, because they stay below 640px. So a coach scrolls a lineup they cannot read the names in, with nothing saying it scrolls.

That overflow is older than this change. But this pass declared that width a touch band and moved three of the five relevant rules into it. Either the other two follow, or the wording gets corrected — leaving both standing is the thing not to do.
