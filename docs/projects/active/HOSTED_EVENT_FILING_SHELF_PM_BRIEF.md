# PM brief — a hosted tournament's money, filed in one place

**Priority:** medium, rides the Club release. **Status:** proposed 2026-10-21, awaiting owner rulings.
Detail: `HOSTED_EVENT_FILING_SHELF_PLAN.md`.

## The problem
When a club treasurer adds an entry for a tournament the club hosts, the tournament's own list of words
holds only "Entry fees". Gate, concessions and sponsorship have to be picked from a general "Tournaments"
list — and money filed there never reaches the event's profit in the By activity report. The report then
quietly understates what the tournament made, and nothing tells the treasurer.

## What changes for the treasurer
- A hosted tournament comes with the words it needs: entry fees, gate, concessions, registration,
  sponsorship, and its usual costs, all under its own name.
- In the tournament's own book, the Add entry list leads with that event's words. Nothing from the other
  events shows.
- In the club's general book, only events in the fiscal year being entered appear, folded to one line
  each until the treasurer types or opens one. Five tournaments do not make a long list.
- Typing still searches every visible word. Words already filed never disappear from an existing entry.

## Why it matters
"Did hosting the tournament pay for itself?" is the question By activity exists to answer. Today the
answer is wrong whenever money is filed on the generic list.

## Who is affected
Club admins and treasurers only. Coaches see no change.

## Success looks like
- A tournament with entry fees, gate, concessions and a cost shows the correct net in By activity.
- A club with 5 hosted tournaments sees at most a handful of event words in any one entry window.
- No saved entry changes its category without someone choosing it.

## Needs from the owner before building
The word list for an event, whether the general book shows event shelves at all, and how far back the
one-time fill-in of existing events goes (it changes live data, so it runs only on an explicit go).
A flat list with a "which tournament" tag was examined and set aside: far larger, and it reverses an
earlier ruling.
