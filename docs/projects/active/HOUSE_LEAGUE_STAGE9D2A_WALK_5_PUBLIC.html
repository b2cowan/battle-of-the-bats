The public schedule: what families see

Written 2026-10-28, walk 5 of 6 for Stage 9d-2a (the season’s schedule, rebuilt). One purpose; sign it off on its own.

What this walk is for

The season’s public schedule now tells families the truth about every game: a cancelled game shows struck through instead of vanishing, a postponed one is listed under Needs a new date instead of reading as on, and every game names its place. It opens on this week. Practices are never public. A save is live (ruling 9d2a·A): families see a change at once and are not emailed.

It passes when

  • “Times in Eastern time.” said once.
  • It opens on this week; earlier weeks fold behind a counted door.
  • Cancelled struck in its week; Postponed under Needs a new date with Was {day}.
  • The place on every game; no practices.
  • A league admin’s change shows on the next load.

Not in this walk

  • Publishing, a “schedule is coming” state, and emails to families (9d-2b).
