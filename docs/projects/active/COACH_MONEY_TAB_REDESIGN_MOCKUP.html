How to read it

  • Newest first, bank-app style — each row's Balance is the balance after it. Scheduled rows sit above Today with projected balances, dashed and blue — never distinguished by colour alone.
  • The balance at Today IS Cash on hand — same number, guaranteed, because every movement that feeds it has a row here. If they could disagree, the register would be wrong by construction; that is a build-blocking test.
  • Direction lives in two columns. A refund on an expense is simply money in; an income reversal is money out. Everything has one home — its date. (This is not the dead "direction column" draft: that was one signed column with a tag on the budget report, running two variance formulas. The register shows no variance, and the report ruling stands.)
  • Rows are doors. A typed record opens the money form. A derived row (dues, fundraising, club — dimmed, chip naming its workspace) navigates there to act; the register never edits it. A scheduled commitment offers Mark paid → Add money, pre-filled, asking when.

The rules it keeps

  • ⛔ Nothing pending a decision appears. An unapproved request is money the club may decline — it lives on the Club tab only.
  • Filters hide the balance. A running balance over a filtered subset looks like cash and isn't; the full book is the only place the balance shows.
  • Displaying dues never touches dues. The schedule of what families owe is read here, managed only on Player Dues.
  • Season-scoped, working season only. A money history book is its own future session, deliberately.
  • The Overview's next-30-days panel becomes a window into this — its rows deep-link in with the overlay on.
