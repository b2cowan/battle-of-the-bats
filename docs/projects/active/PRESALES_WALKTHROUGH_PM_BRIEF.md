# Pre-Sales Walkthrough Layer — PM Brief

**Approved 2026-08-19 · Mockups:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/6f16bc17-d5f3-45b6-bd03-b6df54231f15

## What we're building

A "90-second walkthrough" for each customer type, starting with tournament organizers. It's one
scrolling page: five problems every organizer recognizes ("Saturday, 2:14 PM — eleven texts
asking for the score"), each answered by a **real screenshot of our live demo tournament** and
one plain sentence, each with a quiet "see this screen live" link into the demo. "Start free"
stays the lead ask at top and bottom.

## Why it matters

Our buyers are time-poor volunteers. Today they either read prose (persona pages — there isn't a
single product screenshot anywhere on the marketing site) or get handed the full live demo, which
asks them to explore on their own energy. The walkthrough is the missing middle: it shows the
product solving their Tuesday-night problem with zero decisions required. It's also the link a
cold outreach email has been missing — "see how it works in 90 seconds."

## What the customer sees differently

- A new page linked from the tournament-organizers page (and shareable directly in outreach).
- Nothing else changes: demos stay ungated and untouched, pricing untouched, no new nav item.

## The durable part

Screenshots are captured **by machine from the demo world** (the same fictional club the demos
run on — never real customer data, enforced in code). They can be re-taken in one command every
release, and a check fails when a pictured screen disappears — so the walkthrough can't quietly
start selling a product that no longer looks like that.

## Sequencing (owner-decided)

1. Tournament organizers (now) → 2. present mode: the same page as full-screen slides for live
pitches, plus a PDF leave-behind → 3. head coaches → later: the club-president story (ends in
"express interest" since Club isn't purchasable yet), and a decision on whether demo links can
land on the exact screen shown.

## Success criteria

- A cold prospect can understand what we fix for them in under 2 minutes without clicking anything.
- Outreach emails have a visual link that isn't "go explore our demo."
- Every screenshot on the page is re-takeable in one command and provably current.
