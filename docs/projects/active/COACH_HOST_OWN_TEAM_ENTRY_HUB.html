Product-manager brief

Your team, in your own tournament

Raised by the owner 2026-09-13 from a hosted tournament's Teams page inside his own coaches portal; the proposal was accepted the same day. Planned, not built.

What it does

A coach who runs a tournament from their own coaches portal gets an Add my team button on the Teams page. One click, pick the division, and their own team is in the tournament — already connected to their portal, so it shows up on their Tournaments page right away (under This season as an entry, and under Tournaments you run as the host — both are true). No typing their own name, no emailing themselves, no accepting their own invitation.

The ordinary Add Team form, on every organization's tournaments, learns one thing: when the email you type belongs to a coach who already uses FieldLogicHQ, the form says so and turns on the notification for you — because that notification is how the coach accepts the entry and gets it in their portal. If the email is new to the platform, the form says what happens instead. Nothing else about the form changes.

Underneath, one repair: a coaches portal that was started from scratch — never upgraded from a free team, never claimed a tournament entry — has no way today to recognize an entry its coach makes on someone else's public registration page. That gets fixed for every such portal, existing ones included.

What it deliberately does not do

There is no search of other clubs' teams from the organizer's side. Every connection between a tournament entry and a real team on this platform is made by that team's own coach — registering, or accepting. An organizer picking a team from a directory would attach a roster, a schedule and a chat to a team that never agreed to it, and would open a window onto every club's teams that the product has nowhere else. The worry behind the ask — "my Add Team entry is an orphan even though the coach is right here" — is answered by the two parts above, from the two people who actually own the fact.

Why it matters

The portal has been able to host tournaments since the delegation work landed (2026-09-13). The hosting coach is the one person on the platform who is obviously the team they want in the list, and they are the only one with no door: the club-side "link to rep team" control is deliberately not shown to a coaches portal, and both coach-side doors assume the coach is a guest of someone else's tournament. Today the honest workaround is to register on your own public page like a stranger, or add yourself and then accept your own email.

Who benefits

Every Premium coach who hosts; every organizer on every plan who adds teams by hand; every coach who gets added by hand and is already on the platform.

Two things found on the way

The coach-side Tournaments list can show an entry the record page then refuses to open — once for entries a club links to its rep team, and once for every assistant or team manager on a Premium team (only the coach who personally registered can open a record today). Same root cause, one fix; the plan carries it as Part D for your ruling, with one data question (whose roster a Premium team submits) flagged for deferral rather than solved in passing.

Decisions open

The host's own team defaults to Paid; a staff member you've handed Run tournaments to can click Add my team (the entry is recorded under the head coach regardless); the email check is a real yes/no lookup rather than a conditional sentence; existing from-scratch portals are repaired on production; Part D is in. Recommendations on the Full Plan tab.

Success criteria

A hosting coach gets their team into their own tournament in one click and sees it on their Tournaments page without leaving the portal; an organizer adding a coach who is already on FieldLogicHQ is told so before they save; a from-scratch standalone portal sees the entries its coach makes elsewhere; no organizer can attach an entry to another club's team.

Full assessment

Your team, in your own tournament — implementation plan

Engineering detail lives in docs/projects/active/COACH_HOST_OWN_TEAM_ENTRY_PLAN.md; this tab is the same plan in reading order.

The ask, and the re-frame

Standing on a hosted tournament's Teams page inside his own portal, the owner asked how to add his own team, and whether he should be able to search the platform's other teams "to ensure proper linkage". The first has no answer today — that is the gap. The second is the wrong instrument for the right worry: the worry is that an Add Team entry is an orphan even when the coach is on the platform; the instrument proposed would let an organizer attach an entry to a team that never agreed to it. Every linkage on the platform is written by the coach's own signed-in action. This plan keeps that invariant and closes the gap from the two sides that already own the fact.

What the code does today

F01 — Add Team creates an orphan, always

"Add Team Manually" inserts a registration row — name, coach, email, division — and nothing else. No connection to any team on the platform, the host's own included.

F02 — The only two linkage doors are coach-side

An entry connects to a coach's team when (a) the coach registers on the public page while signed in with the entry's email, or (b) the coach accepts the notify email on the join page — which requires the same email equality. A host who Add-Teams themselves must then accept their own email.

F03 — The club-side link control is gated off for a coaches portal

League/Club organizations get a per-row "Link to rep team" control; a coaches portal's plan deliberately does not carry that module, and the routes behind the control refuse it too. The one person who obviously is the team has no control at all.

F04 — A from-scratch portal has no free-team shadow, so public registration strands it

A standalone portal gets its free-team shadow only from an upgrade or a claimed entry. A direct signup has neither, so the portal can never resolve one. The public form's picker lists only free teams, so this coach sees none, the platform creates a new free team for the entry, and the paid portal never learns of it. The UAT standalone fixture is exactly this case; the Milton Bats portal in the owner's screenshot may be too (verify on prod before the backfill).

W1 — Widened: a club-linked entry dead-ends on click

The coach Tournaments list merges both connections (the free-team one and the club's admin link) and renders every entry as a link. The record page authorizes only through the free-team connection, membership-checked. A club-linked entry has none — 404. The list promises what the record refuses.

W2 — Widened: on a Premium team, only the coach who personally registered can open a record

Same guard: free-team membership, which is written only for the coach who created or adopted the free team. No staff sync exists. Assistants and the team manager pass the Tournaments door and see the entry; tapping it 404s. Same root cause as W1: the record should read the same right the list read.

Out of scope

Any organizer-side lookup of another club's teams; auto-linking by email match on the organizer side; changing who may host (the delegation ruling stands); the free portal's own Tournaments list beyond what Part C touches.

Part A — Add my team

  • Where: the hosted tournament's Teams page, only when the tournament is run from a coaches portal. Everywhere else the page is unchanged.
  • Header: a ghost Add my team button left of Add Team; in the empty state, a second button beside it. Once the team is in, the button is gone (nothing further to do) and the row carries a clickable Your team chip in the slot where a club shows its rep-link chip.
  • Dialog: "Add {Team} to this tournament" — Division (preselected to the current filter), Payment status (default per D1), one line: "Registered as {head coach} · {email}. It shows on your team's Tournaments page right away." — Add my team / Cancel.
  • Who may click: anyone who can reach the page for this tournament — the head coach, and staff holding Run tournaments (D2). No second gate.
  • Server: refuse unless the organization is a coaches portal; resolve the portal's team and its head coach; ensure the free-team shadow (Part C); refuse with 409 if the team is already in this tournament; create the registration as the head coach under the same capacity rules Add Team uses; write both connections (the free-team one that the coach's own pages read, and the rep-team one that public-page recognition reads for delegates).
  • Coach side: the tournament now sits in both halves of the Tournaments page — an entry under This season, the host under Tournaments you run. Both true.

Part B — Email-aware Add Team

  • Every organization's Add Team form. When the email field holds a valid address and blurs, one status line: on a match, "This coach is on FieldLogicHQ. The notification is on — they'll see this entry in their portal the moment they accept." and the Notify checkbox flips on; on no match, "When you notify them, this email becomes their sign-in and the entry lands in their free Coaches Portal." Nothing while empty or invalid.
  • The lookup (D3): organization-admin only, exact lowercased match, returns a yes/no and nothing else, rate-limited per user. What it exposes: an authenticated admin, one email at a time, learns yes/no for an address they already typed — materially the fact the notify email would disclose anyway. Copy-only (no lookup, always the conditional sentence) is the fallback if D3 rules the other way.
  • No behaviour change on save. Acceptance stays the coach's action; the join page is untouched.

Part C — Every portal team has a free-team shadow

  1. Provisioning creates the shadow when neither an upgrade nor a claimed entry supplies one, with both back-links exactly as the upgrade path writes them.
  2. One "ensure the shadow" helper, called by Part A and by the portal's own resolver instead of giving up.
  3. A data-only backfill for every existing from-scratch portal (D4). Data-only migrations are invisible to the migration checker — record it in the release notes by hand.
  4. The public form's team picker labels a portal-backed team with the portal's team name and a "Coaches Portal" note so the coach recognizes it.
  5. The UAT standalone fixture seeds the shadow; the demo coach seed is checked (the build re-seeds a moved demo on its own).

Part D — The record follows the list (W1 + W2) — needs a ruling

The Premium record page already establishes that the viewer holds a coaching assignment on this team. What it does not establish is that this entry belongs to this team. Proposed: one resolver that answers "does this entry belong to this rep team, by either connection", and the Premium page passes that verdict into the shared record as the access fact. The free portal's own record keeps its membership check. Every API the record calls moves with it, or the page opens and its tabs refuse. One of those — roster submission — reads the master roster from the free team, which for a Premium team is the wrong roster; that is where D stops being an access change and becomes a data question. Recommendation: rule D in, defer the roster-source question to its own line. D is a day with that deferral; several without.

Sequencing

C → A → B → D. C first because A cannot connect the entry without it; A before B because A is the owner's actual blocker; B is a form change plus copy; D on its own ruling. C's backfill applies to dev with the build and to prod at promote time, recorded by hand.

Decisions open — recommendations

  • D1 — Payment status of the host's own team. Default Paid: the team owes nothing to its own event, and an Unpaid row would sit in every "who hasn't paid" chase. Editable like any team.
  • D2 — May a delegated runner click Add my team? Yes: the Run tournaments switch hands over the whole page or nothing; a second gate on one button contradicts it. The entry is written as the head coach either way.
  • D3 — Email-aware: do the lookup, or copy only? The lookup — it is what makes the form aware. Copy-only is the fallback.
  • D4 — Backfill existing from-scratch portals on prod. Yes. Without it, an existing standalone coach registering anywhere keeps stranding entries.
  • D5 — Part D in scope? In, with the roster-source question deferred to its own line.

Verification (owner walk drafted when built)

  • Hosted tournament, head coach: Add my team → dialog → row with Your team chip → button gone → coach Tournaments page shows the entry under This season and the tournament under Tournaments you run → the entry's record opens.
  • Same as a staff member holding Run tournaments (D2): button present; the entry's coach and email are the head coach's.
  • Second click impossible (button gone); a direct request is refused as already in.
  • Club organization (uat-test-org): Teams page unchanged apart from Part B's status line; no Add my team.
  • Part B: a known coach email → match line, checkbox on; unknown → the other line; empty → nothing.
  • Part C: a fresh from-scratch standalone portal registers on a public page → the entry appears on its Tournaments page (today it does not).
  • Part D (if ruled in): an assistant opens an entry from the list; a club-linked entry opens from the list.
