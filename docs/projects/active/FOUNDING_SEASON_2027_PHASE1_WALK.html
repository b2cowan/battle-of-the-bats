B

The two dates that must not fire this autumn

The renewal and final-notice campaigns still described the January 1, 2027 cliff, and were scheduled for November 1 and December 15, 2026 — both within weeks. Sending either would have contradicted every other surface in the app. They have moved to the summer, which is the structural fix: they cannot be sent early because the board no longer asks for them.

What each email actually says

For each campaign below: click its Preview (eye) icon on the board and read the email the product renders. It must match the specimen printed here — same subject, same words, same dates. The preview is produced by the same code as the send, so this is the real thing, not a mock-up.

D

The dialog you read last, before real mail goes out

This is the defect that caused the rest of the work. The board’s table already read the subject from the database, so the rewrite looked complete — but the Confirm Send dialog read its own hardcoded copy, and would have quoted you a December 31 subject while sending a September 30 email. It now comes from the same one place as everything else.

Verdict
E

A retired campaign cannot be sent, even on purpose

Taking a row off a screen is not the same as making it impossible. The send route refuses a retired campaign outright, so a stale bookmark, a browser back-button, or a future screen that forgets cannot mail a pitch for a parked product.

Verdict
F

Nothing was thrown away

Go to Platform admin → Email Templates. Retiring a campaign keeps its copy, so the day Club or House League un-parks the email is still written. Deleting the rows would have been tidier and worse: a row deletion is invisible to every check we have, which is how migration 264 ended up stranded and unnoticed.

Verdict
G

The offer bar, walked into both demos

The Founding Season bar sits on marketing pages and a sandbox is an organization, so the two should never share a screen. That is an argument, not an observation — and the failure it predicts is not a visible bar but an empty band where one used to be, because the bar reserves its own height at the top of the document. I measured all of this at phone and laptop width (34 checks, all passed); this part is you seeing it.
