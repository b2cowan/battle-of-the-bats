# The public face, warm — marketing, sign-in and emails — PM brief

Plan: `PUBLIC_FACE_WARM_PLAN.md` · Status: **walked and drawn 2026-10-22, round 1 ruled 2026-10-23**
(https://claude.ai/artifact/M1ehQVxvLzMiTZfJDMtcft). **Runs beside Club Tier, ahead of Platform admin** (owner,
2026-10-22). The sign-in and sign-up screens and the two account emails were done by The first look and reached
customers on 2026-10-20. This project covers the marketing site, every other email and the error screens.

**What changes.** Inside the product, coaches and organizers already work in a warm, calm design: cream pages, plain
sentence-case headings and friendly buttons. Since The first look, sign-up and sign-in look the same. Around them, a
newcomer still meets something else:
- The marketing pages are always dark, and most of their text, including whole paragraphs, is in a typewriter face,
  often in capitals.
- About 50 of the emails we send are a dark card with lime capitals.
- The error screens talk like a computer ("[DIAGNOSTIC]: ROUTE_NOT_FOUND", "Return to Root Node").

After this project, the whole path is one warm, readable experience: the first marketing page, sign-up, every email and
the first screen inside. Error screens say what happened and what to do next.

**What each person sees differently.**
- **A volunteer or organizer looking at us for the first time:** a cream home page with plain headings and buttons
  that say what happens ("Start free"), the same look as the sign-up screen they land on. A link to our site shared in
  a group chat shows a proper preview card instead of a bare address.
- **A coach:** the invitation, the welcome email and the notices look like the verify email they got a minute
  earlier: one friendly design in every message.
- **A parent:** emails from their child's team arrive with **their club's** logo and colour at the top and "sent
  through FieldLogicHQ" at the bottom, so they recognize who it is from. A dues reminder becomes a proper letter
  instead of a bare block of text.
- **Anyone who mistypes an address:** "We can't find that page", with a button home.
- **Someone who chose Dark:** every signed-out page is warm (ruled 2026-10-23), and signing out resets the device to warm. Their Dark comes back when they sign in. On a shared family computer, the next person's first impression is always warm.

**Why it matters.** Our customers are volunteers, coaches and club organizers, not technical buyers. A screen that
looks like a terminal tells them the product is for someone else. They form that impression on the marketing page and
in the first email, before they see the part we have already made comfortable.

**What was decided (owner, 2026-10-23).** The marketing pages leave the dark look, all together in one release, and stay
warm for every visitor. The FieldLogicHQ wordmark is redrawn as its own piece of work before the marketing pages change. Family
emails carry the club's logo and colour. The shared email design moves now, ahead of Club's new emails; the club-branded part
runs alongside Club's own email work. The public tournament pages get their own project afterwards. The order is: the written
identity and rules, then every email on one design, then the marketing pages, then the error screens, then family emails.

**Priority.** Runs beside Club Tier. It touches no club screen except through emails, and the ladder says where it
waits for Club.

**Success looks like.**
- A non-technical volunteer goes from our home page to their first screen inside without meeting a typewriter heading,
  a system word or a dark page.
- Every email wears one warm design, and family emails are recognizably from the family's club.
- The visual identity is written down once.
- You have walked every stage.
