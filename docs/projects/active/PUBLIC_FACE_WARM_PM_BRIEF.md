# The public face, warm — marketing, sign-in and emails — PM brief

Plan: `PUBLIC_FACE_WARM_PLAN.md` · Status: **queued 2026-10-19**. It starts after Club Tier. Whether it runs ahead of
Platform admin is to be confirmed at kickoff; the recommendation is yes. **2026-10-19:** the sign-in and sign-up
screens and the two account emails moved to The first look (`FIRST_LOOK_SIGNUP_PM_BRIEF.md`); this project keeps the
marketing site, the other emails and the system screens.

**What changes.** Inside the product, coaches and organizers already work in a warm, calm design: cream pages, plain
sentence-case headings and friendly buttons. Before they get inside, they meet something else.
- The marketing pages are still dark.
- The sign-in pages have the warm colours but keep the old typewriter capitals.
- Every email we send (welcome, verify, invites, password resets, family notices) is dark with lime capitals.

After this project, the whole path is one warm, readable experience: the first marketing page, sign-up, the first
email and the first screen inside. The words change too. "ACCOUNT SECURITY", "[DIAGNOSTIC]: ROUTE_NOT_FOUND" and
"Staff Access" become plain sentences that say what happens next.

**Why it matters.** Our customers are volunteers, coaches and club organizers, not technical buyers. A screen that
looks like a terminal tells them the product is for someone else. They form that impression on the marketing page and
in the first email, before they have seen the part we have already made comfortable.

**Customer impact.**
- **Every new sign-up** sees it.
- **Every customer who resets a password** sees it. That email used to call them staff; the wording was fixed on its
  own on 2026-10-19, and the look follows here.
- **Every family who gets an email from their club** sees it. We will ask whether those emails should carry the club's
  own logo and colour.

**What we'll decide together.**
- Whether the FieldLogicHQ wordmark stays as it is. Recommended: yes.
- Whether public pages follow the Warm / Dark setting.
- Whether family emails wear the club's identity.
- Whether public tournament and club pages get the softer type while keeping their own colours.

**Priority.** After Club Tier. Recommended ahead of Platform admin, which is internal tooling; this is the shop window.

**Success looks like.**
- A non-technical volunteer goes from our home page to their first screen inside without meeting a typewriter heading
  or a dark page.
- Every email wears one warm design.
- Family emails are recognisably from the family's club.
- You have walked every stage.
