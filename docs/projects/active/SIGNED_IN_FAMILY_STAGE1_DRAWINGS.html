What I found reading the code

  1. One child’s link opens the whole family. The page shows every child on the same sign-up, so a reset that stops only one child’s link leaves the brother’s link opening the same page. The plan says “per registration”; the drawing resets the whole sign-up (ask 6).
  2. “When the season ends” has no single meaning today. The season’s end date is optional and only shown for information. What the product does track is the day the club marks the season finished. Today Pay still works after that (ask 1).
  3. Every email today carries two different family links. The pay button opens this page; the reference line underneath links to the email-and-reference page. Stage 1 leaves one (tab 5).
  4. The brief’s “never sent when nothing is owed” reaches further than the plan’s. Read literally it takes the link out of the sign-up confirmation too, so a waitlisted family would have no way back to their page except asking for it (ask 3).

Your calls

Seven calls. Pick an answer for each, add a note if you like, then copy and paste them back. Nothing is built until you do. Already ruled and not asked again: one name, first names only, the link ends with the season, a club reset, no pay link when nothing is owed, and “Email me my link”.

1 · When does a family’s link stop?

The season’s end date is optional and informational, so the clock has to be the day the club marks the season finished.

2 · The button’s words in an email that asks for money

The plan rules one name. The question is whether a pay email’s button may say what to do there while still opening the same page.

3 · Which emails carry the button when nothing is owed?

The plan names two emails (placement and moved team). The brief says “never sent when nothing is owed”, which also covers the sign-up confirmation.

4 · The club’s door

Where the reset lives, and who can use it.

5 · The two “ended” screens

A season that finished, and a link that was replaced or is broken.

6 · A reset covers the whole sign-up

One child’s link already opens every child on the same sign-up, so stopping one child’s link alone protects nothing.

7 · Do the emails go first-name-only too?

The page drops last names because a forwarded link opens it for anyone. The emails still print “Maya Okafor” in the details panel.

What the design check changed (round 2)

  1. The family emails now wear the shipped envelope: the club’s band at the top, the boxed details and “To pay” panels, the “Sent by … because …” line and “Sent through FieldLogicHQ”. They are service emails, so no unsubscribe link is drawn.
  2. The placement email uses its real words (“The teams are set”), not invented ones.
  3. The club’s page frame matches the site: square-cornered logo, gold rule, section links, the coloured footer with the club’s email and a copy button.
  4. Child cards use the real label-left, value-right rows; a waitlisted child shows no Team row; the reference is in the site’s own type.
  5. Both pay buttons are the real height and width; the bank debit button is the outlined one; the bank amount carries its own processing fee, as it does when the family pays the fee.
  6. The record window has its History section, real history lines, the two-line Previous / Next foot, and no highlighted row the product would not show. The toast is the light pill with a check, and the confirm is one paragraph at the real width.
