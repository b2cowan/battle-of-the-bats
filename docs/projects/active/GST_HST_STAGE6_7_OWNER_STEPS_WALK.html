The things only you can do before the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are published

Written 2026-11-16, after the 6.6 reviews. One purpose: get our addresses receiving mail, read two Stripe settings, put a business phone and mailing address in place, and start the breach record. Nothing here touches the product; the product work that also blocks publication is listed at the bottom, and the agents do it.

Why these block publication

  • Mail. The Terms name support@fieldlogichq.ca, the Privacy Policy names privacy@fieldlogichq.ca, and every product email replies to hello@fieldlogichq.ca. Checked 2026-11-16: the domain has no mail record, so mail to all three bounces today. A policy that gives an address nobody receives fails the law it is written to.
  • Stripe’s retry setting. Terms §8 says a plan whose payment fails keeps working while it is retried and “may end”. Stripe decides how long and what happens after the last retry; only the dashboard shows it.
  • The live invoice. Clubs need our GST/HST number on the invoice to claim their tax back, and no live invoice has been seen yet. The template preview shows it without selling anything.
  • Phone and mailing address. Ontario asks a seller to give a buyer a phone number and a business address. Ruled O1′: no personal name and no home address; a phone line and a mail service’s street address.
  • The breach record. Privacy Policy §10 says we keep a record of every breach. It becomes true the day the record exists, even empty.

It passes when

  • 1 A test email to each of support@, hello@ and privacy@ arrives in the business Gmail, not in spam.
  • 2 You have read Stripe’s retry setting and the live invoice template, and counted the live subscriptions made before Nov 6.
  • 3 You have a business phone number and a mailing address that is not your home, and Stripe’s public details show them.
  • 4 The breach record exists, in the business Google account, with its first line.

Before you start

Accounts
The business Gmail (fieldlogichq@gmail.com), the AWS console (Route 53, where the domain lives), and the Stripe dashboard in live mode (not the sandbox).
A second email account
Any account that is not the business Gmail (your own, or an Outlook/Hotmail one), to send the test emails from. Gmail hides mail you send to yourself through a forwarder, so a test from the business Gmail proves nothing.
Time
About 45 minutes, plus however long a phone number and a mailing address take to set up (part 3 can be done on another day).
Order
Parts 1–4 are independent; do them in any order. Part 5 is last.
Keep private
Do not type the phone number, the mailing address or any account number into this page. The summary is filed in the repo. Tick the step and give the two facts to Claude in the chat at step 6.7.
