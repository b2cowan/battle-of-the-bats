When this shows instead of the door

When the coach’s account reaches anywhere beyond this club:

  • a seat in another organization, in any status (active, invited or suspended)
  • their own team in the Coaches Portal, free or paid
  • coaching a house-league team in another club
  • a family link to another club
  • FieldLogicHQ staff, and the demo accounts

Expect this to be common for coaches. Many run their own team in the Coaches Portal as well as coaching here. So most coach resets will go to support, and the door will appear for the minority whose account is only this club’s. That is the safe default: a reset signs the person out everywhere, so a club may only do it where “everywhere” is its own.

The line never says which of these applies. A club does not learn what else a coach does.

Build notes: the same door value the Members window reads (club, support or none), now also computed for a coach’s membership. The sentence is the one Members shows, with the support address from FIELDLOGICHQ_CONTACT_EMAIL (hello@fieldlogichq.ca). A coach with two-step off sees “Off” and no line. The rule already built for Members counts their own Coaches Portal team (free or paid) and a house-league coaching seat elsewhere as “reaches another place”, so a coach is judged by the same rule, unchanged.

If your own code is asked for first

The product has one “type your code again” window. If the admin’s own last code was more than 15 minutes ago (say 3:10 p.m. and it is now 3:40 p.m.), pressing Reset two-step opens it first. Not drawn here: it is the shipped window from the Account page.

If they cancel it, nothing changes and the person window says: “Nothing changed. Resetting someone’s two-step sign-in needs your code.”

After it works

The question closes, the window shows Two-step sign-in as “Off”, the door is gone, and the page says:

Leah Fournier’s two-step sign-in was reset. We’ve emailed Leah.

The audit line reads “Kim Patel reset Leah Fournier’s two-step sign-in.”

Build notes: the question is the same component and sentences as Members (ManageMemberDialog, ask === 'resetTwoStep'), with the coach’s first name. The reset would need its own coach route, checking the club’s owner or admin role on the server, and it emails the coach. A coach reset must never reach a person the club does not fully own (tab 2).

Asks

Three questions. Each has a recommendation and room for your answer.

A1 · Where does the door sit?

On the Members window it ends the body, as a quiet ink door beside Suspend and Remove. In the coach window there is nothing else in the body to sit beside, and Remove lives in the footer.

  • At the end of the body, as an ink door. It matches the 10-19 ruling that rare doors end the body, and it looks the same in both windows.
  • In the footer beside Remove. It would put a second destructive-looking action next to a red button, and it would make a rare door as loud as Remove.

Recommended: at the end of the body, as an ink door (as drawn).

Your answer:

A2 · Who sees the door?

The staff page already lets only the club’s owner and admins write. A head coach in the Coaches Portal sees their own staff page too, and that is a different door.

  • The club’s owner and admins only. The door shows only to someone who can already remove the coach.
  • Also a head coach, for their assistants. Convenient, but the authenticator is the person’s own, and a reset signs them out everywhere. A club decides that, not a teammate.

Recommended: owners and admins only. A head coach in the Coaches Portal gets no reset door.

Your answer:

A3 · When is the door absent?

Three cases, and what the window says in each.

  • The coach has two-step off: the window says “Off”, no door (there is nothing to reset).
  • It is yourself: no door (use a backup code, as in Members).
  • Only support can reset it (the account reaches another place, tab 2): no door, and one line, with no reason given to the club.

Recommended: all three, as drawn. Expect the third to cover most coaches, because many have their own team.
