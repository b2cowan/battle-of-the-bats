Colour. Only two tones carry colour. Green is something happening for families or on the field (registration open, playing now). 5 · Design review

Checked against the shipped dropdown, the shipped chip and the standing rulings on lettering, row chips, the phone sheet and the tournament event switcher.

Works well

It reuses two shipped parts and invents nothing. The two-line row is the dropdown's existing sub-line row (the club Bank's Account pill); the state is the existing chip with the existing words. No new colour, radius or type size.
The header and the list match by construction. Both ask the same rule for the state, so a season cannot read "Playing now" in one and "Starts Jun 1" in the other.
State is a shape and words, not colour alone. The chosen season is told by the dot, the state by its word.

Issues found

1 · Medium · panel height
The panel stops at 320px. A two-line row is about 57px, so that is five and a half rows; the seven sample seasons, including the chosen one, would scroll. Fix: for this list, raise the ceiling to about 440px (seven rows) and open the list with the chosen season in view. Past that the list scrolls inside itself.
2 · Medium · long names
The panel has no width limit, and its names never wrap. A long name ("Spring Recreational House League 2027 · North Division") stretches the panel off a narrow window. Fix: cap the panel at 360px (or the window minus its margins), cut the name with "…", and never cut the pill. This is the rule the bar already uses for the season's name.
3 · Low · same job, two looks
The tournament switcher and this list now differ. Tournament rows say the phase in words on the second line ("Nov 1–3, 2026 · Game day") with no pill; season rows would carry a pill. They are different controls (the tournament one is the sidebar's event cell with a logo tile), and seasons already have a pill vocabulary with colour that the header wears. Recommendation: keep the pill, because a coloured Ended or Registration open is scannable in a way a word on a line is not. Drawn both ways as ask 1.
4 · Low · the chosen row repeats the header pill
The chosen season shows its pill, which the bar also shows. The panel opens over the bar's pill on desktop, so it is covered, and a list where one row has no pill reads as a season with no state. Recommendation: keep it. Drawn both ways as ask 2.
5 · Low · placement
The pill sits at the end of the row, on the name's line, not centred. It lines up with the olive dot, and a state chip never sits on a line of its own (the 2026-10-12 row-chip ruling).

Build notes for the builder

One opt-in addition to the shared dropdown: a trailing slot on an option (the row's end, name's line), a taller ceiling, and a width cap. Every other caller renders exactly as it does today. The state words, tones and dates come from the season state rule that the header already uses, never retyped. The dates line is the season overview's own date range, formatted with the stored-date helper. The Finished mark added to the row today is replaced by the pill. Because this changes a shared control, the walk should also open the club's other pickers once.
