One account step at every door, one top bar, and Dark that stays dark

Stage 1 is built: every way into the product asks for the account with the same form, the same heading style and a “Create account” button; the top bar reads in sentence case and its menu says “Get started”; every “Coach a team” leads to the same place; and an account set to Dark stays dark through the whole sign-up. Seven short walks, each with one job, and one part for the calls still open. Walk them in any order and sign each off on its own.

Where: the dev site, localhost:3000. A computer is about 1366 × 768; a phone is 390 wide (a real phone, or the browser’s phone view). Warm unless a walk says Dark.
