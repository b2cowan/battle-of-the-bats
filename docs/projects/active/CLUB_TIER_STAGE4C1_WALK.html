Club Tier 4c-1 + the Public site builder · a club makes its own site

Owner QA Ledger §357, rewritten 2026-10-22 for the Public site builder (ruled 2026-10-21, A1–A9). Nobody walked the first version, so this one covers both: what 4c-1 changed on the public pages (the sections in your order, the notice, the About page) and the editor rebuilt around them.

Public site is now five rows that open in place: Look · Home page · About the club · Contacts and documents · Visibility. At a computer, the club’s home sits beside them as a live preview. The look and the on/off switch moved in from Settings. Every change saves itself, except a notice, which waits for Post notice.

The test club is UAT Lakeshore Lynx (navy). Its site already has an Instagram link, two contacts (one a personal Gmail address), three documents under two headings and one question whose answer mentions 2025. Its home order starts as Teams · About the club · House league · Tournaments · Past tournaments. If a test notice is still up, press End now before Part 7. For Part 3, have a logo image with two clear colours ready (any PNG or JPG under 2 MB), and a wide photo under 4 MB.
