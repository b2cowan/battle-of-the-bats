The staff door in the club's bar

Club Tier Stage 4b-1 puts each club's own bar on all its pages. The drawings show "Sign in" for a visitor who is not signed in. They never show what a signed-in visitor sees, and in particular a member of the club's staff.

Today the bar has a quiet door back to the staff side ("⇄ Admin", "⇄ Coaches Portal", or "Roles" for more than one role). It shows for staff of any club. A coach from Riverbend who looks at Lakeshore's site sees a door into their own club, on Lakeshore's page.

A · One quiet door, only for this club's staff

Recommended
What changesSigned in, every visitor sees "Account". Staff of this club also get one ⇄ door, to the right of Account, at the outer edge. It is an icon on a phone and a labelled outline pill at a desk. Staff of another club, and every family, see no door.
WhyClub staff check their own site all the time, and the door gets them back to work in one tap. Limiting it to this club stops the bar showing a family's page a door into someone else's club.
CostIt keeps today's door and today's labels; only who sees it gets narrower. On a phone the club's name loses about 48 px when the door shows, and that happens only to staff.
