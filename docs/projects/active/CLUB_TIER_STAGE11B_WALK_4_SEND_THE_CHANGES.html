The club moves and takes back time, and the send tells who it touched

Written 2026-10-27, walk 4 of 5 for Stage 11b (house league in the club’s schedule). One purpose; sign it off on its own.

What this walk is for

The owner moves one house-league practice’s time by dragging its slot, takes another Tuesday back for a rep team, and presses Send the changes: the practices move or are cancelled, their coaches and families are told once, and everyone who runs house league hears in the bell. Until the send, nothing changes on the house-league side. This is the part that was broken before Part 5 (the send refused on every try).

It passes when

  • The take-back asks first, naming the teams and saying who is told and when.
  • Before the send, the coach still sees the old time.
  • Send the changes lists each house-league team’s change over “House league · U9 Rookie · N coaches and N families”, and counts them in “Send to N people”.
  • After: the Reds’ practice moved, the Blues’ cancelled; the send log, the bell and the season’s Email families say so.

Not in this walk

  • Game time moving (9d-2b: no house-league game sits on club time yet).
  • An email in an inbox: dev sends none. The send log, the families’ Automatic row and the step’s counts show who would have been told.
