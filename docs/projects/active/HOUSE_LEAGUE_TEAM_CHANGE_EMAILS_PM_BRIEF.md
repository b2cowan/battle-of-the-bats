# PM brief: one email per family when teams change after publishing

**Priority:** high before house league goes on sale. It removes the most visible surprise in the registrar's day.

## What changes for the convenor

Today, once a division is published, every drag asks a question and sends an email immediately. After this change:

1. **You move players freely.** No windows, no emails. Families' status pages show the new team at once.
2. **A banner says who hasn't been told:** "3 families haven't been told about team changes", with **Review and email** and
   **Don't email**. A small amber "Not told" chip marks each child it means.
3. **Review and email shows exactly what each family will read**, one row per family. Three siblings are one row and one email.
   A child moved and moved back is not mentioned at all. You can switch a family off, open an example, then send.
4. **Don't email** is a real choice for the moment a parent is standing in front of you. It asks once, clears the banner and is
   written into Email families, so there is a record. Send again on a registration still works later.

## Why it matters

- A family with three children no longer gets three emails, and a back-and-forth drag no longer spams anyone.
- The family is told what is true now ("moved from Cardinals to Hawks"), not every step on the way.
- The convenor, not the software, decides when families hear. That matches how the same screen already behaves before publishing.

## Who sees what

- People who can run the division see the banner, the chip and the buttons. The registrar and other viewers see the board only.
- A finished season shows none of it.

## Trade-offs we chose

- A single move now takes one extra click (the banner's Review and email). We judged the family-level email worth it.
- Between a move and the email, a family who opens the signed-in app can see the new team first. The email is the notice; the app is the record.
- A parent told in person and skipped in the system is treated as told the current team.

## Success looks like

- Three siblings placed one by one produce exactly one email naming all three.
- A move and a move back produce no email and no line in the review window except the quiet reason.
- Reloading, or leaving and coming back, still shows the banner until you choose.
- Every send and every skip appears in Email families with who did it.
- Nobody reaches the old "Move and email" or "Place and email" windows.
