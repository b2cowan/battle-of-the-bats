Product-manager brief

Coach Lineups Deep Dive

Outcome: keep the capable lineup engine, but give coaches an honest preparation state, readable tables, actionable coverage checks, and a review that supports real decisions.

What changes for the coach

The coach works through Batting order, Positions, and Playing time as one visible preparation flow. Auto-fill is the clear primary action and names its active mode. Blank decisions say Open, deliberate sits say Bench, and the table sits on a solid high-contrast ground.

The games list shows Not started, Draft, Ready, or Needs review. In the recommended model, any coach with lineup access can mark ready; the product records who did it, and a later edit returns the lineup to Draft.

Why it matters

Today a one-cell draft can be labelled “Lineup set,” and the attached warning guesses that a Never setting caused the hole without receiving eligibility or cap data. The visual treatment also lets the blueprint grid show through both tables and controls.

Customer impact

Every head or assistant coach with lineup access benefits, especially a first-time coach or someone editing on a tablet at the field. Access rules do not change.

Priority

P0 truth and legibility; P1 workflow and game-level analysis; P2 inning inspector and full Sport Pack vocabulary.

Success criteria

Partial work is never called ready; open-role copy never invents a cause; real conflicts point to exact cells; no page grid shows through the tables; and a coach can explain, build, review, and finish the lineup without needing Help.

Full assessment

Coach Lineups — findings and implementation sequence

P0 · Trust

F01 — Coverage is incomplete; the warning's cause and tone are wrong

Inning 7 lacks P and LF. Alex and Ashlyn are blank and both configured as pitchers, but neither blank assigns P. Both are still under cap and eligible. The product should say what is open, not guess Never.

P0 · Truth

F02 — Any one saved cell becomes “Lineup set”

Replace the boolean with Not started, Draft, Ready, and Needs review. Recommended: persist ready-by/time and return to Draft on edits.

P0 · Visual

F03/F04 — Tables and controls do not follow the approved surface/ink rules

Frames do not paint the card ground; headings use tertiary ink; controls use translucent grounds and faint borders. Align both tables with the standard and use Open instead of a hyphen.

P1 · UX

F05/F06 — Workflow and Auto-fill intent are hidden

Teach the existing three-view sequence, make Auto-fill primary, show its mode, and reveal concise placement rationale on demand.

P0/P1 · Rules

F07–F09 — Manual rule violations and exact correction targets are missing

Validate Never, pitching/rotation caps, minimum play, and conflicts without blocking coach overrides. Mark exact cells and add per-inning completion.

P1 · Analysis

F10/F11 — Playing time and blank-state language need decision support

Use explicit Field/Bench/Open figures, cap headroom, named attention states, and a clear legend. Keep season analytics in Insights.

P2 · Follow-on

F12/F13 — Add an inning lens and retire hard-coded diamond vocabulary

An inning inspector answers who is at every role. Role choices then come from the Sport Pack before another sport is enabled.

Build sequence

  1. Shared validation/readiness model and regression tests.
  2. Solid table/control treatment, exact-cell issues, and inning completion.
  3. Draft/Ready workflow across hub, Overview, Game day, print, and Help.
  4. Playing-time and Auto-fill rationale.
  5. Inning inspector and Sport Pack vocabulary.

Verification

Unit/API/component coverage; typecheck and `verify:changed`; rendered warm/dark checks at 1440, 768, 640, 390, and 361; owner browser walk from first build through ready, edit-back-to-draft, print, and Game day.

Decisions

Recommendations are drawn into the mockup; nothing is implemented until ruled.

D1 · Persist Draft / Ready with ready-by and time?Recommend yes

It gives assistants and head coaches a real handoff. Any edit returns the lineup to Draft. Lower-cost alternative: derived Not started / Draft / Complete, never “Ready.”

D2 · Treat open roles as a neutral draft check?Recommend yes

Warnings are reserved for proven conflicts, cap/Never overrides, and a failed ready attempt. Open work stays visible without accusing the coach.

D3 · Make Auto-fill the primary action and show its mode?Recommend yes

Format, innings, Reshuffle, and Templates stay available as secondary setup/reuse controls.

D4 · Show per-game pitcher cap headroom in Playing time?Recommend yes

It supports this game's decision. Season totals and rest history remain in Insights.

D5 · Defer inning inspector to Phase 4?Recommend yes

It is valuable, but should be built on the corrected shared truth model rather than delaying the P0 trust and contrast fixes.
