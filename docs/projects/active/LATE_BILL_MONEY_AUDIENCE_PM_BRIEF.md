# PM Brief — Money terms go only to people who can open Money

**What it does:** When the club sends a schedule, a review, the final schedule, changes, or a late bill, the bill amounts, payment dates
and "it's on your Money tab" lines reach only the coaches who can open Money. A game-day Assistant or Helper still gets the slots email
and bell for a review, Finalize and changes, just without any money. For a late bill they are not emailed and get no bell.

**Why it matters:** A head coach can withhold Money from an Assistant or Helper on purpose. Today the schedule send ignores that:
those people get the dollars by email, bell and phone notification, plus a sentence ("The bill is on your Money › Club tab") that is
false for them. It breaks the promise that people see what their role opens. A legal review found it on 2026-11-13 and the owner ruled the rule.

**Who benefits:**
- *Head coaches and managers* — their choice to withhold Money actually holds.
- *Assistants and Helpers* — no amounts they weren't meant to see, and no dead "Money" link.
- *Club admins* — the Send window shows who gets which email, and warns when a team's bill reaches nobody.
- *The club* — a bill is addressed only to the people who handle it.
- Club tier only (the schedule send); not on sale yet.

**Expected impact:**
- *Head coach / Manager / Treasurer:* nothing changes.
- *Assistant / Helper:* late bill → nothing. Review / Finalize / changes → slots email and bell, no bill, no correction or credit lines.
- *Club admin:* Send window wording says only Money coaches get the bill. A team with both kinds of coach gets a "see the email [name]
  gets, without the bill" door. A team whose bill reaches nobody is named in a warning, in words.
- Decisions asked of the owner: confirm the same rule covers the review email and bell, the correction and credit lines, and every bell
  that carries a bill. Optional extras: hand-back dollars, notification toggles, the "Bill the corrections off" email, and the review
  email's reply line (see the plan).

**Priority:** High. It is a privacy and product-promise defect with a ruled rule and no database change, and it should be fixed
before the Club tier goes on sale.

**Success criteria:**
- For a head coach, Assistant, Helper and Treasurer, across late bill, Finalize, changes and review: only Money-holders receive any
  dollar figure, payment date, or "Money › Club tab" line, in email, bell, push, and the opt-in email copy.
- A late bill to a team with no Money-holder says so in the Send window; the recipient count and the send log's people count equal
  the emails actually sent.
- Nothing about a Money-holder's email or bell changes.
- Tests per variant pass, and the spelling check is clean.
