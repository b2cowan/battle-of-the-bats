What changes for a coach

  • On a computer, the season figures (Record, Form, Run diff, Close games, Attendance) sit on the same white card as every other coach dashboard. Same layout, same height.
  • On a phone, they are the Overview’s rows: one white frame, one figure per row, each row opening its report. Record and Form share a row, as on the Overview.
  • The Attendance tab’s four figures get the same white frame, two per line on a phone.

Why it matters

  • The strip was the one block on the page not on a card. It read as unfinished next to the chart and What stands out, and next to every other dashboard.
  • On a phone the figures took 317px, more than half the first screen. They now take 207px (−35%), and What stands out starts 110px higher.
  • The Overview and Insights now show phone figures with one shared piece, so the two can’t drift apart.

Trade-offs accepted

  • On a computer, the record’s note is now “Scrimmages left out” — the words the Overview’s Record tile already uses — instead of “Games + tournaments · scrimmages left out”. That made Record half as wide, so Attendance stays on the first line down to a 1,010px window (it wrapped below 1,150px). Removing the note entirely would have bought only 30px more.
  • On a phone, the record’s note doesn’t show at all. The Results tab, one tap away, says “Scrimmages 0-1 (not counted)”.
  • On a phone, the small run bar goes, because “24 scored · 18 allowed” says the same thing.

Who sees it

Everyone who can open Insights. Nothing about access changes, and nothing new is stored. It’s a change to the page’s look only.

Why it looks different

It was drawn that way, and then kept that way by name. When Insights was designed (18 Aug) its figures were a borderless strip — a scoreboard with a hairline above and below. When the shared card look went across the Overview, Money and Skills & Goals (16 Sep), that pass listed this strip among the things to leave different on purpose: it took the new lettering and kept its bare shape. You approved that list as recommended.

No reason for keeping the shape was ever written down — the whole case was “deliberately not cards.” Since then the momentum chart and What stands out, directly beneath it, have both become white cards, so the strip is now the only bare block on a page of cards. It is an exception nobody argued for, and I’d undo it.

The phone pass (stage 6, yesterday) checked this screen and passed it — it asked the wrong question. It checked whether a figure repeated a heading. It never measured what the figures cost: 317px, more than half of a phone’s first screen, and the first finding in What stands out is cut off by the bottom bar.

Your two asks pull against each other if they’re done the obvious way. Giving each figure its own white card, the way the Overview does on a computer, makes the phone view taller: every card adds its own padding and a gap. What does both is one white frame around all the figures, with the Overview’s own phone rows inside it on a phone.

On a phone: the whole first screen

Ask A · phones only

On a phone, what shape do the figures take?

Both options put them on the white card. The difference is how much room they give back.

A1 · The Overview’s rows, in one white frame Recommended

317px → 207px −110px, −35% · the same at 390 and 360 wide

  • Record and Form become one row: the record on the right, the last five results and the streak underneath. That’s the Overview’s Record row, with “Lost 1” added.
  • Every row opens its report and shows the chevron that says so. Rows are 50–53px, above the 44px tap floor.
  • Run diff keeps “24 scored · 18 allowed”. The small bar goes on a phone because the words already say the same thing.
  • Labels read as words (“Run diff”), as on the Overview’s phone rows, instead of small capitals.

The trade-off: the Record figure’s note, “Games + tournaments · scrimmages left out”, doesn’t fit on the row, so it goes on a phone. The Overview’s Record row and the header’s 3-2-1 already show the record without it, and the Results tab, one tap away, says “Scrimmages 0-1 (not counted)”. The computer keeps the note.

A2 · Today’s grid, on a white frame Smaller change

317px → 271px −46px, −15%

  • Closest to today: five cells in the same order, the same bar, hairlines between them.
  • It saves only the strip’s own padding. The record’s note still takes two lines, and Attendance still sits alone on a third row.

Measured and left out: three across (231px), where the notes wrap to four lines in 118px cells; and five rows without folding Form into Record (274px), which is the same length as A2.

Ask B · computers and tablets

On a computer: one white frame, or five cards?

The height barely moves either way. This is only about what it looks like.

B1 · One white frame, five across Recommended

105px → 103px

  • The strip keeps its shape and its dividers. It now sits on the same white, the same hairline and the same corner as the chart and What stands out beneath it, and as every Money card.
  • It still reads as one scoreboard, which matters because four of the five figures open the same Results tab.

B2 · Five cards, like the Overview’s “at a glance” Alternative

105px → 128px +23px

  • Matches the Overview’s tiles exactly: each card raised, with a hover step.
  • That gives five raised buttons, four of which go to the same place, and the record’s note wraps to two lines.
Ask C · the same strip, one tab over

Insights → Attendance uses this strip too

Its four figures (Season, Games, Practices, Recorded) are drawn by the same strip. If only the Dashboard changes, two tabs of one page disagree, and the inconsistency just moves one tab over.

C1 · White frame at every width, two across on a phone Recommended

Phone 214px → 178px −36px · computer 92px → 91px, framed like B1

  • These four figures don’t open anything, and none of them fold together the way Form folds into Record, so rows would buy little here. The grid is the shorter shape.

C2 · The Overview’s rows on a phone too Alternative

214px → 193px −21px

  • Choose this if you want every figure on a phone to be a row. It’s 15px longer than C1.

What doesn’t change

  • Every figure, what it counts and which report it opens.
  • The tab row, the momentum chart and What stands out.
  • The page’s height on a computer.
  • A brand-new season still shows the “hasn’t started filling in yet” message instead of the figures.
  • No new data, nothing stored. It’s a change to the page’s look only.
