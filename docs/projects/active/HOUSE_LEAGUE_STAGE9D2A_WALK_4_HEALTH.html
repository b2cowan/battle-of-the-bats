Schedule health, scored by a league’s rules

Written 2026-10-28, walk 4 of 6 for Stage 9d-2a (the season’s schedule, rebuilt). One purpose; sign it off on its own.

What this walk is for

The season’s schedule gets the tournament’s Schedule health button and window, scored by a house league’s own rules: games on plan, home and away, byes, and clashes between games and practices. A postponed game isn’t counted as played, and a cancelled one is named as not replayed. Each issue has a door to the game or the day.

It passes when

  • The header button shows a score and an amber issue count.
  • The window has four tiles (Games, Home/away, Byes, Clashes), the issues with their doors, Team detail, and the rules said in words.
  • Show them closes onto the day in List; Give it a new date opens the game.
  • The permit is never a point.
  • A reader’s doors read, never change.

Not in this walk

  • Changing the rules: there is no “Adjust the rules” door until 9d-2b (ruling 9d2a·G).
  • A clash: house league refuses two of its own bookings on one diamond at once, so the walk can’t make one.
