Owner QA walk · Admin header bar · Phase 2D · §495

On a computer, the place names itself in the top strip and the club heads the sidebar

Written 2026-11-08. One purpose: the desktop strip (round 4, option B). A short phone check at the end proves the phone did not change.

What this walk is for

On a computer the white bar under the dark strip is gone. The place you are in now names itself in the dark strip (“← Teams │ 15U AAA” and its season chip), View site sits beside the bell, and the place’s tabs are one white band pinned under the strip. The club’s name moved to the top of the sidebar, in a cell exactly as tall as the tab band, so the sidebar’s line and the band’s line run as one line across the screen. On Rep teams and a team the pinned header went from 151px to 93px; inside a tournament from 108px to the 48px strip. Phones keep today’s bar exactly.

It passes when

  • Above 900px there is no white name bar: the place’s name is in the dark strip, where the bar’s name used to start.
  • Overview’s strip says Overview; a team shows ← Teams │ 15U AAA with its season chip and no group; a tournament shows its name, dates and chip, and a long name is cut with “…” before the dates or chip ever are.
  • The club cell’s bottom line and the tab band’s bottom line are one line, with the sidebar open and collapsed.
  • The sidebar’s rows never move up or down between screens with tabs and without.
  • Clicking the club cell opens Overview.
  • On a phone, nothing changed.

Not in this walk

  • Calendar, Schedules and record pages (a team’s History, a child’s page, Plan & billing). Their strip still shows the club’s name, as their bar did; Phase 3 gives each its own name. Part E only checks they are not broken.
  • The coaches portal. Measured identical before and after (its strip and its tab row, on a computer and a phone).
  • A cancelled account’s one page. It has no sidebar to split the strip on, so it keeps today’s strip (“Admin Area”) and its white bar with the club’s name.

Known before the walk

  • The strip’s portal word is the short Admin (your D4). The Workspaces list, Home, the account pages’ way back and the coaches portal’s door keep Admin Area (your answer at this session’s gate).
  • A team’s group (“Senior”) no longer shows on a computer (your D3). It stays in Team details, and the phone’s second line never had it.
  • The test season has one season, so the season switcher beside its name does not show. It renders in the strip from the same piece the phone bar uses.
  • Measured before publishing: one page heading per screen at each width; the two bottom lines meet on the same pixel at 1440 and 1280, open and collapsed; at 901px a long tournament name gives way and its dates, chip, View site, bell, account and Workspaces stay whole; every in-page pinned part (the draft board’s pool) sits just under the new header.
