The Stripe page in Health

Written 2026-11-07. One purpose: staff open one page and are told, in plain words, whether Stripe and the product still agree: updates arriving, updates failing, the morning comparison of every plan, and our billing page’s settings. Read only: nothing on this page can change money, Stripe or a club.

What this walk is for

Before this page, finding out that Stripe and the product had stopped agreeing meant opening Stripe’s dashboard and our own tables. Now Health has a “Stripe” page with four parts: Updates from Stripe (when each feed last spoke, and any update still failing), Our records against Stripe (the result of the morning check), Stripe settings (our billing page against what Stripe reports) and a row that says plainly it cannot read the failed-payment setting.

You will make one update look failed and put it back, make the morning check look old and put it back, and check what each role sees.

It passes when
  • The page opens from Health › Stripe and has no button that changes anything.
  • A failed update shows in the row, the figure, the list and the chip, and all of it goes when it is removed.
  • The morning check’s section shows its last result, agrees with itself, and warns when the check has gone stale.
  • The settings all read OK, and the failed-payment row says it cannot be read: not a pass, not a fail.
  • Support and Product read the page with an eye beside it; Growth cannot.
