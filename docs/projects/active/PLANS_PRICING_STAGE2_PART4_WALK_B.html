Sales, and the accountant package's invoice file

Written 2026-11-08. One purpose: a month's sales figures trace to its bills and agree with the GST/HST return for the same dates, each void and credit note counts in its own month, and the accountant package now carries every invoice and credit note in one file. Read only: nothing on these pages changes money, Stripe or a club.

What this walk is for

Before this, Bookkeeping had no month-by-month sales figure anywhere, and the accountant package said our sales were not in it. Now Bookkeeping › Reports has a third view, Sales: what we invoiced in each month, less voids and credit notes, with the GST/HST, the money collected and the bad debts each in their own column. One sentence ties it to line 101 of the GST/HST return for the same dates. The Accountant package gains one file, invoices-and-credit-notes.csv: one row per invoice and one per credit note, with both the sales and the GST/HST figures.

You will read October (it no longer changes), trace it to its bills, check it against the return, move one void into November and put it back, and open both files.

It passes when
  • October's figures are its bills: the kinds add up to Invoiced, and the void and the credit note sit in October.
  • The sentence's line 101 is the GST/HST return's line 101 for the same dates, and the parts add up.
  • Moving a void's date moves it to that month and changes nothing else: the sale stays in its own month.
  • The Sales CSV holds totals only; the package's invoice file has one row per invoice and credit note, no address, and a scope line that says what it holds.
