A move to the free plan waits for the renewal date: the owner, the emails, the operator

Written 2026-11-04 for Stage 2 (Parts A, B and C: 155c9a615, bcb569bd7, 22335eae4). This is the one owner walk the plan puts at the end of Stage 2. It runs on the dev site against real Stripe test-mode subscriptions.

What this walk is for

Before Stage 2, moving a paid plan to the free plan happened at the click, and the club lost the rest of the period it had paid for. Now the move waits for the renewal date. Until then the club keeps every paid tool and all its tournaments. The owner chooses which tournament stays, can change that choice, and can keep the paid plan at any time. Emails tell them when the move is scheduled, seven days before, and when it has happened. Staff see the waiting move on the club record and can’t change the plan underneath it. When Stripe’s end-of-period message goes missing, Finish this ending now moves the club, never suspends it. This walk proves that whole loop from both sides.

It passes when

  • Moving to Tournament leaves the club on Tournament Plus with every tournament live, and the billing page says the date and which tournament it keeps.
  • A tournament made during the wait is in the choice, and is archived on the date unless it was chosen.
  • Keep Tournament Plus calls the move off. Cancelling replaces it, and keeping after a cancel returns the full paid plan, not the move.
  • Each email arrives once, with the right dates and the right tournament names, even when the daily job runs twice.
  • On the date the club moves to Tournament: the chosen tournament stays, the rest are archived, and the club is never suspended.
  • Staff can’t change the plan while a move waits, and the refusal names the door that comes first.

Not in this walk

  • A real renewal date a month away. Part I ends Move Walk Club’s Stripe subscription early, which fires the same Stripe message the date would. Stage 3’s test-clock rehearsal runs the real date.
  • A Finish that finds Stripe still billing a move. It runs the same code the Stage 1 walk passed for a cancel (H), and unit tests cover it for a move.
  • A club with online payments. None of the walk clubs takes payments online. Unit tests pin the dated words.
  • A Coaches Portal. It has no move to the free plan here. The help articles are Stage 4.

In the project folder: node --env-file=.env.local scripts/seed-plan-move-walk.mjs --inbox <your plain address>. It takes about a minute and builds four Tournament Plus clubs, three of them on real Stripe test subscriptions. Each club’s owner is your inbox with a plus part, +pm1 to +pm4, password UATPassword2026!. It prints each club’s record link.
The clubs
Move Walk Club (+pm1): Spring Classic, Summer Slam (draft) and Fall Cup, for Parts B to E and I. Move Spare Club (+pm4): one tournament, Only Cup, for Part F. Dates
They depend on the day you build the fixture, so steps name them. “The renewal date” is Move Walk Club’s, which the fixture prints (about a month from today; Move Spare Club’s is the same day). “{Month D}” is the same date without the year. “+90 days” means 90 days after the date it follows.
