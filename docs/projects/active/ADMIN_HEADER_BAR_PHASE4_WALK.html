The bar on every kind of screen, end to end

Written 2026-11-09. One purpose: the closing look at the header bar. A short sample of each kind of screen, computer first, then a phone pass.

What this walk is for

Phases 1 to 3 gave every club admin screen a header that names where you are, with the tabs pinned under it and the way back to a record in the header. You walked and passed 87 steps of that. Phase 4 changed no screen. It brought the help articles in line (two sentences were out of date), and checked the spelling, the demo tours and the loose ends. This walk is the last look: one or two screens of each kind, to close the project.

It passes when

  • Every screen shows its name once, in the header, with no second title of its own on the page.
  • The tab row stays put when you scroll, and a record page shows its way back beside its name.
  • The two help sentences (part C) match what you see.

Not in this walk

  • The 87 steps you already passed (§486, §492, §495, §499). Not repeated.
  • The coaches portal, and the navigation review across the three areas: both come after this project.

Known before the walk

  • A cancelled account needs a club whose plan has ended; the test club is not one. Step A9 has a “Not available” answer: tick it if you have no such club on hand.
  • The test club’s schedule is not one its house-league season holds time in, so a club schedule opened from a season shows its own “couldn’t load” line under the header. Not in this walk.
