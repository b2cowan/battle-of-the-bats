Start your club: charge and terms on their own step

Today the charge and terms sit below the fold of one long window. This page draws the window split in two, name and address first, then a review of what you are agreeing to. Drawn from the shipped window and its real wording; the figures are the ones from your screenshot.

View

What changes

  • A step bar under the title, the same one New tournament uses.
  • Step 1 keeps every field exactly as it is. The only new thing is Next, the word the New tournament window uses.
  • Step 2 starts with a read-back of what you typed (club, address, billing address) so the tax row has its reason beside it.
  • The charge, the renewal line and the terms follow. Step 2 fits a laptop window without a scroll. Step 1 can still scroll on a short laptop, but it holds only fields; nothing below its fold is something to agree to.

What stays

  • All wording, all figures, and the rule that the charge is Stripe’s own for the address typed.
  • One press of Start my club still charges once. If a new address changes the charge, the window shows the new figures and asks for a second press.
  • Closing after typing still asks before it discards.

What happens when you change your mind, or the address changes the answer. Only the parts that change are drawn.

Rules this follows

  • Back never wipes. Name, address and the loaded charge are kept; only a changed country or province asks for a new charge.
  • A refusal stays where its cause is. An address Stripe can’t sell to is fixed on step 1, so Next is absent and the foot says why. Step 2 is never reached with a charge that can’t be made.
  • Figures not ready: Next still works; step 2 says “Loading the charge…” and Start waits.
  • Wording is as shipped (the charge-changed sentence is the server’s own); only the step line and the Next button are new.
