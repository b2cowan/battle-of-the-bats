The club gives house league its time

Written 2026-10-27, walk 1 of 5 for Stage 11b (house league in the club’s schedule). One purpose; sign it off on its own.

What this walk is for

An owner gives house league its time from the club’s schedules, the whole of a schedule or a share, and the slot window treats house league as one holder. It ends with the time the next three walks use: Walk · House league weeknights, Kinsmen Park, Tuesdays and Thursdays 6:00–9:00 p.m., Nov 2 – Dec 20, all of it house league’s, less one Thursday given to 15U AAA and one Thursday cleared.

It passes when

  • House league is one row in New schedule, never divisions; all of it makes no drafts and is final at once.
  • Its words say what happens: who divides it, and how many hours.
  • A house-league slot reads Held by · House league, and Give it to lists it under Programs.
  • Unused house-league time goes to a rep team with no question.

Not in this walk

  • Dividing it (walk §400), handing out practices (§401), moving time with practices on it (§402).
  • The bill: house league is never billed, and this schedule carries none.
