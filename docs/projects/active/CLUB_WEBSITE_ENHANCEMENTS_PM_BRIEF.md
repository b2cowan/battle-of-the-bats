# PM Brief — Club Website Enhancements

> Status: on the board. Plan: [CLUB_WEBSITE_ENHANCEMENTS_PLAN.md](CLUB_WEBSITE_ENHANCEMENTS_PLAN.md). Logged 2026-11-24.

**What it does:** Turns a club's public website from a list of what is open into a place that changes every week:
tonight's games and the weekend's results, field closures, news the site posts by itself, photos, sponsors with
proof of what their placement earned, a scoreboard for a TV at the park, and a trophy case. A separate track makes a
prospect's own club site before the first meeting.

**Why it matters:** Today the club home only changes when a registration opens, so a family has no reason to come
back, and the demo shows none of what the club site can do. A president judges the product by the site their families
will see.

**Who benefits:** Families and fans (they find tonight's game and whether the field is open). The club's admins (the
site stays current without a volunteer writing for it). The treasurer and sponsors (a report to bring to a renewal).
The owner in a sales meeting. The club site rides the public site module, so League Plus and Club; any different gate
for a single idea is a `/strategy` call at that idea's stage.

**Expected impact:** A family opens the club site on game day and gets an answer in one look. A club keeps its
sponsors with evidence. A prospect sees their own club, in their own colours, on their own phone.

**Track P, as drawn and ruled (2026-11-25, all 16 asks ruled as recommended):** a prospect's preview is made from a platform admin page, shows the
public site only with a made-up season and no people, is marked on every page, and is claimed with a code from a card
left in the room, through Start a club's own plan, trial and checkout. Its real cost is not the screens: every
production count, list, search and email must learn one "preview" marker, the claim is a new door, and the 30-day
removal is a new job that runs by itself. The one email to the person met cannot send until FieldLogicHQ has a mailing
address. **Built first (P1, 2026-11-25), invisible:** that one marker, read by every count, list, search and email, and a
build check that fails when a new one does not; no email of any kind can now leave the platform without saying which club
it is for, so a preview's mail is refused even where nobody thought to check. Nothing makes a preview until P2.

**Stage 1, as drawn and ruled (2026-11-25, all 11 asks ruled as recommended):** on a game day the club home shows "Today" under Open now: the
club's live games, the rest of the day and the latest results, a running tournament as one row, by time and never by
program. House league pages refresh every 30 seconds while games are on. Three things the drawing found that the plan
had not: a rep team's games reach the home only when its coach sets it to Public (Milton has none set, so its rep scores
would not show); house league has no live score at all, only a final; and today's public rep page shows a running
score as a finished tie, which is fixed first. The work under it is bigger than the screen: house league's publish rule
has to become one rule before the home may read it, and the read gets its own guards so a later edit cannot widen it.

**Priority:** Medium, after the Canadian launch. The launch does not need it; the first sales conversations do. The
owner sets where it sits among the after-launch projects.

**Success criteria:**
- The club home shows something new on any day the club has a game, a result, a field change or a post, with nobody
  writing it.
- A field closure reaches the home page and every affected game from one tap.
- A club can hand a sponsor a season report.
- No public page names a child, and nothing a club enters is sent to an AI service.
- No stage needs a later stage to redraw a page it already shipped.

**How it will be done:** one whole-site pass first (today's site walked, every page drawn once with all ten ideas in),
then one deep dive at a time in dependency order. Proposed, not yet accepted.

**What the whole-site pass recommends (2026-11-24, drawn, not yet ruled):** the home keeps one order every day, with
registration first but limited to two rows, then the day's games, then news, so a family still sees the first live game
on a phone without scrolling. Closing a field is today's house league Rainout widened to a whole park: one act that
posts the notice and marks every affected game. A rep game is marked for its coach, never moved by the club. A player
photo shows only while everyone in it has agreed. The phone banner stays off, which keeps the October ruling. "Email me
when registration opens" is built to anti-spam law, with the club as the sender.

**Club events on the site (drawn and ruled 2026-11-25, all nine as recommended).** Board Ready builds the club's side of events
(making one, sign-ups, camps, paying). This project decides where events show to the public. Ruled: an event with
an open sign-up sits with the other sign-ups at the top of the home. Every other coming event sits in News, and the
News page lists them all. Each event gets one row on the home, never two. There is no separate Events tab, because a
phone's tab row is already full. Each event has its own page; that page is the link a club sends families, and the
Sign up button on it opens Board Ready's form. A champs day is one line on the home on its day, opening a page of that
day's games and scores. Closing a field also lists any camp or event booked there. An event can carry a sponsor who
is counted in the season report. One photo question serves every sign-up, camps included. Success looks like: a camp
sign-up is on the club home the day it opens, a champs day never floods the home, and a family never answers the
photo question twice in two different ways. Drawn and ruled the same day, all as recommended: club events on the schedules people already read. A champs day is named over its games on the house league schedule; a club can list an event on a house league season's or its rep teams' schedules without making it public; and a club event sits, read-only, on a rep coach's schedule beside the club's practices. The photo question's legal review came back the same day and its five calls were ruled as recommended: the question is optional and a blank answer means No; one Yes covers the club's website and its own social media pages, never posters or sponsors; a camp answer and a team answer are separate; a family can change its answer later, even after teams are made; and every club asks it in the same fixed words.

**Close a place (Stage 2, ruled 2026-11-25, built 2026-11-25 to 2026-11-26, walked 2026-11-26).** An owner,
admin or house league admin closes several parks, any set of a park's diamonds, or all the club's fields for a day or
up to two weeks, from the Venue library, the Overview or the house league schedule. In one press: a strip at the top of every club page
says so (never on a tournament's pages) and goes by itself at the end of the last day; house league games there are
postponed or cancelled and their families emailed; a rep game there is marked "Place closed" for families (the family
app, and the team page and club home when its schedule is Public) and "Needs a new date" for its coach, who is emailed
and moves it; a tournament is handed to its own Rain delay. Reopen takes the strip down at once and tells the marked
coaches. It comes with League Plus and both Club plans. Success looks like: on a wet Saturday the admin closes the park
once, a parent learns it from the club's site before driving there, and no rep game is moved by anyone but its coach.
**Closed places, summarized (ruled 2026-11-26, S1–S6).** When closures overlap, the club's site now says it once: a
wider closure speaks for a narrower one with the same days (two diamonds, then all fields, reads as one line), several
parks on one day share one heading with a row each, and past five rows the rest open in place behind "Show N more
places" instead of being only counted. The Close a place window shows the admin that strip before the press, or says
"Your site won't change" when it already says so. A club with no places listed can close all its fields (owners,
admins, and the house league admin from a season's schedule). In the Venue library, everything closed sits in one box
with a row per park, and one Reopen takes a park's diamonds in one press, with one email per coach. Reopen says what
stays closed and never claims the site has gone quiet while it hasn't. Two separate presses on two diamonds behave as
one: the game stays marked until both reopen, and its coach isn't emailed again. Both sessions are built and wait for
the owner's walks. Success looks like: a parent reads one plain line for a rained-out park, never three that say the same thing, and the admin is never
told the site changed when it did not.

**Added from Milton Bats' meeting (2026-11-24, proposed, not drawn or ruled): the club's own feed.** The club wants to
promote wins and tournament trips on its site and already posts to Instagram. Proposed: a coach sends photos from a
phone after a game or a trip; they reach the club already sorted by team, event and date because the schedule knows
who played where; an admin picks what shows on the home's news, the team's page and a Photos page. A post the club
already made on Instagram or Facebook shows by pasting its link. A win can be downloaded as a ready-made image for
the club's own social accounts. Connecting a club's whole Instagram account is left for later: it needs Meta's
approval of us and upkeep by every club. Success looks like: a club with no Instagram still has a lively site; a club
with one never posts twice; and no child's picture shows unless every family on that team has agreed. Five calls of
the owner's come with it (Instagram: how far; who sends and who publishes; a "can post, nothing else" permission; a
recognizable child in a win photo; how much a club may keep).
