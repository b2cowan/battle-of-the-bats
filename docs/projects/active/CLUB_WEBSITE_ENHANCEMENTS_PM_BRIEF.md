# PM Brief — Club Website Enhancements

> Status: on the board. Plan: [CLUB_WEBSITE_ENHANCEMENTS_PLAN.md](CLUB_WEBSITE_ENHANCEMENTS_PLAN.md). Logged 2026-11-24.

**What it does:** Turns a club's public website from a list of what is open into a place that changes every week:
tonight's games and the weekend's results, field closures, news the site posts by itself, photos, sponsors with
proof of what their placement earned, a scoreboard for a TV at the park, and a trophy case. A separate track makes a
prospect's own club site before the first meeting.

**Why it matters:** Today the club home only changes when a registration opens, so a family has no reason to come
back, and the demo shows none of what the club site can do. A president judges the product by the site their families
will see.

**Who benefits:** Families and fans (they find tonight's game and whether the field is open). The club's admins (the
site stays current without a volunteer writing for it). The treasurer and sponsors (a report to bring to a renewal).
The owner in a sales meeting. The club site rides the public site module, so League Plus and Club; any different gate
for a single idea is a `/strategy` call at that idea's stage.

**Expected impact:** A family opens the club site on game day and gets an answer in one look. A club keeps its
sponsors with evidence. A prospect sees their own club, in their own colours, on their own phone.

**Priority:** Medium, after the Canadian launch. The launch does not need it; the first sales conversations do. The
owner sets where it sits among the after-launch projects.

**Success criteria:**
- The club home shows something new on any day the club has a game, a result, a field change or a post, with nobody
  writing it.
- A field closure reaches the home page and every affected game from one tap.
- A club can hand a sponsor a season report.
- No public page names a child, and nothing a club enters is sent to an AI service.
- No stage needs a later stage to redraw a page it already shipped.

**How it will be done:** one whole-site pass first (today's site walked, every page drawn once with all ten ideas in),
then one deep dive at a time in dependency order. Proposed, not yet accepted.

**What the whole-site pass recommends (2026-11-24, drawn, not yet ruled):** the home keeps one order every day, with
registration first but limited to two rows, then the day's games, then news, so a family still sees the first live game
on a phone without scrolling. Closing a field is today's house league Rainout widened to a whole park: one act that
posts the notice and marks every affected game. A rep game is marked for its coach, never moved by the club. A player
photo shows only while everyone in it has agreed. The phone banner stays off, which keeps the October ruling. "Email me
when registration opens" is built to anti-spam law, with the club as the sender.

**Club events on the site (drawn and ruled 2026-11-25, all nine as recommended).** Board Ready builds the club's side of events
(making one, sign-ups, camps, paying). This project decides where events show to the public. Ruled: an event with
an open sign-up sits with the other sign-ups at the top of the home. Every other coming event sits in News, and the
News page lists them all. Each event gets one row on the home, never two. There is no separate Events tab, because a
phone's tab row is already full. Each event has its own page; that page is the link a club sends families, and the
Sign up button on it opens Board Ready's form. A champs day is one line on the home on its day, opening a page of that
day's games and scores. Closing a field also lists any camp or event booked there. An event can carry a sponsor who
is counted in the season report. One photo question serves every sign-up, camps included. Success looks like: a camp
sign-up is on the club home the day it opens, a champs day never floods the home, and a family never answers the
photo question twice in two different ways. Still open: whether club events also show on the house-league and rep schedules families and coaches read (a champs day labelled on its season's schedule; an event for all rep teams or one division).

**Added from Milton Bats' meeting (2026-11-24, proposed, not drawn or ruled): the club's own feed.** The club wants to
promote wins and tournament trips on its site and already posts to Instagram. Proposed: a coach sends photos from a
phone after a game or a trip; they reach the club already sorted by team, event and date because the schedule knows
who played where; an admin picks what shows on the home's news, the team's page and a Photos page. A post the club
already made on Instagram or Facebook shows by pasting its link. A win can be downloaded as a ready-made image for
the club's own social accounts. Connecting a club's whole Instagram account is left for later: it needs Meta's
approval of us and upkeep by every club. Success looks like: a club with no Instagram still has a lively site; a club
with one never posts twice; and no child's picture shows unless every family on that team has agreed. Five calls of
the owner's come with it (Instagram: how far; who sends and who publishes; a "can post, nothing else" permission; a
recognizable child in a win photo; how much a club may keep).
