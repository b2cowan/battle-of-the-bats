# One control height — PM brief

Hub: https://claude.ai/artifact/GFfHZkFRiXAxxybnAiGrWd · Plan: `ADMIN_CONTROL_HEIGHT_PLAN.md`

**What changes.** On a computer, every button and toolbar field in the club and tournament admin is
34px tall, the coaches portal's button height, instead of 38. The portal's lime buttons rise about
2px to match its white ones, so the whole product has one button height. The club Ledger's Tools
button stops sitting short between Export and Add entry. Phones, tablets and touch screens do not
change. Filter pills and status chips keep their own, shorter sizes.

**Why it matters.** The 1 October one-size decision meant "the portal's button", but it set 38 on a
misreading: the portal's buttons measure about 33 (white) and 31 (lime). Matching the admin to the
real number gives one standard across the product, with the portal as the benchmark, and stops
every portal piece borrowed into the admin from needing its own fix.

**Customer impact.** Everyone using the admin on a computer: club owners, treasurers, registrars,
tournament directors. Visual only: nothing is added, removed or moved, and no workflow changes.

**Priority.** Small, styling only. Part of the one-standard formatting work (owner, 2026-09-28).

**Success criteria.** Every admin button and toolbar field measures 34 on a computer; portal buttons
measure 34; nothing at phone or tablet widths moves.
