A payment fails and the club recovers

Written 2026-10-28. One purpose: a paying club’s card fails when Stripe charges it, the owner is told and keeps working, the platform admin can see it, and fixing the card brings everything back. You build a throwaway club on a card that attaches but fails when charged, then make Stripe charge it.

What this walk is for

When a subscription payment fails, Stripe tells our site (the invoice.payment_failed and customer.subscription.updated events). The site marks the club Past due, tells the club’s owners (a bell item and an email), keeps everything open during a grace period, and shows the platform admin a missed payment. When a payment later succeeds, the club goes back to Active, the notice clears and the platform admin sees “Payment recovered”.

How the walk makes a renewal fail without waiting a month. The test card 4000 0000 0000 0341 is accepted when it is saved but refused when it is charged. The club is bought with a 14-day trial (Tournament Plus), so nothing is charged at checkout. In Stripe’s dashboard you then end the trial now, which makes Stripe raise the first real invoice immediately and try the card, exactly what Stripe does on the trial’s last day. The failure is real Stripe behaviour reaching our real webhook; nothing is faked in our database.

Why Tournament Plus on a brand-new club. On dev the League Plus and Club plans are not open to buy (the plan switch says early access, so the checkout refuses them), and the UAT clubs on those plans have no Stripe subscription at all. Tournament Plus is the plan that can be bought through Stripe’s real checkout with a trial. UAT Test Org also holds a Tournament Plus trial, but its owner address cannot receive email, and ending its trial would change a shared fixture. A fresh club you create here is safer and lets you read the email.

Test clocks are not used. Stripe test clocks only work on customers created attached to a clock, and the site’s checkout does not do that, so they cannot move a customer the product made.

It passes when
  • After you end the trial, Stripe’s Billing destination shows invoice.payment_failed and customer.subscription.updated delivered with a 200, and the club’s plan screen says Past due with a note telling the owner to update the payment method.
  • The owner gets a bell item “Subscription payment failed” and one email with that subject; everything in the club still opens (grace period).
  • The platform admin’s Clubs & portals list counts the club as Past due, and its Story reads “A payment was missed”.
  • After the card is replaced and the invoice paid, Stripe’s invoice.payment_succeeded reaches the site with a 200, the club reads Active, the bell item stops asking for attention (“Billing is back in good standing.”), and the Story reads “Payment recovered”.
  • The throwaway club is back on the free Tournament plan with its Stripe subscription cancelled.

Before you start

Setup, not checked. Keep this page private: it holds dev passwords.

Where
The dev site https://dev.fieldlogichq.ca, plus two other tabs: the Stripe dashboard (sandbox “FieldLogicHQ sandbox”, test mode) and Resend › Emails. If a message does not reach your inbox in about two minutes, look in Resend.
Two windows
Use a normal window for the club owner and a private window for the platform admin, so the two stay signed in the whole walk.
Your inbox
A plus-address of a real inbox you can read: yourname+owner1@gmail.com. The club’s owner emails (confirmation, payment failed, plan updated) all go to it.
The club
A new organization you create in Part A, named Dunning Walk Org, on Tournament Plus with a 14-day trial. It is the plan’s price on the pricing page that Stripe will try to charge (it may change; no step depends on a number).
Webhooks
Stripe dashboard › Workbench › Webhooks › the Billing destination › Event deliveries. A delivery that reached our site shows 200. Newest first, event name on each row.
Test cards
Fails when charged 4000 0000 0000 0341 (used at checkout). Approved 4242 4242 4242 4242 (used to recover). Any future expiry, any 3 digits, any postal code, any name. Stripe’s own pages are Stripe’s, so their buttons are described, not promised.
Stripe’s dashboard
Several steps ask you to click in Stripe’s dashboard. Where this walk is not certain a button exists with the name given, the step says so and gives you a fallback. If a fallback also fails, stop and tell me; do not improvise in Stripe.

Sign in as

Club owner
A new account you create in Part A: yourname+owner1@gmail.com · a password you choose (the dev password UATPassword2026! is fine; this is dev only)
Platform admin
uat-platform@fieldlogichq.ca · UATPassword2026! at https://dev.fieldlogichq.ca/platform-admin/login. This account is a Super admin on the dev database, which is the role that can read Stripe’s side. If sign-in is refused, stop at Part D and tell me.
Stripe and Resend
Your own logins to the Stripe dashboard (sandbox, test mode) and to Resend.

Stop check

Things this walk cannot or does not produce on dev:


    
  
  • Stripe giving up after its retries. Stripe’s automatic retries run over days and cannot be sped up without a test clock, which the site’s checkout does not use. The ending itself is the product’s Stripe-initiated cancellation path (customer.subscription.deleted: the club is suspended, its tournaments archived and kept for a restore window). It can be produced by cancelling the subscription in Stripe’s dashboard, but that suspends the club and is a different purpose, so it is not here.

  • The Club-plan billing screen while past due. The Club screen has its own past-due notice, but Club and League Plus cannot be bought through Stripe on dev, and the UAT clubs on those plans have no Stripe subscription. It cannot be reached with a real failure.

  • A Premium Coaches Portal going past due. A coach’s portal is charged at checkout (no trial), so the card that fails later cannot be used to buy it. It would need a card swapped in after purchase and a billing-cycle reset in Stripe’s dashboard. Not tried here.
