## Proposed experience

A sole proprietor opens a private Overview, sees recorded expenses and what needs attention, and adds a purchase through a short, plain-language form. The form asks when the expense was incurred; payment date starts as the same day, and a checkbox reveals a different payment date only when needed. An expense opens to its receipt, details and correction history. Reports show recorded expenses for a chosen period and let the owner open the purchases behind each total. The owner approved incurred dates for expense periods and a separate group for old dates whose meaning is unknown. The technical source-file preview sits in Sources.

The expense reports stay honest about their expense-only data. Profit, cash balance and a complete reconciled ledger wait until Stripe sources and owner-movement classifications are accepted. Club and coach accounts are visual/interaction references only; their money remains separate.

**Stage 3 progress, 2026-10-24:** The Ledger draft now shows recorded expenses beside owner money put in, money taken out and repayments of purchases paid personally. The owner can open a linked expense, correct a transfer and see its history. The screen says this activity is not a reconciled balance. Revenue still needs to accept real Stripe identities and payment, fee, refund and payout examples before income import and reconciliation can be built. The group walkthrough will review the draft and the accountant's owner-movement rules together.

**Stage 4 progress, 2026-10-24:** The owner can download one Accountant package with expense history, owner-transfer history, receipts, a receipt index and a plain-language scope note. A recovery rehearsal replayed the current development expense revisions and receipts into isolated storage and cleaned up after itself. The owner and accountant still need to choose separate backup custody, review the real documents, and complete a first close; the current package does not contain bank statements or accepted Stripe income.

## Why it matters

The previous page made a normal CAD purchase feel like an accounting import: the owner saw the fixture CSV preview first and had to work through a long form, including two amount fields for one CAD total. A recent expense sat at the bottom of the page. Simplifying that daily job makes existing expense and receipt features useful without weakening their history or accountant export.

## Priority and stages

**Priority: high for reliable daily use and handoff.** Expense entry, the first reports, the owner-transfer draft and an accountant package are built for review. The remaining decisions are Revenue's source contract, owner backup custody and retention, accountant classification and source-document review, and proof of one real close. Success means a selected period can be traced to its original records, exported, recovered from a retained copy and signed off with exceptions accounted for.

## Customer impact and success

The authorized bookkeeping owner can record a CAD purchase once, attach or later add its receipt, find and correct it, and obtain a period report whose category and supplier figures open to the underlying expenses. A USD purchase asks for conversion evidence only when needed. The worklist points to missing evidence and unknown facts. Unknown purchase tax stays distinct from a confirmed $0; no report claims a tax credit. CSV and receipt-package exports carry period and date/tax status, with owner comparison still to be walked.
