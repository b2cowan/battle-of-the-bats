The fixture starts in the legacy state on purpose — every club record is unfiled and no arrival has been given a meaning. That is exactly what a real customer's season looks like the day this ships, so it is the state worth walking from.

Three things only you can settle

1 · The Budget card went one term wider than the mockup. Frame 6 scoped the fix to club money. I also made it net money back you record yourself against a vendor refund, because the card was blind to that too and the wording of your decision said "netted money-back returns", not "club money-back". The hub part is where you'd see the difference. Say if you'd rather it counted club money only — it's one term to remove.

2 · The demo tour says nothing about the grant. It appears on the very screen the tour stops at, but your standing ruling caps each stop at a hook plus one proof point and says not to grow that step. The demo world shows it; no sentence points at it. Last step of the walk.

3 · A sentence I corrected outside my lane. The Club tab told coaches an unfiled bill "doesn't appear on Budget vs. Actual" — it always did appear, under Not itemized. That sentence belongs to the forms review, but I had to rewrite the dialog it also lived in, and leaving one copy true and one false was worse — you meet it in the hub and rules part. The forms review should strike it from its list rather than do it twice.
