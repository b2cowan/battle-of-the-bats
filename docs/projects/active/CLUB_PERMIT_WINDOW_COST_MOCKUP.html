A permit's Export, and its cost as rows

The Use workbook is a summary of the whole permit, so its Export moves to the top of the window. The cost keeps two figures in its band, and everything that multiplies (a price per share, the budget line's name) becomes a row.

Proposed 2026-10-11Both asks ruled as recommended 2026-10-11Built on dev 2026-10-11Walk: §296 W2, W8, W9, W12, and W3's Cost step

1What the code does today

Export sits on UseAt the right of the Use heading, the last section. The file it saves carries the permit's reference, place, days, hours, dates, cost, every field's price and its use: the whole permit.
The cost band holds up to four tilesCost · a full field's hour · a half field's hour (only when a field is half) · On the budget. Every other percent shows only in the rows below, so the band itself never grows past four, but at four its labels and the line's name already wrap.
One row per field, then a sentence"What each field costs" lists every field (six for the dome), then a sentence repeats the prices. Nine fields at four prices is nine rows and a long sentence. Another session is reworking these words right now ("75% of a full field", each row's hour); this proposal keeps their words and groups the rows.

5The asks

Export moves to the right of “The permit”, the icon on a phone.
Why
The workbook carries the permit's reference, place, days, hours, dates, cost, prices and use. Above what it exports, on the right (the window rule of 2026-10-09), is the top of the window.
Without Accounting
As today: no Cost section, and their file has no Cost column and no cost in its heading. Export still sits at the top; it saves what that reader can see.
Not this
The window's top bar, beside the pencil and ×. No window puts a door there, and it is the record's identity and its two controls.
The cost: two figures in the band, one row per price, the line's name as a read line.
What changes
  • The band holds Cost and a full field's hour (An hour held when every field is full). No half tile, no budget tile.
  • "What each field costs" folds into Cost: one row per price (Fields 1–4 · Full field · $72.00 an hour), every percent with its own hour.
  • The sentence says how the hour was worked out, not the prices again.
  • On the budget becomes a read line under the rows: the name on one line, its year, Open the line.
Why not rows for the two figures too
Your 2026-10-08 ruling: a window's money figures are one joined band. Two figures never wrap at 640 or on a phone, and the band can no longer grow: a new percent is a row.
The alternative
One row per field, each with its hour (no grouping). Simpler, but a nine-field dome is nine rows saying four prices.
