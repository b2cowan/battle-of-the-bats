# PM brief — Tryout blurb preview

**What changes.** On a team's Tryouts page, the box where the club writes its note to families ("From the club") gets a
small picture beside it on a laptop screen: the real tryout page, as a family sees it on a phone, with the club's
colours and the words as they type. A phone screen looks exactly as it does today.

**Why it matters.** The note is public, and the club could not see where it would land. Now they see the words in place
above the Sign up button, how long they run, and, when sign-ups are closed, a plain line that families can't see the
note right now.

**Who sees it.** The club's administrators on the team's Tryouts page, for the team's live season.

**Customer impact.** Fewer rewrites and fewer "where does this show up?" questions; no new setting to learn.

**Priority.** Small polish on a shipped feature; rides the Club release.

**Success.** A club can write the note, glance right, and know it reads well; nothing on a phone moved; nothing in the
preview can be pressed by accident.
