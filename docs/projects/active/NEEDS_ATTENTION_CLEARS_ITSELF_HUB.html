Needs attention clears itself — PM brief

Plan: NEEDS_ATTENTION_CLEARS_ITSELF_PLAN.md · Found in: Owner QA §322, 2026-10-16 · Priority: high — found in the Club Tier walks, where the club's money people and builders live in this zone; which release carries it is the owner's call.

What changes

  • Needs attention only holds what is still waiting on you. When a request is answered, approved, declined, withdrawn or expires, its notification leaves Needs attention for everyone it went to — the owner, the admins, the treasurer, every coach on the team. It stays readable under All. Nobody has to press Done on work someone else already finished. The bell's number drops with it.
  • It covers every kind that waits on someone: money-back asks, hand-backs without money, payment requests, "I sent the payment", requests holding a payout, schedule reviews, team moves, assistant-coach approvals, a free coach's payment reminder, the club's failed card payment, and a team marked no-show (clears when the team is checked in, the no-show is undone, or the tournament ends).
  • Four old kinds nothing sends any more leave Needs attention, and any still pinned there are cleared.
  • Notifications reach the people who can act on them. A club's failed card payment goes to its owners only (not admins or coaches); an assistant-coach approval goes to the people who hold Rep Teams for that team's group; a no-show goes to the people running check-in for that tournament. Coaches stop seeing the club's billing problems in their bell.
  • Stage 2 (drawn first, not yet ruled): opening something already handled says exactly what happened — "Accepted by Morgan Ellis · Oct 16, 5:06 p.m." — and Open goes to the record, never a reload of the page you are on.

Why it matters

On dev, 1 of the owner's 10 Needs-attention items was actually waiting. A "needs attention" list that is mostly done work teaches people to ignore it — the one real ask hides among the nine finished ones, and every Open on a finished one is a dead end.

Tradeoffs

  • Something settled by an automatic path (an ask whose Sundays passed overnight, a tournament ending) leaves Needs attention the next time the bell is read, not the instant it happens. Settled by a person, it leaves at once.
  • Admins stop receiving "card payment failed" (bell and email). Billing is owner-only, so they could not act on it.

Success

  • After the change, the owner's Needs attention on dev lists exactly the asks still waiting (today: one).
  • Answering an ask on the schedule clears the treasurer's copy too, with nobody pressing Done.
  • No coach's bell lists the club's failed card payment.
  • A new kind can't join Needs attention without saying how it ends (the build fails).

3. Design — Stage 1 (ruled; no new screens)

3.1 One registry, total over the 'act' kinds

A new module (lib/needs-attention.ts) holds NEEDS_ATTENTION_END: a Record over every 'act' event type, each either a settle check (given a batch of notification rows → for each, still waiting or not) or 'retired'. Typed like NOTIFICATION_CATEGORY: a new 'act' kind fails typecheck until it says how it ends. A unit test holds the same (every 'act' kind has an entry; every entry is an 'act' kind).

3.2 Two moments a row is cleared

  • On the spot — the common settle actions call one helper, clearSettled(org, kind, ids), after their write lands: hand-back accept/decline/withdraw; request approve/decline/withdraw; installment received/taken back; team move approve/decline/withdraw; invite approve/decline/cancel/replace; schedule reply/finalize/delete; check-in check in/undo; the billing webhook's recovery. Fire-and-forget, like notify() — a failed clear never fails the action (the next read catches it).
  • On read (the safety net) — when the bell's list or the Notifications page loads, the server runs the registry over that reader's uncleared 'act' rows, clears every one no longer waiting, and returns the list with them cleared (out of Needs attention, still under All). This catches every path the first moment does not: the daily sweep's lapses, time passing (Sundays started, a tournament ended), a deleted record, a fee change, an RPC called from somewhere new, and every stale row that exists today. Batched: one query per kind present, by ids — a reader's uncleared 'act' rows are a handful.
  • The daily sweep is not touched. It is watched automation, and the read-time net already covers what it lapses.

3.3 What "cleared by the platform" writes

cleared_at = now() and read_at = coalesce(read_at, now()) — a settled item stops counting toward the bell's number. No new column: whether a cleared row was settled or Done by hand is answered live by the registry when Stage 2's reader needs it.

3.4 The index

The on-the-spot clear finds every recipient's copy by org + kind + key. Today's indexes are (org_id, created_at) and (user_id, created_at) WHERE read_at IS NULL. Add notifications (org_id, event_type) WHERE cleared_at IS NULL. Migration + DATA_DICTIONARY + snapshots in the same unit of work.

3.5 No-show (ruling 2)

The check-in route's notice gains metadata { teamId, tournamentId }. Settled when the team's check_in_status leaves no_show, the team is gone, or the tournament has ended (its end_date before today in the club's zone, or its status says it is over). An old notice (no metadata): settled once every tournament of that club that was running on the notice's date has ended.

3.6 The retired four (ruling 3)

They move from 'act' to 'retired' in the registry and out of the Needs-attention zone. A data-only migration clears every uncleared row of those four kinds (dev + prod). Their labels stay so an old row still reads.

3.7 Who receives them (ruling 5) — whoever can act, read from the action's own check

  • Card payment failed → the club's owners (billing is owner-only: app/api/billing/* "owner-only"). Today admins also get its email by default; they cannot act on it, so they stop receiving it. ⚠ Visible change — named in the brief.
  • Assistant coach waiting for approval → members holding Rep Teams (module_rep_teams) whose group scope covers the team — the same check the approve route makes.
  • Team marked no-show → members who run check-in for that tournament (canGate + the tournament scope) — the same check the check-in route makes.

Each writer passes explicit userIds; notify()'s fall-back is unchanged (the wider recipient ticket in TODO.md, "Notification engine — recipient-scoping fixes", keeps the rest).

4. Stage 2 — a settled notification says what happened (DRAWN FIRST)

  • The page doors that read an id (?answer=, ?request=) say the specific outcome instead of guessing, and a deleted request says it was withdrawn rather than opening nothing.
  • Whether the All list marks settled rows: an ask for the owner.
