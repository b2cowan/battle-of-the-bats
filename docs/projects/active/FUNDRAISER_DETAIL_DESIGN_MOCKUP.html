Fundraiser Drill-In Redesign

The accepted approach, drawn alone: a drive expands in place inside the Fundraisers list — entries-first, on the sponsor band's construction — and the separate drill-in view retires. Every frame below is the AFTER; earlier rounds' comparisons live in this artifact's version history.

What ships

  1. One tab, one grammar. Drives and sponsors are two bands of one list, and both open in place. The drill-in view, its back link, its page header, its four tiles and its Rank column all retire. Deep links that used to open the drill-in now land on the tab with that drive's row open, scrolled into view — the way sponsor links already behave.
  2. The board shows entries, not the roster. One row per logged amount, sharing the parent table's columns. Un-logged players are a fraction on the meta line and the Record window's own player list — not eleven rows of dashes. Money logged for a player later removed from the active roster stays visible.
  3. The meta line follows the 08-29 ruling — facts only: the credit rule, the participation fraction, the dates, the tags. Nothing that restates the columns above it. The floating "15% player rebate" line and the dues column are gone entirely.
  4. The doors keep their meanings. Record (primary) opens the one recording window locked to this drive. Edit (the house verb — no more "Settings") opens the existing sheet: name, credit %, dates, status, tags, and the guarded delete. Edit amount and Remove stay on the entry row. A closed drive takes no new money but still allows corrections — exactly today's rules, relocated.
  5. One word for the 15%: credit. "Rebate" leaves this surface's headings and labels wherever it still appears (column heading, Edit sheet's field label).

The Record window and its "where it lands" preview; the Edit sheet's fields, foreseeable-refusal dead-Save and guarded delete (only its title stops saying Settings); the season rollup tiles at the top of the tab; the sponsor band; export; and the phone behaviour — the list already stacks to cards at phone width, and the expansion rows ride that the same way the sponsor band's do.

Accepted tradeoffs

A fully-logged drive opens tall (~one row per player who took part) and scrolls with the page, like a busy sponsor. Rank retires — the entries can simply sort largest-first, which is the leaderboard, wordlessly. If a real season shows a coach living inside one big drive daily, a drives-only drill-in can return; nothing here forecloses it.
