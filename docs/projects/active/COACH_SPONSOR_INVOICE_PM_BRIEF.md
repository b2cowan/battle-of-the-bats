# Sponsor Invoice — PM Brief

**Plan:** [COACH_SPONSOR_INVOICE_PLAN.md](COACH_SPONSOR_INVOICE_PLAN.md) · **Drawings:** https://claude.ai/artifact/5XGAZvyBChL2fZxdzqi6Sc

## What it is
A coach opens a sponsor in Money → Fundraising, presses **Invoice**, checks a few pre-filled details,
and downloads a numbered PDF invoice on the team's own paper — crest, team colour, the same polished
look as the family dues statement — to send to the sponsor's accounting department.

## Why it matters
Businesses pay sponsorships through accounts payable, and accounts payable needs an invoice: a number,
a date, who to pay and how. Today coaches make one in Word or send an email that gets bounced back.
A professional invoice makes a volunteer-run team look like an organization a business is comfortable
paying, and it gets the money in sooner.

## What the coach sees
- One new button on the sponsor record. No new screen, no new menu item.
- The first time, they fill in the sponsor's billing contact and how the team gets paid (cheque address,
  e-transfer email, who answers questions). After that, the team's details fold to one line.
- The invoice number is fixed on first download, counting per season (2026-001, 2026-002 …).
- Once the money arrives, the same button prints the invoice **paid in full** with the payments listed —
  the proof-of-payment accounting departments ask for. Part paid shows the balance due.
- The sponsor record notes "Invoice 2026-003 issued Oct 20", so a treasurer can see who has been billed.

## Who gets it
Anyone who can record the team's money, on plans that include PDF documents (Premium coaches portal, and
club teams whose club plan includes them). Not shown at all on other plans.

## Deliberately not in it
No tax of any kind. Always from the team, never the club. No emailing from FieldLogicHQ (the coach sends
it from their own inbox). Sponsors only.

## Priority
Medium — a Premium value add with a clear "this looks professional" moment for coaches and their sponsors.

## Success
- A coach can produce a sponsor invoice in under a minute the first time and one press after that.
- The invoice prints on team paper with no figure that disagrees with the team's books.
- No support question about tax or "who is this invoice from".
