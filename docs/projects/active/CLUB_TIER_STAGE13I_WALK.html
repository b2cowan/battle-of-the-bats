You can always tell where you are and get back

Written 2026-11-06, after Stage 13i Session A (committed f279e6963), Session B (committed 403341b71) and Session C (the help words). One purpose: on every screen the sidebar has the same rows with one lit, the page you are on is a tab under its place’s title, and the way back is named.

What this walk is for

Stage 13i made the club sidebar list places only. It no longer grows when you open a team or a season (the owner’s ran 10 / 21 / 18 / 15 rows before). Each place shows its pages as the tab row under its title, the one Accounting and Families already had. A team and a season are places of their own, with a named way back (“← Teams”, “← Seasons”). Organization’s tiles became tabs. Plan & billing stays in the sidebar’s foot (your Ask 4 B).

It passes when

  • 1–5 The owner, at a desk: the same 10 rows on every screen, one lit; every place’s tabs; into and out of a team and a season by the header and by the row; the season switch; Organization’s tabs; Plan & billing still in the foot; Back and a bookmark land on the same tab; the folded sidebar opens no menus.
  • 6 The owner, on a phone: the bar unchanged, the tabs scroll sideways with the one you’re on in view, and the More sheet lists each place once.
  • 7–8 The registrar (4 rows) and the treasurer (3 rows), desk and phone.

Known before the walk (not defects of this walk)

  • The registrar’s Organization has two tabs, The board and Venue library. The build prompt expected the registrar to have a single door with no tab row. Your Session A answer added “The board” (names and roles, never an email) in Members’ place, so the registrar now has two tabs.
  • On a phone the season’s arrow opens the standard pick-one sheet (the Year pill’s own sheet), not the retired season strip’s sheet.
  • Teams & draft’s division picker no longer shows each division’s accepted count: the amber mark there means waiting.
  • A team’s and a season’s Schedule tab can show a small empty gap above the calendar on a desk when there are no buttons for its row.
  • Someone with no Organization tab at all would have no way to The board. No role on a club plan hits this, because every role holds Venue library.
  • “Also on your plan” is always named, even over one row (a build reading; you didn’t rule it).
  • Fixed in Session C by the layout sweep: a season’s Overview scrolled sideways by 88 px on a phone, and Registrations’ toolbar ran past the edge at tablet width (Export and Add registration out of reach). Both are fixed; steps 3 and 6 walk them.
  • Teams & draft’s team cards spill by 10 px inside their header at 768 and 1440. That was there before 13i and isn’t walked here.
