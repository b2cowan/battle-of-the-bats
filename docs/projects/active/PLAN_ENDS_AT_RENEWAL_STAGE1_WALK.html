A cancel waits for the renewal date: the owner, the emails, the operator

Written 2026-11-02 for Stage 1 (Parts A, B and C: ebffbc1c7, b6ae3de78, 8ce1f4a14). This is the one owner walk the plan puts at the end of Stage 1. It runs on the dev site against real Stripe test-mode subscriptions.

What this walk is for

Before Stage 1, cancelling a paid plan ended it at the click and took away time the club had already paid for. Now a cancel waits for the renewal date. The club stays open until then, and the owner can keep the plan any time before. Three emails tell them what is happening: the date it ends, a reminder seven days before, and a note when they keep it. The operator sees the same waiting cancel on the club record, with Keep and End now. When Stripe’s end-of-period message goes missing, the club shows as overdue, with a Finish button that reads Stripe before it changes anything. This walk proves that whole loop, from both sides.

It passes when

  • Cancelling a paid plan leaves the club open and shows “Ending” with the date. Nothing ends at the click.
  • Keep League undoes it from the billing page, from the email’s button and from Stripe’s own billing page, and each keep sends one “will renew” email.
  • A cheaper plan chosen before the cancel comes back when the plan is kept.
  • Each email arrives once, with the right dates, even when the daily job runs twice.
  • The operator’s Cancel and Keep email the owner only when the box is ticked.
  • An overdue ending is listed. Finish ends it only when Stripe says it ended, with the data kept from the day it should have ended. When Stripe still bills, the club is untouched and only our record changes.
  • End now still ends a plan at once.

Not in this walk

  • Stripe ending a plan by itself on the renewal date. That date is a month away. Stage 3’s test-clock rehearsal covers it, and unit tests cover the code.
  • A Coaches Portal (Premium). No portal on dev has a Stripe test subscription. It runs the same code, and unit tests pin its Premium words. Say if you want a portal part added.
  • The online-payments lines in the Cancel windows. None of the walk clubs takes payments online, and the lines show only for a club that does. Unit tests pin the words.
  • Downgrading to the free plan at renewal (Stage 2) and the help articles (Stage 4).
