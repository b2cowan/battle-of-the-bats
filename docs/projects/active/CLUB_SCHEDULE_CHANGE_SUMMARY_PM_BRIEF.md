# PM brief — unsent schedule changes, summarized

**What:** when a club changes a schedule it has already sent, the changes waiting to go out are now summarized by team
("15U AA gains 18 practices · Tue and Thu · 7:00 p.m. · Prospect Park · Diamond 2 · Dec 1 – Jan 28") instead of one line
per date. The same summary appears on the schedule's Needs attention card, in the Send the changes window, and in the
email each team's coaches receive (where every date is still written out, grouped by month).

**Why it matters:** adding a block of time to a schedule produced 35+ near-identical lines that no one could check, a
confirm window whose most important line (money coming off what a team owes) was buried at the end of a paragraph, and
an email of 17 bullets per team. The admin can now see at a glance which teams are affected and how, and confirm the
same thing they just read.

**Customer impact:** club admins sending schedule changes; team coaches reading the change email. No new data, no new
recipients, no money changes — only how the same facts are laid out.

**Priority:** a quality fix to shipped scheduling (the Add time flow made long lists common).

**Success:** a schedule change for a whole season reads in a few lines per team; the Send window fits two teams in its
first view; the money line is on its own line; the page and the window name places the same way.
