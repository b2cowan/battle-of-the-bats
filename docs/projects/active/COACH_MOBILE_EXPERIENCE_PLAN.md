## 0 · What this is, and how it was read

A holistic re-evaluation of the Premium Coaches Portal **as a phone product**, in the shape of the Practices and Skills & Goals re-evaluations (September 2026): a walk of every screen a head coach reaches on a phone in a game week and a practice week, each one measured; a set of shape questions for the owner (Keep · Reshape · Not yet); and a stage ladder, each stage drawn, ruled, built and walked in turn.

**Method (2026-09-19, `dev`, signed in as the UAT head coach on the UAT Test Team, game day on the fixture).** Playwright drove a 390×844 phone context (iPhone 14/15 class; touch, DPR 2) through 34 screens and a 360×780 context through six of them. Per screen, the browser's own geometry was read (never a screenshot): page height in screens, words on the first screen, controls under the 44px tap floor, words set at ≤11px (the ladder's two smallest steps), pinned/sticky chrome and its height. Interactions were driven by hand: the More sheet to its bottom, the Schedule's three views and the event sheet, a chat room and its composer, the live plan, the lineup grid swiped, the console scrolled, the recording page, a roster row tapped on its blank side. **No sideways scroll on any screen at either width.** The captures and measurements are embedded in the hub's walk tab.

**Standing rulings this plan works inside (not reopened):** the four primary tabs (2026-06-29) — asked about, recommended kept; the masthead's design arc and *the desktop never collapses* (2026-08-19); the four page-action house rules (2026-08-23); the tap floor as a touch-only rule (2026-08-19) and the 641–768 band (2026-08-27); the eight-step type ladder (2026-08-28); the Practices (stages 0–7) and Skills & Goals (stages 0–4) re-evaluations — read here as phone screens only; the Lineups deep dive and §206/§207 (this week).

## 2 · The rules across the walk (Standing back, S.1–S.7)

- **S.1 The bar is right; the roads are wrong.** The four words are learned in a minute; what cannot be learned is that Practice plans is under the fold of a popover, that the day's lineup is a 32px chip, that attendance on the day is 1,150px into the sheet. Fix More (Q 0.1), the card (Q 1.2) and the sheet's order (Q 2.3) before touching the tabs (Q 0.2).
- **S.2 The screen budget.** One identity line at rest (~110px pinned), never two headers (Q 0.3, Q 3.1).
- **S.3 Today first.** Every list mixing past and future opens on today with the past folded above; the Schedule is the last exception (Q 2.1).
- **S.4 One figure, one qualifier.** A phone tile carries a label, a figure and one line (Q 1.1). This is the direct answer to "are we displaying too much on mobile" — the tiles are; the cards mostly are not.
- **S.5 The field floor.** On screens read standing up (console, run, attendance-taking): nothing under 12px, the looked-for value at 14 or above (Q 5.1). A per-surface rule like the tap floor; the ladder untouched.
- **S.6 "Saved" is a fact in the title row**, never a pill over the work (A3).
- **S.7 A table whose columns fit stays a table** on a phone (Q 9.1).

### 4.3 A3 — Saved is transient (revised 2026-09-20)
The autosave state (Unsaved changes · Saving… · ✓ Saved · an error with retry, in the error colour) is a pill at the window's foot — bottom-right on desktop, above the bottom nav on a phone — that is there only while it has something to say: it appears on an edit, lingers about two seconds after the save lands, and fades. **Only the error persists.** Nothing at rest. On the lineup builder, the practice plan, circuits, plan templates and the attendance list. One shared piece, not five.

*History:* drawn and ruled first as a quiet word at the right end of the page-title row, built that morning, and revised the same day on the owner's desktop read — the title row is pinned nowhere, so the word scrolled away exactly when a coach deep in the grid wanted it, and the one state that must never scroll away is a failed save. A transient pill keeps the window's corner (visible wherever the page is scrolled) without being furniture over the work.

### 4.4 A4 — the field floor
A per-surface rule beside the tap floor: the console, the run screen and the event sheet's attendance list declare a field floor — no text under 12px (support), the looked-for value (position, name, score) at 14px (body) or above. Enforced by `check:layout` as a new rule on the declared surfaces. The eight-step ladder is untouched; no ninth size is minted. "Diamond 1" for the field chip; "0 – 0 · tap to score" before a run is entered.
