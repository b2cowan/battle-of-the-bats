Buying from inside the product

All four parts of S4, built 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-22. One purpose: an organization that already runs tournaments here can buy or move to a bigger plan from its own Plan & billing, and nothing it has is lost on the way.

What this walk is for

A free Tournament organization’s Change plan now offers every plan above it, with the true trial before it pays. A paying Tournament Plus organization moves to League Plus on the subscription it already has. A paid plan bought on top of free time no longer ends the free time: when the paid plan ends, the organization goes back to its free plan instead of closing. Part 4 adds the line under For Tournament Organizers’ League Plus and Club cards, the gap under the free-time line, and an email to you when a second subscription slips through.

It passes when every step reads as written. Unit tests hold every gate state. Stripe test-mode proofs ran on throwaway organizations for the starts, the move, the fall-back and the second-subscription alert, and every test customer was deleted afterwards. /review, /billing, /accounting, /legal, /marketing, /design and /docs ran, and their fixes are in.

Not in this walk: the onboarding window’s new sentence. Today that window opens only for a League Plus organization, which by your ruling reads today’s words unchanged; tests hold the other cases. Making a real second subscription (two tabs paying in the same seconds) is also left out: the proof made one twice, and part G checks the emails it sent you.

Before you start

  • Commit Part 4, then push dev so staging rebuilds. Stripe’s test events go to staging, which writes the same dev database. Until staging carries S4, it runs the old rule, and buying Club on the free-time club in part D would end the free time.
  • Restart the dev server (Part 4 adds files).
  • Platform admin on dev asks for two-step: have the authenticator for uat-platform@fieldlogichq.ca ready, or switch staff two-step off on dev for the walk.
