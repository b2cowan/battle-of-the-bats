One attendance record walk · what changed

A parent and a coach now set the same answer for a player: In, Late, Maybe or Out, the latest change wins, and every answer shows who set it and when. This walk replaces §526 from its section 4 on, and re-checks sections 1 to 3 only for what changed for parents. About 50 steps, an hour and a half. Every step says what to open and do (Do) and what you should see (See).

    See

    See
