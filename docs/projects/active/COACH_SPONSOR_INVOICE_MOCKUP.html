Sponsor invoice

A coach opens a sponsor, presses Invoice, checks a few details and downloads a PDF on the team's own paper. The sponsor's accounting department gets a numbered invoice that says who to pay, how, and by when.

Ruled · always from the team Ruled · no tax line or tax wording Ruled · titled "Invoice", never a donation receipt

What changes for a coach

One new button on the sponsor

The sponsor record gains Invoice beside Edit sponsorship. Nothing else on the money screens moves. There is no new menu item and no new screen.

Typed once, reused after

The sponsor's billing contact is kept on the sponsor. How sponsors pay the team (cheque address, e-transfer email, who answers questions) is kept for the team. After the first invoice, that part folds to one line.

A number that stays put

The first download gives the invoice its number and issue date. Downloading again reprints the same number, so the sponsor never sees two numbers for one bill.

Who sees it

Anyone who can record the team's money, on plans that already include PDF documents, like the family dues statement. On other plans the button isn't there at all. It isn't shown greyed out.

Each has a recommendation. "All as recommended" is a complete answer. Pick any you want to change, then copy your answers back into the chat.

Design review applied

The drawings went through the design review before you saw them. It changed these, as proposals for you to accept with the asks:

  • Required stars are plain, in the label's ink. The window is the portal's medium width, and its section titles are sentence case.
  • The window title is "Invoice" with the sponsor's name above it, so a long name can't wrap the title on a phone.
  • After the first invoice, the team's payment details fold to one line with Change.
  • The Amount hint is plain text. A link out of an unsaved window would lose what was typed.
  • The close ask and a refusal are drawn. The foot sentence says what Download keeps.
  • The printed page dropped a rotated PAID stamp, because the house documents have none. "Paid in full" leads the subtitle instead.
  • The printed page dropped a repeated details block. The figures are invoiced · paid · balance due, like the dues statement.
  • The From line respects the team's own document setting. The room's delete control is drawn with its real icon.

Not in this round

  • Tax of any kind. Ruled out. No tax field, line or wording.
  • Club teams invoicing as the club. From the coaches portal it is always the team (ruled). A club-issued invoice would belong to the club's own money screens, as a separate decision.
  • Invoices for anything except a sponsor. Families already get their dues statement. Grants and other income have no invoice.
  • A closed season. Invoicing is a money tool, so it lives on the working season only. A closed season's money shelf keeps showing the sponsor and its arrivals as it does today.
  • Help guide and demo. The fundraising help article gets an Invoice section at build time. The demo picks this up on its own.
