On a phone, the bar names the page and the team rides the small line

On a phone, the bar at the top of every Coaches Portal team page now names the page you are on (“Roster”, “Money”, “2025 Season”), and the page no longer repeats its own title under it — its buttons stay. Under the name, a small line carries your team, a chip when the season is not everyday (Draft, Game day, Complete) and your record; with two or more teams, a tap on the name or that line opens Your teams, now with each team’s crest, colour or initials and its season. One level down (a player, a game, a lineup, a plan) a back arrow at the bar’s left leads back and the bar names where it goes; the game console lost its own arrow for it. The “?” stays at the bar’s right end; More → Help is still the full guide. On Money, Insights, Skills & goals, Lineups and Practice plans the tabs stay pinned under the bar as you scroll. A tablet (641 to 900 wide) gets the same bar with the club and season on its line and its status at the right. Eight walks, one purpose each. About 30 minutes.

Before you start

  • No script to run. Every step uses the test data as it stands.
  • Walk the parts in order, 1 to 8. Each opens with a stop check.
  • The drawings these screens were built to: the Coaches Portal on a phone.
  • Your rulings it was built to: F1–F8, all A — one level down the bar names the way back and the record keeps its name in the page; the small line is the team, one chip, the record; the “?” stays in the bar and More → Help stays the full guide (this replaces the Phase 4b note that it would move into More); the tabs pin under the bar; the Overview keeps its club · season line; the tablet keeps its status; the game page joins the one-level-down list (and, at the build, “the bar’s arrow; remove the console’s”); Your teams uses the computer list’s rows.
  • This replaces older walk checks that look for a one-line team bar on a phone (name · record · “?”), a team name that opens Your teams at the top of the bar, the page’s own title under the bar on a phone, tabs that scroll away on a phone, or the game console’s own back arrow.

Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • The bar is 53 px on every page at 390 and at 768 (the tournament phone’s bar is the same), with scripts off exactly as after load — the page’s name, the small line and the pinned rows’ offset are in what the server sends. The “?” arrives a beat after load into space already held for it, so nothing moves.
  • One visible heading per screen: the bar’s on a named page, the record’s own title one level down (a player, a lineup, a plan). The game page has no heading of its own, as before this build.
  • Pinned on a phone: Money and Insights tabs at 53 to 98; the Ledger’s toolbar under them from 98 to 226 (226 px pinned, plus the 72 px bottom bar: 35% of an 844 screen); the lineup builder’s inning stepper at 53; the player’s tabs dock at 53 once scrolled past.
  • Your teams on a phone: the rows are the computer list’s (tile, name, season); Escape closes it and returns to the line; the phone’s Back closes it without leaving the page; a tap on the dim closes it.
  • The layout check on Overview, Money, Roster, Lineups, Insights, Season’s End, a player, the game console and the team sheet at 390: no new findings.
  • Not walked, for want of a fixture: a season in Draft, a game today (the Game day chip), a long team name, a coach whose every team is between seasons. The build’s tests cover the words.

Your time goes on judgment: does the bar read as “where am I”, is the small line enough to know which team, and do the pinned tabs earn their room on a phone?
