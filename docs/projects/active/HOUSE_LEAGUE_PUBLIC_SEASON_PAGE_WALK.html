An old season’s page follows the season, and points to the next one

Written 2026-11-04 for the public season page build (built, reviewed, help updated). One purpose: from the day registration closes, a season’s public page stops selling the season and shows its dates and standings, and an old season’s page names the club’s newer season that is taking sign-ups.

What this walk is for

While a season opens soon or takes sign-ups, its page is today’s sign-up page. From the day registration closes (the club closed it, or its close date passed), the price, the registration dates, the “spots filled” tiles and the grey notice box go. One line under the title gives the season’s dates. Once a game has a score, each age group’s first three teams show, first place in bold, with Full table →. The band reads Standings, and Final standings only once the club completes the season. An old season’s page (past its last day, or finished) shows a slim card above the title when the club has a newer season taking sign-ups today.

It passes when

  • A season taking sign-ups looks exactly as it did before.
  • A closed, playing, ended or finished season shows no price, no registration dates, no spots and no grey box, and one dates line that reads “Runs”, “Ran” or “This season has concluded. It ran …”.
  • The top three match the standings page, ties read T-1, an unscored age group says “Standings start with the first score.”, and an age group with standings off or an unpublished schedule is not listed.
  • The card names one newer open season with its own button, names two with a button to the house league page, and is absent when there is none or the season is not old.
  • “Already signed up? Email me my link” is on every page.

Known before the walk

  • The fixture’s dates are fixed for November 2026; walk it before Dec 12, 2026, or the playing season turns into an ended one.
  • Three or more newer seasons (“· 1 more”), a season with only one of its dates, a one-day season and a season stuck on “Registration closed” past its last day are proved by unit tests, not walked.
  • §450 is reserved for the 9f-2 walk (Start next season), so this walk is §451.

Not in this walk

  • The standings page and the schedule page (unchanged), the house league index and the club home (unchanged), Start next season (9f-2).
