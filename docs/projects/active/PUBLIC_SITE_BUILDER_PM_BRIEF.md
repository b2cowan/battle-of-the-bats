# Public site builder — PM brief

**Status:** drawn and ruled 2026-10-21 (all nine asks). Rides the Club release. Plan: `PUBLIC_SITE_BUILDER_PLAN.md`.

## What it is

The place a club makes its public website. The club sees its home page beside the controls while it works. Pick a
colour and the bar changes. Hide a section and it disappears. Everything families see is set here: the logo, colours,
type, banner photo, home page, About text, contacts and documents, and whether the site is online.

## Why it matters

Today the site is set up in two places. The words live in the Public site editor, a long page of nine cards. The look
lives in Settings, behind a Save button that admins can't open. Nothing shows the site while you make it. Setting up a
club's website should leave the club saying "that was easy, and it looks great". Today it leaves them hunting.

## What changes for each role

- **Owner:** the whole job in one place, with a short checklist on the first visit ending in **Go live**. Settings
  shrinks to the club's name, web address and deletion.
- **Admin:** can now change the logo, colours, type and banner. Still can't turn the site off or change its address;
  sees those as plain text.
- **Families:** nothing changes for them directly. Clubs that can see their site will build better ones.

## Priority

Inside the Club release, part of the club's public face (Stage 4). It replaces the editor shipped in 4c-1.

## Success looks like

- A new club goes from nothing to a live, branded site in one sitting, without opening Settings.
- An admin can rebrand the site without asking the owner.
- No one has to open the live site in another tab to see what they changed.
