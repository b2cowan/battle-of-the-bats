Schedules: Final and Not Billed

Round 2 drawings for review. Nothing is built. Frames are true size: open this page on the screen you use; scroll a frame sideways if your window is narrower.

What changed since round 1. The status is the point. A schedule's state tells the club whether coaches know about it and whether work is outstanding, so every schedule shows its state as a pill in the header: Draft, In review or Final. Whether it was billed is a second question, answered inside the schedule, so the "Not billed" chip and the extra words in the top line are gone.

This reverses the earlier rule that a sent schedule says nothing more. That rule is why a final schedule shows no status today.

Now every row answers "do coaches have this?" Draft = not sent. In review = sent, waiting on coaches. Final = sent and settled. Amber = it has changed since it was sent and the coaches have the old version. A schedule that is final and unchanged has just the one pill.

One thing to look at: an ended schedule that is Final shows the pill too. That is accurate, but it means the Ended group is a column of green pills. Ask 1 covers whether Final should be green or neutral.

Changed

Why show both Final and amber, instead of letting amber replace it? The schedule is still Final: the coaches received it and it was never reopened. The amber says there is outstanding work on top. If amber replaced Final, a glance would read "something is wrong" with no sign of what state it is in. This is Ask 2.

Who sees it: everyone who can open a schedule, with no money access needed. The state is not a money fact.

Your direction settled most of round 1. These are the calls that remain. Each has my recommendation.

What your ruling already decided (shown so it can be corrected)

  • Every schedule shows its state as a pill: Draft, In review, Final. A final schedule is no longer silent.
  • The pill is on the schedule's header and on every row of the Schedules list.
  • "Not billed" is not a header pill, not a list pill and not in the top line. Billing is read inside the schedule.
  • Not billed gets no to-do in Needs attention.
  • The permits-paid-by-club against billed-to-coaches summary is parked for the accounting conversation.

Ask 1 · What does the Final pill look like?

  • A · Green, the shipped "good" tone. Easy to tell from Draft (grey) and In review (olive) down a list. The Ended group will read as a column of green.
  • B · Neutral grey, the same as Draft. Quietest, but Draft and Final differ only by the word.

Recommend A.

Ask 2 · When a final schedule has been edited, show "Final" as well as the amber pill?

  • A · Both, Final first then amber. The state stays visible and the amber adds the outstanding work.
  • B · Amber replaces Final. One pill, but you lose the state while it is changed.

Recommend A. It does put two pills on a changed row, which fits on the list and in the header.

Ask 3 · Keep the Bill tile change, so an unbilled schedule stops showing $400.00?

  • A · Yes. The tile reads "Not billed" when the schedule is final with no bill. It is the only place billing is mentioned, and it only appears once someone opens the schedule.
  • B · Leave it. Park it with the accounting conversation. The tile keeps showing the permit's price as if it were a bill.

Recommend A. It is a small change, and it is the original reason you could not tell your test schedule had no bill.

Ask 4 · Accept the phone cost?

  • A · Yes. The pill goes on the phone's pinned bar like the other states, adding about 17px to a final schedule's bar.
  • B · Not on the bar. A phone shows the pill on the list only, and the schedule page keeps today's bar.

Recommend A, for consistency. A state that shows on every other schedule and not on the final ones would be the original defect again.

Who sees what

Draft, In review, Final and Changed since sent: everyone who can open the schedules, on a computer and a phone. "Not billed" on the Bill tile: people who see money, on a computer.

After you approve

Your walk step ("It is Final, has no bill, and the Fair shares area has a Bill the teams door") becomes checkable: a Final pill in the header and on the list, a "Not billed" tile, and the Bill the teams door one click away in Fair shares. The in-app help for Schedules gets updated, and the demo clubs follow on their own. I will log the new rule (every schedule shows its state) so later screens follow it.
