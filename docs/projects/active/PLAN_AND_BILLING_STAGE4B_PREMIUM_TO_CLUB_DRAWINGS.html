A Premium coach starts their own club

First pass · October 30, 2026 · drawings for the owner and the second review.

What the coach does · draft words · [from the plan] Stage 4b and §6 B

From Plan & billing, the owner chooses to start their own club. Joining somebody else’s club remains a separate choice. They name their club and check its web address. Their existing team stays, and they keep their roles, including head coach.

A paying coach sees the charge today, including credit for unused Premium time, before confirming. Their billing interval stays the same, with no second trial. A coach on free time instead sees that their Premium free days end, then starts Club’s 90-day trial with a card at checkout.

While the change finishes, the screen says so. When ready, the coach reaches club setup with their existing team already there. The Admin door returns in their Coaches Portal. Until Club opens for sale, the new plan row says “Contact us to move”.
