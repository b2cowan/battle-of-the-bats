What each choice does, and the hint under the field

  • The time at the permits’ prices · $7,650.00. Unchanged. Hint: "Or a total, or each team’s amount, that you choose".
  • A total I choose (was "A part of it"). The Amount box appears, exactly as today. The total is shared by the time, and it can be more or less than the permits. Hint: "Shared by the time each team got".
  • An amount I choose for each team (new). Every Share becomes a box, starting from the time. Hint: "Each team starts at what its time costs. Change any of them."

Why not a second Split choice, as first proposed? Split sits below "Bill the teams for" and "Price the time". If the switch lived in Split, choosing it would have to remove fields above where you clicked, and the window would jump. One field that answers "how much" is easier to read. This is Ask 1.

The smaller version is included. "Adjust one team" is this choice with one box changed. Building it separately would mean a second control for the same idea.

The closing line under the table is the one place the bill is compared with what the permits cost. Today it is plain grey text whether the bill matches or not. Proposed: matching stays quiet; over or under becomes the amber callout, the portal's white panel with a coloured edge. It is a warning only. Nothing is disabled and nothing asks you to confirm.

Ask 4 · A team's time changes after a hand-set bill is sent

  • A · The change is billed at the permit's price, on top of the amount you set (drawn in tab 4). This is what every sent bill does today. "Send the changes" names the hand-set teams, and its existing "Bill the corrections" switch lets the club skip it for a flat deal.
  • B · Never correct a hand-set bill. This is what I recommended in chat. Once a bill is sent its terms never change, so the club would have no way to bill a team that gained time. "Leave it to the club" means leaving it with no tool.

Recommend A. I changed this after reading how sent bills work: B needs a new "change a sent bill" tool, and A needs only a label.
