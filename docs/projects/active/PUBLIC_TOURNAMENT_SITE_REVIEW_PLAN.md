# Public tournament sites — review, simplify the phone, sponsors, and the club link

**Status: LOGGED 2026-10-24 as a follow-up project. Nothing drawn, ruled or built.** Raised by the owner while
walking Club Tier Stage 4d (the club's sponsors). Not part of Club Tier Stage 4; rides after it.

## Why

The owner walked a public tournament site on a phone and found it **extremely text-heavy in places, not easy on
the eyes.** The club's public site has been redrawn (Stage 4) and the first look / marketing pages went warm
(Public Face Warm); the tournament pages are the public surface that has not had a pass.

## What the owner asked for (verbatim intent)

1. **A review of the public tournament sites** — content and flow — to see what can be improved.
2. **Mainly: simplify the phone experience.** Text-heavy areas first.
3. **Sponsor sections on tournament pages**, as the club now has (Stage 4d).
4. **Tournament ↔ club link** (see "The club link" below) — owner wants it considered; the decision of *when* is
   below.

## The club link — two asks, split on purpose

Raised 2026-10-24:

- **A. A "Tournaments" tab in the club's top row**, shown only while the club has a tournament, with an
  on/off switch in the Public site editor like every other part of the club's site. *Today the top row is Home ·
  Teams · Tryouts · House league · Past tournaments; there is no tab for current or upcoming tournaments, only a
  section on the home page.* This is a **club-site** change (Stage 4 territory): it extends the existing
  show/hide/order rows and the tab list.
- **B. The way from a tournament to the club's public page, as advertising for the club.** *Today a tournament
  page links the organization's name back to the club's home, on the live site only.* The owner's point: a club
  wants people attending its tournament to **find the club**, not just have a back link. This is a
  **tournament-page** change and belongs inside this review, because *where* the club shows on a phone page
  depends on how that page is simplified.

**Ruled 2026-10-24 (owner): A is Club Tier Stage 4f, built after the 4e web address is finished; B stays in this
project.** A's record lives in the Club Tier plan §6 Stage 4 ("4f").

## Open questions for the walk (to be drawn, not answered here)

- What a family actually does on a tournament page on a phone (find a game, find a field, see the standings) and
  which sections get in the way of that.
- Which text blocks collapse, move behind a tap, or go away.
- Sponsor strip: same idea and limits as the club's (Stage 4d), or the tournament's own list? Whose sponsors when a
  club runs the tournament?
- The club's presence on the page: a band, a card at the foot, or the existing name link made louder.
- Tournament pages currently keep their own look by ruling (Stage 4b-1, Ask 8's carve-out) — whether that holds is
  one of the asks, not an assumption.
