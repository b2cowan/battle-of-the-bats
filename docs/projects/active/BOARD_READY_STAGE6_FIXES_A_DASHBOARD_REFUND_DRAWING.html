A refund the club makes in its own Stripe dashboard: the books take it at once, and the club finishes it on the deal

Today a refund made in Stripe’s own dashboard only raises a notice: the deal still reads paid, the books still hold the money, and the deal’s Refund button would offer the same money again. This page draws the fix, built from the shipped deal page and its refund window (as committed Nov 24). The example is the same as the card-refund drawing: Harbour Dental’s U9 division sponsorship, $1,000.00 plus 13% HST, $1,130.00, paid by card on Mar 3. On Mar 20 the club gives $200.00 back from its Stripe dashboard; on Mar 22 Dana Ruiz finishes it on the deal. Click any tag to read the build rule behind it, and any blue number to jump to its ask. Words marked DRAFT go through /marketing and /legal. The accounting review behind it is dated Nov 25.

Finish the refund: the amount is Stripe’s, the reason is the club’s, and the button says what happens

The callout’s button, or the payment row, opens the payment’s window. The refund made in Stripe sits in it as the shipped refund block, and “Finish the refund” sits at its foot beside Refund. Finishing is a question raised over that window, built like the shipped refund question: Stripe’s amount and date are fixed, and the club writes the reason the business reads. Finishing issues the credit note, dated today, and emails it with the shipped refund email. On a full refund the club also says what happens to the deal. Whoever may refund on a deal may finish one.

If nobody finishes it

The books are already right. With no credit note the club’s GST/HST is not reduced, which is the safe side for its return. The business hears nothing from FieldLogicHQ. The callout and the notice stay until someone finishes it. Pressing Done on the notice hides the notice only; the deal still shows the refund as “To finish”.

When the sponsor paid the processing fee, a refund in Stripe can return the fee too, and the deal says so

The same deal, but Harbour Dental paid the $41.05 fee and its $5.34 HST: they were charged $1,176.39. A refund from the deal never returns the fee. A refund in Stripe can be any amount up to $1,176.39. The rule: the sponsorship goes back first, and the fee only once all of the sponsorship has gone back. The tax is the same either way, since the fee carries the same 13%. The club’s own processing cost does not change: Stripe keeps its fee, and FieldLogicHQ keeps its fee (to be confirmed by a test before the build).

Two dates: the books take the day the money left, the credit note takes the day it is issued

The General ledger lines are written by themselves when Stripe says the refund went through, dated Mar 20, the day the money left. That keeps the books, Payouts and the bank check on the same day as Stripe. The credit note is issued on Mar 22, when Dana finishes it, and says the card was refunded on Mar 20. If Mar 20 falls in a year already closed, the lines take today, as every sponsor line already does.

A trap the build must keep shut

The daily retry asks Stripe again about any refund from the deal that has been waiting over 15 minutes without Stripe’s reference. A refund made in Stripe arrives with its reference already, and the build keeps it out of that retry, so it is never sent twice.
