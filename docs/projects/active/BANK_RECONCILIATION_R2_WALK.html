A treasurer's month end: set up the club's bank account, check a statement to $0.00, undo it, and add a second account

What this walk is for

The Bank tab is where a club treasurer checks the books against the bank statement. Until now it has only been driven by scripts. This walk is the human half: you do one treasurer's month end on a phone, the way a volunteer would at the kitchen table. You set up the account, tick the statement line by line (including Stripe payouts that arrive as one deposit), add the bank fee the books don't have, and mark it checked. Then you undo it, add a second account at a desk, and void the fee to see what a checked statement does when the books change under it.

It passes when

  • The first-time setup takes the starting day, the balance then and the lines still outstanding then, and opens a statement.
  • On a phone, the Difference stays pinned at the top and Mark checked stays in reach at the bottom while you tick, and every row ticks with one tap.
  • A Stripe payout is one row that opens to show the lines inside it, and a payout that doesn't add up names why.
  • With everything ticked the Difference is $12.40; the bank fee brings it to $0.00; only then can you mark it checked.
  • Undo needs a reason, reopens the statement with its ticks, and the list says who undid it and why.
  • A second account takes a book with it, and the Account pill switches between the two.
  • Voiding the fee in the Ledger shows on the checked statement: the line is marked and the Difference goes back to $12.40.

Not in this walk

  • "Changed since it was checked" (the amber chip and Look again). Nothing in the product records a change yet; that is R3's job. A script checked the screen with a made-up change.
  • A member who can read the Bank tab but not work in it. On the club side there is no such member: whoever can open Accounting can also move club money. The read-only view will be walked on the coach side (R5), where read and write are separate permissions.
  • The closed fiscal year fee window. The scripted walk checked it (a statement ending in the closed 2025–26 year offers today's date).
  • The coach's Bank view (R4–R6), the help articles and the wording pass (R3).
