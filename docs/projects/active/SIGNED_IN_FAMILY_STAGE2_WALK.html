A new coach registers: one email, an honest finish screen

Written 2026-11-02 for Stage 2 (Parts 4–5 and the Part 7 fixes). One purpose: a coach who registers a team on the Register form gets one email with the confirm button inside it, and the screen after Submit says what is really next.

What this walk is for

Before this build a new coach got two emails seconds apart: “Registration Received”, which said their portal account “is ready” while it was still waiting for its email, and a separate “Confirm your FieldLogicHQ email”. The finish screen then offered Create Coaches Portal Account for the account they had just made, which dead-ended at “Confirm your email before signing in”. Now the server files the entry, tells the organizer, makes the waiting account, and sends one email: the registration, with a Confirm my email button in it. The finish screen leads with “Confirm your email to open your portal”, with a quiet “Already confirmed? Sign in” and no big button.

It passes when

  • A new coach gets exactly one email, “We’ve got your registration”, with the confirm button in it, and no separate confirm email.
  • Pressing the button signs them in and opens the team’s registration in their Coaches Portal; pressing it a second time explains itself.
  • The finish screen never offers to create an account, and says “open the link we sent” only when a link really went.
  • The waitlist gets the same: one email, the same button.
  • With the organizer’s “Registration received” email off, the coach still gets a way in: one short confirm email.
  • The join page asks for the address rather than reading it from the link.
  • Every screen reads well at 390 px and at a desk.

Not in this walk

  • The team pay page’s partly hidden receipt address (Part 6). It gets its own short walk.
  • The form’s limits. Reaching them takes about 30 registrations from one computer. Unit tests pin them, and the “Registration Failed” card they show is unchanged.
  • A customised registration email. Only a platform admin can customise it, and none is customised on dev. That case sends two emails by design, and unit tests pin it.
  • The 1-hour expiry itself. Part C uses a link that has already been used, which opens the same sign-in notice.

Before you start

Verification on
In .env.local add a line REQUIRE_SIGNUP_EMAIL_VERIFICATION=true. Leave RESEND_API_KEY= empty for Part A; Part B has you fill it with the dev Resend key. When you save the file, the dev terminal should print a line saying it reloaded the environment. If it doesn’t, stop and restart the server (npm run dev).
Fixture
In the project folder: node --env-file=.env.local scripts/seed-stripe-walk-tournament.mjs --inbox <your plain address>. It builds Stripe Walk Invitational under UAT Rep Club: Walk 13U (empty, so you get a normal Pending entry) and Walk 15U (full, so you land on the waitlist). The organizer’s alerts go to your plain address.
Coach addresses
Your own inbox with a plus part: +s2c0 to +s2c4 (for example you+s2c1@gmail.com). Each becomes an account, and the fixture’s reset does not remove accounts, so a second run needs new tags (+s2d1 …). Any password of 8 or more characters; use the same one throughout.
Team names
S2 Team 0 to S2 Team 4, matching the tag.
Email not there?
Wait 2 minutes, then look in Resend › Emails (search the address).
