9f drawn: the end of a season, and the next one

Three different things share the name “Season end”. Archive does almost nothing. The finished record is mostly built and has two small gaps. Start next season is the one large piece and the one a club will thank us for. The first tab says how I’d split and order them, and where I disagree with the drawing prompt. Every claim about today’s code was re-read; the red flags open what the code does.

Nothing in 9f is ruled yet

  • The plan’s 9f row has no asks. Seventeen are drawn here (9f·1 to 9f·17), each with a recommendation, answerable by paste-back on the last tab. 9f·16 (is Start next season plan-gated?) is for /strategy and has no checkbox.
  • Not reopened: Q14 (several live seasons), Q16 (a finished season reads in place, locked, Money and Email families still open), 9a·4 (every status change asks in a window; the doors end the Overview’s body; families are not emailed), 9b·8, 9b·9, 9c·2b (ratings erased at finish), 9c·10 (coaches invited at Publish), 9d2·15 (the club gives time to house league), 9e·15, the 30-day coach page and sign-up link, and the signed-in family rulings (no new sent links; returning-family pre-fill is Stage 14’s).

Push-backs, before any drawing

The answer, before the detail

  • Two builds, and the small one goes first (9f·1). 9f-1 “Finished, honestly” (S): Archive decided, the word “Final” only once a season is complete, the coach keeps their final place for the 30 days, the Reopen window says ratings are gone, and the season limit counts the right seasons. 9f-2 “Start next season” (M) then builds on a settled set of season states. The prompt leaned the other way. I reversed it because Start next season reads every one of those decisions: built first, it would be built twice.
  • No frozen standings table (9f·2). A finished season is locked; only Reopen changes a result, on purpose, and since 9e-3 every score records who entered or corrected it. A stored final table would be a second answer that disagrees with the games the moment someone reopens to fix a score, which is the one reason to reopen. The real defect is the word: a season past its last day reads “Final standings” to families even when the club forgot to complete it and every score is still editable .
  • Start next season is not a new door; it is “Start from” in the New season window (9f·8). It copies from any season, finished or live, and leaves that season exactly as it was (9f·10, 9f·11). It brings settings, age groups and team names, and the born years move up a year. It never brings anyone’s registration, money, games or club time, and it says so before anything else.
  • Archive has no job worth keeping (9f·4). Today it changes one sentence and one chip on the public page, and it is the only way to free the one-season slot on the free League plan. That plan is parked, and its limit already contradicts its own words . Recommendation: stop offering Archive; a finished season is “Finished”, full stop.

Where the code disagreed with the drawing prompt (every flag opens the lines I read)

  • The free League plan’s one-season limit counts finished seasons too, not just live ones. Its own refusal says “includes one active season”, yet a completed season still holds the slot. Today a free club must archive last year before it can create next year. The tournament fixed the same rule on 2026-10-10 (“a Completed event stops holding the slot”) .
  • Reopen skips that limit. Reopening an archived season on the free plan gives it two live seasons; the status change never checks .
  • Archive differs in more places than one sentence: the public header chip, the club setup checklist’s “runs house league”, and the birthday cut-off card’s sports all ignore an archived season. None of those is a job anyone designed .
  • The public season header still shows “Season complete” and “Archived” chips, which the 9a copy pass retired .
  • Practice length and teams-at-once belong to each age group, not to the season, and born years have no “carried” marker: divisions are typed fresh every year .
  • The coach loses more than the standing line after finish: the Schedule heading stays with no list under it .

The lean I kept

  • Start next season does not close the old season (Q14 allows several live; Money stays open on the old one).
  • No frozen table; the locked season is the frozen table.
  • Pre-fill for returning families is not 9f.
  • The CLAUDE.md line goes in the asks (9f·17), not into CLAUDE.md today.

The lean I overturned

  • Order: the small build first, not Start next season first.
  • Coaches copy off by default: a copied coach becomes an invite email at Publish, to whoever coached last year.
  • Archive: retired, not decided “in the asks” and kept.

Not touched here

  • Ratings carried forward, a player across seasons, returning-family pre-fill (Stage 14 and the family plan’s Stage 5).
  • Club time: a copy starts with none (9d2·15).
  • The plan gate (9f·16, /strategy).

Archive: keep it, give it a job, or retire it

Recommendation (9f·4): retire the door

  • Archive was built as a label in 9a, with its job left to 9f. Read against the code, its only effect a club could notice is one sentence and one chip on the public page .

The finished record

The locked season is the frozen table (9f·2)

  • Standings are worked out from the games every time anyone looks . On a finished season nothing can change a game except Reopen, so the table cannot move by accident.
  • When a club reopens to fix a wrong score, the table should move: that is why they reopened. The score keeps who entered it and who corrected it (9e-3), so the change is not silent.
  • What does need fixing is the word “Final” (9f·3) and the coach’s lost line (9f·6).

Start next season

One window, which already exists, learns to start from a season

  • Doors: the house-league home’s New season button (as today) and the rare door at the end of any season’s Overview (s1). Both open the same window; the rare door presets Start from to its season (9f·8).
  • Who: the league runners, the same people who can create a season today (9f·9).
  • What it says first: what does not come with it. The source season is not touched (9f·11).
  • Result: a new “Getting ready” season with no club time, no families and no invites sent. Publish rules unchanged: coaches are invited only at Publish (9c·10).

Draft Test 2027 isn’t changed. Its families, payments, games and scores stay with it. 3 players still owe $525 there; that stays on its Money.

Warn, never block, and not amber: the copy closes nothing, so there is nothing to settle first. One clause in the statement, only when someone owes. A family that owes last year and signs up this year is not linked: that is Stage 14’s player across seasons.

2 · Reopen after a newer season exists

Allowed on every paid plan (Q14: several live seasons). The Reopen window adds “Draft Test 2028 is not changed.” (s12). The two seasons share nothing, so neither can damage the other. On the free League plan (parked), Reopen is refused when it would make two live seasons, with the plan’s refusal window .

The coaches’ rule (Reopen only while no newer season exists) protects one program year per team. A league has no such thing, so the rule doesn’t transfer.

4 · The free League plan’s one season

Parked, so no customer meets it. With 9f·5: a finished season holds nothing, so a free club can start next season from last year in the ordinary way. It is also capped at one age group and eight teams, so the copy brings at most what the plan allows (the same caps the age-group and team routes already check). Without 9f·5, the window needs a line telling a free club to archive first, and Archive keeps a job.

5 · A season the admin forgot to complete

Draft Test 2027 isn’t changed. It is still running: complete it from its Overview once its scores are in.

Start from is allowed from it (9f·10). The family reads “Ended Aug 28”, not “Final standings”, until it is completed (9f·3). Its Overview shows the “Next step: Complete the season” card as today, with the Start next season door at the body’s end.

6 · Pressing Create twice, or a name in use

Each season’s address is unique, so a second “Draft Test 2028” is refused with today’s words, and the window stays open with the name field marked. Nothing is half-made: the season and everything copied into it are one save.

7 · A source with a division that has no born years, or a free season

An age group without born years copies without them and its checklist line stays open, as for any new age group. A free source (all prices $0) copies “Free”; how-to-pay is then not required (9b·9).
