Owner walk · GST/HST Stage 6 · the two 6.6 fixes · ledger §534

The yearly reminder keeps its date and amount, and a move up during a trial gets its email

Written 2026-11-16, after the build (Part A a9d559062, Part B 873fc7c76). One purpose: see the two fixes the way a customer and a staff member meet them. On the dev site, with real emails to your inbox. About 30 minutes.

What changed

  • The yearly reminder. The renewal date, the amount and its tax, the “to stop it, cancel before…” sentence and the billing button are now added by the product after whatever a staff member writes in the template. An edit can no longer remove them, and an edit’s own button gives way to the product’s, so the email has one.
  • Both billing templates tell staff to keep the email transactional (no offers, no other plans’ prices, no links).
  • A move up during a free trial now sends every owner “The details of your purchase”: the trial continues, nothing charged today, the new plan’s first payment and its estimated tax.

It passes when

  • A The reminder template starts from one plain line with no button, and both descriptions carry the “keep this email transactional” line.
  • B With the template customised (your own line and your own button), both owners’ reminder shows your line first, then the date, amount and cancel sentence, and exactly one button.
  • C A trialing club’s move up shows $0.00 due today, and both owners get the details email with the new plan’s first payment and the trial’s end date.
