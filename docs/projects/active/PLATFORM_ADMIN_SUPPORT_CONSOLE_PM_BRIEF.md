# Platform admin, reorganized for the support team's jobs — PM brief

Plan: `PLATFORM_ADMIN_SUPPORT_CONSOLE_PLAN.md` · Status: **queued 2026-10-10**. It starts when Club Tier and the
tournament admin redesign are done.

**What changes.** The platform admin is where we support customers: fixing a technical problem, changing a plan or a
subscription, looking up an organization or a person and correcting their account, and finding marketing material.
Today it is about 35 pages arranged by what the system holds, in the old dark look nothing else in the product still
wears. After this project it is arranged by the job you are doing, in the same warm design as the rest of the product,
and every section has been drawn, reviewed by you, built and tested against real support jobs.

**Why it matters.** Once customers arrive, support work happens under time pressure. A console organized around the
job, with clear confirmations before anything changes a live account, makes support faster and safer, and makes it
possible to hand some of it to someone else later.

**Customer impact.** Indirect: faster, more accurate support. Nothing a customer sees changes.

**Priority.** After Club Tier and the tournament redesign. It is internal tooling, so it follows the projects
customers will see.

**Success looks like.**
- Each support job you named is a short, obvious path, measured before and after.
- Every section has passed your own walk.
- Nothing in the platform admin wears the old look.
- Every change to a live account asks first and is recorded.
