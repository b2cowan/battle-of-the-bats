Plans end at the renewal date · Stage 0 drawings

First pass · October 31, 2026 · drawn for the owner before any of Stage 1 or Stage 2 is built (plan §3a G12).

What changes for the customer. Cancelling, or moving down to the free plan, no longer takes anything away at the click. The account stays fully open until the renewal date, the end of the period they paid for. Until that date the plan card says when it ends, and one door puts the plan back. Our operators get the same waiting cancel by default. They also get a separate red End now for fraud, abuse or a chargeback, and a way to finish an ending that Stripe never reported to us.

The main drawing choice. The billing page already has a shape for a change that is waiting: the amber line “Moves to Club on {date}.” followed by an olive “Keep Club · Association” link. A waiting cancel is drawn on that same line, not as a new band. Ask 1 puts that to you.
