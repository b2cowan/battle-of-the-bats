What this drawing proposes

  1. On a phone, Filter wears the sliders alone, a 44px square like Search and Tools beside it. No word and no chevron. While filters are on it turns olive and shows how many: the sliders, then 2. This is the rule Tools already follows: its word on a computer, its glyph on a phone.
  2. A line under the toolbar names what is on, only while something is: U11 Girls · Maple Field 2. Picks from one group are joined by commas, and the groups are separated by a dot. The line wraps rather than cuts off, because a filter you cannot see is the problem it solves.
  3. The sheet stops a quarter of the screen short of the top. The dimmed page above it is the tap-away. On a 390 × 844 phone that is 190px of page. Today the sheet climbs to 12px from the top, so there is nothing to tap and the only ways out are the phone's Back gesture or a tap on the bottom bar.

Two places where I went wider than the ask. The sheet's height belongs to the frame that every phone menu shares (Tools, the team and player switchers, the Ledgers' Filter). Any long menu has the same problem, so A1 fixes it in the frame for all of them. And Teams has its own copy of this Filter button, and the two tournament toolbars were built so they would never drift apart, so A3 gives Teams the same change.

A1Which sheets stop short of the top?

Today every phone menu sheet may climb to 12px from the top. A form is different: it holds work in progress, so it keeps its full height in both answers, because a stray tap above it would throw that work away.

A2Does the Filter line show on a computer too?

On 10-01 you ruled that a lit Filter button was the only signal a filter needs. This line revises that ruling for filters. Search stays as you ruled it, with no note after a search.

A3Does Teams get the same?

The Teams page has its own copy of this Filter button. Its line would name the narrowed status and payment picks, for example Accepted, Waitlist · Unpaid. Corrected after the ruling: this ask first said Teams' phone button was worded with a chevron. It was already a sliders square, so on a phone Teams gains the count and the line.

Design check

I reviewed this drawing against the portal's written rules before sending it, and changed four things.

  • A1 now names everything it would change. The first version listed four menus. About a dozen share the frame, and a long notification in the reader is one you would notice.
  • The line is set in the meta-text size (12px, the size of the day row's caption), not 13px.
  • The before caption was corrected. The Day pill is worded too, and the button's width is now given as about 100px, since it was calculated rather than measured.
  • The missing cap is now stated. It is a deliberate difference from the Filter Counts ruling, so your answer covers it.

These rulings already agree with the drawing: a narrowing is quiet at rest and olive while it filters (10-07); amber counts are reserved for things waiting (10-01); Tools already shows its glyph on a phone; a menu's head is its small-capitals label; and the bar stays live under a menu (Sheet Frame D1, D2).

Left out on purpose

  • No Reset or Clear on the line. Reset filters stays at the foot of the sheet. A Clear on the line would be the "N match · Clear" note you cut on 10-01. The empty state's "Clear the filter" stays as it is.
  • No "4 selected" cap. A narrowed filter pill names up to three choices and then reads "4 selected" (Filter Counts, 10-05), because a pill has a fixed width. The line can wrap, and you asked for the filters to be listed, so it names every pick. Ticking four statuses and a division takes two lines on a phone.
  • The line is plain text, not a button. The Filter button directly above it is the way in. A second door would need its own 44px row on a phone.
  • No corner badge on the button. A corner badge already means "something is waiting for you" (the More tab's 3). A count of filters that are on is a state, not something waiting, so the count sits inside the button, as it does on a computer.
  • The Ledgers' worded Filter pill is unchanged. Its neighbours on that strip are worded pills, not icon squares. Only its sheet's height moves, under A1.
  • Teams' phone Filter sheet is unchanged. It is the admin's own sheet, with a Done button, and it already stops short of the top. The first version of this sheet said it was a popover; that was wrong.
  • Raised, not built: a small sliders mark at the start of the line, if at a desk the line reads as a caption for the day now that the word is gone. Step 10 of the walk asks about it.
