Why moving between pages feels jumbled

Each page was built in its own stage, and each one picked its own edges, gaps and ways of drawing the same things. None of the pages is wrong on its own. Moving from one to the next, though, the content starts in a different place, stops at a different place, and draws the same game a different way. Measured from the code:

5different right edges across six pages
3left edges (16, 24 and 32 px from the menu)
8ways a single game is drawn
9ways of saying a game is live (red, green, black, word, pill, edge)
6corner sizes (6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 20 px)
10text sizes, where six would do

The eleven rules

These become one written standard for every public tournament page, with an exceptions list beside it, the way the table standard works. The club round is checked against the same sheet. Numbers match the orange markers on the drawings. Lines in green name the earlier ruling a rule keeps.

One game, two forms, one reading order

A game is what a visitor reads most, and today it changes shape on every page. Both forms below are ones you have already ruled: the stacked form is your phone row (10-29), and the one-line form is the desktop Schedule row you kept the same day. The proposal is to use only these two, everywhere, in the same order: when, then who, then status. All specimens are drawn at true size.

Why the long name wraps today: the live game on the Schedule and Playoffs uses its own grid (the middle gets 2.2 shares of the row), while every other row gives the times and pills a fixed 104 px each and the names everything else. Putting the live game on the same grid as the rest fixes the wrap at 960 px with no other change.

    Already ruled: A1–A10

    Not asked now: kept for the club round

    • Menu on the left vs. tabs in the band. The club's pages put their tabs in the band; a tournament puts them in a left menu. That is the biggest jump between the two sites. A tournament has six to nine tabs and a long name, which will not fit in the band, so I would keep the menu and match everything else. Decided in the club round, with both sites drawn side by side.
    • Band height. The club band is 72 px; the tournament band is 88 px (it carries the dates and the Live now line).
    • The club's own widths. Club pages sit in a centred 1,200 px column and use three reading widths inside it (744, 760, 960). If 960 is approved here, the club round can bring its pages onto 960 on the column's left edge.
    • The word "door". The club kit already uses "door" for a card with a coloured edge and a button, so the standard calls the plain row with a chevron a link row, to avoid two meanings.
    • News and Register follow these rules but are not drawn. They still sit on an older centred layout and get moved onto the 960 column in the build.
