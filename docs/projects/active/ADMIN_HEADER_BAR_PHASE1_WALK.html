Owner QA walk · Admin header bar · Phase 1 · §486

The admin bar names the event or the club, carries its facts under the name, and never moves when you scroll

Written 2026-11-07. One purpose: the pinned white bar at the top of the admin, on a tournament screen and on Overview, on a phone and on a computer, at rest and scrolled.

What this walk is for

Inside a tournament the bar now shows the event’s name first, with a quieter small line under it: the club, the dates and the status chip on a computer, the dates and the chip on a phone. On the club’s Overview the bar’s club name is the page’s only title: the paper heading “Overview” is gone. On every screen the bar keeps its height when you scroll. On a phone it used to shrink and move the chip up beside the name.

It passes when

  • In a tournament the event’s name is on top, and the facts line is under it, never above it in capitals.
  • On a phone the facts line is the dates and the chip, with no club and no stray dot in front of the dates.
  • Scrolling never changes the bar on either screen, at any width.
  • Overview has one title, the club’s name in the bar, and the page starts right under it.
  • The event’s name still opens the tournament’s dashboard.

Not in this walk

  • Rep teams, a season, a team, Accounting, Families and Organization naming themselves in the bar, with their tabs pinned inside it. That is Phase 2. Until then those screens keep their own heading and tabs on the page, and the bar shows the club’s name, as today.
  • The club’s name on the line under a place. It arrives with Phase 2.
  • Calendar, Schedules, record pages and a tournament-only organization’s own screens. That is Phase 3.
  • The Overview’s content: “The club today”, “Still to do” and This morning. Those belong to Club Stage 13b’s walk (§485).
  • The coaches portal. Untouched.

Known before the walk

  • A long event name keeps two lines in the bar, as today. The drawing cut it to one line. On a phone, a name cut to one line could not be read in full anywhere in the bar, so the build kept two. Part E asks you to rule on it.
  • On a phone, a tap on the dates under the event’s name also opens the dashboard. The name’s tap area reaches down over that line so it stays a full thumb’s size, as it reached over the old line above the name.
  • Some small links in the Overview’s “The club today” band are under the thumb-size floor. They belong to Stage 13b, not to the bar.
