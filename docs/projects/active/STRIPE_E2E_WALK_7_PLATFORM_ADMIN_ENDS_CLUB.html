Platform admin cancels a subscription and deletes a Stripe-backed club

Written 2026-10-28. DESTRUCTIVE: this walk permanently deletes a club and its Stripe customer, so it first makes a throwaway club of its own and touches nothing else. It checks that staff can end a paying club safely, in the right order, and that only the right staff roles can.

What this walk is for

This walk deletes things for good. Part A builds a brand-new club called “Walk 7 Throwaway” and buys a real (sandbox) Tournament Plus subscription for it. Every later part ends that one club. Do not point any step at UAT Rep Club or any other club another walk uses; the steps never ask you to.

What you are proving:

  • Who can do what. Support sees neither of the two red buttons. Billing sees Cancel the subscription but not Delete the club. Only the Super admin can delete.
  • The order. A club whose subscription is still running cannot be deleted; the window tells staff to cancel first. After the cancel, the delete goes through.
  • Both halves reach Stripe. Cancelling ends the real subscription in Stripe; deleting with the box ticked removes the Stripe customer.
  • What the club's owner experiences after the cancel (a cancelled-state billing page, an optional email) and after the delete (the club is gone).
  • The trail. Each step is written in the club's Story and in the Audit log with the staff member's login and the reason they typed.

Refunding a charge is not in this walk; it was walked already (the support-console Stage 3 walks) and is not repeated.

It passes when
  • Support sees no red buttons on the club's Access tab; Billing sees only Cancel the subscription; Super admin sees both.
  • With the subscription still running, the delete window refuses and says to cancel first.
  • The cancel asks for a reason, ends the subscription in Stripe straight away, writes a “Subscription cancelled” line in the Story, and the owner then sees a cancelled billing page and cannot use the rest of the admin.
  • The delete asks for a reason and the club's web name typed out, removes the club from the console, removes the Stripe customer, emails the owner (when ticked) and leaves an Audit log entry.
  • Every delivery on the Stripe Billing destination during the walk reads 200.
