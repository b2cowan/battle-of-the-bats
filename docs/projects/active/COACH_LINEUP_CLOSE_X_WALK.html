One plain × on the lineup sheets

Your ruling of 2026-10-07: a close × is a plain glyph, with no outline, no circle and no square. The four lineup-builder sheets that carry a × (Lineup setup, Call up a player, Save as template and Copy from) now draw the same plain × as the game-day sheets and the portal’s windows. Nothing else in them moved, and every way of closing them works as before.

W1 · On a phone

What this walk is for

Every lineup sheet that covers the bottom bar has a way out in its top corner. That × should look the same on all four, and the same as the rest of the portal’s.

It passes when

All four show a plain grey × with nothing drawn around it, each one closes its sheet, and the rest of each sheet looks exactly as it did.

Not in this walk

The side panels (Help, chat, the tour), which still draw an outlined ×. Whether your ruling reaches them is a separate question.

  1. On your phone (or a browser about 390px wide), open More › Lineups and the lineup for vs Probe Rovers. Tap the Setup row (it reads “Auto-fill · …”).

    The Lineup setup sheet. At its top right, a plain grey ×, sitting just below the line of the title. No circle around it.

  2. Tap the ×.

    The sheet closes and you are back on the builder, with the bottom bar showing again.

  3. Tap Call up a player (the person-plus button at the top left of the tools). Look at the ×, then tap it.

    The same plain ×, no circle. The sheet closes.

  4. Tap Tools (⋯) › Save as template. Look at the ×, then tap it.

    The same plain ×, no circle. The sheet closes.

  5. Tap Tools (⋯) › Copy from.

    The full-screen list. The × at the top right is plain, with no square box around it, and it sits in the same spot as on the other three sheets: just below the line of “Copy from”. (It used to sit lower still, level with the gap between the title and the line under it.)

  6. Tap any game in the list, look at the next screen, then tap the ×.

    “What should come across?” with the same plain × beside the ‹ Games back button. The × closes Copy from and you are back on the builder; nothing was copied.

  7. Tap Tools (⋯) › Print.

    No ×, as before. Print is a menu, and a menu has a small-capitals label instead. Tap the dimmed area above it to close it.

  8. Switch the portal to Dark, open the Setup row again, look, close it, then switch back.

    A plain light-grey ×, no circle, easy to see on the dark sheet.

  9. Back in Light, open More › Lineups and the lineup for @ Probe Rovers (the one you screenshotted). Tap Tools (⋯) › Copy from, then tap Templates and Games again.

    The Games | Templates switch at its full height, both words whole, with a clear gap between it and the first month band (October). The list below scrolls; the switch does not get squeezed.

W2 · On a computer

What this walk is for

On a computer only two of these show a ×: Lineup setup (a centred window there) and Call up a player. Those two should match the phone’s, and darken under the pointer the way the notifications × does.

It passes when

Both show a plain ×, the pointer brings up a soft tint behind it, and the two small pop-ups still have no ×.

Not in this walk

The phone (W1).

  1. In a full-width browser window, open the same lineup and click the Setup row.

    The Lineup setup window in the middle of the screen, with a plain grey × at its top right. No circle.

  2. Rest the pointer on the ×, then click it.

    A soft olive square appears behind it and the × turns dark. The click closes the window.

  3. Click Call up a player (the person-plus button), rest the pointer on its ×, then click it.

    The same: plain at rest, a soft tint under the pointer, and the click closes it.

  4. Open Tools › Save as template, close it by clicking elsewhere, then do the same with Tools › Copy from.

    No × on either, as before. They are small pop-ups beside the button, and you close them by clicking anywhere else.
