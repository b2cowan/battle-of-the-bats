Dates and Doors

Two fixes to the panel that opens when a coach taps a spent or received figure on the statement. One stops it saying something untrue; the other stops it dead-ending.

1 · The dates are not missing

A sponsor's row on the statement is one row per sponsor, not per cheque — so it adds up several arrivals, and the panel prints “no date recorded” because no single date fits. Every one of those cheques is dated, and the Months panel two views away shows those dates.

The row should say how many payments it holds, and when they landed.

2 · The panel has no way out

The spent side ends with the sentence “Edit them on Transactions” — it names the destination and then doesn't take you there. There is no button, and for an assistant coach there is nothing clickable in the panel at all.

Its twin on the Months grid has carried doors since August. This one never got them.

Three things to say yes or no to

  1. The date line. 2 payments · May 10 – Jun 14 when there's more than one, the bare date when there's one.
  2. Sponsor and drive rows become links into that sponsor's or drive's own room — ungated, so an assistant coach gets them too.
  3. One footer door, “Open the Ledger”, on every spent-side panel. I've deliberately not added a second “Open Sponsors” button on the sponsor panel: the rows are already the better door, and a hub button beside them lands further from the work. Say the word if you'd rather have both.
