Our tax, as a real buyer meets it on production

Written 2026-11-22. One purpose: see, on the live site and the live Stripe account, what the test-mode walks already passed. You buy nothing.

What this walk is for

Every tax walk so far ran in test mode. Before the first real Canadian sale, look once at the live account and a live checkout. The one thing never seen yet is our GST/HST number on a live invoice. A club needs it on any invoice of $100 or more to claim the tax back.

It passes when

  • Live Stripe shows the Canada registration and prices that add the tax on top.
  • Our name, Ontario address and GST/HST number are on the live invoice template.
  • A live checkout with an Ontario address shows HST 13% on its own line before you pay.

Not in this walk

  • Paying, refunds and credit notes (passed in test mode, §471).
  • The tax on our online-payment fee. That moves to Families pay online.
