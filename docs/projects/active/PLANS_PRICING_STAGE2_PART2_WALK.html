The failed-payment notice on a club’s Money tab

Written 2026-11-06. One purpose: when a club’s payment has failed, the platform admin opens the club’s Money tab and is told, at the top, what is owed, why the bank refused it, how many times Stripe has tried and when it tries next, and every number agrees with Stripe’s own page. Read only: nothing on this tab can charge, refund or change anything.

What this walk is for

Before this change the Money tab showed a failed payment only as a row in the charges list, and its “Stripe and our record agree” line could read like an all-clear while a bill sat unpaid. Now the tab opens with a notice built from what Stripe already reports about the bill. This walk makes a payment fail on a test card, then checks the notice, the bill’s window and the sales row against Stripe’s invoice page, checks that only the roles that read Stripe see it, and checks that it goes when the club pays.

The yardstick is Stripe, not a figure in this page. Amounts, dates and tries change every run, so every step compares the site with what you wrote down from Stripe.

It passes when
  • The notice is the first thing on the tab, above the agree line, and its amount, reason, tax, tries and next try each match Stripe’s invoice and charge pages.
  • No total is ever printed (never “of 4”), and “no further try scheduled” appears only when Stripe shows no next attempt.
  • “See the bill” opens the bill’s window with a Tries line that repeats the notice; the sales row’s second line agrees.
  • Support sees the same notice; Product, who does not read Stripe, sees none; a club with nothing in Stripe shows none and no error.
  • After the invoice is paid and the tab is read again, the notice is gone.
