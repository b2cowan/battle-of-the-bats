# Club schedule landing UX — PM Brief

**Priority:** improve the admin's first read of a sent club schedule. The owner approved the [mockup](CLUB_SCHEDULE_LANDING_REVIEW_MOCKUP.html) and chose to build it in the current session on 2026-10-17.

Today, repeated Sunday assignments fill the first grid view and multiple requests stack above it. The team-by-team financial breakdown sits after the entire grid. A new visitor has to scroll to understand the schedule and what coaches have received.

After this change, a sent recurring schedule opens with a short action queue and an Overview of its usual assignments and exceptions. The full Grid remains the place to inspect and edit a date. A visible Fair shares view replaces the long table below the grid and its duplicate jump link. When there are many hand-back asks, the next three affected Sundays appear first; the rest can be revealed. An unsent update remains visible.

Admins with Accounting access can see requested refund amounts, answer asks, and open the bill. Other schedule admins can see that an ask awaits an Accounting admin, without the money or Answer control. Existing phone Day and By team views stay as they are.

In Grid, admins can select a whole group, such as Senior, without choosing each team one by one. The Team menu then lists teams in the chosen groups so they can narrow the view further. Shared time appears only when one of its teams meets both filters. This is a viewing choice for every schedule admin; it does not change assignments or Accounting access.

When a drag cannot be made because a team already has another slot at the destination time, the warning names that existing slot and facility. Admins can see why a swap was refused and decide which assignment to move first. This explanation is the same for every schedule admin and does not relax the overlap rule.

**Success:** the default desktop view stays useful with ten pending items; a reader can reach every request and date; no schedule edit, send, or billing behaviour changes; and the owner confirms the layout in the browser.
