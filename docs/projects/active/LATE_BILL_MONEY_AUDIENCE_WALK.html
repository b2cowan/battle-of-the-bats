Before you walk

What the change does
  • The late bill (“Send the bill”) goes only to coaches who can open Money. Others are not emailed and get no notice.
  • Send for review, Finalize and Send the changes still go to everyone who can see the schedule. Coaches who cannot open Money get the same email with every money line taken out, and their bell leaves out the dollar figure.
  • The Send window says who gets which email, so you are never surprised.
Mail on the dev site

Every address above ends in .local, so nothing can reach a real inbox. A send will still say “Sent to N people” and may add “couldn’t be delivered”. Either is fine. The doors in the Send window and the bells in the coach portal are the proof, not an inbox.

Figures and counts

Counts (“6 teams”, “15 coaches”) and dollar amounts here were read on 2026-11-13. They are illustrations of what right looks like, not pass conditions. The pass conditions are the relationships: who is named, who is left out, what the email does and does not contain.
