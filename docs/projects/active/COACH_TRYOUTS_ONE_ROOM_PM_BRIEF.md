# PM Brief — Coach Tryouts: One Room

**What changes for the coach.** Tryout day stops being three places. The Tryouts screen keeps its
four stage tabs, and the Tryout day stage gains a one-tap toggle between its three tools — Live
board, Check-in, Score. No more "Score players" button that leaves the page, no more "Back to
Tryouts" links, no more two-level back-out inside the scorer. Every stage and face has a real web
address, so refresh, bookmarks and the browser Back button all behave.

**Desktop finally looks like desktop.** On a big screen the scorer becomes two panes: the player
list stays on the left (with live checkmarks as players finish), the scorecard sits beside it at a
readable width with normal-sized rating buttons, and "Next player" walks down the list. Check-in
gets a capped width and a two-column roster. On phones, nothing moves — the big sunlight-grade
tap targets are deliberate field equipment and stay exactly as they are. Volunteers who open their
scoring link on a laptop get the desktop scorer for free (same surface, both doors).

**The how-to guide stops renting prime space.** The "Run your tryout" card under the title is
gone. "How tryouts work" is now a quiet link at the end of the tab row that opens the same
four-step map. A team that has never touched tryouts sees the map open automatically on arrival;
once setup begins it rests as a link. Nothing to dismiss, nothing stored, still there next August.

**Why it matters.** Tryout day is the portal's highest-pressure hour. Fewer navigations means
fewer chances to lose the thread mid-field, and a credible desktop view means the product doesn't
look like a stretched phone app in the office or a boardroom demo.

**Knock-ons handled in the same change.** The coach demo's "Tryout day" moment and tour step
point at the new addresses; the in-app help guide's three mentions of the old buttons are
reworded; old bookmarks to the two retired pages forward automatically.

**How to test.** Team with a tryout: open Tryouts → Tryout day → flip Board/Check-in/Score, on
desktop and phone; score a player on desktop with Next player; confirm a volunteer link on a
laptop shows two panes; confirm a brand-new team sees the guide open and everyone else sees the
link; follow an old `/tryouts/score` bookmark and land on the Score face.
