Board Ready, Stage 2, round 2 (after /ux, /design and /legal). The board asked for “email distributions to coaches: all rep coaches, all coaches, all house-league coaches.” These drawings answer with live groups, counted from who holds the role today and sent as colleague mail on the club’s shipped send window. Counts and names come from a fresh seeded copy of Harbourtown Minor Ball on dev. The messages and history rows are examples. Every frame is drawn at true size. Who sees the tab

  • Owner, admin: the tab, every group the plan carries, and every row of history.
  • League admin, league registrar: the tab, the House-league coaches group only, and only house-league rows in the history (tab 2).
  • Treasurer, staff, official, coach: no tab.
  • A member limited to some team groups: no tab in this build, the same as Families.

Why not the other homes

  • Families › Emails: it needs the Families grant, which an admin and a league admin don’t hold by default, and its mail is family mail. The two mail paths stay apart.
  • Rep teams or House league: each reaches half the coaches, and “All coaches” would have no home. A second door there is ask 13.
  • The sidebar or the More sheet: they list places only, never an action (N2, N7).

Build notes

Group email becomes a new Organization link, held by the sending seats, so Organization appears for a registrar. The Organization tab list in NAV_STANDARD N11 changes (ask 7). The button is the season screen’s lime with the Mail icon (icon #11), placed through TabActions. The table is EmailLog’s list. A direct visit without the tab gets NotYours.

The rules the list follows

  • One person on three teams gets one email. Their row lists every team (“11U AA, 16U AA · Head coach, Assistant”).
  • The sender never gets a copy. When they coach, the foot adds “You are not counted.”
  • No email on file: listed last as “Won’t get it: no email on file”. This can’t happen to a house-league coach, whose email is required.
  • Invited, not accepted (rep): left out and named, as Ask the coaches and the schedule send do today. House-league coaches get it whether or not they have signed in, as their copies do today.

Colleague mail, never family mail

  • It goes as house mail, replies go to the sender, and it is sent five at a time, like Ask the coaches.
  • No family footer and no unsubscribe: a coach is written to in their role, not as a family on a list. The foot says why they got it and how to be taken off.
  • The “why you get this” line depends on the group: “…you’re on {Club}’s coaching staff”, “…you coach in {Club}’s house league”, “…{Sender} picked you”.
  • No plan name, price, offer or link to pricing or sign-up, ever. A build guard enforces it (tab 9, the /legal box).

Build notes

The foot sentence is the shipped footerStart hint. From is built by familySenderName, which strips quotes and commas, and the foot by platformLineWords. The statement on a phone is shorter: “Emails each coach you pick. Replies come to you.”

4 · The recount at Send

At Send the server reads the group again and compares the people, not just the number. If anyone was added or taken off, nothing is sent, even when the total is the same. The window stays open. The foot says who changed, by name when three or fewer people did, and the button takes the new count. The sender presses Send again. This is the family send’s rule, plus the names.

  • The sender loses the right mid-write: “You can no longer send this. Nothing was sent.”

Why the window doesn’t just send to the new count

The sender read a list and pressed Send for that list. Sending to anyone else, even one more person, means sending to someone they didn’t see. Nothing is sent if anyone was added or taken off, even when the total is the same. The extra press costs a second.

Daily allowance

Family mail allows 3 sends a day per club. This drawing gives coach mail no daily cap, only the shipped just-sent check, whose length the build keeps the same as the family send’s. The family cap protects families who can opt out. Coaches get role mail, and a cap here would mostly block the club’s own work.

9 · Asks

Paste back as “Group email asks: 1 yes, 2 yes, …”, or give a different answer for any. None of these has a ruling yet. The plan’s Q1–Q9 cover other stages.

Already ruled, drawn as ruled
  • Two mail paths, never mixed: this is colleague mail (plan L53).
  • CASL: mail to a named person in their role is not commercial while it carries no plan, price, offer, or pricing or sign-up link.
  • The board tab keeps names and roles only. People is not public (R1).
  • Stage 3 only adds groups. Gates: Board Ready stages are League Plus and the Club plans (Business Decisions 2026-11-10).
  • Roadmap ask 11: the group email row stays Committed for 2026 only if it is built before the roadmap is first shown.
What the /legal review found and changed (written up in CASL_COACH_GROUP_EMAIL.md)
  • Not a commercial message as drawn (CASL s.1(2)), so no unsubscribe is lawful. A fallback exemption covers mail within one organization about its own activities.
  • The foot now reads “Sent through FieldLogicHQ, the software {Club} uses.” (the family mail’s own line), not “Sent with FieldLogicHQ”, which reads like an advert badge.
  • The free-text message is the one commercial risk. A sponsor promotion in it would make the email commercial. Hence the line under the message box. Our Terms already forbid it.
  • The statement names the reply address (“Replies come to you at …”), because the sender’s address goes to every coach.
  • The “why you get this” line now depends on the group (a manager isn’t a coach), and the foot has “Not right for you? Reply and tell {Sender}.” A club-typed house-league address is never checked, so a typo reaches a stranger, and this gives them a way out.
  • Build guard: the sender’s fixed words and sample fail the build on any link, any plan name or price, any “$” amount, or the words pricing, trial, sign up, subscribe, upgrade, discount, offer, buy, “try FieldLogicHQ”, “your own club”, “learn more”, “get started”. It also fails on a foot or From line other than the ones above, or any family footer, club band or unsubscribe part. The admin’s own message is not scanned.
  • Owed: a sentence in the privacy policy (§5/§9) that the club’s coach emails keep the message and recipients’ names, plus a retention clock for that log (to /plan). Stage 3’s account-less groups each get a /legal re-check: a group is computed only from a role the club recorded, never from guardians.
  • No lawyer needed for this email. One narrow question could go to one before Stage 3 sends to volunteers at scale: whether volunteers count as the organization’s “representatives”.

1 · Who may send?

Recommend: owner and admin, every group. League admin and league registrar, house-league coaches only. Nobody else. The league desk already emails house-league coaches today, through the season send’s “copy coaches”, so this reaches no one new. The schedule send to rep staff is owner and admin only, and so is this one. A treasurer, staff, official or coach gets no tab. A member limited to some team groups gets no tab in this build, just as Families refuses them. The screens show names, never addresses, so no seat sees an address it couldn’t before.

2 · Which groups in this build?

Recommend: All coaches · Rep coaches (everyone on the staff, or head coaches only) · House-league coaches (the live season, or one division) · Coaches you pick. Tournament coaches stay out (ask 5). These are what the board named, and every one is computed from data that exists today.

3 · Saved custom lists?

Recommend: deferred. This build has the one-off “Coaches you pick”, and “Send again to these coaches” in the history. A saved list is the hand-kept list the plan rejected. It brings rename, edit and delete screens and a new table, which would push the build past two sessions. Revisit with Stage 3’s People list. “Send again to these coaches” is a new door, and the build can drop it if you’d rather.

4 · Does a rep team’s assistant or other staff count as a coach in All coaches?

Recommend: yes. All coaches is every rep team’s whole staff (head coaches, assistants, managers and other staff) plus the house-league coaches. Rep coaches opens on Everyone on the staff, with Head coaches only one tap away. Said plainly: by default the club’s coach mail goes to team managers too. The manager is often the one who acts on a permit or equipment email, and leaving them out misses the person doing the work. The row’s sub line says who is in it. The seeded club differs by one person (7 vs 6).

5 · Tournament announcements: replace, keep beside, or leave alone?

Recommend: leave alone, and drop “Tournament coaches” as a group here. The window carries one line pointing to the tournament’s Communications while the club has a tournament that isn’t finished. A tournament’s coaches are mostly other clubs’ coaches (3 of the seeded 4), reached for that event, with division and status targeting, a bell and a who-it-reached list already built. Folding them into All coaches would email visiting clubs about the club’s own meetings.
