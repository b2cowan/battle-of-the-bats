What changes as you build

Before dues are set, the amount players need to cover sits below the report. Once set, Player installments belongs in Revenue, linked to the existing dues editor. It is never entered twice.

The balances stay visible in every view. List gives the season result; months and quarters add the timing. A monthly shortage stays flagged even if the quarter ends above zero.

Positive this month does not mean available

Choose “Installments brought forward.” October closes at $3,000, but later costs leave just $1,000 by December. Try a $600 October expense: December now closes at $400.

These are planned receipts and payments. A positive month-end balance cannot prove a bill due early in the month is covered by money arriving later.

The earlier ruling stays — confirmed, not revised

Choose “Lines above the estimate.” Planned expenses still reads $12,000 — your estimate — not the $14,186 of real itemized costs above it. The $2,186 gap doesn’t disappear: it nets into No date yet the same way an estimate below the lines already reserves its remainder there, so one mechanism handles both directions and “Over your estimate” stays visible in red either way.

“Lines so far” stays, as ruled. So does the conditional brackets legend; its wording follows the new revenue-minus-expenses direction.

Included in the implementation proposal

Existing add/edit, import and dues forms remain the editing doors. Exports follow the report; the importer must recognize the new bands and skip derived dues and balances. Read-only users retain their current access.
