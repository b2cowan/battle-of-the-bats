What this picture leaves out, on purpose

  • Unless the owner has given a coach House league, coaches do not see sign-up answers. A coach does see the family name, email and phone for their own team. (Ask 2.)
  • The guardian sign-in tier is not on, so no family account appears here.
  • The email service's own retention is not stated, because its page was not read for this drawing.
Two gaps this drawing exposes. The privacy policy does not yet say that waiting files go within 12 hours or that health and file answers go after 90 or 120 days, though both rules run on production. And ordinary registrations have no removal date at all, and a club cannot delete one itself. All three are logged for the owner, and none is hidden on the picture. One more for a lawyer's eye: any member the owner gives House league to can read health answers.
