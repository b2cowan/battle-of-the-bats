WhatWhy its own
Permits and hoursAny permit, including one already on the schedule. Only time the schedule isn't already using is offered.
From and ToA week, a day or months. Nothing before today once the schedule has been sent.
Teams and how they shareThe same team table as New schedule. Tick one team and it gets all of the time.
Dates off, slot length, changeoverThe part's dates off never reach the original's practices.
BillChosen when you send the changes, with the whole Bill the teams window. It is a new bill: the original bill and its payments stay as they are.
RegenerateWorks on one part. A practice stays on its own part's time and goes only to that part's teams, so practices never move between bills.

The parts share

WhatHow
The pageOne schedule, one grid and one set of views. A Part filter appears only when there is more than one part.
Clash checksA place or a team can't be in two practices at once, across parts. The original's practices count as taken.
Fair sharesHanded out, Short and Clashes count the whole schedule.
Needs attention, Send the changes, Send logOne list and one send for the whole schedule.

What this replaces

  • Add time today aims at every running team's practices a week. If every team already has its target, it makes nothing.
  • Today, added time is billed as a correction on the original bill, at the permit's price. Under this model it gets its own bill.
  • Two open questions from the Add time build go away: a permit's earlier days being cut off without warning, and new dates off reaching the original's practices.

Add time leaves drafts, and Settings… gets the Permits picker. Today Settings… on an existing schedule has no Permits picker, so on a draft Add time is the only way to bring in another permit. Taking Add time off drafts without this would leave a draft stuck on the permits it started with.

What you already said (shown so it can be corrected)

  • A draft is edited, not added to. Add time is for after the schedule is final and sent.
  • Adding time is basically making a new schedule the same way, saved as part of the one it was added to.
  • The new part doesn't change the existing schedule or its billing.
  • Its billing is separate from the old one: 1 team or 10, whatever is set.
  • The phases are draft → final → final with changes → final.

Ask 1 · What an added part is called

  • A · "Time added Nov 16" (drawn). It names itself, so there is nothing to type, and it reads clearly on a coach's bill. A second on the same day is "Time added Nov 16 (2)".
  • B · A name you type, starting as "Time added Nov 16" (e.g. "Spring extension"). It is one more field in a window that is already longer.

Recommend A. B can come later as a rename in Settings… if clubs ask for it.

Ask 2 · Add time on a draft

  • A · Final schedules only. Settings… on a draft gets the Permits picker and Find the time (tab 2).
  • B · Keep Add time on drafts too, as built today.

Recommend A. It is your model, and a draft with two kinds of edit is two ways to do one job.

Ask 3 · How the parts show on the page

  • A · Quietly (drawn): a line under the title per added part, a Part filter, and a Part row on a practice. All of them only on a schedule with time added.
  • B · A "Time on this schedule" list above the grid: one row per part with its dates, teams, permit and bill status, and a Settings… door on each.

Recommend A. B is clearer once a schedule has three or more parts, but it pushes the grid down on every schedule that has any.

Ask 4 · Regenerate and Settings… on a schedule with parts

  • A · One part at a time, chosen at the top (drawn). Regenerating Fall practices never touches Time added Nov 16, and the other way round.
  • B · The whole schedule at once, each part still keeping to its own time and teams.

Recommend A. B regenerates time you didn't mean to touch, and its question has to list two parts' kept practices at once.

Ask 5 · Where the new part's bill is set

  • A · In Send the changes, a line with "Bill it…" (drawn). The bill is set when the time is about to go out, as with Finalize.
  • B · As Add time's step 3, straight after the drafts.

Recommend A. Add time is used by people who schedule; the bill needs Accounting. A keeps those two jobs where they are today.

Ask 6 · Choosing the teams

  • A · New schedule's team table, nobody ticked to start (drawn). One tick means one team gets all of it.
  • B · A separate choice, "Share it toward each team's practices a week" or "Give all of it to [team]". This is what I suggested in chat before your model.

Recommend A. Your model makes it the same form, so the one-team case needs no control of its own.

Ask 7 · A permit already on the schedule

  • A · Offered, with the time already used left out (drawn), and From starting where the schedule's use of it ends.
  • B · Offered as is, letting the clash check refuse overlapping time when the drafts are made.

Recommend A. B shows time that can never be used, and you only find out at the drafts.

What changes from what's built (for the record)

  • Added time is no longer billed as a correction on the original bill (the "P9 B" path). It gets its own bill. Earlier payments are untouched either way.
  • Add time's From/To and its dates off become the part's own. That answers two open questions from the Add time build.
  • Add time leaves drafts. Settings… gains the Permits picker.
