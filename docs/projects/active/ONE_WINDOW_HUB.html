How many windows does the product have?

A window is anything that opens over the page and has to be answered or closed: a form, an “are you sure”, a notice. The product has about 300 of them across the club admin, the tournament admin and the coaches portal. They come in four families plus strays, and two families do the same job in each portal with different looks.

Station 4

What is already the same

The two form windows are closer than they look. This is why the work is two style changes and a set of moves, not a redesign.

  • Title: both use the Barlow headline face at 16px, sentence case.
  • Card: white in Warm, deep navy in Dark, 12px corners, a hairline over the foot.
  • Close: a plain × on a computer; on a phone the form fills the screen, ← replaces ×, and the form covers the bottom bar.
  • Keyboard and Back: both stand on the coaches portal’s window floor (Escape, Tab kept inside, the phone’s Back closes it) since your ruling of 2026-09-27 that the club windows behave like the portal’s.
  • Buttons: a white outlined Cancel and a lime main button, in both.
Standing back

Four rules that cut across the stations

  • R1 · One window piece for both portals. Not two pieces kept in step: one, so they cannot drift again. The admin’s is the more complete of the two (foot slots, refusal line, phone tap sizes, never closing on a stray tap), so it becomes the one piece and the coach windows move onto it.
  • R2 · The coaches portal’s look by default, the admin’s behaviour by default. Look questions follow your benchmark rule. Behaviour questions go to whichever is safer, and that is the admin’s in every case found. Two look exceptions are recommended: the dimmed page (ask Q5), and sentence-case labels (ask Q1), which you ruled for the admin’s settings pages on 2026-10-12 and which read better for the admin’s longer labels.
  • R3 · An “are you sure” is the question window, and a “done” is a pill. No second confirm piece and no browser box; a confirmation that needs no answer fades.
  • R4 · Sheets and side panels are not in this project. The Sheet Frame rules govern them, and the outlined × on the side panels is already an open call of yours.

Verdict

Product-manager brief

One window for the whole product

What changes

Every form window, “are you sure” question and notice looks and behaves the same, whether you are in the club admin, the tournament admin or the coaches portal. The look is the coaches portal’s (paper-tone fields, a narrower window), with the sentence-case labels you ruled for the admin’s settings pages. The behaviour is the admin’s:

  • A stray tap never throws away a half-filled form.
  • Fields and buttons are full tap size on a phone.
  • A short sentence at the foot says what happens next.

Every “are you sure” becomes one question window, the browser’s grey boxes included. A “Done” or “Saved” stops needing a tap and fades on its own.

Why it matters

Today the product has two form windows, three ways to ask “are you sure”, and a handful of strays. A tournament organizer meets two confirm styles on the same Schedule screen. A coach can lose a typed form to a stray tap that the admin would have caught. And every new window is built against whichever family its author happened to copy, which is how the drift started. One window piece ends that for good.

Customer impact

Everyone who uses a window, which is everyone.

  • The admin changes look most: about 84 form windows and 54 questions in the club and tournament admin.
  • The coaches portal changes behaviour most: about 76 form windows.
  • House league changes both: its 17 windows join for the first time.

Nothing changes about what any window asks or does.

Priority

Medium. It is polish, but it touches every screen, so it is cheapest before the Club release puts the club admin in front of paying customers. Stage 1 alone removes the difference you spotted.

Success criteria

  • Open any form or question in any portal, Warm and Dark, computer and phone: you cannot tell which portal built it.
  • No browser grey box anywhere; no notice that needs a tap to dismiss.
  • A build check fails if a new window is built outside the one piece.

Findings

F01 — Labels differ. Admin: sentence case, 12px, weight 650, secondary ink, the look ruled for the admin’s settings pages on 2026-10-12 (Event settings, A71). Portal: small capitals, 12px, 600, tertiary ink, slight tracking. The label is shared by page forms as well as windows in each portal, so Q1 decides both.

F02 — Fields differ. Admin: card fill (white / navy), 40px (44 on a phone), 7px corners. Portal: paper tone (a 5% white wash in Dark), about 36px, 6px corners, no phone floor. In Dark the admin’s field is the card’s own colour behind a thin edge and reads faintly.

F03 — Width differs. Admin 640px (wide 800). Portal 520px (wide 640).

F04 — Spacing differs. Admin 20px sides. Portal 24px.

F05 — The dimmed page differs. Admin: a themed tone (warm brown at 34% in Warm, navy at 55% in Dark). Portal: black at 60% in both, which turns a Warm page a muddy grey.

F06 — The card edge differs. Admin: neutral hairline and a shadow. Portal: a tinted olive or navy line, no shadow.

F07 — A tap outside closes a coach form. The admin’s form never closes on a stray tap; the portal’s closes, and asks first in only the 24 places that wired a discard guard.

F08 — No foot sentence or refusal slot in the portal. The admin’s foot keeps a sentence at the left and a refusal beside the buttons (build-guarded); the portal puts both in the body.

F09 — No phone tap floor in the portal’s window. Fields about 36px and foot buttons 34px on a phone; the admin’s are 44px. This is the portal-wide tap-floor ruling already owed.

F10 — Three confirm looks. The admin’s question window; the older confirm window (14px corners, an icon, a heavier body-face title, a boxed ×, grey Cancel, a blurred backdrop); the browser’s own box on three rep-team screens.

F11 — Notices as windows. Most of the older window’s club uses are “Done / Saved / Plan updated” needing a tap, against the transient rule. Some carry news (the plan change reports restored tournaments). The club already has the save pill and a page notice that fades after eight seconds.

F12 — Phone shape of a question. The older window sits at the bottom of a phone screen, inherited from the oldest shared window style at 768px and below. The coaches portal’s own rule keeps a short window as a small centred card (ten windows use it), and the admin’s question stays centred.

F13 — House league’s 17 windows. Own styles: grey Cancel, banded head and foot, a tap outside closes a form, no phone layout, no keyboard or Back handling.

F14 — The tournament’s old windows. Add team, Add team manually, Resolve facilities, Resolve locations, on the oldest shared style (boxed ×, grey Cancel, blurred backdrop, 8px phone sheet); New room in chat has its own.

F15 — Coach windows without keyboard handling. About 17 hand-built coach windows do not keep Tab inside or answer the phone’s Back.

F16 — One-offs. The player-cap upgrade (old inline styles), the onboarding windows, Close season and Start next season (text colours that ignore Warm).

Scope

  • Every form window, question and notice in the club admin, the tournament admin and the coaches portal.
  • The volunteer score sheet on a computer (a centred card) is checked against the one window in stage 4.
Out of scope. Rooms and the admin’s record windows, if Q15 is ruled as recommended (a room is never a height-capped centred box, 2026-09-02). Sheets, side panels and menus (Sheet Frame governs them; the side panels’ outlined × is an open owner call). In-place confirms inside a foot (the coaches portal’s guarded delete row) are a different pattern and stay. What any window asks, says or does.

Approach

  1. Stage 1 · One look. The admin’s window piece takes the ruled look (Q1–Q6) in its own stylesheet and the club kit’s field styles; the label rule (Q1) is set once for both portals’ labels, pages included. Every admin form and question changes at once. Build note: admin forms laid out for 640px are checked one by one; those with side-by-side fields or tables take the wide size.
  2. Stage 2 · One behaviour. The coach form shell becomes a thin wrapper over the one piece, so the coaches portal gains the no-stray-tap rule, the foot sentence, the refusal line and the phone tap sizes (Q7–Q10). The hand-built coach windows move onto the shell. This settles the owed tap-floor ruling for windows. Build notes: the admin piece is admin-only by convention today; that convention ends here, and a guard says so. The move must keep what the coach shell does today (its discard guard, hiding the phone bar, locking the page’s scroll), and the admin piece renders in place rather than at the top of the page. “Question” already names the coach form shell in the code, so the yes-or-no window takes another name. The tap floor is measured before and after on each window (the 2026-08-19 rule). A foot sentence must be short at 520px, or it takes its own line above the buttons.
  3. Stage 3 · One question. The coaches portal’s “are you sure” provider renders the question window; the tournament’s and club’s uses of the older window become questions, pills or page notices (Q11–Q13); the browser boxes go. A question with three answers, a typed confirmation or a reason field uses the same window. The older window is deleted.
  4. Stage 4 · Strays join. House league’s 17, the tournament’s 5, the one-offs (Q14).
  5. A guard fails the build when a screen builds a window outside the one piece, or uses the browser’s confirm.

Verification

Per stage: focused checks, a typecheck, and the layout check on the screens the stage touched (on staging for the whole-product sweep, per the dev-server rule). An owner walk per stage, Warm and Dark, computer and phone.

Rollout

Coaches have no switch, so their changes reach them with whichever release carries them (Q16). No database change. Help articles describe what windows ask, not how they look, so the guides change only if a window’s words change (none planned). The demo tours point at screens, not windows; the build’s demo-anchor check covers it.
