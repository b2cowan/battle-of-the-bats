The door to the bank check

One purpose: a coach or treasurer who has never checked the bank is invited to, wherever they are in the season, and a reload or a shared link comes back to the same place. You look at the invitation on a team that is still planning, a team that has checked, and a club with no account; follow the new help link in both portals; and reload the coach’s Ledger view and the club’s Account pill. About 20 minutes.

Before you start

  • Nothing to set up. Every team and club above already exists on dev, and nothing in the walk saves, deletes or changes a figure.
  • Two more states are not in the walk: a club with exactly one account that has never been checked, and a club with several accounts where some have never been. I looked at both on temporary accounts, which are gone.
  • Walk the parts in order, 1 to 4. Each opens with a stop check.

Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • A coach who can only read money keeps the plain “Bank: not checked yet ›” line, never the invitation (they have no form to be invited to).
  • A club with several accounts where some are unchecked still reads “1 of 3 not checked yet ›” (it names a count an invitation would hide).
  • The Overview’s door to one account and the fiscal-year close’s door still land on the right account; a check opened from a door opens once, and a reload does not reopen it.
  • The Account pill’s address when the saved account is not one of the club’s: it falls to the first account and the address is corrected.

Your time goes on judgment: does a volunteer notice the invitation, understand it, and find the help?
