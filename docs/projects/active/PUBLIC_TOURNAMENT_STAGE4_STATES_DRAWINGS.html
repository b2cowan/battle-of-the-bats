What the design review changed from the first versionOpen for the list; nothing here changes a ruling
  • Table pins now work as ruled. The Team column had grown to 214 px and hid RD behind PTS at 390. It is now 150 px (the hub's number), numbers right-aligned, so Team, REC and RD show first and RF and RA are one swipe away. At 360, RD peeks out as the cue to swipe.
  • The 8-team state no longer blows the page sideways. The bracket was pushing the whole phone 548 px wide; the bracket now scrolls inside its own box, and the page stays 390.
  • Two pools are two different pools. Both pools showed the same four teams with the same records. Pool B now has its own teams; the counts line (8 final, 4 left) matches the tables.
  • Numbers add up. Runs for and against balance across each table, the best-offence and stingiest-defence panels name the teams the table shows, and the bracket's seeds match the seed list (1 v 4, 2 v 3; 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 3 v 6, 2 v 7). Before, a team outside the top 4 played in the semifinals.
  • No-playoffs state has no Playoffs tab. It showed one.
  • Directions is a symbol, not a button (the hub's drawing and the phone-secondary-action ruling). Zoom buttons reach 44 px wide. The "Live now" chip moved to the card's top-right as in the hub, and the trailing score dims.
  • The bracket shows what it is for: scores, winners in bold, the live game edged green, cancelled and waiting-on-a-result games said in words. Zoom and Full screen appear only on the 8-team bracket; at four teams every name reads without them.
  • "Up next" states are possible ones. A championship with both sides unknown while semifinals were still ahead could not happen (the next game would be a semifinal). The TBD state is now a championship waiting on one semifinal result; the bracket-only and 8-team states lead with their real first game.
  • Door and tap-open rows match the hub: a real link with a chevron, 14 px sub-line, 56 px tall; the sub-line was 12 px.
  • Share is drawn (it was promised in the note but absent). Reading-order numbers are now measured under each Standings and Playoffs specimen instead of asserted.
