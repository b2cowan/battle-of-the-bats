Before you start

  • Stripe’s messages must reach your computer for the last two steps of walk A. In a spare terminal run stripe listen --forward-to localhost:3000/api/billing/webhook and leave it running. Its secret (whsec_…) must match STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET in .env.local; if you had to change it, restart the dev server. In PowerShell write the address as one quoted piece.
  • Walk A before walk E (E starts on the Premium account A leaves Active). Walks B, C and D can go in any order.
  • Never press “End the plan now” in walk C. The walk only reads that window.
  • Dates in the steps are shown as {date}: they depend on the day the fixture was made. The Premium plan was set to renew in late December 2026.
