Owner QA walk · Fee invoice alerts · Part 5, the help article

The help guide tells an owner how they are told an invoice is waiting, and what its numbers mean

Written 2026-11-06 for Part 5. Dev only. Nothing in the database changes in this walk: you only read.

What this walk is for

Parts 1 to 4 built the alerts: a count on the Plan & billing entry, a line at the top of that page, and a red line on every admin page once an invoice is overdue. Part 5 writes it down in the organization guide, under Stripe fees on your online payments, so an owner who sees a red line and wonders what it is, and what happens if they wait, finds the answer. It also records one wording question from the accounting review (Part E).

It passes when

  • The new article, How you’re told an invoice is waiting, opens from its own link and says what you saw in the earlier walks: the count (amber, then red), the line on Plan & billing, the red line on every admin page with no close button, owner only, and a cancelled club’s one page.
  • Searching the guide for the words an owner would use (“overdue banner”, “red banner”, “unpaid invoice”) finds it.
  • The article on the Stripe fees list describes the invoice row as you see it on Plan & billing: one total, any tax on its own line, an Overdue mark and “Was due”.
  • On a phone the article reads cleanly with no sideways scrolling.
  • You have answered the one wording question in Part E.

Not in this walk

  • The alerts themselves. Walks §465, §466 and §468 passed. This walk only reads the help.
  • Platform admin’s copy of the guide. It shows the same text from the same source, so it follows this article without a separate check.
  • The coaches portal. It has no fee invoices, so its help is untouched.
