After a check, every state reads as it does today: "Bank: Operating checked through Nov 8, 2026 ›", "Bank: oldest check Nov 5, 2026 ›", and the amber chip when a check is off.

Three calls were ruled before drawing (invitation replaces only the not-checked states; option A, one line, stays; the coach's setup line goes above the rail). One question comes out of drawing it.

1 · Does the club's invitation carry its quiet second line?

The one-line ruling exists so the heading row never grows. The quiet line makes the unchecked state about 18px taller on a desktop, and one 44px row plus the quiet line on a phone. It goes away at the first check, and the tiles are the only thing it moves.

  • A · Yes, as drawn. Same words as the coach's line, and the second line is what tells a volunteer what the check is for.
  • B · No. The club shows "Check your bank balance ›" alone, so the heading row never grows. The purpose line stays on the coach's side and on the Bank tab's own card.

2 · Anything else on the coach's setup Overview?

Plan and Collect show the line directly above the rail and below the tiles and the payables panel. If you would rather have it directly under the anchor card, say so and I will redraw that one frame.

Build notes from the design review

  • The quiet line is part of the door: one tap target covers both lines (44px floor on a phone). In the frames the dashed box is that target.
  • The quiet line and the chevron use the portal's tertiary text colour, which reads at about 6:1 on white.
  • While the bank read is out, the row holds the loaded invitation's full height on a phone, so the page below does not jump when it lands. A failed read still draws nothing.
  • On the coach's setup Overview the line has a small gap above and below (about 12px), its left edge on the card edge, no box of its own.

Not drawn, and why

The "How the bank check works" link on the Bank view's head line was drawn in the evaluation page (words row 20) and ruled with it. The coach's View pill and the club's Account pill remembering their choice in the address change no pixels.
