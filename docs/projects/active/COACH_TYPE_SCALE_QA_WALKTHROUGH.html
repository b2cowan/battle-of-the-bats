Two rules for this walk

1 · Reset zoom to 100% before you start (Ctrl+0). This whole change is about size. A zoomed tab will make you judge the wrong thing — which is exactly what started this thread.

2 · If a screen has no data, that is "needs a call", not a pass. There may be no live game, no closed season and no tournament in UAT. A step you could not reach is not a step that passed — say so in the notes and I will find you a fixture.
