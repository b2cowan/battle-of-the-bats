Owner QA · §441 · Account sheet on a phone

On a phone, the person icon opens a sheet, and your own side is one row in it.

What this is. On a phone (900 px and narrower), tapping the person icon on a public page no longer opens a small floating menu that ran off the left edge. It opens a sheet from the bottom with who you are, a Go to block for your own side (Admin, Coach, Scorekeeper), your settings, and Sign out. The separate building icon is gone from the tournament band and the club bar on a phone. A desk (901 px and up) is unchanged. This is one walk with real links.

Nothing is pre-ticked or pre-approved. The build’s automated checks passed, but they are not your verdict. The sheet’s colours and its place on screen on each of the four bars could only be reasoned from the code, not measured in a browser, so Parts A to F are the real test of that.

Where and who

Who
Several people, named in each part: an operator (someone who runs the tournament, ideally one who also coaches, so the sheet shows two rows), a fan with an account (no role on the event), a signed-out visitor, a club staff member, a coach of two teams, and a support console staff login. Use a private window per person.
Server
On the computer, leave it at http://localhost:3000. For a phone on the same Wi-Fi, change it to the computer’s address. The box only changes the links on this page.
Widths
A real phone is best. Otherwise Chrome’s device toolbar (Ctrl+Shift+M) at 390, then 360, and 320 for the marketing bar. The swap point is checked at 900 and 901.
The new look
The tournament band is part of the new public look, which is behind a switch. The tournament links on this page turn it on for that browser window, then open the page.
Fixture
“Stripe Walk Invitational” under UAT Rep Club for the tournament page, and UAT Rep Club’s own public site for the club bar. Use the logins you normally use for those. If you want me to name them in chat, say so.
Saved progress
Ticks, notes and verdicts stay in this browser only. Use “Copy this walk’s result” and paste it into chat.

Known and accepted, so they are not fails

  • Going to your own side from a public page is now two taps (open the sheet, tap the row) instead of one. You accepted this (Ask 5). The way back from Admin, Coach and Scorekeeper screens is still one tap.
  • No drag-to-close. The little grab line is only a mark, as on the other sheets. Tap outside, ×, Esc or a row closes it.
  • If the account menu is in the demo sandbox, it is absent by design. Part K checks that the door is still there.
