What “ready” actually means

1The price is clear

Currency, billing period and mandatory fees agree everywhere the buyer sees them.

2The charge is correct

The right product, seller, location evidence and tax registrations drive the final total.

3The books reconcile

Revenue, taxes, fees, refunds, FX and payouts have a traceable, deduplicated record.

4The process repeats

Threshold reviews, tax filings and monthly close have named owners and exception alerts.

First work package

Resolve the operating model

  1. Verify live Stripe settings and existing customer/payment history.
  2. Obtain the accountant’s jurisdiction and fee-treatment assessment.
  3. Set USD subscription amounts and prove the margins.
  4. Compare accounting systems and demonstrate a reconciliation.
  5. Confirm Canada/U.S. Connect capabilities with Stripe.
The customer test

California. Quebec. One consistent promise.

California subscription: the selected USD price stays USD from marketing through renewal. Taxes follow the approved product and jurisdiction rules.

Quebec subscription: the CAD price stays CAD; GST/QST handling follows reviewed registration, customer and product facts.

Cross-border registration: a U.S. family paying a Canadian event may pay CAD. Event currency and subscription currency follow different policies.

The important boundary: automation needs a reviewed model.Stripe can calculate taxes and move money. The accountant and counsel must establish the taxable supply, registration duties and responsible entity. The application and finance workflow must close the remaining reporting and reconciliation gaps. Read the detailed plan.
