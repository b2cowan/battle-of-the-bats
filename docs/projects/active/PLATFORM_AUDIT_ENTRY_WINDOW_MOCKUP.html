Audit log: opening an entry

Today a row in the audit log only shows what it shows. After this change, clicking a row opens a window with the whole entry: who, when, which org, and the old and new values laid out in full. The window carries up to two doors that take you to the thing the entry was about. Example values below are made up for the drawing.

Newadded   Restyledchanged from today   Unchangedas it is today. The frames use the console's real look; open this page on your phone to judge the phone drawing at its real width.

RestyledEach row now opens the entry: a quiet › sits at the right edge (the same one the Orgs and People lists use) and the row tints when you point at it. The raw action key and the small ▶ in the two value columns leave the row, because the window shows them in full. UnchangedToolbar, filters, Export, the org name link and its Filter link (each still does its own job when clicked, without opening the window), and paging.

2 · The window, on a computer

NewThe console's own window (the 640 width the record windows use), opened from the third row above. Reads first, nothing to type. The page behind stays where it was, so closing returns you to the same row and filters.

  • The change. Old and new side by side in full, with the lines that differ tinted. The sentence under the panes says what the tint means, so colour is never the only signal.
  • Same moment. The three entries beside it from this person in this second, one tap each (it swaps this window to that entry). Your UAT setup shows up as one event instead of four unrelated rows. Hidden when there are none.
  • The foot. Two doors in olive text (the console's rule: a control that goes somewhere is olive text; an outlined button is for an act that opens a window). No lime button, since there is no main action here. Copy sits above the panes in plain ink, because it acts in place and goes nowhere; it says "Copied" for a moment after you press it.
  • Going into a same-moment entry. The second window below is what you see after tapping one: a "‹ Update plan and limit" line above the title takes you back to where you were (on a phone, the ← at the top left does the same).

4 · Which doors each kind of entry gets

NewAt most two, taken from what the entry is about. The org's own page, its entries, or the screen where that kind of thing is managed. The rule is the one from the budget report's drill-in: two is a ceiling, and an entry with nothing to open gets none. A door never appears unless the entry carries enough to find its target, so none of them can lead to a missing page. Which exact screen each opens is confirmed against the console when this is built.

What the window deliberately does not have

No Revert, no Edit, no Delete. An entry stores the old value of one field, not the whole record around it, so a revert from here would be a guess. A revert is also a consequential admin action in its own right, and the log is meant to be the record nobody changes. To put something back, go to the org (Open org) and use the screen that makes the change, which asks why and writes its own entry.

Calls for you

  1. 1 · The whole row opens, not just a › at the end.Recommended: the whole row (and the whole card on a phone), with the › as the cue. The org name and its Filter link keep working on their own. Alternative: only the › opens it, which is safer against stray clicks but a smaller target.
  2. 2 · The two value columns lose the ▶, and the raw key leaves the row.Recommended: yes. The window shows both in full, and the key is what makes the Action cell wrap mid-word today. Alternative: keep the ▶ for a quick peek without opening the window.
  3. 3 · Tint the lines that differ.Recommended: yes, with the one-line sentence under the panes. It matters most on the bigger entries where only one value in a long object moved.
  4. 4 · The "Same moment" list.Recommended: yes, and quiet. It turns a burst of four rows into one event, and costs nothing when there is nothing beside it. It does add a level inside the window, so the drawing shows the "‹" way back. Alternative: the console's Newer / Older steps with a position ("3 of 100"), which have no level to come back from, but do not group a burst for you.
  5. 5 · Two doors, and "Show this person's entries" stays out.Recommended: keep to two, in olive text as the console's rule has it. The search box already finds a person by email. Alternative: outlined buttons instead of olive text (you said "button"; the console reserves those for acts that open a window), or swap "Show this org's entries" for the person when the entry has no org.
  6. 6 · The org door's name.The drawing says "Open org", as this page's own Org column does. The console's menu now calls that place Clubs & portals, and a door is named for what it opens. Alternative: "Open club". Either way the other doors use the menu's names: Leads, Staff, Bulk changes, Plans & pricing, Feedback.
