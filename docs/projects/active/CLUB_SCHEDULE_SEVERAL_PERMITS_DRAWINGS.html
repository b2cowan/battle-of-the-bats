A schedule on several permits

One spring season on two towns’ permits, a fourth month added after it was sent, time found by narrowing, dates off by park, and a bill priced per permit or at one rate. Drawn on one fixture: Harbourtown Minor Ball, four rep teams and a house league. Every figure below is computed on this page from that fixture. Windows are drawn alone at true width (New schedule and the bill 800 px, Send 520 px); the schedule page is a 1440 × 900 laptop; the phone is 390 × 844. Stage 13d is not built yet, so its treasurer view is drawn from its ruled plan.
