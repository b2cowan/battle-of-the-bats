Screen 1 · the two Ledgers as they are

Two Ledgers that were ruled to read alike

On 2026-09-30 you ruled that the club’s book reads exactly like the coach’s Ledger, with two deliberate differences. Laid side by side, five more differences show up that nobody decided. Each numbered marker opens what the screen does today and what the proposal changes.

  • Row dates. “Sep 1, 2026” on the coach’s, “Aug 12” on the club’s.
  • Balance rows. The club’s say which day they stand on; the coach’s don’t.
  • How a row opens. One arrow on the club’s; a pencil or a worded button on the coach’s.
  • Phone cards. Two different card layouts for the same kind of row.
  • Payees. The club can tidy its payee list; a team can’t tidy its own.

W1 · The coach’s Ledger at a desk

What this walk is for

The coach’s Ledger read differently from the club’s in five ways nobody chose. Now a row prints the day, the balance lines carry the full dates, every row opens and ends in one arrow, and a row another tab wrote opens a short window that says where it is changed.

It passes when

Every row opens and its last column is one arrow (Record beside it on an unpaid installment, and nothing else); the dates read as the rule says; a derived row’s window names its tab and opens it; a row you recorded opens its form exactly as before.

Not in this walk

The phone (W2), Tools and the Payees page (W3), the club (W4), the payee picker (W5).

  1. Sign in as the coach. Open Money → Ledger (View Timeline; leave Date on Around today).

    Row dates read the day only (“Sep 1”, “Oct 1”). The first line reads STARTING BALANCE · date, 2026 and the last ENDING BALANCE · date, 2026. Open the Date pill: those are the two dates it holds.

  2. Run your eye down the last column.

    Every row ends in one arrow. No pencil and no “Player Dues →” / “Club →” buttons. One row, Diamond permit — fall block — installment 1 of 2 (Overdue), also has Record beside its arrow; no other row has a button.

  3. Look at the names in the What column.

    Regular weight, as before (not bold). The detail after a name (“· Avery Test”) is in the quieter grey.

  4. Click anywhere on a Dues payment row (not on the name).

    A window titled Dues payment · Oct 1: Amount ($… in), Category (Player dues), Family, How it was paid, On. Under the box: “Recorded on Player Dues. Change it there, and the Ledger follows.” Two buttons: Close and Open Player Dues ›.

  5. Press Open Player Dues, then come back to the Ledger.

    The Player Dues tab opens. Back on the Ledger, your filters and place are as you left them.

  6. Click the Chocolate sale row, then the League registration — shared across teams row.

    Chocolate sale: Raised by a player, How it was paid (E-Transfer), “Recorded on Fundraising…”, Open Fundraising. League registration: Installment #2, Due, Status “Overdue by N days”, “The club’s bill, on the Club tab. Pay it there, and the Ledger follows.”, Open Club.

  7. Click Practice balls and tees (a cost you recorded), close it; then click Diamond permit — fall block.

    The first opens its money form, editable, exactly as before. The second opens the bill’s own room, as before. Close both without changing anything.

  8. Press Record on the Diamond permit row.

    The Record a payment form opens, aimed at that installment with its amount suggested. Cancel (don’t save).

  9. Keyboard: press Tab until a row’s name is focused, then Enter.

    The same window or form opens as a click does. Escape closes it.

  10. Open ⋯ Tools in the toolbar (between Export and Add a bill).

    A menu with one heading, THE TEAM’S LISTS, and one item: Payees — “Rename a payee or merge two spellings”. Press Escape.

  11. Set Date to Whole season.

    The first line names the season’s first day with its year. If the team carried money from last season it reads OPENING BALANCE · date · carried from … and the whole line opens Team settings › Money (an arrow at its end, no “Change →” button). If not, it reads Starting balance — tick and note which.

  12. Switch to Dark and repeat steps 1, 2 and 4.

    Everything reads clearly; the window’s box and its sentence are legible on the dark ground.

Ledger Parity

What changes

For a coach, on Money › Ledger:

  • Every row opens when clicked, and ends in one arrow. A row that came from Player Dues, Fundraising or Club opens a short window that says where it is changed, with one button to go there.
  • Rows show the day (“Oct 1”); the Starting and Ending balance lines carry the full dates of the window.
  • On a phone, each row is the same clean card the club’s treasurer sees.
  • A Payees page lets the team rename a payee or merge two spellings into one. It opens from a Tools menu on the Ledger and from the payee picker’s last row.
  • The payee picker shows only the payees the club chose to share, under “Shared by your club”, with one line saying the club sees payments to them.

For a club treasurer: the balance lines gain the year; Transfer and Payees move behind a Tools menu; each club payee can be shared with the teams or kept as the club’s own; and a shared payee shows what each team recorded paying it (with Stage 3b).

Who sees what: anyone who can read the team’s money can open every row and every window; renaming and merging payees is for those who can enter the team’s money. The club’s own payees stay the club’s to manage.

Full assessment

Ledger Parity — plan

Problem and context

The club’s Ledger (Club Tier Stage 3a, ruled 2026-09-30, Ask 6) was built to read exactly like the coach’s, with two deliberate differences: the figure is called Balance, and the Date filter opens on This month. A comparison of the two screens on 2026-10-01, read from the code and measured in the browser, found five more differences with no recorded reason. The plan source is docs/projects/active/LEDGER_PARITY_PLAN.md.

The decisions

D1 — Row dates: the statement convention. Rows print the day only, on both portals; the year is said once, on the balance rows (D2).

D2 — The balance rows name their dates. Both portals: “Starting balance · Sep 1, 2026” and “Ending balance · Oct 31, 2026”. The club’s note under its table goes.

D3 — A row opens from anywhere; one arrow. On the coach’s Ledger every row opens. A typed entry or a bill opens as today. A row another tab wrote opens a read window naming its home, with one button to it: the club’s window for an allocation line, shared rather than copied. The pencil and the worded buttons leave the cell; the name keeps the coach’s regular weight, and the club’s Ledger drops its bold to match (revised by the owner 2026-10-02); Record stays beside the arrow on an unpaid installment (ruled 2026-09-04).

D4 — One phone card. The coach’s phone card takes the club’s card recipe (register K-25), and the balance lines become plain lines between the cards. The recipe moves to the shared kit so the two cannot drift again.

D5 — Payees for every team. A Payees page under the coach’s Money for the team’s own list: rename, merge, never delete one in use. Doors: a Payees button beside Export, the View menu’s foot on a phone, and a last row in the payee picker.

D6 — Payees behind Tools. Not a tab. Both Ledgers end with the book or view menu, Export, Tools (⋯), then the lime add. Tools holds Transfer (club) and Payees, grouped; a sheet on a phone. The payee picker’s last row is Manage payees… (the club’s picker already has it).

D7 — Shared payees. The club marks a payee Shared with teams; only shared payees reach a team’s picker, under “Shared by your club”, with the notice line. A team can merge its own duplicate into a shared payee. The shared payee’s window shows what each team recorded paying it (Stage 3b). Logged with /strategy 2026-10-02 (it widens Club Tier D1 narrowly). Open: D7a (no retroactive view) and D7b (a payee already used starts shared).

Round 3 — open (2026-10-02)

D8 — Payees, a window. The Payees page becomes a window over the Ledger in the bill window’s frame: the list, then a payee in the same window behind “← Payees”. From the payee picker inside an open form it opens over the form and closes back to it. D8a asks the same for the club’s Payees (not drawn).

D9 — A payee’s record. Reads first: Paid this season, Still to pay, Next due; this season’s entries as the Ledger’s own rows; the note; one pencil for name and note; Merge (or Delete when unused) in the foot; Prev and Next. The list’s columns become Paid, Still to pay and Last paid. D9a: this season only. D9b, a defect found while drawing: the bill window prints the payee’s typed name, so a rename or merge never reaches it, and a bill with no typed name loses its payee on the next edit there.

D10 — Tools holds the team’s lists. Payees, Categories & items and Money tags, each opening its existing window; the alternative is a plain Payees button.

Out of scope. Everything on screen 7; the club’s side of Stage 3b; the window-footer ruling you still owe; the sizes of either table, which already match.

Build notes

  • D1 and D2 are one date rule each, in one place per portal; the coach’s register and its Starting / Opening / Ending lines read the window the Date filter already holds.
  • D3: the read window for a derived row reads only what the row already carries (amount, category, date, who, method) and names its tab. It shows for read-only money assistants too: it reads, and its door is a link they can already follow.
  • D5: the team-scoped payee routes are new (list with entry counts, rename, merge), gated on the team’s money-write permission; merging repoints every entry that names the payee in one database step. The club already has that step for its own list (migration 315), so the build either widens it to a team’s list or adds its twin. That is a migration, and the data dictionary changes with it.
  • Guards: no ledger cell on either portal holds a button other than Record; both portals format a ledger date through one rule; the shared phone card is the only phone card the two Ledgers render.
  • Help: the coach’s Ledger articles (opening a row, the dates, Payees) and the club’s Ledger article (the note), by /docs. Demo tours: check any stop that points at a row’s pencil or worded button.

Rollout

One build, D1 to D5 together, behind no flag. One migration if D5 needs a team-scope merge step; it goes to production before the promote that carries the page. A QA walk on this hub when it is built.
