Adjust the rules: a season’s Schedule health, scored by rules you set

Written 2026-10-28 for Stage 9d-2b, Part 6. One purpose; sign it off on its own.

What this walk is for

Schedule health’s window already says the rules a house league season is scored by (walk §411 said there was no way to change them). It now has the tournament’s Adjust the rules door at its foot, for whoever runs the season: three rules, saved on the season. The score above follows each rule as you change it, before anything is saved. This walk changes a season’s rules, watches the score move, closes without saving, saves, and puts the defaults back.

It passes when

  • The door is at the window’s foot for the owner; the three rules open under it: A team can play twice in a day, Home and away can differ by, and one Games per team field for each division.
  • The score and tiles follow the draft live: on Dev House League 2026, 65 becomes 70 with Home and away at 2 games, and 60 with Division A at 3 games per team.
  • Unsaved rules ask before the window closes, and the issue doors that leave the window are gone until they are saved or discarded.
  • Save rules keeps them on the season; Restore defaults and Save puts the defaults back.
  • A registrar reads the rules in words with no door; a finished season has no door either.
  • Generate games’ check follows the season’s rules, and the tournament’s own Adjust the rules still works as before.

Not in this walk

  • A finished season’s refusal on a Save. There is no door to press on one, so the server’s refusal (“This season is finished, so its rules can’t change.”) can’t be reached from the screen.
  • Generate games dropping “plays twice” where the season allows it. Dev data can’t make a team play twice in a day, so Part F reads the other half: the check’s score following the rules.
  • The help articles: they are rewritten once for all of 9d-2b in Part 7.

Before you start

Needs
Nothing to make. Parts A to E use Dev House League 2026 in dev-league-org. Part F uses UAT Rep Club’s 2026 Fall House League and the Part 4 / 9d-2a fixture (see F1). The numbers 65, 70 and 60 are from the dev data on 2026-10-28 (the build’s oracle); if its games have changed since, the numbers move with them. What matters is that each one goes the same way.
Dev server
Restart it first: Part 6 added a route (saving the rules) and new files, and migration 412 (dev only) is what keeps the rules. If the window has no Adjust the rules door, restart it.
Known gap
The save route and the window were committed without being run through the dev server (it needed a restart). This is their first real run, so a Save that says “The rules didn’t save. Try again.” is a finding, not a slip.
Start state
Both seasons on the default rules: A team can play twice in a day No, Home and away can differ by 1 game, Games per team empty for every division. Open Adjust the rules once and look before you begin; if a season is not on these, press Restore defaults and Save rules.
End state
The same defaults on both seasons. D3 puts back Dev House League 2026’s, F3 puts back 2026 Fall House League’s. Nothing else is left changed (Part G saves nothing).
