Nothing ever disappears: a category that isn't part of tonight's plan still shows as a small count badge on every player who has one — tap it and the actual words are right there. Only the WRITING collapses, never the fact that it exists.
iWhy lead with the sentence, not just the count

Tonight's own category is the one thing on this screen a coach actually needs to read and act on — the words themselves ("stay through the ball"), not just that hitting is a topic. Everything else is context worth knowing is there, not worth reading in full unless asked.

iWhy every category still shows, even collapsed

This is the standing rule the whole rail is built on: a player must never look like they have nothing going on just because it isn't tonight's topic. A badge that says "Catching · 1" keeps that promise at a tenth of the space a written-out chip costs.

What this settles, and what's still open

Settled by what's already built: one tag vocabulary already drives both "what this practice is about" and every player's focus-area tag — this proposal doesn't add a new relationship, it changes how the existing one is drawn.

Still open, and bigger than this screen: this is one view of a focus area. The player page is where a coach adds, edits and retires them, and that page needs the same category vocabulary to mean the same thing there as it does here — worth carrying into that conversation rather than settling in isolation.
