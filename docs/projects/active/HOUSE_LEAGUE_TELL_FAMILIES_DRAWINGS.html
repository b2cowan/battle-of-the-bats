Asks

Each has a recommendation. Anything you don’t answer goes in as recommended.

1The banner stays until you act, and only people who run the division see it

Recommended: yes.

No timer, no auto-send, no prompt when you leave the page. The banner is there when you come back. People who can view the division but not run it see nothing. The division picker also gets the small amber count it already supports for divisions that are waiting on something, so you can see an untold division from the list.

2Per family: Don’t email this family, inside the review window

Recommended: yes.

For the family in front of you. A family you switch off stays out of this send and counts as told.

3Don’t email: one confirm, saved to Email families, treated as told

Recommended: yes.

After a skip, each child counts as told the team they are on now. A later move then says “moved from Cardinals”, which is true. Send again on a registration still emails them any time.

4A small amber “Not told” chip on each untold child

Recommended: yes, a chip with words, only on children, never on team cards.

It answers “who does the banner mean?” without opening the window. I drew it as a chip, not a dot: the design log says a plain dot carries no information and removed one for that reason. Tell me if you would rather the board stay completely unmarked.

5The email names only the children who changed

Recommended: changed children only.

A parent with three children whose one child moved sees one line. The alternative lists every child every time, which repeats what they already know and hides what changed.

6Phone: banner buttons under the words, review as a full-screen form

Recommended: yes, exactly as the shipped Callout and form windows behave.

Same button order as desktop (outline, then lime). Every button is at least 44 px tall. The per-family Don’t email button is a text button, so it gets its own 44 px row inside each family.

Also changing, no decision needed

The old line “Moving a player now emails their family” is replaced. The one-player Move and email and Place and email windows are removed. Taking a player off a team still emails nobody. The Place and email help text, the help article and the family-app walk are rewritten after the build.
