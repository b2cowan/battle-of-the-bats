UNCHANGEDHeader chips, title, test-mode line, refund policy.

Two corrections to what I said earlier. (1) Edit mode already saves as you go: choosing a radio saves at once, and the refund policy saves when you click out of it. A small Saved pill appears bottom-right and fades. The check mark means “Done”, so it needs no Save. (2) I proposed a labelled Done, a Saved line and Done under the form. The design review found the product’s rules say no: one pencil-and-check for every record, and Done was taken out of record windows on purpose. So those are dropped. What remains is layout: the form comes first, the rows stay put, and the Stripe buttons step aside.

Seven calls. My recommendation is first in each. Answer by letter, for example “A1 yes, A3 explicit Save”.

  • A1

    Turn the paragraph into table rows

    “Payouts go to” and “Receipts say” join the table; the rates line above it is removed.

    Recommend yes. It ends the paragraph-then-table split and the duplicated rates. Alternative: leave the paragraph and only remove the duplicate.

  • A2

    Is the icon-only pencil a problem across the product?

    Every record in the product opens with the same bare pencil, then a check mark. I first wanted a labelled “Edit” here. That would make this one card differ from all the others.

    Recommend leave it on this card. If you think people miss the pencil everywhere, that is a product-wide change, its own piece of work.

  • A3

    Saving stays exactly as it is

    Autosave, the check mark, and the small Saved pill all stay. No new Save, Cancel, Done or status line.

    Recommend yes. A saved fee choice can’t be taken back by pressing Cancel, so a Cancel button would promise something untrue.

  • A4

    Edit mode shows the same rows

    Editable rows turn into controls; the others stay, faded; “Other Stripe fees” stays.

    Recommend yes. The card stops changing shape, and the form no longer starts halfway down.

  • A5

    Where does Disconnect live?

    Today it is the last line of the card, the product’s “rare door” spot on every record. The only problem is the gap above it. Options: (a) keep it last and tighten the gap; (b) put it on the Open Stripe row, quiet, at the right.

    Recommend (a). It matches every other record in the product (archive, delete, bring back), and the fix is spacing. (b) would make Payouts the one card that does it differently. Either way it is hidden in edit mode (A6).

  • A6

    Hide Open Stripe and Disconnect while editing

    Recommend yes. They aren’t part of the form.

  • A7

    The two wording slips (tab 3)

    Recommend yes to both, routed through /marketing before they are built.
