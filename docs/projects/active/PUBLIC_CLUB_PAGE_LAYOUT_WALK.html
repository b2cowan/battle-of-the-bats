Every club public page, one layout

Written 2026-11-05. Test data only, on the dev database. About 60 minutes. One purpose: clicking through every club public page and into the club’s tournament and back, on a desk and a phone, the content starts, stops and spaces itself the same way, and only the navigation changes place.

What this walk is for

The club build (Parts 1 to 4) put every club public page on one column that starts on the club logo’s left edge, with one title rule, one heading row, one game row, one set of buttons, and a two-way link between a team’s club page and its tournament page. This is the first look at all of it together, including the part no automated check can see: colour. A review found that, on a desk, every focus ring and every filled button’s border in the club’s colour had been dropped by the Part 1 layout. That is fixed. Part B below is how you confirm it.

Calls you made are in this build and are not re-asked: the in-club error page (yes), the record’s results on the game row (yes), house-league games away team first (yes). One more, today: a played game keeps its start time on a phone on the house league’s Schedule and on a past tournament’s record, because those pages have no game page where the time could live. The tournament’s own Schedule still drops it.

It passes when

  • A: clicking through every club page on a desk, nothing but the navigation moves. The content starts at the same left edge (the club logo’s), stops at the same width, and sits the same distance under the band.
  • B: pressing Tab through a page on a desk shows a clear ring on every stop, and every filled button has its border.
  • C: a team’s club page and its tournament page link to each other, for the club’s own team only.
  • D: the house league’s Schedule and Standings and a past tournament’s record sit on the same column and the same game row, and a played game still shows its time on a phone.
  • E: a failed page stays inside the club’s site; a missing page reads the same way.
  • F and G: on a phone every page is one column with nothing scrolling sideways, and between 1,024 and 1,199 px wide the two-column pages stack.

Before you start

Setup, not checked. Some steps change test data or ask Claude to break a page on purpose; they say when to ask. You can also paste the command shown under a step into a terminal in the project folder.

Where
http://localhost:3000 on your computer, dev server running. Use a private window so a sign-in does not change what you see. No sign-in is needed anywhere in this walk.
The club
UAT Rep Club (purple), /uat-rep-club. Its tournament is UAT Rep Club Invitational 2026. The navy rep-only club /uat-rep-only is used once, for tryout days.
On a phone
Either your phone on the same Wi-Fi, at your computer’s address on the network (last walk: http://192.168.2.200:3000), or the browser window narrowed to about 390 px wide.
Window sizes
“On a desk” means a window at least 1,200 px wide. Part G uses about 1,100 px.
Changed for this walk
Three things on dev, all yours to approve and all undone in Part Z: 15U AAA’s schedule is set to public (so its tournament games show on the club page; it is also in the seed script, so a reseed keeps it); one played house-league game has a score (7 to 3); and, for Part E only, Claude makes two pages fail on purpose.
The layout check
Claude ran it on a production build of this code: the 14 club screens at 320 / 361 / 390 / 768 / 1,440 px. It found one real problem, the tournament-game links on a team’s club page measured under the 44 px tap size on a phone (fixed: the link now has a 44 px area with nothing moving), and nothing else. The house league’s Schedule and Standings, the record and the not-found page are not on that screen list, so Claude measured them separately at 390 and 1,536 px: no sideways scroll on any, names at 24 px on the logo edge. Not measured anywhere: colour. That is Part B.
Cannot be shown on this test data
(1) Game rows on a past tournament’s record: the one past record has no games saved in it, so only its heading and standings area can be seen. (2) “Up next” with its Directions button on a tournament game: all four of the club team’s Invitational games are already in the past. Both are pinned by tests, not walked.
Leaves behind
Nothing, once Part Z is done.
