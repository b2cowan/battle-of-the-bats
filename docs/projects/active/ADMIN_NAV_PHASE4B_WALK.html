Navigation across the three portals · Phase 4b, the Coaches Portal’s tabs and help · Owner QA Ledger §517

On a computer, tabs stay pinned under the strip, and the “?” moves up beside the bell

On a computer, every Coaches Portal page that has tabs (Money, Insights, Skills & goals, Lineups, Practice plans) keeps them in a slim band directly under the top strip, full width, so they stay put as you scroll. The page’s buttons (Record, Import, Add player) stay in the page, below the band. The page’s help “?” moves into the top strip, between the bell and your account button, so a page with no buttons no longer spends a row on it. The phone is unchanged.
