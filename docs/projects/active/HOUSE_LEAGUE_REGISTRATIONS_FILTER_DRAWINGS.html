What a registrar can filter by

Today the Filter has one group, Fee. This drawing adds two more: Team, for placing children, and Flags, for the few registrations that need someone to act. Fee keeps its two options. Each option shows how many registrations ticking it would list, given whatever else is ticked, so a zero says "nobody" before you tap.

  • Tags: . Click a tag to read what it means.
  • Desk drawings shrink to fit your window. Use "Show at true size" to see them at 1200 px wide. Phone drawings are 390 px wide, so open this page on your phone to judge them.
  • The sample division (U12, 48 children, four teams, 12 not yet placed) is made up for the drawing. Its counts are worked out from what else is ticked in each frame. The season in the drawing turns owing amber after 14 days, the setting's default.
  • Click a dashed row in any menu, or a tag under a drawing, to read what it does.

3 · On a phone (390 px)

The same menu as a sheet

On a phone the Filter is the sliders symbol, a 44 px square beside Search and Tools. It opens the same options as a sheet above the portal's bar, labelled FILTER, with no close button: a tap on the page or the bar closes it. With three groups the sheet is taller than its three-quarter cap, so it scrolls; every row is 44 px tall. The phone "before" is drawn from the screen's stylesheet, not a screenshot, and the bar is simplified.

5 · Considered and left out

No waiver on record

The registration record doesn't show whether the family accepted the waiver, so this filter would find children without saying why. It would also catch every child the desk added by hand and every sign-up from before the waiver was recorded. If it's wanted, the record shows the waiver first and the filter follows.

Part received

Today "Owing" includes children who have paid part of their fee, and the row says "$80 received · $85 owing". A separate option would change what "Owing" means. Few families pay in parts, so leave it until it comes up.

Signed up with a sibling, jersey, position, sign-up date

These help when building teams, not when finding a registration. The export already has them, and sign-up date suits a sort better than a filter.

Design review · what changed in this round

  • Counts now follow the other ticks (Owing 8 and Received 4 once "Not placed" is ticked), and a zero stays on the menu.
  • "Owing over 30 days" became "Owing 14 days or more": the words come from the season's own amber setting and match its rule. The amber dot is gone, since the row already carries the amber.
  • Switching division drops team ticks, so a tick can never be hidden where you can't untick it.
  • Option B's hint moved into the Team heading's right-hand slot instead of adding a new kind of row.
  • The desk menu is drawn at its real width and cap, so it shows that it scrolls too.
  • The phone sheet is the real one: FILTER label, grab line, no close button, sitting above the portal's bar. Search on a phone is a 44 px square, not an open box.
  • Rows use the product's real lines: "Signed up Jul 3 · Asked to withdraw Oct 20", "Jul 14 · E-Transfer".
  • Fee's table row was wrong: Fee is offered on every tab today. A free season's Filter is described. "Flags" became ask 6.
