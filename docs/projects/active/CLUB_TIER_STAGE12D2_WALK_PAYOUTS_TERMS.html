12d-2, payouts and the terms

Written 2026-10-27 for the 12d-2 build (ffee7864c to 2a171752a). One purpose: a club can match a bank line to the people behind it without Stripe or the owner, and the owner can see, and agree again to, the payments terms.

What this walk is for

The treasurer sees “STRIPE 1,284.50” on the bank statement and needs to know whose money it was. You check the new Payouts tab at the end of Accounting: what it lists, how Find matches an amount, a name and a receipt, what an opened payout holds, and the Export (part A). Then the same list folded on the owner’s card for a club with no Accounting (part B).

Then the terms: who agreed to the payments terms and when, read on the owner’s card (part C). The real test follows: you change the terms’ version on dev, so the owner is asked to agree again, with payments still on (part D). Last, the set-up window gives Continue to Stripe only once the box is ticked (part E).

It passes when

  • Payouts is the last tab in Accounting, for everyone who reads Accounting; a club that never connected has no tab and its address is “Page not found”.
  • Find takes an amount as the bank prints it, part of a name, or a whole receipt number, and nothing else; every list ends honestly (“No payouts in these dates.”, “No payouts match.”, “Stripe didn’t answer. Try again.”).
  • An opened payout lists Date · Who · What · Receipt · Amount, closes on “Paid to the bank account ending … on …”, and no line reads as a bare Stripe word.
  • The treasurer has no button on the tab that moves money; a payout that didn’t arrive is a plain line for them and an amber button for the owner.
  • The owner reads who agreed to the terms, when, and to which version. When the version changes the owner gets a notice and a row that says there are newer terms; online payments stay on.
  • Agree is absent until the box is ticked, in both windows (agree again, and set up payouts). Changing who pays the fee waits for the new terms.

Not in this walk

  • Leaving online payments (disconnect, downgrade, cancel, delete, the ending warning) and the support console: walk §405.
  • Both emails about the terms (the day they change, the reminder 14 days later) are sent by the daily job, and localhost cannot send. They go in the Amplify dev end-to-end pass (part F, held). The owner’s Needs attention item for new terms comes from the same daily job, so its two steps in part D are held too.
  • A payout that didn’t arrive and a manual payout can’t be made on demand in test mode; their steps are held unless one already exists in Stripe’s test account.
  • Paid twice, disputes and Stripe fees: walks §395 and §396.

Before you start

Setup, not checked. Keep this page private.

Dev server
Running at http://localhost:3000. Restart it once before part D’s first step (the builder step changes a shared module).
Payouts in test mode
UAT Rep Club’s test Stripe account pays out daily, automatically, with a 7-day delay. The payouts tab reads Stripe live, so a payout exists only once Stripe has made one. If part A step A3 shows “No payouts yet…”, mark A3 and the steps that need a payout as Held and come back after Stripe’s next daily payout.
To put money in the account that is available at once, a family can pay with the test card 4000 0000 0000 0077 (any future date, any CVC); that payment goes in the next payout. Other test card: 4242 4242 4242 4242.
Stripe window (only for that payment)
C:\tools\stripe\stripe.exe listen --events-from '@accounts' --events "checkout.session.completed,checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded,checkout.session.async_payment_failed,checkout.session.expired,charge.dispute.created,charge.dispute.updated,charge.dispute.closed,charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn,charge.dispute.funds_reinstated,refund.created,refund.updated,refund.failed" --forward-to localhost:3000/api/payments/connect-webhook
Part D changes a constant
One line in lib/online-payment-words.ts: PAYMENTS_TERMS_VERSION. D1 sets it to a date; D10 puts it back and resets UAT Rep Club’s row. Nothing else in the product reads a version from anywhere.
