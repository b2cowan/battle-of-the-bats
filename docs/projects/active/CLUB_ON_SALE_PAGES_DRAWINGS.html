Flip it as-is and a visitor reads "Express interest", "Coming soon" and "We set up Club with you directly"; the Club and League Plus buttons make a tournament organization; there is no Club · Association card; League Plus says "No credit card required" over a trial that takes a payment method. The audience review (added 18 Nov)

The first drawings switched the words over. This review asked a different question: walking each page as a tournament organizer, a league or club board, and a coach, would this page make me start? It reads every public page and checks each promise against what the product does today. What it adds sits on top of the first drawings, in the same plan, as part of S3 and a new S3b.

The five changes that matter most

  1. Let a league or club buyer see the product before they add a payment method. A tournament organizer and a coach each get a live demo. The two plans going on sale have no demo and no picture on any page. The fix inside your "no club demo at launch" ruling: the product's own screens on both pages (tabs 4 and 5), and four plain steps on what happens to the payment method during the trial.
  2. Sell Club to the whole board. The buyer we aim at first runs house league and rep teams together. Today's Clubs page speaks only about coaches: all four "old way" cards, and the heading. The treasurer and the registrar read it too, and one of them has to say yes to the money. New: the club's books in the first sentence, two new "old way" cards, and "What each person at your club gets".
  3. Answer the board's questions on the page. Can we switch mid-season, what happens when the treasurer changes, what do you do with our families' information, what if we stop. No page answers any of them today. New: "Questions a board asks" on both pages and in the pricing FAQ, each answer checked against the product and the published policies.
  4. Group by buyer, as you asked, with two refinements. Three bands on /pricing, three doors in the picker and on the home page, and the home page sends people to their page instead of carrying six plan cards. The refinements are under "Where I disagree".
  5. Stop saying what is not true today. Three pricing answers describe customers we do not have. The Premium card says "Free right now" over a $29 plan. "Start Tournament Plus free" opens a checkout that takes a payment method. These are live on production now and change while closed.

Where I disagree with the planning session's proposal

  • Not a ladder. The proposal called League Plus, Club and Club · Association "a clean three-step ladder". Your brand rule says League Plus and Club are peers for different clubs, and a house league must not be told Club is its next step. The band keeps all three side by side but labels each by what a club runs. Club's label says it includes house league, so a club with both does not buy the smaller plan by mistake.
  • No price on the home page doors, and never $29 on the coach door. Your 7 Aug ruling keeps prices off those cards, and its reason still holds for coaches: every coach starts free, so "$29" overstates the cost. Prices move to three short rows lower on the home page (Ask 24 has the one exception worth arguing).
  • Three audiences for buying, four for landing. A house league board and a club board buy from one band, but they search for different things and need different first sentences. The two pages and the two nav entries stay. Only the order changes.
  • Your 17 Nov ruling says marketing comes after the launch. So the additions are split. What S3 rebuilds anyway is built once in its final shape (the bands, the doors, the nav, the trial steps). The selling sections are a separate S3b that does not hold the flip (Ask 30).
