Your house league, walked through

We set up a practice club that mirrors yours: U7 to U15, house-league games at Sam Sherritt, Lions Park and Kinsmen Park, and Sherwood Park kept for rep. The families are made up. Each division sits at a different point in the season, so we can show a full year in one sitting by following one pretend child from sign-up to a rained-out Saturday.

The walk, in order

  1. Start

    Your club's own sign-up page

    On your phone: the club's public page with the season and each division's open spots. On the laptop: the season's home, with registrations, teams, schedule, standings, money and family email all in one place.

    Start hereLeague admin window
    1. Phone Scan The club's page card. Look over the season and the division cards.
    2. League admin window Walk the season's tabs: Registrations Teams & draft Schedule Standings Money Email families.
  2. 1

    From sign-up to the club's books

    First, a look at the league as it stands: who's registered and where the money is. Then you sign up a pretend U9 child on your phone and pay with a test card. Back on the laptop, you follow that one sign-up through the registrations, the season's money and the club's books.

    Start hereLeague admin window

    Click the season's Registrations tab.

    Before: the league today
    1. Pick U9. Note how many players are Active and how many spots are left.
    2. Money tab: note Owed, Received and Still owing.
    3. Owner window The club home: "The club today" and what the club is owed. Then Accounting, Ledger: the house league fees line.
    The sign-up
    1. Phone Scan the Sign up a player card, then Register now.
    2. Add a child born in 2018 or 2019 (the division picks itself), then the parent. Tick the waiver and Sign up.
    3. Pay by card with 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future date, any 3 digits. The phone shows "You're signed up" with a reference, and the confirmation email arrives.
    After: follow the new registrant
    1. League admin window Back on Registrations, refresh. The new child is under Active, paid, and U9 has one more player.
    2. While you're there, switch to U13, which is full. In To review, tick three and Approve. In Waitlist, Promote one family, and they're emailed that a spot opened.
    3. Money tab: Received has gone up by the fee. The payment is under Every line.
    4. Owner window Refresh the club home: the figures have moved. Then Ledger: the payment is in the house league fees line.
    Look for
    • The right division is picked from the birth year. Nobody sorts kids by hand.
    • The before and after: one sign-up moves the registrations, the season's money and the club's books, with no export or re-typing.
    • The president sees what's owed and what's waiting without asking you for a spreadsheet.
  3. 2

    Rate, draft and publish U9 teams

    You rate a few players 1 to 5, including your pretend child. You run the draft with siblings kept together, then run it again with that turned off to see the difference. You drag one player to another team, then publish. Families and coaches each get an email.

    Start hereLeague admin window
    1. Pick U9. Rate players: rate your pretend child and a few others (the 1 to 5 keys work too).
    2. Run the draft with Keep families together on. Run it again with it off to see siblings split.
    3. Drag one player to another team.
    4. Publish teams, then Publish and email.
    5. Phone Open the email: "The teams are set".
    Look for
    • Balanced teams in one click, with siblings together when you want them together.
    • You can still adjust by hand before anything goes out.
    • Nothing is sent until you publish. Then every family and coach hears at once.
  4. 3

    Change a team after it's published

    In U15, where families already know their teams, you move a player. The change saves right away and emails nobody. A banner counts the families who haven't been told. You review the list, preview the email, leave one family off and send the rest.

    Start hereLeague admin window

    Stay on Teams & draft from Walk 2 and pick U15.

    1. Move one player to the other team. The banner appears: families haven't been told.
    2. Review and email. Open See an example email.
    3. Choose Don't email this family on one, then send the rest.
    Look for
    • You can fix a roster midweek without families getting surprise emails.
    • You choose who hears, and each household gets one email even with two kids moved.
    • Move a player and move them back, and nobody is told anything.
  5. 4

    U9's games and practices, from the club's diamonds to published

    The club has already given house league its field time. You hand U9 a Saturday morning for games and a Tuesday evening for practices. Practices go out to the teams in one step, and families are told their practice times. Then you generate the games, check the schedule's health and publish. Games and practices sit together on one schedule.

    Start hereLeague admin window

    Click the Schedule tab and pick U9 in Division. U9's teams must be published (Walk 2) before families can be told anything.

    Give U9 its time
    1. Tools, then House league's time. Each park's Saturdays and each weeknight is its own row.
    2. On the Sam Sherritt Saturday-morning row, Give it to, then "U9 games".
    3. On the Sam Sherritt Tuesday-evening row, Give it to, then "U9 practice time". Then Done.
    Practices
    1. Tools, then Practice time. Leave 60 minutes, 1 team at once, 1 a week. It reads "Every team gets 1 practice a week." Then Hand out practices.
    2. "Tell the families their practice times" opens by itself. Choose Send to the families.
    Games, then publish
    1. Tools, then Generate games. U9's Saturday time is already there. Next: match-ups, leave Once, then Check the games and Save 6 games.
    2. Open the schedule health button: games, home and away, byes and clashes. Switch to Week to see games and practices together.
    3. Publish schedule, then Publish.
    4. Phone Two emails: the practice times, then the team's schedule is up.
    Look for
    • Games and practices both come from the club's real fields, shared with rep teams, so nothing is double-booked.
    • One step hands every team a fair practice slot, taking turns at the early and late times.
    • Nothing reaches families until you send it. They get the practice times, then the game schedule, one email each.
  6. 5

    Rainout Saturday

    You pick the coming Saturday, postpone every game across the divisions and add a short note for families. One click postpones the games and emails the families. The games wait in a "needs a new date" list. We also run it once without emailing, to show you can undo it before anyone is told.

    Start hereLeague admin window

    Stay on Schedule from Walk 4.

    1. Rainout. Pick the coming Saturday.
    2. Postpone them, all divisions, with Email the families now ticked.
    3. Add a line such as "Fields are closed. New dates to come." and confirm.
    4. Phone Open the email: the team's games changed.
    5. League admin window The games wait under "Needs a new date". Run Rainout again with the email box unticked, then Put all back.
    Look for
    • One decision covers every game that day.
    • Each household gets one email, not one per game.
    • Nothing goes out until you mean it, and you can put everything back.
  7. End

    Back to the president's view

    The club home now has your season's money in it. The same club account also runs the rep teams and the tournament.

    Start hereOwner window

    Refresh the club home.

The extra walks, step by step

A

Unpaid fees

Where it starts: U13 is full. About a third of families still owe, some for longer than others. One family's price was corrected, one was refunded and one fee was cancelled.

Start hereLeague admin window

Click the season's Money tab.

  1. Still owing: each family shows how many days they've owed. Anyone past the club's limit turns amber.
  2. Refunds: the refunded family and why.
  3. Every line: each charge, payment, correction and cancellation in one list.
Look for
  • Who owes, and for how long, without a spreadsheet.
  • Corrections and refunds stay on the record instead of being typed over.
B

Coaches enter scores, standings keep themselves

Where it starts: U11's teams and schedule are published and earlier games are scored. Two scores are waiting to be checked: one a coach entered, and one the other team flagged.

Start hereCoach window

It opens on the coach's start page. Click the U11 Diamondbacks.

  1. Enter the score on an upcoming game, then Save the score.
  2. League admin window Schedule tab, then Scores to check. One is "Coach entered", one is "Reported".
  3. Keep one score and Correct the score on the other.
  4. Phone Scan The standings card. The tie-break rules sit under the table.
Look for
  • Coaches enter their own scores, so you aren't collecting them by email.
  • If a coach disputes a score, you make the call.
  • Families see standings that update themselves, and the tie-breaks are explained.
C

A registrar without a shared login

Where it starts: The club has an owner, a house-league administrator and a coach. Nobody has the registrar role yet.

Start hereOwner window

In the sidebar, Organization, then Members.

  1. Invite. Enter b2cowan+registrar@gmail.com and set the role to League registrar.
  2. Send invite. The invitation arrives by email.
  3. Open "What each role can open" to compare the roles side by side.
Look for
  • A registrar handles sign-ups, fees, refunds and family email.
  • They never see player ratings, and they can't move a child to another division.
  • A treasurer can read the money and nothing else.
D

Email families, with a record of who was told

Where it starts: By this point the walks above have sent the team, schedule and rainout emails, so the log already has entries.

Start hereLeague admin window

Click the season's Email families tab.

  1. Start a new email. Choose "A division", pick U9, write a short note and send it. The send button shows how many families it will reach.
  2. Owner window In the sidebar, Families, then Emails. Each email shows when it was sent, its subject, who it went to, who sent it and how many it reached.
Look for
  • "Did we tell the U9 parents?" is answered in one click.
  • Each season allows up to three typed emails a day, so families aren't buried. Schedule and rainout notices don't count toward that limit.
The families, children and payments in this practice club are made up. Card payments use a test card, so no money moves.
