The event form warns about a late arrival, and each date of a weekly game picks its opponent

Written 2026-11-04 for Club Tier Stage 6e (built, reviewed). One purpose: two small corrections to the coach’s Add/Edit event form. Nothing else on the form or the schedule changed.

What this walk is for

Two things. First, when a coach sets an arrival time later than the start, the form now says so under the Arrival field (a warning, never a block), and the same note rides beside “Arrive …” on the event window, the game page and the practice sheet. Second, when a coach adds a weekly repeating game, each date’s opponent box is now the same opponent picker a single game uses, so the team’s book of opponents is offered on every date.

It passes when

  • An arrival later than the start shows “That’s after the {time} start.” under the field, and not otherwise (earlier, or equal, shows nothing).
  • The form still saves with that warning showing.
  • The event window, the game page and the practice sheet read “Arrive … (after the start)” for a late arrival and nothing extra for a normal one.
  • Each date row of a weekly game offers the team’s opponents with their records, takes a pick or a new name, and its list is never cut off.

Known before the walk

  • No fixture. Use the coach team you normally walk on dev. It needs at least one finished game, so the opponent book has something to offer.
  • The practice “send to families” message does not carry the note (deliberately left alone).
  • The helper that looked at the code believes editing an existing series shows no per-date opponent boxes (only Add does). Step D1 asks you to confirm.
  • Not walked here, proved by unit tests: the equal-times and not-a-clock cases, and the club-zone reading of a stored start.

Not in this walk

  • Everything else on the event form, the schedule views, and the calendar export.
