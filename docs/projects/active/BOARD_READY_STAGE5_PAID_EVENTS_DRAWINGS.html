Paid events: a banquet or a hosted dinner can charge, and a camp becomes an event with a price and sessions

Round 1 (ruled Nov 24) gave a price only to camps, so a $40 banquet would have had to pretend to be a camp. This round draws a price as something any event can have. Drawn on Riverdale Minor Ball’s year-end banquet: Sat, Jun 26, 2027, 6:00 p.m., Riverdale Legion Hall, 120 spots, $40.00 an adult and $15.00 a child, as of Monday, April 12, 2027. Riverdale doesn’t take card payments yet, like every club today, so its own records show e-transfers; the screens that show cards are tagged “once Riverdale takes cards”. Click any number, tag or red flag to read what it refers to. The last tab holds your asks, numbered 20 to 33 so they don’t collide with round 1’s 1 to 19; the first answer is my recommendation and is already selected.

One way to pay for any event; a camp keeps its sessions, ages and waitlist on top

A paid event isn’t a camp. It sells spots, usually first come with nobody to approve, at a price for each kind of person, and its tax usually runs the other way. So the price moves down from Stage 7 into the event itself, and a camp uses it. Seven things the reviews and the code turned up shaped the asks:

What the window does, and doesn’t do

  • The price is per person. One row is one price for everyone; two rows are Adult and Child, as Riverdale has. The kinds are the club’s words.
  • One payment per sign-up, in full. Five people are one charge of $125.00, so the card’s fixed fee falls once (/accounting). No pay in part: house league has none either .
  • The tax default follows the kind, not whether there’s a price. The hint words are /accounting’s.
  • Who pays the card fee follows the club’s choice on Payouts, “Who pays the processing fee”, as camps do. No third choice.

The reviews this round went through, and what they found

Reviews run before this hand-off

  • /legal (2026-11-25): the paid email is the family’s copy and stands alone; the refund policy shows open before paying, with one fixed line; the Payments Terms must name event tickets (in the version that carries the sponsorship addendum); charge only for a held spot, and refund a payment that lands on a gone one by itself; Stripe gets the event and the counts only; the retention clock must take event forms (16A, not built yet); a club can’t cancel a paid event silently. Privacy §5 and Data Terms §12 gain guest names, meals and allergies. Flags for a lawyer: Ontario’s internet-agreement text and whether the Ticket Sales Act reaches a banquet weren’t opened.
  • /accounting (2026-11-25): our own books are unchanged, apart from a new source kind on the per-fee record. Dinner and event tickets are usually taxable for a non-profit club (CRA RC4081); the fundraising-dinner exemption is for charities and public institutions with a real gift. Default by kind; hint words on tab 2; one charge per sign-up, paid in full; a taxable refund issues a credit note, as Stage 6 does. Flags: each club’s charity status is for its accountant; whether we return our own fee when a club cancels is a /strategy call; donations, raffles and alcohol are left out.
  • Shipped pay screens (read 2026-11-25): every word on tabs 3 to 5 that isn’t new is the shipped one (Your sign-up, the pay block, Mark received, the Fee pills, “Who pays the processing fee”, “Refund policy”).
  • /design (2026-11-25): five must-fixes applied. The cancel button is the kit’s danger button. The phone person rows no longer break. Phone links are 44 px. The banquet’s page and Who’s coming have a phone view (tab 5). Riverdale’s own records show e-transfers, and the card screens are tagged “once Riverdale takes cards”. Also applied: every person drawn on Your sign-up, a short “Held” chip with the date once, the folded-persons note moved out of the frame, and the Fee pill’s change from the shipped one named.

Left out on purpose

  • Donations, raffles and a bar at the banquet (/accounting, /legal).
  • A waitlist for a ticketed event (ask 24).
  • Pay in part, or a deposit (house league has neither).
  • Where the banquet shows on the club site: that is the Club Website project’s now (the split, Nov 24). This round draws only the sign-up page a link opens, which is ours.
