# Awards at any event — PM brief

**Hub:** https://claude.ai/artifact/SbdfdYBKa2rxd35xXRmXTe · **Plan:** `COACH_AWARDS_AT_ANY_EVENT_PLAN.md` · **QA:** ledger §235

## What changes for the coach

A coach can give a player an award at **any event on the schedule** — a practice, a team event like a
banquet or pizza night, or a whole tournament — not only at a game. Once the event has started, its
window shows the same **Awards given** section a scored game has: give an award, edit it, remove it.
Games are unchanged: they still unlock once the final score is in.

Wherever an award is shown, it says what it was for in words the coach recognises: **vs Oakville A's**
for a game, **Practice** (or the practice's own name) for a practice, and the event's or tournament's
name otherwise.

## Why it matters

Coaches hand out recognition at practices all the time — hustle, effort, most improved this week — and
at the year-end party. Today the portal only lets them attach an award to a scored game, so practice
recognition goes unrecorded or gets logged as a vague "General" award. The awards given most often at
the youngest ages are the ones the portal can't hold.

## Customer impact

- **Who:** every Premium coach and the staff they let give awards. Access doesn't change.
- **Where:** the event window, the Give an award form, the Awards report, the player profile totals,
  the season leaderboard and Season Wrapped's "most awarded" all count practice awards automatically.
- **Families:** nothing new to see; a practice award appears in a family's season recap like any other.

## Tradeoffs

- Awards unlock when the event **starts**, not before — no empty awards box on upcoming practices.
  Banquet awards prepared days ahead are still recorded from the Awards report.
- Once an event has started, its awards sit **above attendance** on phone and desktop — the same place a
  game's do. Attendance starts a little lower on a started practice. (First drawn below attendance on a
  phone; revised on the owner's first look at the build.)
- Found on the way: an award for an event starting at 8 p.m. Eastern or later was dated the next day (games
  too). New awards now take the event's own day; older ones were not changed.

## Priority

Small, owner-requested, no data change. Closes a gap a coach hits the first week of a season.

## Success criteria

- A coach gives an award from a practice window in the same two taps as from a game.
- No award tied to an event reads "General" or "vs opponent" anywhere in the portal.
- Before an event starts, its window is identical to today's.
