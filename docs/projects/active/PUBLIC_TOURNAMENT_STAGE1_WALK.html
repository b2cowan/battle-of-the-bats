Every tournament page wears the new frame

What this walk is for. Stage 1 put every public tournament page in the club site's daylight frame, behind a preview switch. The organizer's colour band replaces the dark event header, a white tab row pins under it, and an organizer-colour footer sits at the foot. Every page's current content was made readable on the light ground, and the scores ticker became one quiet line. The layouts did not change; they are Stages 2 to 7. Families and the public demo see none of this until Stage 8 turns it on.

It passes when the preview on gives every page the band, the tab row and the footer, and every page reads cleanly on light at phone and desk widths. The ticker must behave as you ruled (F's fades in D's look, an × only). With the preview off, every page has to look exactly as it does today.

What “reads on light” means in this walk

  • Dark text on white or very light grey, with thin hairline edges. No dark panel left over from the old look.
  • Nothing faint or greyed so far it is hard to read. No text smaller than the page's other small print.
  • No glows and no big shadows. Marks that used to glow are in the organizer's colour instead.
  • The same layout as today. A cramped table or a small tap target is a later stage's work, not a fail here.

Not in this walk

  • A crowned champion's banner. No fixture has a champion crowned, so the Champions page is checked in its empty state only. The banner's trophy and label moved into the stylesheet unchanged.
  • Reduced motion (the line shows the newest result, still, with no fades). The review checked it in the code.
  • Which pages carry the ticker. That is Stages 2 and 3; today it rides on every page.
  • Small tap targets and the free plan's banner. Both are already known and parked.
  • “Live now” after a champion is crowned on the last day. You ruled it changes; that is Stage 2's work, so today's behaviour is not a fail here.
