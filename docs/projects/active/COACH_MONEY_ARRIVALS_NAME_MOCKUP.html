Money redesign · Phase 1 · One open name

Money in, or Income?

Phase 1 splits Expenses & Payables into two tabs. The arrivals list moves across to Transactions unchanged — and the build prompt says to rename it Income. That list holds income and money back, which the product teaches are opposites. Three ways to name it, in place.

Why this is a real question and not a label

The register in Phase 3 has Income and Refunds as separate filters, so the word is exactly right there. In Phase 1 the list is not split — it is the same list it is today, with a Kind column doing the telling. A refund sitting under a heading called Income contradicts the panel printed directly beneath it on the empty state:

Shown under this list when it is empty — shipping today

The quick test: a refund isn’t income — a refunded $150 entry means the team spent $150 less, so it reduces that item instead of adding a row.
