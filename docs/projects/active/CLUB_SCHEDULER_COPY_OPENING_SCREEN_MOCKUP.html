Two asks

Ask 1 · Copying is how the window opens, not a field inside it

Recommended
  • Once the club has a schedule, New schedule opens on the club's schedules, newest first, each with its dates and venue. Pick one and step 1 opens already filled in. Start blank opens step 1 empty.
  • A club with no schedule yet goes straight to step 1, as it does today. Settings… on a saved schedule never shows this screen.
  • Opened from a permit's page, the permit is already chosen. The screen then says or start from the permit, and a copy keeps the permit's time.

Why: New tournament opens this way, and the coach's New template asks the same question first. A copy rewrites the name, time, teams, shares and rules, so it is a choice made before the form. That is also why the field floats above the cards today. Rows can show dates where a dropdown shows only names, so two winters at the same dome are easy to tell apart. And picking a schedule, then Don't copy, today leaves the copied teams and rules in the form while the field says nothing was copied. An opening screen cannot get into that state.

Ask 2 · To change your mind after a pick, Cancel and open New schedule again

Recommended

Nothing is saved before Generate drafts, so Cancel loses nothing. The window's back already belongs to step 2 ("← Settings"). New tournament's blank form has no way back to its opening screen either. The alternative: a "← Copy a schedule" back at the top of step 1 that empties the form and returns to the list.

Formatting check (/design, 2026-10-13)

Already applied to the frames above. They are proposals until you rule on the sheet.

  • D1 · Cancel beside the lime. A window that creates something keeps Cancel beside its button as well as the ✕ (ruling of 10-09). New tournament's opening screen has no Cancel. That screen predates the ruling and belongs to the free tier review, so it was not copied here.
  • D2 · "Copy a schedule" said once. The line under the title already says it, so the bold heading and icon that repeated it above the list are gone, and the sentence leads the body.
  • D3 · Step 1's line under the title stays one quiet line. The source's name is no longer bold.
  • D4 · Opened from a permit, the screen names the permit. Without that, nothing on the screen said which permit the time comes from.
  • D5 · Phone details from the kit. The back arrow is 20px, and rows use the phone row padding with a 44px floor.
  • Kept as drawn: the "or" at the end of the body, which is New tournament's own shape. With Cancel beside the lime, it still leads to Start blank.

What stays, and what else moves

  • What a copy fills does not change: its time (unless a permit gives the time), teams, shares and rules, a year on. The name moves a year too: "Winter dome 2026–27" becomes "Winter dome 2027–28".
  • Permit stays a field beside Name. The 10-12 reason still holds, because a club's second year wants both. Since the copy now always comes first, the copy-then-permit order is the only one, and it already works today.
  • The help article says "Copy a schedule at the top fills in…". It gets rewritten with the change.
  • No open walk mentions the field. §300 is closed and §307 doesn't touch it. The change gets its own walk, §309, at the top of this page.
