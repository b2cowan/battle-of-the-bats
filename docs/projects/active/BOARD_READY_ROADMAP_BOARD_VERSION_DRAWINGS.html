What changed, and why it sells better

  1. Shipped comes first. A dated promise from a small company is only as good as its record, so the page opens with the record: 50 releases since late June, and six of them across tournaments, rep teams, house league and security .
  2. The rows are what a club gets, not how we organise the work. New tools for your club; moving your club over, and your data; security and reliability. "Ready for every club" was our milestone, not theirs .
  3. The front page carries 35 cards, not 46. It was 29 when this was drawn: the two product lines came onto it with your 18 November words (one of them became two), and three club website lines were added on 24 November. Five engineering lines (automatic tests, component alerts, the release rhythm and the like) sit on the trust detail page. On a front page, each future security line reads to a board as "so that isn't done today" .
  4. Four lines that say nothing to a customer are gone from the public version: two filler items and two goals that tell a prospect no club has finished a season with us .
  5. The strongest sellers lead each column: families paying online, bringing records over, volunteers and their documents.
  6. "Your data is yours" gets the full width at the foot of the page, as the last thing a board reads .
