What the design check changed

The drawings were checked against the portal’s written rules before this hand-off. Six changes are already in the drawings. Four points are named and left alone, because they concern how the product looks today, not this job.

Changed in the drawings

  1. Remove ends the form’s body; the form’s last row is Cancel · Save alone. The first draft put Remove under Save. The portal’s rule (10-09, sharpened 10-19) puts a record’s rare door at the end of its body. It wears the delete look: a 13px trash and red words at body weight, underlined only on hover.
  2. “Mark unpaid” is ink, not red. It can be undone, so it uses the record-action look, beside Remove fee.
  3. The number is the reorder handle at every touch width. The first draft kept a separate grip at 641–768. The reorder ruling says the handle is the row’s number where the list has one, at touch widths. Its tap sheet carries Move up · Move down · Cancel.
  4. Mark paid wears the row’s worded-action olive (olive words on a 1px olive border, 44 tall), not the neutral grey of today’s button.
  5. Each small link borrows its tap area. Fan view, See all → and the “?” get 44px through a matching negative margin, so no row or card moves. The first draft let Fan view add about 20px of space, on the paid screens too.
  6. The row’s door is a real button named for its record (“Edit Theo Novak”, marked as expanding), with the chevron last. On a phone the chevron sits in the corner.

Named, not changed (today’s product, outside this job)

  • The Paid pill’s colour. It is today’s olive status tint. The rules say status colour lives in a chip, which it is. Whether it should move to the success green is a separate call.
  • The other rows dim while one is open. The product does this today, and no rule covers it either way. The nearest precedents lean against dimming. Kept as today.
  • The free rows are cards with gaps. The row-list rule says a row is not a card on a computer. That is a list job, already named in the plan.
  • Where Mark paid sits on a phone row, beside the door or on its own full-width line, is an open portal question (A11). It is drawn beside the door, as today.

Said out loud on Q1

Option C knowingly departs from two written rules. On a phone the form folds in place, but the 10-19 One Window ruling says a form fills the phone. And these three lists drop the 08-15 money-table idiom (a pencil plus the row) in favour of the row-list rule (the row is a button with a chevron). Both are in the Q1 option text, so a ruling for C is a ruling on them.

Product-manager brief

Free coach tap targets

What changes for the coach

On a phone, every button a free coach taps becomes at least the size of a fingertip (44 pixels). Most of the work is on Roster, Schedule and Fees. Instead of a small pencil and a small trash can on every row, the coach taps the row itself to open it, and Remove sits inside the form that opens. Fees keeps its one-tap “Mark paid” on the row. Undoing a payment moves inside the fee as “Mark unpaid”. On the other screens nothing moves. Small links and buttons get a bigger tap area at the same type size.

Why it matters

The free portal is the front door to Premium, and a free coach is most likely to be using it from a phone at the field. Today they hit 334 undersized targets across 12 screens. The worst is a 15px “Undo”, and the most common is the pair of 38px buttons repeated on every player. Every release sweep reports all 334 as new, which buries any real regression in noise.

Customer impact

  • Free coaches: fewer mis-taps, and a row they can open with a thumb. Roster names and fee names get more room before they are cut off.
  • Premium coaches: three screens change through shared parts. The Fan view link on Overview and Tournaments gets a bigger tap area that borrows the space already below it, so nothing moves. Announcements’ subject, Refresh and Send grow from 40–42 to 44. The paid pages already have these on file as known debt, so this fixes them.
  • Help: one roster sentence (“use the pencil to edit and the trash icon to remove”) and the matching schedule and fees lines are rewritten.

Priority

Medium. Nothing is broken, but until this lands every promote’s layout check reports 334 known findings as new. It is a small build once the drawings are ruled.

Success criteria

  • The free coach screens sweep at 361, 390, 768 and 1440 with no new findings. Anything left is accepted on the owner’s word with a written reason.
  • The paid screens the shared parts reach sweep no worse, and their Announcements entries stop reproducing.
  • A coach can add, open, edit, remove and reorder a player, an event and a fee from a 390px phone without a tap that misses.

Free coach tap targets — plan

The plan’s source of truth is docs/projects/active/FREE_COACH_TAP_TARGETS_PLAN.md. This tab is a reading copy of it.

Problem

On 2026-10-19 the layout upkeep added the free coach portal to the layout sweep. Twelve screens measure clean except for touch-target size: 334 findings at 361, 390 and 768, and none at 1440. They come from twelve distinct controls repeated by the fixture’s rows. Re-measured on staging the same day, the findings are identical key for key and every pair was measured.

Scope

The census rows 1–9 and 11, on the free coach portal. Shared parts are touched only where Q5 says so. Census row 10 (the help page) is put to the owner as Q6.

Out of scope — named, not fixed
  • The free editors’ Save / Cancel. Editing an existing player, event or fee asks for a Save. The owner ruled on 09-24 that editing autosaves and only creating asks. This is drift, but it is a behaviour change, not a size change, so it is its own job.
  • The free lists are cards with gaps. The house row-list standard says a row is not a card on a desktop. That is a separate list-recipe job, and option B would take it on.
  • The paid roster hides its reorder grip on a phone. That goes against the 10-12 reorder ruling (the number is a 44px handle). It is reported as a finding and not changed here.
  • The paid header “?” at 641–768 (34 wide, F-21) belongs to the touch-target debt plan.

Approach (as recommended; each line waits on its ruling)

Q1 — row actions: option C

On Roster, Schedule and Fees, the row’s main area becomes one button that opens the in-place form the pencil opens today. The pencil and trash leave the row. Remove (Remove player · Remove event · Remove fee) moves to the foot of that form, behind the same confirmation it has today. A chevron at the row’s end says the row opens. The form keeps today’s fields, its in-place fold and its Save / Cancel.

Option A keeps the pencil and trash and makes them 44. It is quick and changes no flow, but it costs the name its room. At 390 the roster name drops from 156px to about 130px. A paid fee’s name, 52px today against the 154px “Spring tournament fee” needs, drops to almost nothing. Option B builds a real record for each row, which reads first, has a pencil that flips the whole record, autosaves, has Remove in the foot and goes full screen on a phone. That is the house standard in full, but it means three new record windows in the free portal plus the house row list. The row list carries the paid portal’s whole stylesheet into the free bundle unless that stylesheet is split first. It is several sessions of work and a full help rewrite. C gets the house rule that matters here, row is the door and Remove is inside, at the cost of one said-out-loud gap: the house list rule says a row that opens a form opens it full screen on a phone, and C keeps today’s in-place fold.

Q2 — roster reorder on a phone

At ≤640 the jersey number becomes the 44×44 handle, following the 10-12 ruling. Hold it to drag, or tap it for Move up · Move down · Cancel. At 641–768 the grip stays and grows to 44×44, as on the paid roster. Above 768 the mouse drag is unchanged. The alternative is to hide the grip at ≤640, as the paid roster does: cheaper, but a phone-only coach could then never reorder.

Q3 — fees

Mark paid is 44 tall on the row and stays one tap. A paid fee’s row shows the existing “Paid · Oct 19” pill and the chevron. “Mark unpaid” sits in the fee’s form beside Remove fee. The alternative is a 44px bordered “Undo” left on the row.

Q4 — Add buttons

Add player · Add event · Add fee stay where they are and grow to 44 tall at ≤768. Moving them into a page-header primary, as the paid portal does, is a header redesign the free section shell does not have a slot for.

Q5 — shared parts

  • Fan view: fixed in its shared component. The link gets a 44px-tall tap box and its type stays 0.7rem. Paid change: the paid Overview’s live-event card and every paid Tournaments card get the bigger tap area. It borrows the space below the link with a matching negative margin, so nothing moves. Stage 1 checks each of the five places it appears; where the next card sits closer than 21px, the gap grows there instead.
  • The “?”: 44×44 from the free section holder, matching the paid team header and page header holders. The help button itself is untouched, and no paid screen changes. The free Notifications page uses the paid page header, so its 768 “?” is the paid F-21 debt. I recommend recording that one finding against F-21 with a written reason.
  • Announcements: fixed once in its shared stylesheet. Subject 44, Refresh 44, Send 44, and Refresh contacts 44 at ≤768. Paid change: the paid team’s Announcements gets the same sizes. Its nine on-file entries, which have no reason, stop reproducing and are pruned only on the owner’s word, from a warm staging run.

Q6 — the help page

What’s New (19) and Search this guide (42) are every help page’s, admin included, and the touch-target debt plan owns them. I recommend accepting the six free-help findings with a reason pointing there. The keys carry no fixture name, so they will not re-key.

Q7 — the forms behind the rows

Fields 40 → 44 and Save / Cancel 42 → 44 at ≤768. The Practice · Game · Event pills go from 34 to 40, which with their 4px frame clears 44 for the whole control. The “+ Add date of birth”-style toggles get a 44px tap box. The sweep never opens a form, so these are not in the 334. The proof is a probe that opens one row on each screen and measures it.

Free-only fixes with no question

Upgrade to Premium (both places) 40 → 44 · See all → 24 → 44 tap box · Open → 32 → 44 · Express interest 36 → 44, with a local class beside the global small button, which is not touched.

Proof

Built changes are not on staging until pushed, so the proof runs on the local dev server, after asking whether another session is using it. It runs the twelve free ids at four widths with one worker, plus the paid screens the shared changes reach. Zero unmeasured. A probe opens one row per editor and measures the form. Every baseline change is listed with its reason. Nothing is added to the baseline to make a run pass.

Rollout

One product commit and one records commit, both on the owner’s word. Then /simplify, /review and /docs: the roster, schedule and fees help copy changes under C. A ledger § walk is published on this hub. No migration and no demo story change. The demos run the same free editors and follow the product.
