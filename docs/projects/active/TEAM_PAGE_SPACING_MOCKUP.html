The same page at a desk. The address takes its own line at every width, so there is one rule and no second layout.

What changed

  1. 1One card idiom. “Tryouts” gets its heading above the card, like “About the team” and every other section on the club’s pages (Open now, Teams, About the club, Schedule). The stacked pair now reads as two matching blocks. This is the main fix.
  2. 2The address never splits. It takes its own line on a phone and no longer breaks at a hyphen (“uat-” / “lakeshore-lynx.local”). The sentence above it wraps cleanly.
  3. 3More room between sections. 16 px becomes 20 px, the same gap the club home uses between its sections. A heading still sits closer to its own card (about 10 px) than to the card above it (20 px), so each heading clearly belongs below.
  4. 4The title block breathes. 8 px between the name and “Provincial AAA · 2026 season” (was about 6), and 20 px before the first section (was 16).
  5. 5The team-colour dot sits on the name. It is about 2 px high today because the condensed capitals sit lower in their line than the dot’s centre. It moves down 2 px so it lines up with the middle of “13U AAA”.

Left alone on purpose

The empty space on the right at a desk stays. A team with a schedule puts the schedule in the left column and these cards beside it, so the space fills when there is something to show. The open-tryouts card (green “Tryouts open” chip and button) keeps its own look, because it is a door and not a section. Nothing here changes how the page behaves.
