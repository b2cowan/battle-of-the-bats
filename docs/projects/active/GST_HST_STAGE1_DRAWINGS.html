Where every buyer is: the billing address step

Before anyone pays us for the first time, we ask where they are billed: country, province and street address. This page draws where that question appears on each way of buying, what it looks like on a computer and a phone, what happens with a US address while US sales are closed, where the owner changes it later, and what platform staff see. No tax is charged and no price changes in this stage. All addresses are made-up examples. Everything is a proposal until you rule on the asks in the last tab.

Asked once, at the first paid button, on every way of buying

A club owner who has never paid sees one new window between choosing a plan and choosing monthly or yearly. After that, the address is on file and the step is skipped.

Every paid button opens this window first, wherever the button sits, including the ones added later: if a buyer somehow reaches checkout without an address, the server refuses and the screen opens the window instead of showing an error. A build check fails if a new way to pay skips it.

Who sees it

  • The owner only. Plan & billing is owner-only (ruled 10-29), so nobody else in the club sees or edits the address. This replaces the build prompt’s “others see it read only”: there is no screen for them to see it on.
  • A coach enters their own home address. It covers every portal they pay for.
  • Platform staff read it on the club record’s Money tab and never edit it there (tab 7).

What the check refuses

  • A missing country, province, street, city or postal code: “Enter your city.” and so on.
  • A province that doesn’t belong to the country (only possible if the country changes after a province was picked; the province resets instead).
  • A postal code in the wrong shape: Canada “A1A 1A1” (space optional); US “12345” or “12345-6789”.
  • A street line that is only a PO Box (“PO Box”, “P.O. Box”, “Box 12”, “CP 34”, “Case postale”). “120 Elm Street, Box 3” is accepted.

While saving, and when it goes wrong

  • Saving: Continue reads “Saving…” and can’t be pressed twice. The address is saved before Stripe opens, so a buyer who leaves Stripe’s page and comes back is not asked again.
  • Saving fails: one red sentence at the left of the footer, “We couldn’t save your address. Try again.”, and everything typed stays.
  • Closing with something typed: ×, Cancel, Esc or Back asks “Keep writing” or “Discard” in the footer, as every form window does. A tap outside does nothing.
  • Changing the country: street, unit and city stay; the province or state resets, and the postal code is checked again against the new country.

The tick-box

  • Wording is the provisional text you approved, in the club’s name (“Riverdale Minor Ball is … and is buying”) or the coach’s own (“I am … and am buying”). It is saved with the date and a wording label, so the accountant’s replacement doesn’t lose what each buyer agreed to.

  • The list says “State” and includes the District of Columbia. US territories are not listed (their own check comes later, per U4).

Moving between steps

  • Back on the Address step returns to Team with everything kept; Next brings the address back as typed.
  • Continue to checkout saves the address first (“Saving…”, can’t be pressed twice). If saving fails, one red line above the button, “We couldn’t save your address. Try again.”, and nothing typed is lost.
  • A coach who leaves Stripe’s page and comes back skips the Address step: it is on file.

How it behaves

  • Billing address opens the tab 2 window with the address filled in; its button reads Save. A saved change shows the save pill, as other saves do.
  • The same line and button show on a coach’s own Plan & billing, titled “Your home address” in the window.
  • An owner who changes the country between Canada and the US after paying is saved, and flagged for staff review rather than applied silently (your Ask 4 ruling). The owner sees no difference.
  • An edit made on Stripe’s own billing page comes back to this line. If it isn’t a valid address (a PO Box, say), we keep the old one and flag it for review.

Notes

  • Payout country shows only for a club that takes online payments; Card country only once a card is on file.
  • For a coach’s portal the heading reads “Billing address · the coach’s own”, because the address belongs to the person.
  • Nothing here clears a flag. It clears itself when the facts agree again (the owner fixes the address or the card). A staff “looked at it” button belongs with Stage 3’s tax record, not here. Because US sales are closed, a flag can only appear on the dev site until they open.
  • Reasons a flag shows: a US address with a card or payout country outside the US; a Canadian address with a US card or payout country; a country changed after paying; an invalid edit made on Stripe’s billing page.
