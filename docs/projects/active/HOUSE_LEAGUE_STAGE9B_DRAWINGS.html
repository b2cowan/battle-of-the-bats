1 · Opening several age groups at once

My read: yes to "several at once", but as age groups inside one season, not as several seasons

  • You're right about the job. Clubs open U8 to U17 on the same day, with the same dates, waiver and rules. Today that's one "Add division" window at a time, and the age band can't be edited afterwards.
  • The difference between age groups is exactly what a division holds: its birth years, its size and, from 9b-1, its own price. Everything else they share is the season.
  • Separate seasons per age group would cost what you've already ruled in.
    • A family with a U10 and a U13 couldn't register both in one sign-up (Q5 says they can), and would pay twice.
    • The convenor would have four Money pages, four sets of status changes and four rows on the club home.
    • Families › Email would have four seasons to pick from.
  • So I'd make "Add age groups" the bulk tool. One window adds every age group at once: tap the chips you run, give each its birth years, size and price, and press Add. The club picks the birth years, as you ruled in 4c-2 (FieldLogicHQ never guesses them from "U13"). From next year, "Start next season" (9f) copies them all with the years moved up one.
  • When an age group really runs its own calendar (U17 in spring only), it is a different season. That case is a copy, not a bulk create, and 9f's "Start next season" covers it.

2 · Two ways to email families in one portal

My read: it isn't necessary, and today it's actually three. Make it one: Families' Email families

  • What exists:
    • the season's "Notifications" page;
    • a "Message registrants" window on the season's Registrations screen, which sends through that same page's machinery;
    • Families › Email families, the club's composer built in Stage 7c.
    Only the Families one sends one email per household, counts toward the club's three sends a day, and lands in the club's Emails log. The house-league ones send once per child, bypass the club's cap, and keep their own history that Families never sees .
  • The club already ruled the principle (10-11, "one club send"): one send window, one log shape. The fold-in was deferred, not rejected. Stage 7c left it out of scope, and chat ruling E6 kept email tools as they were while chat was paused.
  • Families › Email can already pick "one house-league season". It's missing three things:
    • a division, a team, or "to review / waitlist" inside a season;
    • a season whose registration has closed but play hasn't started;
    • a door from the season itself.
  • Proposal: the season's "Email families" row (ruled in 9a) and a Registrations "Email" action both open the Families window, already set to this season. The audience list gains division, team and status. The old Notifications page and the Message registrants window retire, and their sent history shows in Families › Emails. One composer, one cap, one log, with two doors into it.
  • Why this isn't the chat project's job: E2's "one Messages screen" merges chat and email. This merges two email windows into the one that already exists. It doesn't pre-empt E2, and it removes a sender that bypasses the club's cap.
