Asks

A · The two clubs get no warning email before September 30, 2027

Club free time ends on the page only. Today's Founding card promises "We'll remind you during the summer". Founding's own summer emails and card window go in Stage 2b, and nothing replaces them. The coach is covered: free time emails a coach at 14 and 3 days.

Recommend: give club free time the same 14-and-3-day reminder the coach already has, as its own small item on the board, built any time before September 2027. It helps every club given free time, not only these two. Not part of 2a: the move does not need it, and the date is ten months away.

B · Tell the holders?

Nothing they hold shrinks and the date is the same; the free-plan club gains Tournament Plus today. Their page swaps the Founding card for the free-time band without notice.

Recommend: no email. /legal checks this before hand-off and I will bring back anything it finds.

C · The coach's free time cannot be extended from the staff screens later

Coach free time limits count from the first give (6 months for a super admin), and the first give is the move's own date, so the end is already past that limit. Ending it early still works. Extending it later would take a script.

Recommend: accept. Note it on the plan.
