Every sale keeps its own tax record, and it does not change after the sale

Written 2026-11-07 for the Stage 3 closeout. Walk A of three: run it first, because Walks B and C read the sale it makes. The scripted Stripe matrix proved a record for every kind of invoice; this walk proves what you can see.

What this walk is for

From Stage 3 on, every invoice we issue keeps a record of its tax: the GST/HST by name, our answer (taxed, zero-rated or missing) and a copy of the buyer’s billing address and card country as they stood at the sale. That copy is the evidence for the GST/HST return, kept for six years. It must not change when the buyer later changes their address, and it must survive the club being deleted. A refund made in Stripe’s dashboard only reduces the tax when it comes with a credit note, and the record must tell those two apart.

It passes when

  • A new coach’s Premium sale shows on its Money tab with HST 13%, our answer Taxed., the Guelph address As it stood on the day of the sale, and Kept: At the sale, when the invoice was issued.
  • After the coach moves to Alberta, the old sale still shows the Guelph address, with The billing address has changed since. under it.
  • A credit note issued in Stripe’s dashboard appears on the sale with its HST; a plain Refund payment in Stripe’s dashboard makes no credit note, and the sale says so.
  • A deleted test portal’s sale record is still in the database afterwards (I read it after your paste-back).

Not in this walk

  • The Needs a look list and the mark. Walk B reads the flag this walk’s plain refund causes.
  • The revenue band. Walk C.
  • A US buyer, a club with its own GST/HST number, a renewal, a plan move, a fee invoice. US sales are closed, and the scripted Stripe matrix (test mode) proved a record with the right answer for each of these. Results are on file with the walk.
  • Records copied by the daily job. The one dev run of the catch-up is read from its own log, not walked.

Known before the walk

  • ⚠ As read 2026-11-07, the deployed dev site does not run Stage 3 yet. Its own daily job that morning had no tax steps, and in the scripted run it wrote no record. Two ways to walk: (1) the dev site is redeployed first (ask me; it needs your yes to push), and then records appear by themselves within seconds and Kept reads “At the sale”; or (2) walk now. After every step that acts in Stripe, say catch up in the chat and wait for my OK: I run the daily catch-up, and the record appears with Kept in amber (“Caught up by the daily job …”). Choose the amber option where a step offers one.
  • Before you start: add three events to Stripe’s TEST billing webhook (Developers → Webhooks → the destination for dev.fieldlogichq.ca/api/billing/webhook → add credit_note.created, credit_note.updated, credit_note.voided). Without them, a credit note made in Stripe’s dashboard reaches our records only through the daily catch-up. Step E2 then asks you to tell me, and I run the catch-up for you.
  • If Stripe’s refund event reaches us before the sale’s record exists, the record can show $0 refunded until the next refund event. You will not meet it in this walk, because the sale is recorded first.
  • Our record copies the address when the invoice is issued. If the deployed dev site missed the event, the daily job writes the record later and its Kept line says so in amber. Step C3 asks which one you see.
