What this walk is for

From Stage 2 on, FieldLogicHQ charges sales tax by where the buyer is. Stage 1 makes sure we know that before anyone pays: every paid button asks for a billing address once, remembers it, and lets the owner change it on Plan & billing only. A club gives the club’s address; a coach buys Premium as a person and gives their home address, which covers every portal they pay for. If the card says a different country, staff see it, and nothing is blocked. Nothing is charged differently today.

It passes when

  • Every paid path asks for the address before Stripe opens: a new club’s plan, a move up on a live plan, coach Premium, and Premium → Club (prefilled from the coach’s home address).
  • A buyer who has answered is never asked again: later purchases say “Billed to …”, and the plan card shows the address with a Billing address button.
  • A PO Box on its own is refused, with words that say why.
  • Stripe’s billing page no longer offers an address edit.
  • A US buyer must tick the declaration, and platform admin shows which wording they ticked.
  • A card from another country flags the buyer on platform admin’s Money tab, never blocks, and the flag clears when the card agrees again.
  • Free buyers are not asked until they press a button that pays.

Not in this walk

  • The Founding next-season choice. It doesn’t open until 2027-06-01, and the server refuses before it reaches the address check. The every-path test pins its check.
  • Reactivate Premium and Keep Premium. They use the same address check as a new Premium sign-up (Part F). No fixture has a lapsed Premium portal.
  • The US hold (“We’re not selling in the United States yet.”). The dev site has US sales switched on for this walk, so it can’t show the hold. Production keeps it, and unit tests pin it.
  • Any tax. That is Stage 2.
