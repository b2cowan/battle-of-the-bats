The draft keeps friends together

Confirmed friend requests now shape the draft. The Run the draft window says what it will do with them and has a Keep friends together switch beside Keep families together. The draft keeps friends on one team in groups of up to 3 children, brothers and sisters counted. Every club’s board gets tidier rows: RTG · Player · Together · Note · $ · Age · ⋯. Together joins family and friends in one count, which turns amber when someone is split off. A player’s ⋯ can keep two friends apart. After the draft, Registrations › Friend requests reads Not honoured and Honoured with the reason, live from the teams, and a registration’s record lists its friend requests.

Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • The draft’s rules. Groups of at most 3 with brothers and sisters, kept in the order Both asked, then a code match, then the earliest confirmed; a family of three has no room; locks win; two children kept apart are never joined, not even through a friend they share (a review finding, fixed); evening out never moves a group; the same draw gives the same teams; a division with no friend requests deals exactly as before. 64 unit tests.
  • The database. What the draft did with each request lands in the same step as the teams. “Take all players off teams” clears it, and finishing the season deletes it with the requests (rehearsed on dev and rolled back). Code already on production keeps working with the new step.
  • Who sees friends. The board’s friend marks, names and filter reach only the people who can open Registrations: owner, admin, league admin, registrar, and anyone the club gives House league to. The draft keeps groups together for anyone who runs it and sends no names back. Build-checked, and the legal re-check agreed.
  • The reasons after the draft, and the record’s lines, are unit-tested against tab 6’s list. Typecheck is clean, and a code review and the legal, words and help passes ran, with their findings fixed or listed in the plan.
