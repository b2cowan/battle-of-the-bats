Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • 69 checks against dev, all passing, each signed in as the real seat. In short:
  • 1 · The “See it live” accounts are turned away at every door that creates something real: a club, a league, a coach team, claiming a tournament entry, and a signed-in tournament sign-up. A real account is not. A build check fails if a door loses it. (You can’t reach these doors from inside the sandbox without leaving it, so this is not walked.)
  • 2 · Behind the screens: staff, a scorekeeper, a registrar and a coach are refused every permit and library read, and the permit file. The plain venue picker still answers everyone. Staff given Run tournaments read again.
  • 3 · Confirm “same player” is refused for an assistant without roster privacy, before any record is touched. “Not the same player” still works for them, and the head coach can confirm. The card hides Confirm whenever the details are hidden (build check). Dev has no returning-player pair on a tryout board to walk, see call C2.
  • 4 · The club’s billing facts (amount, billing period, renewal date, the payment processor’s ids) reach the owner’s and admins’ browsers only. The treasurer, league admin, registrar and a club coach get none, neither in the screen’s data nor in the page itself. Nothing on any screen changes.
  • 5 · The check-in board is no longer sent division fees. The owner and tournament staff still get them. The board looks the same.
  • 6 · Uploads: a web page posing as a PDF is refused on all three upload doors (coach player documents, club player documents, club templates). A real PDF with an odd name is kept, stored as a PDF under a cleaned name, and shown under the name you gave it.
  • 7 · A mistyped week in the house-league schedule’s data shows the current week, not an error. No screen sends a bad week, so there is nothing to walk.
  • 8 · The retry. A save sent twice (what a timeout and a retry do) leaves the new point once. Another section’s point is refused with nothing changed. Walk 3 checks that ordinary editing still works.
  • 9 · “File attached” is a backstop: today everyone who reads permits can open the file, so nobody sees it. An owner still sees the file’s name and size.
  • 10 · Behind walk 2: a limited member is refused a new permit for a team outside their groups. They can’t pick one on screen, so only the server shows this.
  • 11 · “Confirm your email first.” A signed-in parent whose email isn’t confirmed is told to confirm it, instead of “That didn’t go through.” It is almost impossible to reach on dev, so it is a build check.
