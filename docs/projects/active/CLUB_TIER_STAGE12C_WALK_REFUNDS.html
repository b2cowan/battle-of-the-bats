A refund you can see

Written 2026-10-26. One purpose: a refund of a card payment is visible where the desk looks, a refund made in Stripe records itself, and a tournament can refund part of a team’s payment.

What this walk is for

Until now a card refund was a line in the books and little else. You check that it is now seen in every place a person looks: the Fee cell on Registrations, the child’s record (a “To the card” block with Stripe’s reference you can copy), the history, the Money page and the family’s record in Families. Then you check the two ways a refund can start in Stripe instead of here (a part refund on two children’s payment, and a whole refund on a child who is still registered) and that the desk is asked only the one question it must answer. Last, you refund part of a tournament team’s deposit and check that the team’s entry is lowered by the same amount.

You use a family in a private window (no login), the registrar and the owner on a computer, and Stripe’s dashboard (or its command line) for the two refunds made in Stripe.

It passes when

  • Every refund to a card reads the same wherever it appears: the amount the card got, where it stands (pending, reached, made in Stripe), and Stripe’s reference you can copy.
  • A refund made in Stripe is never lost and never asks more than it must: “Choose the child” for a part refund on two children, and three answers for a child who is still enrolled.
  • A team’s part refund lowers its entry by exactly the refund, shows its reason, and leaves the balance owed where it was (entry less net paid).
  • The family’s record and the registration row open the payment where it can be read with its refunds.

Not in this walk

  • Bank debit in any form: it is walk §390.
  • Emails, beyond the held boxes. A step that sends an email has two boxes, “Did it” and “Held”. Tick “Held” and move on: localhost cannot send, so the emails get one end-to-end pass on Amplify dev.
  • The tournament side of “Record the refund sent another way”. That item appears on a team’s payment only after a refund has bounced, which a tournament card payment here cannot be made to do. It is walked on the house-league side in part G (optional); the tournament side is covered by unit tests and gets walked in the Amplify pass.
