Platform highlights

What it actually replaces

Tournament module

Generate brackets in seconds, not hours. Coaches and parents see live scores the moment you enter them — no calls to the scorekeeper's table.

House league module

Open registration, run your draft, publish the schedule, and track standings — all in one dashboard. Parents hear about their registration automatically, and you can message the whole league in one go.

Rep teams module

Coaches manage their own roster, documents, and team finances independently. Organization admins get visibility without owning the day-to-day.

Coaches Portal

The roster in a group text, the lineup in a notes app, and team fees tracked in someone's head. The Coaches Portal puts all of it in one place — whether or not your organization is on FieldLogicHQ.

A1 · Four cards always, or up to six as plans open?

Production shows the four cards in tab 1. Cards 5 and 6 join when League Plus or Club opens.

Recommended: up to six. The house league card leaves production until League Plus is for sale, which is honest. Today's page describes a house league plan nobody can buy yet.

A2 · Change the label above the heading from "Platform highlights" to "The old way"?

"Platform highlights" promises a feature list, which is what the section above already gives. The For Leagues page already labels its problem section "The old way".

Recommended: yes. The heading stays the same.

A3 · Does the permit card wait for Club, or come with League Plus?

The product lets League Plus record permits too. The pricing facts call permits part of the Club release.

Recommended: show it when either plan opens, because that matches what the product does. Say if "part of the Club release" means it should wait for Club.

A4 · Do these problems match what you hear from clubs?

Only the friend-request card (5) comes from a prospect's ask, on the Board Ready list. The others are the problems our own pages for each audience already name. You know the people better than the code does.

Recommended: replace any card that doesn't sound like a real conversation you've had. The treasurer's spreadsheet is an alternate card, held for when Club opens.
