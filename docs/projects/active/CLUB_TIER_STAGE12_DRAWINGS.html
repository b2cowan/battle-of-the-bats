Stage 12 drawn: payouts, one checkout, and the money in the books

The whole stage as its end state: the club connects its payout account (12a), families pay for house league (12b) and teams pay their entry (12c) through one checkout, and every payment lands in the club's books beside the ones marked by hand. Nothing here is built, and no live charge happens before counsel signs off.

Scaled to fit; use the button for true size.

What Stage 12 makes true, and what the census found

A club connects its own payout account once. From then on a family pays on their phone the moment their child is accepted, and a tournament team pays its entry when it registers. The money goes straight to the club, the payment appears in the club's books with nobody typing it, and marking a payment received by hand keeps working beside it. No live charge happens before counsel signs off.

Ruled 2026-10-25: all 15 asks, every one as recommended

  • The owner connects payouts; the treasurer sees the state and the alerts (A1). Tournament Plus connects on the tournament settings page (A8).
  • The club chooses who pays the processing fee, club-wide; families pay it by default (A14). A refund includes the fee by default when the club ends the registration, and not when the family asks to leave (A2).
  • No saved cards (A3). A bank debit is "Processing" until it clears, with the spot held; it shows on the Money band only while there is some (A4, A11). Card in 12a; bank debit in 12c for both flows (L2).
  • Families see Stage 9's screens until the club takes payments (A6); an optional refund-policy link sits beside Pay (A9).
  • A tournament offers deposit or full, deposit pre-selected (A7); its refunds start from the online payment (A12). Card refunds: whoever ends the registration or records a payment in that flow, plus the treasurer (A13). Partial refunds in 12b (L1). Rep-team dues are not in Stage 12 (A5).

Where I disagree with the brief, before the drawings

  • "One writer for money" is not what the code has. There are four writers today: house league's paid tick, the tournament's payment records, the coach's dues book, and the Families hand-off to a coach. Only the first two share anything. So the honest rule for Stage 12 is one online-payment record, landing through each flow's own writer. A card payment for house league goes through the same write as the convenor's mark received, and a tournament card payment goes through the same write as the tournament's record a payment. Drawn that way throughout.
  • House league's writer can't hold an online payment yet. It is a whole-fee tick dated today, with no amount, no date and no "how" . 9b-2 has to give it all three, or 12b has nowhere to put "Paid online, reference …". It is already in 9b-2's drawing (the How column). I'm flagging it so nobody builds 9b-2 as a tick again.
  • The Club plan says "the owner or treasurer connects", but the settings page is the owner's alone, and a treasurer can't open it . That makes the treasurer a real ask (A1), not a detail.
  • Tournament Plus can't reach the club's settings at all . It is a paid org plan, so online payments include it. Payouts needs a home on the tournament settings page too (A8), or Tournament Plus has the feature and no door.
  • "Partial refunds" can't wait for 12d. Stage 9 ruled that correcting a paid price settles the difference, so 12b needs a partial refund to the card on day one (ladder ask L1).
  • A refund the club absorbs is a real cost in the club's book. "The processing fee never in the club's book" holds for revenue. But if the club refunds a family's fee, the processor keeps its share, and that comes out of the club's balance. The refund ask (A2) shows where it lands.
  • The worry about a card hold expiring goes away. The Club plan asked "pay at submit or on acceptance, given a card hold expires". The Stage 9 ruling (pay when accepted) means nothing is ever held: a family pays the moment they're accepted, or later from a link, so nothing expires.

The checkout: one, shared by every flow

There is no separate checkout screen of ours. The pay block is one component, shown wherever Stage 9 put a ghost. Pressing Pay opens Stripe's own secure page, set up for exactly that amount and that way of paying, in the club's name. The family comes back to a receipt. House league and tournaments use the same block, the same Stripe page and the same receipt. Only the lines inside differ.

Why the family picks card or bank debit before Stripe's page

  • The fee differs by the way of paying. To show the fee before paying, the choice has to happen on our side, with each button carrying its own total. Stripe's page then shows one way of paying, with the fee as its own line.
  • Card is the primary button; bank debit is beside it with its wait in words. Both totals are on the buttons, so nobody is surprised on Stripe's page.
  • The club's own "How to pay" text stays underneath as the other way. Mark received keeps working, so a family who pays by e-Transfer is never pushed online.

4 · Paying by hand beside online

Unchanged

  • Mark received on an owing row, Record a payment in Families, and a tournament’s Mark paid and Record a payment all work exactly as before.

New, and why

  • A row that is paid online has no Mark received and no pencil: the amount and date are the bank’s.

Paid twice
