# Milton Bats demo world — PM brief

**What it is.** A pretend Milton Bats Softball Association on our staging system, so the house-league administrator sees his own
league — his divisions, his fields, in his club's purple — running in our product, with made-up families.

**Why it matters.** The administrator decides whether the club takes the free first year. A demo that looks like his club, with every
screen already at the right moment of the season, lets him judge the product instead of imagining it.

**What he sees.** A public sign-up page in Milton purple. Five divisions (U7–U15), each at a different point of the season: one opening
for sign-ups, one ready to draft, one mid-season with scores to check, one full with a waitlist and unpaid fees, one just published
with a team change to tell families about. Eight diamonds across three parks (a ninth, at Sherwood Park, is for rep only).

**What you can do.** Reset the whole club to the starting state with one command, any time — between rehearsals, in the middle of one,
or after the demo. Logins and branding survive a reset, so nobody is signed out.

**Customer impact.** None outside this prospect; nothing here touches production or any real family.

**Priority.** High — the demo is the next sales step.

**Success.** A full dry run of the five must-do walks (sign-up to the books, draft and publish, team changes, schedule, rainout) with
no seeding gaps, and a reset that returns the club to the same starting state every time.
