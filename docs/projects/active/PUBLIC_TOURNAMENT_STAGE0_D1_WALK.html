An unpublished schedule stays private everywhere

What this walk is for. An organizer publishes a schedule one division at a time. Until now a division they had not published still reached families: the tournament home listed its games and results, the live-scores strip showed them, and standings counted them, while the Schedule tab said "coming soon". This walk checks that a division you have not published shows no games on any public page, and that publishing it brings them all back at once.

It passes when every family-facing page shows the U11 games and none of the U13 games, U13 teams sit at 0-0 although their games were played, and publishing U13 makes its games and records appear (Eagles U13 3-0) everywhere you looked.
