Stripe-owned pricing · Stages 2 to 5 · Owner QA Ledger §541

Stripe charges the club, and the club’s books match what it takes

The close of Stages 2 to 5. Each club now has a Stripe account where Stripe takes its own processing fee and FieldLogicHQ keeps only its part. A club on the old account sets up payouts again. Then each payment’s cost lands in the club’s books as Stripe really took it: our processing fee where the club pays it, Stripe’s extra on a card from outside Canada, the fee on a refund, and a dispute’s fee. Stripe no longer sends us anything to bill back, so the fee invoice is gone. You walk it as the club’s owner, with a test team and a test family paying.

Written Nov 18, 2026 against the dev site, in Stripe test mode. Every club, team and child here is test data. Every figure comes from the product’s own fee function or Stripe’s 0.8% foreign-card charge (measured in Stage 1). None of them depends on today’s date.

Not in this walk, and where each is covered

  • A bank debit that fails before it clears (the returned record at once, then Stripe’s $5 “Returned debit fee” hours later, after the daily job), and one taken back after it cleared. Stripe’s test bank page can’t choose a failure. Both ran in the Stage 3 trial through Stripe directly; the live payout test re-checks them.
  • The Payouts tab’s lines, including “FieldLogicHQ’s fee returned”. A new test account’s first payout waits about 7 days, so the tab has nothing to show yet. The payout test covers it.
  • The bank check’s “Stripe fee · not in your books”. It needs a Stripe fee the daily job couldn’t match to a payment, which test mode can’t make on demand. Its tests and the database check cover it; no walk does.
  • “Dispute fee · answered”. Stripe bills the answering fee hours after an answer, so it can’t be seen in one sitting. Whether Stripe returns it on a won dispute is still unseen in test mode (an open item in the plan).
  • The draft payments terms v0.2 and the next privacy policy. The dev site can’t show a draft: the legal pages are switched to published, so dev serves only the Nov 17 versions. You read and approved the narrowed terms sentences on Nov 18. The Before window still links the placeholder text until the terms are published.
  • House-league refunds and disputes: the same book writers as the tournament’s, so one surface each. The emails are covered by the earlier Stage 12 walks and aren’t re-checked here.
