The strip names the page; the organization’s screens get their own sidebar

On a computer, a tournament page is now named in the top strip (“Divisions”, “Teams”), with the event’s dates and phase at the strip’s right end, and the page’s own big title steps aside. The event’s sidebar lists the event’s pages only, ending with “Your notifications”. The organization’s screens wear the organization’s own sidebar: in a tournament-only organization, Settings & access becomes tabs (Members · Payouts · PDF settings · Web address); in a club, those settings live only in the club portal.
