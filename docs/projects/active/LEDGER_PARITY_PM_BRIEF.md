# Ledger Parity — PM brief

Hub (mockup, decisions, plan): https://claude.ai/artifact/EQqEd3s4CBLbnrnPuUVAAo · Plan: `LEDGER_PARITY_PLAN.md`

## What changes

**For a coach**, on Money › Ledger:
- Every row opens when clicked and ends in one arrow. A row that came from Player Dues, Fundraising or Club opens a
  short window that says where it is changed, with one button to go there.
- Rows show the day ("Oct 1"); the Starting and Ending balance lines carry the full dates of the window.
- On a phone, each row is the same clean card the club's treasurer sees.
- A Payees page lets the team rename a payee or merge two spellings into one.

- Payees opens from a Tools menu on the Ledger and from the payee picker's last row (not a tab).
- The payee picker shows only the payees the club chose to share, under "Shared by your club", with one line saying
  the club sees payments to them.

**For a club treasurer:** the balance lines gain the year; Transfer and Payees move behind a Tools menu; each club
payee can be shared with the teams or kept as the club's own; and a shared payee shows what each team recorded paying
it (with Club Tier Stage 3b). Today every club payee already shows in every team's picker, silently — this makes it a
choice and makes it visible.

**Who sees what:** anyone who can read the team's money can open every row and window; renaming and merging payees
is for those who can enter the team's money. The club's own payees stay the club's to manage.

## Why it matters

The owner ruled on 2026-09-30 that the club's book reads exactly like the coach's. A side-by-side check found five
more differences nobody chose; in four of them the coach's Ledger — the benchmark portal — has drifted from the
product's own written rules. A treasurer who also coaches reads the same kind of book two ways today.

## Customer impact

Every team using Money sees the changes on its most-used money screen; club treasurers see one small change. Without
the Payees page, a duplicate payee on a team's books stays a duplicate forever and splits its spending on every report
that groups by payee.

## Priority

Medium. Recommended after the §255 walks close and before Club Tier Stage 3b.

## Success criteria

- A screen-by-screen comparison of the two Ledgers finds no difference outside the recorded "kept" list.
- No ledger row on either portal carries a button except Record on an unpaid installment.
- A team with two spellings of one payee can merge them in under a minute, and its reports then show one payee.
