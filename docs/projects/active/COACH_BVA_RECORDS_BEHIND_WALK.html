Part F

Two questions, not checks

Neither is a defect. Both are calls the build made that want your word on the built screen.

F1

Should a sponsor panel also have an "Open Sponsors" button?

Built: no. Every row in that panel is already a door to the sponsor it names, so a hub button beside them is a second door that lands further from the work — the same reasoning that keeps the writer's plan panel button-free. But the house rule for these panels is "at most two doors: the Ledger, and the thing itself", and a reader who does not realise the rows are tappable would find a button faster. Your call.
F2

Is the count worth it on a row with only two payments?

Built: yes, always when there is more than one. The count is the only thing distinguishing a summed row from a single payment, and the case it protects against is a coach hunting the Ledger for one cheque of a size that never existed. The alternative is showing the span alone ("May 10 – Jun 14") and letting the range imply it. Read A1 again and say which you would rather have.
