6 · Asks

Your eight calls of 2026-11-09 are drawn as ruled. Four small things the drawing had to decide:

D1 · Desk rows: a quiet link, not thirty buttons (tab 3)

"Visible without hover" could mean a bordered button on every row. Thirty of them would bury the one row the box names. The phone settled this on 2026-11-06: the named row keeps its button and the rest show the underlined link.

Recommend: the phone's recipe at a desk too, as drawn.

D2 · The repeat line names the last check's date (tab 4)

The ruled words were "The same $4.50 as your last check". Adding "on Oct 31, 2026" tells the person which check, so they can open it from the list of saved checks below.

Recommend: keep the date, as drawn.

D3 · The banking-app line sits in the window's pinned footer (tab 5)

The club's window already fills a 1440 × 900 screen, so a line at the end of the form would land below the fold while Save stays in view. In the footer it is always right above Save, on a desk and on a phone. It adds about 50px to the footer.

Recommend: in the footer, as drawn. "Use the date it shows" stays as its second sentence (the accounting review's date point).

D4 · The coach's phone box stacks its buttons full width (tab 1)

The club's box already does this on a phone; the coach's lets buttons wrap at their own width. With two long labels the coach's box would read ragged, so it gains the club's rule. This also changes the coach's matching-line box on a phone ("Not in the bank yet" · "Record the missing amount" stack full width).

Recommend: one rule for both portals, as drawn.
