Who owns a tournament’s look: the club, or the tournament

Written 2026-11-03. Test data only, on the dev database. About 40 minutes. One purpose: a club sets one rule for its tournaments’ colours and type, a tournament can follow it or choose its own, and Tournament Plus (no club) stays plain.

What this walk is for

A club can now say “my tournaments wear my look” in one place (its Public site, Look row, new Tournaments part). A tournament’s Branding page gets one Look card in place of the old Theme, Font, Card style, Dark/Light and Hero banner cards, with a real preview of the band families see on a phone. Everything below is behind the preview switch, except the three share images (the picture that shows when a link is shared), which follow the same rule from today.

It passes when

  • A and B: the club’s Tournaments part saves both choices, and choosing Always first asks a plain question that names the tournaments it will change, and does nothing until you confirm.
  • C: under Always every tournament, finished ones too, wears the club’s colours and type; going back restores each tournament’s own look.
  • D: a tournament’s Look card works in all three club cases (follows, own look, locked), and the preview matches the live band.
  • E: Tournament Plus has no “club’s look” anywhere and starts on the plain FieldLogicHQ look.
  • F: with the preview off, the Branding page and the club editor are today’s.

Before you start

Setup, not checked. Keep this page private: it holds dev passwords.

Where

UAT Rep Club (uat-rep-club), set up by Claude for this walk: type is Serif, the club’s colour is purple, its rule is “the club’s look, unless a tournament chooses its own”, and three of its tournaments carry colours of their own: UAT Rep Club Invitational 2026 (pink, on now), Stripe Walk Invitational (ocean blue, coming up) and UAT Rep Club Summer Slam 2026 (crimson, finished). Stripe Walk Pay Direct has nothing of its own (it follows the club).
