Support Console Sent Emails Walk

One question: when a customer says “I never got the email”, can the right staff find out what we sent them and what happened to it, and can nobody else? Every account email is now written to a log as it leaves; this walk looks at it from a person’s record, from Find, and from Health › Sent emails, as each role. Tick what holds, and write what does not. Your ticks stay in this browser; the paste-back at the foot gathers them for the chat. Feedback and its reply are walk 2 (§392); Status and Errors are walk 3 (§393).

  1. What dev can and cannot show. The log is written before an email leaves, so every row below exists on dev. But the words Delivered, Delayed, Bounced and Marked as spam come from the mail service calling our new address, and that call (the Resend webhook) does not exist on dev until the release step. So on dev a row reads Sent (dev has a mail key) or Not sent (dev has none). Those steps are marked release check: they carry a second box, “Held”. Tick “Held” and move on; they are checked once, at the release, with one real test email.
  2. Which of the two is dev? Open .env.local in the project. If RESEND_API_KEY= is empty (it was when this walk was written), nothing leaves the machine and every row reads Not sent: safe, and the walk is written for it. If you add a key and restart, rows read Sent and real mail goes: then use only addresses you own.
  3. Make three logged emails (section 1 does it). A reset link to a customer, a reset link to a staff address, and a tournament registration to an address that has no account. Everything else reads those rows.

⚠ Nothing here emails a real person unless you add a mail key. Use walk-noaccount-1@example.com for the address with no account (any made-up address on an example.com domain will do). The drawings this walk checks against: Support Console Stage 5, drawing E.
