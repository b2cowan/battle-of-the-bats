The question this answers: does the list still do its job? A roster is opened to see who is on the team and to record something against a player. Both frames below are the same screen, the same 736px-tall window, the same twelve players. Only the Family column changed.

What to look at. The Player, Positions and # columns are untouched. A row with a returning-player caption ("Back from the 2026 Season", Priya Nair) keeps its taller row, because it still holds a second line; that is the existing rule for any row with a caption. The envelope after each name is what tells a coach the name opens something. Row heights are measured from this drawing; confirm them in the browser after the build.

One small window over the roster. It opens from the family's name, shows the two things a coach came for, and gets out of the way. Numbers are clickable: tap one to see what it says.

    NewThe window is the shipped coaches-portal window (the kit's record window), drawn at its question width, 26rem. Its × sits where every window's does; Escape and a tap on the dimmed page also close it. The email and phone open a mail or phone app where the device has one; the copy symbol is the same in every case, so it works on a laptop with no mail app too.

    What the cell says for each kind of player, and what a click does. Nothing here needs a new field: every case reads data the row already has.

    The two windows that differ from tab 2

    NewEmail but no phone. A missing value reads "Not on file", so a coach sees the gap instead of a row that quietly isn't there.

    NewContact details but no name. The title says "Family contact" and the line under it names the player.

    UnchangedPhones and small tablets keep hiding the Family column. A coach without access to guardian contacts has the details removed before the page loads, so they see no window and the cell behaves as it does today.

    Four calls, with what I would pick. Reply with letters, for example "A yes, B yes, C no, D yes".

    A
    A window, or a small floating card?The portal's rule is "a window is for a question or a form", and this is a read-only look-up, so it stretches the rule. A floating card avoids that but gets clipped by the table and is harder to use with a keyboard.
    My pick: the window. It behaves like every other window (Escape, Back, focus) and a coach learns nothing new.
    B
    An envelope after each name at rest, or a plain name that only shows it on hover?A control only a mouse can find is not a control on a phone-sized tablet or for a keyboard.
    My pick: the envelope at rest, in the dim ink, so ten rows show ten quiet envelopes and nothing louder.
    C
    Copy symbols on both rows?The email and phone are also links, but a laptop often has no mail app, and a coach pastes a number far more often than they dial it.
    My pick: yes, both. The symbol alone, no word, as other small windows do.
    D
    A guardian with a name but no email and no phone: plain text, or a window that says "Not on file" twice?
    My pick: plain text. A window with nothing in it is a dead end. The player's page is where a coach adds the details, and the cell does not need to say so.

    Build notes

    • Reuse the shared window (KitDialog); do not hand-build a second. The scrim, focus handling, Escape and the phone Back step all come with it.
    • The name, email and phone are already on each roster row, so opening the window makes no extra request and shows nothing the old cell did not already show.
    • The foot link goes to the same Family tab on the player's page that "+ Add a contact" uses today.
    • Nothing changes for phone widths, the player page, or Email families.
