What does not change

  • The band's colour, the logo, the two-line title, the dates, the accent rule and the tab names and order.

  • Sign in stays a word, not an icon.

Considered and left alone

  • Sign in as a person icon. It frees about 26 px but the title still wraps to two lines, and a signed-out family loses the word that says what the control does.

  • Moving the dates out of the band. They are one 16 px line once the pill is gone. Not worth a second place to look.
