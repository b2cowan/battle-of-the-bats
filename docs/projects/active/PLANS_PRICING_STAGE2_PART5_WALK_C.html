Replay a Stripe update

Written 2026-11-08. One purpose: a Super admin can run a Stripe update that did not finish through the product again, see what it did to the club's plan record, and find it recorded; every other role cannot. Writes to dev data only, and the last part puts it back.

What this walk is for

Before this, a Stripe update the product failed to process stayed on Health › Stripe as a line you could read and nothing more. Now a Super admin sees the word Replay on each failed line, and a card, Replay an update by its id, for any other update. A window says what will run and for which club, asks you to say why, runs it, and turns into the answer: the club's plan record before and after. A replay never charges, refunds, changes anything in Stripe or messages the customer.

Two updates were planted on dev for you: a paid bill (so the club's status changes from Past due to Active) and a subscription change (a kind that is refused). You will replay the first, try to replay it again, meet the refusals, find the change-log entries and the club's Story line, then see the page as Billing and Product.

It passes when
  • The window names the update, the club and the last result, asks Why, and says no message is sent.
  • The answer shows Past due → Active, the update leaves the failing list, and the page says Replayed with who and when.
  • A second replay of the same update, a subscription change and a made-up id are each refused in words, with no reason asked.
  • The Audit log and the club's Story keep what the replay changed; Billing sees the page without any door, and Product does not see the reason.
