Paid twice

Written 2026-10-27. One purpose: a team or a family never pays twice without the desk knowing. Recording by hand while a pay page is open warns and closes the page; a payment that lands anyway is flagged and put right.

What this walk is for

Two halves. Stop it: the desk is told when a pay page is open, and recording a payment by hand closes it (parts A and B). Catch it: when a team’s card payment lands after the team has already paid, it is booked, flagged with a pill, a line under the payment and a Needs attention item, and the refund window opens set to the extra; or the club keeps it with a reason (parts C and D). House league already refuses such a payment; it now gets a plain label (part E).

Parts C, D and E stage the real race (the team pays at the same moment the desk records), which needs a little setup: one Stripe window stopped for a minute and one browser setting. Each step says exactly what to do.

It passes when

  • An open pay page is named, with who opened it, the amount and the time, before you record by hand, and it is a note, never a block.
  • Recording by hand closes the open page, and the team or family cannot then pay on it.
  • A team payment that lands after the team paid in full reads “… paid twice”; one that lands on a team owing less reads “… more than owed”. Never the other way round.
  • The refund window opens set: all of it with the fee for paid twice; part of it at the extra otherwise.
  • “Keep it” is a note with a reason, not a credit. The pill and the item clear themselves once the extra is refunded or kept.

Not in this walk

  • Card disputes and Stripe fees: walk §396.
  • A bank debit paid twice. The bank path cannot be staged on localhost; the words and the database checks cover it.
  • Emails, beyond the held boxes. Localhost cannot send; they are in the one end-to-end pass on Amplify dev.

Keep the quotes exactly; PowerShell drops an unquoted @word.
Test card
Use Stripe’s Canadian test card 4000 0012 4000 0000, any future date, any CVC. (The usual 4242… card is issued in the US, which adds a foreign-card fee to the club’s bill: that is walk §396’s subject, not this one’s.)
Staging the race
Parts C, D and E. (1) Stop window 1 with Ctrl+C, so the payment isn’t heard at once. (2) In the private window, on Stripe’s page and before you pay, open DevTools (F12) › the ⋮ menu › More tools › Network request blocking; tick “Enable network request blocking” and add the pattern localhost:3000. Stripe takes the payment, but its return to our page is blocked. (3) The desk records by hand. (4) Untick “Enable network request blocking”, reload the blocked tab, and the payment lands. (5) Start window 1 again with the same command.
A team’s pay link
A team reaches its pay page through an email; localhost cannot send, so build the link yourself. Find the team’s id with node scripts/db-query.mjs --dev -q "select id from teams where name='TEAM NAME'", then from the project folder run:
node -e "const c=require('crypto'),fs=require('fs');const s=fs.readFileSync('.env.local','utf8').split(/\r?\n/).find(l=>l.startsWith('PAY_LINK_SECRET=')).slice(16).trim();const id=process.argv[1];console.log('http://localhost:3000/uat-rep-club/uat-rep-club-invitational-2026/pay/'+Buffer.from(id.replace(/-/g,''),'hex').toString('base64url')+'.'+c.createHmac('sha256',s).update('team-pay:'+id).digest().subarray(0,16).toString('base64url'))" TEAM-ID
