Signed-in family accounts · Stage 4 · drawings

When a child is placed on a team, the parent is invited to sign in

The email that tells a family their child’s team becomes the invitation. Accepting it signs the parent in and connects them to the child. From then on the team’s schedule and the family’s sign-up are on one page in the app. This page draws the email, the accept page, the parent’s first screen, two parents, the calendar, and what the club and coach see. Everything is a proposal until you rule on the asks in the last tab; the three that change the shape of the stage come first. /ux, /design and /legal have been through it, and the consent and privacy words are /legal’s draft.

Is this stage unblocked? Yes, with one fix the build has to carry.

  • Counsel is not a gate (your ruling of 2026-11-04). The guardian tier stays switched off until you turn it on, after the consent wording is real. /legal’s draft is placed on tabs 1, 2, 3 and 5.
  • Stage 14 did what it promised for rep teams. A parent’s connection now hangs on the child, a season roll no longer strands it, and a finished season’s calendar shows an empty calendar.
  • Two gaps remain, and both are this stage’s job. (1) A house-league child cannot be connected at all, because the connection still has to name a rep team and a rep roster entry. (2) A rep child who moves to another team next season loses the parent, because the connection is per team. Ask 3 decides how wide the fix is. It needs a database change.
  • Also found: no invitation email exists today; a rep coach copies a link and sends it themselves. Team chat is paused across the whole product (2026-10-18), and no chat room exists that a parent could join (ask 1).

Can a stranger be invited into another family’s child?

  • House league: accepting proves the inbox, not the parenthood. The invitation goes only to an address on that child’s sign-up, so the person who joins holds that inbox (or was forwarded the email by whoever does). That closes the Stage 3 finding for anyone who did not type the address.
  • Rep is different. The coach can type any address, and the email carries the child’s first name and the team. A mistyped address reaches a stranger, who could join and see the team’s times and places. The guardian box is the only check, and the coach sees who joined.
  • Someone signs up a child with their own address. Stage 14 makes a new child record for every sign-up and never guesses a match, so they reach only the team of the child they signed up. What they gain over today’s email is the practice times and places, which the existing “new team” email already lists. /legal: the tick-box is enough while the page shows only that; it stops being enough the day the page shows attendance, notes or medical details, which then needs the club to verify the parent.
  • The join is the real risk (tab 8): a club joining two records gives each record’s parents the other’s teams. The build guards it.
