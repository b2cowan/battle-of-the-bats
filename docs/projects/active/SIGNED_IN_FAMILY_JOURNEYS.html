How signing up works, and when people get an account

Each journey is drawn twice: once after the census fixes (today's product plus the agreed hardening, no app), and once in the end state, where parents sign in to see their team's schedule and chat. Every badge opens what protects that step.

One rule: you get an account when there's something in it for you

A coach's account is their team: it holds the roster, the schedule and every tournament they enter, so it starts the moment they register, which is how it already works. A parent's account would be empty until their child has a team, so it starts when the child is placed on a team. Before that, a family needs only their emails and one link, "Your sign-up". Fans never need one.

What makes it easy to understand

  • A family has one link, with one name. Today the same page is reached by "Check your registration", "Pay now" and "View your receipt". It becomes one button in every email, "Your sign-up", which always opens the same page.
  • The reference number becomes a number to quote, not a key. Lost the email? "Email me my link" sends it to the inbox again. Nothing typed on screen unlocks a child's details.
  • One invitation, at the moment it means something. The "your child's team" email is the account invitation, so there's never a separate "please create an account" email.
  • One page, two ways in. Once a parent has an account, the app shows the same "Your sign-up" page. A grandparent paying from the emailed link and a parent in the app see the same thing.

Already agreed on 31 October: the account comes at team placement; a signed-in returning parent gets a pre-filled form; the unused tournament claim link is deleted. These four are new, each with a recommendation.

1 · Coaches keep getting their account when they register

It's the opposite moment from parents, on purpose. A coach's account holds the team and is reused for every tournament; a parent's would be empty until there's a team.

Recommendation: yes, keep it. Nothing to build.

2 · Ask for a second parent's email on the house-league form

Today the form takes one guardian. Without a second email, only one parent gets the team invitation, and the other has to be invited by them afterwards.

Recommendation: yes, optional. Both parents get the invitation at placement. Counsel should see it with the rest of the guardian work.

3 · Tidy up tournament registration now, without waiting for the app

One email instead of two for a new coach; a finish screen that says "confirm your email" instead of offering to create an account they've just made; and portal links that don't carry the coach's email address.

Recommendation: yes, as its own small piece. It's independent of the census and of the app.

4 · Show "Your sign-up" inside the app for a signed-in parent

The same page the emailed link opens, listed under the parent's children, so paying and checking status work either way.

Recommendation: yes. One page with two ways in, rather than two pages that can drift apart.
