Practice time: hand out practices

Written 2026-10-27, walk 3 of 5 for Stage 11b (house league in the club’s schedule). One purpose; sign it off on its own.

What this walk is for

A league admin hands U9 Rookie’s practice time out to its teams in one press, and the practices appear where a league admin and a coach look: the season’s Practices view and the coach’s team page. A practice from the club’s time is the club’s to move: the house-league side can’t edit or cancel it. Nobody is emailed by a first hand-out.

It passes when

  • Practice time names the time it hands out and what each team gets, before anything is written.
  • Every team gets its practice a week inside the Tuesdays; the Practices view and the coach’s page show them, “From” the club’s schedule.
  • A practice from the club’s time has no Edit and no Cancel.
  • No family email: the season’s Email families has no new row.

Not in this walk

  • Moving or cancelling them from the club (walk §402).
  • Practices a week above 1 and two at once (ruled 11b·D; try them if you like and note it).
