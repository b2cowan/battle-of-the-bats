Before you start

  • Nothing to set up. Every club, person and demo above already exists on dev.
  • Give a club page a few seconds. The club’s sidebar learns what the club runs a moment after the page appears (it always has). Until then Tournaments and House league can sit under “Also on your plan” and the foot has no Tournaments door. Wait for the page to settle before you judge a club sidebar. On the dev server that can take 10–20 seconds.
  • Walk the parts in order, 1 to 4. Each opens with a stop check.

Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • The word over the sidebar is right on the very first paint (measured with scripts off) and never changes after the page loads: “CLUB” on Overview, Rep teams and Accounting; “EVENTS” in a tournament and in a tournament-only organization.
  • One Help per screen: the desktop account menu in the admin and the Coaches Portal; never in a public, family or support-console menu, never in the phone’s Account sheet; the More sheet’s foot on a phone; the sidebar only in the demos. A cancelled account’s menu shows no Help (its help page would send it to billing).
  • A person whose only program is tournaments keeps Tournaments as a sidebar row and a phone-bar button, before the club runs one too (no such person exists on dev; covered by tests).
  • A club with no current tournament keeps “Also on your plan · Tournaments” and gets no foot door (drawn in tab 12; tests).
  • At 1366 × 768 the foot stays at the bottom and the rows scroll above it (measured).

Your time goes on judgment: do the doors read as the way between portals, and do you find Help where it went?
