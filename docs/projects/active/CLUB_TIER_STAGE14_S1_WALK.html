Start next season now asks what the team is doing

Written 2026-11-04 for Stage 14 S1 (built, reviewed, help updated, no database change). One purpose: a head coach (and a club on a team’s behalf) starts next season by saying whether the same players are moving up or a new group is starting, and the window does exactly that: who comes, what happens to the division, what happens to the birth years, and what the success screen says.

What this walk is for

Start next season used to carry every active player and move the birth years one year later, every time. It now opens with What is the team doing next season? and nothing chosen; the Start button waits until you answer, and says why. The same players, moving up an age group brings the players you tick (active ticked, inactive unticked at the bottom, call-ups never listed), moves an age division up (11U AAA becomes 12U AAA), and leaves the birth years alone. A new group of players, same age group brings nobody unless you tick one box, keeps the division, and moves the birth years one year later. Both the coach’s window and the club’s copy say the same words.

Known before the walk

  • A roll cannot be undone, and this walk rolls. Each roll closes the 2026 Season for real. The fixture has a reset that puts every team back (same links), but it has been tested only against a simulated roll. The first reset after a real roll is in Part E: read its output. It should print “Reset: every fixture team is back to its starting state.” and then “Nothing new to build (all fixtures exist).” If it errors, run the same file with --destroy, then once with no flag. That rebuilds everything with new links; use the links the run prints (they are also written to .probe/s1-walk/CREDENTIALS.md).
  • Dev only, five throwaway teams in three throwaway portals. Nothing touches another walk, club or sign-in. Each team is on a live 2026 Season with 14 players: 12 active (with jersey numbers), 1 inactive, 1 call-up.
  • The coach’s window has no automatic layout check (there is no coach team to run one on). The phone part judges it by eye; the club’s window does have one.
  • Two sentences are held on purpose and are not in the window: any line that sends a coach to a past-player picker (“bring Jonah back from Roster”) waits for Stage 14 S3, which builds the picker; and the line about waivers, the code of conduct and medical forms waits for S4. Today the success screen names who stayed and stops there. See the owner calls at the foot.
  • Two small things changed after the build, in review. The club’s window now keeps the roll’s warnings in the notice (hard to trigger, so not a step), and its “Each returning player’s history” row disappears when your choice brings nobody (Part F has the step).
  • One word to look at, not a step. The coach’s window labels the birth-years line “Birth years for the 2027 Season” (capital S), while the club’s Team details has always said “Birth years for the 2027 season” (lower-case). Say if you want them to match.
  • The help articles were rewritten for both portals (Help › Coaches, and Help › Rep teams). Read them if you like; they are not walk steps. The pre-sales walkthrough’s Start-next-season line belongs to /marketing and was not touched.
  • The dev server must be running the committed build. If Team settings still reads “Your active roster carries forward”, the server is stale: restart it.
  • The numbers in this walk were checked against the real routes before it was written: every team lists 13 players (12 active, 1 inactive, the call-up absent, active first), seasons are live “2026 Season”, divisions and birth years are as below, a coach reads only their own team, the club owner reads the club’s teams, and a signed-out request is refused.

Not in this walk

  • Bringing a past player back later. That picker is Stage 14 S3. Nothing in this walk looks for it, and its absence is correct.
  • Documents (waivers, medical forms) and the money lines (the budget, the fee plan, the opening balance). They are unchanged by this stage.
  • A refused roll (a stale tick, an unknown player) and a tryout warning. The tests cover them.
