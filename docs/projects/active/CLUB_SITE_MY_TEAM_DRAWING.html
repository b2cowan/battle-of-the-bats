Today a house-league coach finds their team through a globe in the corner labelled "Public site", which flips between Home and the team page. This drawing replaces it with a link that says "My team". The club site keeps its own look in every frame below; the yellow dashed ring marks what changed.

  • "My team" sits in the personal corner, left of the account icon. The club's tabs are the same for every visitor and stay that way.
  • "My team" is drawn like a tab (same size and underline) rather than as a small word like "Sign in", because it is a place on this site you go to, the same kind of thing as the tabs.
  • On the team page "My team" is the one lit item: underlined like a tab, and "House league" goes unlit. On every other page it is a plain link. See A3: today "House league" lights on this page for everyone, so this makes the coach's bar differ from a family's on the same page.
  • It appears only for a signed-in coach of a team at this club. Visitors who are not signed in, families, and coaches at other clubs see the bar exactly as today.
  • The globe is gone for anyone whose only role at the club is a team page. BUILT already in this session; real staff keep their door.

Later · families

The same corner is where a signed-in parent would find their child's team: schedule and scores. They cannot be sent to this page, because it is the coach's view with every family's email and a Copy emails button. Parents need their own page first, so this slot is planned now and filled later. Its word is an open question ("My family", or the child's name).

Asks

A1 · Desk: "My team" in the personal corner, as drawn?

Or as a tab after "House league" instead. A tab is in the club's row, so it would appear a moment after the page loads and shift the other tabs.

A2 · Phone: inside the account menu (recommended), or a team icon in the band?
A3 · On the team page, "My team" lights and "House league" goes unlit?

The trade-off: a coach and a family looking at the same team page would see different tabs lit. The other choice is to leave "House league" lit and not light "My team", which is consistent but tells the coach less about where they are.

A4 · Two teams: "My teams" with a list on the desk, and a "My teams" heading in the phone menu (4b)?
A5 · Symbol: the jersey (recommended), or another from section 5?
