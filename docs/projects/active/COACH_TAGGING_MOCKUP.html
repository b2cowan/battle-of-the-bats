Eight things a coach meets as "a tag", two pickers doing one job, and six names on one door. These frames draw the decisions the tagging review needs rulings on — a central home, a quiet contextual door, one picker, one manager, one delete sentence — not every control. Every frame is the warm coaches portal with its real tokens.

What the code says that the planning prompt did not — read before judging the frames

  • No picker shows a usage count today. The combobox has a slot for one, but no caller fills it and the library route returns names only. Every "used on 12 records" below is NEW route work, and it is what makes the delete sentence and the merge choice honest — it is not a copy change.
  • The coach portal has no side-drawer for working surfaces. The thing the stylesheet calls a "slide-over" is a centered 720px modal; the only true right-hand sheet in the whole coach experience is the help drawer. A drawer for the manager (specimen 3B) would be a new idiom — drawn because Q9 asks, captioned as what it is.
  • The club admin already has a Shared library screen (game tags, money tags, award types) — so "Shared by your club — ask an admin" points at a real place, and the frames say so once.
  • The two `.tagChip` definitions collide by cascade order. The later one (blue remove-chip, bold) wins over the earlier toggle pill and over its own .tagChipActive state — so on the game form an applied tag is likely styled the same as an un-applied one. Not verified in a browser (this is a planning chat); the plan should verify and schedule it. GAP
  • On a phone every overlay is already a full-screen sheet (the 2026-07-28 polarity flip). Page vs modal vs drawer is a desktop question; on 360 the three collapse to one shape.

Specimen 7 of 7 · the one that is not a tag

The scouting filter stops dressing like a picker

Scouting tags are a fixed, owner-ratified list from the sport pack — Pitching, Hitting, Baserunning, Defence, Coaching. The filter draws them as the same pill chips a tag picker uses, so a coach who has just minted a money tag reads them as mintable. They are not; the fix is to draw them as what they are — a filter over a fixed list.

The gate · rulings needed before a plan is written

Nine questions, a position on each, and which frame to argue with

#QuestionPosition to argue withFrame
Q1Where does the central home live?Team settings, as a "Tags" section. It is configuration, and the six sections beside it are the team's other once-a-season decisions. A first-class nav entry would give a rare act a hot slot; the team hub's settings group is the same answer one click further away.1A · 1B
Q2Does every apply-screen keep a door?Yes, one quiet door, inside the picker — the dropdown's last row. Otherwise a typo found mid-practice-plan costs a trip to settings. On the Ledger, which has no picker in its toolbar, the Tags filter pill carries the same row.2A · 2B · 4B
Q3Delete-in-use: orphan with a count, merge-only, or refuse?Orphan with the count, and the dialog offers Merge as a live button. A label is not money; a refusal would send the coach hunting for the 12 records to untag by hand. Budget items keep their refusal — an item is a filing key with history.3A
Q4Do budget items and test types adopt the same manager shell?Same shell, own rules, not drawn here. Row = name · count · actions; the item manager keeps refuse-in-use and "use a shared word instead"; test types keep retire/restore and gain nothing. Whether they also move into the Tags section is a separate call — the recommendation is no: budget items are budget content (the 08-16 ruling refused settings for them), and test types belong to the Skills & Goals room.—
Q5Drill kit → Equipment library? Adopt chip or silent import?Yes; the adopt row, never a silent import. An import mints every old spelling as a tag.6B
Q6House word: "tag" or "label"? Is "Manage tags" the one door name?"Tag" — it already carries the product, nine help answers and the demo. "Manage tags…" is the one door; the manager's title is the library's name ("Money tags", "Staff"). "Your tags", "Manage money tags", "Manage game tags" all retire.1A · 2A
Q7Scouting tags: keep fixed — but stop looking like a picker?Keep fixed; draw as a segmented filter.7B
Q8The 50-per-kind cap: surface it, or refusal only?One sentence at the foot of the Tags section, and the refusal stays as the backstop. Not on every picker.1A
Q9Modal, page or drawer for the manager?Page section (3A), navigated to from the door with the library open and the form's typing kept. The modal is refused by the owner's own ruling. The drawer (3B) is drawn honestly: it would be the coach portal's first working-surface drawer, and from the bill form it is a sheet over a modal.1A · 3A · 3B
+Does the picker show counts, or only the manager?Drawn both ways in 2A/2B. Leaning manager only — a coach applying a tag does not need to know it is on 12 things, and the row is cleaner without.2A · 2B
