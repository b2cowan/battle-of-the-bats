# Coach names on team pages: product brief

**Proposed functionality.** The club's owner chooses, once for every team, whose name appears under Coaches on a team's public page:
only coaches who choose to (today), every head coach, every head and assistant coach, or nobody.

**Why it matters.** Today any coach can put their own name on the club's public site and the club has no say. Some clubs want a
consistent public face (every head coach shown). Others have a safeguarding position (no names). Both are the club's call, not each
volunteer's.

**What changes for whom.**
- **Owner:** a new row on Public site, "Coaches on team pages". Choosing either "always" option asks first and says how many people it
  will list. Hiding names, or going back to "coaches choose", just saves.
- **Admin:** sees the choice as plain text; cannot change it.
- **Head and assistant coaches:** where the club has decided for their role, their name switch is replaced by one sentence ("Listed
  by your club" or "Not listed by your club"). Their own earlier choice is kept, and returns if the club changes the setting. A coach
  the club newly lists gets one in-app notice.
- **Managers, treasurers and helpers:** keep their own switch under both "always" choices.
- **Families and the public:** the Coaches shelf on the team page lists whoever the rule says. Nothing else about the page changes.

**Priority.** Medium. It closes a privacy and control gap in a Club Tier feature that is not yet on sale, so it should ship with the
Club release rather than after it.

**Success.** An owner can state the club's policy in one sentence and see it hold on every team page; no coach can publish or hide
their name against the club's choice; no coach is surprised by a listing; nothing is lost when the club changes its mind.

**Open question for the owner.** The family tryout page also prints the head coach's name ("Email the head coach"). Recommended: the
club's choice governs that name too, so "No coach names" really means no names anywhere public.
