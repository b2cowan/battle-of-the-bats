Before you start

Walks 1, 2, 3 and 6 pay nothing and change nothing; the worst they do is open a Stripe page you leave. Walk 4 is permanent for Coach B: that portal becomes a club and there is no way back down. Use a portal you made for it.

Check phone steps at 390px wide (browser dev tools, device mode). If a step does not match what you see, mark the part Issue and write what you saw; the screen may have moved since this was written.

Three screens nobody drew, built from the nearest shipped pattern. A last payment that failed, a portal that holds more than one team (or has moved into another club), and “your upgrade is going through” after the money is taken. They show as a notice inside the window. Tell me if you want any of them redrawn.

Cannot be walked, and why (the automated tests cover each):

  • “Contact us to move”. That is the Club row before Club is on sale. Dev has every plan on sale, so you will see “Start my club” instead. Production will show “Contact us to move” to every paying Premium owner until Club opens.
  • A coach whose last payment failed and a portal with more than one team. No dev portal is in either state, and neither can be forced.
  • A coach who once paid, then was given free time (the window shows the full charge due today and no second trial). No such portal exists on dev.
  • A helper or assistant typing the billing address (refused, no row in their menu). No such account exists on dev.
  • “Taking longer than usual” and “We couldn’t confirm”. These need the payment confirmation to be late or to fail, which cannot be forced.
  • A free-time coach returning from paying while their portal is locked. That is the race behind Walk 6; the unit tests mock the browser header, so only Walk 6 can show whether it holds.
