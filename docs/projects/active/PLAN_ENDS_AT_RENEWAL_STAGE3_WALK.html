Stripe's own renewal, rehearsed: what each club ended up as, seen as the owner and as staff

Written 2026-11-05 for Stage 3 (the rehearsal f1225ef96; the two defects it found, F1 f8ff3570f and F3 16992dc85). Fourteen clubs were run on Stripe test clocks against the dev site this morning; they are kept there for this walk.

What this walk is for

Every earlier test of "plans end at the renewal date" used stand-ins for Stripe. This morning a script gave fourteen clubs a real Stripe test subscription each, on a clock it could move forward, and let Stripe send its own messages to the dev site at the renewal. It found two real defects, both fixed and re-run, and one place where Stripe behaves differently from the plan (F2, already ruled). The script's report says what happened in numbers. This walk is the human half: you open the clubs it left behind and check that what an owner and an operator see matches the report.

It passes when

  • A cancel ends on the renewal date, monthly, annual and during a trial, with the data kept for 90 days from that date and exactly two emails.
  • A trial that was cancelled never got a "trial ending, add a card" email.
  • A move to the free plan keeps the picked tournament, archives the others (including one made during the wait), and never suspends the club.
  • Keep after a cancel over a cheaper move renews as the cheaper plan, at its price (F1 and F3 fixed).
  • End now ends at once with its typed reason, and the past-due club shows F2 as ruled.

Not in this walk

  • Stripe's portal page itself. The rehearsal sets what the portal does through Stripe's API; the page was proven in the Stage 1 walk (§444, C2).
  • The seven-day reminder and Finish this ending now. They run on the real calendar, not the clock; the Stage 1 and Stage 2 walks cover them.
  • A real bank decline, and a Coaches Portal ending. Not rehearsed; the report says so.
  • The live Stripe account. Its portal setting is created the first time anyone opens the live portal; that read is recorded in the plan as owed.
