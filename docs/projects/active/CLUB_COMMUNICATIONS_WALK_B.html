Writing to coaches and to the board, from the same window the families use

Written 2026-11-20, after Session 3 (the windows, committed cc99f58e9). One purpose: writing to coaches and to the board. Walk A (the place, who sees what) passed 35 of 35 on 2026-11-20.

What this walk is for

New email is now one lime button with a choice inside: To families, To coaches, To the board. You write to coaches as the owner (all of them, the rep staff, the house-league coaches, or three you pick), see who the email reaches and how it will arrive, and watch a coach change hold the send. Jules Moreau, a league admin, can reach only the house-league coaches. You email the board; Avery Chen, the treasurer, has her own Communications with one button, Email the board. A board email picked for two people stays invisible to the board member who wasn’t picked. Then you send again from a record, finish two emails that stopped partway (only the right seat can), and look at it all on a phone.

It passes when

  • 1 The menu offers each seat only what it may write to; a treasurer’s button names its one choice.
  • 2 The coach window shows every group with a live count, the pick, who gets it and the email itself, with no address anywhere.
  • 3 Where replies go is the same two rows and the same inbox tick in all three windows, and the board window opens on “Replies come to me” with no tick.
  • 4 A league admin sees and names only house-league coaches.
  • 5–7 The board email reaches its four board members; the treasurer writes to the board and sees only board emails she sent or received; a picked email stays private.
  • 8 “Send again to these people” opens the window with the pick filled in and sends nothing.
  • 9 A stopped email can be finished only by a seat that may write to that group.
  • 10 A coach changing while you write holds the send and says who changed.
  • 11 On a phone New email keeps its words and each window is a full-screen sheet.

Known before the walk (not defects of this walk)

  • Three things were drawn and are not built. “See who gets it” has no “Nobody on the staff” or “No coach entered yet” rows (the engine does not report them); the coach foot does not say “You are not counted.” (the board’s does); the reply rows and the inbox tick were built from the shipped Who rows, as no drawing shows them.
  • The tournament pointer line shows for any tournament with an active status, here “Harbourtown Invitational 2026”, not only one on its game days.
  • The family window now opens on “Replies go to {Club}” when the club has a contact address (it opened on the sender before). You switch it back with “Replies come to me”.
  • The “?” on Communications still opens the old emailing article; the Close session rewrites it.
  • Nothing leaves the machine. Every address in this copy, the club’s contact address included, ends in .local, which the product never sends to. The sends are real; the mail service is never asked.
  • Part 10 changes the data (it removes a house-league coach from a team), so do it after parts 2 to 9. Parts 2 to 9 quote the coaches and board as they are before it.
