What each marker says

1

The bill’s schedule is the last tinted section inside a form

It came from the same shared style as the event form’s boxes; when those flattened this morning I kept this one because its August drawing was built on the tint. Your ruling ends that exception.

2

Flat, and bounded by what it already had changed

The block keeps its eyebrow heading, its two tools on the right, the count, the column heads and the ✓ line — that is enough to read as one thing without a box. It separates from the fields above and the More door below by space, the same rule the event form’s groups follow now. The ✓ / short / over line keeps its green, amber or red — that is a status, not a panel.

3

Phone cards go hairline-on-white changed

Each installment was a white card on the tint; the tint is gone, so the cards keep their hairline and sit on the form’s white. The budget line’s period grid paints its own phone cards with the tint — it takes the same treatment, so the three schedule editors (bill, budget line, dues) agree on a phone.

4

On a page, the same panel wears a blue hairline

Season’s End frames four of its blocks this way. The Staff screen dropped exactly this treatment on 2026-09 for a plain white card and a hairline, and left Settings and Season’s End “for a separate call” — this is that call.

5

A panel on paper becomes a white card with a hairline changed

Two grounds, one rule: inside a white form a section goes flat and separates by space; on the paper page a panel becomes a white card with a hairline. No tinted third surface anywhere.

6

Small ones count too

The repeating dates’ header on the event form is a tinted row — the last tint on the form you approved this morning. The inventory tab lists the rest of these: attendance rows, the week view’s day columns, the player page’s mini rows, the practice run’s “You’re on” line, the lineup peek, the link-a-club card.

7

A header row is a row changed

Same weight, a hairline beneath — it already had the bold count to say it is the header.

8

The item-create tray wore its own blue

The inventory missed it because it is painted with a literal blue rather than the shared tint — the one place in the coaches portal still blue on the warm look too (it had its own olive override). It also carried “(optional)” on Suggested $ and a Title Case “Add Item”.

9

A hairline tray, not fully flat built

It keeps a hairline because it is an inline tool with its own Cancel / Add item inside a form that has a footer of its own — the coach has to see what the inner Cancel cancels. That is exactly the shape the bill editor’s “Repeat monthly” tray has, so the two agree. “Suggested $” loses its tag; “Add item” takes sentence case. The picker is shared by nine forms (every money form, the club tab, the drives and sponsors), so all of them take it at once.

Full plan — the tinted panel retires

Ruled 2026-09-21 · built on dev 2026-09-21 (CSS only, no migration) · source: docs/projects/active/COACH_TINTED_PANEL_RETIREMENT_PLAN.md

The rule

  • Inside a white form, a section goes flat and separates by space.
  • On the paper page, a panel becomes a white card with a hairline.
  • State fills (selected / on / now / drag-over / active) keep the colour.

What changed

  • White card + hairline: every phone card portal-wide (the shared row recipe and every table-as-cards), the roster's phone rows, Season's End's four blocks and its checklist items (their blue hairline becomes the house hairline), the link-a-club card, the player page's mini rows, the week view's day columns, the attendance rows, the lineup peek's stats strip, the budget sandbox note.
  • Fill off, own border stays: the attendance status group and badge, the lineup builder's "not playing" tray, the lineup peek's empty state, the roster's pitch chip, the practice rail badge, the practice run's "You're on" line (keeps its olive border), the scroll hint (gains a hairline), the sample-budget fence (keeps its dashed lime).
  • Fill off, nothing replaces it: money table footers, the drive hand-in row, the month view's day-name header, the repeating-dates header on Add Event.
  • The bill's payment schedule: flat — a group of the form; its phone installment cards white with a hairline, and so are the budget line's and the dues editor's (one shared rule now, no per-editor overrides).
  • The item picker's "add an item" tray (every money form): a hairline tray on white in place of a literal blue; "Suggested $" without its tag; "Add item".

Left alone, by kind

The state fills; the game console's identity chips (position, period, who — its own day-of palette, flagged); the dues matrix's pinned-column ground (mechanics); the budget checklist's open-category tint (open is a state); admin surfaces; three dead selectors.

Verification

CSS purity, selector, contrast and spelling gates clean; the full changed-files gate clean. Rendered check clean on attendance, roster, player, lineup builder and payables; Season's End's four findings reproduce with the pre-sweep stylesheet (A/B'd) — pre-existing.

Second wave (D4)

The family and public pages (~20 panels) and the tryout day cards (~14) — same rule, own drawing, own session.
