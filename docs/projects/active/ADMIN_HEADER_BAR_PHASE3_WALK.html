The screens with no place of their own name themselves in the header

Written 2026-11-09. One purpose: the header on the Phase 3 screens. Computer first, then a phone pass.

What this walk is for

Until now, Calendar, Schedules, Notifications, House league’s Seasons, the Public site, Plan & billing and the record pages one level down (a schedule, a team’s History, one past season, a child, Rename team URLs, a club schedule read from a season) showed the club’s name in the header and their own title on the page. Now the header names the screen, the record pages put their way back there too, and the title on the page is gone. The page’s own buttons stay at its top right. A tournament-only organization’s list and settings now name the organization instead of whichever event was picked last. Built to round 5 (your Q1–Q6, all as recommended).

It passes when

  • Each screen in parts A and B shows its name once, in the dark strip on a computer and in the white bar on a phone, and has no title of its own on the page.
  • A record page shows its way back (“← Schedules”, “← 15U AAA”, “← History”…) beside its name, and its chip; a past season also shows its team as quiet grey words beside the name.
  • While a record loads you see a placeholder (“Schedule”, “Child”) and its way back, then the name. Never the club’s name first.
  • Someone a screen refuses sees the club’s name in the header and the refusal’s own heading.
  • The tournament organization’s list, Settings & access, Members and Plan & billing name the organization; an event’s pages, Event settings included, name the event.
  • The Calendar’s phone View menu is the first thing in its toolbar row.

Not in this walk

  • Club setup (“Get your club ready for the season”) keeps its own heading (your Q1).
  • Places with tabs (Rep teams, a team, a season, Accounting, Families, Organization) and the coaches portal: measured identical before and after, node for node, on a computer and a phone.
  • A team’s account in Accounting: still a view of Accounting’s Teams tab (2026-10-16), unchanged.

Known before the walk

  • Measured before publishing (1440 × 760 and 390 wide): one page heading on every screen, the header’s; nothing pinned grew on a computer (the strip alone, 48px); on a phone a record’s bar is two lines (53–54px), as a team’s and a season’s already are; nothing scrolls sideways; a slowed-down first load goes from the placeholder to the name with no club name in between.
  • The test club’s schedule is not one its house-league season holds time in, so the club-schedule page (B6) shows its own “couldn’t load” line under the header’s placeholder Schedule. The header’s shape is still checkable there.
  • The test child has no date of birth on file, so “Born …” does not show on B4. It sits beside the name the way the past season’s team does (B3).
  • The Calendar’s phone View button keeps its shipped hairline border (A7 said keep the shipped look). The round 3 drawing drew it borderless; the shipped control never was.
  • Two help sentences now read wrong (the Calendar’s “icon beside the title”, the Public site’s “top of the page shows an Online chip”): offered to the help agent, not changed here.
