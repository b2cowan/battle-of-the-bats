What changed since the October bank-debit drawings, and where I disagree

  1. "Processing" now means two things on the same page. 12b built "Payment processing" as a cost line in the season's book. A11 ruled "Processing" as the Money band's word for a debit still clearing. I recommend "Clearing" for the debit (ask 5). It changes a ruled word, so it's your call.
  2. The old drawings never said what stops a second payment. The pay page decides "owing" from the season's book, and a clearing debit isn't in the book yet, so as built the family would see Pay again the next day (ask 6).
  3. A child declined while a debit clears becomes the common case. A card payment lands in seconds; a debit takes days. I recommend the ending answers the money question up front (ask 7).
  4. A debit can come back after it clears, not only while clearing (a bank can reverse a debit the account holder disputes). The October drawings only had "returned while clearing". Drawn in frame 3.
  5. Who is told changed. The October drawings sent a returned debit's notice to "the convenor". Under §0f it goes to the flow's desk: the house-league desk, or whoever runs tournaments.
  6. Not settled here; for the build to check in Stripe's current docs, then /billing: whether some banks make the family verify their account (which changes "up to 5 business days" and adds a "needs your action" state), how long a refund to a bank account takes, how long after clearing a debit can be reversed, and what a returned debit costs the club.

After · the record after a part refund (ask 3)

The rule that has to be chosen. A team owes the tournament's entry fee less what it paid, and there is no per-team fee today. So a $40.00 refund either leaves the team owing $40.00, or it lowers what this one team owes. A cancelled game means the second. That needs one new thing: an adjustment to this team's entry, shown on the record with its reason. I recommend it (ask 3, A). Without it, every part refund reads as a debt.
