What the club sees, the emails, and the help

The club’s owners and admins now see each person’s two-step state on Members (and in the export), and Overview tells them who still needs to set it up until the last one does. The owner gets a one-time card saying the club can require it. A person whose 14 days start gets an email, and another 3 days before the deadline; turning two-step on or off, or changing a password, emails the account. A new help article covers all of it. Seven walks, one purpose each. About 40 minutes; you need your phone’s authenticator app and the fieldlogichq@gmail.com inbox.

Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • Every state from the server, as owner, admin and treasurer, probed on dev: the treasurer’s Members list carries no two-step field at all, and the Overview read refuses a treasurer. A suspended person reads only On or Off.
  • Members now reads only this club’s own people (your ruling); before, it read one platform-wide page of 1,000 accounts and anyone past it showed “(unknown)”.
  • Each grace email goes once per person per deadline, even if two runs overlap; a failed send is tried again the next hour; the job fits inside the app’s 30-second limit, and a big club’s emails go out over a few hours. With no mail key nothing is attempted.
  • The five emails have no link, say so, carry no offer, and say only what really happened (a sign-out or an old-phone removal that didn’t finish is said plainly).
  • The copy pass changed words on Parts 1–3’s screens too (e.g. “Your club now requires two-step sign-in”, “your authenticator app” everywhere, the lost-phone choices each say what happens next, the Settings card says owners are emailed too). You will meet some of them in passing.

Your time goes on judgment: is the column quiet enough, does the Overview reminder read as useful rather than nagging, are the emails clear to a volunteer, and does the help answer what you’d ask?
