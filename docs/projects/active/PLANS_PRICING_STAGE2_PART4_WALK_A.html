Renewals, under Customers

Written 2026-11-07. One purpose: Billing, Support and you can see, on one dated list, what renews or ends across every club and which bills are unpaid, from our own records, without opening Stripe. Read only: nothing on this page changes money, Stripe or a club.

What this walk is for

Before this page, "who renews this month?" or "which bills are unpaid?" meant opening each club's Money tab, or Stripe. Now Customers has a Renewals page with two views. Coming up lists what renews or ends in the dates you pick: renewals, trial ends, free time and Founding Season endings, with the price on file before tax. Unpaid bills lists the bills our own record holds as open or written off. Today’s Past due and Trials ending rows now open these lists. Two fixes ride along: Trials ending no longer counts a trial whose date has passed, and the monthly figure on Business numbers leaves comped clubs out and says how many.

You will make one trial look overdue and put it back, make one club look past due with no bill and put it back, export both lists, and check who sees the page.

It passes when
  • Renewals opens from Customers, after Free time, for you, Billing and Support only. It has no button that changes anything.
  • Every chip opens its explanation when pressed, and a pick narrows the list but not the band.
  • A trial past its date shows once, in amber, and leaves Trials ending (on the page and on Today).
  • A past-due club with no bill in our record shows in amber with "No bill in our record", never as nothing owed.
  • The exports open in Excel and as CSV with the same rows, and the Audit log records each one.
  • Business numbers and Today say how many comped clubs the monthly figure leaves out.
