W1 · An event that doesn’t track payments

What this walk is for

This is the case from your screenshot: an event set to “Not tracked here” showed an amber “Unpaid” on every accepted team, under a Registration health that said nothing was missing. The walk uses an event that has real fees on file, so it also proves an old fee doesn’t leak through while payments are off.

It passes when

With “Not tracked here” chosen, no screen in steps 4–15 shows a fee, an amount owed, “Paid”, “Unpaid” or a payment control. After the put-back, the Payment column returns with the same amounts as step 2.

Not in this walk

What coaches see. Their tournament page drops its Fee row, the public registration form drops its payment panel, the acceptance email drops its Payment section, the registration-received and game-day emails drop their payment sentences, “Payment recorded” never sends, and payment reminders are refused. Walking any of those means emailing or registering a team, so unit tests and a render check hold them. Also not here: the production event from your screenshot. It changes when this reaches production at the next release.

  1. Signed in, switch to UAT Plus Org and pick UAT Phase 2C Plus Championship 2026. Open Event Settings (sidebar, Setup) and open the Fees & payments card.

    Fee model reads Per division, and the card lists each division’s fee (U11 Girls $475, U13 Boys $525, U15 Open $575).

    If it already reads “Not tracked here”, an earlier walk left it switched. Stop here and say so in the notes.

  2. Open Teams and choose the U11 Girls division.

    The before picture: a Payment column, with Comets U11 Girls reading Owes $400 in amber and Falcons U11 Girls reading Paid.

  3. Back in Event Settings › Fees & payments, set Fee model to Not tracked here and wait for Saved. Then open the Coach emails card.

    Under the Fee model: “No fees are set up, so payments aren’t tracked here. If teams pay, they pay you directly.” Coach emails lists five automatic emails: Registration received, Team accepted, Registration declined, Schedule published, Game-day reminder. Payment recorded is gone.

  4. Open Teams, U11 Girls, at full computer width.

    The table’s columns are Team, Coach and the pool or slot column. No Payment column. No amber anywhere in the list.

  5. Look at the At a glance card, then open its Registration health row.

    The card has Registration health and Registration rows, with no Payments row between them. Opened, Registration health shows three tiles side by side (Teams, Missing email, Needs action) and no Payments tile, with no gap where it was.

  6. Click Filter.

    Status (and Group by, if the division has pools). No Payment section. Close it.

  7. Open Tools › Select many and tick Comets U11 Girls. Look at the action bar, then press Done without pressing any action.

    Accept, Waitlist and Reject (plus “Move to pool” if the division has pools). No Deposit paid, Paid in full or Send a reminder.

  8. Click Comets U11 Girls to open its record. Close it without changing anything.

    No Payment section: no “Owes”, no Mark paid, no deposit or total-paid fields.

  9. Click + Add team, look, then press Cancel.

    The Add Team Manually window with Division on a line of its own, full width. No Payment status field.

  10. Narrow the browser to a phone (about 390px) and look at the U11 Girls rows.

    Each row’s line under the team name gives the pool (where there is one) and the coach. No “Owes” or “Paid” on any row.

  11. Back at full width, open Check-in for the same event. Open Comets U11 Girls’ sheet, then close it without pressing anything.

    The table’s columns are Team and Roster, then the arrival column. No Payment column, and no “… teams still owe” line under the arrival bar. The team’s sheet has its Roster section but no Payment section.

  12. Open Communications › New message. Open the Start from list, then tick Email the teams. Close with Cancel without sending.

    Start from offers a blank message and four templates: no “Payment reminder”. Under Email the teams, the pickers are Teams and Division only, with no Payment.

  13. Open Divisions and click U11 Girls. Close it without changing anything.

    Its settings list Game length, Tie-breakers and Contact. No Fee line (it read “$475 · $150 deposit by May 28” before step 3).

  14. Back on Teams, open Export › Excel and open the downloaded file.

    After “Registered at” the next column is Waitlist position. None of Payment status, Deposit paid, Total paid, Payment readiness status, Amount due or Payment due date.

  15. Open the Dashboard for this event and find its Payments card, if the board shows one. Your call: it still shows a single row reading “Not tracked here”, which links to Event Settings. That wording was ruled on 10 Oct, so it was left as it is.

  16. Put it back: Event Settings › Fees & payments, set Fee model to Per division and wait for Saved.

    The card lists each division’s fee again, unchanged ($475, $525, $575), and Coach emails lists Payment recorded again.

  17. Open Teams, U11 Girls.

    The Payment column is back: Comets U11 Girls Owes $400 in amber and Falcons U11 Girls Paid, the same as step 2.

W2 · An event with no fee amount

What this walk is for

The second half of the same finding. An event that tracks payments but has no fee amount set also showed an amber “Unpaid” on every accepted team. The amber means money is owed, and with no fee nothing is. The row now reads “—” until someone marks the team paid.

It passes when

Teams and Check-in show “—” for both accepted teams, nothing is amber, and the team’s record offers Mark paid without calling the team unpaid.

Not in this walk

Pressing Mark paid. It would email the team’s contact, so the step only looks. A team marked paid with no fee reads “Paid”, and a unit test holds that.

  1. Switch to UAT Test Org and pick UAT Import Smoke Tournament. Open Event Settings › Fees & payments. Don’t change anything.

    Fee model reads Choose how fees work (none chosen yet), so payments are tracked but no fee is set.

  2. Open Teams (division U12).

    A Payment column reading — for both accepted teams, Smoke Existing Team and Smoke Import Commit mpxbvc1u. No amber “Unpaid”.

  3. Open Smoke Existing Team’s record. Look at its Payment section, then close the record without pressing Mark paid.

    A Payment section holding only a Mark paid button. No “Unpaid” and no amber.

  4. Open Check-in for the same event.

    A Payment column reading — for both teams, and no “… teams still owe” or “All teams are paid.” line under the arrival bar.
