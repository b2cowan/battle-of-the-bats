Mark a GST/HST return period as filed

What this walk is for

You finish a return, mark its period as filed, and later something changes: a supplier's GST/HST number arrives for a receipt that was held. This walk checks that the filed figures stay exactly as you filed them, that "Changed since you filed" says what changed and what to do, that the released credit shows up on the next return's line 106 once, and that marking the period again keeps the history.

It passes when the filed figures never move, every change reads with its "what to do", the released credit appears once on a later return, a period that hasn't ended or overlaps a filed one can't be marked, and nothing tax-related appears anywhere but this view.

Not in this walk: the accountant package files (Session D), the worksheet's own lines and windows (Session B's walk), anything on the Money tab or a club screen.

Before you start: this walk writes, and a filing can't be deleted

  • It adds one expense ("Walk test supplier") and marks October 2026 as filed, twice. The app has no way to remove a filing; that is the point of it.
  • While October 2026 stays filed on dev, any other dates that overlap it (for example the whole of 2026) read "overlaps" there. The last part asks whether to keep or remove the walk's rows; removing them is a one-off clean-up on dev with the database's admin login, never the app.
  • Storage for filings is on dev (applied November 7, 2026). If the question ever says "Bookkeeping storage is being updated", stop and tell me.
