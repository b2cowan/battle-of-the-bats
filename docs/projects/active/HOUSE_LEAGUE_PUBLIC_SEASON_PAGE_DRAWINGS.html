A finished season should read as a result, not a closed sign-up form

Today a family who opens a finished season sees the sign-up page with one sentence swapped in: the price, a “0 / 40 spots filled” bar, a grey notice and two buttons. The thing they came for (who won, how their child’s team did) is a click away. These drawings give the page two shapes: the sign-up shape while families can register, and the season shape once registration closes, which leads with the standings.

The page’s job changes when registration closes

After registration closes nobody can sign up, and the family wants the season itself: the table, the games, and (once it is over) the result. Where I changed my mind since the chat

  • “Email me my link” stays on a finished season. I said it had no purpose there. It does: a family’s sign-up page stays open for 30 days after the club completes the season, and for as long as they still owe a fee. A family who owes money or needs a receipt gets back in through this line. It stays where it is, unchanged.
  • There is no champion in house league. Nothing records a playoff or a winner; the closest thing is first place on the final table. The drawings say “Final standings” and highlight the first-place row. They never write “Champion”, which would claim something the product doesn’t record.
  • Seasons are not linked to each other. Nothing records that Fall 2026 follows Summer 2026. The “next season” line can only mean “a newer season at this club that is taking sign-ups”. Tab 4 shows what that means for a club running two seasons at once.

“The next season” has to mean something the product knows

An old season’s page is what a family finds through a search or an old bookmark. If the club is taking sign-ups for a newer season, that is the most useful line on the page. But nothing links one season to the next: Fall 2026 is not recorded as following Summer 2026. So the honest rule is “every newer season at this club that is taking sign-ups, by name”. That is right for a club running one house league at a time, which is the common case. For a club running two seasons side by side (allowed since Q14), a U9 family might also see a U13-only season. I think that is acceptable: it is the same club, and both are named.
