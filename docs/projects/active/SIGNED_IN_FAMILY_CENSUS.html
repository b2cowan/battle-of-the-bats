Harden the house-league pay link (your Q2)

  • Show only what paying needs: each child's first name, their status, what's owed, and receipts. Drop the surnames and the guardian's email from the page. A forwarded email then reveals the least.
  • Stop working at the end of the season. Old links in old inboxes don't stay open for years.
  • Let the club reset one family's link ("Send a new link"), which cancels the old one. Today the only way to cancel one link is to cancel every family's and every tournament team's at once.
  • Don't put it in emails where nothing is owed. Placement emails carry it today even when the family has paid.
  • Replace "email + reference number" with "Email me my link". The family types their email and we send the link to that inbox. The reference stays printed in emails, but as a number to quote to the club, not a key.

Depends on nothing technically. It must land before online payments are switched on. The same reset-and-expire pattern can later go on the tournament team pay link.

4

The account arrives when the team is confirmed (your Q1)

  • When a child is confirmed on a team, the guardian's email gets "Set up your account to see the schedule and team chat". Opening it requires creating an account or signing in, which is the same safe shape as today's guardian invite.
  • A parent who already has an account (a returning family, or a sibling on another team) is connected without a new invite. Where the match isn't certain, a club person confirms it, the same way the Families book already handles duplicate parents.
  • The schedule, practices, chat, the calendar feed (Q3) and the In / Out / Maybe reply all live behind that account.

Depends on Stage 14 (so next season's confirmation reconnects to the same child) and counsel (the guardian connection is switched off pending the privacy and anti-spam review).

What the Stage 14 child record has to carry

Today there's no child record. A child exists only as a row per season: a house-league sign-up, a tryout sign-up, a rep roster entry, each with the name and birth date typed again. Your Q1 answer makes the moment that matters exact: when a child is confirmed on a team, the parent's account is connected to that child. For that connection to survive into next season, the child record has to carry:

  1. The parent-to-child connection itself. Today it's tied to one season's roster entry, so next season's roster disconnects a verified parent.
  2. Every season's sign-up and tryout row, so a returning child's confirmation lands on the same child and the parent sees both years.
  3. Consents and waivers. Stage 14 already plans to move documents and waivers onto the child, with a "needs re-signing this season" state. The sign-up consent belongs on the same list; today it's tied to one sign-up row.
  4. The parent as a person, not a club member, so one parent with children at two clubs isn't caught by the one-home-club rules.

One tension to settle before Stage 14 is drawn

The Families book ruled that a household is confirmed by a person, never guessed, and called "what identifies a child?" the wrong question. A child record that lasts across seasons is a lasting child identity. Stage 14 needs to say how a returning child gets matched to their record: proposed by name and birth date, then confirmed by a club person (the way returning players are already checked), never matched automatically.

A · In house league, which moment is "confirmed on the team"?

A child is first accepted into the season (often with the fee due), and later placed on a team, sometimes months apart.

Recommendation: placed on a team. That's when there's a schedule and a chat to see. Until then the pay link covers what a family needs.

B · Can a returning parent sign up while signed in?

Under Q1, signing up never creates an account. But a parent from last season already has one.

Recommendation: yes, optionally. A signed-in parent gets the form filled in from last season. The public form keeps working for everyone else. Either way, no account is created at sign-up.
