# The free tournament tier — PM brief

Plan: `FREE_TOURNAMENT_TIER_REVIEW_PLAN.md` · Hub: `FREE_TOURNAMENT_TIER_REVIEW_HUB.html` · Status: **the deep review
is done and the owner ruled the same day (2026-10-12). The mockups were drawn the same day on the hub's Mockups tab
(v5 after the design review) and the owner ruled all twelve presentation asks as recommended. Built and committed 2026-10-16
(plan §12); the owner's walk is on the hub's QA Walk tab.**

**The build (2026-10-16).** A free organizer and a Premium coach host now run an invite-only event: they add each team,
and the coach's email is the invitation to claim it. Public registration, the review queue, the waitlist, fees and
payment tracking, check-in, the Staff kit and volunteers, and Tournament Chat are gone from their screens and refused by
the server. Every paid tool they reach for wears the same padlock line, which says "Free for you through September 30,
2027" to a Founding Season organization still on free. The composer says the email limit before Send. A coach's event
keeps its PDFs and carries the free badge and banner. Five calls were made at the build start: no special handling for
older events (production had no free event), the server refuses too, Coach emails is three lock lines (the game-day
reminder stays Tournament Plus; in the walk the owner removed the card on free altogether, since nothing in it
applies), the coach's claim keeps one team to one entry on every plan, and divisions have no
"spots" on free.

**The drawings (2026-10-12).** Fourteen true-size drawings of what a free organizer and a coach host see, before and
after: Teams becomes a list of who is in, by pool; a team's record keeps the team, its coach, its pool and notes; Event
settings keeps five cards (four since the walk: Coach emails left); the board keeps Teams, Schedule and Messages; Check-in, the Staff kit and Chat leave every
menu; Public site's six Locked cards become one line; a fan's page loses only the Register button, and a coach's event
gains the free badge and banner. Every paid tool a free organizer reaches for shows the same padlock line. The twelve
asks, ruled as recommended, settle the details only drawings could: for example, on a live event the phone bar shows
Dashboard, Results, Schedule and Messages (Dashboard moved first by the owner during the walk); the host enters scores from the field on the same quick scoring screen
volunteers used; the free Teams export is one locked line; and a Founding Season organization's locks say Tournament
Plus is free for it through September 30, 2027.

**What the owner decided (2026-10-12).**
- **Free is the invite-only event.** The host adds the teams and each coach gets an invitation email to claim theirs.
  No public registration, fees, payment tracking, waitlist, check-in, Staff kit or volunteers on free; any event style;
  no size cap. Open events (teams register and pay) are Tournament Plus.
- **One lock style, only where it helps.** A free organizer meets a single padlock line, opening the Tournament Plus
  panel, at the moment they reach for a paid tool (generators, imports, exports, Summary, Reuse, branding, more
  events). Areas a free event never uses are simply not there. Tournament Chat is not there either.
- **A Premium coach's event follows free,** keeps PDF exports, and its public pages carry the same Powered-by badge and
  sign-up banner as any free event.
- **Founding Season:** the offer stays exactly as sold. On October 1, 2027 an organization that doesn't pay keeps
  everything it built; open registration closes and the Plus tools lock.
- **Events made before the build keep today's tools until they finish.**
- **Marketing:** free is sold as the invite-only plan, and registration, fees and payments lead Tournament Plus. Today's
  false claims get fixed now, before the build, and the Facts doc gets the free plan's list as the one source.
- **The unused email route is removed** (done the same day, as its own change).

**What changes.** The free Tournament plan is redefined around who actually uses it: someone hosting a small event,
often a coach from their Coaches Portal. Every feature an organizer can meet gets one answer: included, shown with a
lock, or not shown at all.

**What the review found.**
- **Today's free plan is a full registration-and-payments product.** A free organizer can take public registrations,
  accept and decline, run a waitlist, track who paid and set fees, and hand out the Staff kit to volunteers. Only the
  tools around those (custom questions, exports, reminders, dashboards) are paid. So the owner's starting view is a real
  change to what free means, and every marketing page that says "free registration" changes with it.
- **The proposed line is how teams get in, not size or format.** A free event is one the host fills by adding teams
  (invite-only). Tournament Plus is for events teams register and pay to enter. The review recommended keeping
  scorekeeping volunteers on free; the owner ruled that all volunteers move to Plus, so on a free event the host and up
  to two staff enter the scores.
- **A Premium coach's event runs on the free plan's rules** (plus PDFs). The recommendation keeps it that way, matching
  how the portal is already sold ("scrimmages or local events").
- **Founding Season doesn't give Tournament Plus automatically** (the owner picks it on the plan screen), and nothing
  turns it off on October 1, 2027 yet. The review proposes the rule for what a returning-to-free organization keeps:
  everything it built, read-only where it's a Plus tool.
- **Production has no free events at all**, so no existing customer loses anything today. Three real accounts exist,
  all in the Founding Season.
- **Two things to fix regardless:** an old, unused email route that any organizer could use to send unlimited mail
  from our address (a separate small fix, recommended now), and marketing that already promises Plus tools as free.

**Why it matters.** A clear line ("invite your teams free; open registration and payments on Plus") makes the free
plan simpler to use and Tournament Plus easier to explain and sell. Today a small host meets screens that don't apply
to them, seven different lock styles, and controls that look free and then refuse.

**Customer impact.**
- Free organizers: a smaller, cleaner product — add teams, build the schedule, post live scores, with one consistent
  lock where a paid tool would help.
- Tournament Plus organizers: no change; registration, payments and volunteers become what they pay for.
- Premium coaches: their hosted event follows free, keeping PDFs and one-click "Add my team", with the free badge on its
  public pages.
- Founding Season organizations: no change until October 1, 2027; then their records stay and the Plus tools lock.
- Today, nobody on production loses anything: no free event has ever run there.

**Priority.** Right after the tournament admin redesign closes, while those screens are fresh.

**Success looks like.**
- Every tournament feature has one written answer (included, locked or hidden), logged as a business decision and
  matched in the plan settings and the Facts doc.
- A free organizer can run a small invite-only event start to finish without meeting a screen that doesn't apply.
- Every lock looks the same and opens the plan; nothing works and then refuses.
- The pricing page, the organizer page, the home page and help say exactly what the product does.
