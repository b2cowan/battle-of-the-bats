# The free tournament tier — PM brief

Plan: `FREE_TOURNAMENT_TIER_REVIEW_PLAN.md` · Status: **queued 2026-10-10**. It starts when the tournament admin
redesign is done, and may run while Club Tier finishes.

**What changes.** The free Tournament plan is redefined around who actually uses it: someone running a small exhibition
event, often a coach hosting from their Coaches Portal. Every feature an organizer can meet is decided one of three
ways: it's included; it's shown with a lock (because seeing it sells the upgrade); or it isn't shown at all (because a
small event doesn't need it). The owner's starting view is that registration management, payment tracking and staff
kits belong to Tournament Plus.

**Why it matters.** Today the free plan shows a lot of the paid product. A small organizer meets screens that don't
apply to them, and the line between free and Plus is blurry. A clear line makes the free plan simpler to use and makes
Tournament Plus easier to explain and to sell.

**Customer impact.**
- Free organizers see a smaller, cleaner product.
- Tournament Plus organizers see no change.
- **Two groups need a decision before the build:**
  - coaches whose $29 Premium Coaches Portal includes a tournament (today it runs on the free plan's rules);
  - Founding Season organizations, who drop from comped Tournament Plus to free when the offer ends.

**Priority.** Right after the tournament admin redesign, while those screens are fresh. It may run alongside Club
Tier's last stages.

**Success looks like.**
- Every tournament feature has one written answer (included, locked or hidden), logged as a business decision and
  matched in the plan settings.
- A free organizer can run a small exhibition event start to finish without meeting a screen that doesn't apply.
- The pricing page says exactly what the product does.
