Owner QA · §432 · Public tournament pages Stage 5

Teams answers which teams are in and where each stands. A team's page answers when it plays next.

What this walk is for. Stage 5 rebuilt the Teams list and a team's own page, behind the preview switch. Teams is a division dropdown, a search box, then each division's teams with a quiet third line: record, place, and the next game (or “Live now”). A team's page leads with the game that matters (the live game, or the next one), then later games, then results, then the record and one door to the division's standings. Following and alerts exist only in the installed app: in a browser there is no star, no Follow, no Add to calendar, no My-team bar and no rail card. A division the organizer has held back appears nowhere. With the preview off, both pages are exactly today's site.

It passes when with the preview on, Teams groups, orders and searches correctly and its row taps open the right page; a team's page is right in every state (live, between games, results only, champion, runner-up, pool play, no games); live changes arrive on the open page; a held-back division leaves no trace; in a browser nothing about following shows; the admin preview shows plain rows that do nothing; you approve the desk layouts; and with the preview off, nothing changed.

  • Real installed-app behaviour (the star, the unfollow sheet, Follow and Add to calendar on a team's page) was never exercised by the build, so Part O is yours to run; it is optional.
  • The results line under the tabs shows only while there is something to show (today's results, a live game, the next games), and its × hides it for the day on that device.
  • The “Share” in the event band and the team page's “Share this team” are two different buttons by design; Part E checks you can tell them apart.
  • A failed background refresh stays silent by ruling (a sign for it is a later follow-up).
