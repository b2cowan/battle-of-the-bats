How the rows are put together

  • Where each column comes from. When, What it paid for, Tax, Amount and the sale’s state come from our record. Card, failure reasons, disputes and refund state come from Stripe when it answers. A sale whose record is not written yet (an invoice issued a minute ago) shows from Stripe and fills in on the next read.
  • The count and the band skip void sales. “Show older sales” pages through our records, newest first; the records begin May 1, 2026, so before that the list is Stripe’s charges, as today.
  • The duplicate check keeps working. “Charged twice” compares two paid sales for the same month, the same as today’s two paid charges.
  • A phone keeps the shipped card layout (one card per row, the When as its title): Tax becomes one more labelled line.
  • Empty: “No sales in these 12 months.” (today: “No charges in these 12 months.”). While it reads: the one shipped line, “Reading Stripe…”, until the answer arrives; our record and Stripe come back together, so nothing jumps.
  • A link to a charge (Find’s “open this charge”) opens the sale that charge paid, at the same window. The Story tab’s charge entries do not change.

Why this is needed (it is not in the plan)

  • Every check is about a sale that already happened. A sale from October does not change, so without a mark it stays on the list, on the strip, on the dot and in Today’s count for good. Within weeks the count is noise.
  • The mark is kept beside the record, never in it (the record stays exactly as Part 1 wrote it): who, when, why. The window then shows “Looked at Nov 8 by sam@… : {why}” where the flag line was.
  • It is a small new table and a migration, so it is a build cost Part 2 did not have. It needs /review and an /accounting check that a looked-at sale is never left out of the return.
  • Shown to Super admin and Billing only (the people who refund). A new problem on the same sale (say, a later refund with no credit note) puts it back on the list.
  • After the click: the small window closes back to the sale’s window, which says “Marked as looked at.” in the shipped green notice; the flag line becomes “Looked at Nov 8, 10:15 a.m. by sam@fieldlogichq.example: {why}”, and the list, the strip, the dot and Today’s count drop by one. A mistake is undone from that same line: a quiet “Put it back on the list” door, which asks for a why too.
  • Support sees the list and Today’s count but not the door: they pass it to Billing. The window says so in one line where the door would be (“Billing or Super admin marks a sale as looked at.”), never a dead button.
