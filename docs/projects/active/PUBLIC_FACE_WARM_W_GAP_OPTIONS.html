One question: should the F and the L touch?

Today the F's middle arm runs straight into the L, so the two initials are one shape. This page draws three answers on the same olive tile, at the sizes the mark is actually used. The corners, the stroke weight, the colours and the placements are the same in all three. Only the gap between the F and the L changes. Nothing is built.

What the drawings showed me

I suggested the middle option before drawing any of this. Having drawn it, I would adjust that. The small gap is a clear improvement at large sizes, but it softens at 24 pixels, which is the notification badge. The fully separated version is the only one that still reads as an F beside an L at 16 and 24 pixels. It is also the widest, and it gives up the single-block silhouette. If you want the one-block look, choose the small gap and accept a soft notch at small sizes. If the small sizes matter most, choose separated. I would not keep the touching version: at 24 pixels it is a block with a hole in it.

What changes, and nothing else

Joined, as it is

The arm of the F ends inside the stem of the L. This is the refined mark from the previous drawing, unchanged.

Small gap

The F's arm is shortened by a little and stops short of the L. The gap is 3 parts in 64, about one pixel on a 20 pixel icon. The overall block is the same size as Joined.

Separated

The arm keeps its full length and the L moves right by 6, so the two letters are clearly apart. The mark is about 15% wider. At 16 pixels the gap is one pixel, because no smaller gap can be drawn.
