Families and teams pay online — a short re-check on the dev site

Written 2026-10-28. One purpose: prove, on the real dev site with real webhooks and real email, that a house-league family and a tournament team can pay by card, get the right emails, and be refunded. Everything here already passed on your own computer in October; the email steps were held because that computer cannot send mail.

What this walk is for

Online payments (Club Tier Stage 12) were walked in full on your own computer (house league: walks §380 and §389; the refund and part-refund screens: §389; tournament teams: §376; bank debit §390; paid twice and disputes §395 and §396). Every step that needed an email was ticked “Held”, and nothing has yet been run against the dev site’s real Stripe webhooks. This walk is the short regression plus those held emails, run once on https://dev.fieldlogichq.ca.

You will use two children in house league and one visiting team in the tournament. Between them they send about ten emails: two sign-up emails, three receipts, two pay links, and the refund emails. You check each one arrives, says what the screen says, and carries the same receipt number as the page and the desk.

It passes when

A payment is never shown as “Paid” before the site has recorded it. A declined card records nothing. Each receipt email arrives in the club’s name with the same receipt number as the pay page and the desk row. Each refund email says where the money went. The Stripe dashboard and the “Dev: club payments” deliveries list agree with what the desk shows, and every delivery is a 200.

Before you start

Setup, not checked. Keep this page private: it holds dev passwords.

Where
Everything is on https://dev.fieldlogichq.ca (the dev site), not on your own computer. The Stripe dashboard is in test mode (“FieldLogicHQ sandbox”). No real money moves anywhere.
Your inbox
A real inbox that accepts plus-addresses: yourname+fam1@gmail.com arrives in yourname@gmail.com. This walk uses three: +fam1 (the first child), +fam2 (the second child) and +team1 (the visiting team). The .local addresses on the sign-in card cannot receive mail, so never use them as the family’s or the team’s email.
If an email does not show in about 2 minutes
Open Resend › Emails. Every message the site sends is listed there with its subject, who it went to, and whether it was delivered or bounced. Look in your spam folder as well. Part G uses that list as the final check.
Test cards
Approved: 4242 4242 4242 4242. Declined: 4000 0000 0000 0002. Any future expiry date, any three digits, any postal code. Stripe’s page asks for an email and a name on the card; any will do.
Webhooks
In the Stripe dashboard (test mode) › Workbench › Webhooks you will look at the destination named Dev: club payments and its Event deliveries tab in part G. You do not run anything on your own computer.
Phone-sized window
The family steps are meant to be seen on a phone. Use your phone, or make the browser window narrow (a private window about 400 pixels wide). Use a private window for each family or team so they are not signed in as the owner.
