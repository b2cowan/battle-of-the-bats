Six hand-offs, each one person's work reaching another. Every one of them was walked on 2026-11-06 and re-opened today on a fresh club copy. All four Highs still reproduce. Three things in the code change what the ruled answers have to do, and they're in the red boxes below.

1. There is no "give house league time" button. Time is given when a schedule is finalized.

House league gets its time as one of the holders in a club schedule. It counts as "given" once that schedule is final. Today, finalizing tells the rep coaches and nobody in house league. "Send the changes" tells the league admin only when games or practices moved; new time on its own tells nobody. So the league admin's notice (Ask 14) fires at those two moments: Finalize and send, and a change that adds or takes back house league time.

The copy shows it: the sent schedule "Saturdays at Kinsmen, December 2026" gave house league 2 hours a week, and the schedule page says "2 house-league games on Dec 5 are for the league admins to move". Jules Moreau (league admin) has "No notifications yet".

2. Ask 15 closes one of four ways to bill a team twice. This club has already done one of the other three.

Schedules and Accounting each check only whether the budget line has room left. Neither sees what the other has billed, and a draft reserves nothing. On this copy, Accounting has already billed "Fall ball diamond fees 2026" ($1,800.00 to 15U AAA and 13U AAA) on Diamond permits. A schedule made from the Fall diamonds 2026 permit sees "$450.00 left", is refused when it's sent, and then the builder picks "No budget line". That bills those two teams twice and nothing says so. Ask 15 A warns in New allocation, but not in the Bill window. Ask 4 on tab 8 is whether 13d closes the Bill window side too.

3. The league admin's "Club time · not divided" tile would say a sentence that's already on that page.

13b left a "Club time" tile for 13d on the league admin's This morning. Since then, fix-now put "The club has given this season time. None of it is divided into games or practices yet. House league's time →" on the same Overview, 16 px above This morning. A tile would repeat it. Ask 6 recommends not building it.
