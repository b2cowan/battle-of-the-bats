Owner QA walk · Admin header bar · Phase 2 · §492

Every club place names itself in the pinned bar, its tabs pinned inside it

Written 2026-11-07. One purpose: the bar on Rep teams, a team, a season, Accounting, Families and Organization, on a computer and a phone, at rest and scrolled, as the owner and as people who see less.

What this walk is for

The six places in the club admin used to draw their title and tab row on the page, under a bar that only said the club’s name, and the tabs scrolled away. Now the bar names the place you are in (a team’s name, the season’s name, “Accounting”…), carries its small line of facts, and holds the place’s tabs. The page under it starts with its own content. Scrolling never moves the bar or the tabs, so you can switch tabs from the bottom of a long ledger.

It passes when

  • Each place shows one title, in the bar. No place repeats its title or its tab row on the page.
  • The tabs stay pinned when you scroll, on a computer and on a phone, and the open tab is always the one underlined.
  • A team and a season have a way back (“← Teams”, “← Seasons”; the arrow alone on a phone).
  • On a phone, Accounting keeps its sideways tab row, and the open tab is in view without swiping.
  • Someone who sees less sees only their own tabs, never an empty row, and a place they cannot open keeps the club’s name in the bar.
  • Nothing on the page slides under the taller bar.

Not in this walk

  • Calendar, Schedules and record pages (a team’s History, a child’s page, Rename team URLs, Plan & billing). They keep their own heading on the page for now; Phase 3 moves them. Part F only checks they are not broken.
  • The pages’ own content. Only the bar and where the page starts under it.
  • The coaches portal. Untouched.
  • The season switcher beside a season’s name. It only shows when a club has two seasons; the test club has one. It was built and measured in session A.

Known before the walk

  • Accounting’s Payouts tab is there from the first moment. The test club has connected payouts, so its row has nine tabs, Payouts last.
  • On a phone Families’ Duplicates tab has a record card whose email line runs a little past the card’s edge. That was there before this change; it is reported to the Families work, not to the bar.
  • On a phone, a team’s small line drops the team’s group (“Senior”) so it fits, as drawn. A computer shows it.
  • The bar never says which fiscal year Accounting is on. The Year pill in each tab’s toolbar keeps that (your ruling A6).
