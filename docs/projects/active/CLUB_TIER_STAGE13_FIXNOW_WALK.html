The eight fix-now defects are gone

Written 2026-11-06, after the fix-now build (built, not yet committed). One purpose: each of the eight defects the Stage 13 walk found (and the sidebar review added) no longer happens. One part per defect, on a copy of the walk’s club.

What this walk is for

The Stage 13 walk of Harbourtown Minor Ball found screens that said something false, or buttons that did something worse than they promised. None of the eight changes a screen’s shape. Each one makes an existing screen tell the truth: the setup checklist lets a Club owner go; removing a member who is also a parent keeps their family sign-in; “Evenly” splits the dome evenly and says what 1 a day leaves open; house league’s “no time yet” line is true; the tab title drops the tournament on club pages; the Overview’s Organization row loses its stray spaces; a rep team’s page is current when a season window closes; and only one sidebar row lights inside a season.

It passes when

  • 1 After “Go to Overview”, the owner’s next sign-in lands on the Overview, and the Overview still offers “Continue setup”.
  • 2 Removing a member who is a connected parent says “Their account is kept … They can still sign in to see their family.”, and after Remove that person can still sign in and see their family.
  • 3 On the dome at Evenly and 1 a day, the form says “At 1 a day, 7 teams can use 175 of the 450 slots.”, and every draft gives every team 25 (175 in all). At 3 a day the plain hint is back and all 450 go out.
  • 4 The season’s Overview reads correctly in all three states: no time, time given but not divided (with a door to House league’s time), and divided (no line). A league admin is never sent to Schedules.
  • 5 House league and Organization tabs carry no tournament name; a tournament page does.
  • 6 The Overview reads “Organization: members, plan & billing (8 of 15 teams), settings”.
  • 7 When Start next season and Close the season finish, the page already shows the new state when the banner appears.
  • 8 Inside a house league season only the season’s own row is lit, not “Seasons” as well.

Not in this walk

  • The drafts’ “7 teams short” warning saying why, and Permits as a door. Both are 13c.
  • Telling the league admin when time arrives. That is 13d.
  • The sidebar’s shape, chevrons and the lone “Seasons” / “Site editor” rows. That is Ask 25 and 13i, after a drawing session.
  • The Slots column per team in New schedule still shows each team’s share (64 or 65) at 1 a day. The new hint gives the real figure; the column is left as it is (noted for 13c).
  • The house league time window’s own sentence for a league admin (“An owner or an admin gives it, in Schedules.”) is unchanged. Only the season Overview’s line changed.
