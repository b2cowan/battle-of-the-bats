What I need from you

A is the gate: nothing is built until you say yes. B and C are the two judgment calls inside the drawing; each has a recommendation.

A · Build to these drawings?

The start card (tab 1), the form right after Start here (tab 2) and the start’s names (tab 3), desk and phone.

Recommend: yes.

B · After a start, the end date is empty, not guessed

The form could fill the last day of the statement’s first month (Oct 31 for a start on Oct 1). Statements don’t all run by calendar month (many run the 15th to the 14th), and a wrong guess that reads as filled-in gets saved. Empty, with the cursor in it and the line saying what to type, costs one date pick.

Recommend: empty (as drawn).

C · The banking-app hint waits while the after-start line shows

“Use the posted balance your banking app shows…” is the right advice for a check of today, and the wrong one for a paper statement’s ending balance. While the after-start line shows, the hint is hidden; from the first save on, the form is the ordinary one and the hint returns.

Recommend: hide it (as drawn).
