The refusal that waits to be asked

Deleting a sponsorship is a once-in-a-record act. Explaining why you can’t is currently permanent furniture — two lines above the footer, on every visit, answering a question most coaches never ask. Three ways to give the answer only when it is wanted. Specimens are true size (640px, the width this modal renders at) — press the buttons.

Where I land

A, and I’d take B if you like the hand-off. Both reclaim the space; the difference is whether the refusal only explains or also delivers. C reclaims the same space and pays for it with a control that says nothing on touch, which is a worse product than what ships today.

The drive’s Settings sheet carries the identical strip and should move with it — whichever you pick, the two doors keep one grammar.
