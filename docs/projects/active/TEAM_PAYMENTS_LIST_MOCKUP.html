Four calls this drawing asks you to make

Each has a recommended answer. Nothing runs on live data until you say go.

1 · Payments already on teams Recommended: each team that already has a paid figure gets one row, "Earlier payment", with no date. Lines already in a tournament's book stay as they are and are not posted twice. Alternative: ask each organizer to date them. Nobody can be asked to do that for every team before the new window works.
2 · Which dates are allowed Recommended: any date up to today. A date in a closed fiscal year is refused with a sentence saying so, the same as every other ledger write. Alternative: allow it and post to the closed year. That breaks the lock the club's treasurer relies on.
3 · The spreadsheet import Recommended: a Deposit Paid or Total Paid figure higher than what is recorded adds one "Imported payment" for the difference, dated the day of the import. A lower figure is skipped and the preview says to remove the payment on the team. Alternative: let an import lower what is recorded. One wrong column then voids real ledger lines.
4 · Changing a recorded payment asks first Recommended: Record and Save change are buttons you press. The rest of the window still saves as you type. A change to a payment on an Accounting plan voids a ledger line and posts a new one, so saving every keystroke would bring back today's problem. Alternative: autosave a payment's edits like name and email. Each pause then writes a void and a new line.

Design check

What the review changed in these drawings, and what it left alone.

  • Changed: Received and Still due are plain sentence-case headings, not small capitals. Small capitals are for read-only labels over a value, not for a section title in a window.
  • Changed: the In ledger pill sits beside the payment's name at every width. It was hidden on a phone, and a row's chip keeps one place at every width.
  • Changed: Remove this payment is its own line at the end of the change form, a trash symbol and red words, and it asks first and says what happens to the ledger line. It was squeezed onto the same row as Save and Cancel.
  • Changed: Amount and Paid on carry a plain asterisk, How it arrived is bare. Fields are 36 px at a desk and 44 px on a phone. Dates are one line, tabular figures.
  • Left alone: the Record form opens in place under the row you pressed. A second window over this window is not allowed, and the form keeps Cancel beside Record.
  • Left alone: "Owes" stays amber, as the line reads today. Left alone: payment dates print as "Oct 9" with no year, like ledger rows.
  • Left alone: Mark paid stays the wide one-tap button. It is the quick path; Record is for a specific amount or a date that is not today.

What else changes, and what does not

  • Mark paid still takes one tap. It records one payment for what is owed, dated today, and it still sends the same email only when it completes the fee. A part payment sends nothing, as today.
  • The dashboard's "collected" number gets more honest. Today a team with only a deposit counts as $0 collected on the dashboard and $150 in the ledger. After this, both read the same payments.
  • The coach sees the real date. "Paid · Oct 9" comes from the last payment's date. Today Mark paid never sets it.
  • Check-in, bulk Paid in full, Record one payment for several teams, imports and exports all write through the same list, so a payment looks the same wherever it was made.
  • Plans that can only read payments see the list and no Record buttons, as they see the two figures today.
