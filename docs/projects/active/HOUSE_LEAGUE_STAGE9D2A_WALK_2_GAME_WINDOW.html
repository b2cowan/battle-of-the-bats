A game’s window: read first, a state is a door

Written 2026-10-28, walk 2 of 6 for Stage 9d-2a (the season’s schedule, rebuilt). One purpose; sign it off on its own.

What this walk is for

Every game opens one window that reads first. The pencil turns the whole record into its form and ✓ saves. The old Status dropdown is gone: postponing, cancelling, putting a game back on and giving it a new date are doors at the end of the window, each asking first and saying families aren’t emailed.

It passes when

  • A reader gets the facts only; whoever runs the season gets the pencil and the doors.
  • A score is the pencil’s, both or neither.
  • Postpone moves a game to Needs a new date with “Was {its date}”; Give it a new date puts it back as an ordinary game (no “Moved”).
  • Cancel strikes it in place; Put it back on undoes it.
  • Delete asks, and says what it removes.

Not in this walk

  • The permit line and See the permit in this window (walk §413).
  • Emails to families (9d-2b). Moving a game only within the division’s time (an open owner ask, below).
