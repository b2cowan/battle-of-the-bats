## What changes for the coach

A coach can split players into pairs or groups anywhere players are listed in a practice plan, not only
inside a rotating circuit. On any block, "Split into groups…" sits next to "Choose players…"; a station
that runs on its own gets the same door. The practice keeps one list of tonight's groups at the top of
the plan, and a later block reuses a set ("same pairs as the warm-up") instead of drawing again. A set
can also be brought forward from an earlier practice this season, so pitcher-and-catcher pairs are made
once, not every week. The run screen, the station coach's phone, the printed sheet and the staff email
all name the pairs.

## Why it matters

Partner work is most of a youth warm-up, and battery work is part of every baseball and softball
practice. Today groups exist only inside a circuit with two or more rotating stations, so "warm-up in
pairs" means faking stations or writing names in a note, and every circuit draws its own groups.

## Customer impact

- **Coaches who write plans** (Coach Premium): fewer taps, and pairs that carry across blocks and weeks.
- **Staff and helpers on the field:** see who is paired with whom on their phone and on paper.
- **No change to who sees what:** names appear wherever the plan already shows them, under the same
  permissions.

## Priority

## Success criteria

- A no-station block goes into pairs in three taps or fewer from its Players line.
- A second block reuses tonight's pairs in two taps; moving one player updates both.
- Batteries come forward from last week without being re-made.
- No coach is unsure which groups a block uses: every block names its set, and a shared set says where
  else it is used.
