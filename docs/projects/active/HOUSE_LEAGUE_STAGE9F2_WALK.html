Start next season: a new season from last year’s, in one window

Written 2026-11-04 for Stage 9f-2 (built, reviewed, help updated). One purpose: a league runner starts a new house-league season from an earlier one, sees exactly what comes across and what never does, and the new season tells them which lines to check.

What this walk is for

Create season now opens with Start from. Pick a season, and the name, dates, settings, age groups, team names and practice settings arrive for you to check. Families, payments, ratings, games, practices, emails and the club’s time never come across, the old season is left exactly as it was, and nobody is emailed. A rare door at the end of a season’s page, Running it again? Start next season from this one…, opens the same window on that season.

Known before the walk

  • The fixture is two throwaway clubs with their own sign-ins (below). It touches no other club, walk or sign-in, and a re-run rebuilds only them, including every season this walk makes. Dev only; it refuses production.
  • Walk Summer 2026 is the finished season to copy. 4 age groups, 22 teams, 31 coaches (each invited once, none accepted), 3 players who still owe $175 each. The dates are tied to the calendar: Walk Fall 2026 ends Dec 12, 2026; after that it reads Ended instead of Playing now. Re-run the fixture if you walk later.
  • The season and team links change every time the fixture runs. Use the ones in this page’s “Where” card only if you have not re-run it; otherwise use the links the fixture prints.
  • Migration 432 is on the dev database only. Production needs it before 9f-2 ships.
  • The dev server was restarted this morning, after the build. If a screen looks stale, restart it.
  • The numbers in this walk were checked against the real routes before it was written: the copy of Walk Summer 2026 gives 4 age groups, 22 teams, no coaches (31 when ticked, none invited), dates 364 days later, birth years one year later, and a refusal from the free plan, a registrar and another club’s season.

Not in this walk

  • Publishing teams (that is when copied coaches are invited) and opening registration for real families. This walk stops at “it asks first”.
  • A source with more than 1,000 teams or coaches, and a request typed by hand. The tests and a probe cover them.
  • Any upgrade or plan-gate wording. That ruling (9f·16) is still open, so none was built.
  • The help articles. Read them if you like (Help › House league › Creating and configuring a season); they are not walk steps.
