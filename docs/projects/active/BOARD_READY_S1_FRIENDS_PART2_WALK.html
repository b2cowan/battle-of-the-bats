Already proved by tests and my own pass (don't re-do these)

  • The deletion works. On dev, marking a season Complete (rehearsed and rolled back) took its friend requests from 1 to 0 and its friend codes from 4 to 0, in the same step that erases ratings. The nightly cleanup also sweeps any season 60 days past its last day. A season with no end date counts a year from its start. A family saving at the moment the season is completed stores nothing.
  • Who reads and who decides. Only the people who can open Registrations get the requests at all. Only those who work registrations can decide, and never on a finished season. Every decision is checked against this club and this season. A match must be a live sign-up in the asking child's own division.
  • The matching rules (capitals, accents, hyphens and apostrophes ignored; “Jon” similar to “Jonathan”; a first name alone never makes a single best match or a pair; a loose first name in another division is Not signed up), and every row of this fixture landing where the drawing puts it, are unit-tested.
  • Files. Friend names never reach “Excel with contact details”. The CSVs are written so a cell can't run as a formula. Excel files are left as typed (a phone “+1 519…” stays as it is).
  • Typecheck, lint and the guard tests are clean. A four-lens code review ran, and its findings are fixed.
