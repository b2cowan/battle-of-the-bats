What Stage 7 makes true

A club that hosts a tournament gets its own teams into it in one step, and its entry fees land in the club's books by themselves, where the Budget vs. Actual can answer "did the tournament pay for itself?". A club can email every family it has in one send. And a coach's Tournaments pages work on a phone and are named after the tournament.

For the club admin

"Add our teams" on a tournament's Teams page enters any of the club's teams at once: accepted, linked to the team, and on its coach's schedule once games are set. Marking a visiting team paid writes the fee into the tournament's book, filed under a word the club picked once. Budget vs. Actual gains By activity. Families gains Email families, with one log of every email the club sent a family.

For the head coach

Tournaments is a short list with the dates in front. A tournament's page is titled by the tournament, has a help door, says the team's status once, leads to the team's games on its Schedule, and saves the event's head coach as it is typed. Every change lands in the free portal too: it shares the page.

Where I widen the question

1 · The plan sends a tournament's fees to the wrong book (Ask 1 decides the rest)

The plan recommends "an automatic income entry on the org ledger". But a club's tournament already has its own book. The Accounting door on a tournament opens it, it counts in the club's Cash on hand, and every line in it already needs a budget word (3b's rule). Only the club's own book counts in the Statement today; the code says a tournament's book "keeps its own money until Stage 7 decides how it joins the budget". So the right question is not "which ledger gets the fee?". It is "how does the tournament's book join the club's year?". My answer: fees land in the tournament's book; the tournament becomes one category on the club's budget (named for it, holding its entry fees and its costs), exactly as a coach files a team's tournament; the book's lines join the Statement through the words they already carry; and By activity is the coach's report shape, unchanged. That is what makes "did it pay for itself?" answerable at all. A fee in the club's general book would mix one event's money into everything else.

2 · A club's own team is not a customer

Today the club's own 15U AAA sits in its own Invitational's table reading Paid and coach "—", while the coach's page for the same entry reads "No fee set by the organizer". If fees post automatically, a club charging its own team would record income that is only money moving inside the club, and the team's own book is the coach's (D3). So an own team enters with no fee, and the table says "Our team". If a club wants its teams to pay toward hosting, that is an allocation, which Stage 3 already built.

3 · "Message every family" is the email the chat pause already kept, not the Messages product

The chat pause (ruled 2026-10-18) says messaging returns later as "Messages", announcements with replies, for families, after counsel. It also says the email tools stay as they are, and folding them into one screen is the first step of that later project. The scheduler's Ask 9 (ruled 2026-10-08) already said Stage 7's family send uses the one club send window and its log shape. So Stage 7 draws an email: no replies, no push, no new name. It lives on Families, because the de-duplicated list of every household already lives there. I do not call it "Messages": that word belongs to E2. The tournament's phone bar already calls Communications "Messages"; that collision is noted, not fixed here.

4 · Where the plan is right

The coach's Tournaments findings all hold, and the deep dive's shape still fits. The real risk there is not design: an assistant coach can change the event's head coach today, because the server never checks the role. The drawing fixes the screen and the build must fix the server. The fee write is the other risk: the house league's version of it (the pattern the plan says to copy) still has six defects, so Ask 4 asks whether Stage 7 builds the one write both use.

The rules the drawing shows

  • One write. Every way of marking a fee paid writes one line per payment (a deposit, then the balance). Undoing a mark voids its line with who and when; it never deletes, and a voided line can never come back to life.
  • A refund is not an undo. A team that paid and withdrew is refunded with a money-out line someone types (Stage 12 handles refunds paid online).
  • The club's own team writes nothing (Ask 2).

  • The audience comes from Families, the club's one de-duplicated list of households: a guardian with two children on two teams gets it once. "Every family" counts only families with a child on a live team or in an open season, never a tryout-only applicant.

  • Who can send: anyone with Families access (owners always; others by the per-person grant). A cap: 3 club-wide sends a day (Ask 7).
