More than once

A drive lets the whole team hand money in as many times as it likes — two hoodie tables on two weekends are two events. A player gets exactly one amount, which you overwrite. Nobody chose that: the one-per-player rule predates whole-team entries, and the team entry landed in a gap in it. This proposes making the player side match, without making the board noisier for the drive where nobody hands in twice.

The asymmetry, in one line. On the same tab, a sponsor can send five cheques and each is its own dated arrival; the whole team can hand in five times and each is its own dated entry; a player hands in five times and the drive remembers one number and one date. Only the third is a running total, and only the third makes the coach do the arithmetic.

1 · The board keeps its leaderboard, and grows a fold

The drive's board answers who raised what. That has to stay true at a glance, so a player is still one row — their total. The individual hand-ins live under it, reached the same way every other money row on the portal opens.

One hand-in, no fold
Casey and Devon are byte-for-byte what they are today — their date inline, Edit and Remove on the row, no chevron. A drive where nobody hands in twice looks exactly like the drive above. The fold is the exception speaking.
Received
On a folded row it is the most recent hand-in — the column answers "when did money last come in from them", which is the question a coach chasing a drive is asking.
Edit and Remove
Move onto the hand-in they act on. A summary row has nothing to edit; a single-hand-in row keeps them, because there is no ambiguity about which one it means.
Credit
Each hand-in carries the share stamped at the time, and the row sums them. If you change the drive's share mid-season, the board can now say so honestly instead of re-multiplying one merged number.
The fraction
3 of 12 players logged is unchanged — it counts distinct players, not rows. The team's count still counts entries, because that is what "2 team entries" means.

The one new clause
"Avery Test's total for this drive becomes $576.00" — added only when the player already has a hand-in. It is the whole defence against the risk below, and it appears exactly when that risk exists.
What is deleted
The "N players already have an amount logged" sentence, the "every player already has an amount" dead end, and the rule underneath both.
The one thing this gives up, stated plainly. Today, recording $60 for Avery twice by mistake is refused — the second save is turned away. After this change it succeeds and Avery quietly has $120. The proposed answer is not to refuse it but to say what will happen before the save, in the sentence the form already writes. That is the same trade this product made when a bill lowered stopped being a collection: state the consequence, don't block the door. If you'd rather keep a guard, the alternative is a soft confirm on a same-day, same-amount repeat — one extra tap on a rare shape, rather than a rule that shapes the whole board.

3 · The rules, in one place

QuestionAnswer
What is a row on the board?A participant — a player, or the whole team — carrying their total for this drive. It was a single amount; it becomes a sum.
What is under the fold?Each hand-in, oldest first, with its own date, amount and credit.
When is there a fold?Only when a participant has two or more hand-ins. One hand-in renders exactly as it does today.
What does Received show?The most recent hand-in's date.
Who gets Edit and Remove?The hand-in. On a one-hand-in row that is the row itself, unchanged.
Removing the last hand-in?The participant leaves the board — same as removing an entry today.
What happens to the credit?Each hand-in keeps the share it was stamped with; the row sums them. A share changed mid-drive no longer rewrites history.
Does the ledger change?Each hand-in is already its own ledger line. Two hand-ins are two lines, dated correctly — which is more honest than one line whose date moves.
Does anything move money?No. Every existing entry becomes a one-hand-in participant, and no figure on any screen changes.
Phone?The board is already a card list on a phone. The participant card carries the total and opens; hand-ins are cards under it.
Three things I need from you before this is built.

1 · The word. This page says hand-in to keep the prose readable, but the product's own word is entry ("plus 1 team entry"). Introducing a second word for one object is exactly what the one-spelling rule exists to stop, so my recommendation is that the screens keep saying entry and "hand-in" never ships. Say if you'd rather have the friendlier word everywhere — it is a bigger change than it looks, because it touches the facts line, both confirms, help and the demo narration.

2 · The accidental double. Consequence sentence only (recommended), or consequence sentence plus a soft confirm on a same-day same-amount repeat?

3 · Does the whole team get the same fold? Grouping its entries under one row is what makes the board one grammar — but it also means the team's two hoodie tables stop being visible without a click, and F3b of the §157 walk deliberately celebrated seeing them both. My recommendation is yes, fold it, because a board where one participant type stacks and another doesn't is the asymmetry this whole proposal exists to remove.
