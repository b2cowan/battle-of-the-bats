House league, walked end to end

House league is the club's biggest program and its biggest revenue line, and it is the least finished area of the product. This page walks it the way a volunteer convenor meets it, from setting up a season to closing it, at a desk and on a phone. It then proposes a ladder of stages and draws the first one. Nothing has been built.

How to read this

  • Walk holds 12 stations. Each has the captures (open the full size), what I measured, my read, and the shape questions. Tick Keep, Reshape or Not yet, and leave a note.
  • A red flag opens the finding: what the code does today, where you're looking, and what I'd change. Click any tag ( ) to read why it's there.
  • Standing back holds the rules that cut across the stations, including the payments contract (the next stage after this one is payments, so everything here has to leave room for it).
  • Ladder is my proposed order of stages, sized, to be tested against the walk.
  • 9a drawn is the first stage at true size: before = today's capture, after = drawn. Use the Warm/Dark switch on the right of the tab bar. Open this page on a phone to judge the phone drawings at their real size.
  • Asks & paste-back gathers every question in one list and builds the summary you paste back to me.

Captured on the dev fixture club "Dev League Org" (League plan) as its owner. Seasons: 2025 finished, 2026 running, 2027 taking sign-ups. I extended it for this walk with a third division with its own capacity and age band, a venue with two diamonds and a permit, one family who paid $150 and was then declined, a cancelled game, and a guardian with two children. The 2026 games are in June, so "this week" has no games in it. Two fixture artifacts, not product bugs: the 6:00 a.m. game times, and 9 of the 10 paid ticks in 2026, which predate today's fee writer and so have no line in the season book.

The short version

  • The frame is the first problem. A club admin can open every house-league screen and save nothing . The season's own home and its money page aren't in the side menu . On a phone there's no way into a season's pages at all except through card links.
  • The money says things that aren't true. The season book shows $0.00 outstanding beside its own $2,100 expected and $150 collected . A family who paid $150 and was declined vanishes from the books . Editing the fee rewrites what every family owes .
  • The public side contradicts itself. The 2027 page says "Registration open" and, on the same screen, "Registration closes August 1, 2026". It is October 24, and the form still takes sign-ups . The club home hides the season that is actually being played .
  • Re-verified against the code: email escaping, rate limits, reply-to addresses, export dates, the cross-tenant IDs, the "cancel game" misclick and the fee-to-ledger seam are already fixed; I drew no fix for them. Two claims in the brief were wrong: there are no browser confirm() pop-ups (the house-league windows are in-page; the real gap is one-click status changes), and the trust plan's "six" email templates are seven.

Q1When does a family pay: at sign-up, or once accepted?

Interacts with auto-approve under capacity (an existing season switch: with it on, a sign-up is accepted at once), the waitlist and declines. Pay-at-sign-up needs the spot taken atomically (F10) and makes a decline a refund. Pay-on-acceptance keeps declines free but needs a "hold" rule (Q3). Q3 and Q4 only arise if this is "when accepted".

Q3If Q1 is "when accepted": does an accepted, unpaid registration hold its spot, and for how long?

An unpaid child who holds a spot keeps a waitlisted child out, so the convenor needs a way to let the spot go.

Q6Ending a registration after money was received

Today the payment is silently erased. Either way, a refund is recorded as money out, never as the payment disappearing. That is honest books now, and the hook 12b needs for "refund the card first". Who records it follows Q17: whoever can mark a payment received can record a refund; the treasurer reads it on Money.
