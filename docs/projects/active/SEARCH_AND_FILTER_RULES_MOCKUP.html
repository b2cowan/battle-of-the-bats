Left picks the list; right works inside it

The rules the recent tournament screens follow, written down so the coaches portal and the club admin can follow them too. The coaches portal's own toolbar was built on the same split (a count or a view on the left, actions pinned right). Only Practice plans and the table standard's written line put search on the left.

Which list: the view choice, or the countspaceSearchFiltersTools ⋯
RULE 1 · LEFT

Which list you are looking at

The view choice: the day, the division, Needs you · All games, the arrival buckets. Changing it swaps the whole list. A screen with no view choice shows its count there instead ("24 drills").

RULE 2 · RIGHT

Find, narrow, act, in that order

Search, then filters (the tournament's one Filter button, or the portal's named pills such as Tags), then Tools ⋯. The space between the two groups is deliberate, so neither side crowds.

RULE 3 · PAGE ACTIONS

Where each portal keeps them, at the far right

The tournament screens keep Export, Add game, Gate view and Scorekeeper in the page header. The coaches portal keeps Export and Create at the end of its toolbar. Neither moves.

RULE 4 · SHAPE

Rounded picks which list; square-cornered narrows or acts inside it

What changes WHICH list you see is rounded: view pills, the view picker (Schedule's Day), and the search field you type into. What narrows or acts inside the list is square-cornered: Filter, Tags, Tools, Export and every button. Today's screens mostly follow it already; Check-in's arrival buttons and Teams' division picker are the exceptions.

RULE 5 · ONE SEARCH

Rounded, with a magnifier, 16rem

The portal's rounded field (your 17 September "one look across the portal"), now on the tournament screens too, with the magnifier the tournament field had. One desk width everywhere; the height follows the room (44px in the practice and skills rooms, the admin's height elsewhere).

RULE 6 · ONE VIEW PILL

Rounded, its count in the lighter ink

Every row of view choices with counts uses Results' pill: rounded, the word in its weight, the number lighter beside it, olive when chosen.

RULE 7 · THE REGISTER EXCEPTION

Many filters and a running figure get a row of their own

A screen whose list is narrowed by three or more kinds of filter shown as named pills, and which carries a running figure those filters change (a balance, cash on hand), gives the filters a row of their own under the toolbar, with the figure at its right end. The top row keeps the view and the verbs. On a phone the row is one Filter button with the figure beside it.

Today that is the club Ledger and the coach's Ledger. A screen with many filters and no figure (the Calendar, a schedule's detail page) follows Rule 2.

Round 3 is withdrawn. Search after the view crowded the left and left the right empty. This round keeps today's tournament layout as it was committed, and brings the other portals to it.

Its clash pill is a status that doubles as "Clashes only", not a filter, and the time-zone note belongs on the date row, as that ruling says. Under Rule 7 it is not a register (no running figure), so the recommendation is to move its filters right together with the coach schedule's (Ask 11), and round its view switch (Rule 4). On a phone it opens its views from a glyph beside the title; nothing there changes. The bookings are examples.

ASK 4

Recommend yes. Check-in's arrival buttons adopt it on a desk, on the organizer's board and the gate's (one board, as ruled 7 August and 29 September). It amends the 29 September "four equal buttons" to pills sized to their words.

ASK 5

Recommend yes. Four rounded pills with counts do not fit 390 on one line. The bar puts the count over the word in four equal parts, and the gate's pinned bar is the same shape.

Recommend yes. "24 drills", "2 of 24 drills" while narrowed. It is Rule 1 for a screen with no view choice, and it matches Skills & Goals.

ASK 7

Recommend yes. It is a view choice (Rule 4); Schedule's Day pill is the shape to match. It also keeps its waiting dot and amber count.

ASK 8

Families: search goes right, away from the Show pill

Recommend yes. It amends this morning's Families ruling, which put search beside the Show pill at a desk: the Show pill holds the left and search moves right. Its build is under way in another session (not yet committed), so ruling now lets it land in the right place.

ASK 9 · RULED 17 OCTOBER

The ledgers are the exception: B, a row of their own (Rule 7)

Recommend B. The club's money screens were ruled as two decks on purpose: the top deck is what is read plus the verbs, and the filters take a strip of their own with the figure at its right edge, "because the figure answers the same question the filters shape" (30 September). A would crowd the line; C reverses that ruling. You ruled it as an exception, written as Rule 7 so a future register screen (a payments register, say) qualifies by the same test.

ASK 11 · THE CALENDAR

The Calendar and the coach schedule move their filters right, together

Recommend yes. They fail Rule 7's test (three filters, no running figure), so Rule 2 applies: Venue, Program and Team go right, before Export, on both screens at once, since the Calendar wears the coach schedule's toolbar (9 October). The clash pill narrows to "Clashes only", so it joins them. This changes my earlier "leave the Calendar alone": with Rule 7 written down, leaving it would make a second, unwritten exception.

ASK 10 · THE OLIVE PICKER

The olive picker (Book, Year, View) becomes rounded

Recommend yes. It keeps its job and its olive face from the 7 October "two faces" ruling (it chooses what is read); only its corners change, so that every view choice is rounded (Rule 4). It reaches about ten club screens and the coaches portal's matching pickers.
