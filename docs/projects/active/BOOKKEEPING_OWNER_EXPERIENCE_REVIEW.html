Stage 3 · dependent on Revenue

Proprietor ledger

Add income and cash movement only after their real sources and classifications are agreed.

—
  • BKX-3.1Accept the versioned Stripe source contract and real example movements.

    Revenue owns source IDs, seller/tax attribution, fees, refunds and payouts.

  • BKX-3.2Define owner contributions, draws and reimbursements as separate movements.

    A personal expense is counted once; paying the owner back is not another expense.

  • BKX-3.3Build the source-linked ledger and safe import.

    Each line opens its source; repeated or interrupted imports keep one movement identity.

  • BKX-3.4Add reconciliation and period reporting for accepted income and cash movements.

    A payout is a transfer; organizer money stays outside proprietor revenue.

  • BKX-3.5Walk normal, refund, fee, payout, duplicate and recovery cases.

    Record the owner and accountant evidence before calling the ledger ready.

Stage exit: The owner can open every line's source and reconcile income, expenses and transfers without double-counting a payment or payout.

Stage 0 decisions

Resolve these against the owner journey before changing stored money meanings.

D1 · OWNER DIRECTION

Two dates, one short default

Enter an incurred date. Paid date defaults to it; “Payment date is different” reveals a second date. Proposed report rule: expense periods use incurred date; future payment/cash views use paid date. Existing “paid or incurred” rows need a separate legacy mapping.

D2 · APPROVED

Unknown tax is not $0

Store an unknown amount separately from confirmed $0. Let the owner record receipt tax without claiming a credit.

D3 · DIRECTION APPROVED

Start with a short category list

Offer recognizable choices and a clear way to add one. The exact initial labels still need selection; keep old spelling history readable.

D4 · APPROVED

Put the receipt in the entry flow

Show receipt upload beside the core expense fields and make “add it later” clear. Missing receipts appear in the review queue.

D5 · APPROVED

Three monthly answers

Show how much was spent, which purchases need evidence, and how much was paid personally.
