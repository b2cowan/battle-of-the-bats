Stage 0 decisions

Resolve these against the owner journey before changing stored money meanings.

D1 · APPROVED

Two dates, one short default

Enter an incurred date. Paid date defaults to it; “Payment date is different” reveals a second date. Expense periods use incurred date; future payment/cash views use paid date. Existing “paid or incurred” rows stay marked for review.

D2 · APPROVED

Unknown tax is not $0

Store an unknown amount separately from confirmed $0. Let the owner record receipt tax without claiming a credit.

D3 · APPROVED

Start with a short category list

Use the seven starter labels below and a clear way to add one. Keep old spelling history readable.

D4 · APPROVED

Put the receipt in the entry flow

Show receipt upload beside the core expense fields and make “add it later” clear. Missing receipts appear in the review queue.

D5 · APPROVED

Three monthly answers

Show how much was spent, which purchases need evidence, and how much was paid personally.

Five journeys to walk

1. Ordinary CAD purchase

Overview → Add expense → expense date, supplier, one CAD amount, category, purpose, payment source and receipt → Save → open the saved record from Expenses. Check that the amount was typed once.

2. USD purchase

Add expense → choose USD → enter original USD and CAD amounts with conversion source/date → Save → open the record. Check that the USD evidence is visible without cluttering CAD entry.

3. Receipt to add later

Add expense → leave receipt empty → Save → find the item in Needs your attention → open it and attach the receipt through a correction. Check that saving without a file is clear.

4. Paid on a different day

Add expense → set expense date → check Payment date is different → enter payment date → Save → open both dates on the record. Check that clearing the box restores the same-day default.

5. Correct a mistake

Expenses → open a record → Correct expense → change one fact and give a reason → Save → return to the record. Check the previous revision and receipt remain accessible.

The owner walk remains open until these paths are tried and any confusing step is recorded in the checklist.

Starter categories to review

Software & online servicesAdvertising & marketingEquipment & suppliesProfessional servicesTravel & mealsBank chargesOther

These are entry labels, not tax classifications. The owner can add a category; “Web services” in the example above shows a custom label. Existing category text stays with its revisions; no automatic renaming or merging is proposed.

Legacy-date treatment to confirm

The live form and CSV have one “paid or incurred” date. For existing revisions, keep that recorded value and label it Recorded date · meaning unconfirmed. Leave the new incurred and paid dates unknown until evidence or the owner establishes each date. A correction can confirm them without overwriting the old revision.

Proposed expense-period reports use confirmed incurred dates. Show unresolved legacy entries and their amount in a separate Dates to review group so they do not silently disappear from the owner’s view. Exports should include the original recorded date, both new dates when known, and a date-status column. Review actual stored rows before selecting a migration. This is a recorded-expense view; an accountant decides tax recognition, including prepayments. See CRA accounting methods.
