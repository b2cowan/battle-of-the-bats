Refunds you can see: the row, the record, the book, the family’s email

In the 12b walk a family paid by card for a child the club had just declined. The club refunded it, Stripe has it, and nothing in the product says so: the registration’s Fee column reads "—". The owner ruled three things (plan §0g): the refund shows on the registration, a refund made in the club’s Stripe dashboard records itself, and the family gets a refund email in the club’s name. This page draws where and how. Nothing here is built.

Ruled 2026-10-26: all six asks, every one as recommended

  • The refund sits on the Fee cell's second line, with an amber pill only when it failed (1). The first line keeps the fee figure (2).
  • One refund block in every house-league refund email, and an email for the two cases that send nothing today (3).
  • The Money tab stays the book, with one closing line for a refund that never entered it (4).
  • A refund made in Stripe records itself whenever it can be pinned on one child; a person only for "whose was it?" and "what now?" (5). The family is emailed when Stripe accepts it (6).

Where I disagree with the brief, before the drawings

  1. "A column called Fee now means two things" was settled in 9b-2. The Fee cell already tells the registration’s money story: owing, received, refunded, kept. A refund belongs in it. So the choice isn’t whether, it’s how much the cell carries. I recommend the cell’s existing two lines, with the card’s part on the second line and an amber pill only when a refund failed (ask 1, A). Amber, because it’s the one state where someone must act, and the tournament row already shows the same words in amber. The other two placements are drawn small.
  2. The cell’s figure should stay the fee, not what the card got. The owner’s example read "Refunded $156.75", which is the fee plus the processing fee. The same cell read "$150.00 received" a week earlier, and the Money tab’s refund line says $150.00. So the first line says "$150.00 refunded" and the second line carries " to the card". A parent quoting their statement still finds their number on the row (ask 2).
  3. The Money tab is the season’s book, so it shouldn’t list money that never entered it. A refund that never booked gets one closing line under the Refunds list, naming the child and linking to the row (ask 4). Listing it among the book’s lines would make the list’s total disagree with the band.
  4. The brief’s example dividing line for a dashboard refund ("record only a full refund on an ended registration") leaves the book wrong in the common case. The money has already left the club’s Stripe balance, and no one at a keyboard can change that. A person adds only two things: which child it was, and what happens to the registration. So I recommend recording every refund we can pin on one child without guessing (the whole payment, or any amount on a one-child payment), whatever the registration’s state. A person is asked only "whose was it?" (a part refund on a payment for two or more children) or "what now?" (a child still enrolled whose fee went back) (ask 5).
  5. The email question has an answer the brief didn’t expect. A refund Stripe has accepted is booked at once (ruled), and the ending email already says "refunded" then. So "no email until it lands" would contradict what’s built. I recommend: email when Stripe accepts it; never for a refund we couldn’t confirm with Stripe (it waits for Stripe’s event); never to the family for a failed one (ask 6).

b · The row’s record window: the refund in full

The record opens from the player’s name. It reads first and has no edit state of its own. The refund gets its own block right under the Fee section’s facts, one block per card refund. Full detail lives here so the row can stay at two lines. The one act a refund can need (a failed one, sent another way) is a button inside its block, and it’s also listed in "What you can do", as every act is today.

Who sees the block (from /ux): everyone who can open the record sees it. Nothing in it is more private than what the row already shows: the card’s last four, a Stripe reference, and who pressed Refund. Only the house-league desk (owner, club admin, league admin, registrar) sees the button. The treasurer reads the same block from the Money tab and never gets the button (§0f).

Journeys (/ux)

1 · The registrar takes a parent’s call: "Did you refund me?"

Who, where: a registrar on a laptop or a phone, with the parent on the line. How long it should take: under 20 seconds.

  1. Search the child’s name on Registrations (it finds every tab).
  2. Read the Fee cell: "$150.00 refunded · Oct 26 · to the card". That’s the parent’s number and the day.
  3. If asked for proof: open the record, press Copy on the Stripe reference, read out or paste the card and date.

What goes wrong: the parent sees nothing on their statement yet. The record says "5 to 10 business days" and when it reached Stripe. Pending says Stripe is waiting on the bank. Failed shows the amber pill, and the registrar arranges another way. A refund made in Stripe that’s still waiting to be matched shows "not matched yet" on the child’s row, so the registrar doesn’t say "no".

2 · The club admin refunds in Stripe’s dashboard and expects the book to follow

Who, where: an owner or club admin in Stripe, who may never open our product that day.

  1. They refund the whole payment in Stripe.
  2. Within a minute: the refund line and its processing fee line are in the book, the row reads "in Stripe", and the family’s email has gone.
  3. If the child was still enrolled: one Needs-attention item, Withdraw or Email the pay link.
  4. If it was part of a payment for two children: one item, "Choose the child".

What goes wrong: Stripe’s event is late, so the row says nothing for a while. The day after, the book can’t disagree with Stripe for long, because a late event still records itself.

3 · The family

They get one email per act, in the club’s name, with the amount, the card, the day and a reference. They get none for a refund that didn’t happen, and nothing that says "refunded" before Stripe has accepted it. When the club refunded in Stripe but hasn’t decided what happens to the registration, the email waits for that decision, so the family isn’t told "refunded" and then "please pay" a day apart.
