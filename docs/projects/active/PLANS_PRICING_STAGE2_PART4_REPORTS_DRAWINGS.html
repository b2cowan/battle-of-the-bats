The six points in the brief, tested against the code

1 · Which are missing? Agree, wider

Trials ending is a count on Today and a filter on Clubs & portals; renewals sit on the club record. What is missing is one dated list. So renewals and trials become one list, “Coming up”. Two corrections: Founding clubs are stored as active with no subscription, not as trials, and the trial count also counts a trial whose end date has passed (finding F2).

2 · Revenue overlaps the return Partly disagree

Same records, different figure. The return is gross by tax period, keeps a bare void, leaves out sales before registration. /accounting ruled sales by month nets voids and credit notes, includes pre-registration sales, and shows collected and written off apart. So it is a second view on the same engine’s rows, with one sentence that reconciles it to line 101. The accountant package says today that Stripe income is NOT in it; the export answer is to add sales to that package.

3 · “Owner-only” is not a role Agree, re-framed

Two audiences. The lists show a club’s next bill and unpaid bills, exactly what Online payments and the Money tab already show those staff one club at a time (Super admin, Billing, Support). The money totals are the owner’s books: Bookkeeping, by owner email. Three options drawn in “Where it lives”.

4 · “Stripe fees” means two things Agree

Fees billed back to clubs exist (Online payments). Stripe’s fee on our own sales exists nowhere. /accounting: card-processing fees carry no GST/HST, Stripe’s Billing and Tax fees do (monthly tax invoice), and no rule needs them stored. One Bookkeeping expense a month covers the books. Storage is drawn as a choice with its cost.

5 · Failed payments are expensive Disagree with the premise

Our own invoice record holds every bill with its before-tax, tax, total, status and date, and Stripe’s paid, void and written-off updates keep it current. So an unpaid-bills list across clubs needs no Stripe call. Only the bank’s reason and the next try stay on the club’s Money tab, one door per row.

6 · Business numbers is not revenue Agree, plus a defect

It is “about $X a month at list price, before tax” for active clubs right now. It also counts every Founding club at the Tournament Plus price while they pay $0 (finding F1). The sales view says so in a callout.

What the reviews said (2026-11-07)

/accounting What the figures call revenue

Sales are what was invoiced, by issue day, paid or not. A void and a credit note come off in their own month; a written-off bill is a bad debt, not less sales; tax is never revenue; CAD at the Bank of Canada rate for the invoice day (the record already does this); sales before registration count and are marked. Stripe’s card-processing fee carries no GST/HST; its Billing and Tax fees do. Nothing needs storing for the books. A yearly plan counts in the month billed; whether to hold part of it back at year end is the accountant’s call.

/legal Who sees customers’ billing

Need-to-know staff use for billing follow-up: our own purpose, no notice needed. An individual coach’s unpaid portal is that coach’s personal information, so the Renewals exports are “justified”, not “none”. Lists stay free of names, emails, addresses and cards. The accountant’s sales file has no address. Owner items for the privacy policy draft: “every club or person record they open is logged” (lists were never logged), and a row naming our accountant.

“Stripe fees” is two different figures, and one word for both will be misread. One is money clubs pay us (income). The other is what Stripe charges us (an expense). They are drawn apart and named apart.

Exists Fees billed back to clubs

When a family’s online payment costs us a Stripe fee (a returned debit, a dispute, a foreign card), we bill it to the club, with tax on top. Shown today on Health › Online payments and the club’s Money tab. On Sales by month it is income: the “fees” kind in a month’s window when it was billed on its own bill; inside “plans” when it rode on the plan’s bill.

Name on screen: “Fees billed back to clubs”.

Nowhere Stripe’s fee on our own sales

What Stripe keeps from each payment a club makes for its plan, plus Stripe’s Billing and Tax product fees. A business expense. The books need the month’s total and Stripe’s monthly tax invoice, not a per-payment copy.

Name on screen, if it is ever shown: “Stripe’s fees to us”.
