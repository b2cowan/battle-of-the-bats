The “?” is in the same place in all three portals

The page’s “?” no longer sits at the end of the page’s own top row. On a computer it is in the top bar, between the bell and your account button (View site · bell · “?” · account · Workspaces), in the tournament admin, the club and the Coaches Portal. On a phone it is the last thing in the bar at the top, after the globe. A page whose top row held only the “?” has no row any more, so its content starts about 50 px higher; a page with buttons keeps them where they were. The Coaches Portal’s notifications, Plan & billing and Join a club pages put theirs in the top bar on a computer too, and Roster’s two button rows are one on a computer.
