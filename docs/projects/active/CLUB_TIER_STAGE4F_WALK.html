A Tournaments tab

Written 2026-10-25. One purpose: a family on any page of a club's site sees Tournaments in the tabs and reaches every tournament the club hosts on one page (current and upcoming, then past), and the club's admin shows or hides it with the Tournaments switch, with Past tournaments as a sub-option under it.

What this walk is for

Drawn at the Stage 4f drawings (all seven asks option A, plus Past tournaments as a sub-option); words in CLUB_TIER_STAGE4F_COPY.md (your yes, 2026-10-25). The Past tournaments tab is gone: its list lives at the bottom of the new page, and its old address forwards there.

It passes when the tab is on every club page and in view on a phone, the page shows the right tournaments in the right part with the right words, each row opens the right place, the old address and archive pages behave, and the editor's sub-option appears, disappears and keeps its setting.

Not in this walk: the home's Tournaments section (unchanged by ruling), a tournament's own pages and the club-to-tournament link (the Public tournament site review), help articles (/docs runs after this walk), the club's own web address (no address is connected on dev, so that check waits for a live one).
