The new look, as a customer meets it once the switch is gone

Written 2026-11-04. Test data only, on the dev database. About 45 minutes. One purpose: every public tournament page shows only the new look, with nothing to switch, and the Public site page that sets it is right on each plan.

What this walk is for

Stage 8 removed the preview switch. There is no old look to fall back to and no link to turn anything on: a family, a fan and an organizer all see the new pages. This is the last look before the stage waits to ship. Two things changed in this session that the walk also checks: a tournament page's buttons and highlights now always wear the same colour as its band (they used to follow an older rule and could disagree), and a tournament’s share picture no longer prints zero teams when the Teams page is off.

It passes when

  • A and B: every public tournament page, on a computer and a phone, opens in the new look, with no preview link, no old tab row and no sideways scroll. The band, the buttons and the highlights on a page share one colour.
  • C: on Tournament Plus the Public site page is Pages, Look and Logo, and a colour chosen there reaches the band and the buttons.
  • D: on the free plan the same page shows Pages and one lock line, and the public page wears the plain FieldLogicHQ look.
  • E: a club on “Always the club’s look” has every tournament in the club’s colour, buttons included.
  • F to I: the dashboard’s Public site row ticks once a colour is set, the home-screen icon is FieldLogicHQ’s, the demo tour lands on every step, and the help says all of this.
