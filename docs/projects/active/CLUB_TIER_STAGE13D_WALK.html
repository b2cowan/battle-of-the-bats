Owner QA walk · Club Tier Stage 13d · Hand-offs between people · §528

Each hand-off reaches the person it is for, and nobody is shown a door they can’t open

Written 2026-11-14, after Stage 13d Session A (time, committed ff171af17), Session B (money, committed 7912fa1b3) and Session C (the coach’s request, committed cec8115cd). One purpose: when one person needs something from another (time, a bill, a payment method), it lands on the right person’s screen in words they can act on, and the people who can’t act see no button for it.

What this walk is for

Harbourtown’s league admin has two house league seasons the club has given no time: 2027 Spring House League (never asked) and 2027 Winter Skills League (a draft that asked on Nov 5, 9 days ago). You will ask for the Spring’s time, give it by sending a schedule, and watch the ask clear and the bell reach the league admin. Then the money side: two schedules not yet sent carry bills (Spring tune-up practices, $400.00 on Diamond permits, and Winter dome extra hour, $900.00 on the dome line, in review), and each person sees them in the way their role allows. Last, a coach asks the club to pay (or says how they are paying), and the treasurer’s Approve already knows the method.

It passes when

  • 1–4 The league admin asks for time, the owner and admins hear it on This morning, the owner gives time by sending a schedule that gives only house league time, and the league admin hears it and the season line changes.
  • 5 The admin without Accounting sees the ask (giving time is not money) and no money door.
  • 6–7 The treasurer reads what is owed to the club and the drafts being billed (read only); owners and admins with Accounting open the schedule from them; the Bill window names what Accounting already bills; the tournament shows “In the club’s book”.
  • 8–9 The registrar sees none of it; the phone reads the same as rows.
  • 10–12 The coach says how the money should move; the treasurer reads it, Approve is pre-filled, and the coach then reads “Paid”.
  • 13 A new allocation from a line with a draft bill still saves (it warns, never blocks).

Known before the walk (not defects of this walk)

  • The send in part 3 reaches nobody by email. The schedule you send gives house league time only and holds no team, so the Send window says “0 coaches” and “Nothing is emailed to a family”. The only people told are the league admins, admins and owners, by a bell. That is how this walk stops at a send without mailing anyone.
  • The owner gets no bell for the time they give. The sender is never told about their own send. The league admin and the two admins are.
  • One defect was found while setting this walk up and is fixed in the working tree, not yet committed: the tournament page never showed “In the club’s book” for anyone, because the page did not copy it from the server’s answer. It is one line, with a test (step 7.3 reads it). Commit awaits your yes.
  • Winter’s ask is left standing. Its dates (Jan 9 – Feb 27, 2027) hold no time, so the schedule you send in part 3 gives time to the Spring only. The card goes from two seasons to one, not to none.
  • Part 13 is last because it changes the books ($450.00 more billed to teams, the line down to $0.00 left). Everything that reads those figures comes first.
