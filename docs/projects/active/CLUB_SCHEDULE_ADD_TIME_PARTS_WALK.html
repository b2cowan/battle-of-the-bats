Add time to a sent schedule as a part of its own, bill it separately and send the whole family at once

Written 2026-11-17, after Session 3 (839645c5c) and the Close’s words pass. One purpose: a club adds more time to a schedule already sent, and each addition is its own part with its own bill. The families hear once, the coach sees a second charge, someone without Accounting sees no money, and a part can be taken off before or after it is sent. Nothing from Session 3 has been tried in a browser before this walk.

What this walk is for

Harbourtown sent Fall practices to three teams (14U AA, 15U AA, 13U AAA), built from Milton evenings and billed in three payments. You add Halton Hills evenings to it three ways (two parts that are sent, one that you take off, one that you send free), and watch what each person sees: you as the owner, the coach of 14U AA, a scheduling admin who does not hold the club’s Accounting, and a registrar who has no Rep Teams. Part 5 sends the family while a second admin changes one part under you, so only part of it goes out.

It passes when

  • 1 Add time makes a named part, “Time added Nov 17”, with its own teams, and moves nothing already on the schedule or its bill.
  • 2 Send the changes sends the schedule’s own change and the new part together: one row and one email per team, the part’s bill on a line of its own, set with Bill it…, no money in the coaches’ lines.
  • 3 The coach of 14U AA sees a second charge named for the part, the first bill’s payments are as they were, and a bell for the new bill.
  • 4 A reader without Accounting sees “Bill set” with no figure and no dollar sign anywhere, and cannot send what holds a bill.
  • 5 A send refused part-way tells only the families of what landed and says the rest is still waiting.
  • 6 A part sent free gets its own “sent without a bill” row; a part taken off before its first send is gone, with no one told; a window open on it closes.
  • 7 A part taken off after its first send leaves at the next send, credited on its own bill.
  • 8 A person without Rep Teams neither sees nor sends a part that holds rep teams.

Not in this walk

  • Reading the emails. Every address in this club ends @dev.local, so a send is attempted and some may count as “couldn’t be delivered”. That is expected here and is not a failure. The email’s words are read in the Send window’s preview of what a coach gets, if you want them.
  • Building a schedule on two permits, Find the time and the bill’s price per permit: that is walk 1 (§531).
  • Regenerating one part and the played-practices rules: walk 2 (§532, parts 2 to 4). ⚠ Walk 2’s part 1 describes the old Add time (a correction on the first bill) that this build retired; do not use it, this walk replaces it.
  • Exact figures that move with the calendar. Dollar figures here are pinned as a relationship (“$90.00 times the hours shown”).
  • The four owner calls below are not counted in the progress.
