0 · The measurement

What a coach and a helper actually get on a phone today

Every number read from the browser’s own geometry — never from a screenshot.

Neither phone project ever listed tryouts, and the One Room build (§81, August) left the phone “byte-for-byte”. Measured for the first time since, the room holds up in places — the check-in rows and the helper’s scorecard are genuinely good field equipment — and fails in three that matter: the coach can score a player without seeing which player (F02), the live board shows one player per screen (F05), and every helper’s link wears the public website’s bar (F01).

1 · The room on Tryout day

One player on the first screen — and the stage tabs don’t fit

Why a list that puts the ranking first is allowed here: the board is ranked today at every width, and nothing about that changes. What the phone keeps is the part that stops a rank reading as a verdict — the sentence that says how it is ranked, directly above it; the helper count on every row (a 4.4 from one helper is not a 4.4 from three); and a no-show marked as a no-show. Product-manager brief

Tryouts from a phone

Measured and drawn 25 September 2026. Eight decisions waiting on the owner. Nothing built.

What changes on tryout day

At the gate (check-in). The list of kids already works in sunlight — big rows, big bib numbers, a clear checked-in state — so it stays. What changes is the strip above it: today it slides up under the team name when you scroll, taking the “9 of 14 checked in” count with it. It will stay in view, with search, Add player and Print as one row of thumb-sized buttons. Adding a walk-up puts the cursor straight in First name. And one new button, Not here yet, shows only the kids who haven’t arrived — the question the gate asks at 8:55 a.m. with sixty names on the list.

Scoring a player. Today, if a coach scrolls down the list and taps the ninth player, they land halfway down that player’s scorecard with the name and number off the top of the screen — a coach can score a kid without seeing which kid. Opening a player will open their scorecard as its own full screen, name and number pinned at the top and Done pinned at the bottom, every time. It is the exact screen the helpers already get on their link, so there is one scorer to learn, not two.

Watching the live board. Today each player takes a whole card — one player per phone screen, with the rank and the bib number both printed as “#1”. The board becomes a ranked list, one line per player: place, bib and name, score, and how many helpers scored them. Eight players fit on the first screen instead of one. Tap a player for the category-by-category breakdown, as Decide already does.

Deciding. Five blocks of explanation (two of them the same sentence) sit above the first player today. They fold into one short note that still says, in the owner’s words, nothing is sent to anyone. Every button on the board — Offering / Waitlist / Not this season, Add to roster, the family’s note — reaches thumb size; today 69 of them don’t.

The top of the room. The stage (Set up · Tryout day · Decide · Build team) moves into a chip beside the page title that opens a small menu — at phone width only three of the four stage tabs fit today, and “Build team” is off the edge with no sign it is there. That frees the top row for the three things a coach flips between all morning: Live board · Check-in · Score.

For a helper (a volunteer scoring on their own phone). Their link today shows FieldLogicHQ’s public website bar — Discover · Pricing · Sign in — printed on top of the team name and the Blind badge. It goes. The “back to all players” link grows from a sliver to a real button, and the link’s expiry time reads the way every other time in the product does (“12:45 p.m.”).

What is deliberately not changing

Nothing is sent to families, from any button. The boards stay ranked by score — the same order the desktop shows — with the line that explains the order directly above them and the helper count on every row. Coaches still see names; the names switch still governs helpers only and stays everywhere it is today. Scores stay whole numbers; the scorecard, the setup checklist and the tryout report keep their shapes. The desktop is untouched.

Why it matters

Tryout day is the most phone-bound day of a coach’s year — check-in at a gate, scoring on a diamond, helpers on cold links — and the fall tryout window is open now. Two of the findings are not cosmetic: a coach can score the wrong child without seeing it, and every volunteer who opens a scoring link sees a sales bar over the scorer.

Customer impact

Every coach who runs a tryout on the Premium Coaches Portal, and every helper they invite. No change for families, club admins, or on the desktop.

Priority

The helper’s link first (smallest change, most exposed screen, in front of parents this month), then the open player, then the gate and the top of the room, then the boards. A test fixture comes first of all, so each step is measured by the build and not by hand.

Success criteria

  • On a 390px phone the first ranked player on the live board is on the first screen, and at least six are visible at once (today: one).
  • Opening any player to score — from anywhere in a long list, on either door — shows that player’s name and number and the Done button without scrolling.
  • Zero controls under 44px on check-in, both scorer doors and Decide at 390 and 360; nothing under 12px on check-in and the scorer.
  • A helper’s link shows no public-site bar at any width.
  • The layout sweep measures seven tryout screens (today one).

Tryouts from a phone — the plan

The canonical copy is docs/projects/active/COACH_TRYOUTS_PHONE_PLAN.md; this tab carries its findings, decisions and ladder.

How it was measured

Playwright at 390×844 and 360×780 (touch), 768×1024 (touch) and 1440×900 (mouse); every number from the browser’s geometry. Three specimens: a marker team provisioned the tryouts smoke test’s way (its own team and head coach, 14 candidates, three helper links minted through the product, 105 scores, three decisions — torn down, teardown asserted); the UAT Test Team (read only — it holds an empty tryout workspace, which is also why nothing was provisioned on it: marker rows would have landed inside your tryout, the Score face mints a self-evaluator on it, and the names switch stamps a write-once record); and the coach demo’s 11U (read only, 28 candidates). Sixty-candidate figures are extrapolated from measured row heights; nothing that size was seeded.

Findings

F01 — The helper’s scoring link wears the public site bar

The volunteer’s link renders FieldLogicHQ’s public bar (Discover · Pricing · Sign in, 72px, fixed) over the scorer’s own sticky header at 390, 360, 768 and 1440. The public bar renders on every address it doesn’t recognise, and the token route is one. A production defect.

F02 — The coach’s open player loses its name and its Done

On the room’s Score face the scorer sits in a card that clips it, which turns its pinned header and pinned Done into ordinary ones (header 267 → 17 → −184, Done 1,120 → 870 → 669 as the page scrolls). Open the ninth player from a scrolled list: the name is 184px above the screen and the first buttons at 9px, under the masthead; at 360 the first category is above the screen too. §81’s “phone untouched, sticky Done” holds for the helper’s link only.

F03 — “All players” is an 18px target

92 × 18px, both doors — the only way back besides Done.

F04 — 542px of head before the first ranked player

Title to 99; stage tabs 99–146 with Build team and the guide off the right edge at 390; face row 164–216; hint 231–250; the board card’s head 287–448 (161px); helper chips 463–528; first player at 542. Check-in 435, Score list 312, Decide 590.

F05 — A Live board row is 172px, and prints the rank and the bib the same way

Four stacked lines per player at ≤560; “#1 / #1 Mason Okafor”. 64px on one line at 768 — only the phone pays.

F06 — Decide: 590px of explanation, 69 targets under the floor

Five blocks before the first player, two repeating one sentence. Rows 165–216px. 69 controls under 44 (73 at 360, 74 at 768, 134 on the demo’s 28): piles 40px, the categories toggle 16px, “Not scored yet” 37px, the note toggle 16px. 40 words at 10px.

F07 — Check-in’s pinned header slides under the masthead

Sticky at top 0 under a masthead sticky 0–37: the count and names switch hide once the list scrolls. 165px pinned; search 38px, buttons 35px. The rows pass (56px, bib 20, name 16). At 60 candidates nothing answers “who hasn’t shown?”.

F08 — A walk-up costs a tap before the first letter

First name not focused on open; Add & check in 41px. 3 taps + typing → 2.

F09 — The field floor, missed in three places

The scorer’s “Not checked in” divider at 11px (both doors); Decide’s “3 evals”, “no score”, “no email on file” at 10px; from the code, the “Possible returning player — verify” button at 10.5px, about 20px tall, and the confirmed marker at 11.2px.

F10 — Tryout history has no door

0 links anywhere. On a live season it duplicates the room; on a closed season the season gate redirects before it mounts. On the UAT team: “0 candidates — level with 2025 Season” over an empty table. Its help article promises a door that doesn’t exist.

F11 — Helpers disagreeing is invisible at rest

Only the opened breakdown says “these helpers are 3.0 apart”. Named and stopped: the flag is the Draft Brief, out of this project.

F12 — Two names for one board; two precisions for one figure

“Live board” (face) / “Live scoreboard” (card); evaluators / helpers / evals; “avg 3.62” beside “avg 4.6”.

F13 — The helper’s link tells the time in the device’s clock

“Link active until Sun 27 Sept, 12:45” is built from the phone’s locale; the house clock is “12:45 p.m.”, built in one place.

F14 — The family’s pages

Work on a phone (one column, 1–2 screens). Inputs 38–41px, headings 11px. The landing never says when or where — only the coach’s free text — though the sessions exist.

F15 — The sweep measures one tryout screen, and its baseline describes a room that no longer exists

One entry, the landing; 23 accepted findings naming controls retired in August; no tryout screen under the field-floor rule.

F16 — The tablet band

At 768 the scorer is the phone flow (129×48 buttons — fine on touch); Decide has 74 targets under 44 — fixed by T4b if scoped to ≤768.
