Sponsorships, Part 3a: the money

Part 3a is the money half of a sponsorship deal. Once a business sends its details, the club issues an invoice with the tax added, the business can download it, and the club records each payment as it arrives. Every payment writes lines into the club’s books, and a payment recorded by mistake can be voided, which marks those lines void and puts the amount back to owing.

This walk follows one deal, “Walk Harbour Dental”, from its link to its receipt and then into the books. Eight walks, 46 steps. Eight owner calls sit at the foot, outside the count.

The two kinds of window

  • Owner window: your ordinary browser window, signed in as the club owner. It is the club’s side: Deals, the deal page, Accounting.
  • Private window: a private or incognito window, signed out. It is the business’s side: it opens the link and sees only what a business sees. Keep it open for the whole walk; you reload it several times.
  • Steps say which window to use. “Reload” means reload that window’s page.
