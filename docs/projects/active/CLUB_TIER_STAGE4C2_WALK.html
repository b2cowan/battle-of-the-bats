The test club is UAT Lakeshore Lynx, a baseball club. Its live teams, as seeded, with the line families should read at the start (January 1): 15U AAA 2012–2013 · 13U AAA 2014–2015 · 16U AA 2011 (one year) · 15U AA 2012–2013 · 14U AA 2013 (one year) · 12U AA 2015 (one year) · 11U AA 2016–2017 · 9U AA 2018 or later · 10U Development none. Only 15U AAA has sign-ups open. 15U AAA's tryout blurb begins “Open to players born 2012 and 2013.” 13U AAA's begins “Last year's 2025 roster returns …”.

Part C moves the date birthdays count from, then puts it back. Part E starts a real 2027 season on 13U AAA and cannot be undone, so do it last if you want to keep the fixture as it is; Parts G and H don't depend on it.
