# Themed Date Picker — PM Brief

**What:** on a computer, every date and time field in FieldLogicHQ opens our own calendar or time list instead of
the browser's. Dates read "Oct 25, 2026" and times read "8:00 a.m." in the field itself, instead of "yyyy-mm-dd"
and "--:-- --". Phones and tablets keep the device's own picker.

**Why it matters:** the browser calendar was the one control in every form that looked like the operating system,
not the product: a different font, blue links, a Sunday-first week, and a placeholder customers have to decode.
It also wrote times two ways.

**Customer impact:** every role that enters a date, including coaches (money, schedule, roster, tryouts), club
admins (seasons, permits, money), tournament directors (setup, schedule), families (registration birthdates) and
platform staff. Nothing they save changes, only how they pick it. Birthdates get faster because the month
and year are one click away.

**Owner calls (2026-10-25, all as recommended):** one unit of work; a solid olive picked day; range shading on paired
dates; and every time field included, so the product writes one clock everywhere.

**Priority:** polish, not a blocker; done as one sweep so no screen is left half-converted.

**Success criteria:**
- No browser date/time control left on a desk, with a build check that keeps it that way.
- Typing a date still works, and keyboard users can do everything without a mouse.
- No date ever saves a day off from what was picked.
- Phones are unchanged.
