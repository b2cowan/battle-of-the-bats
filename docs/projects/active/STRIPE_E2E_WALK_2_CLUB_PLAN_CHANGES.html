Owner QA walk · Stripe end-to-end · Walk 2

A club changes, cancels and downgrades its plan

Written 2026-10-28. One purpose: prove, with real Stripe and real webhooks on the dev site, what the owner sees when a club opens the Stripe billing portal, moves to a smaller size at renewal, downgrades to the free plan, and cancels. Buying a plan, reactivating, and moving UP with proration are not repeated here.

What this walk is for

Buying a Club plan, reactivating it, and moving up a size with Stripe’s own prorated figure already passed with real money-moving steps (§244, 2026-09-27). What was never checked against the real Stripe dashboard and the dev site’s real webhooks is everything that goes the other way, plus the Stripe-hosted portal. This walk covers five things the owner can do to a paying club, in the order least destructive first:

  • The Stripe portal (“Payment method & invoices”) — what the owner sees when Stripe sends them back.
  • Moving down a size (Club · Association → Club) — it waits for the renewal, nothing is credited, and “Keep” takes it back. You confirm the scheduled change exists in Stripe and is gone after “Keep”.
  • Downgrading to the free plan — the review screen, the owner-only rule, what is archived, and what Stripe does to the subscription.
  • Cancelling — the review screen, the immediate suspension, the emails, then coming back.

The code does not work the way the words “downgrade” and “cancel at renewal” might suggest: a downgrade to the free plan and a cancellation both end the Stripe subscription at once, with no credit, and only the Club size move waits for the renewal. The walk asks you to confirm each of those against what Stripe shows.

It passes when

The portal returns the owner somewhere sensible, and nothing about the plan has changed on return. A scheduled size move shows on the plan page, shows as a scheduled change on the subscription in Stripe with the same date, and both are gone after “Keep”. A non-owner never sees a downgrade or cancel door. A downgrade archives exactly the tournaments you did not keep and ends the Stripe subscription, and the club is not suspended afterwards. A cancellation suspends the club at once, ends the subscription in Stripe, sends exactly one cancellation email, and reactivating brings the club back. Every Billing webhook delivery you look at is a 200.

Part B needs the Club plans on sale

On the dev site the plans Club and Club · Association are still marked early access. The shortcut that opened them for the 2026-09-27 walk (a cookie) is switched off on the dev site, so the page shows “Contact us to move” instead of the move button.

To run part B, a platform admin opens both plans first: Platform admin › Plans & pricing — propose “Live” for Club and for Club · Association, then approve the two requests in the Approval Queue. That changes the shared dev database for everyone, so part B ends with a step that puts it back. If you would rather not, do the first step of part B, tick “Contact us to move”, and skip the rest of B; nothing else in the walk depends on it.

Stripe cards
Approved: 4242 4242 4242 4242. Any future expiry date, any three digits, any postal code. Stripe’s page also asks for an email and a name: any will do.
Webhooks
Stripe dashboard (test mode) › Workbench › Webhooks › the destination named Billing › Event deliveries. After a step that changes the subscription, a delivery of the named event type should appear within about a minute with a 200 next to it.
Emails this walk sends

Only these exist in the code: “Your plan has been updated” on a downgrade (C), “Your subscription has been cancelled” on a cancel (D), and “Welcome back to FieldLogicHQ” when a cancelled club is reactivated (D). Moving down a size, keeping the plan, and the portal send nothing. A reminder that a trial is ending also exists, but Stripe sends that three days before a trial ends, so it is not part of this walk.

If an email does not arrive in about 2 minutes, open Resend › Emails: every message the site sends is listed there with its subject, recipient, and whether it was delivered or bounced.

Cannot be produced on dev:
  • The renewal actually happening. The scheduled move to Club only takes effect when the billing period rolls over (a trial end or a month away). Nothing here advances Stripe’s clock, so “the plan really changes to Club on that date” and the matching webhook are not proved; only the schedule’s existence and its release are.
  • A move refused because the club has too many teams (Club holds fewer teams than Club · Association). It needs more teams than the fixture has.
  • The trial-ending reminder email (Stripe sends it three days before a trial ends).
  • Downgrade to a paid lower plan and downgrade from Club: the page only offers “Downgrade to Tournament”, and only from Tournament Plus. A Club has no downgrade button, only the size move and cancel.
  • A club with online payments connected (the extra “online payments stop” lines inside the downgrade and cancel reviews). Plan Walk Club has no connected Stripe account; those lines are covered by the Stripe payments walks.
  • Cancelling inside Stripe’s portal (if part A showed a button). That path ends the subscription from Stripe’s side; the code treats it as a Stripe-started cancellation, which this walk does not exercise.
  • A Premium Coaches Portal cancel (a different screen, a different email, a longer retention). Not walked here.
