Proposed · the same

All six ruled A on 2026-11-26. The shaded choice is the ruled one. S1 and S2 replace the earlier ruling of one line per closed place (K4). S4 replaces one row per diamond in the Venue library.

S1 · A closure inside a bigger one, on your site

  • A · The bigger one speaks for it, for as long as it covers the same days. A diamond closed longer keeps its own line.
  • B · Every closure keeps its own line.

S2 · Several closed places on your site

  • A · One heading for the day and one row per park. A single park stays one sentence.
  • B · One sentence per park, each in bold.
  • C · Leave it: one sentence per diamond.

S3 · A long list on your site (try the “8 parks” case)

  • A · Up to five rows all show. Past five, four show and “Show 4 more places” opens the rest where it is.
  • B · Always show every row. Nothing to press, but a phone’s first screen fills up.
  • C · “And N more places are closed” with no way to see them, as today.

S4 · The Venue library

  • A · One box, one row per park and dates.
  • B · One box, still one row per diamond.
  • C · Separate cards, as today.

S5 · Reopening a park with several diamonds closed

  • A · One window with the diamonds ticked, one press, and each coach gets one email.
  • B · A Reopen for each diamond, as today. Needs the “row per diamond” choice in S4.

S6 · Reopening all fields while some are also closed on their own

  • A · The window names them and offers one tick to reopen them too. It starts unticked.
  • B · The window names them. You reopen each yourself.
  • C · Reopening all fields reopens everything.

Fixed whatever you choose: Reopen never says your site has stopped saying a place is closed while it is still saying it, and a park with only some diamonds closed is no longer tagged “Closed today”. S5 and S6 are new behaviour and the larger part of the work; the combined coach email would get a legal wording check before it is built.
