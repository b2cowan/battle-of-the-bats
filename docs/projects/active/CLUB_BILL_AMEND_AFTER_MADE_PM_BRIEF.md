# PM brief — a club changes one team's payment arrangement after the bill is made

**Proposed functionality.** When a team agrees a different way to pay with the club's president, the president changes
that team's remaining payments (their dates and amounts) in one place, without reopening the schedule or touching any
other team. The team's coach sees the new payments and is told by email what moved.

**Why it matters.** Clubs settle money by conversation. Today a bill, once made, cannot change, so the president's only
options are a credit or working outside the product. A club that can't record a payment arrangement keeps a side
spreadsheet, and the product stops being the club's book.

**Customer impact.** Presidents and treasurers: one door on the team's money row, a short form, a clear record of who
changed what. Coaches: their Money tab always shows the real plan, and they hear about a change from the club, not from
a surprise.

**Priority.** After Stage 13c's walk and the layout sweep (it re-opens the same money screens). Needs a drawing session
and the owner's rulings before any build. Not blocking the Club tier going on sale unless a pilot club asks for it.

**Success.** A president moves a team's unpaid payments in under a minute; the coach's tab matches; paid payments
never move; the budget line's "left" and the club's totals do not change; every change leaves a readable record.
