Our revenue figures are before tax, and a refund counts once at what it took off revenue

Written 2026-11-07 for the Stage 3 closeout. Walk C of three: run it after Walk A (it reads Tax Record Team) and adds one new coach of its own.

What this walk is for

The band at the top of a club’s Money tab (Paid, last 12 months, before tax and Refunded), the Story, Business numbers and Today now speak before tax, because the tax we collect is not our revenue. A refund that came with a credit note counts at the note’s before-tax figure. A refund made in Stripe’s dashboard with no credit note cannot be split, so it counts at the amount refunded and the band says so. Each refund counts once.

It passes when

  • Tax Record Team’s band reads $29.00 paid before tax and $15.00 refunded: the credit note’s $10.00 before tax plus the $5.00 with no note, counted once each.
  • A coach whose only refund came with a credit note reads Refunded, before tax, and its Story names the split.
  • Business numbers and Today say before tax beside the monthly figure.

Not in this walk

  • Comparing the band with a GST/HST return. The band counts by the day a sale was PAID; the return counts by the day it was invoiced. They are different views on purpose.
  • Our own records unreadable. There is no safe way to break the records read on dev. Unit tests cover the wording (“includes tax; our records could not be read”).
  • Sales in another currency. Ruled 2026-11-07: recorded, not built, until before the first US club is billed in US dollars.
