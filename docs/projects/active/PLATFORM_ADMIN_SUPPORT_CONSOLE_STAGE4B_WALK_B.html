Free Time Walk B

One question: everywhere free time on a Coaches Portal is refused or fenced, does the product say no, say why in one line, and change nothing? You look at who gets the Give door and who does not, the one line that stands where the door would be, the Give and Change windows when you ask for too much, a stale window, where Put it back is allowed, every cancel and bulk change that has to leave a free-time portal alone, and the coach’s own save routes while Premium is on. The last section ends the free time, because that is the only way to meet the “second time needs a Super admin” rule. Tick what holds and write what does not. Your ticks stay in this browser; the paste-back at the foot gathers them for the chat.

What this walk is for

Walk A gives free time and shows it; walk C pays for it. This one is the fence around it. A refusal that only exists on the server (the coach’s Cancel, the free team’s save routes, a club’s End it now) cannot be clicked, so those steps use the browser console, and they are optional: each has a “Did it” box and a “Skipped it” box. The dev database has no example for five of the door’s absent lines. Those steps say so and how the line would be met; you read them and tick “Read it”.

It passes when

  • Super admin, Billing and Support get the Give door; Product, Growth and Read only get the tab and no door and no line.
  • Each absent-door line below reads as written for a real dev coach, and none of them has a button beside it.
  • Asking for a day that is not after today, or past the cap, shows the server’s red sentence in the window and gives nothing.
  • A window opened before someone else changed the end says so, and changes nothing. Put it back sits on one row only.
  • No cancel, club End it now or bulk change touches a portal on free time. The free team’s saves are refused while Premium runs.

Not in this walk

Giving free time, what the coach sees, the bands and the Free time list (walk A). Paying and Keep Premium (walk C). The sixth give inside seven days needs five earlier gives by one person; dev cannot show it.
