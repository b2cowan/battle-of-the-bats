Six calls before the build

The drawings in the hub (End state drawn → S3) are the spec. I checked them against today's code. Three calls were in the build prompt (G1, G2, G6). Three more came out of the code (G3, G4, G5). Each has a recommendation; pick, add a note if you like, then press Copy my answers at the bottom and paste them into the chat.

A new organizer

After naming their organization they see one heading, “How do you want to start?”, and two short choices: Tournament Plus and Tournament. The plan they picked on the pricing page is already chosen and the screen says so. Both buttons show on a laptop without scrolling, and the chosen one shows on a phone. League Plus and Club are no longer on this screen.

Founding Season still shows Tournament Plus at $0. Nothing changes about what they're charged.

A Premium coach

Founding Season: the welcome gives the dates once, in one sentence, with one button, “Open your team”. The repeated capitals pill is gone.

Paid: after Stripe they no longer land on a dark marketing page. They see the same card in the app's own frame: “Setting up your portal”, then the welcome, then their team. Neither coach reads a date that isn't true for them.

Phone and computer: the same column on both. On a laptop the plan step's two choices sit side by side.

G1

The welcome tells a paid coach only what is true

build prompt Q1

Today only a Founding Season coach ever sees the welcome; a paid coach goes from Stripe straight into the portal. Ask 5b puts both on one card, so the drawn Founding sentence would tell a paid coach “Nothing is charged before October 1, 2027”, which is false. The code also showed a second problem. The welcome decides by the calendar (is the sign-up window open?), not by whether this coach holds the comp. After January 1 a coach who still qualifies gets the comp but reads only “Your Premium Coaches Portal is ready.” The fix: the card reads the team's own record.

G2

The wait after paying has three outcomes, not one

build prompt Q2, plus one the code found

The waiting screen checks for about 50 seconds. If the portal isn't ready by then, it stops and offers Check again. The welcome email (sent the moment the portal exists) carries the link, so “we'll email you” is true today. There's also a quiet bug: opening this page without a payment (an old bookmark, a copied link) waits 50 seconds and then says “Your payment went through”. That's false. That case gets its own card, straight away.

One more thing the drawing says. The waiting line promises “we'll take you straight in”, but Ask 5b says the card turns into the welcome. Both can't be true. I recommend the card turning into the welcome, so a paid coach sees the same “Premium is on” moment as a Founding coach, and dropping “straight in” from the waiting line. The portal's own first-visit summary (“your team came with you”) still shows after “Open your team”, exactly as today.

G3

The plan step's frame: the drawing has the app's top bar, the code can't give it one cheaply

found in the code

The drawing puts the plan step under the white top bar and phone tabs, like steps 1 and 2. In the code the plan step isn't a sign-up screen. It lives inside the organization's admin area, in its bare “focused” frame: no top bar, no side rail, no tabs. That frame carries on into the next screen, the New tournament window. Giving it the app's bar means moving the plan step to its own address outside the admin. Its links, its return from Stripe and its tests would all move with it. That's a bigger change than a restyle.

I recommend A. The plan step is the hinge into the admin: everything drawn inside the page is built exactly as drawn (heading, step line, two choices, Compare every plan), and the only difference is that the bar above it is absent, as it is today. Rule 1's list of screens that wear the consumer frame doesn't include the plan step. Both buttons sit higher without the bar, so the fold is easier to meet.

G4

“Preselected and says so”: the free pick is lost today, and nothing says so yet

found in the code

Two gaps against the Ask 4 ruling. (1) The pricing page's “Start free” on Tournament carries no plan. Someone who picked Tournament arrives with Tournament Plus lit up in lime, the opposite of what they picked. Only Tournament Plus carries its pick today. (2) The drawing marks the chosen card but has no words saying it came from the pricing page.

I recommend carrying the free pick too, and saying so in the sub line, as steps 1 and 2 already do (“You picked Tournament Plus, billed monthly, from the pricing page…”). Step 1's sentence learns to say “Tournament (free)” without a billing period. Today's direct link to Run a tournament, and anyone arriving with no pick, still gets Tournament Plus first, as ruled. The pricing page looks exactly the same; only where its Tournament button leads changes.

G5

Tournament Plus without the Founding offer was not drawn

found in the code

After the sign-up window closes, an organization without the comp sees the list price. The drawing only shows the $0 card. Without the offer, the price follows the billing choice, and the choice drops its “Then”. Prices come from the plan settings, never typed in.

G6

League Plus and Club's pricing buttons: a guard, not a build

build prompt Q3

The plan says every organization plan's pricing button points at the old sign-up. That stopped being true in Stage 1. All of them now point at Run a tournament, and nothing is wrong today because League Plus and Club have no start button while they aren't on sale. The risk is the day one of them opens: its button would make a tournament organization. Instead of building a door nobody can use yet, I'd add a check that fails the build if a plan that isn't on sale starts sending people to the tournament door. When League Plus opens it gets its own door, as ruled.

Design review, already applied to these drawings

The review pass ran before you saw this page. These are proposals until you rule the calls above. The small price line and fine print are now 14px, the first run's fine-print size, not 12px. The billing choice has a visible “Billing” label and a drawn check mark, as the built two-option control does. The phone tabs now match the built bar (taller, sentence case, real icons). “Compare every plan” sits in the column's foot row with a drawn icon, not a typed arrow. The waiting card uses the portal's one loading line (a pulsing dot and a sentence) in place of a spinner. The “can't find” card uses the same quiet ring as the others, because amber is only ever a notice's edge. Spacing and colours now match the built column. The laptop's two-up layout is named as an exception to the one-column rule, forced by the fold rule.

Measured on the built page, not here: whether the heading wraps to two lines on a phone, the real fold positions, every screen in Dark, and that the admin's bare frame picks up the warm colours.

Kept exactly as they are

The pricing page and the marketing home: the same cards, the same look. The plan step gets its own two short rows instead of reusing the big marketing cards. That means nothing on the pricing page can change by accident, and the $0 rule stays one rule shared by both. The “change plan” window inside the admin keeps the full pricing cards, its close and its “Continue free”. It only opens for a League Plus organization today. The Founding Season rule for who sees $0 is untouched (settled 2026-10-20). Home's waiting-team card, the locked start door, the Founding door and every Account-First guard stay as built.
