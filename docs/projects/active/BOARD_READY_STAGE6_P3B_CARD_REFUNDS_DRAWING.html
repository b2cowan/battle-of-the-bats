Refunding a sponsor’s card payment: all of it ends the deal, part of it keeps it, and the processing fee never goes back

You ruled on Nov 23 that card refunds are drawn before any code: the shipped refund window, whole or part, never the fee, and the deal after a full and after a part refund. This page draws exactly that, from the shipped tournament and house-league refund windows, on the deal page as it stands today (committed Nov 23). The example is Harbour Dental’s U9 division sponsorship: $1,000.00 plus 13% HST, $1,130.00, paid by card on Mar 3 with the club paying the $41.05 processing fee. Click any tag to read the build rule behind it, and any blue number to jump to its ask. Words marked DRAFT go through the /marketing pass. Nothing here works until online payments are on, which on production waits for Club Tier Stage 12.

A payment opens as its own window; a card payment has Refund at its foot, never Void

Today a payment is a line on the Payments card with Void at its end. A card payment carries more (its card, its processing fee, Stripe’s reference, its refunds) and a list row holds no buttons, so the row opens the payment’s own window. Void stays for a cheque or e-transfer, where it means the payment was recorded by mistake. A card payment can’t be voided because the money really moved; the only way back is a refund.

The refund window starts with nothing picked: all of it, or part of it with an amount, and a reason either way

It opens over the payment’s window. As in the shipped windows, nothing is picked and there is no button until the club chooses, because a refund is money leaving the club’s account. There is never a fee box: the shipped windows offer the fee back only when the payer paid it and the club is ending things, and a sponsorship refund never returns the fee (round 2 tab 1).

What the press does

  • The card first, then the books. The refund goes to the card; when the card company accepts it, two money-out lines land in the General ledger (tab 4) and the business is emailed. If the card company refuses it, nothing changes and the window says so in place, as the shipped windows do.
  • All of it also ends the deal in the same press (ask 3). A part refund leaves the deal as it was.
  • The fee. When the club paid it, it stays a Payment processing cost. When the sponsor paid it, it stays income under Club sponsorship and a cost the same size, as round 2 drew it. Neither is touched by a refund.
