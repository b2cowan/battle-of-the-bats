Nothing in Fundraising could be deleted. Now some of it can.

A sponsorship or a fundraiser started by mistake was permanent — you could edit it forever and never remove it. Both can go now, but only while they are empty shells. The moment money has landed, the delete button dies where it stands and tells you which door to use instead. Ticks save in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back.

The one thing this walk is really testing

The refusal, not the delete. Underneath, a fundraiser's rows are wired to vanish with it — so an unguarded delete would silently erase several dated income rows at once and leave their money on the books with nothing to explain it. Everything greyed out in this walk is the only thing standing between a mis-tap and that.

So a greyed-out button is a PASS, not a bug. What would be a bug: a delete that looks available and then fails, a refusal that doesn't say how much money is in the way, or a refusal that points you at a door you can't actually reach.
