Nothing in Fundraising could be deleted. Now some of it can.

This walk covers two changes to the same screens: the guarded deletes, and one rule about dates. A sponsorship or a fundraiser started by mistake was permanent — you could edit it forever and never remove it. Both can go now, but only while they are empty shells. The moment money has landed, the delete button dies where it stands and tells you which door to use instead. Ticks save in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back. Amended 2026-08-31: a fundraiser now opens in place in the list — the separate drive screen is gone — so part N is new and parts C and D walk the new shape. The one thing this walk is really testing

The refusal, not the delete. Underneath, a fundraiser's rows are wired to vanish with it — so an unguarded delete would silently erase several dated income rows at once and leave their money on the books with nothing to explain it. Every refusal in this walk is the only thing standing between a mis-tap and that.

So a delete that refuses is a PASS, not a bug. What would be a bug: a delete that goes through when money is on the books, a refusal that doesn't say how much is in the way, or a refusal that points you at a door you can't actually reach.

⚖ One thing changed after you saw it (mockup option A). The reason used to sit permanently above the footer. It doesn't any more — press Delete and the row answers, with either the confirm question or the reason. So the delete button looks ordinary until you press it. That is the new behaviour, not a regression.
