Support Console Stage 4

Stage 4 is the owner's second job from the kickoff: give a customer free time on a plan, with an end date, and have the product take it back by itself. Every drawing is at true size: 1440 pixels wide for a desk, 390 for a phone. On a narrower window each one scrolls sideways inside its own box. Before is a capture from today's dev server, in the Stage 2 look you walked on Oct 25. After is drawn on that same shell, in the product's real warm and dark colours. Blue tags say what changes and why, and each one opens when you click it. The Warm / Dark switch at the top flips every drawing. Your questions sit beside the drawing each one decides, each with my recommendation.

Anything marked example is invented for the drawing. Anything marked Stage 3, 5 or 6 is not part of this stage and is not drawn as if it were built. Nothing on this page states a price or writes a plan's limits as new words: the console's figures are worked out from the plan settings, as they are today. Prices and limits are /billing's and /strategy's, and the customer's words go through /billing and /marketing before they are built.

READ THIS FIRST

Two places where I think the prompt asks the wrong thing

1 · "The product reads 'is this still in force?' every time." Today it reads the plan without asking, in about twenty places.

The timed grants built so far change only two things, a section and a billing status, and they are added after the club's plan and limits have been read. Everywhere else, the product reads the plan stored on the club directly: the gate every request passes through (which sends a Tournament-tier club away from the club pages), sign-up forms, chat, the venue tools, notifications to fans, the public site and its sitemap, the tournament and team limits, and the billing page itself. A browser tab also keeps the plan it loaded until it is refreshed. So a whole-plan grant is not a console screen. It is a change to how the whole product answers "what plan is this club on right now". Done any other way, a club given Club would be refused the club pages by the first gate it meets.

What I recommend instead of a patch: one door that answers "what plan, what limits, until when", used by every place that reads a plan, with a build check that fails when new code reads the stored plan directly. It is the largest part of Stage 4, and it is what makes "ends by itself, no job, nobody remembering" actually true everywhere. Question 1.

2 · "Founding Season becomes a group of grants." Moving it now would decide how September 30, 2027 ends.

A Founding club is not on a grant. At sign-up its plan is written as Tournament Plus, and a separate "comp period" row marks it as Founding by its end date. Nothing ends it: the desk's own note says "nothing in the product turns a comp off by itself", and how the turn-off runs is the Founding Season plan's own decision, due May 2027 (a manual runbook if under about 50 accounts, a job if more). If Stage 4 moved each Founding club onto a grant, every one of them would drop to the free Tournament plan by itself on October 1, 2027. That is a business decision about the Founding cohort (/strategy's), not a console tidy-up, and it would also rewrite the sign-up and checkout paths that grant the comp, which stay open until December 31, 2026.

What I recommend: fold Founding Season into the console's view now (one Free time list, Founding as a named group, the desk's card and 2028 columns kept) and move its access onto grants when the Founding turn-off is decided. You ruled "Founding Season folded in" on Oct 24; this is a narrower reading of that ruling, said out loud. Question 4.

WHAT STAFF AND CUSTOMERS WILL SEE

Six changes, in plain words

  • Give free time, in one window. On a club's Access tab, "Add an override" becomes Give free time: a whole plan, sections, or a billing status, through a day you pick, and why. The window says the consequence in one sentence before anything happens: what the club gets, until when, and what it goes back to by itself.
  • The whole plan, for a while. New. A club on Tournament can be given Tournament Plus or Club for a set time. While it runs, every part of the product treats the club as on that plan; the day after it ends, every part treats it as back on its own plan, with nothing scheduled to make that happen.
  • The record shows it. A chip in the title ("Club, free through Mar 31, 2027"), a Free time figure in the band, the running list on Access, and Story entries for given, changed, ended early and ended by itself, with Put it back on the ones it can undo.
  • Free time, one list. The menu's Founding season entry becomes Free time: everyone on free time, with Founding Season as a named group that keeps the desk's card and 2028 columns.
  • Today's queue is real. "Overrides ending" becomes Free time ending: every timed grant ending in the next 14 days, counted by club.
  • The customer sees it. Their Plan & billing page says "Free through March 31, 2027" and what happens the day after, turns amber in the last 14 days, and says plainly when it has ended.

Deliberately not in Stage 4: anything that reads or changes Stripe, including pausing a paying club's charges (Stage 3). An email when free time ends (it needs its own ruling, and account emails are not logged until Stage 5). Status and the sent-email log (Stage 5). Club web addresses (Stage 6). Free time on a Coaches Portal (question 10).

Question 3 · A club that pays through Stripe: refuse a plan grant, or allow it?

Stripe overwrites a paying club's plan on every subscription event, so a grant would survive only because it is read on top. Meanwhile Stripe keeps charging for the club's own plan, and the club's billing page would offer to cancel or move a subscription for a plan it is not using. The console cannot see Stripe until Stage 3, and your rule R7 is that the money truth is shown, never remembered.

Recommended: refuse a whole-plan grant while a Stripe subscription is live (drawn above); sections and billing status stay available. "Don't charge them for a while" is a Stripe credit or pause, which stays in Stripe (your Q7). Revisit once Stage 3 shows Stripe beside us. The other answer allows it as "trying a higher plan" with a line on their billing page that they are still billed for their own.

Question 5 · "Comp period": retire it now, or keep offering it until every club on it has ended?

It grants nothing. Its only working use is to mark a club as Founding Season, by its exact end date, and about thirty places read it for that (sign-up, checkout, the desk, the 2028 choice, the Founding emails). The console and Bulk changes also offer it to staff, with any date, beside choices that do grant things.

Recommended: stop offering it in this stage (the window and Bulk changes lose it), and leave existing rows reading as they are. Founding's rows keep marking the cohort until question 4's later step moves them. Any non-Founding comp row reads on the Access list as "Billing tag, gives nothing" until its date. Retiring it from the data waits for that later step, because the sign-up path writes it until December 31, 2026.

Question 8 · What the customer is told, and the words for it

The product already says "Free through September 30, 2027" for Founding, in approved copy. "Free until" would be a second phrase for one idea, and your one-spelling rule treats that as a bug. Telling them by email is a customer-facing send, needs its own ruling, and would go unlogged until Stage 5.

Recommended: the billing page only, in three states (while it runs, amber in the last 14 days, and the day after for 30 days), worded "Free through {last free day}", the house phrase, everywhere a date of free time is shown: the customer's page, the console's chip, list and band. No email in this stage; if you want one, it is its own ruling with /marketing and comes after Stage 5 logs sends.

Question 10 · Free time on a Coaches Portal

A portal can be given an override today, but it would not open the portal. Whether a Premium portal is live is read from the portal's own record, which grants never touch, so a grant would show on its billing page and change nothing. The coach Founding comp is given by the portal's own checkout, the same way.

Recommended: no free time on a Coaches Portal in Stage 4. On a portal's record the door is absent, with one line saying why, and a portal's Founding comp stays as it is. Making portals read grants is its own piece of work in the coach portal's billing, best done with question 4's later step.

Question 9 · The switch off: refuse, or show the window with a warning?

With the switch off, a grant is saved, shown as running, and does nothing. That is the one outcome worse than not giving it: staff believe it worked, and the customer's page says "free through" a plan they cannot use.

Recommended: refuse. The server refuses the write and says why; the door is absent and one line on the Access tab says so; the Free time page carries the same red line while it is off. Production is on, so this only bites in dev or a new environment.

MONEY AND SAFETY

Giving a plan away is a money-adjacent write

A grant is revenue not collected, given by one person with a sentence. So it is designed and reviewed the way a refund would be. Below: who may give it, the longest period, what is kept, what stops someone giving free Club time to themselves or a friend, and the security review's threat list, run over the design.

Who, how long, what is kept

  • Who gives it: Super admin and Billing, the people who already change plans. Support and Product see it and do not give it.
  • How long: a plan grant always ends. Billing up to 6 months, Super admin up to 12. Longer than that is a plan change, done with the pencil, where it shows as a plan, not as free time.
  • What is kept: who, when, the club, what, through when, the reason, and every change to the end, each a Story entry and an audit entry. The reason is required. The audit log's own reason column is Stage 3's; until then the reason travels inside the entry, as Stage 2's changes do.

What stops free Club time for yourself or a friend

  • Yourself: the server refuses a grant to a club the staff member belongs to (drawing F, 2).
  • A friend: nothing technical can know who your friends are. What it can do is make every grant seen: it is on the club's Story with your name and reason, on Free time's "Given by staff" view, and in Today's queue before it ends. The cap limits the cost of one bad grant. Super admin reads all of it.
  • No one gives a club a plan quietly: no bulk plan grant, no grant without an end, no grant over a paying club (question 3).

The design pass

The design pass looked at every frame in Warm and Dark against the written standards, the design log and the real pages captured from dev. It found fourteen defects; all are fixed above except the one I kept on purpose (End it now stays lime), which is listed with the rule conflicts below.

  • The customer page invented parts the real page does not have: a chip, a title-case Active, a band without its eyebrow, a Site tab. Redrawn on the real page's own parts: the ACTIVE badge, the band Founding wears (eyebrow, icon box, headline, one sentence), the Billing card at the foot, the tournament header and the Messages tab on a phone. The end is said once on the card and explained once in the band. "By yourselves" became "automatically".
  • Refusals that refused a choice they still offered. Inside the window, the whole-plan row is replaced by one line and the other two choices stay. Where the console knows in advance (the switch off, your own club, a portal), the door is absent and one line says why (drawing F was rebuilt that way).
  • Doors that open windows are outlined buttons (your Oct 1 ruling): Give free time, Change the end date, and a new End it now on each Access row, which had been missing.
  • The date field is the shared one, with its calendar; the quick picks are gone (they would have been a new part). The past-your-limit state is one field, not two.
  • Numbers reconcile: Free time ending 2 is two clubs inside 14 days; Everyone counts 6 distinct accounts.
  • One spelling: "free through" everywhere, including the Founding row that said "given until"; the band's last figure reads Billing when nothing renews.
  • The record: the chip is a short "Free time" with the plan and day beside it in the body face; the Founding view keeps the desk's summer note, Owner last seen and its Organization kind.
  • Small ones: one width for the window; the Given by staff list's Club column no longer wraps; frames no longer crop their last row.
