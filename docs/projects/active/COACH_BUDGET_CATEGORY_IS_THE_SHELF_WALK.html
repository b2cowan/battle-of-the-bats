The CSV reads back — with one known gap you will see in the preview

Known gap

Budget → Import, choose the CSV you just downloaded, and read the preview. Cancel — do not commit it.

What you will see, and why it is not a Flag on this build. Import reads every row of a sheet as a cost — the file has no column for "money in" — and matches rows only against your cost lines. So each money-in line (Chocolate Sale, Team Sponsor…) arrives in the preview as a new line under its category rather than matched to the line it came from; commit it and you would gain a cost line inside a revenue category. This predates the build (a Fundraising heading did the same), but the build widens it: those lines now sit under Tournaments and Other Income, beside real cost lines, so the phantom no longer looks out of place. /review found it on 2026-09-09; the fix — Import learning the file's two bands — is queued as its own piece of work in a peer session, not folded into this one.
