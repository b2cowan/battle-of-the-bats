Owner QA walk · Stripe end-to-end · Walk 1

A coach buys Premium

Written 2026-10-28. One purpose: a brand-new coach pays on Stripe’s real checkout page and the team becomes Premium, and every way that purchase can go wrong (declined card, failed 3-D Secure, walking away) leaves nothing granted and nothing duplicated. You create two new coaches and cancel both at the end.

What this walk is for

This walk follows a coach from “I want Premium” to “the team shows Premium”. The coach starts at the Coaches Portal sign-up (/coaches/start), confirms their email, names a team, pays on Stripe’s own hosted page (not a fake shortcut: the fake checkout is switched off on dev), and comes back to a page that says Premium is on. Stripe then tells our site by webhook, and the site makes the portal and sends the welcome email.

It uses two new coaches so every kind of ending can be tried on a coach who has no portal yet:

  • Coach 1 pays with the plain approved card (Part A), then you look at what a Premium coach sees afterwards, including what happens when they try to buy a second portal (Part B).
  • Coach 2 is the one who has trouble: a declined card (Part C), walking away from Stripe’s page (Part D), a 3-D Secure card that fails and then passes (Part E). After each failure the walk checks that nothing was granted and no team was made.
  • Part F cancels both test subscriptions, one through the product’s own Cancel and one in Stripe’s dashboard, so the sandbox is left clean.

Where the product does not do something a customer might expect (an email about a declined card, a receipt), the step is a ruling: it says what the code does today and you tick what actually happened.

It passes when
  • Coach 1 and coach 2 each land on a page that says Premium is on for their team, the team’s Overview carries a Premium chip, and each gets exactly one welcome email.
  • The Billing destination in Stripe shows checkout.session.completed and customer.subscription.created delivered with a 200 for each purchase.
  • After the declined card, the failed 3-D Secure and the walk-away, coach 2 has no portal, no welcome email, and no subscription in Stripe, until the one purchase you complete on purpose.
  • A coach who already has a Premium portal cannot reach a second Stripe checkout; they are told so on the form.
  • Both subscriptions end up cancelled in Stripe, and each coach gets one cancellation email.

Before you start

Setup, not checked. Keep this page private: it holds dev passwords.

Where
The dev site https://dev.fieldlogichq.ca. Keep two other tabs open: the Stripe dashboard (sandbox “FieldLogicHQ sandbox”, test mode) and the Resend dashboard › Emails. If a message does not reach your inbox within about two minutes, look for it in Resend (subject, recipient, delivered or bounced).
Two inboxes
Use plus-addresses of a real inbox you can read: yourname+coach1@gmail.com for coach 1 and yourname+coach2@gmail.com for coach 2. Both land in your one inbox. Pick any password of 8 or more characters for each (the dev password UATPassword2026! is fine; this is dev only). Use a private window for coach 2 so the two never share a sign-in.
Team names
Coach 1’s team: Walk Premium Bears. Coach 2’s team: Walk Premium Hawks. The names show up in the welcome and cancellation emails, which is how you tell the two apart.
Webhooks
Stripe dashboard › Workbench › Webhooks › the Billing destination › Event deliveries. A delivery that reached our site shows 200. Newest first; the event name is on each row. Several events arrive for one purchase (a payment-method event, an invoice event); the two this walk names are the ones that make the portal.
Test cards
Approved 4242 4242 4242 4242 · declined 4000 0000 0000 0002 · 3-D Secure 4000 0025 0000 3155. Any future expiry, any 3 digits, any postal code, any name. Stripe’s page is Stripe’s, so its wording and button names are described here, not promised.
Price
Premium has no free trial and no Founding Season freebie any more, so the first charge happens at checkout. The price is whatever the pricing page shows today (it may change); this walk never relies on a number.
How a coach reaches billing
There is no menu item in the Coaches Portal that opens the coach’s billing screen today. You get there by typing the address (Part B shows how). That is a known gap, written up as a ruling in Part B.
