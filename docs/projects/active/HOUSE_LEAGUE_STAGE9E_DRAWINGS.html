Push-backs, before any drawing

The answer, before the detail

  • Three builds, not one (ask 9e·1). 9e-1 Standings and the score rule, then 9e-2 Rainout, then 9e-3 Coach scores. Standings come first because 9f freezes them and because “does a coach’s score count?” can only be answered against a defined table. Rainout needs nothing from the other two and uses machinery that already exists. Coach scores is the only new permission and the only new record, so it goes last, with the most settled picture under it.
  • “By sport pack” changes no number anyone sees today. House league offers softball and baseball, and both packs say exactly what the code already does (2 for a win, 1 for a tie). The real standings faults are different: the table is public for a division that is still a draft, a division with no scores shows a wall of zeros, and ties past run differential are settled by team order without saying so .
  • A coach can enter the score for their own home games, and it counts at once (9e·7, 9e·8). The club sees who entered it and can correct it; the other team’s coach can say “not right” but cannot overwrite. Nobody is emailed (ruled).
  • A rainout is one window that moves many games into the machinery that exists (9e·10 to 9e·13): pick the day, the divisions and the games; postpone (default) or cancel; one email per family per division. The honest limit is drawn, not hidden: an Undo exists only until families are told .

What the code showed that the prompt did not say (every flag opens the lines I read)

  • The sport is stored and never read. Nothing that scores or ranks a league game reads the season’s sport; only softball and baseball can be chosen; both give identical values .
  • Standings ignore whether a division is published. Anyone with the link sees a table of zeros for a draft division, and the page never explains how ties break past the second rule .
  • The pencil’s “both empty = back to scheduled” rule is built in the window only. A direct call to the server with both scores empty marks the game played, and standings count a 0–0. A small server fix, drawn into 9e-1 .
  • The tournament’s “pending score” is not a coach feature. It holds scores entered by a volunteer or staff member when the organizer asks to review; an owner’s save is final at once. It does count pending scores in the table, with an “Unofficial” note .
  • A postponed game keeps its old date (it reads “Was Thu, Jun 17”), and the urgent send never counts toward the rolling cap .
  • A send is per division. A household with children in two divisions gets two emails from one rainout. The window says so before you press .
  • Practices are not the league admin’s to cancel when they came from the club’s time, and cancelling one the convenor booked by hand emails nobody .
  • 9f depends on a table that is computed live, every view, and the “Final standings” tag is just a date. Whatever 9e decides about ties and points is what 9f must freeze .
  • Which rule applies to a finished season: the club’s house-league seasons follow Q16 (in place, locked), not the coaches’ portal’s one-page rule. Both standings and score entry lock the same way .

Standings

The answer

  • Same table, honest rules. The table keeps points, then run differential, then runs for, and adds head-to-head between exactly two tied teams. Teams still level share the place (“T-3”). No coin, and no silent fall back to team order (9e·3).
  • The page says how it ranks. A short “How the table is ranked” box replaces the hard-coded footer, and it reads from the same rules the table uses, so the two cannot disagree (9e·2, 9e·3).
  • A division’s table appears when its schedule is published, and until the first score it lists the teams with dashes and says “Standings start with the first score” (9e·4).
  • A coach sees one quiet line on their own team page: “1st of 6 · 4-1-0” (9e·6). A division can opt out of a public table (9e·5).
  • Not built, deliberately: a per-season points override door. Both offered sports give 2/1/0 and nobody can choose another sport. It can be added when a sport with different points is offered.

Scores

The answer

  • The home team’s coach enters the score, from their own team page, once the game’s start time has passed (9e·7). Both scores or neither, as the pencil already requires.
  • It counts at once in the standings and on the schedule, and the club sees “Entered by Alex Moreau” (9e·8). A held-until-approved score would make the table lag and turn the league admin into the bottleneck coach entry is meant to remove.
  • The other coach cannot overwrite; they can say “not right” (9e·9). The score stays as entered, the game gets a flag, and the league admin corrects it. The entering coach can change their own entry until it is flagged or the club touches it.
  • The league admin has the last word and can correct any score. Nobody is emailed about a score or a correction (ruled).
  • Needs a new record (who entered it, when, flagged or not): the league’s game has none today . The coach’s route must be a new one, signed in as the coach and checked against the coach’s own team, never the admin gate .

Rainout

The answer

  • One window, on the schedule’s header (9e·10): pick the day, the divisions and the games; postpone (the default) or cancel; say whether to email families now. Postponed games go to “Needs a new date”, where the existing “Give it a new date” door gives each one a date on time the club has allocated (9e·11).
  • Games only. The window names tonight’s practices and says where they change, and touches none of them (9e·10). A club practice is the club’s to change; the practice the convenor booked by hand has no family email at all today .
  • Families are emailed in the same action by default (9e·12), one email per household per division, with the teams’ coaches copied. Rain is urgent, and the existing urgent send already works this way for one game. A tick turns it into “hold in Changes to send”.
  • The window says what it will do before you press: how many emails, how many households get two (children in two divisions), and what is not told (a draft division) .
  • Undo is real only while nobody has been told (9e·13). After the send, the result says plainly that emails can’t be taken back, and offers “Give them new dates” instead .
  • A plan question, flagged for /strategy, not decided (9e·15): the tournament sells its rain delay at Tournament Plus; house league gates postpone, cancel and the urgent send by module and role only.
