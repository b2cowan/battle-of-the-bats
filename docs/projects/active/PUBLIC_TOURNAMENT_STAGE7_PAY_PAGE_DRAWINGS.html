What changes
  • Band: 72 px with a tab row → 52 px plus the 3 px accent rule, the name only (one line, as the hub drew it). No tabs, no Sign in (A4), no Share. The name goes back to the Overview (A3).
  • No side rail, today's games line, my-team dock, follow/alerts/install prompts, or free-plan badge and banner.
  • Heading: the band already names the event, so the small line above the team becomes "Team payment" and the team takes the big title, as the hub drew it at Ask 6. The small line stays the shipped 11 px (the hub drew 14). The title size is A7: 32 px as the hub drew, or the written page-title size of 20.
  • "Test payment: no money moves." shows only while the organizer's payment account is in test mode.
  • The pay block itself is the shipped one: the same words, amounts, choices and buttons.
  • One way to ask a question, under the pay block (A1). The footer drops its page list, host line and email.
  • Still private and never indexed.
Checked by eye on the 390 frame
  • The first Pay button sits above the fold on the 390 frame with the deposit choice open. Part 2 measures it on the real page at 360, 390 and 1280.
  • The buttons are 48 px and the band name, contact line and footer credit 44. The pay block's own "Refund policy" link is 12 px text with no tap height (A8).

Paying: owing, not finished, confirming

The states a coach sees on the way through a payment. Only the middle of the page changes; switch the view to see just that. The test line shows only in test mode.

Paid, clearing, nothing to pay, or refused

After paying, the link keeps working as the receipt. A team the organizer has not accepted gets one sentence and no buttons.

Not taking payments here: pay the organizer directly

The event does not take online payments: the organizer has no payment account, has switched it off, does not track the fee here, or the plan has no online payments. The coach sees the fee, the due dates and the organizer's own how-to-pay words.

A link that doesn't work

A mangled or old link, or one opened under another tournament's address. The address still names a real tournament, so its band can show.

A free event

A free event never takes online payments (they come with Tournament Plus and up), so its pay page is always Pay the organizer directly, or the receipt-free paid in full. The band is the neutral near-black with no mark, and the button colour follows it.

The desk, 1280 wide

Today a desk shows an 88 px band, "Hosted by" and Sign in, and a 248 px side rail beside the pay block. After: the band runs full width at 52 px, and the page is one centred column at the pay block's own width (640 px). Scroll sideways inside the frame on a narrow screen.
