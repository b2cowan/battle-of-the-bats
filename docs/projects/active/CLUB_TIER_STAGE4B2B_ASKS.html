Ask 3 · the edges of the three states

Where open, not open yet and finished meet

One rule decides a team's state, and every surface reads it: the Tryouts page, the tab, the tryout page, the home and the team page. That means no two surfaces can disagree. The drawings settle the middle cases. These are the edges.

Ask 4 · the fixture

Seed tryout days on the test club

The walk needs a team that's open with days, one not open yet, one finished, and a cancelled day. The test club has none of these. Today only 15U AAA is open, and no team has days.

Recommended: in the test club's seed (dev only, never production):
  • 15U AAA stays open, gains three days starting 10 days out, and one cancelled day.
  • 13U AAA gets two days 3 weeks out with sign-ups closed (not open yet).
  • 14U AA gets two days 10 days ago with sign-ups closed (finished).
The dates are worked out from the day the seed runs, so the fixture never goes stale. That covers both groups, so the Tryouts page shows two bands and the home shows the single row. I won't seed during a sweep.
