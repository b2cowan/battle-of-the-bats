Asks

Five calls for you. The sign-up page was ruled to stay as it was when the closed-registration page was redesigned, so this needs your yes before it is built.

1 · Add the button row to the sign-up page?

Recommended: yes, the row (tab 1). Alternatives: the full tables (tab 3), or leave the page as it is and rely on the club site menu.

2 · Where does the row sit?

Recommended: under the dates and price, above the age groups. The only place today for these links is the bottom of the page, below every age group card. On a phone that is five cards down, which is a large part of why you could not find it. After registration closes, the page puts Schedule at the bottom, below the standings (tab 2, state 5), so the two pages would differ in position. Top-of-page is the better answer for a family who is already signed up; the alternative is the bottom, matching the closed page, which keeps the sign-up content exactly where it is.

3 · Schedule only when something is published

Recommended: yes. Today, before registration opens, a Schedule button shows at the bottom whether or not anything is published. The row would replace it and appear only once a schedule is. I have not checked what the Schedule page shows when nothing is published, so confirm that this loses nothing.

4 · Standings appears with the first score

Recommended: yes. It follows the age groups that keep standings and the first scored game, exactly like the closed-registration page. A club that turns standings off for every age group never sees the button.

5 · The Milton demo keeps its dates

Correction to what I told you earlier: closing registration before the season starts would break the live sign-up walk and the U13 waitlist, because the demo needs registration open and games played at once. The demo is exactly the case this fix is for. No date change is needed once the row exists.
