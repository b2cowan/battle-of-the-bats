The club’s switches, and who can reset a lost phone

A club’s owner can now require two-step sign-in for the board and, separately, for coaches (Organization › Settings); an admin reads the switches. Each person covered gets 14 days, with a reminder at sign-in, then setting it up comes first. The club’s owner or an admin can reset a member’s two-step (Members) or a coach’s (the team’s staff page), but only when that account reaches nothing beyond the club. Support resets an owner with a confirmation email, a notice to the club’s other admins, and a 24-hour wait that a real code cancels. Six walks, one purpose each. About 50 minutes; you need an authenticator app on your phone and the fieldlogichq@gmail.com inbox.

Not in this walk: the Members column, the Overview notices, the two 14-day emails, help and the policies (Part 4). The words on every screen and email are drafts for Part 4’s copy pass.

  • On dev a support reset waits 3 minutes instead of 24 hours (a dev-only setting that production never reads), and the hourly job does not reach your computer: press Run it now when it is due.

Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • Who a club may reset, as a rule: owners and admins only, never themselves or the owner, only when two-step is on, and only when the account reaches no other organization (a seat anywhere else in any status, their own Coaches Portal team, a house-league team in another club, a family link to another club). Checked against every fixture before this page was written. “Lost your phone?” uses the same rule.
  • A reset signs the person out first, then takes two-step off in an order that leaves it ON if anything fails part-way; the club’s reset asks for your code before it starts; a deliberate swap of either order is caught by the build.
  • Turning a requirement on needs the owner’s own two-step; turning it off asks for the code before anything is saved and emails the club’s other owners, admins and treasurer.
  • A person’s own 14 days start at every place a covered seat begins (an invite accepted, added directly, a role change into a covered seat, reinstated, ownership handed over, a coach’s invite accepted, a team moving into the club), and only when the club already requires it.
  • Support’s run claims a reset before it acts, so two runs, a hold or a cancel can never reset twice; the link’s token is stored only as a hash and works once, for 3 days; a backup code at sign-in cancels a waiting reset too.

Your time goes on judgment: do the switches read as a decision the owner makes once, does the reminder feel passable and the “after” screen fair, is the reset door quiet enough, and does support’s path feel safe to an owner reading the emails?
