Three calls this drawing asks you to make

Each has a recommended answer. Nothing runs on live data until you say go.

1 · The words an event comes with Recommended: money in is Entry fees, Gate / admission, Concession revenue, Registration revenue and Sponsorship. Money out is Permits & fields, Officials, Awards and Supplies. The money-out words are a draft. I take the final list from the standard Tournaments shelf and the coaches' event words before building, and show you the difference.
2 · Does the general book list events? Recommended: yes, only events in the fiscal year being entered, folded to one line each. Typing an event's name or a word still finds it. Alternative: no. A treasurer files an event's money from the tournament's own book. The list is shortest, but general-book entries for an event become a second trip. Try it with the switch above.
3 · Existing events Recommended: give every existing hosted event the new words, dev first, with a before and after count per event. Entries already filed on the generic words are not moved. Alternative: only events from this fiscal year on. Older events keep one word, which is fine because nothing new is filed to them.

Design check

What the review changed in these drawings, and what it left alone.

  • Folded event rows are real buttons with the word count and a turning arrow, 34 px at a desk and 44 px on a phone. They are headings that open, not a new kind of row.
  • The event's own shelf leads in the picker's existing lead style (olive on the olive tint), so no new colour is added. It is the same device the bills-you-owe group uses.
  • Typing opens what matches and shows only the matching words. The honest cost: in the general book, "gate" shows one row under each of this year's events. That is correct, because they are different events, and typing an event's name narrows it to one.
  • The generic Tournaments shelf is hidden for money in, inside an event's own book only. It stays for money out (fees the club pays to enter other tournaments) and everywhere in the general book.
  • Left alone on purpose: the CLUB and STANDARD tags, the field's look, the create-a-word row, the rule that a saved entry's own word always stays listed.
