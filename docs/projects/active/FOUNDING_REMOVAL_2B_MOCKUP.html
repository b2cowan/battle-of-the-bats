1The Story's old lines.

Today the two old lines read "Founding Season: Tournament free through…". That plan is wrong: 2a lowered the stored plan so free time could lift it. The recommendation is to let these old marks read the way every other comp period in the Story reads. Two things come with that. The Why column keeps the word. Two dates sit side by side: the old mark reads "until Oct 1, 2027" and the free time "through Sep 30, 2027", for the same end. Ask C.

2"Comped clubs not counted".

It counts clubs with any running comp-period override. Founding was the only thing creating those, so it is zero once the 2a step runs. Free time never enters monthly revenue, because the club's own plan is the $0 one, so a free-time version would also always say zero. Recommend removing it from Today and Business numbers; /accounting confirms. Ask D.
