The rules, on the page and on paper

Stage 10 gives the public Rules page numbered sections and a “Download rules” button that opens a letter-size PDF: the club’s or organizer’s look, a stamp saying when it was made, a code to the live page, the sections and points as the page shows them, and the downloads written out. You walk it as a visitor on a computer and a phone, read the file, narrow it to a division, check each look, try the refusals, and look at the organizer’s preview.

Before you start

  • The test clubs are already built on dev (three organizations: s10walk-club, s10walk-plus, s10walk-free). To rebuild them: node .probe/s10-rules-walk-fixture.mjs setup. To remove everything this walk made: node .probe/s10-rules-walk-fixture.mjs undo.
  • Walk them in order. Walks 4, 5 and 8 change something and put it back in their last step.
  • Open the PDFs in your browser’s own viewer (Chrome, Edge or Safari). They open in a new tab.
  • Steps marked “optional” are skippable: a printer or a phone camera. Skipping one doesn’t stop a Pass.

Already proved by tests and my own pass (don’t re-do these)

  • The refusals under pressure. A held-back division’s section never prints, whichever way it is asked for: View all, a made-up saved division, a cookie naming the held-back division, or a garbage cookie. A tournament that is a draft, a Rules page switched off, a cancelled club and another club’s slug are all “Not found”.
  • If the divisions can’t be read (a database blip), the file says it couldn’t be prepared instead of printing a held-back section. Found and fixed in the review.
  • Odd characters. An emoji or a non-Western letter used to garble its whole line in the file; now accents fold to their plain letter and anything with no Western form is left out. Found and fixed in the review.
  • A very long section (100 points, 1,000 characters each) pages without losing or repeating a point. A logo that can’t be read prints no logo and the download still works.
  • Layout. At 361, 768 and 1440 pixels the narrowed page has no sideways scroll and no tap target under 44 pixels. The “Division:” bar’s own two low-contrast spots are the known old styling, listed under “Later · Light-frame touch-ups”.
  • The 29 PDF documents the product makes still pass their check, with the Rules file among them.
