House league money, honest

Written 2026-10-25. One purpose: the money on a house-league registration is told the truth everywhere — marked received with a date and how, refunded as money going back, kept with a reason, corrected with a reason — and every figure (Registrations, Money, the Overview, Families, the status page, and on the Club plan the Budget vs. Actual Statement) agrees with the book.

What this walk is for

Drawn on the 9b drawings, tab "9b-2 Money"; words in HOUSE_LEAGUE_STAGE9B2_COPY.md. Your rulings apply (plan §0e): a refund is its own line going out; an ending asks what happened to the money, with nothing picked; Undo is for a payment that never came; a corrected price on a paid registration refunds a lower price and leaves a higher one owing; a child who has paid keeps their price on a move; old registrations have no day count; Withdraw emails only when money moved.

It passes when every row shows its money in one of the drawn states, every money press records exactly one honest line (or refuses in plain words), no ending loses money silently, the Money band adds up to the rows, and on the Club plan the fees row on Budget vs. Actual equals the season’s Money page.

Not in this walk: online payment and the "no longer payable" page (Stage 12b), partial refunds, teams on the status page (9c), the season email composer’s audiences (9d), the family’s email of G1 (§375, joins the end-to-end payments test). Two known gaps, not issues: a child who holds a spot can’t be moved into a FULL division to join its waitlist (only a waitlisted child can), and the Statement’s season sub-row has no link to that season’s Money page.
