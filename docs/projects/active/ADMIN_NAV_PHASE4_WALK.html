Navigation across the three portals · Phase 4, the Coaches Portal on a computer · Owner QA Ledger §514

On a computer, a team cell heads the sidebar and the top strip names the page

On a computer, the Coaches Portal sidebar is now 240 wide and never folds its groups. It opens with your team: its crest (or its colour, or its initials), its name and season; with more than one team, a click on it lists them. The team bar above the page is gone. The strip at the top says “Coach”, then the name of the page you are on (one level down into a record: “← back” and where it goes), and the team’s facts at its right end (club, season, record, the Game day chip). The page’s own title row loses its title where the strip already says it; the “?” is last, a page with only a “?” keeps it at the right, and the scouting reminder is a slim notice under that row. A closed season is named in the strip (“2025 Season”) with one door in the sidebar. The phone is unchanged. Eight walks, one purpose each. About 35 minutes.
