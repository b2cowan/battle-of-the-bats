A Premium coach, a shared link, and a second team

What a coach who already has a team meets on a tournament’s registration page, in the order they meet it. Today’s screens are drawn from the code; the evidence is a read-only production lookup of the coach who reported it.

What happened

The coach owns one live Premium Coaches Portal, Milton Bats U13 Purple. Under every Premium team sits a free team that the registration form reads. His is the free team he made in June for last season’s Battle of the Bats, and it still carries that team’s name, Milton Bats U13 White.

The stations

Organizers share registration links in WhatsApp and Facebook groups. Those apps open links in their own browser, which does not share the phone’s sign-in. A coach who is signed in on Safari arrives signed out.

This is the main road in, not an edge case. Any fix that only helps a signed-in coach misses most of them.

Register Your Existing Team — PM brief

What changes for the coach

A coach who already has a team on FieldLogicHQ registers that team for a tournament instead of making a new one. The registration form starts by asking for their email. If they have an account, they sign in right there and their team is offered first, under the name their Coaches Portal shows, already chosen. Making a separate team is still possible, and the form says plainly what that means. After they submit, the tournament shows up in their Coaches Portal.

On a Premium team, the head coach and the team manager can register the team. The head coach can give that to anyone else on staff from the Staff page, one switch per person, and whoever holds it also sees and looks after those entries on the team's Tournaments page. Staff without it see the team on the form with who to ask, rather than an empty list.

Why it matters

On 2026-10-13 a Premium coach registered for a tournament through a WhatsApp link and ended up with a second, separate team. The form offered his Premium team under an old name he didn't recognize, so he made a new one. His Premium portal now can't see that tournament: not on its schedule, not on its Tournaments page, and roster submission reads the wrong team.

The way in is the problem. Organizers share registration links in group chats, and chat apps open links in their own browser, signed out. Today's form treats every signed-out visitor as new, and only finds out who they are when they press Submit.

Customer impact

  • Every Premium coach who registers from a shared link. They are our paying coaches, and the tournament is the first thing their portal should light up with.
  • Every assistant coach and team manager on a Premium team. Today none of them can register their own team.
  • Organizers get entries linked to the coach's real team, so roster submission and the coach's schedule work.
  • Doing nothing means more stray teams, more Premium portals that don't show their own tournaments, and support fixes by hand.

Priority

High, and partly now. Two small fixes (the right team name on the form, and Sign in returning to the page) can ship ahead of the redraw, and the affected coach's entry can be moved today. The redraw follows once the drawing is ruled.

Success criteria

  • A Premium coach who opens a registration link signed out registers their existing team without being sent back to the form.
  • No new free team is created for a coach who has a Premium team, unless they choose "A different team".
  • A team manager on a Premium team can register the team with no change from the head coach.
  • Every new entry by a Premium coach appears in their portal's Tournaments page the moment they submit.

  • Re-check before writing: the entry has exactly one coach-side link, to the stray team; the target portal is live and standalone and the person is on it; the stray team is otherwise empty (players, events, fees, announcements, other entries), or the fix stops; the target team is not already in that tournament (one team, one entry, new since A5, and the move must refuse rather than make a duplicate).
  • Moves with the one link, because each of these reads through it: the portal's Tournaments page and the entry's record; the team notices; roster submission, which then reads the portal's free team (the Part D deferral; a stray's own roster would be left behind, which is why "empty" is a precondition); the tournament coach chat, whose seats are re-synced from the link, so the portal's coaches join at the next sync. The sync never removes anyone, so the stray team's members keep their seat. That is harmless in A5's shape, where they are the same coach, and a decision to make when they are not.
  • Stays, because it is the organizer's: the entry itself (its name, division, status, payments, slot, check-in, the roster already sent), its contact email (the claim key), and the coach and coach email recorded at registration. A moved entry keeps the head coach it was registered with; the coach changes it on the portal's tournament page. Any claim rows for the entry stay as they are.
  • Afterwards: the stray team and its membership are removed only when empty, as A5 did.
