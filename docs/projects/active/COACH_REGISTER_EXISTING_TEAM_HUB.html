Product-manager brief

Register Your Existing Team — PM brief

What changes for the coach

A coach who already has a team on FieldLogicHQ registers that team for a tournament instead of making a new one. The registration form starts by asking for their email. If they have an account, they sign in right there and their team is offered first, under the name their Coaches Portal shows, already chosen. Making a separate team is still possible, and the form says plainly what that means. After they submit, the tournament shows up in their Coaches Portal.

On a Premium team, the head coach and the team manager can register the team. The head coach can give that to anyone else on staff from the Staff page, one switch per person. Staff without it see the team on the form with who to ask, rather than an empty list.

Why it matters

On 2026-10-13 a Premium coach registered for a tournament through a WhatsApp link and ended up with a second, separate team. The form offered his Premium team under an old name he didn't recognize, so he made a new one. His Premium portal now can't see that tournament: not on its schedule, not on its Tournaments page, and roster submission reads the wrong team.

The way in is the problem. Organizers share registration links in group chats, and chat apps open links in their own browser, signed out. Today's form treats every signed-out visitor as new, and only finds out who they are when they press Submit.

Customer impact

  • Every Premium coach who registers from a shared link. They are our paying coaches, and the tournament is the first thing their portal should light up with.
  • Every assistant coach and team manager on a Premium team. Today none of them can register their own team.
  • Organizers get entries linked to the coach's real team, so roster submission and the coach's schedule work.
  • Doing nothing means more stray teams, more Premium portals that don't show their own tournaments, and support fixes by hand.

Priority

High, and partly now. Two small fixes (the right team name on the form, and Sign in returning to the page) can ship ahead of the redraw, and the affected coach's entry can be moved today. The redraw follows once the drawing is ruled.

Success criteria

  • A Premium coach who opens a registration link signed out registers their existing team without being sent back to the form.
  • No new free team is created for a coach who has a Premium team, unless they choose "A different team".
  • A team manager on a Premium team can register the team with no change from the head coach.
  • Every new entry by a Premium coach appears in their portal's Tournaments page the moment they submit.

2. Findings

F1 — The form opens as a new-team form for a signed-out coach. Its first box is a blank Team Name and its last is "Create a Password — this sets up your free Coaches Portal". The only hint for a returning coach is one grey line under the password box. Links shared in WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and mail apps open in the app's own browser, which does not share the phone's sign-in, so most coaches arrive signed out.

F2 — The account is recognized last. The email is checked only when the coach taps Submit on the Review step. A match signs them in and sends them back to the form to pick a team, then Review and Submit again. A wrong password ends on an error card.

F3 — The picker names a Premium team by the free team underneath it. The picker lists the account's free teams and labels a Premium team's twin with the twin's own name. The twin is named once, when it is made, and never follows the portal. This is what happened to this coach.

F4 — "New Team" gives no warning. Choosing it makes a separate free team that the Premium portal never sees. Nothing on the form says so.

F5 — Only the portal's owner sees the team. The twin has one member, the portal's primary owner. A head coach who took over the team, a team manager or an assistant gets an empty picker and can only make a new team.

F6 — Sign in loses the form. The public bottom bar's Sign in sends the coach to sign in and then to their own portal, not back to the registration they were on.

F7 — A misfiled entry cannot be moved. Once an entry is linked to a team it stays linked. The coach has no way to move it, and the organizer side links only to its own club's teams.

5. Stage 1 — what the coach sees

Step 1, your email. The form opens with one question. If the email already has an account, the next screen asks for the password right there (with Forgot password). If not, the coach goes on to today's form, with the email already filled in.

Step 2, which team. A signed-in coach sees the teams they can register, as cards. A Premium team is listed first, under the name the portal shows, with a Coaches Portal tag, and it is already chosen when it is the only one. "A different team" is last and says plainly that it makes a separate team the portal won't show. Below the cards: the name the team goes by in this tournament, the division and the organizer's own questions.

Staff and D1. Staff who hold the permission see the team like the head coach does. Staff without it see the team greyed with who can register it, so they never mistake the empty space for "I have to make a new team".

Step 3, review. Says which team the entry joins and that the tournament will show in that portal.

A coach who is already signed in (the same browser, or arriving from their portal) starts at Step 2.

Staff sheet. A new switch, Register for tournaments, sits under Run tournaments in the Sensitive group. The head coach always holds it. The Team manager role starts with it on; Assistant coach, Team treasurer and Helper start with it off. It applies to Premium teams only (a club's teams are out of scope).

6. Asks

A1 — Email first, or a sign-in door above today's form? Recommend email first. A door only helps a coach who reads it; this coach typed a new team name into the first box. Email first finds the account before any team name is typed. It reveals nothing new: today's form already tells anyone at Submit that an email has an account. Cost: a brand-new coach answers one extra screen.

A2 — May the coach edit the name the team goes by in this tournament? Recommend yes, filled in with the portal's name. This coach typed "Milton Bats Purple - Jackson", which may be how the organizer asks teams to be named. Today, picking a saved team replaces whatever was typed with the saved name. The portal keeps its own name either way.

A3 — Do existing team managers get the switch? Recommend yes. The 09-13 ruling that managers are not backfilled was about Manage staff, the master key, which is never widened by default. Registering for a tournament is not a master key, and without a backfill your "default to team manager" is false on every team that exists today, this coach's included.

A4 — Ship the two small fixes now, ahead of the redraw? Recommend yes. The picker naming the portal's team correctly would have saved this coach on its own, and Sign in returning to the page is a one-line change. Neither needs a drawing.

A5 — Move this coach's entry to his Premium team now? Recommend yes. It is one entry and an empty stray team. The organizer's entry does not change (same team name, same status); only the coach side moves. Then remove the empty stray team. A data fix on production, done only with your OK.

7. Design review of the Stage 1 drawings (/design, 2026-10-13)

Checked against the written rules and the decisions log. The in-repo design system, principles and Milton palette files named by the design agent do not exist; the review used the decisions log and the product's own styles.

Applied to the drawings before hand-off (proposals until ruled):

  • Input text is 16px on a phone. The drawing had 15px; the consumer rule (2026-07-13) is 16px so iOS does not zoom on focus.
  • "Change" and "Not you?" sit in a row at least 44px tall, the consumer tap floor.
  • The greyed staff card names the people who can register, "Brett Jackson (head coach) or Alex Morgan (team manager)", instead of "your team manager" (a team may not have one) and the switch's name (staff-sheet wording on a public page).
  • Drawing notes inside the staff sheet read as notes, not product copy, and the new row's green band is labelled as the drawing's highlight.

Checked and kept:

  • Team cards, not a dropdown. The 2026-08-22 ruling that a pick-one field is a dropdown exempts rows that carry a sentence under each option. These do, and the sentence under "A different team" is the fix for F4, so it must be visible without opening anything.
  • The greyed card for staff without the switch. The 2026-10-01 ruling keeps locked rows off everyday menus but keeps them on a page someone surveys, where "is this missing, or just not mine?" needs an answer. A registration form is that kind of page.
  • The organizer's purple on the buttons and the chosen card, as today's form uses the event's theme colour.
  • Title Case buttons ("Continue", "Sign In", "Review Registration") match the public form as built.
