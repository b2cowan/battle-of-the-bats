Record GST/HST on what you buy, and see the credit each purchase earns

Written 2026-11-06 for the Stage 4a closeout (Sessions A–E). It runs on your own Bookkeeping, on the development store, against the build as committed. The automated tests prove the credit rules row by row; your ticks prove what you see and type.

What this walk is for

An expense now says which tax it paid (GST, HST at its rate, PST, QST, other), the supplier’s GST/HST number, how the supplier is registered with CRA, and the kind of purchase. From those, the form and the record show the credit on your GST/HST return in one sentence with its reason. You never type a credit. A receipt of $100 or more with no supplier number holds its credit and shows in Needs attention, and saving is never blocked. Reports shows the tax you paid, by type, never a credit. The registration date is a setting you can change, and every credit follows it.

It passes when

  • Each row of the drawing’s credit table gives the figure and the sentence the walk names.
  • A missing number goes round the full loop: saved without it, listed in Needs attention, opened at the number, added, and the credit counts.
  • Simplified sellers, a BC two-tax receipt and an expense saved before this change all read as drawn.
  • Reports’ Purchase tax paid shows the full tax by type, each row opens its expenses, and the export follows it.
  • Moving the registration date to May 1 gives a June service its credit, and moving it back takes the credit away again.

Not in this walk

  • The GST/HST return worksheet, “Before you file” and Mark as filed. They are Stage 4b.
  • A deemed credit on equipment you held when you registered. The form never works it out; it says to ask your accountant (C5 reads that sentence).
  • The production Bookkeeping store. There isn’t one yet (Bookkeeping Stage 5).
  • Two tabs changing the registration date at the same moment. The tests cover it; the second save is told the date changed.

Before you start

Where
http://localhost:3000/platform-admin/bookkeeping, signed in as yourself (the Bookkeeping allowlist). The dev server is already running this build.
Prefix
Every supplier in this walk starts with G4A-. Reports’ Find box narrows every figure to the walk’s own expenses with it.
Dates
Today’s date for every expense except the June service in Part K. On Reports keep a period that includes today (This month is fine).
Receipts
None needed. Leave each receipt empty; “Receipt missing” in Needs attention is expected and not part of this walk.
Numbers
Any made-up number works, for example 812345678RT0001. The form stores it as printed.
Registration date
It should read September 26, 2026 when you start (A1). Part K changes it and puts it back.

Calls to make as you go

  • Not checked keeps its reason (H2). Changed today on /review: the “Counted for now…” warning is added after the business-use sentence instead of replacing it, so a part-use figure always says why.
  • Equipment bought before the registration date is amber (C5). Changed today: it asks you to see your accountant, which is an action, so it wears amber. A service before the date stays neutral.
  • The quiet lines under Purchase tax paid (J3). As you ruled: the full-tax sentence, and no pointer to a return view until 4b builds one.
  • Change date’s window (K3). It uses the shipped question with two quiet answers, “Keep it” and “Change date”, not the drawing’s lime button.

A held credit’s closed row says “credit $x held”. Copy as new of a “Type not set” expense refuses at Save until you choose a type.
