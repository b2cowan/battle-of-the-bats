Telling families their practice times

Written 2026-10-31 for Stage 9d-3, Session 1 (Parts 1–4). One purpose; sign it off on its own. Walk B (the coach's line, copy email and Subscribe) comes after Session 2.

What this walk is for

Handing out practices emails nobody. The step after it asks “Tell the families?”, and nothing goes until Send is pressed. A second hand-out for a team whose families were already told reopens the same step as a change, for exactly the teams whose practices differ. This walk reads that step, its email (through “See an example”; dev has no email key) and the history it leaves.

It passes when

  • The amber line under the practice time says how many teams’ families haven’t been told, and opens the step.
  • The step’s numbers match what the fixture printed. The example email lists every date with its place on each line, and has no link and no button.
  • “See them” names the families who won’t get it, and fixing or adding an email brings them in.
  • After a send the line is gone, and the season’s Email families history has a Practice times sent: U9 Rookie row.
  • Handing out again with nothing changed says the told teams don’t change and sends nothing. Handing out again with a change sends the change email (Now / Was) to the told teams.
  • A send mixing a changed team and a never-told team gives a family on both one email with both parts.
  • A registrar sees none of it.

Not in this walk

  • Real mail. Dev has no email key, and on dev a household the transport skipped counts as reached. So “Reached 9 of 9” is right on dev; a real partial send is read on the first production send.
  • The words of the moved-child email. It has no preview on dev; the unit test pins them, and they are read on the first production move. Part F checks only that the move does not bring the reminder back.
  • The club’s own Send the changes refreshing a told team (it needs a club schedule send; it is code-checked).
  • The coach’s team-page line, the coach’s copy email and the house-league coach’s Subscribe: Session 2, walk B.
  • The season-wide “not told” total beside the season’s other waiting counts. It is not built yet; only the division’s line is.
