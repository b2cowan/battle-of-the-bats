Support console, stage 1 · the warm foundation · drawn 2026-10-24

Support Console Stage 1

Stage 1 makes the platform admin look and behave like the rest of FieldLogicHQ. It does not change what any screen does. Every drawing is at true size (1440 pixels wide, a desk screen) and on a narrower window it scrolls sideways inside its own box. Before is a capture from today's dev server. After is drawn with the product's real warm and dark colours. Blue tags say what changed and why, and each opens on click. Use the Warm / Dark switch at the top to flip every drawing. Your five questions sit beside the drawing each one decides.

WHAT STAFF WILL SEE

The same console, in the product's look

  • What changes. The dark neon console becomes the product's admin look: a top strip with the FieldLogicHQ mark and an account menu, a menu that folds, a standard page title, white buttons with one lime action per screen, and normal type instead of all capitals in a typewriter face.
  • Nothing moves. Every menu entry keeps its place under today's group names, and every button stays where it was. A support person who knew the console yesterday finds everything where it was.
  • Warm or Dark. The console follows the same Warm / Dark setting as the rest of the product, warm by default, switched from the account menu.
  • The sign-in page wears the same look. It is always warm, like every other signed-out page (question 5).
  • Left for Stage 2. Find, the menu regrouped by job, Today in place of Overview, and the customer record with its Story. Overview and the club record get a colour pass only, so they sit in the new look without being rebuilt.
BEFORE THE DRAWINGS

Five things the code and the rules showed that the build prompt did not say

  • The shared account menu does not fit a staff member. It always carries Notification settings and Account settings, which open the customer account area, and Send feedback, which would send feedback to our own inbox. The prompt asked only for a new sign-out destination. The menu needs a second change to hide those rows. This is your question 3 below.
  • Overview and the club record cannot be left alone in Warm. Their stylesheets lean on the product's dark colours. Most of them turn warm on their own, but the pale green, red and amber that mark good, bad and caution on a dark ground do not, and on cream paper they would be hard to read. A colour-only pass fixes it without touching their layout. This is question 4.
  • Help leaves the menu body. The navigation standard puts Help at the foot of every menu, so the System group keeps only Dev tools, which exists only on dev. On production that group disappears. Help can open in place (question 2) instead of a new tab.
  • The sign-in page cannot follow a Warm / Dark setting. The build prompt said it should, but a signed-out visitor has no setting, and the rule since 2026-10-23 is that every signed-out page is warm and signing out resets the browser to warm. So the sign-in is always warm. This is your question 5.
  • The private finance group is titled "Private finance", not "Bookkeeping". Bookkeeping is its only entry, and it still shows only for the owner's email allowlist. Nothing about who sees it changes.

Also noted: the console names the paid coaches plan two ways today, "Coaches Portal" on Organizations and Plans & pricing and "Premium Coaches Portal" on Overview. The drawings keep each screen's own word, because Stage 1 changes no words. The plan facts doc says the paid plan is the Premium Coaches Portal, so the words pass in Stage 2 should settle on that. Also noted: the account menu's Appearance control is a two-way segmented row. That is shared with the coaches portal and is not one of the console's form choices, so rule D5 does not reach it. It stays as it is.

Question 1 · What does the strip call the portal, and the browser tab?

Recommended: "Support console". It names the job the console does, and it is what the end-state drawings already showed. Staff and the code say "Platform admin". The tab would read "Support console — FieldLogicHQ". The word is yours.

Question 2 · Does Help open in place, or in a new tab?

Recommended: in place. Every portal opens its help inside itself, and it keeps the menu on the left so you can go back to what you were doing. Today it opens in a new tab with no menu. In place also means the System group no longer holds Help.

Question 3 · Sign out, and which rows the account menu shows staff

Recommended: both, as drawn. (1) Sign out returns to the console's own sign-in page, not the customer sign-in the menu sends everyone else to. (2) The three customer rows are hidden in the console. This changes the shared account menu, which the coaches and club portals also use: one new sign-out destination and one switch to hide the rows. Nothing changes for them.

Question 5 · The sign-in page: always warm, or follow a saved Warm / Dark choice?

Recommended: always warm. A person who is not signed in has no Warm / Dark control, and your 2026-10-23 rule makes every signed-out page warm and resets the browser to warm on sign-out. A Dark sign-in would only appear in a browser that never signed out, which is rare and looks like a bug. The build prompt said the sign-in should follow the setting, and that was written before this rule was applied to it. Once signed in, the console follows the person's own choice.

Question 4 · Overview and the club record: a colour pass, or leave them rough until Stage 2?

Recommended: a colour-only pass, no layout change. Most colours on these two screens turn warm on their own. The pale good, bad and caution colours do not, and they would read faintly on cream paper. Fixing the colours is a small job, and Stage 2's rebuild is not wasted by it, because that rebuild replaces the layout, not the palette. The alternative saves a few hours now and leaves two screens looking unfinished in Warm until Stage 2.

SPECIMEN F

Below tablet width: the menu still opens, nothing scrolls sideways

This is not a phone design. Your ruling is desktop only, with Status and Today readable on a phone in later stages. Stage 1 only has to keep two things true on a narrow window. The menu opens from a button in the strip and sits on top of the page, with the touch sizes the navigation standard gives a phone (16 px rows, 44 px tall). A wide list becomes cards, as Organizations already does, and the page itself never moves sideways. The drawing is a 390-pixel phone.
