Owner QA walk · Online payments · Real money

The first real club’s first real payment: the club connects Stripe, a real payer pays, the money reaches its bank

Rewritten 2026-11-24 (first written 2026-11-23 around your own club and card). One purpose: prove, once, on the live site, that a real club can connect its own Stripe account, that its first real payment shows everywhere it should, and that the money reaches the club’s bank. You watch and check our side; the club does the club’s part; a real team or family pays. This is the run-through that closes the Canadian launch. 35 steps.

What this walk is for

Every earlier walk of online payments ran in Stripe’s test mode on the dev site. This one runs on production with real money, and it has to be real business: Stripe’s own testing guide says its Services Agreement prohibits testing in live mode with real card details, so you do not pay a club of your own with your own card. Instead you sit with the first real club while it connects, and you follow its first real payment through our records to its bank.

The things only a real run can show are the closest looks: Stripe’s live onboarding, whether bank debit is switched on for a live club, whether Stripe’s news of the payment reaches us, and the payout itself.

It passes when
  • The Payouts card goes from “Not set up” to “Taking payments” with no “Test mode” anywhere, and records that the club’s owner agreed to the current Payments Terms.
  • The first real payment shows on the club’s record with a receipt number and no test tag, and the club gets the “Payment received” notification.
  • FieldLogicHQ’s two Connect destinations in Stripe show the payment delivered and no failed deliveries.
  • A payout appears (on Accounting › Payouts, or in the club’s Stripe dashboard) and the club confirms the matching deposit reached its bank.

Before you start

Setup, not checked. Keep this page private.

Where
The live site https://www.fieldlogichq.ca. Keep two tabs of your own ready: FieldLogicHQ’s own Stripe dashboard with the Live switch on (https://dashboard.stripe.com/webhooks) and your platform admin.
The club
A real customer club on Tournament Plus or higher (League Plus or Club if its families will pay), with a billing address in Canada outside Québec (a Québec address is refused; if the club is in Québec, this walk waits for another club). Its owner does Parts A to C on their own login: only the owner can connect payouts, and Stripe’s sign-in code will be asked for.
The time
Agree about 30 minutes with the club’s owner for Parts A and B, together in person or on a screen share. Part C happens whenever the club sends its first pay link and the payer pays; Part E runs over the following days.
What it costs you
Nothing. The money is the club’s real business: a fee a real team or family really owes. You never pay, never refund, and never type card or bank details.
The payment
Whatever the club charges first: a tournament team’s entry fee or a house-league family’s registration fee. Both paths are written into Part C. If the payer chooses bank debit, it takes up to 5 business days to clear; Part C waits for it.
Their private details
The club’s bank account, the signing officer’s identity and the payer’s card go on Stripe’s pages only. On a screen share, ask the owner to stop sharing while Stripe’s form is open. Write no personal or bank details in the notes.

Sign in as

Club owner
Their own production login (their email, their password, their two-step code) at https://www.fieldlogichq.ca/auth/login. You never sign in as them, and the UAT test logins do not exist on production.
You
Your platform-admin login at https://www.fieldlogichq.ca/platform-admin/login, and FieldLogicHQ’s own Stripe dashboard with the Live switch on.
Payer
The club’s real team contact or family, not you. They get the pay link by email from the club and pay on their own time.

Stop check

Settle first (an owner call, not a box): if sponsorship card payments are on production when this club connects, the sponsorship version of the Payments Terms must be published before Part A. The legal index holds it as owed “before the first production club connects”.

Then Part S, on your own. If either Connect destination is missing from FieldLogicHQ’s Stripe, do not book the club; tell me. If in Part A the Payouts card is missing, online payments are not switched on for production yet (or the club’s plan does not include them): stop there and tell me.

What this walk leaves behind: the club connected and taking payments (that is the point), one real payment on its record, and a payout in its bank. Nothing needs putting back.

If something goes wrong with money: the club’s owner stops, writes down what the screen said and the time, and you tell me before anyone presses anything again. If a payer says “Confirming your payment” was slow, they must not pay a second time: check the club’s record and Stripe first.
