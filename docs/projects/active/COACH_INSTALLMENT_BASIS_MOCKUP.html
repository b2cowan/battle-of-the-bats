Adding a date splits it again

In either split mode the amounts are a consequence, not an input. Press + Add, give the new row a date, and every amount recalculates — the same money divided across players and periods, which is the behaviour you asked for. Rounding remainders land on the last installment so each player's total is exact to the cent.

The trade, stated plainly. Right-hand frame: a coach who wants to charge everyone $400 can do it in four taps instead of being sent to build a budget first — and the budget nudge is still on screen, as a link rather than a wall. What this deliberately leaves alone

The safe replace

Re-running still replaces the schedule and still skips anyone who has already paid, naming them afterwards. The confirm step is untouched.

Who can do it

Still gated on permission to change team finances. Assistants without it never see the button.

Both doors, one sheet

Budget Plan and Player Dues keep opening the same sheet. Every mode works identically from either — see Call 1 for the one place they differ.

The over-planned warning

Line items above your estimate still show the existing red note. It sits above the new picker.

Everyone gets the same schedule

Per-player exceptions remain a Player Dues job, not something this sheet tries to do.

Preview before confirm

Two steps, always. Nothing writes dues in one click, in any of the three modes.
