Our billing records tell the truth about Stripe

Written 2026-11-01. Test mode only. Stage 0 changed almost nothing you can see, on purpose: it makes what we record about each customer’s plan true. This walk looks at the few places where that shows. Part S makes two fresh accounts, then come six short walks; sign each off on its own.

What this walk is for

Each walk has one job:

  • S · set-up: a fresh club and a fresh coach, each paying through Stripe, so nothing depends on an earlier walk.
  • B · a customer’s Plan & billing page prints what Stripe actually charges them.
  • C · on a club’s Money tab, staff see a real next charge, or a plain “None” when there is none.
  • D · when a plan’s price is replaced, customers still paying the old price are still recognised as on that plan.
  • E · when a bank disputes one of our subscription charges, you get an email and a line on the record, and nothing is suspended.
  • F · the billing portal opens on our own settings: no plan switching there, and cancel waits for the end of the period.
  • G · the new daily check emails you when Stripe and our records disagree, and only once a day.

It passes when

  • B and C: every comparison matches (the pass is “the same as Stripe”, never a number written here).
  • D: the plan stays recognised while the old price is retired, and no “price we do not know” email arrives.
  • E: two dispute emails in all, two Story lines, and the portal keeps working.
  • F: no plan change in the portal; cancel ends at the period end.
  • G: the email matches the outcome, the line you check is true, and a second run sends nothing.
