Family emails in the club's name

Built 2026-10-23. One purpose: every email a family gets wears its club: a band in the club's colour with the club's name, a logo or initials tile, a thin accent line, one line saying where a reply goes, and FieldLogicHQ as a quiet line at the foot. In the inbox the sender reads "[Club] via FieldLogicHQ".

What this walk is for

Fourteen emails are covered: Email families, Message this family, a coach's announcement, the house-league season email, the game-change notice, the dues reminders, the tryout receipt and the seven registration emails. You see them through the screens that show them (the two Families samples, the dues preview, the platform admin's template editor) and, if you want, in a real inbox in part F. The free-tier coach announcement and the generic View email are deliberately unchanged.

It passes when the club's name always reads on its band, the sample matches what the email will be, nothing scrolls sideways on a phone, and the reply line names the right place.

Not in this walk: the one-email-per-child change to the season email (its own stage, not built yet); the real mail clients are checked only in part F, which you may skip.
