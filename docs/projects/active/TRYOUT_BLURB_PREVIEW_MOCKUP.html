Tryout blurb preview

The “From the club” box on a team’s admin Tryouts page changes what families read on the public tryout page, but the club can’t see that page while typing. This sheet draws a small preview beside the box. It shows on a laptop-wide window only. A phone shows what it shows today.

Words, layout and colours inside the preview are the real public tryout page’s, drawn at phone width because that is where most families read it. The club colour here (navy) stands in for whatever colour the club saved. The “Preview as” buttons in drawing 1 are for this sheet only and are not part of the product.

Five calls for you

Ask 1How much of the pageRecommended: the title block, “From the club”, and the Sign up button.Not the back link, the tryout days or the contact line, which are not the club’s words. Drawing 3 compares the two sizes.
Ask 2Where it showsRecommended: right of the box, inside the Sign-ups card, on a laptop-wide window.When the Sign-ups card is wide enough for the box and a 360 px preview side by side (about 1,300 px and up with the sidebar open). Narrower, and on a phone, no preview and nothing moves.
Ask 3When it updatesRecommended: as the club types, before the autosave.A preview that lags the box would mislead. Type in drawing 1 to see it.
Ask 4It follows the page’s real stateOpen, Not open yet, or Closed.When families would not see the words (closed), the preview shows the page without them and says so once, in amber. The box’s own “when families read this” sentence steps aside whenever the preview already says it.
Ask 5Name“How families see it”, with “Updates as you type” beside it.Nothing in it can be clicked; the Sign up button is a picture.
