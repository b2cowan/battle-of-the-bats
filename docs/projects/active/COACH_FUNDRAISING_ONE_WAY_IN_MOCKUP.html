Coach money · fundraising, sponsors, the budget plan

Where fundraising money actually goes

It started as “why does the row say From a drive?” It ends as one rule the coach never has to be told: the category decides everything. Fundraising and sponsorship money is always recorded on the Fundraising tab; everything else is always typed in; and a line reports under the shelf it was filed on.

8 September 2026 Model agreed with the owner · nothing built Exhibits at true size, warm coach theme
The model
The category a word sits in decides who fills its number in and where it reports.
Fundraising · Sponsorship Always filled in from the Fundraising tab A fundraiser says which Fundraising line it is raising for; a sponsor says which Sponsorship line. The standard words and the team’s own, from that shelf only. Never typed into the money form.
Tournaments · Other Income · anything a coach invents Always typed in Recorded under “Other money in”, exactly like a cost. No drive or sponsor is ever involved.
On Budget vs. Actual A line reports under its category’s shelf Fundraising under Expected fundraising, Sponsorship under Expected sponsorship, everything else under Expected other income — grouped by category inside it.

What this replaces: a note on the budget picker telling coaches where the app would make them stand later; a refusal in the money form that fired only if a budget line happened to exist; and a whole design about blocked rows and “carry me there” switches. None of it is needed once fundraising money has exactly one way in.

What a coach can no longer do — said plainly

Fundraising and sponsorship money cannot be recorded without a fundraiser or sponsor record. A coach who ran a bake sale and banked $265 has to create a fundraiser called “Bake sale” first — three fields, pre-filled where it can be — and log the money to it for the whole team. That is one more step than typing it in. In return they get a record with a board and a date, the money lands on the line they planned, and the product has exactly one way for fundraising money to exist, which is what makes every screen above so short. I think the trade is right. It is a trade.

What ships with it — or the model isn’t honest

Five things the model implies that a build could skip and still look done.

1
Load-bearing

The whole-team entry, in the same release

It is the only way team-raised money gets in once the typed path closes. And the drive’s board must show which entries carried a family share, or a coach reads a missing credit.

2

A word added to Fundraising or Sponsorship is born a fundraising word

Today every coach-made word is born “typed”, which is why Bake sale money reports as other income. Under the model the shelf decides at creation — and finding five is fixed as a side effect. Zero words affected today; that changes the first time a coach adds one.

3
The one that bites

Existing drives and sponsors get their “Raising for” carefully, not by default

A team that budgeted only Merchandise sales sees their drive money on that line today. Default their drive to Fundraising drive and it jumps to an unplanned row while Merchandise goes blank. Rule: link an existing record to the line today’s report already places it on; fall back to the standard word only when there isn’t one.

4

Check production for money typed against the four words before removing them

Budget lines were measured last week and were clean. Typed arrivals weren’t. If any exist they need a home before the words leave the list — most likely re-filed onto a fundraiser or sponsor.

5

The coach demo’s narration and the help guides

Both describe today’s Record flow, including “Other money in” taking fundraising words. This is the surface that has gone quietly stale three releases running. Same unit of work.

Decided along the way

The model
The category decides everything. Fundraising and Sponsorship words are always filled in from the Fundraising tab; everything else is always typed; a line reports under its category’s shelf.
Other money in
Fundraising and Sponsorship words are removed from it, not blocked. The first question already routes.
The budget note
Comes off, and nothing replaces it. The coach is choosing a kind of money.
Raising for
Pre-filled with the standard word, filtered to its own shelf, changeable. Neither required nor nudged.
Grants
No sponsor-or-grant switch. “Raising for” is the difference. Three labels say “grant” where they didn’t.
Tournaments
Reports as other income, as its own category group. Settled, not assumed.
A coach’s own category
Reports as other income, stated at the moment a word is created, never asked as a question.

One call still open: is a whole-team entry allowed on a drive that has a family share percentage set? I recommend yes, with the board drawn as in screen F — the team row shows a dash where a share would be, and the facts line counts it separately.
