What the audit found

Cancelling already keeps the plan to the renewal date, live

It went out in the November 5 release, so the Terms’ biggest promise is already true. The kickoff and the draft both said otherwise; the draft’s notes are corrected. Three live Stripe settings still need reading (Lookups tab).

“I agree” has about eight doors, not one

Accounts are made by the club sign-up, the coach sign-up, the team sign-up, a staff invitation, the account a tournament registration makes for the coach, and the “follow this tournament” account. The last two never show a sign-up page. And the Terms only talk to people who buy plans. Invited coaches, volunteers and parents hold accounts but appear nowhere. That is decisions O5, O7 and O8.

The privacy policy is mostly true, and four sections need new sentences

New since the draft: one record per child across seasons, the full billing address kept on every tax record, the staff billing lists, and our own bookkeeping in AWS.

Deleting an organization leaves its uploaded files behind

That includes signed medical and waiver forms. I checked this one myself. Decision P7.

The biggest legal risk: no purchase-confirmation email

Ontario law says a coach buying a yearly plan must get a copy of the agreement within 15 days. If they don’t, they can cancel within 30 days. No email does this today. It’s step 5 of the work.

The payments terms can wait

Online payments are locked off in production twice, so no club can reach the placeholder text. They get written before you switch payments on.

What customers will see

  • One tick at sign-up: “I agree to the Terms of Service and have read the Privacy policy.”
  • A one-tick screen on their next sign-in, for people who already have accounts.
  • Terms and Privacy links in the site footer, in every portal’s account menu, on the account page and in our own emails.
  • A confirmation email for every purchase, with a copy of the Terms.

Stage 9 (help, demos, release)

  • Can start now: everything about the tax itself, including the address step, the tax lines, the demo check and the test-mode walk.
  • Can’t finish yet: any help line that quotes the Terms (refunds, cancelling, “what you agreed to”), and the first paid sale of a plan not yet on sale.
