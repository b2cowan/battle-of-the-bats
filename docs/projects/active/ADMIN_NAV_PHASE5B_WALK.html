The player page: tabs from the start, the name is the switcher

A player’s page now works like the other tabbed rooms at every width: its five tabs sit at the top from the moment the page opens, and the top bar names the player (on a computer, “← Roster │ Avery Test”). The summary card shows on Details only; its chips line keeps only the years with the team; a coach without money access gets three tiles across; on a phone the card is the dashboard’s rows. The “Switch player” dropdown row is gone: the player’s name with a small ▾ is the switcher — a list under the name on a computer, the roster sheet on a tablet or phone — and it keeps your tab and saves an edit you were still typing before it moves. “Off the roster” sits beside the name in the top bar. A long name wraps on a phone, and on a computer the team’s facts give way before the name is cut. Six walks, one purpose each. About 25 minutes.

Written Nov 12, 2026 against the dev site. Nothing here is real data: the club, the team and its players are test data. Figures quoted as “for example” may drift as other walks record attendance or dues; the pass condition never rests on them.
