The Money Overview when the season hasn’t started yet

Four numbered sections and six “nothing here yet” cards sit below the tab bar on a team that has set nothing up. This compares what ships today against three ways of cutting it.

What was already decided, and what wasn’t

The Money Overview redesign removed the 1 · Plan → 4 · Review card stack for exactly the reason you raised — it duplicates the tab bar, so the screen carried two navigation systems. But the removal was scoped to a season already running (dues generated, money moving). Setup seasons were deliberately left as they were, on the argument that the walk is useful onboarding.

The screenshot is a team at the setup stage, so it is behaving as designed. The question on the table is whether that carve-out should stand.

What’s actually wrong with it

The journey is told three times on one screen — once in the anchor card’s “Plan → Collect → Spend → Review” sentence, once in the tab bar, and once again as four headed sections. Four tiles report $0, $0, $0 and a dash. Nine of the ten blocks below the fold say some version of “nothing here yet.” The emptiest possible team gets the longest possible page.

Three ways to cut it

Same coach, same empty team

All three keep the anchor card — it is the one block doing real onboarding work.

A · Anchor + rail

Recommended

The four sections collapse into the one-line-per-destination rail the running-season dashboard already uses. The journey survives as the rail’s row order and three quiet step markers — orientation without four headings. Tiles stay hidden until there is money to report.

Your call

Four decisions

These are separable — you can take the first and leave the rest.

  1. Does the setup-season carve-out stand?

    If it does, nothing changes and the screenshot is the answer. If it doesn’t, the journey stack leaves the Overview at every stage.

  2. Which replacement — A, B or C?

    A is the recommendation: it is the only one where the Overview keeps one shape from a team’s first day to its last game, and it reuses what already ships.

  3. Do the four tiles hide until there’s money?

    $0 / $0 / $0 / — is four cards saying what the anchor card already said. Proposed: show them once any money has moved, which is the “budget built” render above.

  4. Does “What’s coming up” stay on a setup season?

    It is a 30-day forward look with nothing in any of its three columns until dues exist. Proposed: it appears when it has something to show, on both the setup and running views.
