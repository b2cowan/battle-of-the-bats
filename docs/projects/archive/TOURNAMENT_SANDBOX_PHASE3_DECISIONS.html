Decision D5 · open since 2026-08-03 — recommend option A

When a signed-in customer presses "See it live"

Today (option C) their own session is protected and they get the fan side only — the organizer's seat, which is the demo's whole argument, silently never opens for them. The people most likely to press this button late in a decision are exactly the people already signed in. Recommendation: option A — ask once, plainly.

Restyled
The screen itself already exists (built in Phase 1, currently hard to reach). What changes is when it appears: today a signed-in visitor's operator presses dead-end at the fan page; under option A they get this one honest question instead.
New
"Stay in my account — watch as a fan" returns them to the fan side with their session intact — declining is a first-class choice, not a dismissal. The question is asked at the press, each time an operator door is used without the demo session; in practice that is once.
Unchanged

When the switch is confirmed, tour steps 3–6 resolve to the real screens automatically (that fallback machinery already exists and is pinned by test).

Phase 3a · recommend BUILD — small, and it demos a differentiator

Seal the Season Opener into the Digital Ledger

The morning-after moment currently ends at the Post-Event Summary. Sealing the Opener gives that story a final beat the product is genuinely rare in having: a permanent, read-only public record. The org's Digital Ledger shelf stops being empty in the demo, and "the record keeps itself" becomes "…and it's sealed."

Unchanged The real Digital Ledger shelf (it exists today for every org) — what's New is a seeded record in it. The org's public home then shows its existing "Archives" door instead of hiding it.

⛓ Sealed ✓This tournament's final record is preserved in the Digital Ledger.View the sealed record →

New One line on the Post-Event Summary (the tour's closing screen) — the year ends with the paperwork done and the record sealed. Not a new tour step; the six-step tour is untouched.

The honest cost. A sealed record is immutable by design, but the demo re-anchors its dates daily — so the demo's nightly self-repair must also re-anchor the sealed record's dates, quietly rewriting a thing the product promises never to rewrite. That is fine mechanically (it is our fictional org and the same self-repair discipline as everything else) and invisible to a visitor, but it is the one place the demo's machinery bends a product promise behind the curtain. Named here so it is chosen, not discovered.

Phase 3c · recommend DEFER — it belongs to the coach sandbox build

The "See it live" chooser (organizer | coach)

When the coach sandbox exists, the one door serves two demos and a visitor picks their seat. Building the chooser now would mean shipping a door with a dead second card — the exact anti-dead-end rule this project is built on. The plan already assigns it to whichever sandbox ships second; this mock exists so that build starts with direction, not a blank page.
