Recommended, in one list

  1. The Round-robin generator’s window, two steps (A61): settings, then drafts. The lime is in the pinned foot of each step; nothing to scroll to.
  2. Who plays first (A62): one bracket, one per pool or tiers; then how many teams; then how they’re seeded, naming the division’s own pools. Formats in the public pages’ words. The jargon goes.
  3. The playoff plan is saved with the bracket, and nothing shows before it is (A63). Today families read “Top 4 advance · Single elimination” from a default nobody chose.
  4. Only the rules that act (A64): rest between a team’s games stays; four inert rules and the drafts dial go; new: the diamond the final is played on.
  5. A fit check before you generate (A65), in plain arithmetic, that warns and never refuses.
  6. Only drafts that differ, named by what differs (A66), read by round beside them (A67), with what saving does read first.
  7. A played game is never deleted (A68); a bracket with a played game keeps its shape.
  8. “Playoffs are set” waits for the moment it is true (A69). Today the first save anywhere pushes it to every following family.

Findings

  1. F93

    Two looks. The Round-robin generator was rebuilt on the kit’s window (walked §294); this one is the old modal: 700px wide, uppercase mono labels, numbered sections, a boxed ×, tinted panels, number steppers, 30px two-button switches, 28px time boxes, 16px boxes. 12 controls sit under the house’s 34px. Two limes on one screen: Generate a draft in a tinted panel and Save this bracket in the foot.

    Fixed by PG1–PG8: the sibling’s window and parts (A61).
  2. F94

    Generate is 1,578px down the scroll at a desk (2.4 bodies) and 2,194px on a phone, below a facility list of four boxes for four diamonds. When you press it the draft appears further down, out of sight, and the window widens from 700 to 1,360px.

    Fixed: Generate drafts in the pinned foot; the drafts are their own step (A61).
  3. F95

    The order and the words. It asks “Crossover Rules” before how many teams advance. Words a coach does not use: Crossover Rules, Global Reseed (Top vs. Bottom Seeding), Regeneration Scope, Playoff Timing, Turnover Time (min), Scheduling Priorities, Best of 24, Effort, Consolidation Game, BYE R1, “Playoff Draft Health”.

    Fixed: Who plays first; words from the round-robin window and the public pages (A62).
  4. F96

    Choices that can’t apply. “No Crossover (Each Pool Plays Own Finals)” is offered to a division with no pools; choosing it hides Teams advancing and shows an empty per-pool panel (U15 Open, above). “Standard (Pool A vs. Pool B Crossover)” names pools A and B; U11 Girls’ pools are Red and Blue.

    Fixed: a choice shows only where it applies, in the division’s own pool names (A62).
  5. F97

    The playoff plan families read is never saved. The public standings draw a cut line and say “Top 4 of 6 advance”, the Playoffs page says “Single elimination · Top 4 advance”, a team’s page says whether it is in a playoff spot, and the Bracket view’s caption reads “the top 4 of the round robin”. All four read the division’s saved plan. The generator never writes it: a division made in New tournament starts at “top 4, single elimination”, a default nobody chose, and keeps it whatever bracket you build. On dev today three brackets are labelled with the wrong format: two double-elimination brackets read “Single elimination”, and one single-elimination bracket reads “Double elimination”.

    Fixed: the plan saves with the bracket; nothing shows before it does (A63).
  6. F98

    Four of the six scheduling rules don’t act on a bracket. The engine counts each placeholder as its own team (“1st Pool Red”, “Semifinal 1 winner”), and each plays once, so “Max / day”, “Avoid back-to-back” and “Reduce facility moves” never bite. “Balance early/late” only keeps games off the day’s first and last slots. Only the minimum rest between rounds, and the number of drafts it tries, do what they say.

    Fixed: Rules keeps rest; the rest go; new “Final played on” (A64).
  7. F99

    Two scores for one draft. “Best of 24 playoff drafts · 93 · Health 93/100” sits above “Playoff Draft Health 100/100”. The bracket’s health only drops for a broken rest or for three or more games in one day for one team, and on the same days the bracket decides the second, not the draft. So every draft that keeps the rest rule on the same days scores the same: U11 Girls’ finals at 11:30 a.m., 12:30 p.m. and 2:15 p.m. all measure 100. A score can’t choose between playoff drafts.

    Fixed: cards name what differs, no score (A66).
  8. F100

    “No Playoff Draft Found” doesn’t say why. U13 Boys, all 8, double elimination on Sunday: the window answers “Try adding time, selecting more facilities, or lowering minimum rest.” The reason is arithmetic. The bracket is 15 games, 7 rounds one after another (counting the second final, if it’s needed): about 16 h 30 min with 90-minute games and 60 minutes’ rest. Sunday 9:00 a.m. to 8:30 p.m. holds 11 h 30 min, and more diamonds don’t shorten a chain.

    Fixed: the fit check says it before you generate (A65).
  9. F101

    Replace bracket deletes every playoff game and its score, behind a red “cannot be undone”. The defects pass (F70) stopped the Round-robin generator from ever deleting a played game; this one still can. Its other mode, “Build from current”, keeps played games but can mix them with a draft of a different shape.

    Fixed: a played game is never deleted; a played bracket keeps its shape (A68).
  10. F102

    “Playoffs are set” goes out on the first save, to everyone. The first bracket saved anywhere in the tournament sends staff “🏆 Playoffs are set: the seeding is locked and the knockout stage is on”, and sends every family following a team “🏆 The playoff bracket is set! See the seeding & who plays who”. It goes before anything is published, before the round robin is played (no seeding exists yet), and once per tournament, so the second division’s bracket says nothing. Walk 13 recorded the staff half as an open question; the family push is the wider half.

    Answered by A69.
  11. F103

    Dead branches. The window carries a free-plan lock and its sentence, but it never opens on the free plan (the Tools row shows the upgrade instead). It carries a “Start from standings” preset that nothing sets. “Missing Information · Continue Anyway” is reachable only from “Bracket structure only”, where missing times are the point.

    Removed at the build (no ask).
  12. F104

    Generate before the diamonds load drafts on made-up ones. Pressed while the diamond list is still loading, the draft uses “Playoff Facility 1, 2” with every real diamond ticked. Seen once in four captures.

    Fixed at the build: Generate waits for the window’s reads (no ask).
  13. F105

    The day list prints the browser’s own date (“Sat 17 Oct” on one machine, “Sun, Oct 18” on yours). The house’s is “Sun, Oct 18”, which the Round-robin generator uses.

    Fixed: the sibling’s Day field (no ask).
  14. F106

    A venue that is its own one diamond reads twice: “Lions Diamond - Lions Diamond” in the draft, “Lions Diamond / Lions Diamond” in the picker. Already on the follow-ups list; seen again here.

    Fixed: the sibling’s Where reads a one-diamond venue once (§294).

What the build carries without an ask, and what this doesn’t touch

The build carries (one answer each)

  • F103: the free-plan lock and its sentence, the “Start from standings” preset and the “Missing Information” question are removed.
  • F104: Generate drafts waits for the window’s reads (teams, diamonds, games).
  • F105, F106: the sibling’s Day field and Where.
  • The errors read in the window, as the sibling’s do: “There is room for 12 games and the draft needs 15…” in the error band, never a separate box with Close.
  • The save is one step: the removal and the new games either both happen or neither does (today it deletes, then saves).
  • A replace once teams are known (after the round robin has filled the seeds) is told the way the round robin’s is: a game that moves is told as a move.
  • One game length (A39) as built: the window starts at the division’s length and saves it on every game.

Not drawn, not changed

  • Build bracket (the free tool) and Edit bracket: names only, as Stage 3 ruled.
  • The Bracket view (S6) and the coin toss (S7): as built.
  • Club Stage 11: When, Where and the drafts frame carry the Round-robin generator’s “Shared candidate · Club Stage 11” tag; nothing here adds to the club’s scheduler.
  • Reseeding after every round (the highest seed left plays the lowest) is not offered today and is not proposed: no one has asked for it.
  • The tie-breakers stay on the division’s record; the seeding sentence names whose tie-breakers decide.
  • Found, not changed: three lists reorder the other way. Stage 5 built Rules’ sections, a section’s links and files, and Registration questions with Tools → Reorder (a mode with Move up / Move down on a phone), not the portal’s hold-and-drag with a tap for Move up / Move down. The seed list here follows the portal (your 12 Oct note); those three are yours to call.
