# PM Brief — One Money Book (fold Payables into Transactions)

**Status: ✅ APPROVED AND BUILT ON DEV 2026-08-28 — owner QA §119 owed; no migration.** All six
decisions taken with the owner's approval, including the tab name **Ledger** and the object word
**bill**. Plan (now also the engineering record, incl. §0's five recorded mockup deviations):
`COACH_PAYABLES_LEDGER_FOLD_PLAN.md`. Mockups: the "One Money Book" Artifact.

## What changes for a coach

Today money that has moved and money that is owed live on two tabs — Transactions and Payables —
even though they are two readings of the same records, and tapping a bill on either lands on the
same page. After this change there is **one book**: the Transactions tab, with a **View** switch —
**Timeline** (the register, unchanged, still the default), **By bill** (what you owe, grouped and
foldable), and **By due date** (the payment schedule with its month bands). The Payables tab
disappears; the tab bar drops from eight entries to seven. *Add a commitment* moves up beside the
page's other actions so it is reachable from any view. Old Payables links and bookmarks keep
working — they land on the right view of the one book.

## Why it matters

- One fewer place to learn; the tab bar stops implying the team has two money books.
- The owed-money reading stops being a destination and becomes what it really is — an arrangement
  of the book, beside the timeline it settles into.
- It removes a whole face from the upcoming money forms-review's scope before that session starts.

## What is deliberately NOT changing

The rows, statuses, folds, club-bill handling, both exports, the commitment's own page (the
self-saving record), the Record button and its rules, and the Timeline's quiet defaults. The
proposal is a relocation, not a redesign — the mockups label every element New / Moved / Unchanged
/ Retired, and only the tab itself is Retired.

## The trade-off, honestly

The tab bar today answers "what do we owe?" in one tap. After the fold that answer is one view
switch away — mitigated by remembering each coach's last view per team (decision 3 in the plan).

## Owner decisions required (in the mockup artifact)

Go/no-go · where the Add-a-commitment door lives · whether the view is remembered · whether the
tab keeps the name "Transactions" · the new wording of the future-date refusal.

## Success criteria

A coach can do everything on the one book they could do across the two tabs, with no retraining
beyond finding the View switch; every old link lands somewhere honest; help and the demo tell the
one-book story the day it ships.
