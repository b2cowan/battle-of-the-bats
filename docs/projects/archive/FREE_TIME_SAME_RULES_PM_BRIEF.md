# Free time: the same rules for clubs and coaches — PM brief

**What changes.** Two small fixes that make free time work the same way for a club and for a coach:

- **A super admin can give a coach free time with no end date**, or to any date, the way they already can for a club. Today a
  coach's free time stops at six months from the first give, even for a super admin. Billing and Support keep their 90 days.
- **A club whose free time is ending gets an email** two weeks before and again three days before, the way a coach already does.
  Today a club only finds out if someone opens the billing page in the last two weeks.

**Why it matters.** The three accounts that held the Founding Season now hold ordinary free time to September 30, 2027. The two
clubs were told they'd be reminded in the summer; without this, nothing reminds them. And staff can't extend the coach later
from the screens.

**Customer impact.** A club with free time hears about its end before it happens. A coach given open-ended free time sees no
"free through" date, as a club does today.

**Priority.** Later lane, ongoing quality. Built after the Founding code is removed, and well before September 2027.

**Success.** A super admin gives a coach free time with no end date; a club with free time ending gets its two emails; a grant
with no end date gets none.
