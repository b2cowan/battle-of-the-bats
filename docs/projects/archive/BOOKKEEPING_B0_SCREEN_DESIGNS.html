Bookkeeping screen designs

Four proposed views for recording and reviewing proprietor expenses. The examples are synthetic. These are screen designs, not live records or a promise that every control is built.

Why this exists: The first working page was built before these designs were shown. B0.4 remains open for your review. The proposed layout gives the register, entry form, record detail and reports separate space; the current page combines the form and report on one screen.
