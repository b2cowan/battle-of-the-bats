Product-manager brief

A team’s own payments

What changes for the club treasurer

When a treasurer bills several teams and lets one of them pay on its own schedule, that team’s payments read as a short list: one numbered line per installment, each with its date and amount under “Due date” and “Amount”, the same way a coach’s installment schedule reads. Today they are loose boxes with no labels that wrap across the row like words in a sentence, and on the cream row they are hard to see at all.

Why it matters

This is where a treasurer types money and dates for a team. With six installments, today’s row is twelve unlabelled boxes over four lines, and matching each date to its amount takes counting. A wrong pairing becomes a family being told the wrong amount on the wrong day.

Customer impact

  • Treasurers: clubs that bill teams through allocations see it whenever one team pays differently from the rest. That is occasional, but it carries the most money per keystroke in the window.
  • Every form box on a cream row becomes readable again, in this window and wherever else one appears. The colour problem arrived with today’s One Window change.
  • Coaches and families: nothing changes for them.

Priority

Small, and it rides with the Club release, which is not on sale yet. The colour part should go before One Window’s owner walk closes, because that walk would otherwise pass a screen with invisible boxes.

Success criteria

  • A team paying in six installments reads as six lines with no counting.
  • Every box on a cream row is visible at a glance.
  • The row and its dropdown use the same word for the schedule.

A team’s own payments · plan

Status. Drawn 2026-10-18 from the owner’s two screenshots of New allocation. A1–A3 ruled as recommended on 2026-10-18 and built on dev the same day, with the 36px field height from earlier in the session (Screen 1’s Pay by tag). Owner walk: §338 in the QA ledger, and the QA Walk tab here.
Design pass, 2026-10-18 (before hand-off). Ten changes, all drawn:
  • Rows numbered “#1, #2…”, the coaches’ installment-editor pattern, at both widths. The first draft said “Payment 1”, which brought back the second word A3 removes.
  • The question is an ordinary field label over its dropdown.
  • Required stars on “Due date *” and “Amount *”.
  • The amount box is 9rem, the ruled money width.
  • The warning sits under the list and wraps on a phone.
  • An empty amount box shows “$0.00”.
  • The Due words break only at the “·” in a narrower window.
  • An open row under By amount is drawn, so its share box’s colour change can be seen.
  • A2’s grounds are named, and it is Warm only.
  • The window’s Notes field is drawn.
The pass also found three places where the written rules disagree (Open points, below).

Findings

F01 — The boxes wear the row’s colour

One Window (committed 992243fb on 2026-10-18) made every admin form box the cream paper tone. An open team row and every group heading row are that same cream, so a box on them is only an outline. In Warm both are exactly #F8F4ED. The ruling never considered a box on a cream ground. The toolbar dropdown kept its white fill for this very reason.

F02 — A payment is two loose boxes that wrap like words

The label, the dropdown and every payment’s date and amount sit in one line that wraps wherever the width runs out. The approved drawing showed one small box per payment (“Nov 15 · $115.63”), which can’t be typed into, so the build split each payment into two unlabelled 144px boxes. At three payments the row takes three lines. At six it is twelve boxes over four lines. The label squeezes onto two lines, and amounts sit left, unlike every other figure.

F03 — Two words for one schedule

The Due cell says “Its own · 3 payments” and the dropdown directly under it says “Its own · 3 installments”. The dropdown’s words were ruled: the bill’s schedule leads and says so, and every other choice says it is the team’s own. The Due cell’s words were drawn separately, probably chosen because they fit the column.

Asks for the owner

A1 — One line per payment

Recommended. The coaches’ installment editor is the benchmark, and this copies it. “This team pays in” becomes an ordinary field label over its dropdown, like every other field in the window. Below it, column labels with plain required stars (“Due date *”, “Amount *”), since a grid’s columns are its labels and Create refuses a blank one. Then one line per installment, numbered #1, #2… in a narrow right-aligned column, with a 10.5rem date box and a 9rem amount box set right (the content-class widths ruled for money and dates). An empty amount box shows the window’s “$0.00” placeholder. The add-up warning and the way back (“Use the bill’s schedule”) sit under the list, and the warning wraps on a phone. The same grid serves a phone, with a narrower amount box. The screen reader names say “installment N” where they say “payment N” today.

Alternatives: keep today’s wrapping line and only fix widths, which still wraps from four installments up and leaves the boxes unlabelled. Or replace the red sentence with a sum line under Amount (“$37.09 short”), the way the team list’s closing row reports its difference. That is a bigger change and is not drawn.

A2 — A box on a cream row is white

Recommended. One rule in the form-table kit, Warm theme only: a box standing on a cream ground takes the white card fill. The grounds are named, not “anything cream”: an open row and a group heading row in the table, and an open record and a group band on a phone. A box on a white row keeps the paper tone, so the One Window ruling stands everywhere else. It is recorded as an amendment to that ruling (Q2, the field), and it has precedent: the toolbar dropdown already keeps the card fill because paper on paper is only an outline. Dark does not change, because there a box is already a lighter wash on a darker row. One consequence to accept: a team’s share box (By amount, By percentage) turns white while its row is open and cream again when the row folds (screen 3). Alternative: make the open row white instead. That loses the cream that ties the form to its team, and it doesn’t help the group heading rows.

A3 — One word for the schedule

Recommended. Due says “Its own · 3 installments” on a computer and on a phone, the dropdown’s ruled words. At the real font, “Its own · 6 installments” is about 159px and today’s Due column holds about 143px. The column goes from 22% to 26% of the table (about 190px), and the 4% comes from Team, whose names use about 215 of its 273px. Share is untouched, because By percentage needs its width for the box, the % and the dollars. Where the window is narrower than 800px (a screen between 641 and about 800px wide), the words break only at the “·”, onto two lines, rather than running into the arrow. Alternative: keep “payments” in Due and accept two words.

Open points from the design pass

  • Which style a column label inside a form wears. The written rules give five: the table standard’s condensed uppercase heading, the form table’s mono uppercase heading, the club kit table’s sentence case, the coaches’ installment editor’s small uppercase, and the field label. The drawing uses the field label (sentence case), because these words are the only visible labels the boxes have, and the One Window ruling made every field label sentence case. If that holds, the coaches’ uppercase installment headings are the odd ones out.
  • The cream grounds A2 relies on are not in the table exception register. The table standard says an item row is never tinted. The form table tints an open row and a group row cream anyway, and sets group names in small capitals. Whatever A2 rules, the register should record the form table’s grounds.
  • Olive text that acts inside a form (“All · None”, “Use the bill’s schedule”) is not covered by the 10-01 button ruling, which covers toolbars and window feet. It is drawn as built.

Scope

  • New allocation: a team’s own payments (open row and phone record), its Due cell and the Due column width.
  • The form-table kit, Warm only: boxes on its named cream grounds take the white fill. This reaches every form table, including the New tournament window’s Divisions step. The build starts with a census of every form table with a box on one of those grounds.
Out of scope

The dropdown’s choices and their words, which are ruled. Changing a bill’s terms after it is made, which is ruled out of 3d. Dark theme, where a box already stands off its row.

Seen while drawing, not in this plan: “nothing left over” in the closing row is meant to be quiet but renders bold ink, because its quiet style only applies inside a different table. It is a one-line fix for whoever owns the closing row.

Rollout

Screen and kit styling plus the window’s markup and one word. No data change and no migration. The demo sandboxes follow the build on their own. The in-app help article on allocations only says a team’s Due “then says so”, without quoting the words, so it needs no change.
