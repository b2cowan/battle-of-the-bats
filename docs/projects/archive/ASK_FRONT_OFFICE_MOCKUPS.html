Why the bar must not look like a text box: Phase A can't accept typing. A bar styled as an input invites a coach to tap and type, then do nothing — the one interaction the whole feature's honesty promise can't afford. So it reads as a bar with a “Choose a question” hint. Phase B drops the hint and the same bar becomes typeable, with no relearning.
