The bar across the top of the two no-login demos has buttons shorter than 44px on phones and tablets. Two ways to fix it are drawn beside today's bar. Computers do not change. Nothing is built yet.

Open this page on your phone to judge the sizes by thumb. "Show only what differs" draws a dashed box around the area that answers a tap. The coach demo uses the same bar in its warm colours and would change the same way.

The four step circles only show above phone width. In both options they sit further apart so each one has its own 44px. The "Walk it again" and "Back" buttons, which appear later in the tour, would follow the option you pick.

Which one should be built?

I recommend B. The bar keeps its look and costs the least screen, which matters on a demo where the product underneath is the point.
