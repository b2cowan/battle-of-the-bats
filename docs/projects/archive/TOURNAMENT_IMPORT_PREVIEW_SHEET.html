The import preview, as a summary

“Instead of showing row by row, wouldn’t it be better to show more of a summary? i.e. x amount of rows successful, x amount with errors (open to see the rows with errors and why), etc.? Also, is there a reason the heights of the button and dropdowns at the top row are different?” (owner, 12 Oct)

The proposal in one line: the preview becomes one frame of group rows, Can’t import · To add · To update · No change, each with its count. Only Can’t import opens by itself, because it is the only part you act on. Everything else is one click away.

  • The heading becomes plain “Preview”. The counts are said once, on the groups, and a group with nothing in it is not drawn.
  • A note many rows share (“Home Team matched by name…”) is said once on its group with its count. A note only one row has stays on that row, and those rows lead their group.
  • An added game shows when and where; an update shows the column as your file names it, and its old and new value.
  • The template row: one height (built in the working copy, not committed yet), and the format choice sized to its word, so the template choice stops cutting its own words off.

Findings

  • F1Every row is listed, one by one

    Today the preview lists the file’s rows twenty at a time, each with its verb and its first note. A file of 150 games is 150 rows to page through to learn three numbers, and the rows you can act on (the ones that can’t import) are the smallest part of it.

    Proposal: one frame of group rows with their counts; only Can’t import opens by itself (A1, A2).
  • F2Two heights in one row

    The template choice and the format choice were form fields (40px at a desk); Download template beside them is a button (34px). The “one control height” ruling puts a row of controls at 34.

    Built in the working copy: both choices take the toolbar height. Not committed yet (A5).
  • F3The template choice cuts its own words off

    “This event’s games, to update them” shows as “…to update then” at a desk: the format choice is 7.5rem wide for the word “Excel”, and the template choice gets what is left.

    Proposal: size the format choice to its word (5.5rem). The template choice gets about 30px back, enough for its whole label at 640 (A5).
  • F4One note on every row, and a second one never shown

    “Home Team matched by name. Team ID is safer for future imports.” is printed on every added game. The same rows also carry “Away Team matched by name…”, but a row shows only its first note, so that one never appears.

    Proposal: a note several rows share is said once on its group with its count, and the Home Team and Away Team notes, which give the same advice, are one line; a note one row has stays on that row, and those rows lead the group (A3).
  • F5An update names the column, not the change

    “Updates · Start Time” says something changed, not what to. The preview already holds the old and the new value of every change.

    Proposal: “Start Time: 9:00 a.m. → 10:30 a.m.”, the column as your file names it (A4).
  • F6The heading repeats the counts, including a zero

    “Preview · 2 to add · 0 to update · 1 can’t import” says each count before the list says it again, and names a group with nothing in it.

    Proposal: the heading is “Preview”; each count is said once, on its group; an empty group is not drawn.

Asks

  • A1The preview as group rows

    Can’t import · To add · To update · No change, in that order, each with its count at the right and a down chevron. Nothing else in the window changes shape.

    Recommended: yes.
  • A2Which groups open by themselves

    Only Can’t import, at every file size. A three-row file then costs one click to see its two good rows.

    Option B: open every group when the file has ten rows or fewer. A second rule to remember for one click saved. Recommended: only Can’t import, always.
  • A3Notes said once

    A note two or more rows in a group share is said once on the group row, with its count in front; notes that give the same advice (Home Team and Away Team matched by name) are one line. A note one row has stays on that row, and those rows lead their group, so a closed group still tells you it holds something to read (“2 rows have a note of their own, listed first.”).

    Recommended: yes.
  • A4What a row says when its group is open

    An added game: its day, time and field (“Sat, May 15 · 9:00 a.m. · Diamond 1”). An added team: its division. An update: each change as the column your file names it, with its old and new value (“Start Time: 9:00 a.m. → 10:30 a.m.”). A row that can’t import: why, in amber, as today.

    Recommended: yes. The words are /marketing’s to settle at the build.
  • A5The template row

    One height for the two choices and the button (built, F2), and the format choice sized to its word (F3).

    Recommended: yes; commit the height fix with this build.
  • A6All or nothing stays

    The coaches portal’s import windows import the good rows and leave the rest out. This one refuses the file until every row can import, and keeps doing so: a game with no Game ID in the file always imports as new, so after a partial import, fixing the one row and choosing the file again would add every other game a second time. Nothing to draw; recorded so the reason is the right one.

    Recommended: keep all or nothing, and correct the design log’s reason to this one.
  • A8On a phone, the window brings the preview up once a file is read

    On a phone the Preview heading sits about 610px down an 800px screen, under the template and file sections, so only its first group row shows. Once a file is read, the window scrolls the Preview to its top. Nothing moves at a desk, where the preview is already in view.

    Option B: leave it; the person scrolls. Recommended: scroll to it, on a phone only.
  • A7The coaches portal’s import windows stay as they are

    The budget and schedule imports there are short pastes a coach fixes in place, row by row, in an editable table. A summary would hide the cells being fixed.

    Recommended: leave them.

Formatting check

The drawings against the written rules (the table and list standard, the one control height, the window’s foot).

  • Group row (standard §2): larger than its items (14/700 over 14/600), the neutral wash, its figure right and tabular, a down chevron in the last cell, a real button that says whether it is open.
  • Items: indented one gutter under their group’s title; the row number is a narrow lead column at a desk (4.25rem, not the 10.5rem date column) and moves onto the caption’s line on a phone.
  • A status said once: the counts live on the groups only; the heading no longer repeats them, and the foot says only what the button would have said.
  • Colour never alone: amber carries “Can’t import” and its reasons, always in words, with the warning mark beside the group.
  • One lime: Import in the clean state; none while a row can’t import (the button is absent, the sentence stands in its place).
  • Heights: 34 at a desk for the template row, the file button and the foot; 44 on a phone for every control and every group row.
  • No link in a row: an item opens nothing; “Show all 120 rows” is the list’s own quiet door at its end.

/design review, 12 Oct: five changes, applied to these drawings

  • One line for one piece of advice. Home Team and Away Team “matched by name” were two lines on every closed To add; they are one (“Teams matched by name…”).
  • A mark only where it means something. A plus beside “To add” read as a create button (“+ U11” is one, elsewhere); only Can’t import keeps its amber mark, and the column stays on every group row so the titles align.
  • The row number is not part of the error. On a phone “Row 4 ·” moved onto the caption in amber; it is now in the dim ink, and only the reason is amber.
  • The standard’s hover. A group row tints with the olive hover every opening row uses.
  • The preview below the fold on a phone. Raised as A8 rather than drawn.
  • For the build, not the drawing: each group’s button names its group and count for a screen reader (the 2 Sep fold ruling); the layout sweep cannot see inside a closed group, so the import’s swept state opens one.
