## Owner screens and transaction meaning

The [four visual screen designs](../archive/BOOKKEEPING_B0_SCREEN_DESIGNS.html) are a proposed B0.4 flow for owner review. They separate the expense register, entry form, receipt/correction detail and report/export view. The current dev page is a combined first slice built before this review; the designs do not claim those separate screens have been implemented or approved.

The Bookkeeping page has an expense form and a report/register beside it. The owner records supplier, date, category, business purpose, invoice currency/amount, CAD amount, actual purchase tax, review status, business-use share, payment source, conversion evidence and receipt. Search and period/category filters show recorded business spend and purchase tax awaiting review. An expense can be corrected by giving a reason; a void also needs a reason. Each revision and its receipt is retrievable.

One expense identity has numbered immutable revisions; only the highest revision contributes to a report. A voided highest revision contributes zero but earlier revisions remain exportable. A simultaneous stale correction is rejected by the `(expense_id, revision)` unique key and must be retried against the latest record. Receipt upload precedes insert and is removed if the insert fails; receipt bytes are never overwritten.

Amounts are integer cents. The gross CAD amount includes tax. The recorded business spend is `round-half-up(gross CAD cents × business-use percent / 100)`; the personal share is excluded. Purchase tax is a memo amount; **no input tax credit is calculated or claimed**. USD invoices keep their original amount and a required conversion source/date note. A personally paid expense is a business expense with a prospective owner-contribution offset, not a bank withdrawal. Later reimbursement is a separate movement and must not book the expense again.

For the later reconciled journal, each accepted movement gets one stable identity and balanced debit/credit legs. Expense gross business share debits its expense category and credits business cash/card payable or owner contribution according to payment source. Tax recovery, assets, deferred costs and recognition timing are accountant-reviewed reclassifications; reports here show recorded spend, not taxable profit. A correction reverses the prior posting and posts the replacement atomically. The current first slice stores the source document revisions and derives expense reports; it does not assert a settled journal balance.

## Receipt access, backup and recovery

The storage bucket is private and has no anonymous/authenticated object policy. Every database read and file download goes through the exact-email platform-session gate. Test with owner, another platform role, club admin, coach and signed-out session before live use; a hidden link is not a security test. Keep original bytes and stable paths across corrections. The operating backup will include database revisions plus all referenced storage objects, encrypted and held outside Git; a restore drill must reconstruct an expense, its history and both old/current receipts. Supabase project backup alone is not evidence that storage objects can be recovered. Retention location and restore owner are B4 decisions.

[CRA electronic record-keeping guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/ic05-1/electronic-record-keeping.html) calls for readable electronic records, retained source documents, documented corrections and periodic proof that backups can be restored. It also raises record-location and retention requirements. The Supabase project region, a separate readable Canadian copy, retention schedule and restore test need an owner/accountant review before this becomes the sole record store.

## Proposed Stripe CSV v1 handoff

One row is one **financial movement**, not one webhook event. Required columns: `schema_version` (`1`), `provider`, `environment`, `platform_account_id`, `connected_account_id`, `movement_id`, `movement_type`, `source_object_id`, `parent_movement_id`, `occurred_at_utc`, `currency`, `gross_cents`, `tax_cents`, `fee_cents`, `net_cents`, `seller_kind`, `tax_liability_kind`, `status`, `policy_version`. Optional: `invoice_id`, `payment_id`, `refund_id`, `payout_id`, `service_from`, `service_to`, `evidence_url`.

The dedupe key is `(provider, environment, platform_account_id, connected_account_id, movement_id)`. `movement_id` must remain stable across repeated exports and webhook replays. `source_object_id` links back to Stripe; it is not the dedupe key when one object yields multiple distinct balance movements. Amounts are signed from the platform Stripe balance's perspective: `net_cents = gross_cents - fee_cents`; collected tax is a component of gross, not added again. A payout out has negative gross/net, a processor fee has positive `fee_cents` and negative net. Import preview rejects duplicate keys, unknown movement kinds, bad arithmetic, mixed currencies in a movement, missing parent on refund/fee reversal, and a status that cannot be posted. An interrupted import can resume by this key. Payout rows move Stripe balance to the receiving account; they never become income. Organizer registration gross is not proprietor income; only an accepted application-fee movement and its tax/cost allocation can enter this book.

The [CSV v1 sample](../archive/BOOKKEEPING_SOURCE_CSV_V1_SAMPLE.csv) illustrates F01 with a sale, separate processor fee and payout transfer. Its identifiers are synthetic. The mapping is **proposed**, not yet accepted by the Stripe/revenue owner. The [B3 fixture set](../archive/BOOKKEEPING_B3_IMPORT_FIXTURES.csv) adds refund, fee-refund, Connect application-fee, payout, duplicate and incomplete-link preview cases; the preview-only adapter at `lib/bookkeeping-source-import.ts` is pure and cannot persist or post. Synthetic fixtures F01–F06 in `REVENUE_ACCOUNTING_PHASE0_FIXTURES.csv` exercise subscription, registration, refunds, payer shortfall and pending payout arithmetic; they are scenarios, not import rows. The revenue workstream must confirm the actual balance transaction IDs, seller/tax liability, fee payer, refund linkage, service period and export columns before B3 posting is enabled. No Stripe API calls, charge creation or event ingestion are in this slice.

[Stripe's Balance Transaction reference](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance_transactions/object) supplies the unique transaction ID, source, amount, fee, net and reporting category; `amount - fee = net` is its stated balance arithmetic. [Stripe's payout report guide](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/payout-reconciliation) says its payout reconciliation report is for automatic payouts and points manual payouts to the Balance report. The producer must identify which reporting path applies to the actual Stripe account before the importer accepts a payout mapping.
