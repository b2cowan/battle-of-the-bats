# Notifications Open in Place — PM brief

**Ruled 2026-10-05** (all nine recommendations accepted, after three rounds of drawings). Hub:
https://claude.ai/artifact/X78EK19TCyba8wTLAfCfyZ

## What changes

- **On a computer, the bell opens a drawer** on the right holding the whole list of notifications. Clicking one opens it
  beside the list: what kind it is, the day and time it arrived, the full title and message, and a button that says
  where it goes ("Open the request"). Clicking another swaps the message; the list never goes away. The drawer replaces
  the "See all" link, because it holds everything that page did.
- **On a phone**, where neither portal has a bell, the Notifications page under More opens a notification as a sheet
  above the bottom bar. Coaches have had this since September 25; admins get it now.
- **Three actions, each with its own word.** A notification is *read* when you open it, or when you press Mark all read,
  which now really marks everything. A Needs-attention notification is *Done* when you have dealt with it (the button
  that used to say Clear). The *trash* deletes any notification from your own list, with Undo for a few seconds.
- **The button lands on the thing.** A notice about one request opens that request, not the whole list.

## Why it matters

Today the bell cuts every notification to one line, and a click leaves for a page it never names, sometimes the page
you are already on. The notifications that matter most, the club's money decisions, are the longest, so they are the
most cut off. A treasurer either clicks blind or ignores the bell.

## Who it affects

Every club treasurer and tournament admin, and every coach on a computer. Admins on a phone get the reader sheet.
Coaches see the word Clear become Done. The reader behaves the same for every role.

## Tradeoffs

- With a message open the drawer covers most of the page: two thirds of a 1280 window, nearly all of a 1024 one.
- Reaching the page takes two clicks instead of one; in return you know where you are going.
- Delete is undoable for a few seconds, then gone from your list. The request or payment it was about is never touched.
- Mark all read no longer keeps the bell's red count up for an unread decision; the amber Needs-attention count does.

## Priority

Medium. The club money notifications are new and are the main reason a treasurer opens the bell; the club tier is in
the run-up to customers. Built in three steps, one chat each.

## Success criteria

- Any notification can be read whole without leaving the screen you are on, on a computer or a phone.
- You can read one notification after another without going back to the list.
- Every onward button says where it goes before you click it; none reads "Open in admin" to an admin.
- A notification about one record opens that record.
- Read, Done and Delete each do one thing, and their words say which.
