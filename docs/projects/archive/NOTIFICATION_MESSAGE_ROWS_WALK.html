Every step says what to open and do (Do) and what you should see (See).

3 · “Score submitted” made by real scores

The Dev Test Org’s Live Demo — Game Day tournament asks for approval before an official’s score is final, and has two semifinals still to play: Bears U11 at Halton Hawks U11 Jr and Milton Bats U11 Purple (Jackson) at Lions U11, both on Oct 28, 2026. Use two windows: Window 1 for the scorekeeper, Window 2 for the people who should be told. The notice reaches everyone who can open Results (here owner, admin, both staff, the scorekeeper and a second owner and admin), never the person who entered it. The scorekeeper’s own entry is the one that waits for approval.

  1. Do
    Window 1: sign in as the scorekeeper and open http://localhost:3000/dev-test-org/scorekeeper. Set Day to score to Oct 28, 2026, open Bears U11 at Halton Hawks U11 Jr, enter 3 for Bears U11 and 5 for Halton Hawks U11 Jr and press the button.
    See
    The button reads Submit for review and a note above it says the organizer reviews the score. After saving, “Sent for review” appears. If Field scores shows no games for that day, press Needs a call.
