## Question
The owner wants any event to carry a price per person (for example $40 an adult, $15 a child), first come first served, paid at sign-up. This answers eight questions on that shape. Recommendations, not legal opinion; a club's own legal duties are out except where the product creates our exposure. Nothing is built.

## MUST CHANGE (before a paid event can be built as proposed)
1. **A paid sign-up's confirmation email is the family's copy of the agreement, so it stands alone.** Ruling 6A (link only, no pay line) is right for a FREE event and wrong for a paid one: for an Ontario consumer, the internet-agreement rules ask for the main terms in a copy the buyer can keep within 15 days (`ONTARIO_CONSUMER_INTERNET_AGREEMENTS.md`). The "Your sign-up" link dies 30 days after the event, so a link-only email is not a copy. Carry the facts in the email body.
2. **The club's refund policy is shown OPEN on the page before the family pays, and a paid event cannot be saved without one.** This settles backlog L17 for events: a link beside Pay is not "clearly and prominently". Add one fixed line the club cannot edit (below), because Payments Terms §8 already makes the club answer for refunds when it cancels.
3. **Payments Terms do not cover this sale.** §2 and §9 allow payments only for "registrations, programs, team fees and tournament entries"; a dinner ticket is none of them, so a club selling one is outside the Terms it agreed to. Name "event tickets" (and "camps") in the same new version as the sponsorship addendum. Every new version asks every connected club to agree again, so publish ONE version before the first club connects (0 agreements today).
4. **Never charge for a place that is not held.** The place is held first, the pay page opens, and a payment that arrives after a place is gone is refunded in full automatically (the family never sees a charge for nothing). No stored card, no automatic charge (as 13A and 14A).
5. **No person's name, meal or allergy goes to Stripe.** The payment line is the event and counts only ("Year-end banquet: 2 adults, 2 children"). House league sends first names and full names with the season today (census 2026-11-18); do not copy that to events. Keeps Privacy §6 true without a new sentence about guests.
6. **The retention clock must take event-owned forms** (round-1 finding 1, ruled 16A, still unbuilt). A banquet's allergy answers would otherwise be kept as long as the organization exists. Same release condition: mig 516 running, and no retention sentence in policy before it runs.
7. **A club cancelling a paid event cannot do it silently.** The Cancel window shows "{n} people have paid {total}" and asks the club to send the notice (Q2). The email goes only when the club presses it, so ruling 9A ("sends no email by itself") stands.

## Recommendations, 1–8
1. **Before paying** the page shows: who sells (the club, and "FieldLogicHQ provides this page and is not part of the sale"), the event, date, time and place, each price line with count, any processing fee as its own line BEFORE the total, the total in Canadian dollars, tax as `/accounting` words it, the club's refund policy open, the fixed cancel line, a way to reach the club, a review step where each person and price can be changed, and a button that names the amount. The rules reach a family whose total is over $50 (the threshold in the statute as read earlier); one $40 adult is under it, a family is not, so build for all. Whether a volunteer club is a "supplier" under the Act was not settled; treat it as one, it costs a few lines. The Ticket Sales Act, 2017 (admission to a sporting, recreational or cultural event in Ontario) may reach a banquet; I could not read its exemptions, so the all-in total above is the safe shape either way. Lawyer's-eye flag.
2. **The club cancels or moves.** The product owes the family no email by law (the club does), but it owes a path: cancel offers a pre-ticked "Tell the people signed up" email (transactional, to sign-ups only, no other event, no pitch) and a refund of everything paid; the default refund includes the processing fee the family paid, the club's cost under Payments Terms §4/§6, which the club may change in its window. A moved date or place sends the same notice and lets any family ask to withdraw with a full refund (the club decides; the notice says so). Free events stay as ruled: a Communications message. The sign-up page, once cancelled, says "Cancelled" and the refund line.
3. **The confirmation email** is transactional and most likely not a CEM while it adds no promotion (same class as `CASL_LATE_BILL_EMAIL.md`; s. 6(6) lifts consent only, so it still carries the club's name, Reply-To to the club and its contact line). It MAY carry: what was bought, per-line prices, total, the pay line (paid on {date}, card brand and last four; or "To pay, how"), the refund policy text and cancel line, the Your sign-up link. It may NOT carry: another event, a camp, a calendar link, an event-page link, "bring a friend". Pay link only on the unpaid or waitlist-offer case (the ruled exception).
4. **A banquet that is full says "Sold out", no waitlist.** A held, offered, expiring place (13A) is a second payment-and-email machine for a one-night event, and the "Ask to withdraw" request already lets the club free a place by hand. Keep the waitlist for camps. The count "{n} places left" is live from paid and held places.
5. **Names, meals, allergies.** Ruling 16A covers it once built (finding 6), but the clock removes only health and file answers; names, counts and meal choices stay with the sign-up while the organization exists, and the retention sentence must say so. An allergy answer is health information (sensitive); a dietary preference (vegetarian, halal, kosher) can point to belief, so ask ONE question, "Allergies or other food needs", built in as health-marked, plus a separate ordinary "Meal" question that feeds the caterer's counts. The caterer's allergy list leaves only through the logged health-answers export (Part 3), never the plain export. The payer types other adults' and children's names; ask only names, no guest emails.
6. **Unpaid sign-ups while online pay is off.** Fine to hold a place without payment if the page says so plainly, no card is asked, and the hold ends. The club sets "Hold unpaid places for N days" (default 7, never past the closing day); the place is then released with one transactional email, so "places left" stays true. Words say "To pay" never "You owe".
7. **Policy text** (new version each; none before the feature ships): Payments Terms §2/§9 (above); Privacy §5 events, guest names, meal and allergy answers, no guest emails; §9 retention sentence (after mig 516); Data Terms §12 same; Privacy §6 unchanged if finding 5 holds. Terms: nothing. The round-1 list for camps stands.
8. **Receipt.** Heading "Receipt". Payer name as typed, the counts and price lines ("2 adults × $40.00, 2 children × $15.00"), no guests' names (a banquet needs no child-care claim, so the camp reason for a child's full name does not apply). The club's name, address and GST/HST number only as `/accounting` words it. No eligibility sentence. Link life as camps (18A): 30 days after the event; "Email me my link" issues a fresh one; the confirmation email is the permanent copy, so the link ending costs nothing. No name in the URL.

## The minimum family-facing words (verbatim; braces are fills)
- Above Pay: "Sold by {Club}. FieldLogicHQ provides this page and is not part of the sale."
- Lines: "{n} adults × $40.00 · {n} children × $15.00 · Processing fee $X.XX · Total $XXX.XX (Canadian dollars)"
- Refund policy heading with the club's own text open beneath, then: "If {Club} cancels this event, it refunds what you paid."
- Review: "Check your sign-up. You can change anything here before you pay."
- Button: "Sign up and pay $XXX.XX". Pay-later mode: "Hold my places", with "Nothing is charged here. Pay {Club} {how} by {date}. Your places are held until then, then released."
- Email opener: "This is your copy of your sign-up. Keep it."
- Cancel notice: "{Club} has cancelled {Event} on {date}. It will refund what you paid, back the way you paid. Your bank decides when it shows."
- Request: "Ask to withdraw". After: "Asked on {date}. {Club} decides under its refund policy above."
