# Group email to coaches (Board Ready Stage 2) — CASL, the sender's address, account-less recipients
**Last verified:** 2026-11-16 (CASL s.1(2), s.6(1), s.6(2), s.6(5), s.6(6) and Electronic Commerce Protection Regulations SOR/2013-221 s.3(a)–(b) read on laws-lois.justice.gc.ca today; PIPEDA not re-fetched, reasoning from `PRIVACY_POLICY_REQUIREMENTS_PIPEDA.md`; CRTC bulletin 2012-548 not read)
**Re-verify by:** 2026-11-16 + 12 months, or a trigger: the email gains a link, a plan/price/offer line or a promotional foot; Stage 3 adds any group; a group is computed from guardians; Bill C-36; first Québec customer.
**Depends on facts:** `LEGAL_FACTS.md` § two privacy roles; § Where data lives (Resend); `CASL_MEMBER_INVITE_EMAILS.md` (the s.1(2) reading this applies); `CASL_LATE_BILL_EMAIL.md` (the s.6(6) correction and the regulation exits).

## Question(s) this answers
A club admin sends one free-text email to a live group (all coaches, rep staff, house-league coaches, a one-off pick): house mail, From "{Club} via FieldLogicHQ", Reply-To the sender, one email per recipient, five at a time, no family footer, no unsubscribe, a foot reading "Sent with FieldLogicHQ". Is it a commercial electronic message (CEM)? What does the sender's exposed address, the free text, Stage 3's account-less groups and a never-signed-in house-league coach change?

## Finding
1. **Not a CEM as drawn.** s.1(2) tests whether, having regard to content, links and contact details, it is reasonable to conclude one purpose is to encourage participation in a commercial activity. A meeting notice to a club's own staff is not. So s.6 (consent, identification, unsubscribe) is not engaged and "no unsubscribe" is lawful. Same reading as `CASL_MEMBER_INVITE_EMAILS.md`.
2. **Second exit if a regulator read it as a CEM:** SOR/2013-221 s.3(a)(i) takes out a message sent by a representative of an organization to another representative of it that concerns the organization's activities. "Representative" is not defined in the text read; rep staff and house-league coaches are plausible, parent helpers less so. Use as a fallback, never as the design basis. The s.6(6) transaction limbs do not fit (and lift consent only, not the unsubscribe).
3. **What turns it into a CEM is content, and the content is the sender's free text.** A pasted sponsor promo, an apparel sale, a paid clinic or a registration push to coaches is a commercial purpose; the club's own admin wrote it, but FieldLogicHQ "causes or permits" the send (s.6(1)). Code cannot read the body. The controls are a plain line at the message box and Terms §5 (already forbids commercial email that does not meet CASL; no change needed there). A guard cannot cover the body; it covers everything the product adds.
4. **"Sent with FieldLogicHQ" is low risk without a link, but it is the wrong shape.** An unlinked brand line identifies who sent; it is not an offer. It reads, though, as the viral "Sent with {product}" badge, which is promotion in the common understanding, and s.1(2) counts contact details and links as evidence of purpose. The shipped family line is "Sent through FieldLogicHQ, the software {Club} uses." (`lib/email-club-identity.ts:238`), which says what the product is for this reader and cannot be mistaken for an advert. Use that wording, no link, no logo-as-link.
5. **Sender's address (PIPEDA).** Exposing the Reply-To is consistent with the purpose the sender chose (to be replied to), and each coach gets their own copy so no coach's address reaches another coach. The Reply-To is the address of the account that pressed Send, which may be a personal mailbox. Low risk; the fix is transparency to the sender, not a block. The same Reply-To is already used by Ask the coaches, the schedule send and the late-bill send.
6. **A coach entered by the club who never signed in.** Changes nothing for CASL (not a CEM, so no consent is asked of anyone). For privacy: the club collected the address and FieldLogicHQ holds it as the club's service provider (`LEGAL_FACTS.md`); the coach agreed to no FieldLogicHQ terms and does not need to. Two real consequences: (a) they cannot manage anything themselves, so the email must say how to be taken off; (b) the address is typed by an admin and never verified, so a typo sends the club's message, and the coaches' names in the log, to a stranger. Existing exposure in the coach copies; this send widens it from 1 to n.
7. **Stage 3 (account-less groups).** The conditions that keep a later group non-commercial: see Recommendation 5.
8. **The history keeps the body and the recipients' names.** Free text that can name a child or a person, with no stated retention. Same class as backlog L9/L16; the privacy draft needs a sentence and `/plan` a clock.

## What the product does today
Checked against the drawing (`BOARD_READY_STAGE2_GROUP_EMAIL_DRAWINGS.html` tab 3, tab 9 asks 8–12) and `lib/families-email-coaches.ts` L11–21, L63–80 (house mail, `sendEmail` with `kind` and `replyTo`, `inParallel(…, 5, …)`), `lib/email.ts:37–45` (From built from a name already cleaned by `familySenderName`, `lib/email-club-identity.ts:135`). **Not built:** the group sender, its guard, its log. The drawn foot line "You get it because you coach for the club" is not true of a team manager or helper (ask 4 counts all rep staff).

## Recommendation
1. **Keep the design; change four things in the drawing.**
   - Foot: "Sent through FieldLogicHQ, the software {Club} uses." instead of "Sent with FieldLogicHQ".
   - Reason line by group, not "because you coach": "You get it because you're on {Club}'s coaching staff" (rep staff, incl. managers and assistants), "…because you coach in {Club}'s house league", "…because {sender} picked you". Stage 3 groups each write their own true reason.
   - Add one removal line: "Not right for you? Reply and tell {Sender}." (accuracy and a stop route for people with no account).
   - Drop the "Reply to this email to reach {Sender}." line when the sender has no email on file (the line would be false).
2. **The window says where replies go**, with the address: "Replies come to you at {address}." Add under the message box: "Keep it to the club's own business. Don't use this to sell or promote anything, for you or for a sponsor." No forbidding of links in the body by code; the line carries it.
3. **Reuse `familySenderName`** for "{Club} via FieldLogicHQ" so a club name with a quote or comma cannot break the header.
4. **Privacy draft and `/plan`:** one sentence in §5/§9 (the group-email log: sender, group, body, recipients' names); a retention clock for the log (finding 8). An unverified typed address is already true of the coach copies; the removal line is the mitigation.
5. **Stage 3 conditions (each group, before it is built):** (a) a recipient is in the group because the club recorded that role for that person, never because they are a guardian or a family on a list (a "parent helpers" group must not be computed from the family list: that is the family path with its unsubscribe and daily allowance, and a back door round both); (b) the foot's reason line is true for that group; (c) the message is about the recipient's own role or obligation (a shift, a document they owe), never a shared list of who owes what and never a document type that reveals a medical or child detail; (d) no commercial content, same guard; (e) a way off the list that works with no account: the removal line plus the club's own record; honour the club's "Don't email" record for people who have no other way to say stop; (f) any link to an account-less person is a single-person link to their own record, allowed only through a named allowlist, never a pricing or sign-up link; (g) re-run this file's checklist before the build, as `CASL_MEMBER_INVITE_EMAILS.md` L4 requires.

## The build guard (fails the build)
Applies to the group-email sender's rendered HTML and text, built from fixed sample words and a plain body, and to its words file.
- **Zero links:** no `<a`, `href=`, `http:`, `https:`, `www.`, `mailto:` in the rendered output. (Stage 3 may add an allowlist of named role-link builders; nothing else.)
- **Zero commercial words in anything the product adds:** no plan name or price read from `lib/plan-config.ts`, no `$` amount, and none of: pricing, price, per month, free trial, trial, sign up, sign-up, subscribe, upgrade, discount, offer, buy, "try FieldLogicHQ", "your own club", "learn more", "get started".
- **The foot is exactly** `Sent through FieldLogicHQ, the software {Club} uses.` as plain text, and the From name is exactly `{Club} via FieldLogicHQ` from `familySenderName`.
- **Never the family envelope:** the sender may not import the family footer, the club band, an unsubscribe builder or `lib/families-email.ts`; it may not call a family kind. (The family-email manifest already guards the other direction.)
- The guard cannot and should not read the sender's body; the free text is covered by the line in Recommendation 2 and Terms §5.

## Lawyer's eye needed?
No for the email as drawn with the changes above. Two narrow points a lawyer could confirm if the club tier goes on sale: whether volunteers and house-league coaches are "representatives" for SOR/2013-221 s.3(a)(i) (our fallback exit), and whether an unlinked brand line in the foot of club mail is promotion. Yes before Stage 3 sends to parent helpers or volunteers at scale, if the owner wants a view on the "representative" question for people who are not staff.
