## Question(s) this answers
What the published Payments Terms (2026-11-24) must now say for a sponsor's card payment; whether it needs a new version; CASL class of the invoice, receipt and refund emails; the "5 to 10 business days" line and the `re_` reference; the refund-policy link; the silent dispute; what the published texts promise that the product does not do.

## Finding

**1. What the published text says today, and the gap.** `lib/legal-documents/payments/2026-11-24.md`: §1 a Payer is "a family, team or person" (L9); §2 the seller sells "registration, program, team fee or tournament entry" (L13); §4 the fee setting is one of two "for the whole organization" (L48-L53), neither part refunded (L61-L66); §6 the club decides refunds, writes a policy payers "can read" beside Pay, the fee never comes back (L93-L103); §7 a dispute is answered in Stripe and "FieldLogicHQ can gather the facts of the payment for you to copy" (L107-L113); §9 the club agrees to take payments "only for … registrations, programs, team fees and tournament entries" (L137); §10 "Refunds and disputes of payments already made keep working in FieldLogicHQ" and no deletion "while … an open dispute" (L149-L150, L154-L155). A sponsorship card payment is outside the §2 and §9 lists, has its own fee row (not "the whole organization"), and its disputes do not work in FieldLogicHQ at all. The addendum draft fixes each.

**2. A new version is needed, and fresh acceptance comes with it.** The text, not the number, decides: a club that agreed to 2026-11-24 agreed to take payments only for the §9 list, so sponsor card payments would breach what it agreed to. The product has no sponsorship switch: with payouts connected, a refund policy, and the launch switch on, every deal shows the card button (`lib/sponsor-card-server.ts` `sponsorCardFacts`, L37-L46), and our fee is charged, the club paying by default. That widens what the club does and what it pays, so after a club has agreed it is the §13 "increases what it pays" class: 30 days' email notice. **Production today: 0 payout accounts, 0 agreements, online payments off** (`LEGAL_INDEX.md` Payments Terms row), so publishing it BEFORE the first club connects makes the question vanish: nobody is asked twice. Moving `PAYMENTS_TERMS_VERSION` asks every connected club to agree again and emails its owners whatever the size of the change (`lib/online-payment-words.ts` L70-L74; `lib/online-payment-owner-notices.ts`). The Privacy version moves with it and owes its sponsor sentences. Who accepts: the club's owner at Payouts. **The business does not accept our terms and should not be shown them** (FieldLogicHQ is not the seller; a link would suggest otherwise). Its page shows the club's refund policy and the club's own privacy link when the club set one (`SponsorDealContent.tsx` L38-L47, L498-L509; `ClubPrivacyLink` returns null without one), and Stripe's page carries Stripe's notice. PIPEDA: the page collects business contact information, the club's s.4.01 exemption to rely on (`BOARD_READY_STAGE6_SPONSORSHIPS_DRAWING_REVIEW.md` §2); it fails for an individual sponsor (a family, a sole proprietor on a home address), where the club's own openness duty (principle 4.8) is met by the club's privacy link; no fixed notice from us, and a "Privacy" link to OUR policy on the club's page would put us in the controller's seat. Data held about the payer, checked against the dictionary and mig 530: payer email (from the business's own form, prefilled into Stripe), card brand and last four, Stripe session, payment-intent and refund ids, receipt and invoice numbers, amounts, the free-text refund reason (up to 300 characters, shown to the business), and the name of the club member who made the refund. Nothing else; no full number, no IP. Law 25: no Québec club can connect (Québec billing address refused); a Québec business paying an Ontario club is plausible but not researched; trigger: first Québec payer.

So the "not a CEM" position is the one that holds and it holds **only while no promotion is added** (no "sponsor again next year", no package line). Checked: the words are billing and refund facts, the button is "See your sponsorship"/"See the invoice", no offer. **Identification** (s.6(2)(a)-(b), sender and the person on whose behalf, plus a way to readily contact one): all three go from "{Club} via FieldLogicHQ", carry "Sent by {club}, {mailing address}.", the club's contact email and a Reply-To to it, and the envelope's unlinked "Sent through FieldLogicHQ, the software {club} uses." No unsubscribe is needed on the primary reading and none is carried (the sponsorship stop list rightly does not hold them). Two soft spots, low: (a) the refund email prints the club's free-text reason, so a club that typed a pitch there would turn it promotional (the window says "the business reads this in the refund email"; no filter); (b) "Send the invoice" bills a business that left the card page unfinished and never chose cheque or e-transfer; it is still its own submitted form, so it stays within "agreed to enter into", but the invoice's pay block must stay free of any offer. The lawyer's-eye point from Part 3a stands (whether FieldLogicHQ is a sender or only the club's service provider; FieldLogicHQ has no mailing address of its own on a club's mail).

**4. "It takes 5 to 10 business days to appear" and the `re_…` reference.** Stripe's own page (accessed 2026-11-23): the customer "sees the refund as a credit approximately 5-10 business days later, depending upon the bank"; refunds made soon after the charge may appear as a reversal where "the original charge drops off the customer's statement, and a separate credit isn't issued"; a failed refund can take up to 30 days to return. So the product's sentence ("It takes 5 to 10 business days to appear", `REFUND_BLOCK_WORDS.arrives`, `online-payment-words.ts` L225; `DEAL_WORDS.card.doneBody` L553 and `reachedSub` L560, the club-side lines "on their statement") states a card-network time as ours and as certain. It is not a promise of ours to keep, but it reads as one; a business whose refund shows as a reversal, or arrives on day 12, was told otherwise by the club's email. Recommendation (for `/marketing`, owner call O-S3): "It can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the card company" in the email and "It can take 5 to 10 business days to appear on their statement" in the two club lines. The `re_…` id (`REFUND_BLOCK_WORDS.ref` "Refund reference", `online-payment-words.ts` L221, fed `refund.stripeRefundId` at `sponsor-invoice-email.ts` L139): it is not personal data on its own, but Stripe's own page says the number a business's bank can trace is the ARN, STAN or RRN, not a `re_` id, so to the business it is noise that looks like a lookup number. Options: drop the row on the sponsor email (the credit note number and invoice number already identify it), keep it for the club only, or relabel "Stripe reference" (the club-side word, L1217). Recommended: drop it from the business-facing email (owner call O-S6; the same shared block is used on the family refund emails, so a drop there is a separate owner call and a change to shared words).

**5. The refund-policy link.** It is the club's own text, from the Payouts card's "Refund policy" setting, shown in place under the card button on both the form and the pay-an-issued-invoice block (`SponsorDealContent.tsx` L38-L47, L329, L490). Nothing frames it as FieldLogicHQ's. The card button is absent without one: `takesPayments` requires `!refundPolicyMissing` (`lib/online-payment-account.ts` L104-L106) and `sponsorCardFacts` returns null otherwise. Exposure: the club's own obligation, as a seller, for what its refund policy says and for how prominently it is disclosed (backlog L17, not researched: a collapsed link versus open text; sharper if the sponsor is an individual, `ONTARIO_CONSUMER_INTERNET_AGREEMENTS.md`). Ours: only that the Payments Terms promise the policy is readable beside the button, and it is. The page does not name Stripe; Stripe's own page does. No legal issue; the unnamed processor is a wording choice, not a disclosure failure, because the business sees Stripe's checkout before paying.

**6. The dispute silence.** Code: `applyCardDispute` returns before any write for a sponsorship (`online-payment-landing.ts` L562-L566); no dispute row, no notice; the deal stays Paid; the money Stripe takes back is not booked. Two consequences beyond the Terms text: the club's delete check reads only `online_payment_disputes` (`lib/online-payment-endings.ts` L126, L145-L150), so a dispute of a sponsor's payment cannot hold up a deletion; and the club-side dispute help (evidence gathering, the dispute tile in the platform console) never shows it. Nothing in the help tells the club that FieldLogicHQ will warn it, which is honest. What the Payments Terms must say is drafted in §7 and §10 of the addendum. The one sentence the product DOES keep: a refund is refused while the card's bank disputes the charge (`refundSponsorCard` L2091-L2096, a live Stripe read that fails closed).

**7. Money services.** No change. A direct charge on the club's own Stripe account, our application fee taken at the payment, nothing held, hosted checkout: the same shape as the family flow (`RPAA_FINTRAC_MONEY_SERVICES_STATUS.md`). The payment is incidental to the Sponsorships tool (a deal, an invoice and a credit note), not a standalone payment line. The trigger to re-check (payments sold without a plan) is not hit: a deal exists only with the Sponsorships module.

## Addendum 2026-11-24: the after-walk fixes (a cancelled credit note, its email, the arrival sentence, the Terms)
**Reviewed:** the code on dev (`lib/club-sponsor-invoice.ts` `creditNoteLayout`, `lib/sponsor-invoice-email.ts` `emailDealRefundCancelled`, `lib/club-sponsorship-deal-words.ts` `DEAL_WORDS.card`, `lib/online-payment-words.ts` `REFUND_BLOCK_WORDS.arrives`), the draft addendum, `docs/agents/accounting/CREDIT_NOTES_ON_REFUNDS.md` and the accounting dispute addendum of today. **Not re-fetched today:** ETA s.232, SOR/91-44 and CASL (the card review's reading of CASL stands; s.232 rests on accounting's 2026-11-06 reading of Memo 12.2). Where a point is for an accountant or lawyer it says so.

**1. The cancelled credit note: keeping it is the right shape; the PDF had two defects (fixed today).** What happened: a refund was booked and its note emailed; the card's bank then sent the refund back. No consideration was actually reduced, so the note describes an adjustment that did not occur and has to be withdrawn. A **debit note is the wrong tool** (it records an INCREASE in consideration; here the consideration never fell). Keeping the note, marked cancelled, with its number retired and never reused, is the safer form than deleting it or giving its number to a later refund: a numbered document that was sent stays on the record (accounting's six-year retention rule, `RECORDS_RETENTION_AND_LOCATION.md`) and a number never means two things. Two defects in the product's PDF, both fixed in code today: (a) its table still read "Refund of the sponsorship" and "HST 13% refunded $23.01" under a "cancelled" title, so a line on it still looked like a tax reduction that stands (every row now begins "Cancelled:"); (b) its "Issued" day fell back to the day the refund was OPENED, because the database clears a failed refund's booking day (it now reads the day the refund email went, which is the day it was booked and sent). The two tiles ("not refunded", "{tax} not refunded") are the right figures: they say what the note was meant to refund and did not. **For the club's accountant (not a gate):** if a GST/HST return that included the refund's tax reduction was already filed, the reduction is reversed in a later period as an adjustment that increases net tax (accounting's return-lines file puts those on line 104; the accountant confirms the line); the books already reverse the refund lines. **For the business:** if it is a GST/HST registrant it may have adjusted its own records when it received the note; the email tells it the note is cancelled, and the product gives it no tax advice, which is right.

**2. The closing line "invoice N is not reduced by it": accurate and safe as worded; keep it.** "It" is the cancelled note: the line says that note no longer reduces the invoice, which is true, and says nothing about the invoice's tax position beyond that. It would only become wrong if it were reworded to "invoice N is not reduced", because a later refund (a NEW note, a new number) can reduce it. Do not add tax guidance to it.

**3. The correction email: fine as drawn.** The envelope's Reply-To is the club's contact email (`sponsor-invoice-email.ts`, the same `replyTo: contactEmail` as the refund email). CASL: the same class as the refund notice (section 3 above): a factual correction about a transaction the business agreed to, no promotion, one button "See your sponsorship" to its own deal page; most likely not a CEM, with s.6(6)(b) as the fallback and the same condition (it stays so only while no offer is added). Sending it only to the contact the club already invoiced, and only when the first refund email actually went (the stamp), is right. Could it read as an admission that the club owes the money? Not as now written: it says the refund did not reach the card and the note is cancelled, and nothing more (the earlier drafts' "will be in touch" and "the money has not reached you" were removed by `/marketing` today for exactly this reason). The credit-note clause matches what the business was sent because it prints the same refund's number; it is dropped when the invoice carried no tax because no note was ever sent. Left open, unchanged from Part 3a: whether FieldLogicHQ is the sender or only the club's service provider (a lawyer's-eye point).

**4. The arrival sentence: O-S3 applied exactly; the rest of the product still carries the flat version.** `REFUND_BLOCK_WORDS.arrives` now reads "It can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the card company.", which is the section 4 recommendation word for word; applying it to the team, house-league and sponsor emails and windows is a softening and reduces no one's rights. **Still saying it flat** (the same soft spot, low): `lib/league-pay-words.ts` L64, `lib/house-league-money-words.ts` L485, L562, L760, `lib/online-payment-words.ts` L343 and the shared `goesToCard`, and help articles `lib/help-content/house-league.tsx` L1808 and L1582, `tournaments.tsx` L1272. **Owner call O-S7: one wording pass (`/marketing`, then `/docs`) to make them all say it the same way.** I recommend yes; nothing is owed to anyone if it waits. **O-S6 is unchanged:** the sponsor email drops the Stripe `re_` reference; the family and team emails still print a "Refund reference" row with it. It is not personal data and not a legal exposure; it is noise to a family. Your call whether the drop extends to them (recommended: yes, as a change to the shared block, with the club-side windows keeping it).

**5. The Payments Terms.** The published 2026-11-24 text needs nothing for the cancelled note (it describes no credit notes). The DRAFT addendum is updated to 0.2: the product now records a sponsor's dispute, raises a notice and books a loss, so the 0.1 bullet "handled in Stripe, and only there" was no longer true and is rewritten, and the two §10 narrowings (a sponsor's dispute "only in Stripe" and "does not hold up a deletion") are withdrawn because the product's own deletion check now sees a sponsor's dispute row; §6 gains the "refund does not reach the card" bullet. **Notice email to a club: none.** Production holds 0 agreements and 0 payout accounts (index row), so publishing the new version before the first club connects asks no one twice; if it publishes after, §13's 30 days' notice applies, as the draft already says. O-S2 is answered (build, walk §564 call (b)); O-S4 falls away.

## Addendum 2026-11-26: a refund the club makes in its own Stripe dashboard (Stage 6 fixes, Part A)

This replaces what the drafts through 0.3 and the earlier text above say about a dashboard refund not being recorded.

**Findings.**
1. **The drafts were no longer true.** Payments Terms draft 0.3 §6 said a refund in the club's Stripe account is not recorded, and its fee and email sentences covered only a refund from the deal.
2. **Asking again without returning the fee.** A business that paid the processing fee, was refunded only the sponsorship and was marked "They still owe it" would have been asked for the fee a second time, beside a credit note saying the invoice was reduced. Closed by the first ruling below.
3. **Silence about money still owed.** Neither the refund email nor the business's page said the invoice was owing again. Closed by the second ruling.
4. **The email has conditions.** It goes only when the club has a mailing address and a contact email for sponsors (the sponsorship contact, else the club's public contact email: `offerContactOf`) and the business has an address on file. The finish window says when the club's details or the address are missing; the window for a refund from the deal says only that the business has no address.
5. **A refund after the bank already took the payment back** (a lost dispute) is not booked and has no email; the club is told. A dispute won later books it.
6. **New data:** for a refund made in the club's Stripe account, when it was made and Stripe's reason code (the code shows to the club only; the date reaches the business on its page, email and credit note), and the name of the member who finished it.

**Owner rulings 2026-11-26.**
- **"Fee first, no note":** "They still owe it" is offered only once everything the business paid on that payment, fee included, has been refunded and no earlier refund on it has a credit note. Money owed again gets no credit note (the price didn't change), whether chosen or because the remaining payments no longer cover the invoice.
- **"Yes, say it":** the refund email and the business's page say when the invoice is still owing.
- **"Update drafts":** next draft versions for his approval (Payments Terms addendum 0.4, Privacy addendum 0.2). Nothing published changes.

**Open.**
- An email that couldn't go has **no resend**, and **nothing retries** a finished Stripe refund's email (a second Finish answers "already"). To `/plan`.
- Whether an **Express account can refund in its Stripe dashboard** is still unproved (the fee probe of 2026-11-25 refunded through the API as the account), and the club-facing Payouts hint tells clubs to do it. Prove it before the hint ships.
- A refund **from the deal** beside another standing payment still reopens the deal and issues a credit note; and a Stripe finish that leaves money owed again *without being asked* does not look at an earlier credit note on the payment (accounting addendum 2026-11-26).
- An invoice owing again keeps its **processing fee line**, payable by cheque (said in §4 of 0.4; a product question).
- **A lawyer could look** at asking again for money that was refunded.

**No lawyer gate** (owner 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-04): the owner decides when a lawyer looks.

## Addendum 2026-11-27: the sponsor page when the invoice is owing again (before a before/after drawing)
- **Refund policy.** Stripe's website checklist asks most businesses to explain their refund policy clearly, and its dispute guide asks for a screenshot of how it was shown at checkout. Ontario CPA 2002 s.1 (e-Laws Word copy, read today): a "consumer" excludes "a person who is acting for business purposes", so O. Reg. 17/05 disclosure reaches only a sponsor buying personally. Card network rule text not opened (L17 stays open for families). Published §6 "can read it beside the Pay button" is met by visible text or a link; draft addendum §9 "(which shows beside the card button)" is true only of visible text. Recommended: keep it visible (owner 2026-11-24) and label it as the club's.
- **Heading and figures.** No legal rule. No "payment due" heading while a card payment is received but unrecorded, or while confirming; no "overdue" unless the due date passed. A "Still owing" row satisfies "Yes, say it" if the amount stays attributed to the club.
- **Processing fee off the amount owing.** The lower-exposure direction, but it changes the invoice, not just this page: draft §4 ("the line stays on the invoice") and the PDF, emails and books move with it (`/accounting`).
- **To `/plan`:** the policy text shown is not kept with a payment (`lib/online-dispute-read.ts` L92 reads today's text; sponsorships have no facts copy); the setup hint (`lib/online-payment-words.ts` L544, L554) leaves out sponsors.

## Addendum 2026-11-27: the sponsor page after a lost dispute (before a drawing)
Code read only; no source fetched; card network rules on asking again after a dispute not opened.
- **Wording.** "{amount} still owing" and "{club} shows this invoice as still owing…" are safe: attributed to the club, no accusation. Name the event, not who asked for it, and show no reason code: "Card payment reversed by the card issuer". Add "Questions? Contact {club} before you pay."
- **No date on the reversal row.** `dispute_lost_at` is the time we booked the loss (mig 532 L64), not the day the money left the cardholder's bank or Stripe.
- **Show only on a booked loss with money owing** (`disputeLost`, deal not withdrawn). Never on an open dispute, a bank's question, a won or part dispute.
- **Card button.** No rule read forbids it; it is a new payment the business starts. Owner call.
- **Earlier part refund plus a booked loss:** invoice, payment, refund and reversal rows do not add up to the amount owing, and the page today says "after the refund". Needs its own drawing or no sum (`/plan`).
- Unchanged: no email to the business (owner, walk §565).
