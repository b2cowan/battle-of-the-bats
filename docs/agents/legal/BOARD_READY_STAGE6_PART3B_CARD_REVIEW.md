# Board Ready Stage 6 Part 3b: a business pays a club's sponsorship by card, and the club refunds it
**Last verified:** 2026-11-23 (code read on dev; CASL s.1(2), s.6(1), s.6(2), s.6(6) re-read on laws-lois.justice.gc.ca today through a summarising fetch, with s.6(6)'s opening words confirmed on page 1 of the Act; Stripe's refund page read today; PIPEDA and Québec Law 25 not re-fetched)
**Re-verify by:** the build is committed; the first club connects payouts on production; first Québec club or Québec business payer; Bill C-36; an edit to any of the five emails or the card words; the card rate or the default fee payer changes
**Depends on facts:** `LEGAL_FACTS.md` § two privacy roles, § Payments; `ACCOUNTING_FACTS.md` (seller). Reuses `BOARD_READY_STAGE6_SPONSORSHIPS_DRAWING_REVIEW.md` (Parts 2 and 3a), `CARD_SURCHARGE_PAYER_FEE.md`, `RPAA_FINTRAC_MONEY_SERVICES_STATUS.md`, `ONTARIO_CONSUMER_INTERNET_AGREEMENTS.md`, `docs/agents/accounting/CREDIT_NOTES_ON_REFUNDS.md`. The draft text: `drafts/PAYMENTS_TERMS_SPONSORSHIP_CARD_ADDENDUM.md`.

## Question(s) this answers
What the published Payments Terms (2026-11-24) must now say for a sponsor's card payment; whether it needs a new version; CASL class of the invoice, receipt and refund emails; the "5 to 10 business days" line and the `re_` reference; the refund-policy link; the silent dispute; what the published texts promise that the product does not do.

## Finding

**1. What the published text says today, and the gap.** `lib/legal-documents/payments/2026-11-24.md`: §1 a Payer is "a family, team or person" (L9); §2 the seller sells "registration, program, team fee or tournament entry" (L13); §4 the fee setting is one of two "for the whole organization" (L48-L53), neither part refunded (L61-L66); §6 the club decides refunds, writes a policy payers "can read" beside Pay, the fee never comes back (L93-L103); §7 a dispute is answered in Stripe and "FieldLogicHQ can gather the facts of the payment for you to copy" (L107-L113); §9 the club agrees to take payments "only for … registrations, programs, team fees and tournament entries" (L137); §10 "Refunds and disputes of payments already made keep working in FieldLogicHQ" and no deletion "while … an open dispute" (L149-L150, L154-L155). A sponsorship card payment is outside the §2 and §9 lists, has its own fee row (not "the whole organization"), and its disputes do not work in FieldLogicHQ at all. The addendum draft fixes each.

**2. A new version is needed, and fresh acceptance comes with it.** The text, not the number, decides: a club that agreed to 2026-11-24 agreed to take payments only for the §9 list, so sponsor card payments would breach what it agreed to. The product has no sponsorship switch: with payouts connected, a refund policy, and the launch switch on, every deal shows the card button (`lib/sponsor-card-server.ts` `sponsorCardFacts`, L37-L46), and our fee is charged, the club paying by default. That widens what the club does and what it pays, so after a club has agreed it is the §13 "increases what it pays" class: 30 days' email notice. **Production today: 0 payout accounts, 0 agreements, online payments off** (`LEGAL_INDEX.md` Payments Terms row), so publishing it BEFORE the first club connects makes the question vanish: nobody is asked twice. Moving `PAYMENTS_TERMS_VERSION` asks every connected club to agree again and emails its owners whatever the size of the change (`lib/online-payment-words.ts` L70-L74; `lib/online-payment-owner-notices.ts`). The Privacy version moves with it and owes its sponsor sentences. Who accepts: the club's owner at Payouts. **The business does not accept our terms and should not be shown them** (FieldLogicHQ is not the seller; a link would suggest otherwise). Its page shows the club's refund policy and the club's own privacy link when the club set one (`SponsorDealContent.tsx` L38-L47, L498-L509; `ClubPrivacyLink` returns null without one), and Stripe's page carries Stripe's notice. PIPEDA: the page collects business contact information, the club's s.4.01 exemption to rely on (`BOARD_READY_STAGE6_SPONSORSHIPS_DRAWING_REVIEW.md` §2); it fails for an individual sponsor (a family, a sole proprietor on a home address), where the club's own openness duty (principle 4.8) is met by the club's privacy link; no fixed notice from us, and a "Privacy" link to OUR policy on the club's page would put us in the controller's seat. Data held about the payer, checked against the dictionary and mig 530: payer email (from the business's own form, prefilled into Stripe), card brand and last four, Stripe session, payment-intent and refund ids, receipt and invoice numbers, amounts, the free-text refund reason (up to 300 characters, shown to the business), and the name of the club member who made the refund. Nothing else; no full number, no IP. Law 25: no Québec club can connect (Québec billing address refused); a Québec business paying an Ontario club is plausible but not researched; trigger: first Québec payer.

**3. CASL (the Act's text read today; the CRTC bulletin page returned 403, so no CRTC wording is cited).**
| Email | Where | Class |
|---|---|---|
| "Send the invoice" and the issued/revised invoice | `lib/sponsor-invoice-email.ts` `emailDealInvoice` (kind `club_sponsorship_invoice`) | Most likely not a CEM: its purpose is to bill a deal the business itself submitted (s.1(2) looks at the content, links and contact information for a purpose of encouraging commercial activity). Fallback s.6(6)(b) "facilitates, completes or confirms a commercial transaction" the recipient "previously agreed to enter into". |
| "Paid in full" receipt | same, `moment 'paid'`, claimed once | Same: confirms a completed transaction. |
| Refund notice | `emailDealRefund` (kind `club_sponsorship_refund`) | Same: factual notice of a refund on a transaction. |
Fallback, if a regulator did read one as a CEM: s.6(6) opens "Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply", so it lifts consent only; s.6(1)(b) and s.6(2)(c) (an unsubscribe mechanism per s.11(1)) would still bind. So the "not a CEM" position is the one that holds and it holds **only while no promotion is added** (no "sponsor again next year", no package line). Checked: the words are billing and refund facts, the button is "See your sponsorship"/"See the invoice", no offer. **Identification** (s.6(2)(a)-(b), sender and the person on whose behalf, plus a way to readily contact one): all three go from "{Club} via FieldLogicHQ", carry "Sent by {club}, {mailing address}.", the club's contact email and a Reply-To to it, and the envelope's unlinked "Sent through FieldLogicHQ, the software {club} uses." Sending refuses without a mailing address or a contact email (`clubCanEmailInvoice`, `sponsor-invoice-email.ts` L73, L125). No unsubscribe is needed on the primary reading and none is carried (the sponsorship stop list rightly does not hold them). Two soft spots, low: (a) the refund email prints the club's free-text reason, so a club that typed a pitch there would turn it promotional (the window says "the business reads this in the refund email"; no filter); (b) "Send the invoice" bills a business that left the card page unfinished and never chose cheque or e-transfer; it is still its own submitted form, so it stays within "agreed to enter into", but the invoice's pay block must stay free of any offer. The lawyer's-eye point from Part 3a stands (whether FieldLogicHQ is a sender or only the club's service provider; FieldLogicHQ has no mailing address of its own on a club's mail).

**4. "It takes 5 to 10 business days to appear" and the `re_…` reference.** Stripe's own page (accessed 2026-11-23): the customer "sees the refund as a credit approximately 5-10 business days later, depending upon the bank"; refunds made soon after the charge may appear as a reversal where "the original charge drops off the customer's statement, and a separate credit isn't issued"; a failed refund can take up to 30 days to return. So the product's sentence ("It takes 5 to 10 business days to appear", `REFUND_BLOCK_WORDS.arrives`, `online-payment-words.ts` L225; `DEAL_WORDS.card.doneBody` L553 and `reachedSub` L560, the club-side lines "on their statement") states a card-network time as ours and as certain. It is not a promise of ours to keep, but it reads as one; a business whose refund shows as a reversal, or arrives on day 12, was told otherwise by the club's email. Recommendation (for `/marketing`, owner call O-S3): "It can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the card company" in the email and "It can take 5 to 10 business days to appear on their statement" in the two club lines. The `re_…` id (`REFUND_BLOCK_WORDS.ref` "Refund reference", `online-payment-words.ts` L221, fed `refund.stripeRefundId` at `sponsor-invoice-email.ts` L139): it is not personal data on its own, but Stripe's own page says the number a business's bank can trace is the ARN, STAN or RRN, not a `re_` id, so to the business it is noise that looks like a lookup number. Options: drop the row on the sponsor email (the credit note number and invoice number already identify it), keep it for the club only, or relabel "Stripe reference" (the club-side word, L1217). Recommended: drop it from the business-facing email (owner call O-S6; the same shared block is used on the family refund emails, so a drop there is a separate owner call and a change to shared words).

**5. The refund-policy link.** It is the club's own text, from the Payouts card's "Refund policy" setting, shown in place under the card button on both the form and the pay-an-issued-invoice block (`SponsorDealContent.tsx` L38-L47, L329, L490). Nothing frames it as FieldLogicHQ's. The card button is absent without one: `takesPayments` requires `!refundPolicyMissing` (`lib/online-payment-account.ts` L104-L106) and `sponsorCardFacts` returns null otherwise. Exposure: the club's own obligation, as a seller, for what its refund policy says and for how prominently it is disclosed (backlog L17, not researched: a collapsed link versus open text; sharper if the sponsor is an individual, `ONTARIO_CONSUMER_INTERNET_AGREEMENTS.md`). Ours: only that the Payments Terms promise the policy is readable beside the button, and it is. The page does not name Stripe; Stripe's own page does. No legal issue; the unnamed processor is a wording choice, not a disclosure failure, because the business sees Stripe's checkout before paying.

**6. The dispute silence.** Code: `applyCardDispute` returns before any write for a sponsorship (`online-payment-landing.ts` L562-L566); no dispute row, no notice; the deal stays Paid; the money Stripe takes back is not booked. Two consequences beyond the Terms text: the club's delete check reads only `online_payment_disputes` (`lib/online-payment-endings.ts` L126, L145-L150), so a dispute of a sponsor's payment cannot hold up a deletion; and the club-side dispute help (evidence gathering, the dispute tile in the platform console) never shows it. Nothing in the help tells the club that FieldLogicHQ will warn it, which is honest. What the Payments Terms must say is drafted in §7 and §10 of the addendum. The one sentence the product DOES keep: a refund is refused while the card's bank disputes the charge (`refundSponsorCard` L2091-L2096, a live Stripe read that fails closed).

**7. Money services.** No change. A direct charge on the club's own Stripe account, our application fee taken at the payment, nothing held, hosted checkout: the same shape as the family flow (`RPAA_FINTRAC_MONEY_SERVICES_STATUS.md`). The payment is incidental to the Sponsorships tool (a deal, an invoice and a credit note), not a standalone payment line. The trigger to re-check (payments sold without a plan) is not hit: a deal exists only with the Sponsorships module.

## What the product does today
Checked at the cited lines above and in `lib/sponsor-card-server.ts` (the page opens or is absent; the sponsor-pays fee follows the Payouts row only before an invoice; an issued invoice's fee is always the club's, L12-L15, L152-L155), `lib/online-payment-fee.ts` L116-L132, `lib/club-sponsorship-deal-words.ts` card block L471-L560, `lib/help-content/org.tsx` L1809-L1811 and L2316-L2420, migration 530 and the dictionary entries for `club_sponsor_deal_refunds` and `club_sponsor_deal_payments` (card columns).

## Recommendation
See the four owner calls in the draft (O-S1 to O-S4) and these: publish the new Payments Terms version before the first club connects (O-S1); keep the sponsor dispute silent and say so in the Terms (O-S2) or build a notice (route to `/plan`); soften the 5-to-10-day lines (O-S3); drop `re_…` from the business-facing refund email (O-S6); consider saying "The processing fee isn't refunded" beside "Includes a $X card processing fee" on the card page (`DEAL_WORDS.card.feeNote`), where today it is first said in the refund email (`E.refundFeeKept`, `online-payment-words.ts` L308) (O-S5: the fee is the club's own charge, a sponsor paying it and then being refunded in full gets back everything but the fee; telling it before paying is the safer sentence; for `/marketing`).

## Defects to route to `/plan` (a published or shipped sentence that promises more than the product does for a sponsorship payment)
1. `lib/legal-documents/payments/2026-11-24.md` L149-L150, "Refunds and disputes of payments already made keep working in FieldLogicHQ": a sponsor's dispute does not work in FieldLogicHQ. Fix: addendum §10, new version.
2. Same file L154-L155, "We will not delete an organization while money is still in motion: an open dispute…": the check cannot see a sponsor's dispute (`online-payment-endings.ts` L126). Fix: the narrowed sentence in the addendum, or a build that counts them.
3. Same file L109-L111, "FieldLogicHQ can gather the facts of the payment for you to copy": no dispute row exists for a sponsorship, so there is nothing to gather. Fix: addendum §7.
4. Same file L48 "…for the whole organization": false once the sponsorship row exists (separate setting, and an issued invoice is always the club's). Fix: addendum §4.
5. Same file L13 and L137 (the sale and the allowed list leave out sponsorships): a club that takes a sponsor's card is outside its own agreement. Fix: addendum §2, §9.
6. `lib/club-sponsorships-words.ts` L256, `payouts.refundHint`: "Refund the fee line yourself if you want the sponsor made whole." The product offers no way to do it (a refund "never includes the processing fee"); the only way is a refund in the club's own Stripe dashboard, which the product then does not book and flags as "A refund made in Stripe isn't in your books". Fix: say "in Stripe, then record it in your Ledger" (`/marketing`, `/docs`).
7. `lib/legal-documents/privacy/2026-11-24.md` L183 and L199: the Stripe row names "the player names on the payment" and "the payment is between the family and the club". Over-silent for a business payer, not over-promising; carried by the next Privacy version (already owed, `BOARD_READY_STAGE6_SPONSORSHIPS_DRAWING_REVIEW.md` §5).
8. Soft: `lib/help-content/org.tsx` L1809 ("families and teams can read it beside the pay button"): leaves out a sponsor, who can too. And `club-sponsorships-words.ts` L251 "You receive the full invoice": true at Stripe's standard Canadian price; Payments Terms §4-§5 say the club receives less on a card issued outside Canada or a dispute fee, so the hint is exact only for the ordinary payment. `/docs`.
Nothing found that promises the club is told of every dispute, that refunds are always available, or that a refund includes the fee: the Payouts card, help articles, Terms, Privacy and Data Terms say none of these.
