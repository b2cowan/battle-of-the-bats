## §1. About this policy (replaces one table cell)

Players, parents and guardians, registrants, members, sponsors and other businesses that a club, league, tournament or coach puts into FieldLogicHQ

## §4. Information about website visitors and the public (replaces two bullets)

- **Cookies.** We use only the cookies needed to run FieldLogicHQ: keeping you signed in, remembering a choice such as
  which division you were looking at, and noting for a day that you opened a document you are asked to agree to.
  **We do not use advertising or tracking cookies**, and we do not use third-party analytics. Your browser's own storage
  also holds a few preferences on your device (for example, teams you choose to follow on a tournament site); they stay
  on your device.
- **Error reports.** If a page fails, we record technical details to fix it, which can include the page's web address,
  your browser type, your internet (IP) address and, if you are signed in, your account and its email address. These
  reports are deleted automatically after 30 days; a short entry for each kind of error is kept longer (§9).

## §5. Information that clubs put into FieldLogicHQ (replaces the first sentence of the section)

Clubs, leagues, tournaments and coaches use FieldLogicHQ to manage their members and sponsors.

## §5 (adds one bullet at the end of "What a club may keep")

- **About sponsors and other businesses, including a person or family who sponsors a club:** the name to be shown, a
  contact's name, email address and phone number, invoice address, website, logo, the answers to the club's sponsorship
  questions and any files attached, a record of each document agreed to (the exact words, the version, who and when,
  with no internet (IP) address), and each sponsorship: what was offered, its price and tax, who at the club offered or
  withdrew it, its invoice, payments and refunds (with the reason the club gives for a refund), and, for a card payment,
  the card's brand and last four digits, Stripe's reference numbers (and, for a refund the club makes in its own Stripe
  account, when it was made and Stripe's reason code) and any dispute.

## §5 (adds two paragraphs after "Emails a club sends through FieldLogicHQ")

**Sponsorship offers.** When a club emails a business a sponsorship offer through FieldLogicHQ, we keep a record of the
offer in the club's account: the business's name and email address; the reason the club chose for why it may email them
(they paid the club in the last two years, asked about sponsoring in the last six months, gave the club the address, or
published it for business use); the date that reason rests on (when a club offers again to a past sponsor, the date of
its last payment); who at the club sent it; and when. When a business unsubscribes, or the club marks it "Do not offer
again", we keep the address, the date and how it happened. The club can't remove that.

**A sponsor's page.** A club gives a business a private link to its sponsorship page; no account is needed. What the
business enters there goes to the club's record of that business. Anyone who has the link can see the details entered
there and the sponsorship's invoice and payments, and can change the details while the page's form is open. The link
keeps working after the sponsorship is paid; the club can replace it at any time.

## §5 (replaces "What we don't do")

**What we don't do:** FieldLogicHQ does not show a player's name, date of birth or roster on any public page. A club can
publish its schedules, standings, team names, the names of coaches who choose to show theirs, and its sponsors' names,
logos and websites. A logo that has been on a club's pages can still be opened by someone who has its web address after
it comes off those pages.

## §5 (replaces the first sentence of the last paragraph)

**If you are a parent, guardian, member or sponsor** and have a question about information a club holds about you or
your child — including asking to see, correct or delete it — **please contact the club first.**

## §6. Who we share personal information with (replaces the end of the Stripe row's "What it receives")

For club payments: the payer's email, card and what is being paid for, and, for a family's payment, the player names on
the payment

## §6 (replaces the "When a club takes payments online" paragraph)

**When a club takes payments online,** the payment is between the payer (a family, or a business that sponsors the club)
and the club: the club is the merchant, and the payment is processed in the **club's own Stripe account**. Stripe handles
that information under its own terms with the club.

## §8. Children (replaces the second paragraph)

We treat children's information, and health information about anyone, as **sensitive**: it is never shown on public pages,
uploaded documents open only through short-lived private links, and our error reports remove email addresses, and details
labelled as a date of birth or a phone number, from the technical details they capture (a report can still name the
signed-in account it happened to, §4).

## §9. How long we keep information (replaces the "Error reports" bullet)

- **Error reports**, including each error's technical details, are deleted after 30 days. We keep a short entry for each
  kind of error (its type, the page's address with any private link's secret removed, and how often and when it
  happened) to tell if it returns; some have no set end date. The detailed log of records our staff opened is deleted
  after 90 days; performance measurements, after one year. The list of pages our staff visit in our own tools is kept
  with no set end date today.

## §9. How long we keep information (adds two bullets after "Friend requests")

- **Sign-up files and health answers** (§5). The files a family attaches to a club's sign-up form, and the answers to
  questions the club marks as being about health, are removed 90 days after the club marks the season finished, or 120
  days after its last day if it is never marked finished. A season that is reopened after being finished waits until it
  is finished again. A file attached to a sign-up or sponsorship form but never sent in is deleted within about a day (a
  sponsor's logo is kept with its sponsorship). Copies of the answers in our routine database backups are gone within 7
  days.
- **Sponsors** (§5). A club's records of its sponsors, their deals, answers, files and logos, the record of the offers it
  sent, and the list of businesses it must not offer again are kept while the organization exists, with no separate time
  limit, and deleted with the organization. A club can delete a sponsor's record only while it has no deal; the record of
  an offer sent, and of an unsubscribe, stays.

## §12. Unsubscribing from emails (adds a paragraph at the end)

A club's **sponsorship offer** to a business has its own unsubscribe link. It stops that club's sponsorship offers to
that business, at every address the club has on its record of the business. Invoices, receipts and refund emails about a
sponsorship still come. A club also can't offer a sponsorship to an address that has unsubscribed from its emails to
families.

## For a lawyer's eye

- **§4, §8 and §9, error reports (0.3).** Naming the signed-in account in §4 and narrowing §8's claim correct a text
  that is live today; judged minor under §13. A lawyer could say whether correcting an overstated protection needs
  notice. Separately, the alert email we send ourselves on a serious error carries the error message and stays in our own
  mailbox past 30 days; `/legal` recommends dropping the message from that email.
- **§5 and §9, the offer record and the stop list** are deleted with the organization. CASL puts the burden of proving
  consent on whoever claims it (s. 13) and a violation can be pursued up to three years after a designated person learns
  of it (s. 23(1)); a deleted club leaves neither record behind. The private right to sue was repealed before it came
  into force. Whether FieldLogicHQ is itself a sender is the open question that decides if this is our exposure. Owner 2026-11-25: left as
  built; a lawyer looks when the owner chooses.
- **§5, a sponsor who is a person or a family** is outside the business-contact exemption (PIPEDA s. 4.01); the club's
  own openness duty then applies, met today by the club's privacy link where it set one.
- **§9, sign-up clean-up:** every other answer and the sign-up record stay with no time limit (backlog L9, "limited
  retention").
- **§13:** whether naming sponsors is a "new way" of using information that needs the notice email.
