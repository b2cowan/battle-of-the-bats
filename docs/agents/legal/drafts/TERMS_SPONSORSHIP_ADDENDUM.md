## §4. Your organization's information (replaces the first paragraph)

**It is yours.** Everything your organization or team puts into FieldLogicHQ — rosters, player and family details,
registrations, tryout results, documents, schedules, messages, money records, and your sponsors' details, including what
a sponsor sends you on its sponsorship page ("Customer Data") — belongs to you. We do not claim ownership of it.

## Data Terms §11. Getting your information out, and deleting it (adds one bullet after "While you use FieldLogicHQ")

- **Sign-up files and health answers** collected by your organization's sign-up form are removed 90 days after you mark
  the season finished, or 120 days after its last day if it is never marked finished (a reopened season waits until it is
  finished again). Download what you need to keep before then.

## For a lawyer's eye

- **Data Terms §11.** An automatic removal of a club's records sits beside §2's "you decide how long to keep it". A
  lawyer could say whether naming it needs the §18 notice once a club holds such records.
- **§4.** A sponsor's logo is someone else's property that the club publishes. The sponsor page asks the business to
  confirm it may use the logo and allows the club to show it; §5 already bars uploading what the club has no right to
  share. A lawyer could say whether the club's licence from its sponsor should also be named in these Terms.
