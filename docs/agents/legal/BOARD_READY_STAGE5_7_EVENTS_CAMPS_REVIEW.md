**Re-verify by:** the Stage 5/7 build prompt; Stage 3 (people/instructors) drawn; the family app ships; first Québec club; Bill C-36; any edit to the retention sweep (mig 518/516)
**Depends on facts:** Stage 4 forms (Parts 1-3 reviews), Privacy/Data Terms/Payments Terms 2026-11-24, `BUSINESS_DECISIONS.md` 2026-10-31 "Your sign-up" ruling, `MINORS_IMAGES_AND_CLUB_FEED.md`, `CLUB_COMMUNICATIONS_STAGE1.md`, `CASL_LATE_BILL_EMAIL.md`

## Question(s) this answers
Six asks on the Stage 5/7 shape (children's information and the instructor's view, waivers and consents, CASL per email, the public Events tab, policy text owed, shape changes). Nothing is built. A club's own waivers and duties are out; only our exposure is answered.

## Findings
1. **MUST CHANGE in the shape: the retention clock does not reach events or camps.** The 90/120-day removal of health answers and file answers is keyed to forms owned by `league_seasons` and to the season's finish or last day (mig 518:246-248, `league_season_last_day`). A camp or banquet form has neither. As drawn, a camp's health answers and a banquet's allergy answers would be kept for as long as the organization exists, against the Privacy §5/Data Terms §12 promise the clock backs. Fix: the sweep takes forms owned by an event; clock = **90 days after the event's (camp's last session's) end date**; a declined, withdrawn or never-placed child's health answers and files go at the earlier of 30 days after that decision or the same clock (owner call on the 30). Built-in banquet/camp templates mark allergy/dietary/medication questions as health. Same release condition as Stage 4: mig 516 running; no retention sentence in policy before it runs.
2. **Instructor view.** Today's promise (Privacy §5, Data Terms §12): answers are "readable only by the people who can open [the club's] registrations". An instructor who opens health answers breaks that sentence, so it must be rewritten in the same version as the feature. Recommended instead of "all health answers behind a tap": the club marks, per question, "an instructor may see this" (default off; typically allergies, medications, "anything we must know in an emergency"); the instructor's tap opens only those answers, only for a child in their session, only from the day before the first session to the day after the last, each opening recorded with the instructor's name (fail closed: no line, no answer; this is the F2 owner call in the Part 3 review, and for this door the recommendation is fail closed). Nothing downloadable or printable; no payment status, amounts, address, date of birth, email, other sessions, other camps, waitlist, or form answers beyond the marked ones.
3. **Roster minimum:** child first and last name, age (never date of birth; the camp keeps birth year only, which is good), a "health note: yes/no" marker, and the guardian name and one phone **behind a tap** (an emergency and pickup purpose; same session-only, same dates; opening need not be logged but may be, owner call). Access ends by date and when the instructor is removed.
4. **Instructors must be 18 or older as the product stands.** Privacy §8 and Terms (§ "Age") say every account holder is 18+ and "we do not knowingly let a child create an account". A junior leader (16-17) given a sign-in contradicts both. Either the instructor invitation refuses under-18s (recommended, v1) or the age promise and a parental route are rewritten (owner call; lawyer's eye).
5. **Link life vs the receipt.** The 2026-10-31 ruling ends "Your sign-up" when the season ends, first names only. A camp receipt is needed months later (tax filing) and carries the child's full name. Recommended: the link ends 30 days after the last session; "Email me my link" (already ruled) issues a fresh one while the sign-up record exists; receipt on that page. **Owner question:** full child name on a link-only PDF (the holder already holds the family's email and first names) is a step past "first names only". Recommend yes, for a paid sign-up only, name as the family typed it, nothing cached, no name in the URL.
6. **The receipt must not claim eligibility.** "Eligible for child care expenses" is our tax claim; overnight vs day camp and instruction vs care drive it. Heading "Receipt", the facts the CRA asks of the payee, no eligibility sentence; routed to `/accounting` (the wording, payee identifiers, GST/HST on an Exempt/Taxable camp, the Overnight flag as a tax input).
7. **Waitlist move-up must not create a debt silently.** Auto-accept plus auto-move-up means a family owes money after a spot opens, without a fresh yes. Recommended: "A place opened. Yours until {date}; pay or say you can't come", no stored card, no automatic charge, and the lapse email says the place was released. If the club's auto-accept switch is on the child is accepted, and the same email carries the refund policy line and the withdraw route (L17 stays open).
8. **Waivers and consents (what the product creates exposure for).** (a) A guardian statement as a fixed first line of the camp form ("I am this child's parent or legal guardian, or have their authority") so the tick is never described as more than it is; we do not verify the signer. (b) Copy never says "signed waiver", "legally binding" or "we confirm they read it" (Part 1/3 rules stand). (c) **Photo permission is its own yes/no question, neither answer chosen for the family, never a condition of signing up** (OPC #2025-005, `MINORS_IMAGES_AND_CLUB_FEED.md`); the camp template ships it unrequired and the builder refuses "required" on it. (d) Medical-treatment consent and authorized-pickup people are the club's questions: ship them as ordinary questions, pickup names marked "someone other than you; only what the club needs", and keep the Ontario health-card warning from Part 1. Whether a parent's tick binds a child's own claim is the club's law and a lawyer's question; the product only must not imply it.

## CASL, email by email
Not a CEM on its face while it carries no promotion and only the two allowed links (`CASL_LATE_BILL_EMAIL.md`; `s.6(6)` lifts consent only, so every email still carries the club's name, Reply-To to the club, and its contact line).
- **Sign-up received, accepted, withdrawn/refund, session changed/cancelled:** transactional. The "Your sign-up" link only. A cancellation must not pitch another camp (that sentence would make it a CEM). No event-page link, no calendar link (2026-10-31 ruling: no other links to families).
- **Moved up from the waitlist, with the pay link:** yes, allowed: it completes a transaction the family asked for, and the pay link is the ruled exception. Nothing else added.
- **"Camp is open" to last year's families:** **a CEM** (it invites paying for a service). The club is the sender. Bases: a paid camp within 2 years, or an inquiry within 6 months, or express consent; a free-event sign-up gives none of the first two; "membership" depends on the club's own rules. It must go through the Club Communications send (sender, mailing address, unsubscribe, suppression), never from Events. Agree with the proposal: **no announcement emails from the Events feature.**
- Instructor invitation and "you were given session X": account notices, as the member invitation (`CASL_MEMBER_INVITE_EMAILS.md`).

## Public Events tab
- Never show: instructor names by default (the club may type them in its own description; youth leaders possible), any child, roster, sign-up list, or who is on the waitlist; "Our families" and "Club staff" events must not appear on any public page, feed, sitemap, share image or public JSON. "Our families" means signed in to the family app; until it exists, do not offer it as a public page behind an unlisted address (that is public once forwarded), or label it plainly.
- Helper line on the description field: no children's names or health details. Champs day shows divisions and teams, never rosters.
- **"3 places left" is fine**: a count, no personal information. It must be computed live from accepted and held places (a stale or invented number is a misleading claim), and "Full, join the waitlist" must be true.
