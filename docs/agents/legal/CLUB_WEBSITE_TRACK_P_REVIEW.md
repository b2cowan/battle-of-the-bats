## Question(s) this answers
1. Is the claim step (tab 6 step 3) adequate for Terms accepted on a club we built, who answers for the preview before the claim, and does the Terms or Data Terms text change?
2. Gaps in the marking (tab 5) against the Stage 0 list.
3. The one email to the person we met (tab 8, ask P13): CASL, consent basis, the draft, forwarding, and the alternative B.
4. The removal record and the "This preview has ended" page (tab 7).
5. Anything else exposure-creating.

## Finding

### 1. The claim
**Adequate in shape, with four fixes.** The Terms stay per person, ticked at the account step, and are checked again at checkout (`lib/legal-documents/terms/2026-11-24.md` §1: "If you accept for an organization, you confirm you are allowed to bind it"; §20). Nothing about *that* is special to a claim. What is special is that the club's name, logo and colours were put there by us, and that **the person claiming holds nothing but a printed code.**

- **Who answers for the preview before the claim: FieldLogicHQ, entirely.** We chose the name, logo, tagline and every sample; the club did nothing and agreed to nothing. Our exposure in that period is the logo and name (copyright in the artwork, trademark and passing-off if anyone reads the page as the club's own), any inaccurate statement about a real club, and CASL on the one email. The Terms do not reach it: the club has no account, and Terms §15 (the organization covers our costs for "information the organization put into or published through FieldLogicHQ") must not be readable as reaching a preview the club never touched.
- **Data Terms: no change.** A preview holds no Customer Data (no people, tab 3); before the claim we are the publisher, not a service provider. After the claim the kept name, logo and colours are the club's own information like anything else it enters.
- **The claim line as drawn says the right thing but has no contractual home** ("nothing in it is yours to answer for" sits outside the Terms; §19 is a whole-agreement clause). It is still good evidence of what we told the person. Do not add a release of claims against the club: a release hidden in a tick, signed by someone whose authority we cannot check, is weak and looks sharp.
- **A new server rule is needed that the drawing does not state:** the claim is a new door that makes someone an organization owner, so it must refuse without the current Terms accepted (as `app/api/org/create/route.ts` does) and join the acceptance census tests; the claim tick must be kept as an append-only record (account, club, time, the exact sentence shown, the code used; no IP), kept like the other acceptance records (at least six years, Terms §20).
- **The code is a bearer secret on a card left on a table.** Anyone who picks it up can claim "Oakridge Minor Softball" and become the owner of a club that carries the real club's name and logo. The tick ("I'm allowed to claim this club") is an assertion we cannot check. This is the largest exposure in the claim flow, and the real club's only remedy is to write to us. See Recommendation 3.
- **"Someone at the club has already claimed it" (the fail state) asserts a fact we do not know.** It should say the club has been claimed, and give the rightful club a way to say so.

### 2. The marking
Stage 0's list (the band on every page, the footer repeat, "Sign in" removed, sponsors "Sample", every form closed with "nothing you type is kept") is all in the drawing and the words are unchanged. **Gaps:**
- **A link pasted into a message or a social post shows the club's name and logo as its own** (the page title and share card are the club's), with no band. Noindex does not stop a link preview. Title, share title and share description must carry the preview words, and a preview refuses the share image, poster and calendar feed routes: a calendar subscription would put sample games on a real family's phone.
- **The band scrolls away** (the drawing says so; the footer repeats it). Acceptable only if "Sample" sits in the names of the made-up things themselves, because a screenshot of a standings table or a live card carries no band. The tournament is named "Oakridge Indoor Classic" and the house league "2027 House League, Sign up by Mar 31, Registration open": both read as the club's real event and real registration. They need "Sample" in their own name.
- **No way for the real club to say "take it down" from the page.** The footer should offer the removal route; it is the cheapest mitigation of the trademark and copyright exposure.
- **Made-up names that may be real.** "Northgate Hardware", "Cedar Lane Physio", "Two Rivers Pizza", "Marlow Heights Mavericks", "Fenwick Bay Foxes" are generic enough that a real business or team may exist, in the club's own region. The sample is fixed (P4 A), so it is a one-time check, not a per-preview cost.
- **"Nothing you type is kept" is a promise the server must keep.** The page has no fields, but a crafted request to a registration, tryout, contact or follow route for a preview club must be refused and write nothing, and its body must not be logged. Privacy policy promises about what the product does count as defects when untrue (this agent's own rule).
- **The "one line about the club"** ("ages 6 to 17") is a statement of fact about a real organization that we typed. Keep it to what we can stand behind ("Softball in Oakridge"), no ages, numbers or dates.
- **Robots line (tab 1, "a robots line").** `lib/robots-rules.ts` lists each demo slug in the public robots.txt. Doing that for previews would publish every prospect club's name to anyone who reads robots.txt, contradicting "unlisted". Use the noindex tag and an `X-Robots-Tag` header, and no robots.txt line and no sitemap entry. (robots.txt also stops a crawler reading the noindex.)
- **Québec.** The make window refuses a club already on FieldLogicHQ but not a Québec club. A preview is our commercial publicity in English in front of a Québec organization, and the claim fine print already says "outside Québec for now" (backlog L7). Refuse a Québec club in the make window.

### 3. The one email (tab 8; ask P13)
- **It is most likely a CEM.** Its content invites the person to make the club theirs with a claim code, which leads to a paid plan (s. 1(2): one of its purposes is to encourage participation in a commercial activity). None of the s. 6(6) consent exceptions fits (a quote or estimate "requested by the person" is the nearest and it is not a quote). Treat it as a CEM; the drawing already does.
- **Consent basis: implied consent under s. 10(9)(c)**, the address was disclosed to us by the person, with no sign they did not want such messages, and the message is relevant to their role. It holds only if the person is a club officer, board member or similar (a bystander parent is not) and gave the address themselves. **Strengthen it** by saying out loud in the meeting "I'll send you one email with the link, is that all right?": that is oral express consent, which the Regulation allows (SOR/2012-36 s. 4: a request may be obtained "orally or in writing"; its identification and withdrawal content is satisfied by us being the person in the room, but the CRTC bulletin on how oral consent is proved was not read). **s. 13 puts the proof on us**, so the product needs a record: the date the address was given, that it was given in person, the person's role (not their name), the send date. The drawing shows no field for any of this and does not show where the address is typed or kept.
- **Sender identification and unsubscribe, against the draft:** identification by business name is met ("FieldLogicHQ"; the Regulation asks for the name the sender carries on business under). **Not met as drawn:** the mailing address is a blank (`FIELDLOGICHQ_SELLER_MAILING_ADDRESS` is `''` today by the owner's 2026-11-17 ruling, which said to fill it "before any promotional email"; this is that email). s. 6(2)(b) and SOR/2012-36 s. 2 require a mailing address plus one of a phone, an email or a web address, valid for 60 days after the send (s. 6(3)). support@ and the web address cover the second half. **The email cannot be sent until the owner has a mailing address.** This is an owner decision, and it settles the Ontario consumer-agreement gap in the same stroke.
- **The unsubscribe link must not live on the club row.** It has to work 60 days after the send (s. 11(2)) and be honoured within 10 business days (s. 11(3)), but the preview is deleted 30 days after the meeting and the existing marketing opt-out is a column on the organization (`organizations.email_marketing_opt_out`, `email-sender.ts` skips on it). A club row that is deleted takes the opt-out with it. Key the opt-out to the address in its own small table that outlives the club; one press, no sign-in, offering "no more email from FieldLogicHQ" (all CEMs), the same confirm-page shape as `CASL_UNSUBSCRIBE_CONFIRM_STEP.md`. Because the email will be forwarded with its link, the confirm page must say which address it will stop (masked).
- **The draft's words:** fine except that "Everything on it is a sample" is not true of the name and the logo, and the footer can say more about why they got it. Exact words below.
- **Forwarding is the person's own message, not ours.** The email says "to share with your board", which is permission, not an instruction. It stays that way if we add no forward button, no "send to a colleague" box, no collection of other addresses, no per-recipient tracking and no reward for passing it on. The code is not in the email, so a forward carries nothing a board member could misuse. Risk: low.
- **Alternative B (email the board at published addresses): no, not in this track.** CASL would allow it on paper (s. 10(9)(b): conspicuously published, no "no unsolicited messages" statement, relevant to their role), but (a) a board member's address on a club page is personal information of a volunteer, and the PIPEDA "publicly available" exception is narrow (SOR/2001-7; a website page is not obviously a "publication", the OPC has read it narrowly, see `CLUB_WEBSITE_STAGE0_RULINGS_REVIEW.md`); (b) it collects names and addresses of people from the web, the thing Stage 0 ruled out for the page; (c) it moves from "we wrote to one person who asked" to cold outreach on the back of a site we built with their logo; (d) each recipient becomes a suppression record. If it is ever wanted, only role addresses the club publishes for contact (info@, registrar@), not named people, and a separate review.

### 4. The removal record and the "ended" page
- **What the record keeps** (club name, web address, who made it, meeting date, logo source, made and removed dates) holds no personal information about the club's people: a club's name is not personal information, "who made it" is our own staff, the logo source is an address on the club's site. **Keep it that way: the record must not hold the person's name, role or email.** The one-email record (address, date given, basis, send date) is a different record about a person and sits apart, with its own clock: **three years after the send** (the consent evidence period used in the Stage 0 CASL line), then deleted; an unsubscribe entry is kept for as long as we send any CEM, as the address only.
- **"Nothing in it was kept" over-claims** because the record exists. Say what was deleted.
- **Public ended page, permanent, reachable by guessing a slug** (the slug is the club's name): it confirms to anyone that FieldLogicHQ prepared a preview for that club, which is our pipeline and the club's private business dealing. Legal exposure is small, but it is gratuitous. Keep the name (the visitor arrived by a link meant for this club) but **for 90 days after removal, then the ordinary "We can't find that page"**; never show the meeting, who made it or the logo.
- **Privacy text:** one sentence in the next Privacy version (§3, beside "Early-access and contact forms"): if you give us your email address in a meeting about your club, we keep it with the date to send what we told you we would send and to show you agreed, for three years, and you can unsubscribe. A new kind of person (a prospect) but the same kind of data and purpose as the existing form row, so most likely minor under the Terms/Privacy change rules, but the owner reads that call.

### 5. Anything else
- **A real club's name and logo on our page:** the exposure is copyright (the artwork is reproduced and stored by us) and trademark/passing-off if someone is confused. It is bounded by the rulings (own published logo, marked, noindex, short life, no people) and by a visible removal route. It is the item to hand a lawyer first if the owner ever chooses to (flagged at Stage 0).
- **The live game with made-up teams from made-up towns:** harmless if the names are checked once and the tournament says "Sample" (finding 2); the 2026-09-02 reason (no faked scoreboard) is kept by the owner typing the score.
- **The logo-source field:** fine and useful evidence. Require a web address, so a person's profile page cannot be typed into it.
- **The claim code and "5 tries an hour":** fine as a rate limit if it counts per club as well as per visitor (otherwise a spread-out guess is unlimited), and the code stored so staff can show it. A person mistyping "7K4 PQ2" five times in the room is locked out for an hour; allow a restart from staff's window.
- **The claim record and "Claimed by" on Prospect clubs:** show the claimant's email (masked) to staff so the owner can check it against the person met (Recommendation 3).

## Recommendation
Exact changes, in the order the build meets them:

1. **Claim step 3 words (tab 6).** Replace the line above the tick with:
   "FieldLogicHQ made and published this preview, using the club's name, logo and colours from its own site. The club did not make it and is not responsible for it. From here the club is yours, and so is what you put on it. The name, logo and colours stay until you change them."
   Replace the tick with: "I'm allowed to claim this club for Oakridge Minor Softball and to agree to the Terms on its behalf."
   The claim refuses without current Terms accepted; the tick is kept as an append-only record, six years.
2. **Terms, next version (not before; no change to a frozen file):** add to §3 a short paragraph, "A preview we built for your club. Before a club claims it, a preview site we made is ours, and we answer for it. When someone claims it for the club, the name, logo and colours on it become the club's information and these Terms apply to it from that day. We remove a preview when the club asks." It favours the customer, so a smaller change under §18 (no re-acceptance). Bundle with the next Terms version; the claim line stands alone until then. **Data Terms: no change. Privacy: the one sentence in finding 4.**
3. **Claim safety (to `/plan`, owner call):** the Prospect clubs claimed row shows the claimant's masked email and the time; staff can undo a claim until a plan starts (no plan bought, nothing else yet exists); the "claimed first" fail state reads "This club has already been claimed. If you're with the club and didn't claim it, write to support@fieldlogichq.ca."
4. **Marking additions:** page title, share title and share description say "(preview)" and carry the band's words; share image, poster, calendar feed and every form route refuse a preview and write nothing; footer reads "Prepared by FieldLogicHQ for {club}. Not their site yet. To have it removed, write to support@fieldlogichq.ca."; "Sample" in the name of the tournament, the house league season and each sponsor (already) and the sample towns and teams checked once; the about line carries no ages, numbers or dates; no robots.txt line and no sitemap entry (noindex and `X-Robots-Tag` only); the make window refuses a Québec club.
5. **Email words (tab 8).** Body, second paragraph: "Everything on it is a sample except the club's name, logo and colours, which we took from your own site. It stays up until Thu, Jan 7, 2027. If you would rather it came down sooner, reply to this email and we will remove it." Keep "To make it your club, use the claim code on the card from the meeting." Footer: "FieldLogicHQ, {mailing address}, support@fieldlogichq.ca. You're receiving this one email because you gave us this address when we met about Oakridge Minor Softball on Tue, Dec 8. Unsubscribe." Sent only after the mailing address is set, only to an address the person gave in person, with the dated record described above.
6. **P13: A.** Do not build alternative B.
7. **Ended page:** "FieldLogicHQ prepared a preview site for Oakridge Minor Softball. It was removed on Thu, Jan 7, 2027, and its pages, images and sample information were deleted." Shown 90 days, then the generic page. The removal record holds no person.

**Owner questions:** (a) a mailing address (PO box or mail-service address is enough for CASL; the same address would close the Ontario consumer gap), because the email cannot go without one; (b) the Terms paragraph in the next Terms version, or a Terms version of its own before the first claim; (c) the claim safety in 3 (build or accept the wrong-claimant risk); (d) the ended page for 90 days, or permanent; (e) the Privacy sentence, who reads it as minor.
