## The findings the numbers make visible

1. **15px is the most common body size in the app, and no stylesheet asks for it.** 109 desktop
   renders have a cell size different from their table's declared size; the global `td` element
   rule (0.9375rem) wins over every table-level `font-size` that was left to inherit. The coach
   ladder's `--type-body` never reached a list cell; the platform admin's "0.75rem" never applied
   on ten of sixteen pages.
2. **The coach list hairline is paper-coloured in the warm skin.** `--white-05` remaps to
   `--home-paper`; on a white card it is a 2% difference. Twenty-two renders draw it.
3. **The register's zebra references a token that does not exist** (`--white-4`) and has never
   painted; the one-surface plan recorded it as an earned exception from the stylesheet.
4. **Hover is a global accident.** `tbody tr:hover` in globals tints every table row in the app;
   on the opaque-celled money grids it fires and paints nothing, on the insights and devBoard
   tables it tints rows that open nothing.
5. **Platform admin column headings are 8.8px** — below the token floor — on fourteen tables, in
   sixteen hand-copied recipes whose padding drifts from 0.45 to 0.85rem.
6. **The Months view's figure doors are 26px tall on a phone** while the Statement's are 44px;
   the layout gate has never addressed the Months view, so it has never been measured.
7. **Nothing club-side could be rendered**, which is the coverage gap the owner can close with one
   fixture decision (a member on `qa-families-fixture`, or seeding the club fixture org).
