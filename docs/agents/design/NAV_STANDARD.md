# Nav standard

**Ruled 2026-10-17** by the owner, all fourteen asks of the Nav Consistency Review as recommended (*"looks good, i agree
with your recommendations"*). Hub: https://claude.ai/artifact/RtcrWCxpfuNMkTRQMK8pA3 (source
`docs/projects/active/NAV_CONSISTENCY_REVIEW_HUB.html`). **Rewritten whole 2026-11-12** (navigation Phase 6) to describe the
product as it ships after navigation Phases 0–5 (plan of record `docs/projects/active/ADMIN_NAV_EVALUATION_PLAN.md`, rulings
E1–E16 and the phase gates). Where this page and the code ever differ, the code is the fact: fix this page, and tell the owner.

**Covers:** the sidebar, the top strip, the phone bar and the phone More sheet of the club admin, the tournament admin (the
same sidebar, in tournament mode) and the Premium Coaches Portal; the free Coaches Portal where a rule names it (it is out of the
navigation project, E13); and the **platform admin's support console** (since 2026-10-24, Support console Stage 1, owner
F1/D4: its top strip, folding menu, foot and narrow-width panel follow N1–N6 and N9).

A new nav row, sheet row, icon or tile is checked against this page, the way a table is checked against
`TABLE_AND_LIST_STANDARD.md`. A departure is written into the exceptions below when it is made, never discovered later.
Guards: `tests/unit/nav-standard-guard.test.ts` holds N1, N4–N10 and the 13i place rules; the others are named in each row.

**One grammar, three portals.** A sidebar headed by the place's cell (the club, the event, the team); a top strip that names the
portal; tabs pinned in a band where a place has pages; a phone bar that names the page with the place on a small line under it;
a More sheet in one order; doors to the other portals at the sidebar's foot and in the sheet's You; Help in the account menu.
**A tablet (641–900 px) wears the phone's chrome:** every sidebar and strip hides at 900 px and under, and the bar and sheet
take over (CSS at 900/901, never a width read in script). The only tablet extras are on the Coaches Portal's bar (N3).

## Exceptions (different on purpose)

- **The club sidebar groups places; the coach sidebar groups pages.** A club runs several programs, each a place whose
  pages are its tabs (N11) — nothing opens under a club row; a coach runs one team and its page groups are labels over
