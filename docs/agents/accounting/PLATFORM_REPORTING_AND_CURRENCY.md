# Digital-platform reporting (ITA Part XX) and foreign-currency conversion
**Last verified:** 2026-10-31
**Re-verify by:** 2027-10-31, or when online payments are switched on in production, or when clubs sell merchandise
through the platform
**Depends on facts:** FieldLogicHQ facilitates payments from families/teams to clubs (clubs mostly non-profits);
online payments OFF in production 2026-10-31; any USD revenue

## Question(s) this answers
- Must FieldLogicHQ file CRA's annual digital-platform seller report?
- How are USD amounts converted for GST/HST and income tax?

## Finding
**Part XX (in force 2024-01-01):** platform operators report **reportable sellers** of **relevant activities** —
sale of goods, rental of immovable property, **personal services** (time- or task-based work by individuals at a user's
request), rental of transport — by **January 31** for the prior calendar year. Late filing penalty $100–$7,500; $500 per
seller who withholds a TIN. A pure payment processor is not a platform, but adding payment collection to a service that
connects sellers and users doesn't remove it (CRA guidance para 2.11). Excluded sellers: governmental entities, listed
entities, small goods sellers (< 30 sales and ≤ $2,800), large rental entities. An "excluded platform operator" must
demonstrate yearly that sellers can't profit or none are reportable. Selling a season registration or tournament entry
is not obviously a "personal service"; **club merchandise would clearly be a sale of goods.** The guidance doesn't
address non-profit sellers.

**Currency:** GST/HST — convert on the day tax becomes payable (or payment day, or monthly average), consistently,
using the Bank of Canada, a chartered bank, or the conversion source actually used (Memo 3-6). Income tax — Bank of
Canada rate for the day the amount arises (or the closest preceding day); averages only when rates are stable
(Folio S5-F4-C1).

## Recommendation
Get a one-paragraph written view from the accountant on whether FieldLogicHQ is a reporting platform operator **before
the first January 31 after online payments go live**, and record it here. Keep payment-related seller data (legal name,
address, business number) available in case the answer is yes. For USD: use Stripe's payout conversion or the Bank of
Canada daily rate and stick with one method.
