# A club's sponsorship invoice, payments and GST/HST collected (Board Ready Stage 6 Part 3a)
**Last verified:** 2026-11-26 (correction addendum: the dashboard refund as built, read from migration 542 and the code, no source re-read); 2026-11-25 (dashboard-refund addendum, Stripe docs read; earlier: 2026-11-23 Part 3a: read from the code, migration 528 and the existing return-lines file; Part 3b addendum below: migration 530 and GI-200 re-read)
**Re-verify by:** 12 months; a CRA change to the invoice-information tiers; before a club books GST/HST collected into a return; if revised invoices gain a credit-note path
**Depends on facts:** none of FieldLogicHQ's own (this is the PRODUCT's feature for a club's own tax; scope note: a club's tax is outside this agent's charter, so this is a product-correctness check, not advice to any club)

## Question(s) this answers
- What must a registered club's sponsorship invoice show, and does the PDF show it?
- Is the proportional tax split on part payments right? Is "GST/HST collected" on payment the right timing?
- Is a revised invoice with the same number acceptable? Is the paid invoice a valid receipt?

## Finding
- **Tiers (since 2021-04-20, `GST_HST_ON_SUBSCRIPTIONS_CANADA.md`):** under $100: name, date, total; $100 to $499.99: add the tax (amount or rate) and the supplier's GST/HST number; $500+: add the buyer's name, a description and the terms. ($30/$150 is the old table.) The PDF carries all of them on a taxed invoice: club name, issued date, total, "HST 13%" row with its amount, "GST/HST no. ..." at the top (only when taxed), Bill to, description with deliverables, "Due {date}" while unpaid. Untaxed: no tax words, no number, as ruled.
- **Tax point is the invoice, not the payment** (RC4022, `GST_HST_RETURN_LINES.md` §6): GST/HST on an invoice is reportable on the earlier of payment and due date, in practice the invoice date, paid or not. The ledger line "GST/HST collected" is posted on the payment date (cash). It reads right as cash held, but it is NOT the figure for return line 103 while invoices are unpaid.
- **Part payments:** proportional split, last payment takes the remainder, void drops its share and the next payment re-takes it; the sum of live tax parts always equals the invoice tax. Correct and cent-exact.
- **Revised invoice, same number:** acceptable while nothing has been paid and the period is open. A reduction after the period was filed needs a credit note (s.232; RC4022; a bare void earns no line 107 adjustment). Migration 528 overwrites the original figures, so the original invoice cannot be reproduced from the books.
- **Paid invoice as receipt:** valid ITC documentation (the information tiers are met by the same page). Never "tax receipt".
- **"Generated by FieldLogicHQ" footer:** a tool credit, not a supplier claim; the club can switch it off on paid plans.

## Recommendation
1. Say plainly, once (help article or a hint on the GST/HST collected row), that the figure counts when money arrives and that the return counts from the invoice date; show invoiced-not-yet-paid tax somewhere a treasurer can read it.
2. Keep the original figures when an invoice is revised (migration 528 is dev-only so far, so it can still be amended).
3. Snapshot the club's GST/HST number on a taxed invoice so a later registration change cannot blank it on a reprint.
4. A club that pays CRA records an ordinary expense, which Budget vs. Actual counts as Spent while the tax collected is not in Collected; give that payment its own excluded word.

## Addendum 2026-11-23: Part 3b, a sponsor pays by card, the fee, the credit note (read from the code; GI-200 re-read today)
**Reviewed:** `lib/online-payment-fee.ts` (`sponsorCardFee`), `lib/sponsor-card-server.ts`, migration 530 (card landing, refund open/book/failed, void refusal), `lib/club-sponsor-invoice.ts` (refund sums, fee row, credit note layout), the invoice PDF, `isFeeRefund`, the platform item. Scope as above: the product's correctness, not advice to any club. The credit-note rule itself (Memo 12.2 ¶15-23; SOR/91-44 s.3) is in [CREDIT_NOTES_ON_REFUNDS.md](CREDIT_NOTES_ON_REFUNDS.md) and is not restated; that file is about OUR invoices, so the club-side answer of 2026-11-23 ("a refund on a taxed invoice needs a credit note") lived only in the build notes until now.

**Arithmetic, confirmed to the cent from the code.** $1,000.00 + 13% = $1,130.00. Fee = 3.5% + $1.50 on $1,130.00 = $41.05 (`onlineFee`, half-up). Club pays: sponsor pays $1,130.00, club nets $1,088.95. Sponsor pays: fee tax = round($41.05 × 13%) = $5.34, charge $1,176.39, tax total $135.34 ($130.00 + $5.34), club receives $1,135.34 (the charge less the fee), keeps $1,000.00 after remitting $135.34. A $200.00 part refund = $176.99 + $23.01 HST (200 × 130/1130 = 23.0088, SQL and TS agree); the fee ($41.05) and its HST ($5.34) are excluded from "refundable" ($1,130.00), so the club keeps them; refunding the last of what is refundable ends the deal Withdrawn. **A card landing books FOUR ledger lines, not five:** sponsorship income, fee income (only when the sponsor paid it), GST/HST collected, Payment processing cost (three when the club pays; fewer again with no tax). Stripe's "extra" on a foreign card is a separate later line, not part of the landing.

**What holds.** (1) Passing the fee on as its own invoice row with the club's rate on it is the conservative tax answer (under-collecting is the club's exposure; over-collecting is remitted). (2) Income gross, fee as a separate cost, tax under its own word, never income or spending. (3) Credit note meets s.3 on every point in the topic file: titled credit note, the club's name and GST/HST number, the sponsor's name, the issue date, the tax adjusted and its rate, and the invoice's number and date (beyond Memo ¶20's "should"). A separate PDF per refund is right (what Stripe does for ours); the invoice stays unrevised and lists the refund. The 0.00-tax-part case cannot over-claim: it needs a refund of about $0.04 at 13%, the adjustment is then nothing, and the refund that empties the payment takes the remainder, so the credit notes' tax always sums to the invoice's tax (not the fee's). (4) Refund lines carry the REFUND day (`club_sponsor_book_day(org, NULL)` = today, Toronto), never the payment's, and the credit note's issue date is that same day, so the adjustment lands in the period it is issued; a filed period is never moved.

**What departs from an earlier answer, and a correction of mine.** `BOARD_READY_STAGE6_SPONSOR_PAYMENT_FEE_REVIEW.md` said (GI-200 "not re-read") that tax follows the underlying supply, so the club's HST applies to a passed-on surcharge line. **GI-200 (March 2023, page of 2023-09-29), read today, says the opposite for a true credit card surcharge:** one charged solely for accepting the card, imposed by the merchant, and shown and charged separately from the price and every other fee is consideration for a separate financial service and "will not be subject to the GST/HST". But the same sheet says a fee that is in whole or part for the use of property such as an online payment platform "is not a credit card surcharge". The product's fee is that second kind (it includes FieldLogicHQ's margin, is charged on every card, and a bank-debit version exists), so taxing it as part of the sponsorship's consideration is the better-supported reading, and the safe one. The cost of a wrong reading: if it were a pure surcharge the sponsor is over-charged $5.34 on $1,176.39 (the club still remits what it collected; the sponsor can ask for it back). **The catch is consistency:** the club's Payouts hint (`lib/club-sponsorships-words.ts:255,261`) frames the fee as a surcharge for the networks' 2.4% cap (this fee is about 3.6% of $1,130.00 against an acceptance cost of about 2.9%), while the tax treatment frames it as not a surcharge. The two answers must be the same answer; the tax position leans on "platform fee", and the network rules treat a service or convenience fee as a different, narrower thing (FCAC; `docs/agents/legal/CARD_SURCHARGE_PAYER_FEE.md`). The hint also leaves out that networks restrict surcharging debit and prepaid cards and require advance notice (that file); wording is `/legal`'s.

**Fee absorbed by the club (the default, and always on an issued invoice).** Booked as one gross cost under Sponsorship > Payment processing; income is the whole invoice. Correct. The tax inside FieldLogicHQ's application fee (about $0.92 on $1,130.00, R1) is potentially an input tax credit for a registered club making taxable supplies; Stripe's own fee carries none. The product records no tax on the fee and claims no credit (no club-side credit field exists), which under-claims, the safe direction; the document that would support a claim is the open Q2 in `STRIPE_OWNED_PRICING_ACCOUNTANT_QUESTIONS.md`.

**Defects (routed to /plan).**
1. **A lost sponsor card dispute is silent and unbooked.** `lib/online-payment-landing.ts:562-566` returns after a `console.warn`; income, GST/HST collected and "Paid" stay, while Stripe takes the money (the $15 dispute fee IS booked, `bookDisputeFee`). Not an accepted limitation: the books overstate income and the club is not told. A loss should reverse the payment's lines without a credit note (the club did not refund; the sponsor still owes, so the deal returns to owing and the tax stays in the return until written off as a bad debt, line 107, `GST_HST_RETURN_LINES.md` §8). The club's hand route today is a manual Ledger expense, which counts as spending and cannot carry the tax part (I found no manual route that writes "GST/HST collected"; only the deal's own payment and refund code does); the accountant adjusts the return.
2. **A credit note for a refund that later bounces stands.** `club_sponsor_card_refund_failed` (migration 530:696-713) voids the two lines but keeps `credit_seq`, and the note was already emailed; only the download link disappears (`lib/club-sponsor-invoice-pdf.ts:34`). The sponsor holds a credit note for money that did not reach the card; the club reduced its tax on it. Needs a cancelled-note message and an instruction to the club.
3. **A refund made in the club's Stripe dashboard gets a notice only** (`online-payment-landing.ts:2166-2175`): no booking, no credit note, so the GST/HST stays unreduced (safe) and the club cannot claim the adjustment. Offer to book it with its credit note.
4. **The club's GST/HST number on the invoice and the note is read live, not kept** (`lib/club-sponsor-invoice-pdf.ts:61`, `clubInvoiceLayout`): Part 3a recommendation 3 is still open; a credit note reprinted after the number changed or was cleared can lose the registration number s.3 requires.
5. Latent: `creditNoteLayout` prints "-C1" for a refund with no number (`lib/club-sponsor-invoice.ts:242`); unreachable today because the PDF route refuses one.

**Soft points.** The invoice never nets refunds (`invoiceStanding` counts gross): a part-refunded invoice still reads "Paid in full" with a Refunds table; "paid, after refunds" exists only on the credit note (`club-sponsor-invoice.ts:266`). Fine for tax (invoice plus note), but the walk brief says the invoice shows it, which is wrong. After a full refund the club keeps the fee and its HST on a Withdrawn deal; remitting that tax is the safe side. A deal with a cheque and a card payment refunded in full on the card stays Paid (the cheque holds money). The fee income sits under Club sponsorship, so that line reads $1,041.05 beside a $1,000.00 plan, offset by the cost (net right, line gross). The platform item's fixed id is club-only (`coach-budget-item-tiers.ts:71-73`). Fee-tax rounding is half-up and the invoice total of the two roundings equals one rounding here.

**Owner calls.** (a) Keep taxing the passed-on fee (recommended: yes) and state the position in the hint, or treat it as a surcharge and stop taxing it. (b) The sponsor-paid fee income under Club sponsorship (gross, as built) versus netting it against Payment processing. (c) Whether the invoice itself should show net-of-refunds standing. (d) Whether a lost dispute ships as a documented limitation (not recommended: defect 1).

**A professional could look at.** Whether the passed-on fee is a GI-200 surcharge or taxable consideration (a club's accountant; or a CRA ruling), and the same for FieldLogicHQ's D1; bad-debt relief on a lost dispute; the first club return carrying a credit note; the credit on the tax inside our fee; whether the kept fee and its HST after a full refund should come back; and, for a lawyer, surcharge versus service fee, debit and prepaid cards, advance notice and Québec (`/legal`).

## Addendum 2026-11-24: a sponsor's card dispute (walk §564 owner call (b), ruled "build a notice and the books reversal first")
**Reviewed:** the Part 3b addendum above (defect 1), `GST_HST_RETURN_LINES.md` (lines 103, 104, 107; the bad-debt and credit-note rows, re-read today), and `lib/online-payment-landing.ts` (the sponsorship early return in the dispute handler; `bookDisputeFee`). Scope as above: the product's correctness, not advice to any club. No new source was needed: nothing here turns on a rule not already in the topic files.

1. **A dispute LOST: reverse the payment's receipt lines, with no credit note.** The club did not refund anyone, and the sponsor's bank, not the club, took the money back; the supply is not reduced and the sponsor still owes the invoice, so no s.232 adjustment arises (Memo 12.2 ¶22-24 needs a credit note the club issues). Book, dated the day the loss is recorded and never the payment's day: the sponsorship income out, the passed-on fee income out (only when the sponsor paid it), and the GST/HST collected line out, as the refund's lines do. **Leave the processing-fee cost line standing** (Stripe keeps its fee on a lost dispute; the cost was really incurred) and **leave the dispute fee booked** (`bookDisputeFee` already books it as a cost when the dispute opens). If the dispute covers only part of the charge, reverse in proportion exactly as a part refund splits (the last takes the remainder), so the tax reversed never exceeds the tax collected.
2. **The deal goes back to Invoiced with that amount owing again. Not Withdrawn, not left Paid.** Paid would overstate what the club holds; Withdrawn is the club's decision (it takes the logo down and frees the place), not the dispute's. The invoice is not revised and its GST/HST is not adjusted: line 103 counts tax charged or collectible on the invoice date and is gross (credit notes and bad debts do not reduce it). If the club gives up on the money, the tax comes back only as a **bad debt on line 107**, in the period it is written off, on a return filed within four years of that return's due date (RC4022), and only because the sale was reported and the tax remitted; a later recovery goes on line 104. The product books none of that; it tells the club it exists.
3. **A dispute WON: put it back.** Void the reversal lines, mark the payment as holding money again, and return the deal to Paid (the refund-failed function does the same dance). The dispute fee: do not hard-code $15 or assume it comes back; read what Stripe's balance transaction says and mirror what the team and league win path already books for the fee. A win that arrives after the club has recorded the loss and chased the sponsor by another way is the case a person sorts out (the notice says so).
4. **A dispute that is only OPEN: a notice, no books.** Stripe has already taken the amount from the club's balance, but nothing is decided; reversing now would be wrong half the time. The notice's job is to say: answer it in Stripe by the due date, and do not refund this sponsor meanwhile (the refund route already refuses while a dispute is open; two ways of paying the sponsor back is the real loss).
5. **A professional could look at:** the bad-debt claim on a lost dispute (whether the club writes it off and when); that the ledger is cash-shaped while line 103 follows the invoice, so the ledger's "GST/HST collected" will drop on a loss while the return does not (a treasurer reading one against the other needs the accountant); and the first return that carries a lost dispute. No gate.

## Addendum 2026-11-25: a refund made in the club's own Stripe dashboard (Stage 6 fixes, Part A)
**Reviewed:** `lib/sponsor-card-server.ts` and `lib/stripe-connect.ts` (`createCheckout`, `refundToCard`), `applySponsorRefundEvent` and `settleSponsorRefund` in `lib/online-payment-landing.ts`, the addenda above, `CREDIT_NOTES_ON_REFUNDS.md` and `GST_HST_RETURN_LINES.md` §10 (re-read). Scope as above: the product's correctness, not advice to any club. Stripe pages read 2026-11-25 are listed under Sources.

**Charge type, from the code and the docs.** The sponsor's card is a **direct charge on the club's connected account** (the Checkout Session is created with `stripeAccount`; `payment_intent_data.application_fee_amount` is FieldLogicHQ's margin; the account's fee setting is `stripe`, so Stripe's own fee, refunds and disputes come out of the club's balance). Stripe: refunds of direct charges debit the connected account; **"Application fees aren't automatically refunded when issuing a refund"** (they come back only if the platform asks, `refund_application_fee`); and on a dispute, "regardless of outcome, Stripe doesn't automatically refund the application fee"; **"Stripe's processing fees from the original transaction aren't returned"** on any refund. The docs say nothing about a refund the connected account makes ITSELF in its dashboard, where the platform's flag does not exist; the safe reading is that nothing is returned. **A test-mode probe (dashboard refund on a test connected account, then read the application fee's `amount_refunded`) should confirm that before build.** Rule for the books either way: the Payment processing cost line moves only by what Stripe's data shows moved, never by assumption.

**One fact that simplifies everything.** The sponsor-paid fee carries the same rate as the sponsorship (13% of $41.05 = $5.34), so tax is 135.34 / 1,176.39 = 13/113 of every dollar of the charge. Whatever order a part refund takes, the **tax reversed is the same to within a cent**; only the split between sponsorship income and fee income changes.

1. **Q1, the date.** Two dates, each its own thing, never one backdated. The **ledger lines** carry the day the money left (Stripe's refund `created`, Toronto), or today when that year is closed (the existing rule). The **credit note** carries the day it is actually issued, the day the club confirms it. Reason: the ledger is cash-shaped and the bank check and Payouts match by date, so a refund booked weeks after the money moved would make the Payouts view and bank check disagree with Stripe; the s.232(3) period is the one in which the note is issued, so a note dated before it was written could land in a filed period (our own file, §10, found no CRA text allowing a late-recorded note dated into a filed period: UNCONFIRMED, accountant), and backdating a document is wrong on its face. With auto-booking (Q4) the two dates are normally the same day anyway; they differ only for a club that confirms the note later, and that is correct. If the owner keeps "record by hand" instead, use the recording day for both and print "Stripe returned this on {date}" on the row and the note. A recording after the year closes books into the open year (no change to the rule).
2. **Q2, the credit note.** **Yes, one is needed, issued only when the club confirms it, never automatically.** A refund that includes tax is a reduction of consideration (s.232(2)); the deduction exists only with a note issued (Memo 12.2 ¶22-23), the club's tax stays unreduced without it (safe) and the sponsor is never told. Automatic issue would put the club's name, a tax claim and an email to a business on a refund the app did not author and whose reason it does not know. The proportional rule stays (tax part = amount x invoice tax / invoice total, last takes the remainder), now over the whole charge when the fee is included. Weeks between the refund and the email is acceptable ("reasonable time", ¶15) provided the note states the day the sponsor was refunded; show the club a "note owed, N days" nudge. A sponsor who claimed the tax must adjust (¶26), so the email should say so plainly. The email's "the fee isn't part of this refund" line must go when the fee was returned.
3. **Q3, a returned fee.** Book the excess as a **reversal of the fee-income line and its HST**, and show it on the credit note as its own row at the same rate, so the note's tax equals the tax actually reversed. **Split a part refund sponsorship first, the fee only after the sponsorship is fully returned.** Reason: it matches what the in-app refund already treats as refundable, the processing service was still performed on a part refund, and the tax is identical either way (13/113). Pro rata is a second rule to explain for no tax gain. Later in-app refunds must count the sponsorship part against "refundable" and the fee part against a separate "fee returned", so the fee is never returned twice. **Payment processing cost does not move** (Stripe keeps its fee, FieldLogicHQ keeps its application fee); the club is simply out Stripe's and our fee on the refunded amount, which is correct. If the probe above shows the application fee came back, reduce that cost line by exactly `amount_refunded`.
4. **Q4, at once or offered.** **Argue partly against the lean.** Waiting overstates income, GST/HST collected AND Cash on hand in a cash-shaped ledger by the whole refund, which misleads exactly the treasurer the ledger is for; the "safe for tax" argument is true of the return (line 103 is gross and line 107 needs the note) and so is an argument for waiting on the NOTE, not on the money lines. **Recommend two steps: the money lines book automatically when Stripe says succeeded (actor "Stripe dashboard", reason from Stripe's refund `reason` field, plainly marked as not reviewed); the credit note, the sponsor email and the deal's outcome wait for the club.** The deal then reads owing again, as after a lost dispute (above, item 2), not Withdrawn: Withdrawn takes the logo down and is the club's decision, offered in the same window. The family flows may record automatically because they issue no tax document; this one does. If the owner prefers the lean as drawn, the cost is real but bounded: show the deal "refund waiting" and subtract it from "Cash on hand" at once so the band is right even before recording.
5. **Q5, pending then failed.** Count a pending refund against refundable at once (it will almost surely leave; a second in-app refund would pay twice) and **book nothing while pending**, matching the app. Stripe says a card refund goes pending for up to 45 days when the balance is short and then fails, that a dispute opened on a pending refund fails it (`charge_for_pending_refund_disputed`), and that a refund can fail even after the bank returns it (up to 30 days). So the existing bounce path (void the two lines, mark the credit note cancelled, email a correction) must serve auto-booked refunds too, and a failed or canceled one releases the count.
6. **Q6, before the payment has landed.** Do **not net**. The invoice is issued when the payment lands and shows what the sponsor was charged; book the landing on its day, then the refund as its own record, dated the later of Stripe's refund day and the landing day, with the note after the invoice exists. Netting would make the books and the invoice disagree with Stripe's charge. A full refund of a never-recorded payment stays as today (no books, no invoice, no note; same logic as R3 in `CREDIT_NOTES_ON_REFUNDS.md`). A professional could look at: a late landing dates the invoice later than the sponsor's payment, and RC4022's tax point is the earlier of payment and due date.
7. **Q7, after a lost dispute.** **Post no lines and issue no note.** The dispute loss already reversed the receipt lines; booking again reverses twice. Stripe states "you can't issue a refund outside the dispute process while the dispute is open", so this arises only after a LOSS (the docs do not say a lost charge cannot be refunded; not found, so treat it as possible). A refund then pays the sponsor a second time, money the sponsor still owes the club: raise a notice ("this charge was taken back by the dispute; this refund returned it twice; ask the sponsor to return it"), and keep it out of refundable. If the club instead decides to forgive the debt, that is a credit note with the tax part and no lines (the lines are already out), a separate step, not this one.

**Defects and owner calls (to `/plan`).** (a) The notice "refund from the deal's payment next time" leaves refundable unchanged, so a later in-app refund can return money already returned (the Part A defect). (b) A dashboard refund must reduce refundable the moment it is pending. (c) Probe the dashboard-refund application-fee behaviour before build. (d) Owner calls: money lines automatic and note by the club (recommended) versus everything by hand; a returned fee sponsorship-first (recommended) versus pro rata; deal reads owing, not Withdrawn, after a dashboard refund. **A professional could look at:** the credit note dated after the refund, a note dated into a filed period, the sponsor's own ITC adjustment, and the fee-income reversal under the GI-200 reading. No gate.

## Addendum 2026-11-26: correction to the 2026-11-25 addendum's Q2 and Q4, and the rule as built
**Reviewed:** `supabase/migrations/542_a_refund_made_in_stripe.sql` (built and applied on dev 2026-11-26), `lib/club-sponsor-invoice.ts` (`owingBlock`, `finishOwesAgain`, `creditNoteLayout`), `lib/sponsor-invoice-email.ts`. No source re-read: this applies the rule already on file (a credit note adjusts tax only when the consideration is reduced; the dispute addendum, item 1). Same scope: the product's correctness, not advice to any club.

**What I got wrong.** Q2 said a refund that includes tax needs a credit note once the club confirms it; Q4 said the deal then reads owing again. Together they are a contradiction: a credit note says the price came down, "owing again" says it did not. Where the money stays owed there is no reduction of consideration and **no credit note** (Q2 holds only where the price really comes down). Q4's "reads owing again, not Withdrawn" as an automatic step is also not what was built: the money lines book by themselves and the deal's state moves only when the club finishes.

**The defect it produced.** A sponsor who paid the $46.39 fee ($41.05 + $5.34 HST) on $1,176.39, was refunded only the $1,130.00 sponsorship and was marked "still owe it", was asked for $1,176.39 again: **the fee paid twice** ($46.39 still held by the club), beside a credit note saying the invoice was reduced by $1,130.00.

**The rule as built** (owner 2026-11-26: "Fee first, no note"; 542 section 10).
1. "They still owe it" is taken only when everything the sponsor paid on that payment, the fee included, has gone back (refunds that succeeded) and no earlier refund on it has a credit note.
2. A finish that leaves the money owed again issues **no credit note**, whether the club chose it or what else stands no longer covers the invoice, and neither does any later finish on that payment. The "credit note owed" words come off the tax line where its year is open (a closed year's line is never edited, so it keeps them although no note will follow).
3. Otherwise a taxed refund finished from Stripe gets its note, dated the day it is issued. The note now **names the day the money left** when that differs, and **says the fee amount returned** ("$X of it is the processing fee you paid, tax included").

**What the books show** (sponsor paid the fee; amounts before and after tax as in the 2026-11-23 addendum).

| After | Income | GST/HST collected | Processing cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| The card payment of $1,176.39 | $1,041.05 ($1,000.00 + $41.05 fee) | $135.34 | $41.05 |
| Both refunds, $1,130.00 and $46.39 | $0.00 | $0.00 | $41.05 stays (the club is out $41.05) |
| Repayment of $1,176.39 | $1,041.05 | $135.34 | the $41.05 stays; a card repayment adds its own processing cost, paid by the club |

The invoice is never revised, so its tax (line 103, by invoice date) is unchanged throughout and no line 107 adjustment arises. The ledger is cash-shaped: if the refunds and the repayment fall in different periods, its "GST/HST collected" falls and returns while the return does not. The repayment books as sponsorship income in one line (no separate fee-income line): the fee row is now just part of what is owing.

**Open follow-up, to `/plan`.**
- **A refund from the deal** of one card part-payment, beside another standing payment that doesn't cover the invoice, still reopens the deal **and** issues a credit note (`club_sponsor_card_refund_book`; the same contradiction, shipped earlier in 530 to 534).
- **The same shape remains on one Stripe path:** a part refund finished with a credit note, then the rest of that payment refunded while another payment stands, leaves the deal owing the whole payment again with the first note still standing (the earlier-note check runs only when "They still owe it" is chosen). The second refund gets no note, but the first one's reduction is asked for again.

**A professional could look at:** returning money but keeping the debt with no credit note; collecting a "Processing fee" row by cheque (an invoice owing again keeps the row, and nothing was spent on a card for that payment). No gate.
