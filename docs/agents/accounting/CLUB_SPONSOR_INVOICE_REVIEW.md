# A club's sponsorship invoice, payments and GST/HST collected (Board Ready Stage 6 Part 3a)
**Last verified:** 2026-11-23 (Part 3a: read from the code, migration 528 and the existing return-lines file; Part 3b addendum below: migration 530 and GI-200 re-read)
**Re-verify by:** 12 months; a CRA change to the invoice-information tiers; before a club books GST/HST collected into a return; if revised invoices gain a credit-note path
**Depends on facts:** none of FieldLogicHQ's own (this is the PRODUCT's feature for a club's own tax; scope note: a club's tax is outside this agent's charter, so this is a product-correctness check, not advice to any club)

## Question(s) this answers
- What must a registered club's sponsorship invoice show, and does the PDF show it?
- Is the proportional tax split on part payments right? Is "GST/HST collected" on payment the right timing?
- Is a revised invoice with the same number acceptable? Is the paid invoice a valid receipt?

## Finding
- **Tiers (since 2021-04-20, `GST_HST_ON_SUBSCRIPTIONS_CANADA.md`):** under $100: name, date, total; $100 to $499.99: add the tax (amount or rate) and the supplier's GST/HST number; $500+: add the buyer's name, a description and the terms. ($30/$150 is the old table.) The PDF carries all of them on a taxed invoice: club name, issued date, total, "HST 13%" row with its amount, "GST/HST no. ..." at the top (only when taxed), Bill to, description with deliverables, "Due {date}" while unpaid. Untaxed: no tax words, no number, as ruled.
- **Tax point is the invoice, not the payment** (RC4022, `GST_HST_RETURN_LINES.md` §6): GST/HST on an invoice is reportable on the earlier of payment and due date, in practice the invoice date, paid or not. The ledger line "GST/HST collected" is posted on the payment date (cash). It reads right as cash held, but it is NOT the figure for return line 103 while invoices are unpaid.
- **Part payments:** proportional split, last payment takes the remainder, void drops its share and the next payment re-takes it; the sum of live tax parts always equals the invoice tax. Correct and cent-exact.
- **Revised invoice, same number:** acceptable while nothing has been paid and the period is open. A reduction after the period was filed needs a credit note (s.232; RC4022; a bare void earns no line 107 adjustment). Migration 528 overwrites the original figures, so the original invoice cannot be reproduced from the books.
- **Paid invoice as receipt:** valid ITC documentation (the information tiers are met by the same page). Never "tax receipt".
- **"Generated by FieldLogicHQ" footer:** a tool credit, not a supplier claim; the club can switch it off on paid plans.

## Recommendation
1. Say plainly, once (help article or a hint on the GST/HST collected row), that the figure counts when money arrives and that the return counts from the invoice date; show invoiced-not-yet-paid tax somewhere a treasurer can read it.
2. Keep the original figures when an invoice is revised (migration 528 is dev-only so far, so it can still be amended).
3. Snapshot the club's GST/HST number on a taxed invoice so a later registration change cannot blank it on a reprint.
4. A club that pays CRA records an ordinary expense, which Budget vs. Actual counts as Spent while the tax collected is not in Collected; give that payment its own excluded word.

## Addendum 2026-11-23: Part 3b, a sponsor pays by card, the fee, the credit note (read from the code; GI-200 re-read today)
**Reviewed:** `lib/online-payment-fee.ts` (`sponsorCardFee`), `lib/sponsor-card-server.ts`, migration 530 (card landing, refund open/book/failed, void refusal), `lib/club-sponsor-invoice.ts` (refund sums, fee row, credit note layout), the invoice PDF, `isFeeRefund`, the platform item. Scope as above: the product's correctness, not advice to any club. The credit-note rule itself (Memo 12.2 ¶15-23; SOR/91-44 s.3) is in [CREDIT_NOTES_ON_REFUNDS.md](CREDIT_NOTES_ON_REFUNDS.md) and is not restated; that file is about OUR invoices, so the club-side answer of 2026-11-23 ("a refund on a taxed invoice needs a credit note") lived only in the build notes until now.

**Arithmetic, confirmed to the cent from the code.** $1,000.00 + 13% = $1,130.00. Fee = 3.5% + $1.50 on $1,130.00 = $41.05 (`onlineFee`, half-up). Club pays: sponsor pays $1,130.00, club nets $1,088.95. Sponsor pays: fee tax = round($41.05 × 13%) = $5.34, charge $1,176.39, tax total $135.34 ($130.00 + $5.34), club receives $1,135.34 (the charge less the fee), keeps $1,000.00 after remitting $135.34. A $200.00 part refund = $176.99 + $23.01 HST (200 × 130/1130 = 23.0088, SQL and TS agree); the fee ($41.05) and its HST ($5.34) are excluded from "refundable" ($1,130.00), so the club keeps them; refunding the last of what is refundable ends the deal Withdrawn. **A card landing books FOUR ledger lines, not five:** sponsorship income, fee income (only when the sponsor paid it), GST/HST collected, Payment processing cost (three when the club pays; fewer again with no tax). Stripe's "extra" on a foreign card is a separate later line, not part of the landing.

**What holds.** (1) Passing the fee on as its own invoice row with the club's rate on it is the conservative tax answer (under-collecting is the club's exposure; over-collecting is remitted). (2) Income gross, fee as a separate cost, tax under its own word, never income or spending. (3) Credit note meets s.3 on every point in the topic file: titled credit note, the club's name and GST/HST number, the sponsor's name, the issue date, the tax adjusted and its rate, and the invoice's number and date (beyond Memo ¶20's "should"). A separate PDF per refund is right (what Stripe does for ours); the invoice stays unrevised and lists the refund. The 0.00-tax-part case cannot over-claim: it needs a refund of about $0.04 at 13%, the adjustment is then nothing, and the refund that empties the payment takes the remainder, so the credit notes' tax always sums to the invoice's tax (not the fee's). (4) Refund lines carry the REFUND day (`club_sponsor_book_day(org, NULL)` = today, Toronto), never the payment's, and the credit note's issue date is that same day, so the adjustment lands in the period it is issued; a filed period is never moved.

**What departs from an earlier answer, and a correction of mine.** `BOARD_READY_STAGE6_SPONSOR_PAYMENT_FEE_REVIEW.md` said (GI-200 "not re-read") that tax follows the underlying supply, so the club's HST applies to a passed-on surcharge line. **GI-200 (March 2023, page of 2023-09-29), read today, says the opposite for a true credit card surcharge:** one charged solely for accepting the card, imposed by the merchant, and shown and charged separately from the price and every other fee is consideration for a separate financial service and "will not be subject to the GST/HST". But the same sheet says a fee that is in whole or part for the use of property such as an online payment platform "is not a credit card surcharge". The product's fee is that second kind (it includes FieldLogicHQ's margin, is charged on every card, and a bank-debit version exists), so taxing it as part of the sponsorship's consideration is the better-supported reading, and the safe one. The cost of a wrong reading: if it were a pure surcharge the sponsor is over-charged $5.34 on $1,176.39 (the club still remits what it collected; the sponsor can ask for it back). **The catch is consistency:** the club's Payouts hint (`lib/club-sponsorships-words.ts:255,261`) frames the fee as a surcharge for the networks' 2.4% cap (this fee is about 3.6% of $1,130.00 against an acceptance cost of about 2.9%), while the tax treatment frames it as not a surcharge. The two answers must be the same answer; the tax position leans on "platform fee", and the network rules treat a service or convenience fee as a different, narrower thing (FCAC; `docs/agents/legal/CARD_SURCHARGE_PAYER_FEE.md`). The hint also leaves out that networks restrict surcharging debit and prepaid cards and require advance notice (that file); wording is `/legal`'s.

**Fee absorbed by the club (the default, and always on an issued invoice).** Booked as one gross cost under Sponsorship > Payment processing; income is the whole invoice. Correct. The tax inside FieldLogicHQ's application fee (about $0.92 on $1,130.00, R1) is potentially an input tax credit for a registered club making taxable supplies; Stripe's own fee carries none. The product records no tax on the fee and claims no credit (no club-side credit field exists), which under-claims, the safe direction; the document that would support a claim is the open Q2 in `STRIPE_OWNED_PRICING_ACCOUNTANT_QUESTIONS.md`.

**Defects (routed to /plan).**
1. **A lost sponsor card dispute is silent and unbooked.** `lib/online-payment-landing.ts:562-566` returns after a `console.warn`; income, GST/HST collected and "Paid" stay, while Stripe takes the money (the $15 dispute fee IS booked, `bookDisputeFee`). Not an accepted limitation: the books overstate income and the club is not told. A loss should reverse the payment's lines without a credit note (the club did not refund; the sponsor still owes, so the deal returns to owing and the tax stays in the return until written off as a bad debt, line 107, `GST_HST_RETURN_LINES.md` §8). The club's hand route today is a manual Ledger expense, which counts as spending and cannot carry the tax part (I found no manual route that writes "GST/HST collected"; only the deal's own payment and refund code does); the accountant adjusts the return.
2. **A credit note for a refund that later bounces stands.** `club_sponsor_card_refund_failed` (migration 530:696-713) voids the two lines but keeps `credit_seq`, and the note was already emailed; only the download link disappears (`lib/club-sponsor-invoice-pdf.ts:34`). The sponsor holds a credit note for money that did not reach the card; the club reduced its tax on it. Needs a cancelled-note message and an instruction to the club.
3. **A refund made in the club's Stripe dashboard gets a notice only** (`online-payment-landing.ts:2166-2175`): no booking, no credit note, so the GST/HST stays unreduced (safe) and the club cannot claim the adjustment. Offer to book it with its credit note.
4. **The club's GST/HST number on the invoice and the note is read live, not kept** (`lib/club-sponsor-invoice-pdf.ts:61`, `clubInvoiceLayout`): Part 3a recommendation 3 is still open; a credit note reprinted after the number changed or was cleared can lose the registration number s.3 requires.
5. Latent: `creditNoteLayout` prints "-C1" for a refund with no number (`lib/club-sponsor-invoice.ts:242`); unreachable today because the PDF route refuses one.

**Soft points.** The invoice never nets refunds (`invoiceStanding` counts gross): a part-refunded invoice still reads "Paid in full" with a Refunds table; "paid, after refunds" exists only on the credit note (`club-sponsor-invoice.ts:266`). Fine for tax (invoice plus note), but the walk brief says the invoice shows it, which is wrong. After a full refund the club keeps the fee and its HST on a Withdrawn deal; remitting that tax is the safe side. A deal with a cheque and a card payment refunded in full on the card stays Paid (the cheque holds money). The fee income sits under Club sponsorship, so that line reads $1,041.05 beside a $1,000.00 plan, offset by the cost (net right, line gross). The platform item's fixed id is club-only (`coach-budget-item-tiers.ts:71-73`). Fee-tax rounding is half-up and the invoice total of the two roundings equals one rounding here.

**Owner calls.** (a) Keep taxing the passed-on fee (recommended: yes) and state the position in the hint, or treat it as a surcharge and stop taxing it. (b) The sponsor-paid fee income under Club sponsorship (gross, as built) versus netting it against Payment processing. (c) Whether the invoice itself should show net-of-refunds standing. (d) Whether a lost dispute ships as a documented limitation (not recommended: defect 1).

**A professional could look at.** Whether the passed-on fee is a GI-200 surcharge or taxable consideration (a club's accountant; or a CRA ruling), and the same for FieldLogicHQ's D1; bad-debt relief on a lost dispute; the first club return carrying a credit note; the credit on the tax inside our fee; whether the kept fee and its HST after a full refund should come back; and, for a lawyer, surcharge versus service fee, debit and prepaid cards, advance notice and Québec (`/legal`).
