## Capacity, seats & inclusions

- **Club teams:** all team types (rep, **select, development**) count **equally** toward the 15 / 30 band caps. **No discounted "select" tier.** Above 30 teams = custom quote.
- **Club includes the whole coaching staff's Premium portals** — no per-team fee. **The "$19/team beyond 3" meter is retired** (Decided / not yet built).
- **Coach bridge:** standalone Premium Coaches Portal is $29/mo until the coach's org joins Club, at which point the portal is **included** in Club (coach stops paying). *(The old "$19/team, $10 less than standalone" bridge is gone.)*
- **Free-tier staff seats (3):** count admin/staff only. **Officials & scorekeepers are exempt** (✅ LIVE — `officialsFreeSeats: true` in config; the 2026-06-22 changelog entry shipped long ago, this line had never caught up). **Paid ORG tiers = unlimited seats.** **The standalone Premium Coaches Portal keeps the free-tier 3-seat guard on its bundled tournament-admin side ONLY** (ratified 2026-08-10, `BUSINESS_DECISIONS.md`): the cap covers the coach + org-side co-organizer/staff invites for the portal one included tournament; **coaching staff — assistants and helpers — is unlimited on every tier** (team-level invites, no plan gate, no org-member row). The officials exemption extends to the portal in the same ruling (✅ built 2026-08-10, same unit of work).
- **Module inclusions**: Tournament Plus = tournaments + comms workflow; League Plus adds public site + house league **+ Families**; Club adds accounting + rep teams (+ whole-staff portals) **+ Families**.
- **Families area = League Plus and Club bands** (owner decision 2026-08-17, `CLUB_FAMILIES_BOOK_PLAN.md` §1.2; **BUILT on dev 2026-08-17** — `module_families` in `lib/plan-config.ts`). Club-admin household records: the family worklist, the family page, the duplicate review queue, message-the-household, and per-family data export. **No new SKU, no price change** — it rides the existing module-entitlement mechanism. **Off by default for every role** even where the plan carries it: a member needs an explicit per-person grant (owners always have it). Not on Tournament / Tournament Plus / the standalone Premium Coaches Portal — households only exist where a club runs several teams, and the area is **absent, not upsold** (no teaser tile).
- **Family layer = Premium Coaches Portal / Club** (Decided 2026-08-01; **follower tier BUILT on dev 2026-08-01**). A Premium portal now includes the parent/guardian-facing experience: the revocable **team family link**, the coach's approval queue, the per-team **Schedule visibility** setting (Staff only / Families / Public link), the one-list **family schedule view**, shareable **game pages**, the **calendar subscription feed**, and **game-change / final-score alerts** to connected families. Basic/free teams get none of it. **No new SKU, no price change, no per-family fee, and no new plan key** — it rides the existing per-team entitlement check (`getTeamScopedRepTeamAccess`), with org-native Club teams passing via the org plan, so `lib/plan-config.ts` and `lib/plan-features.ts` are deliberately **untouched** by this build. The **guardian** tier (child-linked: the player band and the announcements archive) is **BUILT on dev but SWITCHED OFF** behind a server-side flag that refuses every guardian write while off — **turning it on is gated on PIPEDA/CASL counsel sign-off** recorded in `BUSINESS_DECISIONS.md`. See `COACH_PORTAL_CHUNK_D_COUNSEL_PACKET.md`. **No packaging consequence either way:** the guardian tier rides the same premium team entitlement as the rest of the family layer — no new SKU, price, band or feature key, so this table is unaffected by the switch's state.

- **PDF document customization = Premium Coaches Portal too** (Decided 2026-08-21, `BUSINESS_DECISIONS.md`; **BUILT on dev 2026-08-22** — PDF Export Quality Phase 1). Customization of exported documents (logo/crest, accent colour, footer) was Tournament Plus and above via `pdf_template_settings`; the standalone **Premium Coaches Portal (`team`) now carries an explicit grant** in `lib/plan-features.ts` `PLAN_FEATURE_GRANTS`, alongside its existing `pdf_exports` grant. Ships with the two-layer branding model: every Premium coach portal (standalone AND club-owned) gets a team-level "How your documents look" card (crest/accent/footer) in team settings; club PDF settings stay the club-admin surface and the inherited default for its teams' paper. **Basic (free) Coaches Portal unchanged — no PDF export, so no customization surface. No price, plan name, capacity band or SKU moved; `lib/plan-config.ts` untouched** (feature keys live in `lib/plan-features.ts`).
- **Roster export = Premium Coaches Portal inclusion** (Decided 2026-08-23, `BUSINESS_DECISIONS.md`; **already shipped** — the ruling ratifies behaviour, it does not change a gate). A coach on the standalone **Premium Coaches Portal** exports their own team's roster in **Excel, CSV and PDF**, including the two roster PDFs added by PDF Export Quality Phase 2 (the wall copy, and the contacts sheet for coaches holding family-contact access). **Basic (free) Coaches Portal unchanged** — it has no PDF export and operates on a separate, org-less surface. ⚠ **There is no feature key for this and none should be added.** The gate is architectural: the coaches-portal roster is reachable only through a rep-team staff membership, which exists only for a Premium Coaches Portal team or a club-owned Club team — so `lib/plan-config.ts` and `lib/plan-features.ts` are **deliberately untouched** (the `team` grant already carries `pdf_exports`, which is all the PDF path consults). **No price, plan name, capacity band or SKU moved.** ⚠ **Do not read plan inclusions out of `lib/export/catalog.ts`** — its `minPlan`/`moduleGate` fields are *descriptive documentation that nothing enforces at runtime*, and the `coaches-roster` entry asserted Club-only for months while the product never behaved that way. That drift is what produced this ruling; `lib/plan-features.ts` plus the surface's own guard are the truth.
- **Club Shared Book = Club only** (Decided 2026-08-04, `BUSINESS_DECISIONS.md`; **BUILT on dev 2026-08-05**). A club's teams can opt in to read each other's opponent scouting books — book line, observations, and each team's own record against a shared opponent, labelled by team and writer, read-only across team lines. Gated by the feature key **`club_shared_book` → `club`** (so Club and Club · Association; League Plus and below get nothing). **Explicitly excluded from the standalone Premium Coaches Portal (`team`)**: the feature's value is a club with several teams, and the ruling is **absent, not upsold** — a single-team customer sees no switch, no layer and no locked tease anywhere in the app. **No new SKU, no price change, no per-team fee, no capacity implication.** Two opt-ins guard it: the club admin enables it for the org, then each head coach opts their own team in, and a team reads the club's books only while sharing its own.
