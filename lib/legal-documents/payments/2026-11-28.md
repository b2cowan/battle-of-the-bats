## 1. What these Payments Terms cover

These Payments Terms are added to our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) for an organization that takes payments online
through FieldLogicHQ. They apply from the moment an owner of your organization ticks "I agree to the FieldLogicHQ
payments terms" before setting up payouts. Words that the Terms of Service define, such as "you", "we" and "Customer
Data", mean the same here. Where these Payments Terms and the Terms of Service say different things about online
payments, these Payments Terms apply.

"**Payer**" means a family, team, person or business that pays your organization online through FieldLogicHQ. A **sponsor** is a business or person that pays your organization for a sponsorship deal.

## 2. Who sells, who runs the software, and who processes the payment

- **Your organization is the seller.** When a payer pays online, they are paying your organization for your registration, program, team fee, tournament entry or sponsorship. The sale is between your organization and the payer. Receipts and the payer's card or bank statement show your organization's name.
- **FieldLogicHQ provides the software.** We build the pay page, send the receipt in your name, and record the payment in
  your books. We are not a party to the sale, we do not sell anything to the payer, and we do not receive or hold the
  payment.
- **Stripe processes the payment.** To take payments online, your organization opens its own Stripe account through
  FieldLogicHQ and accepts Stripe's own agreements, which are between your organization and Stripe. Stripe checks your
  organization's identity and bank account; we do not. Stripe may ask for more information, pause payments, hold a payout
  or close your Stripe account under its agreements, and we cannot override its decisions.

## 3. Where the money goes

- Each payment goes into your organization's Stripe account. Stripe pays it out to your bank account on Stripe's
  schedule. **FieldLogicHQ never holds the payment.**
- At the moment of each payment, Stripe takes its own fee for processing it, and takes our fee out of it and pays it to
  us (§4). What is left is your organization's.
- Your Stripe account belongs to your organization. The person who set up payouts signs in to it from the Payouts card,
  and your payouts are listed in FieldLogicHQ for the people who read your organization's money.

## 4. The processing fee, and our fee

- **What it is.** Each online payment carries a processing fee, at the rates shown on your Payouts card and in "Before
  you set up payouts": one rate for a card payment and another for a bank debit. The processing fee is made of two
  parts:
  - **Stripe's fee** for processing the payment, which Stripe charges your organization directly, under your
    organization's agreement with Stripe and at Stripe's prices (§5); and
  - **our fee**, which is **our charge to your organization** for FieldLogicHQ's online payment features: the pay page,
    receipts in your name, and recording each payment in your books.

  We set our fee so that, at Stripe's standard Canadian prices, the two parts together equal the processing fee. Where
  Stripe charges more than its standard Canadian price, for example for a card issued outside Canada (§5), the two parts
  together come to more than the processing fee.
- **How it is paid.** Stripe takes both parts out of each payment as it is made: its own fee for itself, and our fee for
  us. Your organization receives the rest.
- **Who pays it in the end is your choice.** On the Payouts card, your owners choose one of two settings for registrations, team fees and tournament entries (sponsorships have their own setting, below):
  - **the payer pays it:** your organization adds the processing fee to what the payer owes, as your own charge, shown
    as "Processing fee" at checkout and on the receipt. Your organization receives the full amount it asked for; or
  - **your organization pays it:** the payer pays only what you asked for, and your organization receives that amount
    less the processing fee.

  Either way, our fee is our charge to your organization, taken from each payment, and Stripe's fee is your
  organization's to pay Stripe. On a payment where Stripe charges more than its standard Canadian price, your
  organization receives that much less (§5). When the payer pays the processing fee, your organization is passing its own cost on as its own charge to the payer, and §9 applies to that
  charge. A change to this setting applies to payments started after the change; anything already paid stays as it was.
- **Sponsorships have their own setting.** If your organization uses Sponsorships, the Payouts card has a second row, "Processing fee on sponsorships". Your owners choose one of two settings, separate from the one above:
  - **your organization pays it** (the default): the business pays exactly what its invoice says, and the processing fee comes out of the payment as your organization's cost; or
  - **the business pays it:** your organization adds the processing fee to what the business pays by card, as its own charge, shown as "Processing fee", with GST/HST on it when the invoice carries tax. Your organization receives the full amount of the invoice.
- **When the business pays it, the setting counts only before an invoice exists.** On an invoice that has already been issued, your organization always pays the processing fee, whatever the setting says, and an issued invoice never gains a fee line. §9 applies to a processing fee that the business pays, in the same way as to a payer's.
- **Only a card payment adds a fee.** No processing fee is added to a sponsorship paid by cheque, e-transfer or cash. One case to know: if an invoice that already has a processing fee line becomes owing again (after a refund in your Stripe account, §6, or a lost dispute, §7), the line stays on the invoice, so the amount owing includes it however the business pays. If the business then pays by card, your organization pays the processing fee on that new payment.
- **This setting is not the tax on our fee.** The GST/HST on a processing fee the business pays is your organization's own charge to the business (§8). Any tax on our fee stays included in our fee (above).
- A change to the sponsorship setting applies to card pages opened after the change; a payment already made stays as it was.
- **Sales tax.** Any sales tax that applies to our fee, such as GST/HST, is **included in our fee**, worked out from your
  organization's billing address. Nothing is added on top.
- **Neither part is refunded** when you refund a payment or when a payer wins a dispute: we keep our fee, and Stripe
  keeps its own under its pricing. So a processing fee your organization gives back in a refund is your organization's
  cost. The one exception: when a bank debit fails before it clears, our fee on that payment comes back to your
  organization automatically. Stripe returns or keeps its own fee under its pricing, and may charge a separate fee for
  the failed debit (§5). A bank debit that the payer's bank takes back after it has cleared is not this exception: it is
  handled as a dispute, and neither part comes back (§7).
- **Changes to our rates.** We will email your organization's owners **at least 30 days before** a rate goes up. The new
  rate applies to payments started on or after the date in the email. A lower rate can apply straight away.

## 5. Stripe charges your organization directly

Stripe's fees for your organization's online payments are **charged by Stripe to your organization**, under your
organization's agreement with Stripe and at Stripe's prices, and they come out of your organization's Stripe balance.
**FieldLogicHQ does not bill them, and we bill your organization nothing for online payments beyond our fee (§4).**

Besides its fee for processing each payment, Stripe charges fees for some payments and events. They include fees for:
- **a dispute:** a fee when a payer disputes a card payment, and a further fee if your organization answers it. Winning
  the dispute does not return the first fee; whether the answering fee comes back is set by Stripe's pricing;
- **a bank debit** that fails, or that the payer's bank takes back after it has cleared; and
- **a card issued outside Canada**, and **a currency conversion** where one is needed. This can happen whenever a payer
  from another country pays you, for example a visiting team at your tournament.

**What your organization receives can vary.** On a payment that carries one of these fees, and on any payment where
Stripe charges more than its standard Canadian price, your organization receives less than the amounts in §4. Stripe
publishes its current prices on its website.

**Stripe can change its fees** under its agreement with your organization. We do not set them and cannot change them.

**If your organization's Stripe balance goes below zero,** for example after a refund or a lost dispute once the money
has been paid out, **your organization owes the difference to Stripe.** Stripe may take it from later payments or
debit your organization's bank account, under its agreement with your organization. FieldLogicHQ does not cover it.

## 6. Refunds

- **Your organization decides refunds.** You set your own refund policy and decide each refund. We do not decide refunds
  or make them on your behalf.
- **Your refund policy.** Your owners write your refund policy on the Payouts card before online payments start, and
  payers can read it beside the Pay button before they pay.
- **Refunds come out of your organization's Stripe balance.** What happens when the balance is too low is set by your
  agreement with Stripe.
- **Neither part of the processing fee comes back on a refund** (§4). When you include the processing fee in a refund,
  the whole of it comes out of your organization's balance.
- **After you disconnect** (§10), refunds of payments already made are made in Stripe.
- **Refunding a sponsor.** Your organization decides each refund of a sponsorship, and makes it from the deal in FieldLogicHQ, with a reason, or in its own Stripe account. From the deal, you can refund all of what can go back or part of it. We do not decide refunds or make them on your behalf. Anyone at your organization who can open Sponsorships can refund from the deal, and can finish a refund made in Stripe.
- **The processing fee.** A refund from the deal never includes the processing fee or its GST/HST, including a fee that the business paid. A refund in your Stripe account can include it, after all of the sponsorship: what you refund there counts as sponsorship money first, and as the fee only once all of that payment's sponsorship money has gone back. Either way, neither part comes back to your organization (§4). Whether your organization returns a fee the business paid is between your organization and the business.
- **A refund made in your Stripe account.** A Stripe refund is recorded on the deal and in your books when Stripe confirms it. While Stripe is still sending it, the deal already shows it as on its way, and it counts against what you can refund from the deal. The credit note and the business's email wait until you finish the refund on the deal, with a reason. The business's sponsorship page lists the refund even before you finish it.
- **Whether the deal ends.** A refund from the deal that gives back the last of the deal's sponsorship money ends the deal. When a refund made in your Stripe account gives back the last of it, you choose as you finish it: "It has ended" or "They still owe it". If another payment on the deal still stands, the deal does not end. When all of one payment's sponsorship money has been refunded and the other payments no longer cover the invoice, the deal reads as owing again.
- **When you can choose "They still owe it".** Only once everything the business paid on that payment, the processing fee included, has been refunded, and only if no earlier refund on that payment has a credit note.
- **No credit note for money still owed.** When you finish a refund made in your Stripe account and it leaves the money owing again, because you chose "They still owe it" or because the remaining payments no longer cover the invoice, no credit note is issued for that refund, or for any later refund of that payment. The price of the sponsorship has not changed.
- **A refund after the bank has taken the payment back.** If you refund, in Stripe, a payment that the business's bank has already taken back through a dispute you lost (§7), we do not put the refund in your books and no credit note is issued, because that money is already out of them. We tell your organization that the business has been paid back twice. If you later win the dispute, we record the refund then, for you to finish.
- **What we send.** We email the business a notice of a refund, with your reason: for a refund from the deal, when it reaches the card; for a refund made in Stripe, when you finish it. When the invoice carried GST/HST, we also issue a credit note for the refund, in your organization's name, except as set out above. We can send the email only if your organization has a mailing address and a contact email for sponsors (the one in your sponsorship settings, or your public contact email) and the business has an email address on file. If the email cannot go, telling the business is up to your organization. When a refund leaves the invoice owing, the email and the business's sponsorship page say that it is still owing, and how much.
- **If a refund does not reach the card.** If the card company sends back a refund that is already in your books, we take it out of your organization's books and tell your organization in its notifications. A credit note issued for that refund is marked cancelled (its number is kept and never used again). If we had emailed the business about the refund, we also email it (on the same conditions as above) that the refund did not reach its card, and that the credit note is cancelled if there was one. A Stripe refund that fails before it reaches your books changes nothing in them. Your organization decides what to do next: refund again, or send the money another way and record it.
- **How long it takes.** A refund goes back to the card the business paid with. It can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the card company; we cannot say exactly when.
- **No refund while the card is disputed.** We will not make a refund of a card payment while the business's card company is disputing it (§7).
- **A payment the deal cannot take.** If a card payment arrives for a deal that has been withdrawn, or that was invoiced another way in the meantime, we do not record it, and the money stays in your organization's Stripe account. Your organization refunds it in Stripe. We remind your organization that it is there.

## 7. Disputes and returned bank debits

- **A payer can dispute a payment** with their card issuer or bank. While it is decided, Stripe takes the disputed amount
  from your organization's Stripe balance.
- **Your organization answers it, in Stripe.** The person who set up payouts signs in to Stripe to send the evidence.
  FieldLogicHQ can, for payments other than a sponsorship, gather the facts of the payment for you to copy, but cannot answer a dispute for you. The card issuer
  or bank decides the outcome.
- **If the payer wins,** the disputed amount stays with them and neither part of the processing fee is returned (§4).
  Stripe's dispute fees come out of your organization's Stripe balance (§5).
- **If a bank debit fails before it clears,** the payment never reaches your books: it is marked returned and the payer
  owes the amount again. Our fee on it comes back (§4); any fee Stripe charges for the failed debit comes out of your
  organization's Stripe balance (§5).
- **If the payer's bank takes back a bank debit after it has cleared,** it is handled as a dispute. It is marked
  returned by the bank in your books, and the payer owes the amount again. Neither part of the processing fee comes back
  (§4), and Stripe's dispute fee comes out of your organization's Stripe balance (§5).
- If your organization's Stripe balance goes below zero, §5 applies.
- **A dispute of a sponsor's card payment is answered in Stripe, and recorded in FieldLogicHQ.** For a sponsorship payment, FieldLogicHQ records the dispute and tells whoever at your organization opens Sponsorships when it opens and when it is decided. Your organization answers it in Stripe, under its agreement with Stripe; we cannot answer it for you. **If your organization loses it,** the amount Stripe takes back comes out of your organization's books in the same way as the payment went in, the deal returns to owing, and no credit note is issued, because your organization did not refund anyone and the business still owes the invoice. **If your organization wins it** after that, the payment is put back. We do not decide for your organization whether to ask the business to pay again or to withdraw the deal, and we do not adjust the GST/HST on an invoice that has been issued; your organization (with its accountant) decides how to treat the tax on an amount it does not recover. Neither part of the processing fee is returned (§4); Stripe's dispute fees come out of your organization's Stripe balance (§5). A dispute that covers only part of a payment, or a payment that was never recorded here, is not booked by us: we tell your organization so that it can record it.

## 10. Switching online payments off, and ending

- **Disconnecting.** Your owners can disconnect payouts at any time from the Payouts card. Pay pages stop opening for new
  payments. Payments already made stay in your books, refunds of them are made in Stripe, and Stripe's fees on them are
  still owed to Stripe under your agreement with Stripe. Your Stripe account stays your organization's; to close it, close it with Stripe.
- **When your plan ends or moves to a plan without online payments,** open pay pages close and no new ones open.
  Refunds and disputes of payments already made keep working in FieldLogicHQ.
- **We may stop online payments for your organization** under §11 of the Terms of Service, or if Stripe stops providing
  the service to your organization.
- **If we stop offering online payments altogether,** we will email your owners at least 30 days before, where we can.
- **Deleting your organization.** We will not delete an organization while money is still in motion: an open dispute,
  or a bank debit still clearing.
